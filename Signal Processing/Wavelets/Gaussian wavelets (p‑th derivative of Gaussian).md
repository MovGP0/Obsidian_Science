Continuous family:

$$
\psi_p(t) = \frac{d^p}{dt^p}\left[e^{-t^2/2}\right]
$$

In the frequency domain:

$$
\mathcal F\{\psi_p\}(\omega) \propto (i\omega)^p \; e^{-\omega^2/2}
$$

> [!note]
> - Fourier transform behaves like $(j\omega)^p e^{-\omega^2/2}$.
> - These wavelets have $p$ vanishing moments and no compact support.
> - This wavelet has p vanishing moments and infinite support.
