> [!abstract] 
> In 1687, [Sir Isaac Newton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isaac_Newton) published the *[Principia Mathematica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Principia_Mathematica)*.
>  Newton developed his findings into three fundamental laws of the motion of matter.

> [!note]
> **Newton’s three laws of motion** are:
> - An object will remain at rest or at constant velocity unless acted upon by an external force, when viewed in an inertial reference frame.  
> - The vector sum of the forces on an object is equal to the rate of change of velocity multiplied by the mass of the object.  
> - When a body exerts a force on a second body, the second body exerts a force equal in magnitude and opposite in direction on the first body.
