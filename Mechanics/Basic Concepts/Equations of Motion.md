As a particle evolves through time, *Newtonian Mechanics* characterizes the behavior of the particle with an **equation of motion**, which — when applied to the system — will result in **deterministic** differential equations that give complete predictability for the scenario.

When a system is deterministic, this means that, if the current state of a system is known and the equations of motion that describe the evolution of the system are known, past and future states can be determined with complete predictability.

For a system of $n$ dimensions to be deterministic, we need to know $2n$ pieces of information about the system. So, for a free particle that can move in three-dimensional space, we might choose the **position and velocities** or the **position and momenta** along each of the axis $x, y, z$.
