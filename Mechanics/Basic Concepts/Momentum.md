Momentum $\vec{p}$ is the product of the particle’s mass and its velocity.

It is therefore a vector quantity:

$$
\vec{p} = m \vec{v}
$$

If we take the time derivative of both sides we obtain

$$
\frac{d}{dt} \vec{p} = m \frac{d}{dt} \vec{v}
$$
We use Newton’s second law to see that the time derivative of the [[Mechanics/Basic Concepts/Momentum]] is equal to the resultant force on the system.

We can therefore rewrite Newton’s second law by saying *force is the rate of change of momentum*:
$$
\vec{p} = m \dot{\vec{r}}, \quad \vec{F} = \dot{\vec{p}}
$$
> [!note]
> Knowing the position and momentum provides us with complete **Laplacian determinism** of the system, provided we know the equations of motion and have chosen initial conditions.
