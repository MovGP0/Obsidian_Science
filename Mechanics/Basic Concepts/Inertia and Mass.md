A particle’s **inertia** is its resistance to change in its state of motion, whether it be at rest or at a certain velocity.

Inertia is quantitatively measured by the particle’s mass $m$: the more massive an object is, the larger the force required for a given acceleration.

Forces are "influences" that change the state of motion of a system.
