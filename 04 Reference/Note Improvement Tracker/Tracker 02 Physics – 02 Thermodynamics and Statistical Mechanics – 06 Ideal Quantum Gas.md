## Correctness findings

The review found 2 possible errors or notation conflicts. 2 of them are verified (2 fixed); the other 0 are not verified yet. Each item links to the full suggestion entry.

- [[#N1416|N1416]] Internal molecular modes and the ideal quantum gas: Symbol clashes: $\mu$ is both the reduced mass ${\mechanical{\mu}}$ (diatomic section) and the chemical potential ${\energy{\mu}}$. $V$ is both volume ${\mechanical{V}}$ and the bond potential ${\energy{V}}(r)$. $n$ is the vibrational quantum number, the number density, and (in the table) amount of substance. $P$ is both ${\mechanical{P}}_{\mathrm{cm}}$ and pressure. Rename them, for example $m_r$, $U(r)$, $v$ and $\mathbf p_{\mathrm{cm}}$. **Fixed:** Renamed reduced mass to m_r, bond potential to U(r) and U_eff, vibrational quantum number to v, P_cm to p_cm; added table rows for m_r, U, p_cm and p_rel
- [[#N1416|N1416]] Internal molecular modes and the ideal quantum gas: The quantity table has entries that are wrong for this note: $\mechanical{M}$ "Stellar mass" (here molecular mass, listed twice), $n$ in $\unit{mol}$, $\Omega$ "Multiplicity" (here ${\energy{\Omega}}$ is the grand potential), $g$ "Landé factor" (here internal degeneracy), and $\theta,\phi$ orientation angles and $P_{\mathrm{int}},P_{\mathrm{ext}}$, which never appear. Remove or correct these rows. **Fixed:** Removed Stellar mass, n in mol, Omega Multiplicity, Lande g, theta/phi rows; reduced pressure row to P
