---
title: "Kinetic Energy in Generalized Coordinates"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Kinetic energy is quadratic in generalized velocities for time-independent coordinates. Explicit time dependence adds linear and constant terms.

Let $\mechanical{\vec r}_i=\mechanical{\vec r}_i(q,t)$ for generalized coordinates $q_k$. The particle velocities are

$$
\mechanical{\vec v}_i=\sum_k\frac{\partial\mechanical{\vec r}_i}{\partial q_k}\dot q_k
+\frac{\partial\mechanical{\vec r}_i}{\partial t}.
$$

The total kinetic energy is

$$
\energy{T}=\sum_i\tfrac12\mechanical{m}_i\lVert\mechanical{\vec v}_i\rVert^2
=\energy{T}_2(q,\dot q,t)+\energy{T}_1(q,\dot q,t)+\energy{T}_0(q,t).
$$

Here $\energy{T}_2=\tfrac12\sum_{kl}a_{kl}(q,t)\dot q_k\dot q_l$ is quadratic in the generalized velocities, $\energy{T}_1=\sum_k b_k(q,t)\dot q_k$ is linear, and $\energy{T}_0$ is independent of them. For scleronomic constraints and a time-independent choice of coordinates, $\energy{T}=\energy{T}_2$, a homogeneous quadratic form.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{\vec r}_i$ | $\unit{m}$ | Position | The Cartesian position of particle $i$. |
| $\mechanical{\vec v}_i$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Velocity | The velocity of particle $i$. |
| $\mechanical{m}_i$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | The mass of particle $i$. |
| $q_k,\dot q_k$ | — | Generalized coordinate and velocity | The configuration parameters and their time derivatives. |
| $t$ | $\unit{s}$ | Time | The independent variable for the motion. |
| $\energy{T},\energy{T}_2,\energy{T}_1,\energy{T}_0$ | $\unit{J}$ | Kinetic energy | The total and its components by degree in the velocities. |
| $a_{kl},b_k$ | — | Kinetic coefficients | The coefficients of the quadratic and linear terms. |
