---
title: "Derivation of Lagrange's Equations"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Substitute the generalized-coordinate virtual displacements into d’Alembert’s equation to obtain one equation for each independent coordinate.

Start with [[09.06 d’Alembert’s Equation in Symbolic Form|d’Alembert’s equation]], $\sum_i(\mechanical{\vec F}_i-\mechanical{m}_i\mechanical{\vec a}_i)\cdot\delta\mechanical{\vec r}_i=0$. Substitute $\delta\mechanical{\vec r}_i=\sum_k(\partial\mechanical{\vec r}_i/\partial q_k)\delta q_k$.

$$
\sum_k\left[Q_k-\sum_i\mechanical{m}_i\mechanical{\vec a}_i\cdot
\frac{\partial\mechanical{\vec r}_i}{\partial q_k}\right]\delta q_k=0.
$$

Here $Q_k$ is the generalized force. Each bracket is zero because the variations $\delta q_k$ are independent.

**Identity.** The inertial term can be written in terms of kinetic energy.

$$
\sum_i\mechanical{m}_i\mechanical{\vec a}_i\cdot\frac{\partial\mechanical{\vec r}_i}{\partial q_k}
=\frac{d}{dt}\left(\frac{\partial\energy{T}}{\partial\dot q_k}\right)-\frac{\partial\energy{T}}{\partial q_k}.
$$

The proof uses $\partial\mechanical{\vec v}_i/\partial\dot q_k=\partial\mechanical{\vec r}_i/\partial q_k$ and $d(\partial\mechanical{\vec r}_i/\partial q_k)/dt=\partial\mechanical{\vec v}_i/\partial q_k$.

**Result.** The equations of motion in generalized coordinates are

$$
\frac{d}{dt}\left(\frac{\partial\energy{T}}{\partial\dot q_k}\right)
-\frac{\partial\energy{T}}{\partial q_k}=Q_k,\qquad k=1,\ldots,n.
$$

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{\vec F}_i$ | $\unit{N}$ | Active force | The applied force on particle $i$. |
| $\mechanical{m}_i$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | The mass of particle $i$. |
| $\mechanical{\vec a}_i,\mechanical{\vec v}_i$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-2}},\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Acceleration and velocity | The actual particle acceleration and velocity. |
| $\mechanical{\vec r}_i,\delta\mechanical{\vec r}_i$ | $\unit{m}$ | Position and virtual displacement | The position of particle $i$ and its virtual change. |
| $q_k,\dot q_k,\delta q_k$ | — | Generalized coordinate, velocity, and variation | The independent coordinate and its time derivative and variation. |
| $Q_k$ | — | Generalized force | The force conjugate to $q_k$. |
| $\energy{T}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Kinetic energy | The total energy of motion. |
| $t$ | $\unit{s}$ | Time | The independent variable for the motion. |
