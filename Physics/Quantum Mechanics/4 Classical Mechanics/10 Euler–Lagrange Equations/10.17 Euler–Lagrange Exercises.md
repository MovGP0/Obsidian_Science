---
title: "Euler–Lagrange Exercises"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Construct Lagrangians and obtain equations of motion and conserved momenta.

## Exercise 1

**Basic.** Write the Lagrangian of a simple pendulum and derive its equation of motion.

**Result.** $\energy{L}=\tfrac12\mechanical{m}\mechanical{\ell}^2\mechanical{\dot\theta}^{\,2}+\mechanical{m}\mechanical{g}\mechanical{\ell}\cos\mechanical{\theta}$ and $\mechanical{\ddot\theta}+(\mechanical{g}/\mechanical{\ell})\sin\mechanical{\theta}=0$.

## Exercise 2

**Basic.** Write the free-particle Lagrangian in Cartesian coordinates and verify that the Euler–Lagrange equations give $\mechanical{\ddot{\vec r}}=\vec 0$.

## Exercise 3

**Basic.** Write the Lagrangian of a pendulum whose azimuth rotates about the vertical at prescribed constant angular speed $\mechanical{\omega}$.

**Result.** $\energy{L}=\tfrac12\mechanical{m}\mechanical{\ell}^2(\mechanical{\dot\theta}^{\,2}+\mechanical{\omega}^2\sin^2\mechanical{\theta})+\mechanical{m}\mechanical{g}\mechanical{\ell}\cos\mechanical{\theta}$.

## Exercise 4

**Basic.** A mass $\mechanical{m}$ slides without friction on a vertical guide. A spring of stiffness $\mechanical{k}$ pulls it toward the origin. For upward coordinate $\mechanical{z}$, find the Lagrangian and equation.

**Result.** $\energy{L}=\tfrac12\mechanical{m}\mechanical{\dot z}^2-\tfrac12\mechanical{k}\mechanical{z}^2-\mechanical{m}\mechanical{g}\mechanical{z}$ and $\mechanical{\ddot z}+\mechanical{\omega}^2\mechanical{z}=-\mechanical{g}$, with $\mechanical{\omega}^2=\mechanical{k}/\mechanical{m}$.

## Exercise 5

**Intermediate.** Write the Lagrangian of the planar double pendulum and identify any cyclic coordinates.

**Result.** Neither standard pendulum angle is cyclic in gravity.

## Exercise 6

**Intermediate.** A free particle moves on a cylinder of radius $\mechanical{R}$. In coordinates $(\mechanical{\phi},\mechanical{z})$, write the Lagrangian and equations of motion.

**Result.** $\energy{L}=\tfrac12\mechanical{m}(\mechanical{R}^2\mechanical{\dot\phi}^{\,2}+\mechanical{\dot z}^2)$. The rates $\mechanical{\dot\phi}$ and $\mechanical{\dot z}$ are constant, giving helical motion in general.

## Exercise 7

**Intermediate.** A particle follows a helical guide $\mechanical{z}=c\mechanical{\phi}$ at fixed cylindrical radius $\mechanical{\rho}$ in uniform gravity. Find the Lagrangian and equation for $\mechanical{\phi}$.

**Result.** $\energy{L}=\tfrac12\mechanical{m}(\mechanical{\rho}^2+c^2)\mechanical{\dot\phi}^{\,2}-\mechanical{m}\mechanical{g}c\mechanical{\phi}$ and $\mechanical{\ddot\phi}(\mechanical{\rho}^2+c^2)=-\mechanical{g}c$.

## Exercise 8

**Intermediate.** Show that in central motion the coordinate $\mechanical{\phi}$ is cyclic and $\mechanical{p}_\phi=\mechanical{L}_z$ is constant.

## Exercise 9

**Challenge.** Write the Lagrangian of a symmetric top with a fixed point in Euler angles $(\mechanical{\phi},\mechanical{\theta},\mechanical{\psi})$. Identify the cyclic coordinates.

## Exercise 10

**Challenge.** A mass $\mechanical{m}$ is constrained to a circle of radius $\mechanical{R}$ rotating with constant angular speed $\mechanical{\omega}$ about a vertical diameter in gravity. Write the Lagrangian in terms of the angle $\mechanical{\theta}$ from the downward vertical and find the equilibria.

**Result.** The bottom $\mechanical{\theta}=0$ is stable for $\mechanical{\omega}^2<\mechanical{g}/\mechanical{R}$ and unstable for $\mechanical{\omega}^2>\mechanical{g}/\mechanical{R}$. The top $\mechanical{\theta}=\pi$ is always unstable. For $\mechanical{\omega}^2>\mechanical{g}/\mechanical{R}$, two stable equilibria appear at $\cos\mechanical{\theta}=\mechanical{g}/(\mechanical{R}\mechanical{\omega}^2)$.

## Exercise 11

**Intermediate.** Write the one-dimensional Lagrangian for two equal masses $\mechanical{m}$ joined by a spring of stiffness $\mechanical{k}$. Use center-of-mass coordinate $\mechanical{R}$ and relative coordinate $\mechanical{r}=\mechanical{x}_1-\mechanical{x}_2$. Find the equations of motion.

**Result.** $\mechanical{\ddot R}=0$ and $\mechanical{\mu}\mechanical{\ddot r}=-\mechanical{k}\mechanical{r}$ with $\mechanical{\mu}=\mechanical{m}/2$, when the relative coordinate is measured from its equilibrium value.

## Exercise 12

**Intermediate.** For $\mechanical{\ddot x}+2\signal{\gamma}\mechanical{\dot x}+\signal{\omega}^2\mechanical{x}=0$, find the Lagrangian and the Rayleigh function.

**Result.** $\energy{L}=\tfrac12\mechanical{m}\mechanical{\dot x}^2-\tfrac12\mechanical{m}\signal{\omega}^2\mechanical{x}^2$ and $\energy{\mathcal R}=\tfrac12\mechanical{b}\mechanical{\dot x}^2=\mechanical{m}\signal{\gamma}\mechanical{\dot x}^2$ with $\mechanical{b}=2\mechanical{m}\signal{\gamma}$.

## Exercise 13

**Intermediate.** A disk rolls without slipping down an incline of angle $\mechanical{\alpha}$. Write the Lagrangian in terms of its rotation angle $\mechanical{\theta}$ increasing downhill.

**Result.** $\energy{L}=\tfrac12(\mechanical{M}\mechanical{R}^2+\mechanical{I})\mechanical{\dot\theta}^{\,2}+\mechanical{M}\mechanical{g}\mechanical{R}\mechanical{\theta}\sin\mechanical{\alpha}$.

## Exercise 14

**Intermediate.** Find the Lagrangian for a pendulum whose length $\mechanical{\ell}(t)$ is a prescribed function of time.

**Result.** $\energy{L}=\tfrac12\mechanical{m}(\mechanical{\dot\ell}^{\,2}+\mechanical{\ell}^2\mechanical{\dot\theta}^{\,2})+\mechanical{m}\mechanical{g}\mechanical{\ell}\cos\mechanical{\theta}$. The constraint is rheonomic.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\energy{L}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Lagrangian | The function that determines the equations of motion. |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | The mass of the particle or pendulum bob. |
| $\mechanical{\ell}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Pendulum length | The distance from the pivot to the bob. |
| $\mechanical{g}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-2}}$ | Acceleration due to gravity | The magnitude of the downward gravitational acceleration. |
| $\mechanical{\theta},\mechanical{\dot\theta},\mechanical{\ddot\theta}$ | $\unit{rad},\unit{rad\,s^{-1}},\unit{rad\,s^{-2}}$ | Pendulum angle and derivatives | The angle from vertical and its first two time derivatives. |
| $\mechanical{\ddot{\vec r}}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-2}}$ | Acceleration | The acceleration vector of the particle. |
| $\mechanical{\omega}$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Angular frequency | The imposed angular speed or natural frequency in the stated example. |
| $\mechanical{k}$ | $\unit{N\,m^{-1}}$ | Spring constant | The stiffness of the spring. |
| $\mechanical{z},\mechanical{R},\mechanical{\rho}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Height and radii | The vertical coordinate and cylinder or circle radii. |
| $\mechanical{\dot z},\mechanical{\ddot z}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}},\unit{m\,s^{-2}}$ | Vertical velocity and acceleration | The time derivatives of the vertical coordinate. |
| $\mechanical{\phi},\mechanical{\psi}$ | $\unit{rad}$ | Orientation angles | The azimuthal angle or the Euler angle of spin. |
| $\mechanical{\dot\phi},\mechanical{\ddot\phi}$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}},\unit{rad\,s^{-2}}$ | Azimuthal rates | The first and second time derivatives of azimuth. |
| $c$ | $\unit{m\,rad^{-1}}$ | Helix coefficient | The height gained per unit angle. |
| $\mechanical{p}_\phi,\mechanical{L}_z$ | $\unit{kg\,m^2\,s^{-1}}$ | Axial angular momentum | The conserved azimuthal momentum. |
| $\mechanical{R},\mechanical{r},\mechanical{x}_1,\mechanical{x}_2,\mechanical{x}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Position | The center-of-mass, relative, particle, or oscillator coordinate. |
| $\mechanical{\ddot R},\mechanical{\ddot r},\mechanical{\ddot x}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-2}}$ | Acceleration | The second time derivative of the indicated position. |
| $\mechanical{\mu},\mechanical{M}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Reduced or total mass | The reduced mass of the pair or the mass of the disk. |
| $\signal{\gamma}$ | $\unit{s^{-1}}$ | Damping rate | Half the linear damping coefficient divided by mass. |
| $\signal{\omega}$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Oscillator frequency | The undamped natural angular frequency. |
| $\mechanical{\dot x},\mechanical{\dot\ell}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Velocity | The rate of change of the oscillator displacement or pendulum length. |
| $\mechanical{b}$ | $\unit{kg\,s^{-1}}$ | Linear damping coefficient | The proportionality between damping force and velocity. |
| $\energy{\mathcal R}$ | $\unit{W}$ | Rayleigh dissipation function | The function that gives the linear damping force. |
| $\mechanical{\alpha}$ | $\unit{rad}$ | Inclination | The angle of the rolling incline. |
| $\mechanical{I}$ | $\unit{kg\,m^2}$ | Moment of inertia | The moment of the disk about its center. |
| $t$ | $\unit{s}$ | Time | The argument of the prescribed pendulum length. |
