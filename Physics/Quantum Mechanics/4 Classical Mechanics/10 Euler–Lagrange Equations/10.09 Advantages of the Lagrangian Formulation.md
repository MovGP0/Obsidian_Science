---
title: "Advantages of the Lagrangian Formulation"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The Lagrangian formulation handles ideal constraints, preserves the form of the equations under coordinate changes, and reveals conserved momenta.

- **Constraint forces.** Independent generalized coordinates incorporate ideal holonomic constraints directly.
- **Coordinate changes.** Rewrite the Lagrangian $\energy{L}$ in new generalized coordinates. The equations retain their Euler–Lagrange form.
- **Conserved quantities.** Cyclic coordinates give conserved conjugate momenta.
- **Connection to quantum mechanics.** The action $S=\int\energy{L}\,dt$ also appears in Feynman's path integral formulation.

> [!note] Analytical Mechanics
> Different choices of generalized coordinates can give different expressions for the same Lagrangian. The form of the equations of motion is invariant.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\energy{L}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Lagrangian | The function used to obtain the equations of motion. |
| $S$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Action | The time integral of the Lagrangian. |
| $t$ | $\unit{s}$ | Time | The independent variable for the integral. |
