---
title: "Landau Symbols"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Big O, little o, and asymptotic equivalence describe relative size near a limit.

Fix a limit for $x$ and take $g(x)$ nonzero near the limit wherever a ratio is used.

- **Big O.** $f(x)=O(g(x))$ if a constant $C>0$ bounds $\lvert f(x)\rvert\le C\lvert g(x)\rvert$ for all $x$ sufficiently close to the limit.
- **Little o.** $f(x)=o(g(x))$ if $f(x)/g(x)\to0$.
- **Asymptotic equivalence.** $f(x)\sim g(x)$ if $f(x)/g(x)\to1$.
