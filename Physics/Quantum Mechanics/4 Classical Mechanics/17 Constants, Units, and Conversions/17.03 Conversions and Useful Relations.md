---
title: "Conversions and Useful Relations"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Common conversions for pressure, energy, power, angular speed, and length.

| Original unit | Equivalent value |
| --- | --- |
| Standard atmosphere | $\quantity{1}{atm}=\quantity{101325}{Pa}=\quantity{760}{Torr}$ |
| Bar | $\quantity{1}{bar}=\quantity{10^5}{Pa}$ |
| Kilowatt-hour | $\quantity{1}{kW\,h}=\quantity{3.6\times10^6}{J}$ |
| Thermochemical calorie | $\quantity{1}{cal}=\quantity{4.184}{J}$ |
| Electronvolt | $\quantity{1}{eV}\simeq\quantity{1.602\times10^{-19}}{J}$ |
| Mechanical horsepower | $1\,\mathrm{hp}\simeq\quantity{745.7}{W}$ |
| Revolutions per minute | $1\,\mathrm{rpm}=(2\pi/60)\,\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}\simeq\quantity{0.1047}{rad\,s^{-1}}$ |
| Ångström | $1\,\text{Å}=\quantity{10^{-10}}{m}$ |
