title: "Typical Orders of Magnitude"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Representative scales for motion, gravity, pressure, and energy.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| — | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Typical vehicle speed | About $\quantity{30}{m\,s^{-1}}$. |
| — | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Sound speed in air | About $\quantity{340}{m\,s^{-1}}$. |
| — | $\unit{km\,s^{-1}}$ | Escape speed from Earth | About $\quantity{11.2}{km\,s^{-1}}$. |
| — | $\unit{min}$ | Orbital period at an altitude of 400 km | About $\quantity{92}{min}$. |
| — | $\unit{J}$ | Earth's rotational energy | About $\quantity{2.1\times10^{29}}{J}$. |
| — | $\unit{kg}$ | Typical atomic mass | About $\quantity{10^{-26}}{kg}$. |
| — | $\unit{Pa}$ | Atmospheric pressure | About $\quantity{10^5}{Pa}$. |
| — | $\unit{Pa}$ | Maximum pressure at the ocean floor | About $\quantity{10^8}{Pa}$. |
| — | $\unit{kJ}$ | Kinetic energy of an athlete | About $\quantity{1}{kJ}$. |
| — | $\unit{J}$ | Energy of a lightning strike | About $\quantity{10^9}{J}$. |
