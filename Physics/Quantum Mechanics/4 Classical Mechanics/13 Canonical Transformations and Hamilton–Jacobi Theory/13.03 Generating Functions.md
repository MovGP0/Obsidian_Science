---
title: "Generating Functions"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Four types of generating functions construct canonical transformations using different pairs of independent variables.

A **generating function** constructs a canonical transformation explicitly. The four standard types differ in their choice of independent variables from $\{q,p,Q,P\}$.

The two variational principles are equivalent if their action forms differ by an exact differential.

$$
\sum_k p_k\,dq_k-\energy{H}\,dt=\sum_k P_k\,dQ_k-\energy{K}\,dt+dF.
$$

Choose $(q,Q,t)$ as independent variables and compare coefficients of the differentials to obtain the type 1 relations. Legendre transformations give the other three types.

**Type 1.** $F_1(q,Q,t)$.

$$
p_k=\frac{\partial F_1}{\partial q_k},\qquad P_k=-\frac{\partial F_1}{\partial Q_k},\qquad
\energy{K}=\energy{H}+\frac{\partial F_1}{\partial t}.
$$

**Type 2.** $F_2(q,P,t)$.

$$
p_k=\frac{\partial F_2}{\partial q_k},\qquad Q_k=\frac{\partial F_2}{\partial P_k},\qquad
\energy{K}=\energy{H}+\frac{\partial F_2}{\partial t}.
$$

This is the most common type. For example, $F_2=\sum_k q_k P_k$ generates the identity $Q=q$, $P=p$.

**Type 3.** $F_3(p,Q,t)$.

$$
q_k=-\frac{\partial F_3}{\partial p_k},\qquad P_k=-\frac{\partial F_3}{\partial Q_k},\qquad
\energy{K}=\energy{H}+\frac{\partial F_3}{\partial t}.
$$

**Type 4.** $F_4(p,P,t)$.

$$
q_k=-\frac{\partial F_4}{\partial p_k},\qquad Q_k=\frac{\partial F_4}{\partial P_k},\qquad
\energy{K}=\energy{H}+\frac{\partial F_4}{\partial t}.
$$

**Example. Harmonic oscillator.** For $\energy{H}=p^2/(2\mechanical{m})+\tfrac12\mechanical{m}\signal{\omega}^2q^2$, take $F_1(q,Q)=(\mechanical{m}\signal{\omega}q^2/2)\cot Q$. The type 1 equations give

$$
p=\mechanical{m}\signal{\omega}q\cot Q,\qquad
P=\frac{\mechanical{m}\signal{\omega}q^2}{2}\csc^2 Q.
$$

Substitution gives $\energy{K}=\signal{\omega}P$. Therefore $\dot Q=\signal{\omega}$ and $\dot P=0$, with solutions $Q=\signal{\omega}t+Q_0$ and $P=\energy{E}/\signal{\omega}$. The inverse transformation gives the usual harmonic motion.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $q_k,p_k,Q_k,P_k$ | — | Canonical variables | The old and new coordinates and momenta. |
| $F,F_1,F_2,F_3,F_4$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Generating functions | The functions whose derivatives relate the old and new variables. |
| $\energy{H},\energy{K}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Hamiltonians | The Hamiltonians in the old and new coordinates. |
| $t$ | $\unit{s}$ | Time | The independent variable for the motion. |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | The oscillator mass. |
| $\signal{\omega}$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Angular frequency | The natural frequency of the oscillator. |
| $\energy{E}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Energy | The constant value of the oscillator Hamiltonian. |
