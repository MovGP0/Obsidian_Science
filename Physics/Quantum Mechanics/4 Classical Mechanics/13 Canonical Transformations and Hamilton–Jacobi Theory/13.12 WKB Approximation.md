---
title: "WKB Approximation"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The leading WKB phase satisfies the classical Hamilton–Jacobi equation. The next order gives the amplitude away from turning points.

The semiclassical **Wentzel–Kramers–Brillouin approximation** connects the Schrödinger equation and the Hamilton–Jacobi equation. In one spatial dimension, substitute $\psi=A(q)e^{i\energy{S}(q)/\hbar}$ into the stationary Schrödinger equation.

$$
-\frac{\hbar^2}{2\mechanical{m}}\psi''+\energy{U}\psi=\energy{E}\psi.
$$

Expand in powers of $\hbar$. The term of order zero gives

$$
\frac{(\energy{S}')^2}{2\mechanical{m}}+\energy{U}(q)=\energy{E}.
$$

This is Hamilton's characteristic equation for $\energy{W}(q)$. In the classically allowed region,

$$
\energy{S}(q)=\pm\int\sqrt{2\mechanical{m}\bigl(\energy{E}-\energy{U}(q)\bigr)}\,dq=\pm\int p(q)\,dq,
$$

where $p(q)$ is the positive momentum magnitude. As $\hbar\to0$, away from turning points, the wave functions are modulated waves with $\psi\sim e^{i\energy{S}/\hbar}/\sqrt{p(q)}$. Their phase follows the classical Hamilton–Jacobi solution.

**Example. Bohr–Sommerfeld quantization.** For a closed orbit with two simple turning points, the phase accumulated in one period must be a multiple of $2\pi$ after the turning-point phase shifts. The Maslov correction is $1/2$, giving

$$
\oint p\,dq=2\pi\hbar(n+\tfrac12),\qquad n=0,1,2,\ldots
$$

Thus the action $J=(2\pi)^{-1}\oint p\,dq$ takes the values $J=\hbar(n+\tfrac12)$. For the harmonic oscillator this gives $\energy{E}_n=\hbar\signal{\omega}(n+\tfrac12)$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\psi,A$ | $\unit{m^{-1/2}}$ | Wave function and amplitude | The normalized one-dimensional wave function and its slowly varying amplitude. |
| $q$ | $\unit{m}$ | Position | The spatial variable in the stationary equation. |
| $p(q)$ | $\unit{kg\,m\,s^{-1}}$ | Momentum magnitude | The local classical momentum in the allowed region. |
| $\energy{S},\energy{W},\hbar$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Action functions and scale | The WKB phase action, characteristic function, and reduced Planck constant. |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | The particle mass. |
| $\energy{U},\energy{E},\energy{E}_n$ | $\unit{J}$ | Potential and total energy | The potential, stationary energy, and quantized energy level. |
| $J$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Action | The phase integral divided by $2\pi$. |
| $\signal{\omega}$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Angular frequency | The natural frequency of the harmonic oscillator. |
