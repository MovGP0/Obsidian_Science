---
title: "Coefficient of Restitution"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The coefficient of restitution is the ratio of separation speed to approach speed along the collision direction.

For a real collision between the elastic and perfectly inelastic limits, define the **coefficient of restitution**.

$$
e=\frac{\lvert\mechanical{v}'_2-\mechanical{v}'_1\rvert}
{\lvert\mechanical{v}_2-\mechanical{v}_1\rvert}.
$$

The numerator is the relative speed of separation after the collision. The denominator is the relative speed of approach before it. In this model $e=1$ is elastic, $e=0$ is perfectly inelastic, and $0<e<1$ is intermediate. The change in kinetic energy for a one-dimensional collision is

$$
\Delta\energy{K}=-\tfrac12\mechanical{\mu}(\mechanical{v}_1-\mechanical{v}_2)^2(1-e^2).
$$

### Example — Bouncing Ball

A ball falls from height $\mechanical{h}_0$ and bounces on the ground. With constant $e$ and negligible air drag, the height after bounce $n$ satisfies $\mechanical{h}_n=e^2\mechanical{h}_{n-1}=e^{2n}\mechanical{h}_0$. Neglect the duration of contact. The total time for all the bounces follows from a geometric series.

$$
T_{\mathrm{tot}}=\sqrt{\frac{2\mechanical{h}_0}{\mechanical{g}}}\,\frac{1+e}{1-e}.
$$

It is finite for $0\le e<1$ and diverges as $e\to1$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $e$ | 1 | Coefficient of restitution | The separation speed divided by the approach speed. |
| $\mechanical{v}_1,\mechanical{v}_2,\mechanical{v}'_1,\mechanical{v}'_2$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Velocity | The initial and final signed velocities of the colliding bodies. |
| $\mechanical{\mu}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Reduced mass | The effective mass of the relative motion. |
| $\Delta\energy{K}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Kinetic energy change | The final kinetic energy minus the initial kinetic energy. |
| $\mechanical{h}_0,\mechanical{h}_n$ | $\unit{m}$ | Height | The release height and the maximum height after the stated bounce. |
| $\mechanical{g}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-2}}$ | Acceleration due to gravity | The magnitude of the downward acceleration in free fall. |
| $T_{\mathrm{tot}}$ | $\unit{s}$ | Total elapsed time | The sum of the initial fall time and all subsequent flight times. |
