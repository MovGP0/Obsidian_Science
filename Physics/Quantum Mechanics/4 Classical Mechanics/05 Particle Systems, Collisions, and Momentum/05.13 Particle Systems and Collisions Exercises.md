> [!TLDR]
> Apply momentum and energy balances to particle systems, collisions, and rockets.

## Exercise 1

**Basic.** Three bodies have masses $\mechanical{m}_1=\quantity{1}{kg}$, $\mechanical{m}_2=\quantity{2}{kg}$, and $\mechanical{m}_3=\quantity{3}{kg}$ at positions $\mechanical{\vec r}_1=(0,0)\unit{m}$, $\mechanical{\vec r}_2=(2,0)\unit{m}$, and $\mechanical{\vec r}_3=(0,3)\unit{m}$. Find the center of mass.

**Result.** $\mechanical{\vec R}=(4/6,9/6)\unit{m}=(2/3,3/2)\unit{m}$.

## Exercise 2

**Basic.** In an elastic one-dimensional collision, $\mechanical{m}_1=\quantity{2}{kg}$ moves at $\mechanical{v}_1=\quantity{5}{m\,s^{-1}}$ toward a stationary $\mechanical{m}_2=\quantity{3}{kg}$. Find the final velocities.

**Result.** $\mechanical{v}'_1=\quantity{-1}{m\,s^{-1}}$ and $\mechanical{v}'_2=\quantity{4}{m\,s^{-1}}$.

## Exercise 3

**Basic.** Repeat Exercise 2 for a perfectly inelastic collision. Find the common final velocity and the kinetic energy loss.

**Result.** $\mechanical{v}'=\quantity{2}{m\,s^{-1}}$ and $\Delta\energy{K}_{\mathrm{loss}}=\quantity{15}{J}$.

## Exercise 4

**Basic.** Find the speed gain of a rocket with exhaust speed $\mechanical{u}=\quantity{3}{km\,s^{-1}}$ and mass ratio $\mechanical{m}_0/\mechanical{m}_f=5$.

**Result.** $\Delta\mechanical{v}\approx\quantity{4.83}{km\,s^{-1}}$.

## Exercise 5

**Intermediate.** A mass $\mechanical{m}$ collides elastically with an identical stationary mass. Prove that the two final velocities are perpendicular when both are nonzero.

## Exercise 6

**Intermediate.** In a ballistic pendulum, a projectile of mass $\mechanical{m}=\quantity{0.01}{kg}$ embeds in a block of mass $\mechanical{M}=\quantity{1}{kg}$. The block rises by $\mechanical{h}=\quantity{0.05}{m}$. Find the initial projectile speed.

**Result.** $\mechanical{v}\approx\quantity{100}{m\,s^{-1}}$.

## Exercise 7

**Intermediate.** A ball of mass $\mechanical{m}$ moves in a circle of radius $\mechanical{r}$ at angular speed $\mechanical{\omega}$. Calculate $\mechanical{\vec L}$ about the center.

**Result.** $\mechanical{L}=\mechanical{m}\mechanical{r}^2\mechanical{\omega}$. The vector is perpendicular to the plane.

## Exercise 8

**Intermediate.** A disk of moment of inertia $\mechanical{I}_1$ rotates at $\mechanical{\omega}_1$. It couples to a stationary coaxial disk of moment of inertia $\mechanical{I}_2$. Find the common final angular velocity.

**Result.** $\mechanical{\omega}'=\mechanical{I}_1\mechanical{\omega}_1/(\mechanical{I}_1+\mechanical{I}_2)$.

## Exercise 9

**Challenge.** Two particles interact through a central force. Show that their relative motion takes place in a plane.

## Exercise 10

**Challenge.** A chain of length $\mechanical{L}$ and linear mass density $\mechanical{\lambda}$ starts from rest with length $\mechanical{x}_0$ hanging over the edge of a table. Neglect friction. Find the speed of the chain when its upper end leaves the table.

**Result.** $\mechanical{v}(\mechanical{L})=\sqrt{\mechanical{g}(\mechanical{L}^2-\mechanical{x}_0^2)/\mechanical{L}}$.

## Exercise 11

**Intermediate.** A ball bounces with coefficient of restitution $e=0.8$. How many impacts does it take for the rebound height to fall below $1\%$ of the initial height?

**Result.** $\mechanical{h}_n/\mechanical{h}_0=e^{2n}<0.01$ requires $n>\log(0.01)/(2\log(0.8))\approx10.3$. The first such impact is $n=11$.

## Exercise 12

**Intermediate.** For a neutron hitting carbon ($A=12$), find the maximum fraction of the incident kinetic energy transferred in a head-on elastic collision.

**Result.** $4A/(A+1)^2=48/169\approx0.284$, or $28.4\%$.

## Exercise 13

**Intermediate.** A ball of mass $\mechanical{m}_1=\quantity{1}{kg}$ moving at $\mechanical{v}_0=\quantity{5}{m\,s^{-1}}$ collides elastically with a stationary ball of mass $\mechanical{m}_2=\quantity{2}{kg}$. Find the final laboratory velocities.

**Result.** $\mechanical{v}'_1=\quantity{-5/3}{m\,s^{-1}}$ and $\mechanical{v}'_2=\quantity{10/3}{m\,s^{-1}}$.

## Exercise 14

**Intermediate.** A two-stage rocket has exhaust speed $\mechanical{u}_1=\quantity{3}{km\,s^{-1}}$ and mass ratio $4$ in the first stage. The second stage has exhaust speed $\mechanical{u}_2=\quantity{4}{km\,s^{-1}}$ and mass ratio $5$. Find the total ideal speed gain.

**Result.** $\Delta\mechanical{v}=\mechanical{u}_1\ln 4+\mechanical{u}_2\ln 5\approx(4.16+6.44)\,\unit{km\,s^{-1}}\approx\quantity{10.6}{km\,s^{-1}}$.

## Exercise 15

**Challenge.** Prove König's theorem, $\energy{K}=\tfrac12\mechanical{M}\mechanical{V}_{\mathrm{CM}}^2+\energy{K}_{\mathrm{rel}}$, from the definition of kinetic energy. Use $\sum_i\mechanical{m}_i\mechanical{\vec v}'_i=\vec 0$, where $\mechanical{\vec v}'_i=\mechanical{\vec v}_i-\mechanical{\vec V}_{\mathrm{CM}}$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{m}_1,\mechanical{m}_2,\mechanical{m}_3$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | The masses of the particles. |
| $\mechanical{\vec r}_1,\mechanical{\vec r}_2,\mechanical{\vec r}_3,\mechanical{\vec R}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Position | The particle positions and their center of mass. |
| $\mechanical{v}_1,\mechanical{v}'_1,\mechanical{v}'_2,\mechanical{v}'$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Collision velocity | The initial or final signed velocities of the bodies. |
| $\Delta\energy{K}_{\mathrm{loss}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Kinetic energy loss | The kinetic energy dissipated during impact. |
| $\mechanical{u},\Delta\mechanical{v}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Exhaust speed and speed gain | The relative exhaust speed and the ideal rocket speed increase. |
| $\mechanical{m}_0,\mechanical{m}_f$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Rocket mass | The initial and final masses of the rocket. |
| $\mechanical{m},\mechanical{M}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | The masses of the bodies in the equal-mass and ballistic pendulum examples. |
| $\mechanical{h},\mechanical{h}_0,\mechanical{h}_n$ | $\unit{m}$ | Height | The pendulum rise or the initial and rebound heights of a bouncing ball. |
| $\mechanical{v}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Speed | The speed of the projectile or chain. |
| $\mechanical{r}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Radius | The radius of the circular path. |
| $\mechanical{\omega},\mechanical{\omega}_1,\mechanical{\omega}'$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Angular velocity | The angular velocity for circular motion or for the disks. |
| $\mechanical{\vec L},\mechanical{L}$ | $\unit{kg\,m^2\,s^{-1}}$ | Angular momentum | The vector angular momentum and its magnitude about the center. |
| $\mechanical{I}_1,\mechanical{I}_2$ | $\unit{kg\,m^2}$ | Moment of inertia | The rotational inertia of each disk. |
| $\mechanical{L},\mechanical{x}_0$ | $\unit{m}$ | Chain lengths | The total chain length and the initial hanging length. |
| $\mechanical{\lambda}$ | $\unit{kg\,m^{-1}}$ | Linear mass density | The mass of the chain per unit length. |
| $\mechanical{g}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-2}}$ | Acceleration due to gravity | The magnitude of the downward acceleration in free fall. |
| $e$ | 1 | Coefficient of restitution | The ratio of the separation speed to the approach speed. |
| $A$ | 1 | Nuclear mass ratio | The approximate mass of the carbon nucleus relative to the neutron. |
| $\mechanical{v}_0$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Incident speed | The speed of the ball before impact. |
| $\mechanical{u}_1,\mechanical{u}_2$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Stage exhaust speeds | The relative exhaust speeds of the two rocket stages. |
| $\mechanical{M},\mechanical{m}_i$ | $\unit{kg}$ | System and particle masses | The total mass and individual masses in the kinetic energy decomposition. |
| $\energy{K},\energy{K}_{\mathrm{rel}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Kinetic energy | The total kinetic energy and the kinetic energy relative to the center of mass. |
| $\mechanical{\vec V}_{\mathrm{CM}},\mechanical{V}_{\mathrm{CM}}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Center-of-mass velocity | The velocity of the center of mass and its magnitude. |
| $\mechanical{\vec v}_i,\mechanical{\vec v}'_i$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Particle velocity | The velocity of particle $i$ in the laboratory or center-of-mass frame. |
