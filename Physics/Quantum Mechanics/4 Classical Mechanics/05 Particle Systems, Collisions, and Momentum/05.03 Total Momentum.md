---
title: "Total Momentum"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The total momentum equals the total mass times the center-of-mass velocity. A component stays constant when the corresponding net external force component is zero.

> [!definition] Definition 5.2 — Momentum of a System
> $$
> \mechanical{\vec P}=\sum_i\mechanical{m}_i\mechanical{\vec v}_i=\mechanical{M}\mechanical{\vec V}.
> $$

> [!theorem] Theorem 5.1 — Conservation of Momentum
> If the net external force is zero, $\mechanical{\vec P}$ is constant. Each component of $\mechanical{\vec P}$ is constant when the corresponding component of the net external force is zero.

**Proof.** If the total mass stays constant and $\mechanical{\vec F}_{\mathrm{ext}}=\vec 0$, then

$$
\frac{d\mechanical{\vec P}}{dt}=\mechanical{M}\mechanical{\ddot{\vec R}}
=\mechanical{\vec F}_{\mathrm{ext}}=\vec 0.
$$

### Example — Rifle Recoil

A rifle of mass $\mechanical{M}$ starts at rest. It fires a bullet of mass $\mechanical{m}$ at speed $\mechanical{v}$. If $\mechanical{V}$ is the recoil speed, conservation of momentum gives $0=\mechanical{m}\mechanical{v}-\mechanical{M}\mechanical{V}$. Thus $\mechanical{V}=\mechanical{m}\mechanical{v}/\mechanical{M}$. The momenta of the rifle and bullet have equal magnitudes and opposite directions.

> [!note] Physics I
> For many problems, setting $\mechanical{\vec P}_{\mathrm{initial}}=\mechanical{\vec P}_{\mathrm{final}}$ gives the result without a detailed analysis of the forces.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{\vec P}$ | $\unit{kg\,m\,s^{-1}}$ | Total momentum | The vector sum of the individual particle momenta. |
| $\mechanical{m}_i,\mechanical{M}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | System masses | The mass of each particle and their total mass in the theorem. |
| $\mechanical{\vec v}_i,\mechanical{\vec V}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Velocities | The particle velocities and the center-of-mass velocity. |
| $\mechanical{\ddot{\vec R}}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-2}}$ | Center-of-mass acceleration | The acceleration caused by the net external force. |
| $\mechanical{\vec F}_{\mathrm{ext}}$ | $\unit{N}$ | Net external force | The sum of all forces from outside the system. |
| $t$ | $\unit{s}$ | Time | The independent variable for the momentum derivative. |
| $\mechanical{M},\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Rifle and bullet masses | The separate masses of the rifle and bullet in the example. |
| $\mechanical{v},\mechanical{V}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Bullet and recoil speeds | The speeds of the bullet and rifle relative to the initial rest frame. |
