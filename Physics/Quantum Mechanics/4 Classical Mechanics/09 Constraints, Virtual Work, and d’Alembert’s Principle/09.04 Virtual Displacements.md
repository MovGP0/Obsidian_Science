title: "Virtual Displacements"

> [!TLDR]
> A virtual displacement is an infinitesimal change of configuration that satisfies the constraints at a fixed time.

> [!definition] Definition 9.3 — Virtual Displacement
> A **virtual displacement** $\delta\mechanical{\vec r}_i$ is an infinitesimal displacement compatible with the constraints at fixed time. It need not occur in the actual motion.

In generalized coordinates,

$$
\delta\mechanical{\vec r}_i=\sum_k\frac{\partial\mechanical{\vec r}_i}{\partial q_k}\,\delta q_k.
$$

The variations $\delta q_k$ are independent for independent holonomic coordinates. The actual displacement includes the explicit time term.

$$
d\mechanical{\vec r}_i
=\sum_k\frac{\partial\mechanical{\vec r}_i}{\partial q_k}\dot q_k\,dt
+\frac{\partial\mechanical{\vec r}_i}{\partial t}\,dt.
$$

**Example.** For a simple pendulum, $\mechanical{\vec r}=(\mechanical{\ell}\sin\mechanical{\theta},-\mechanical{\ell}\cos\mechanical{\theta})$. The derivative with respect to $\mechanical{\theta}$ is $(\mechanical{\ell}\cos\mechanical{\theta},\mechanical{\ell}\sin\mechanical{\theta})$. Multiplying by $\delta\mechanical{\theta}$ gives the virtual displacement, tangent to the circle and perpendicular to the cord.

Virtual displacements identify infinitesimally close configurations that satisfy the constraints. Their independent components correspond to the degrees of freedom.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{\vec r}_i,\mechanical{\vec r}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Position | The Cartesian position of a particle or pendulum bob. |
| $\delta\mechanical{\vec r}_i,d\mechanical{\vec r}_i$ | $\unit{m}$ | Displacement | The virtual or actual change in position. |
| $q_k,\delta q_k$ | — | Generalized coordinate and variation | An independent configuration parameter and its variation. |
| $\dot q_k$ | — | Generalized velocity | The time derivative of the generalized coordinate. |
| $t,dt$ | $\unit{s}$ | Time | The time coordinate and its differential. |
| $\mechanical{\ell}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Pendulum length | The distance from the pivot to the bob. |
| $\mechanical{\theta},\delta\mechanical{\theta}$ | $\unit{rad}$ | Pendulum angle and variation | The angle from the downward vertical and its virtual change. |
