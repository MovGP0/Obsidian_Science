---
title: "Generalized Forces"
---

> [!TLDR]
> A generalized force is the coefficient of a generalized-coordinate variation in the virtual work.

> [!definition] Definition 9.4 — Generalized Force
> The generalized force associated with $q_k$ is
>
> $$
> Q_k=\sum_i\mechanical{\vec F}_i^{\,\mathrm{act}}
> \cdot\frac{\partial\mechanical{\vec r}_i}{\partial q_k}.
> $$

The virtual work of the active forces is

$$
\delta\energy{W}=\sum_i\mechanical{\vec F}_i^{\,\mathrm{act}}\cdot\delta\mechanical{\vec r}_i
=\sum_k Q_k\,\delta q_k.
$$

**Forces from a potential.** If $\mechanical{\vec F}_i^{\,\mathrm{act}}=-\partial\energy{U}/\partial\mechanical{\vec r}_i$, then $Q_k=-\partial\energy{U}/\partial q_k$. This is the form most often used in the following applications.

### Example — Simple Pendulum

Take $q=\mechanical{\theta}$ and $\mechanical{\vec r}=(\mechanical{\ell}\sin\mechanical{\theta},-\mechanical{\ell}\cos\mechanical{\theta})$. The position derivative is $(\mechanical{\ell}\cos\mechanical{\theta},\mechanical{\ell}\sin\mechanical{\theta})$, and the force of gravity is $\mechanical{\vec F}=(0,-\mechanical{m}\mechanical{g})$. The generalized force is $Q_\theta=-\mechanical{m}\mechanical{g}\mechanical{\ell}\sin\mechanical{\theta}$. The same result follows from $-\partial\energy{U}/\partial\mechanical{\theta}$ with $\energy{U}=-\mechanical{m}\mechanical{g}\mechanical{\ell}\cos\mechanical{\theta}$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $q_k$ | — | Generalized coordinate | An independent parameter that describes the configuration. |
| $Q_k$ | — | Generalized force | The force conjugate to $q_k$. Its unit is energy divided by the unit of $q_k$. |
| $\mechanical{\vec F}_i^{\,\mathrm{act}}$ | $\unit{N}$ | Active force | The applied force on particle $i$ apart from the ideal reaction. |
| $\mechanical{\vec r}_i$ | $\unit{m}$ | Position | The Cartesian position of particle $i$. |
| $\delta\mechanical{\vec r}_i$ | $\unit{m}$ | Virtual displacement | The displacement of particle $i$ consistent with the constraints. |
| $\delta q_k$ | — | Coordinate variation | The virtual change in generalized coordinate $q_k$. |
| $\delta\energy{W}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Virtual work | The work of the active forces for the virtual displacement. |
| $\energy{U}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Potential energy | The energy from which the conservative forces are obtained. |
| $Q_\theta$ | $\unit{N\,m}$ | Generalized torque | The force conjugate to the pendulum angle. |
| $\mechanical{\theta}$ | $\unit{rad}$ | Pendulum angle | The angular displacement from the downward vertical. |
| $\mechanical{\ell},\mechanical{\vec r}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Pendulum length and position | The cord length and bob position relative to the pivot. |
| $\mechanical{\vec F}$ | $\unit{N}$ | Weight | The gravitational force on the pendulum bob. |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | The mass of the pendulum bob. |
| $\mechanical{g}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-2}}$ | Acceleration due to gravity | The magnitude of the downward acceleration in free fall. |
