title: "Newtonian Dynamics Exercises"

> [!TLDR]
> Use Newton's laws to calculate forces and accelerations in practical systems.

## Exercise 1

**Basic.** A body of mass $\mechanical{m}=\quantity{5}{kg}$ slides down a plane inclined at $\mechanical{\alpha}=30\unit{{}^\circ}$. The coefficient of kinetic friction is $\mechanical{\mu}_d=0.2$. Calculate the acceleration along the plane.

**Result.** $\mechanical{a}=\mechanical{g}(\sin\mechanical{\alpha}-\mechanical{\mu}_d\cos\mechanical{\alpha})\approx\quantity{3.20}{m\,s^{-2}}$.

## Exercise 2

**Basic.** An Atwood machine has masses $\mechanical{m}_1=\quantity{2}{kg}$ and $\mechanical{m}_2=\quantity{3}{kg}$. Calculate the acceleration and tension.

**Result.** $\mechanical{a}=\mechanical{g}/5\approx\quantity{1.96}{m\,s^{-2}}$ and $\mechanical{T}=\quantity{12}{kg}\,\mechanical{g}/5\approx\quantity{23.5}{N}$.

## Exercise 3

**Basic.** A car follows a flat curve of radius $\mechanical{R}=\quantity{50}{m}$ at speed $\mechanical{v}=\quantity{15}{m\,s^{-1}}$. What minimum coefficient of static friction prevents the car from skidding?

**Result.** $\mechanical{\mu}_s\ge\mechanical{v}^2/(\mechanical{g}\mechanical{R})\approx0.46$.

## Exercise 4

**Basic.** A horizontal force $\mechanical{F}=\quantity{50}{N}$ pulls a block of mass $\mechanical{m}=\quantity{10}{kg}$. The coefficient of kinetic friction is $\mechanical{\mu}_d=0.3$. Calculate the acceleration.

**Result.** $\mechanical{a}=(\mechanical{F}-\mechanical{\mu}_d\mechanical{m}\mechanical{g})/\mechanical{m}\approx\quantity{2.06}{m\,s^{-2}}$.

## Exercise 5

**Intermediate.** A platform rotates at $\mechanical{\omega}=\quantity{2}{rad\,s^{-1}}$. It carries a block at distance $\mechanical{r}=\quantity{0.5}{m}$ from its axis. The coefficient of static friction is $\mechanical{\mu}_s=0.4$. Does the block slip relative to the platform?

**Result.** $\mechanical{\omega}^2\mechanical{r}=\quantity{2}{m\,s^{-2}}<\mechanical{\mu}_s\mechanical{g}\approx\quantity{3.92}{m\,s^{-2}}$. The block does not slip.

## Exercise 6

**Intermediate.** A projectile launches vertically upward at speed $\mechanical{v}_0$ through a medium with drag $\mechanical{f}=-\mechanical{b}\mechanical{v}$. Write the equation of motion and determine the terminal velocity.

**Result.** With upward positive, $\mechanical{\dot v}=-\mechanical{g}-\mechanical{b}\mechanical{v}/\mechanical{m}$. The terminal velocity during the fall is $\mechanical{v}_{\mathrm{lim}}=-\mechanical{m}\mechanical{g}/\mechanical{b}$.

## Exercise 7

**Intermediate.** Two blocks of masses $\mechanical{m}_1$ and $\mechanical{m}_2$ are in contact on a smooth horizontal surface. A horizontal force $\mechanical{\vec F}$ pushes the first block. Calculate their common acceleration and the contact force between them.

**Result.** $\mechanical{a}=\mechanical{F}/(\mechanical{m}_1+\mechanical{m}_2)$ and $\mechanical{F}_c=\mechanical{m}_2\mechanical{F}/(\mechanical{m}_1+\mechanical{m}_2)$.

## Exercise 8

**Intermediate.** A block of mass $\mechanical{m}$ hangs from a cord and accelerates upward at $\mechanical{a}$. Calculate the tension.

**Result.** $\mechanical{T}=\mechanical{m}(\mechanical{g}+\mechanical{a})$.

## Exercise 9

**Challenge.** In a frame fixed to Earth, show that a projectile launched vertically upward lands west of its launch point. Find the deflection in terms of latitude $\mechanical{\lambda}$ and maximum height $\mechanical{h}$.

**Result.** The magnitude of the westward deflection is

$$
\Delta\mechanical{x}\approx\frac{8}{3}\mechanical{\omega}_T\mechanical{h}
\sqrt{\frac{2\mechanical{h}}{\mechanical{g}}}\cos\mechanical{\lambda}
=\frac{4}{3}\frac{\mechanical{\omega}_T\mechanical{v}_0^3}{\mechanical{g}^2}\cos\mechanical{\lambda},
$$

where $\mechanical{v}_0=\sqrt{2\mechanical{g}\mechanical{h}}$.

## Exercise 10

**Challenge.** A uniform chain of length $\mechanical{L}$ and linear mass density $\mechanical{\lambda}$ hangs over the edge of a table. Initially, the hanging length is $\mechanical{x}(0)=\mechanical{x}_0<\mechanical{L}$. Neglect friction and write the equation of motion for the hanging length $\mechanical{x}(t)$.

**Result.** $\mechanical{\lambda}\mechanical{L}\mechanical{\ddot x}=\mechanical{\lambda}\mechanical{x}\mechanical{g}$, so $\mechanical{\ddot x}=(\mechanical{g}/\mechanical{L})\mechanical{x}$. The equation has an exponentially growing solution.

## Exercise 11

**Intermediate.** A sphere of radius $\mechanical{R}=\quantity{0.5}{mm}$ and density $\mechanical{\rho}_s=\quantity{7800}{kg\,m^{-3}}$ falls through glycerol of viscosity $\mechanical{\eta}=\quantity{1.5}{Pa\,s}$ and density $\mechanical{\rho}_f=\quantity{1260}{kg\,m^{-3}}$. Find the terminal velocity with buoyancy included.

**Result.** $\mechanical{v}_\infty=2\mechanical{R}^2(\mechanical{\rho}_s-\mechanical{\rho}_f)\mechanical{g}/(9\mechanical{\eta})\approx\quantity{2.4\times10^{-3}}{m\,s^{-1}}$.

## Exercise 12

**Intermediate.** A body of mass $\mechanical{m}$ is on a plane inclined at angle $\mechanical{\alpha}$ with coefficient of static friction $\mechanical{\mu}_s$. Find the critical angle between static equilibrium and sliding.

**Result.** $\tan\mechanical{\alpha}_c=\mechanical{\mu}_s$.

## Exercise 13

**Intermediate.** Two blocks of masses $\mechanical{m}_1=\quantity{3}{kg}$ and $\mechanical{m}_2=\quantity{5}{kg}$ are connected by an ideal cord. Both slide on a surface with $\mechanical{\mu}_d=0.2$. A horizontal force $\mechanical{F}=\quantity{30}{N}$ acts on the first block. Find the common acceleration and cord tension.

**Result.** $\mechanical{a}=(\mechanical{F}-\mechanical{\mu}_d(\mechanical{m}_1+\mechanical{m}_2)\mechanical{g})/(\mechanical{m}_1+\mechanical{m}_2)\approx\quantity{1.79}{m\,s^{-2}}$ and $\mechanical{T}=\mechanical{m}_2(\mechanical{a}+\mechanical{\mu}_d\mechanical{g})\approx\quantity{18.8}{N}$.

## Exercise 14

**Intermediate.** A ball launches upward with speed $\mechanical{v}_0$ in a medium with linear drag $-\mechanical{b}\mechanical{v}$. Find the maximum height and compare it with the result for zero drag.

**Result.** With the positive terminal speed $\mechanical{v}_\infty=\mechanical{m}\mechanical{g}/\mechanical{b}$,

$$
\mechanical{h}_{\max}=\frac{\mechanical{v}_\infty}{\mechanical{g}}
\left[\mechanical{v}_0-\mechanical{v}_\infty\ln(1+\mechanical{v}_0/\mechanical{v}_\infty)\right]
<\frac{\mechanical{v}_0^2}{2\mechanical{g}}.
$$

## Exercise 15

**Challenge.** A particle is constrained to a horizontal circle of radius $\mechanical{R}$. A spring of constant $\mechanical{k}$ and zero rest length connects it to the center. Describe the motion. Find when the spring supplies exactly the centripetal force required for uniform circular motion.

**Result.** No force acts tangentially, so the particle moves with constant angular speed $\mechanical{\omega}=\mechanical{v}_0/\mechanical{R}$, fixed by its initial tangential speed. The spring force $\mechanical{k}\mechanical{R}$ equals the required centripetal force $\mechanical{m}\mechanical{\omega}^2\mechanical{R}$ when $\mechanical{\omega}=\sqrt{\mechanical{k}/\mechanical{m}}$, or $\mechanical{v}_0=\mechanical{R}\sqrt{\mechanical{k}/\mechanical{m}}$. At other speeds, the constraint force supplies the difference. The tangential coordinate is cyclic, so there are no tangential oscillations.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{m}, \mechanical{m}_1, \mechanical{m}_2$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | The mass of the sliding body or the loads of the Atwood machine. |
| $\mechanical{\alpha}, \mechanical{\alpha}_c$ | $\unit{rad}$ | Inclination | The angle of the plane above the horizontal and its value at the onset of sliding. |
| $\mechanical{\mu}_d$ | 1 | Coefficient of kinetic friction | The ratio of kinetic friction magnitude to normal force. |
| $\mechanical{a}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-2}}$ | Acceleration | The acceleration along the incline or the magnitude of the acceleration in the Atwood machine. |
| $\mechanical{g}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-2}}$ | Acceleration due to gravity | The magnitude of the downward acceleration in free fall. |
| $\mechanical{T}$ | $\unit{N}$ | Tension | The force transmitted by the cord. |
| $\mechanical{R}, \mechanical{r}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Radius | The distance from the center of a curve or from the rotation axis. |
| $\mechanical{v}, \mechanical{v}_0, \mechanical{v}_{\mathrm{lim}}, \mechanical{v}_\infty$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Speed or velocity | The instantaneous, initial, or terminal value for the stated problem. |
| $\mechanical{\mu}_s$ | 1 | Coefficient of static friction | The ratio of limiting static friction magnitude to normal force. |
| $\mechanical{F}, \mechanical{\vec F}$ | $\unit{N}$ | Applied force | The force acting horizontally on the block. |
| $\mechanical{\omega}$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Angular speed | The rate of rotation of the platform. |
| $\mechanical{f}$ | $\unit{N}$ | Drag force | The signed force exerted by the medium. |
| $\mechanical{b}$ | $\unit{kg\,s^{-1}}$ | Linear drag coefficient | The proportionality between drag magnitude and speed. |
| $\mechanical{\dot v}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-2}}$ | Vertical acceleration | The derivative of the signed vertical velocity. |
| $\mechanical{F}_c$ | $\unit{N}$ | Contact force | The force one block exerts on the other. |
| $\mechanical{\lambda}$ | $\unit{rad}$ | Latitude | The angle north or south of the equator. |
| $\mechanical{\lambda}$ | $\unit{kg\,m^{-1}}$ | Linear mass density | The mass per unit length of the chain. |
| $\mechanical{\omega}_T$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Earth's angular speed | The magnitude of Earth's rotation rate. |
| $\mechanical{h}, \mechanical{h}_{\max}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Maximum height | The greatest vertical distance reached above the launch point. |
| $\Delta\mechanical{x}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Westward deflection | The magnitude of the horizontal displacement at landing. |
| $\mechanical{L}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Chain length | The total length of the chain. |
| $\mechanical{x}, \mechanical{x}_0$ | $\unit{m}$ | Hanging length | The instantaneous and initial lengths that hang over the edge. |
| $\mechanical{\ddot x}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-2}}$ | Chain acceleration | The second time derivative of the hanging length. |
| $t$ | $\unit{s}$ | Time | The time elapsed since the initial state. |
| $\mechanical{\rho}_s, \mechanical{\rho}_f$ | $\unit{kg\,m^{-3}}$ | Density | The mass density of the sphere or surrounding fluid. |
| $\mechanical{\eta}$ | $\unit{Pa\,s}$ | Dynamic viscosity | The viscosity of glycerol. |
| $\mechanical{k}$ | $\unit{N\,m^{-1}}$ | Spring constant | The stiffness of the spring. |
