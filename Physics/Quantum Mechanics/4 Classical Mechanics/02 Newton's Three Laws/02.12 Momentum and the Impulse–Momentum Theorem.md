---
title: "Momentum and the Impulse–Momentum Theorem"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The impulse of the net force over a time interval equals the change in momentum.

The **impulse** of a force over the time interval $[t_1,t_2]$ is

$$
\mechanical{\vec J}=\int_{t_1}^{t_2}\mechanical{\vec F}\,dt.
$$

> [!theorem] Theorem 2.1 — Impulse–Momentum Theorem
> The change in a particle's momentum equals the impulse of the net force.
>
> $$
> \mechanical{\vec J}=\Delta\mechanical{\vec p}=\mechanical{\vec p}(t_2)-\mechanical{\vec p}(t_1).
> $$

This is the integral form of Newton's second law. It is particularly useful for collisions, in which large forces act for a short time.

**Impulsive forces.** An impulsive force becomes very large while its impulse $\mechanical{\vec J}=\int\mechanical{\vec F}\,dt$ remains finite as $\Delta t\to 0$. In that limit, the momentum changes abruptly by $\mechanical{\vec J}$.

### Example — Ball Rebounding from a Wall

A ball of mass $\mechanical{m}=\quantity{0.2}{kg}$ strikes a wall at $\mechanical{v}_0=\quantity{5}{m\,s^{-1}}$ and rebounds at $\mechanical{v}_f=\quantity{-3}{m\,s^{-1}}$. The wall's impulse on the ball along the axis of motion is $\mechanical{J}=\mechanical{m}(\mechanical{v}_f-\mechanical{v}_0)=\quantity{-1.6}{N\,s}$. If contact lasts $\Delta t=\quantity{0.01}{s}$, the average force is $\mechanical{F}\approx\mechanical{J}/\Delta t=\quantity{-160}{N}$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{\vec J}, \mechanical{J}$ | $\unit{N\,s}$ | Impulse | The time integral of the force or its component along the axis of impact. |
| $\mechanical{\vec F}, \mechanical{F}$ | $\unit{N}$ | Force | The force during the interval or its signed average component. |
| $t, t_1, t_2$ | $\unit{s}$ | Time | The integration variable and the endpoints of the interval. |
| $\Delta t$ | $\unit{s}$ | Duration | The length of the interval during which the force acts. |
| $\mechanical{\vec p}, \Delta\mechanical{\vec p}$ | $\unit{kg\,m\,s^{-1}}$ | Momentum and change in momentum | The particle's momentum and its change over the interval. |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | The mass of the ball. |
| $\mechanical{v}_0, \mechanical{v}_f$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Impact and rebound velocities | The signed velocities before and after contact with the wall. |
