---
title: "Third Law – Action and Reaction"
---

> [!TLDR]
> When two bodies interact, each exerts a force on the other with the same magnitude and opposite direction.

> [!definition] Definition 2.3 — Newton's Third Law
> If body $A$ exerts force $\mechanical{\vec F}_{A\to B}$ on body $B$, then $B$ exerts force
>
> $$
> \mechanical{\vec F}_{B\to A}=-\mechanical{\vec F}_{A\to B}
> $$
>
> on $A$. The forces have the same magnitude and opposite directions.

In the form used here, the action and reaction forces have the same line of action. They act on different bodies, so they cannot cancel in the force balance of either body alone.

**Consequence.** The internal forces sum to zero for the system as a whole. This is the basis for conservation of [[05.03 Total Momentum|total momentum]], covered in Chapter 5.

### Example — Two Masses Connected by a Cord

Two bodies of masses $\mechanical{m}_1$ and $\mechanical{m}_2$ are connected by an ideal cord with negligible mass and no extension. The cord transmits the same tension $\mechanical{T}$ to both. It pulls the first body toward the second with force $\mechanical{\vec T}$ and pulls the second toward the first with force $-\mechanical{\vec T}$. These internal forces sum to zero.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{\vec F}_{A\to B}, \mechanical{\vec F}_{B\to A}$ | $\unit{N}$ | Interaction forces | The forces the two bodies exert on each other. |
| $\mechanical{m}_1, \mechanical{m}_2$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | The masses of the two connected bodies. |
| $\mechanical{T}, \mechanical{\vec T}$ | $\unit{N}$ | Tension | The magnitude and directed force transmitted by the cord. |
