---
title: "Fundamental Forces and Contact Forces"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Gravitation and electromagnetism are the fundamental interactions relevant to macroscopic mechanics. Electromagnetic interactions between neighboring atoms give rise to contact forces.

## Fundamental Forces

The fundamental interactions relevant on macroscopic scales are:

- **Gravitation.** The [[06.02 Law of Gravitation|gravitational force]] is $\mechanical{\vec F}=-\mechanical{G}\mechanical{m}\mechanical{M}\hat r/\mechanical{r}^2$.
- **Electromagnetism.** The Lorentz force is $\mechanical{\vec F}=\electric{q}(\electric{\vec E}+\mechanical{\vec v}\times\magnetic{\vec B})$.

The strong and weak nuclear forces act at subnuclear scales. They do not enter this classical description.

## Contact Forces

At macroscopic scales, electromagnetic interactions between neighboring atoms produce contact forces between surfaces. This category includes:

- The normal force $\mechanical{\vec N}$, perpendicular to the surface. It comes from the resistance of the atomic lattice to compression.
- Friction $\mechanical{\vec f}$, parallel to the surface.
- Tension $\mechanical{\vec T}$ in a cord.
- The elastic force of a spring, $\mechanical{\vec F}=-\mechanical{k}\Delta\mechanical{\vec x}$.
- Drag from a medium such as air or liquid.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{\vec F}$ | $\unit{N}$ | Force | The gravitational, Lorentz, or spring force in the stated model. |
| $\mechanical{G}$ | $\unit{N\,m^2\,kg^{-2}}$ | Gravitational constant | The constant in Newton's law of gravitation. |
| $\mechanical{m}, \mechanical{M}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | The masses of the two bodies in the gravitational interaction. |
| $\mechanical{r}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Separation | The distance between the two masses. |
| $\electric{q}$ | $\unit{C}$ | Electric charge | The charge on which the Lorentz force acts. |
| $\electric{\vec E}$ | $\unit{V\,m^{-1}}$ | Electric field | The field that gives the electric part of the Lorentz force. |
| $\magnetic{\vec B}$ | $\unit{T}$ | Magnetic flux density | The field that gives the magnetic part of the Lorentz force. |
| $\mechanical{\vec v}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Velocity | The velocity of the charged body. |
| $\mechanical{\vec N}$ | $\unit{N}$ | Normal force | The contact force perpendicular to the surface. |
| $\mechanical{\vec f}$ | $\unit{N}$ | Friction force | The contact force parallel to the surface. |
| $\mechanical{\vec T}$ | $\unit{N}$ | Tension | The force transmitted by a cord. |
| $\mechanical{k}$ | $\unit{N\,m^{-1}}$ | Spring constant | The ratio of elastic force magnitude to displacement magnitude. |
| $\Delta\mechanical{\vec x}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Displacement | The displacement from the spring's equilibrium position. |
