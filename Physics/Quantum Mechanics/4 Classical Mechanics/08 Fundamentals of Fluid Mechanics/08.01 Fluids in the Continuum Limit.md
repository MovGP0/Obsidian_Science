---
title: "Fluids in the Continuum Limit"
---

> [!TLDR]
> A continuum model replaces individual molecules with fields such as density and velocity that vary in space and time.

A fluid contains an extremely large number of molecules, of order $10^{23}$ per mole. Following each molecule is impractical. In a **continuum description**, continuous fields that vary from point to point replace the microscopic quantities.

**Density field.** The density $\mechanical{\rho}(\mechanical{\vec r},t)$ is the mass per unit volume in a small element around $\mechanical{\vec r}$. The element must be small enough to behave like a point on the macroscopic scale. It must also contain enough molecules that statistical fluctuations are negligible.

**Velocity field.** The velocity $\mechanical{\vec v}(\mechanical{\vec r},t)$ is the mean velocity of the molecules in the volume element. In the **Eulerian description**, an observer stays at a fixed point to describe the flow.

**Pressure and temperature.** Pressure $\mechanical{p}(\mechanical{\vec r},t)$ and temperature $\thermal{T}(\mechanical{\vec r},t)$ are also continuous fields.

**Validity.** The continuum model applies when the relevant macroscopic length scales are much greater than the molecular mean free path. For a gas at atmospheric pressure, the source gives a mean free path of about $\quantity{70}{nm}$. This is small compared with ordinary macroscopic flow scales.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{\rho}$ | $\unit{kg\,m^{-3}}$ | Mass density | The mean mass per unit volume in a small element of fluid. |
| $\mechanical{\vec r}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Position | The location at which the fluid fields are evaluated. |
| $t$ | $\unit{s}$ | Time | The time at which the fluid fields are evaluated. |
| $\mechanical{\vec v}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Fluid velocity | The mean molecular velocity in the local fluid element. |
| $\mechanical{p}$ | $\unit{Pa}$ | Pressure | The local isotropic normal stress in a fluid at equilibrium. |
| $\thermal{T}$ | $\unit{K}$ | Temperature | The local thermodynamic temperature. |
| — | $\unit{m}$ | Molecular mean free path | The mean distance a molecule travels between collisions. |
