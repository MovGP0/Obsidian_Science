---
title: "Archimedes' Principle"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The upward buoyant force on a body equals the weight of the liquid it displaces.

> [!theorem] Theorem 8.2 — Archimedes' Principle
> A body submerged in a liquid experiences an upward buoyant force
>
> $$
> \mechanical{\vec F}_A=-\mechanical{\rho}_f\mechanical{V}\mechanical{\vec g},
> $$
>
> where $\mechanical{\rho}_f$ is the liquid density and $\mechanical{V}$ is the submerged volume in this formula.

**Proof.** The pressure force on the closed wetted surface of a fully submerged body is $\mechanical{\vec F}_A=-\oint\mechanical{p}\,d\mechanical{\vec A}$. The area vector points outward. By the divergence theorem and hydrostatic equilibrium, with $\hat k$ upward,

$$
\mechanical{\vec F}_A=-\int\nabla\mechanical{p}\,dV
=\mechanical{\rho}_f\mechanical{g}\mechanical{V}\hat k,
\qquad \nabla\mechanical{p}=-\mechanical{\rho}_f\mechanical{g}\hat k.
$$

**Floating.** A body floats on the surface if its mean density is less than the liquid density. Now let $\mechanical{V}$ be the total volume of the body. The submerged fraction is $\mechanical{V}_{\mathrm{imm}}/\mechanical{V}=\mechanical{\rho}_{\mathrm{body}}/\mechanical{\rho}_f$. If the mean body density is greater than the liquid density, the body sinks.

### Example — Iceberg

With ice density $\mechanical{\rho}_{\mathrm{ice}}\approx\quantity{920}{kg\,m^{-3}}$ and seawater density $\mechanical{\rho}_W\approx\quantity{1025}{kg\,m^{-3}}$, the fraction above water is $1-\mechanical{\rho}_{\mathrm{ice}}/\mechanical{\rho}_W\approx0.10$. About $10\%$ of the iceberg is visible above the water.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{\vec F}_A$ | $\unit{N}$ | Buoyant force | The net upward force exerted by liquid pressure. |
| $\mechanical{\rho}_f,\mechanical{\rho}_W$ | $\unit{kg\,m^{-3}}$ | Liquid density | The density of the surrounding liquid or seawater. |
| $\mechanical{\rho}_{\mathrm{body}},\mechanical{\rho}_{\mathrm{ice}}$ | $\unit{kg\,m^{-3}}$ | Body density | The mean density of the floating body or the ice. |
| $\mechanical{V}$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Volume | The submerged or total volume, as specified. |
| $\mechanical{V}_{\mathrm{imm}}$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Submerged volume | The part of a floating body's volume below the surface. |
| $\mechanical{\vec g},\mechanical{g}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-2}}$ | Gravitational acceleration | The downward acceleration vector and its magnitude. |
| $\mechanical{p}$ | $\unit{Pa}$ | Pressure | The local hydrostatic pressure. |
| $d\mechanical{\vec A}$ | $\unit{m^2}$ | Oriented area element | The surface element directed along the outward normal. |
