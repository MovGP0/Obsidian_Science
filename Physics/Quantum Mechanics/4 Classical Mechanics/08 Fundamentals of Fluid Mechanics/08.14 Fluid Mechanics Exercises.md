> [!TLDR]
> Apply the laws of hydrostatics and fluid flow to pressure, buoyancy, and flow rate.

## Exercise 1

**Basic.** Find the hydrostatic gauge pressure at depth $\mechanical{h}=\quantity{100}{m}$ below sea level for seawater of density $\mechanical{\rho}=\quantity{1025}{kg\,m^{-3}}$.

**Result.** $\mechanical{p}=\mechanical{\rho}\mechanical{g}\mechanical{h}\approx\quantity{1.005\times10^6}{Pa}\approx\quantity{9.92}{atm}$.

## Exercise 2

**Basic.** An aluminium sphere of density $\mechanical{\rho}=\quantity{2700}{kg\,m^{-3}}$ and volume $\mechanical{V}=\quantity{10^{-3}}{m^3}$ is immersed in water. Find its buoyant force and apparent weight.

**Result.** $\mechanical{F}_A=\mechanical{\rho}_W\mechanical{V}\mechanical{g}\approx\quantity{9.81}{N}$. The apparent weight is $(26.5-9.81)\unit{N}\approx\quantity{16.7}{N}$.

## Exercise 3

**Basic.** Water flows through a horizontal Venturi tube with $\mechanical{A}_1=\quantity{4}{cm^2}$, $\mechanical{A}_2=\quantity{1}{cm^2}$, and $\mechanical{v}_1=\quantity{0.5}{m\,s^{-1}}$. Find the pressure difference.

**Result.** $\mechanical{v}_2=\quantity{2}{m\,s^{-1}}$ and $\Delta\mechanical{p}=\tfrac12\mechanical{\rho}(\mechanical{v}_2^2-\mechanical{v}_1^2)=\quantity{1875}{Pa}$.

## Exercise 4

**Basic.** A hole lies $\mechanical{h}=\quantity{0.5}{m}$ below the free surface. Find the Torricelli efflux speed.

**Result.** $\mechanical{v}\approx\quantity{3.13}{m\,s^{-1}}$.

## Exercise 5

**Intermediate.** A wooden cube of density $\mechanical{\rho}_H=\quantity{700}{kg\,m^{-3}}$ and side length $\mechanical{L}=\quantity{0.10}{m}$ floats in water. What fraction lies above the surface?

**Result.** $1-\mechanical{\rho}_H/\mechanical{\rho}_W=0.30$, or $30\%$.

## Exercise 6

**Intermediate.** For an isothermal atmosphere at $\thermal{T}=\quantity{273}{K}$, find the scale height and the pressure at $\mechanical{z}=\quantity{5}{km}$ relative to the reference pressure.

**Result.** $\mechanical{H}=k_B\thermal{T}/(\mechanical{m}\mechanical{g})\approx\quantity{8000}{m}$ and $\mechanical{p}/\mechanical{p}_0=e^{-5/8}\approx0.535$.

## Exercise 7

**Intermediate.** Water of viscosity $\mechanical{\eta}=\quantity{10^{-3}}{Pa\,s}$ flows in a pipe of radius $\mechanical{R}=\quantity{5}{mm}$ and length $\mechanical{L}=\quantity{1}{m}$ under a pressure drop $\Delta\mechanical{p}=\quantity{100}{Pa}$. Find the Poiseuille flow rate.

**Result.** $\mechanical{Q}=\pi(0.005)^4\cdot100/(8\cdot10^{-3})\,\unit{m^3\,s^{-1}}\approx\quantity{2.45\times10^{-5}}{m^3\,s^{-1}}$.

## Exercise 8

**Challenge.** Derive Bernoulli's equation directly from $\mechanical{\vec F}=\mechanical{m}\mechanical{\vec a}$ for a volume element of an ideal fluid.

## Exercise 9

**Challenge.** Show that in steady, ideal, incompressible flow, Bernoulli's equation relates points on different streamlines in a connected region when the flow is irrotational, $\nabla\times\mechanical{\vec v}=\vec 0$.

## Exercise 10

**Intermediate.** Find the speed of sound in helium at $\quantity{300}{K}$ with $\gamma=5/3$ and $\mechanical{m}=\quantity{6.64\times10^{-27}}{kg}$.

**Result.** $\signal{c}_s=\sqrt{\gamma k_B\thermal{T}/\mechanical{m}}\approx\quantity{1019}{m\,s^{-1}}$.

## Exercise 11

**Intermediate.** Find the Reynolds number for a car of characteristic length $\mechanical{L}=\quantity{4}{m}$ at speed $\mechanical{v}=\quantity{30}{m\,s^{-1}}$ in air of density $\mechanical{\rho}=\quantity{1.2}{kg\,m^{-3}}$ and viscosity $\mechanical{\eta}=\quantity{1.8\times10^{-5}}{Pa\,s}$.

**Result.** $\mathrm{Re}=\mechanical{\rho}\mechanical{v}\mechanical{L}/\mechanical{\eta}\approx8\times10^6$, in a regime where the flow can be strongly turbulent.

## Exercise 12

**Intermediate.** Find the speed of a tsunami in an ocean of depth $\mechanical{h}=\quantity{4}{km}$.

**Result.** $\mechanical{v}=\sqrt{\mechanical{g}\mechanical{h}}\approx\quantity{198}{m\,s^{-1}}$.

## Exercise 13

**Intermediate.** A cylindrical tank holds water to depth $\mechanical{H}=\quantity{1}{m}$. The outlet area $\mechanical{A}_f$ is much smaller than the tank cross-sectional area $\mechanical{A}$. Find the time until the tank empties.

**Result.** Torricelli's law and mass conservation give $t=(\mechanical{A}/\mechanical{A}_f)\sqrt{2\mechanical{H}/\mechanical{g}}\approx(\mechanical{A}/\mechanical{A}_f)\quantity{0.45}{s}$.

| $\mechanical{h}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Depth | The vertical distance below the free surface. |

| $\mechanical{v},\mechanical{\vec v}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Speed or velocity | The speed in the stated flow or propagation example, or the fluid velocity field. |

| $\mathrm{Re}$ | 1 | Reynolds number | The ratio of inertial to viscous effects. |
| $\mechanical{H}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Initial water depth | The depth of the water in the cylindrical tank. |
| $\mechanical{A},\mechanical{A}_f$ | $\unit{m^2}$ | Tank and outlet areas | The cross-sectional area of the tank and the area of the opening. |
| $t$ | $\unit{s}$ | Drain time | The time needed to empty the tank. |
