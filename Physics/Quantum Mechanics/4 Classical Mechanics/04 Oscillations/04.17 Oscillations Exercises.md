title: "Oscillations Exercises"

> [!TLDR]
> Calculate the periods, energies, and responses of free, damped, and driven oscillators.

## Exercise 1

**Basic.** A mass $\mechanical{m}=\quantity{0.5}{kg}$ is attached to a spring with $\mechanical{k}=\quantity{200}{N\,m^{-1}}$. Calculate $\signal{\omega}$, $\signal{T}$, and $\signal{f}$.

**Result.** $\signal{\omega}=\quantity{20}{rad\,s^{-1}}$, $\signal{T}=\quantity{0.314}{s}$, and $\signal{f}=\quantity{3.18}{Hz}$.

## Exercise 2

**Basic.** Find the small-amplitude period of a simple pendulum of length $\mechanical{\ell}=\quantity{1}{m}$.

**Result.** $\signal{T}\approx\quantity{2.01}{s}$.

## Exercise 3

**Basic.** An oscillator has amplitude $\mechanical{A}=\quantity{0.10}{m}$ and spring constant $\mechanical{k}=\quantity{50}{N\,m^{-1}}$. Find its total energy.

**Result.** $\energy{E}=\quantity{0.25}{J}$.

## Exercise 4

**Basic.** An underdamped oscillator has $\signal{\omega}_0=\quantity{10}{rad\,s^{-1}}$ and $\signal{\gamma}=\quantity{1}{s^{-1}}$. Find the time for the amplitude to fall to $10\%$ of its initial value.

**Result.** $t=-\ln(0.10)/\signal{\gamma}\approx\quantity{2.30}{s}$.

## Exercise 5

**Intermediate.** Expand through order $\mechanical{\theta}_0^2$ to show that the pendulum period is $\signal{T}(\mechanical{\theta}_0)\approx\signal{T}_0(1+\mechanical{\theta}_0^2/16)$.

## Exercise 6

**Intermediate.** A $\quantity{1}{kg}$ mass is connected to two springs in parallel, with $\mechanical{k}_1=\quantity{100}{N\,m^{-1}}$ and $\mechanical{k}_2=\quantity{200}{N\,m^{-1}}$. Find the period.

**Result.** $\mechanical{k}=\mechanical{k}_1+\mechanical{k}_2=\quantity{300}{N\,m^{-1}}$ and $\signal{T}\approx\quantity{0.363}{s}$.

## Exercise 7

**Intermediate.** Find the period of the same mass with the two springs in series.

**Result.** $\mechanical{k}=\mechanical{k}_1\mechanical{k}_2/(\mechanical{k}_1+\mechanical{k}_2)=\quantity{200/3}{N\,m^{-1}}$ and $\signal{T}\approx\quantity{0.770}{s}$.

## Exercise 8

**Intermediate.** A driven oscillator has $\signal{\omega}_0=\quantity{5}{rad\,s^{-1}}$, $\signal{\gamma}=\quantity{0.1}{s^{-1}}$, and $\mechanical{F}_0/\mechanical{m}=\quantity{1}{m\,s^{-2}}$. Find the amplitude at resonance.

**Result.** $\mechanical{A}_{\max}\approx\mechanical{F}_0/(2\mechanical{m}\signal{\gamma}\signal{\omega}_0)=\quantity{1}{m}$.

## Exercise 9

**Challenge.** For the underdamped oscillator, show that the logarithmic decrement of successive peaks is $\delta=\ln(\mechanical{x}_n/\mechanical{x}_{n+1})=\signal{\gamma}\signal{T}_d$, with $\signal{T}_d=2\pi/\signal{\omega}_d$. Express $\delta$ in terms of the quality factor.

**Result.** $\delta=\pi/\sqrt{\signal{Q}^2-1/4}\approx\pi/\signal{Q}$ for $\signal{Q}\gg1$.

## Exercise 10

**Challenge.** A pendulum has variable length $\mechanical{\ell}(t)=\mechanical{\ell}_0[1+\varepsilon\cos(2\signal{\omega}_0t)]$, with $\signal{\omega}_0=\sqrt{\mechanical{g}/\mechanical{\ell}_0}$. Explain why the oscillation grows exponentially for suitable values of $\varepsilon$. This is parametric resonance.

## Exercise 11

**Intermediate.** Calculate the quality factor of an oscillator whose amplitude falls by half in $N=50$ complete oscillations.

**Result.** From $e^{-\signal{\gamma}N\signal{T}}=1/2$, $\signal{\gamma}N\signal{T}=\ln 2$. For weak damping, $\signal{Q}=\signal{\omega}_0N\signal{T}/(2\ln 2)\approx\pi N/\ln 2\approx226$.

## Exercise 12

**Intermediate.** A pendulum of length $\mechanical{\ell}=\quantity{0.5}{m}$ swings with amplitude $\mechanical{\theta}_0=30\unit{{}^\circ}$. Estimate the correction to the period relative to the harmonic limit.

**Result.** $\signal{T}/\signal{T}_0\approx1+\mechanical{\theta}_0^2/16\approx1+(0.524)^2/16\approx1.017$. The period is about $\quantity{1.44}{s}$.

## Exercise 13

**Intermediate.** At resonance, find the average power transferred to a driven oscillator with $\mechanical{F}_0=\quantity{5}{N}$, $\signal{\omega}_0=\quantity{10}{rad\,s^{-1}}$, $\signal{\gamma}=\quantity{0.5}{s^{-1}}$, and $\mechanical{m}=\quantity{1}{kg}$.

**Result.** $\energy{P}=\mechanical{F}_0^2/(4\mechanical{m}\signal{\gamma})=\quantity{12.5}{W}$.

## Exercise 14

**Basic.** A mass $\mechanical{m}=\quantity{0.1}{kg}$ on a spring makes 20 oscillations in $\quantity{5}{s}$. Find the spring constant.

**Result.** $\signal{f}=\quantity{4}{Hz}$, $\signal{\omega}=\quantity{8\pi}{rad\,s^{-1}}$, and $\mechanical{k}=\mechanical{m}\signal{\omega}^2\approx\quantity{63.2}{N\,m^{-1}}$.

## Exercise 15

**Challenge.** Analyze two equal masses connected by three equal springs of stiffness $\mechanical{k}$. Write the two normal modes and show how beats arise when only one mass is initially excited.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | The mass of the oscillator. |
| $\mechanical{k},\mechanical{k}_1,\mechanical{k}_2$ | $\unit{N\,m^{-1}}$ | Spring constant | The stiffness of a spring or its combination with another spring. |
| $\signal{\omega},\signal{\omega}_0,\signal{\omega}_d$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Angular frequency | The free, undamped, or damped angular frequency. |
| $\signal{T},\signal{T}_0,\signal{T}_d$ | $\unit{s}$ | Period | The duration of one oscillation under the stated conditions. |
| $\signal{f}$ | $\unit{Hz}$ | Frequency | The number of oscillations per unit time. |
| $\mechanical{\ell},\mechanical{\ell}_0$ | $\unit{m}$ | Pendulum length | The distance from the pivot to the bob, including its nominal value when the length varies. |
| $\mechanical{A},\mechanical{A}_{\max}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Amplitude | The displacement amplitude and its maximum over driving frequencies. |
| $\energy{E}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Total energy | The conserved energy of the free undamped oscillator. |
| $\signal{\gamma}$ | $\unit{s^{-1}}$ | Damping rate | The exponential amplitude decay rate. |
| $t$ | $\unit{s}$ | Elapsed time | The time over which the amplitude decays. |
| $\mechanical{\theta}_0$ | $\unit{rad}$ | Pendulum amplitude | The maximum angular displacement from equilibrium. |
| $\mechanical{F}_0$ | $\unit{N}$ | Driving force amplitude | The peak magnitude of the harmonic force. |
| $\mechanical{x}_n,\mechanical{x}_{n+1}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Peak displacements | Two consecutive peaks of the same sign. |
| $\delta$ | 1 | Logarithmic decrement | The natural logarithm of the ratio of consecutive peaks. |
| $\signal{Q}$ | 1 | Quality factor | The ratio of the natural angular frequency to twice the damping rate. |
| $\varepsilon$ | 1 | Relative length modulation | The amplitude of the periodic length change divided by the nominal length. |
| $\mechanical{g}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-2}}$ | Acceleration due to gravity | The magnitude of the downward acceleration in free fall. |
| $N$ | 1 | Cycle count | The number of complete oscillations in the stated time. |
| $\energy{P}$ | $\unit{W}$ | Average power | The average rate at which the drive supplies energy. |
