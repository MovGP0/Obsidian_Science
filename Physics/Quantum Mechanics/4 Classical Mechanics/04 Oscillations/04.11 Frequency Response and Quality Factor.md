---
title: "Frequency Response and Quality Factor"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The quality factor relates the damping rate to the natural frequency. A high value gives a narrow, high resonance peak.

The steady amplitude $\mechanical{A}(\signal{\Omega})$ of a driven oscillator is characterized by the **quality factor**.

$$
\signal{Q}=\frac{\signal{\omega}_0}{2\signal{\gamma}}.
$$

- In the weak-damping approximation, $\signal{Q}$ is the phase advance in radians at the natural frequency during one energy decay time. The energy envelope falls by a factor $e^{-1}$ in that time. The amplitude falls by the same factor in twice the time, or about $2\signal{Q}$ radians.
- High $\signal{Q}$ gives a high, narrow resonance. The relative half-power bandwidth is $\Delta\signal{\Omega}/\signal{\omega}_0\approx 1/\signal{Q}$.
- Low $\signal{Q}$ means stronger damping and a broad, less pronounced resonance.

**Typical quality factors given in the source.**

- Tuning fork: $\signal{Q}\sim 10^3$.
- Quartz watch oscillator: $\signal{Q}\sim 10^6$.
- Superconducting microwave cavity: $\signal{Q}\sim 10^{10}$.
- Laboratory pendulum: $\signal{Q}\sim 10^2$ to $10^3$.

**Phase shift.** The phase lag $\signal{\delta}(\signal{\Omega})$ between the applied force and the displacement varies continuously from near zero when $\signal{\Omega}\ll\signal{\omega}_0$ to near $\pi$ when $\signal{\Omega}\gg\signal{\omega}_0$.

At $\signal{\Omega}=\signal{\omega}_0$, the lag is $\pi/2$, so the driving force and velocity are in phase. This explains the efficient energy transfer at power resonance.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{A}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Steady displacement amplitude | The amplitude of the steady response at the driving frequency. |
| $\signal{\Omega}$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Driving angular frequency | The angular frequency of the applied force. |
| $\signal{\omega}_0$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Natural angular frequency | The angular frequency of free motion without damping. |
| $\signal{\gamma}$ | $\unit{s^{-1}}$ | Damping rate | The exponential decay rate of the amplitude in the underdamped regime. |
| $\signal{Q}$ | 1 | Quality factor | The natural angular frequency divided by twice the damping rate. |
| $\Delta\signal{\Omega}$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Angular bandwidth | The separation of the half-power frequencies. |
| $\signal{\delta}$ | $\unit{rad}$ | Phase lag | The phase by which displacement lags the force. |
