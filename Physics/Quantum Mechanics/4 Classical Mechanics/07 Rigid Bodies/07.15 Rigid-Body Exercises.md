---
title: "Rigid-Body Exercises"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Apply moments of inertia, rotational dynamics, and conservation laws to rigid bodies.

## Exercise 1

**Basic.** Find the moment of inertia about the central symmetry axis of a disk with $\mechanical{M}=\quantity{2}{kg}$ and $\mechanical{R}=\quantity{0.30}{m}$.

**Result.** $\mechanical{I}=\mechanical{M}\mechanical{R}^2/2=\quantity{0.090}{kg\,m^2}$.

## Exercise 2

**Basic.** Find the moment of inertia of the same disk about a parallel axis at distance $\mechanical{d}=\quantity{0.20}{m}$.

**Result.** $\mechanical{I}=(0.090+2\cdot0.04)\unit{kg\,m^2}=\quantity{0.170}{kg\,m^2}$.

## Exercise 3

**Basic.** A rod of mass $\mechanical{M}=\quantity{1}{kg}$ and length $\mechanical{L}=\quantity{1}{m}$ hangs from one end. Find the period for small oscillations.

**Result.** $\signal{T}=2\pi\sqrt{2\mechanical{L}/(3\mechanical{g})}\approx\quantity{1.64}{s}$.

## Exercise 4

**Basic.** A symmetric top has $\mechanical{I}_3=\quantity{5\times10^{-3}}{kg\,m^2}$, $\mechanical{\omega}_s=\quantity{200}{rad\,s^{-1}}$, $\mechanical{M}=\quantity{0.5}{kg}$, and $\mechanical{d}=\quantity{0.10}{m}$. Find its precession angular frequency.

**Result.** $\signal{\Omega}=\mechanical{M}\mechanical{g}\mechanical{d}/(\mechanical{I}_3\mechanical{\omega}_s)\approx\quantity{0.49}{rad\,s^{-1}}$.

## Exercise 5

**Intermediate.** Use integration in spherical coordinates to show that a solid sphere has moment of inertia $(2/5)\mechanical{M}\mechanical{R}^2$ about a diameter.

## Exercise 6

**Intermediate.** A solid sphere of radius $\mechanical{R}$ and mass $\mechanical{M}$ rolls without slipping down an incline from height $\mechanical{h}$. Find its speed at the bottom.

**Result.** $\mechanical{v}=\sqrt{10\mechanical{g}\mechanical{h}/7}$.

## Exercise 7

**Intermediate.** A uniform rod of length $\mechanical{L}$ and mass $\mechanical{M}$ rests on a frictionless surface. A ball of mass $\mechanical{m}$ hits one end elastically at speed $\mechanical{v}_0$, horizontally and perpendicular to the rod. Find the rod's center-of-mass velocity just after the collision.

**Result.** $\mechanical{V}_{\mathrm{CM}}=2\mechanical{m}\mechanical{v}_0/(\mechanical{M}+4\mechanical{m})$, $\mechanical{\omega}=12\mechanical{m}\mechanical{v}_0/[\mechanical{L}(\mechanical{M}+4\mechanical{m})]$, and $\mechanical{v}'=(4\mechanical{m}-\mechanical{M})\mechanical{v}_0/(\mechanical{M}+4\mechanical{m})$.

## Exercise 8

**Intermediate.** A disk of mass $\mechanical{M}=\quantity{2}{kg}$ and radius $\mechanical{R}=\quantity{0.20}{m}$ stops in $\quantity{5}{s}$ from $\mechanical{\omega}_0=\quantity{10}{rad\,s^{-1}}$ due to axle friction. Find the magnitude of the average braking torque.

**Result.** $\mechanical{\tau}=\mechanical{I}\mechanical{\omega}_0/\Delta t=(0.040\cdot2)\unit{N\,m}=\quantity{0.080}{N\,m}$.

## Exercise 9

**Challenge.** Prove that torque-free rotation about the intermediate principal axis, with $\mechanical{I}_2$ between $\mechanical{I}_1$ and $\mechanical{I}_3$, is unstable. Show that rotation about axes 1 and 3 is stable.

## Exercise 10

**Challenge.** Write the Lagrangian of a symmetric top with a fixed point in Euler angles $(\mechanical{\phi},\mechanical{\theta},\mechanical{\psi})$. Identify two cyclic coordinates.

## Exercise 11

**Intermediate.** A solid sphere, disk, and ring of equal mass and radius roll down the same incline from height $\mechanical{h}$. Which reaches the bottom first?

**Result.** $\mechanical{v}_{\mathrm{sphere}}>\mechanical{v}_{\mathrm{disk}}>\mechanical{v}_{\mathrm{ring}}$. The sphere arrives first.

## Exercise 12

**Intermediate.** An ideal yo-yo is a disk of mass $\mechanical{M}=\quantity{0.1}{kg}$ and radius $\mechanical{R}=\quantity{0.05}{m}$ with its cord wound around the rim. Find the linear acceleration as it falls.

**Result.** $\mechanical{a}=\mechanical{g}/[1+\mechanical{I}/(\mechanical{M}\mechanical{R}^2)]=2\mechanical{g}/3\approx\quantity{6.54}{m\,s^{-2}}$.

## Exercise 13

**Intermediate.** A compound pendulum consists of a uniform rod of length $\mechanical{L}$. Its pivot is at distance $\mechanical{d}$ from the center of mass. Calculate the small-amplitude period and minimize it with respect to $\mechanical{d}$.

**Result.** $\mechanical{d}^*=\mechanical{L}/\sqrt{12}$.

## Exercise 14

**Intermediate.** A uniform door of mass $\mechanical{M}=\quantity{30}{kg}$ and width $\mechanical{w}=\quantity{0.9}{m}$ rotates about an edge. Find the torque that accelerates it from rest to $\mechanical{\omega}=\quantity{1}{rad\,s^{-1}}$ in $\quantity{0.5}{s}$.

**Result.** $\mechanical{I}=\mechanical{M}\mechanical{w}^2/3=\quantity{8.1}{kg\,m^2}$ and $\mechanical{\tau}=\mechanical{I}\mechanical{\omega}/\Delta t=\quantity{16.2}{N\,m}$.

## Exercise 15

**Challenge.** A symmetric top has moment $\mechanical{I}_3$ about its symmetry axis and equal transverse moments $\mechanical{I}_1=\mechanical{I}_2$. Prove that, in torque-free motion, $\mechanical{\vec\omega}$ rotates about the symmetry axis in the body frame at signed angular frequency $\signal{\Omega}=(\mechanical{I}_3-\mechanical{I}_1)\mechanical{\omega}_3/\mechanical{I}_1$. The source calls this the Chandler wobble, the term used for the corresponding motion of Earth's rotation axis.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{I},\mechanical{I}_1,\mechanical{I}_2,\mechanical{I}_3$ | $\unit{kg\,m^2}$ | Moment of inertia | The rotational inertia about the stated axis. |
| $\mechanical{M},\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | The mass of the rigid body or the incident ball. |
| $\mechanical{R},\mechanical{L},\mechanical{d},\mechanical{d}^*,\mechanical{w}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Length | The radius, rod length, axis displacement, optimum pivot distance, or door width. |
| $\mechanical{g}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-2}}$ | Acceleration due to gravity | The magnitude of the downward gravitational acceleration. |
| $\signal{T}$ | $\unit{s}$ | Period | The duration of one small oscillation. |
| $\mechanical{\omega}_s$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Spin angular speed | The angular speed about the top's symmetry axis. |
| $\signal{\Omega}$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Precession angular frequency | The angular frequency of the top's precession. |
| $\mechanical{h}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Height | The vertical drop along the incline. |
| $\mechanical{v},\mechanical{v}_0,\mechanical{v}',\mechanical{V}_{\mathrm{CM}}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Speed or velocity | The speed at the bottom or the signed velocities of the ball and rod in the collision. |
| $\mechanical{\omega},\mechanical{\omega}_0,\mechanical{\vec\omega},\mechanical{\omega}_3$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Angular velocity | The rotation rate in the stated case, including the top's vector and axial component. |
| $\mechanical{\tau}$ | $\unit{N\,m}$ | Average torque magnitude | The magnitude of the torque that stops the disk. |
| $\Delta t$ | $\unit{s}$ | Time interval | The disk braking time or the time to accelerate the door. |
| $\mechanical{\phi},\mechanical{\theta},\mechanical{\psi}$ | $\unit{rad}$ | Euler angles | The orientation coordinates of the symmetric top. |
| $\mechanical{a}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-2}}$ | Linear acceleration | The downward acceleration of the yo-yo. |
