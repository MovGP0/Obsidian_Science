---
title: "Symmetric Top and Precession"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Gravity makes the axis of a fast symmetric top precess about the vertical. The precession rate decreases as the spin rate increases.

A **symmetric top** has $\mechanical{I}_1=\mechanical{I}_2\ne\mechanical{I}_3$, where $\mechanical{I}_3$ is the moment about its symmetry axis. With a fixed point $O$, weight $\mechanical{M}\mechanical{g}$ can produce regular precession.

**Precession rate.** The symmetry axis is tilted by angle $\mechanical{\theta}$ from the vertical. It turns about the vertical at angular frequency

$$
\signal{\Omega}\approx\frac{\mechanical{M}\mechanical{g}\mechanical{d}}{\mechanical{I}_3\mechanical{\omega}_s},
$$

where $\mechanical{d}$ is the distance from $O$ to the center of mass and $\mechanical{\omega}_s$ is the spin rate. For a fast top, $\mechanical{\omega}_s\gg\signal{\Omega}$, and the result is approximately independent of $\mechanical{\theta}$.

**Derivation.** The torque $\mechanical{\vec\tau}=\mechanical{\vec d}\times\mechanical{\vec F}=\mechanical{\vec d}\times(-\mechanical{M}\mechanical{g}\hat k)$ is horizontal and perpendicular to the plane of $\mechanical{\vec d}$ and $\hat k$. From $d\mechanical{\vec L}/dt=\mechanical{\vec\tau}$, it turns the angular momentum about the vertical at $\signal{\Omega}=\mechanical{\tau}/\mechanical{L}_\perp\approx\mechanical{M}\mechanical{g}\mechanical{d}/(\mechanical{I}_3\mechanical{\omega}_s)$.

### Example — Precision Top

For $\mechanical{\omega}_s=\quantity{1000}{rad\,s^{-1}}$, $\mechanical{I}_3=\quantity{10^{-4}}{kg\,m^2}$, $\mechanical{M}=\quantity{0.1}{kg}$, and $\mechanical{d}=\quantity{0.05}{m}$, the precession period is $\signal{T}_p=2\pi\mechanical{I}_3\mechanical{\omega}_s/(\mechanical{M}\mechanical{g}\mechanical{d})\approx\quantity{12.8}{s}$.

> [!note] Analytical Mechanics
> The Lagrangian description of a rigid body uses the Euler angles $(\mechanical{\phi},\mechanical{\theta},\mechanical{\psi})$ as generalized coordinates. The symmetric top is integrable using cyclic variables. See [[10.10 Lagrangian of a Rigid Body]].

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{I}_1,\mechanical{I}_2,\mechanical{I}_3$ | $\unit{kg\,m^2}$ | Principal moments of inertia | The two equal transverse moments and the symmetry-axis moment. |
| $\mechanical{M}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | The mass of the top. |
| $\mechanical{g}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-2}}$ | Acceleration due to gravity | The magnitude of the downward gravitational acceleration. |
| $\mechanical{\theta}$ | $\unit{rad}$ | Tilt angle | The angle between the symmetry axis and vertical. |
| $\mechanical{\phi},\mechanical{\psi}$ | $\unit{rad}$ | Euler angles | The other two orientation coordinates in the Lagrangian description. |
| $\mechanical{d},\mechanical{\vec d}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Pivot-to-center displacement | The lever arm and its magnitude. |
| $\mechanical{\omega}_s$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Spin angular speed | The rotation rate about the symmetry axis. |
| $\signal{\Omega},\signal{T}_p$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}},\unit{s}$ | Precession rate and period | The angular frequency about the vertical and the time for one revolution. |
| $\mechanical{\vec F}$ | $\unit{N}$ | Weight | The gravitational force on the top. |
| $\mechanical{\vec\tau},\mechanical{\tau}$ | $\unit{N\,m}$ | Torque | The torque of weight about the fixed point and its magnitude. |
| $\mechanical{\vec L},\mechanical{L}_\perp$ | $\unit{kg\,m^2\,s^{-1}}$ | Angular momentum | The angular momentum vector and the magnitude of its horizontal component. |
| $t$ | $\unit{s}$ | Time | The independent variable in the torque equation. |
