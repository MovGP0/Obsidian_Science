---
title: "Rigid Bodies"
---

> [!TLDR]
> A rigid body has fixed distances between its parts. Its motion separates into translation and rotation.

A **rigid body** is a system of particles whose mutual distances stay constant during motion. This is the standard model for macroscopic objects with negligible deformation. A rigid body in three dimensions has six degrees of freedom: three for translation of the center of mass and three for rotation. Its dynamics has one equation for the center of mass and another for angular momentum about the center of mass.

## Chapter Summary

- [[07.02 Rigid-Body Kinematics|Rigid-body motion]] gives $\mechanical{\vec v}_P=\mechanical{\vec v}_O+\mechanical{\vec\omega}\times(\mechanical{\vec r}_P-\mechanical{\vec r}_O)$.
- The [[07.03 Center of Mass and Moment of Inertia|moment of inertia]] is $\mechanical{I}=\int\mechanical{\rho}\mechanical{d}_\perp^2\,dV$.
- The [[07.04 Parallel-Axis Theorem (Huygens–Steiner Theorem)|parallel-axis theorem]] gives $\mechanical{I}=\mechanical{I}_G+\mechanical{M}\mechanical{d}^2$.
- For [[07.05 Rotation About a Fixed Axis|rotation about a fixed axis]], $\mechanical{\tau}=\mechanical{I}\mechanical{\ddot\theta}$ and $\energy{K}=\tfrac12\mechanical{I}\mechanical{\omega}^2$.
- The [[07.06 Inertia Tensor and Principal Axes|inertia tensor]] $\mechanical{\mathbf I}$ is diagonal in its principal axes with entries $\mechanical{I}_1,\mechanical{I}_2,\mechanical{I}_3$.
- [[07.08 Euler's Equations|Euler's equations]] describe rotation in the body-fixed principal-axis frame.
- For a [[07.10 Symmetric Top and Precession|fast symmetric top]], regular precession has $\signal{\Omega}\approx\mechanical{M}\mechanical{g}\mechanical{d}/(\mechanical{I}_3\mechanical{\omega}_s)$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{\vec v}_P,\mechanical{\vec v}_O$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Point velocities | The velocities of two points fixed to the body. |
| $\mechanical{\vec r}_P,\mechanical{\vec r}_O$ | $\unit{m}$ | Point positions | The positions of the two points. |
| $\mechanical{\vec\omega},\mechanical{\omega},\mechanical{\omega}_s$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Angular velocity | The rotation vector or its rate about the given axis. |
| $\mechanical{I},\mechanical{I}_G,\mechanical{I}_1,\mechanical{I}_2,\mechanical{I}_3,\mechanical{\mathbf I}$ | $\unit{kg\,m^2}$ | Rotational inertia | The moments about the specified axes or the full tensor. |
| $\mechanical{\rho}$ | $\unit{kg\,m^{-3}}$ | Density | The mass per unit volume. |
| $\mechanical{d},\mechanical{d}_\perp$ | $\unit{m}$ | Distance | The distance between axes, from a mass element to the axis, or from the top's pivot to its center of mass. |
| $\mechanical{M}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | The mass of the rigid body. |
| $\mechanical{\tau}$ | $\unit{N\,m}$ | Torque | The net torque about the fixed rotation axis. |
| $\mechanical{\ddot\theta}$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-2}}$ | Angular acceleration | The rate of change of angular speed about the axis. |
| $\energy{K}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Rotational kinetic energy | The kinetic energy associated with rotation. |
| $\signal{\Omega}$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Precession rate | The angular frequency at which the top's axis rotates about the vertical. |
| $\mechanical{g}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-2}}$ | Acceleration due to gravity | The magnitude of the downward gravitational acceleration. |
