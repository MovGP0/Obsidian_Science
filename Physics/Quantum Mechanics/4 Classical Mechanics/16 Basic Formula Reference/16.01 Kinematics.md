> [!TLDR]
> Reference formulas for velocity, acceleration, projectile motion, circular motion, and Galilean relative velocity.

- **Velocity and acceleration.** $\mechanical{\vec v}=\mechanical{\dot{\vec r}}$, $\mechanical{\vec a}=\mechanical{\ddot{\vec r}}$.
- **Constant acceleration in one dimension.** $\mechanical{v}=\mechanical{v}_0+\mechanical{a}t$, $\mechanical{x}=\mechanical{x}_0+\mechanical{v}_0t+\tfrac12\mechanical{a}t^2$, $\mechanical{v}^2=\mechanical{v}_0^2+2\mechanical{a}(\mechanical{x}-\mechanical{x}_0)$.
- **Projectile returning to its launch height.** $\mechanical{x}_g=\mechanical{v}_0^2\sin(2\alpha)/\mechanical{g}$, $\mechanical{y}_h=\mechanical{v}_0^2\sin^2\alpha/(2\mechanical{g})$.
- **Circular motion.** $\mechanical{v}=\mechanical{\omega}\mechanical{R}$, $\mechanical{a}_c=\mechanical{v}^2/\mechanical{R}=\mechanical{\omega}^2\mechanical{R}$, $\signal{T}=2\pi/\mechanical{\omega}$ for positive angular speed.
- **Galilean relative velocity.** $\mechanical{\vec v}=\mechanical{\vec V}+\mechanical{\vec v}'$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{\vec r},\mechanical{x},\mechanical{x}_0,\mechanical{x}_g,\mechanical{y}_h,\mechanical{R}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Position and lengths | Position, initial position, range, maximum height, and circular radius. |
| $\mechanical{\vec v},\mechanical{v},\mechanical{v}_0,\mechanical{\vec V},\mechanical{\vec v}'$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Velocities and speeds | Instantaneous, initial, frame, and relative velocities. |
| $\mechanical{\vec a},\mechanical{a},\mechanical{a}_c,\mechanical{g}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-2}}$ | Accelerations | The total, constant, centripetal, and gravitational accelerations. |
| $\alpha$ | $\unit{rad}$ | Launch angle | The angle above the horizontal. |
| $\mechanical{\omega}$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Angular speed | The rate of circular motion. |
| $t,\signal{T}$ | $\unit{s}$ | Time and period | The elapsed time and time per revolution. |
