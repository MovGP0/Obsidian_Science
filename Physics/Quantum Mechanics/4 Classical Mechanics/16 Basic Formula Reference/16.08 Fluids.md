> [!TLDR]
> Reference formulas for pressure, buoyancy, continuity, and fluid flow.

- **Pressure and hydrostatic balance.** $\mechanical{p}=d\mechanical{F}_\perp/d\mechanical{A}$ and $d\mechanical{p}/d\mechanical{z}=-\mechanical{\rho}\mechanical{g}$ for upward $\mechanical{z}$.
- **Constant-density fluid.** $\mechanical{p}(\mechanical{h})=\mechanical{p}_0+\mechanical{\rho}\mechanical{g}\mechanical{h}$ at depth $\mechanical{h}$.
- **Buoyancy.** $\mechanical{\vec F}_A=-\mechanical{\rho}_f\mechanical{V}\mechanical{\vec g}$.
- **Steady flow in a tube.** $\mechanical{\rho}\mechanical{A}\mechanical{v}=\mathrm{const.}$
- **Bernoulli's equation along a streamline.** $\mechanical{p}+\tfrac12\mechanical{\rho}\mechanical{v}^2+\mechanical{\rho}\mechanical{g}\mechanical{z}=\mathrm{const.}$ for steady inviscid incompressible flow.
- **Poiseuille flow.** $\mechanical{Q}=\pi\mechanical{R}^4\Delta\mechanical{p}/(8\mechanical{\eta}\mechanical{L})$ for fully developed laminar flow in a circular tube.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{p},\mechanical{p}_0,\Delta\mechanical{p}$ | $\unit{Pa}$ | Pressures | The local and reference pressure and the pressure difference. |
| $\mechanical{F}_\perp,\mechanical{\vec F}_A$ | $\unit{N}$ | Forces | The normal force component and buoyant force. |
| $\mechanical{A}$ | $\unit{m^2}$ | Area | The area element or tube cross section. |
| $\mechanical{z},\mechanical{h},\mechanical{R},\mechanical{L}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Lengths | The elevation, depth, and tube radius and length. |
| $\mechanical{\rho},\mechanical{\rho}_f$ | $\unit{kg\,m^{-3}}$ | Densities | The local density and density of displaced fluid. |
| $\mechanical{g},\mechanical{\vec g}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-2}}$ | Gravitational acceleration | The magnitude and vector of gravity. |
| $\mechanical{V}$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Displaced volume | The fluid volume displaced by a body. |
| $\mechanical{v}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Flow speed | The fluid speed. |
| $\mechanical{Q}$ | $\unit{m^3\,s^{-1}}$ | Volumetric flow | The volume transported per unit time. |
| $\mechanical{\eta}$ | $\unit{Pa\,s}$ | Dynamic viscosity | The viscosity of the fluid. |
