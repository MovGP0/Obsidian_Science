title: "Canonical Transformations and Hamilton–Jacobi Theory"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Reference formulas for canonical changes of variables, the Hamilton–Jacobi equation, and actions.

- **Canonical bracket.** $\{Q_k,P_l\}=\delta_{kl}$, with $\{Q_k,Q_l\}=\{P_k,P_l\}=0$.
- **Generating functions.** Types 1 to 4 use $F_1$ to $F_4$ with different choices of independent variables.
- **Hamilton–Jacobi equation.** $\energy{H}(q,\partial\energy{S}/\partial q,t)+\partial\energy{S}/\partial t=0$.
- **Autonomous Hamiltonian.** $\energy{S}=\energy{W}(q,\alpha)-\energy{E}t$.
- **Action and angle.** $J=(2\pi)^{-1}\oint p\,dq$ and $\dot\theta=\partial\energy{H}/\partial J=\signal{\omega}(J)$ for the angle conjugate to $J$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $q,p,Q_k,P_l,\alpha$ | — | Canonical variables and constants | The old and new canonical variables and separation constants. |
| $F_i,\energy{S},\energy{W},J$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Generators and actions | The generating functions, principal and characteristic functions, and action variable. |
| $\energy{H},\energy{E}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Hamiltonian and energy | The Hamiltonian and its fixed value in the autonomous case. |
| $\theta$ | $\unit{rad}$ | Angle | The coordinate conjugate to the action. |
| $\signal{\omega}$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Angular frequency | The rate of advance of the angle. |
| $t$ | $\unit{s}$ | Time | The independent variable for the motion. |
