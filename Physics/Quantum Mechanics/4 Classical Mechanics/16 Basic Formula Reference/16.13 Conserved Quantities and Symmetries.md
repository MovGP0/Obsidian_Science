title: "Conserved Quantities and Symmetries"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Reference list of common continuous symmetries and the conserved quantities they generate.

| Continuous symmetry | Conserved quantity |
| --- | --- |
| Spatial translation | Total linear momentum $\mechanical{\vec P}$. |
| Rotation | Total angular momentum $\mechanical{\vec L}$. |
| Time translation | Energy function $\energy{E}$. |
| Galilean boost | $\mechanical{M}\mechanical{\vec R}_{\mathrm{cm}}-\mechanical{\vec P}t$. |

Noether's theorem associates a conserved quantity with each continuous variational symmetry.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{\vec P}$ | $\unit{kg\,m\,s^{-1}}$ | Linear momentum | The total linear momentum. |
| $\mechanical{\vec L}$ | $\unit{kg\,m^2\,s^{-1}}$ | Angular momentum | The total angular momentum. |
| $\energy{E}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Energy function | The conserved quantity for time translation. |
| $\mechanical{M}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | The total mass of the system. |
| $\mechanical{\vec R}_{\mathrm{cm}}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Center of mass | The position of the center of mass. |
| $t$ | $\unit{s}$ | Time | The time variable in the boost charge. |
