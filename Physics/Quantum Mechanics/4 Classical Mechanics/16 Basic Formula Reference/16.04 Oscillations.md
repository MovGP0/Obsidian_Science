title: "Oscillations"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Reference formulas for harmonic, damped, and driven oscillators and for small-amplitude pendulums.

- **Harmonic oscillator.** $\mechanical{\ddot x}+\signal{\omega}^2\mechanical{x}=0$, $\signal{\omega}=\sqrt{\mechanical{k}/\mechanical{m}}$, $\signal{T}=2\pi/\signal{\omega}$.
- **Small oscillations near a potential minimum.** $\signal{\omega}^2=\energy{U}''(\mechanical{x}_0)/\mechanical{m}$.
- **Damped oscillator.** $\mechanical{\ddot x}+2\signal{\gamma}\mechanical{\dot x}+\signal{\omega}_0^2\mechanical{x}=0$.
- **Steady forced oscillation.** $\mechanical{A}(\signal{\Omega})=(\mechanical{F}_0/\mechanical{m})/\sqrt{(\signal{\omega}_0^2-\signal{\Omega}^2)^2+4\signal{\gamma}^2\signal{\Omega}^2}$.
- **Simple pendulum.** $\signal{T}=2\pi\sqrt{\mechanical{\ell}/\mechanical{g}}$.
- **Physical pendulum.** $\signal{T}=2\pi\sqrt{\mechanical{I}/(\mechanical{M}\mechanical{g}\mechanical{d})}$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{x},\mechanical{x}_0,\mechanical{A},\mechanical{\ell},\mechanical{d}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Displacement and lengths | Position, equilibrium, drive amplitude response, pendulum length, and center-of-mass offset. |
| $\mechanical{k}$ | $\unit{N\,m^{-1}}$ | Spring stiffness | The force per unit displacement. |
| $\mechanical{m},\mechanical{M}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Masses | The oscillator and physical-pendulum masses. |
| $\signal{\omega},\signal{\omega}_0,\signal{\Omega}$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Angular frequencies | The natural and driving frequencies. |
| $\signal{\gamma}$ | $\unit{s^{-1}}$ | Damping rate | The exponential decay rate of the free amplitude. |
| $\signal{T}$ | $\unit{s}$ | Period | The time for one oscillation. |
| $\energy{U}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Potential energy | The potential expanded about a minimum. |
| $\mechanical{F}_0$ | $\unit{N}$ | Force amplitude | The amplitude of the applied drive. |
| $\mechanical{g}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-2}}$ | Gravitational acceleration | The local field magnitude. |
| $\mechanical{I}$ | $\unit{kg\,m^2}$ | Moment of inertia | The pendulum inertia about the pivot. |
