---
title: "Gravitation and Motion Under Central Forces"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Reference formulas for Newtonian gravity, orbital elements, and the virial theorem.

- **Newtonian gravity.** $\mechanical{\vec F}=-\mechanical{G}\mechanical{m}\mechanical{M}\hat r/\mechanical{r}^2$ and $\energy{U}=-\mechanical{G}\mechanical{m}\mechanical{M}/\mechanical{r}$, with $\mechanical{G}\simeq\quantity{6.674\times10^{-11}}{m^3\,kg^{-1}\,s^{-2}}$.
- **Angular momentum.** $\mechanical{\vec L}=\mechanical{m}\mechanical{\vec r}\times\mechanical{\vec v}$ is constant for a central force on a particle of mass $\mechanical{m}$.
- **Effective potential for relative motion.** $\energy{U}_{\mathrm{eff}}=\energy{U}+\mechanical{L}^2/(2\mechanical{\mu}\mechanical{r}^2)$, where $\mechanical{L}$ is the angular momentum of the relative motion.
- **Inverse-distance orbit.** $\mechanical{r}(\phi)=\mechanical{p}/(1+e\cos\phi)$, $\mechanical{p}=\mechanical{L}^2/(\mechanical{\mu}\alpha)$ for $\energy{U}=-\alpha/\mechanical{r}$.
- **Kepler's third law for a light satellite.** $\signal{T}^2=4\pi^2\mechanical{a}^3/(\mechanical{G}\mechanical{M})$.
- **Escape and circular speeds.** $\mechanical{v}_{\mathrm{esc}}=\sqrt{2\mechanical{G}\mechanical{M}/\mechanical{r}}$, $\mechanical{v}_{\mathrm{circ}}=\sqrt{\mechanical{G}\mechanical{M}/\mechanical{r}}$.
- **Virial theorem for bounded motion.** $2\langle\energy{K}\rangle=n\langle\energy{U}\rangle$ for $\energy{U}\propto\mechanical{r}^n$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{G}$ | $\unit{m^3\,kg^{-1}\,s^{-2}}$ | Gravitational constant | The Newtonian coupling constant. |
| $\mechanical{m},\mechanical{M},\mechanical{\mu}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Masses | The two masses and the reduced mass. |
| $\mechanical{r},\mechanical{\vec r},\mechanical{p},\mechanical{a}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Position and orbital lengths | The radial coordinate, relative position, semilatus rectum, and semimajor axis. |
| $\mechanical{\vec F}$ | $\unit{N}$ | Gravitational force | The force on the orbiting mass. |
| $\energy{U},\energy{U}_{\mathrm{eff}},\energy{K}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Energies | The potential, effective potential, and kinetic energy. |
| $\mechanical{\vec L},\mechanical{L}$ | $\unit{kg\,m^2\,s^{-1}}$ | Angular momentum | The conserved vector and its magnitude. |
| $\mechanical{\vec v},\mechanical{v}_{\mathrm{esc}},\mechanical{v}_{\mathrm{circ}}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Velocity and speeds | The orbital velocity, escape speed, and circular speed. |
| $\phi$ | $\unit{rad}$ | Orbital angle | The angle measured from periapsis. |
| $e,n$ | $1$ | Eccentricity and power | The shape of the orbit and the exponent in the virial relation. |
| $\alpha$ | $\unit{J\,m}$ | Potential strength | The positive inverse-distance coefficient. |
| $\signal{T}$ | $\unit{s}$ | Orbital period | The time per revolution. |
