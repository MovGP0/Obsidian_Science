---
title: "Selected Hamilton–Jacobi Equations"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Reference Hamilton–Jacobi solutions for a free particle and a harmonic oscillator, with the separation used for Kepler motion.

- **Free particle.** $\energy{S}=\alpha q-\alpha^2t/(2\mechanical{m})$.
- **Harmonic oscillator.** $\energy{S}=(\energy{E}/\signal{\omega})(\phi+\sin\phi\cos\phi)-\energy{E}t$, where $\sin\phi=q\sqrt{\mechanical{m}\signal{\omega}^2/(2\energy{E})}$ on the chosen branch.
- **Kepler problem.** The equation separates in spherical coordinates. See [[13.09 Application to the Kepler Problem]].

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\energy{S}$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Principal function | The action function that generates the transformation. |
| $\alpha$ | $\unit{kg\,m\,s^{-1}}$ | Momentum constant | The separation constant for the free particle. |
| $q$ | $\unit{m}$ | Position | The one-dimensional spatial coordinate. |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | The free-particle or oscillator mass. |
| $\energy{E}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Energy | The constant oscillator energy. |
| $\signal{\omega}$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Angular frequency | The natural oscillator frequency. |
| $\phi$ | $\unit{rad}$ | Phase angle | The angle in the oscillator solution. |
| $t$ | $\unit{s}$ | Time | The independent variable for the motion. |
