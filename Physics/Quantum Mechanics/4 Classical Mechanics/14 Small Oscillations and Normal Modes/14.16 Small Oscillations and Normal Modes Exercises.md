---
title: "Small Oscillations and Normal Modes Exercises"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Practice finding mode frequencies and eigenvectors and applying them to coupled oscillators.

1. **Basic.** Verify the frequencies and modes of two identical masses connected by three identical springs, with the outer springs attached to walls.
   **Result.** $\signal{\omega}_1=\sqrt{\mechanical{k}/\mechanical{m}}$ is symmetric, and $\signal{\omega}_2=\sqrt{3\mechanical{k}/\mechanical{m}}$ is antisymmetric.
2. **Basic.** List the four natural frequencies of a periodic chain of $N=4$ masses.
   **Result.** $\signal{\omega}_j=2\sqrt{\mechanical{k}/\mechanical{m}}\lvert\sin(\pi j/4)\rvert$ for $j=0,1,2,3$. The frequencies are $0$, $\sqrt{2\mechanical{k}/\mechanical{m}}$, $2\sqrt{\mechanical{k}/\mechanical{m}}$, and $\sqrt{2\mechanical{k}/\mechanical{m}}$.
3. **Basic.** For the double pendulum with $\mechanical{m}_1=\mechanical{m}_2=\mechanical{m}$ and $\mechanical{\ell}_1=\mechanical{\ell}_2=\mechanical{\ell}$, write the mass and stiffness matrices $M$ and $V$ for small oscillations.
4. **Intermediate.** Calculate the two natural frequencies of the equal double pendulum.
   **Result.** $\signal{\omega}_\pm^2=(\mechanical{g}/\mechanical{\ell})(2\pm\sqrt2)$.
5. **Intermediate.** Prove that the sound speed of a linear chain with lattice spacing $\mechanical{a}$ is $\mechanical{c}_s=\mechanical{a}\sqrt{\mechanical{k}/\mechanical{m}}$.
6. **Intermediate.** For the nonlinear pendulum with amplitude $\theta_0=\pi/4$, estimate the period correction relative to the harmonic limit.
   **Result.** $\signal{T}(\pi/4)\simeq\signal{T}_0[1+(\pi/4)^2/16]\simeq1.039\signal{T}_0$.
7. **Intermediate.** Write the nonlinear pendulum equation in Hamiltonian form. Sketch its trajectories in $(\theta,p_\theta)$, including the equilibria and separatrix.
8. **Challenge.** Find the first period-doubling point $r_1$ of $x_{n+1}=rx_n(1-x_n)$, starting from the fixed point.
   **Result.** $r_1=3$.
9. **Challenge.** Prove that the regular compact connected invariant level sets of an integrable Lagrangian system with two degrees of freedom are two-dimensional tori in phase space.
10. **Intermediate.** Three equal masses $\mechanical{m}$ are in a line, connected by two equal springs $\mechanical{k}$, with free ends. Calculate the three natural frequencies and identify the modes.
    **Result.** $\signal{\omega}_0=0$, $\signal{\omega}_1=\sqrt{\mechanical{k}/\mechanical{m}}$, and $\signal{\omega}_2=\sqrt{3\mechanical{k}/\mechanical{m}}$.
11. **Intermediate.** Find the dispersion relation $\signal{\omega}(\signal{q})$ for an infinite chain of alternating masses $\mechanical{m}$ and $\mechanical{M}$ joined by equal springs $\mechanical{k}$. Identify the acoustic and optical branches.
    **Result.**

    $$
    \signal{\omega}^2=\frac{\mechanical{k}(\mechanical{M}+\mechanical{m})}{\mechanical{M}\mechanical{m}}\left(1\pm\sqrt{1-\frac{4\mechanical{M}\mechanical{m}\sin^2(\signal{q}\mechanical{a})}{(\mechanical{M}+\mechanical{m})^2}}\right).
    $$

    For unequal masses, a frequency gap separates the acoustic and optical branches.
12. **Intermediate.** Estimate the order of magnitude of the maximum Lyapunov exponent of the double pendulum in a chaotic regime.
    **Source result.** $\lambda$ is of order $\sqrt{\mechanical{g}/\mechanical{\ell}}$, so the Lyapunov time can be shorter than the harmonic period $\signal{T}_0$. The value depends on the trajectory.
13. **Challenge.** Calculate the first three period-doubling points of the logistic map numerically. Compare the ratio of successive spacings with the Feigenbaum constant $\delta\simeq4.669$.

> [!note] Source wording
> The source asks to verify convergence from the first three points. These points give one spacing ratio. More bifurcation points are necessary to observe convergence.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{m},\mechanical{m}_1,\mechanical{m}_2,\mechanical{M}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Masses | The oscillator masses, pendulum bobs, and the second mass in the alternating chain. |
| $\mechanical{k}$ | $\unit{N\,m^{-1}}$ | Spring stiffness | The stiffness of each spring. |
| $M,V$ | — | Mass and stiffness matrices | The matrices for the linearized double pendulum. |
| $\mechanical{\ell},\mechanical{\ell}_1,\mechanical{\ell}_2,\mechanical{a}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Lengths | The pendulum lengths and the spacing of neighboring masses. |
| $\mechanical{g}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-2}}$ | Gravitational acceleration | The local field magnitude. |
| $\signal{\omega}_j,\signal{\omega}_\pm,\signal{\omega}$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Angular frequencies | The discrete frequencies and the lattice dispersion. |
| $\signal{q}$ | $\unit{m^{-1}}$ | Wavenumber | The spatial phase rate of a lattice wave. |
| $\mechanical{c}_s$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Sound speed | The long-wavelength propagation speed of the chain. |
| $\theta,\theta_0$ | $\unit{rad}$ | Angles | The pendulum angle and maximum angle. |
| $p_\theta$ | $\unit{kg\,m^2\,s^{-1}}$ | Angular momentum | The canonical momentum of the pendulum. |
| $\signal{T},\signal{T}_0$ | $\unit{s}$ | Periods | The nonlinear period and its small-amplitude limit. |
| $\lambda$ | $\unit{s^{-1}}$ | Lyapunov exponent | The exponential separation rate of nearby trajectories. |
| $r,r_1,x_n,\delta$ | $1$ | Logistic-map quantities | The control parameter, first period-doubling point, state, and Feigenbaum constant. |
