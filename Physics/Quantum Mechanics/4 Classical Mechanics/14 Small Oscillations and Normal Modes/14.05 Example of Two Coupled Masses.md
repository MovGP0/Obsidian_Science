---
title: "Example of Two Coupled Masses"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Two identical masses attached to walls and coupled by a spring have in-phase and out-of-phase modes.

Two equal masses $\mechanical{m}$ are attached to walls by springs of stiffness $\mechanical{k}$ and to each other by a spring of stiffness $\mechanical{k}'$. The coordinates $\eta_1,\eta_2$ measure displacements from equilibrium. The Lagrangian is

$$
\energy{L}=\tfrac12\mechanical{m}(\dot\eta_1^2+\dot\eta_2^2)-\tfrac12\mechanical{k}(\eta_1^2+\eta_2^2)-\tfrac12\mechanical{k}'(\eta_1-\eta_2)^2.
$$

The matrices are $M=\mechanical{m}I$ and

$$
V=\begin{pmatrix}\mechanical{k}+\mechanical{k}'&-\mechanical{k}'\\-\mechanical{k}'&\mechanical{k}+\mechanical{k}'\end{pmatrix}.
$$

**Diagonalization.** The equation $\det(V-\signal{\omega}^2M)=0$ gives $(\mechanical{k}+\mechanical{k}'-\mechanical{m}\signal{\omega}^2)^2-\mechanical{k}'^2=0$. The stiffness eigenvalues are $\mechanical{k}$ and $\mechanical{k}+2\mechanical{k}'$, so the squared frequencies are $\mechanical{k}/\mechanical{m}$ and $(\mechanical{k}+2\mechanical{k}')/\mechanical{m}$.

- **Symmetric mode.** $\signal{\omega}_1=\sqrt{\mechanical{k}/\mechanical{m}}$ and $\vec u_1=(1,1)^T/\sqrt{2\mechanical{m}}$. The masses move in phase, and the middle spring has no change in length.
- **Antisymmetric mode.** $\signal{\omega}_2=\sqrt{(\mechanical{k}+2\mechanical{k}')/\mechanical{m}}$ and $\vec u_2=(1,-1)^T/\sqrt{2\mechanical{m}}$. The masses move in opposite phase. The middle spring changes length, and this mode has the higher frequency.

The general solution is

$$
\begin{pmatrix}\eta_1\\\eta_2\end{pmatrix}
=\frac{Q_1}{\sqrt{2\mechanical{m}}}\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\end{pmatrix}
+\frac{Q_2}{\sqrt{2\mechanical{m}}}\begin{pmatrix}1\\-1\end{pmatrix},
$$

where $Q_1,Q_2$ are independent oscillator coordinates.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | The mass of each moving body. |
| $\mechanical{k},\mechanical{k}'$ | $\unit{N\,m^{-1}}$ | Spring stiffness | The wall-spring and coupling-spring stiffnesses. |
| $\eta_1,\eta_2$ | $\unit{m}$ | Displacements | The positions measured from equilibrium. |
| $M$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass matrix | The matrix of kinetic coefficients. |
| $V$ | $\unit{N\,m^{-1}}$ | Stiffness matrix | The matrix of potential coefficients. |
| $\energy{L}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Lagrangian | The kinetic energy minus the potential energy. |
| $\signal{\omega},\signal{\omega}_1,\signal{\omega}_2$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Angular frequencies | The trial frequency and the symmetric and antisymmetric frequencies. |
| $\vec u_1,\vec u_2$ | $\unit{kg^{-1/2}}$ | Mode vectors | The mass-normalized displacement patterns. |
| $Q_1,Q_2$ | $\unit{kg^{1/2}\,m}$ | Normal coordinates | The amplitudes of the two displacement patterns. |
