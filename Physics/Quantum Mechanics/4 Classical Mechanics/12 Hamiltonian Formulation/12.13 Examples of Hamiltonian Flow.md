> [!TLDR]
> The pendulum phase portrait separates small oscillations, the separatrix, and rotations.

## Pendulum flow in phase space

The pendulum phase portrait in $(\theta,p_\theta)$ has three types of trajectories.

- **Small oscillations.** Closed trajectories surround the equilibrium at $\theta=0$, $p_\theta=0$.
- **Separatrix.** The trajectory approaches the unstable equilibrium at $\theta=\pm\pi$, $p_\theta=0$ asymptotically. The approach takes infinite time. The two angular positions represent the same point on the phase cylinder.
- **Open trajectories in the unwrapped plane.** At energy $\energy{E}>\mechanical{m}\mechanical{g}\mechanical{\ell}$ the pendulum makes full rotations. The sign of $p_\theta$ determines the direction.

**Period inside the separatrix.** The period depends on energy. It diverges logarithmically as the trajectory approaches the separatrix. At very small oscillation energies it approaches $\signal{T}_0=2\pi\sqrt{\mechanical{\ell}/\mechanical{g}}$.

## Kepler flow in reduced phase space

In plane polar canonical variables $(\mechanical{r},\phi,\mechanical{p}_r,p_\phi)$, $p_\phi=\mechanical{L}$ is constant. The reduced phase space has variables $(\mechanical{r},\mechanical{p}_r)$ and energy

$$
\energy{E}=\frac{\mechanical{p}_r^2}{2\mechanical{\mu}}+\energy{U}_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mechanical{r}).
$$

For nonzero angular momentum, bound motion at $\energy{E}<0$ gives closed radial phase-space trajectories. The orbits in physical space are ellipses. The radial phase-space trajectories are open for $\energy{E}\ge0$. The physical orbits are parabolas at $\energy{E}=0$ and hyperbolas at $\energy{E}>0$.

> [!note] Source terminology
> The source uses the conic names for the reduced trajectories. These names describe the corresponding paths in physical space.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\theta,\phi$ | $\unit{rad}$ | Angles | The pendulum angle and orbital azimuth. |
| $p_\theta,p_\phi,\mechanical{L}$ | $\unit{kg\,m^2\,s^{-1}}$ | Angular momenta | The conjugate momenta for the angles and the conserved Kepler angular momentum. |
| $\energy{E},\energy{U}_{\mathrm{eff}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Energies | The total energy and the effective radial potential energy. |
| $\mechanical{m},\mechanical{\mu}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass and reduced mass | The pendulum bob mass and the reduced mass of the Kepler system. |
| $\mechanical{\ell},\mechanical{r}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Lengths | The pendulum length and orbital radial coordinate. |
| $\mechanical{g}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-2}}$ | Gravitational acceleration | The acceleration magnitude at the pendulum. |
| $\signal{T}_0$ | $\unit{s}$ | Small-oscillation period | The limiting pendulum period. |
| $\mechanical{p}_r$ | $\unit{kg\,m\,s^{-1}}$ | Radial momentum | The momentum conjugate to the radial coordinate. |
