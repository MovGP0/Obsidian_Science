---
title: "Hamiltonian Formulation"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Hamilton's first-order equations describe motion in phase space using generalized coordinates and their conjugate momenta.

The Hamiltonian formulation replaces generalized velocities $\dot q_k$ with conjugate momenta $p_k$. The Hamiltonian $\energy{H}(q,p,t)$ replaces the Lagrangian $\energy{L}(q,\dot q,t)$. The resulting first-order equations have a symmetric form and define a flow in phase space. Phase space is central to classical mechanics and has an analogue in quantum theory.
