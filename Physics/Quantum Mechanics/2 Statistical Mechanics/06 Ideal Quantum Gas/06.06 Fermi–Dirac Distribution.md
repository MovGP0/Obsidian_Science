> [!TLDR]
> Fermi–Dirac occupations are bounded by one and approach a step at zero temperature.

The Fermi–Dirac law follows from the empty and occupied contributions to one fermionic state. See [[06.06.01 Derivation]] and [[06.06.02 Properties of the Fermi–Dirac Distribution]].
