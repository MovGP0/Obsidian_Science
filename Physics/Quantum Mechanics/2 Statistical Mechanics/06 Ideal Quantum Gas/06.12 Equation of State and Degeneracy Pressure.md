> [!TLDR]
> A filled Fermi sea exerts pressure at zero temperature because Pauli exclusion requires occupation of states with nonzero momentum.

At low temperature, a Fermi gas differs greatly from the classical gas. At ${\thermal{T}}=0$, states are occupied up to ${\energy{\varepsilon}}_F$. The resulting **degeneracy pressure** is

$$
{\mechanical{P}}_0=\frac25 {\mechanical{n}}{\energy{\varepsilon}}_F,
\qquad {\energy{\varepsilon}}_F=\frac{{\energy{\hbar}}^2}{2{\mechanical{m}}}\left(\frac{6\pi^2{\mechanical{n}}}{{\mechanical{g}}_s}\right)^{2/3}.\tag{6.10}
$$

This pressure persists at zero temperature. Its origin is quantum state filling rather than thermal agitation. Fermions must fill states with progressively larger momentum.

**Example 6.3 — Electrons in copper.** Take ${\mechanical{n}}\simeq8.5\times10^{28}\,\unit{m^{-3}}$ and ${\mechanical{g}}_s=2$. With ${\energy{\hbar}}\simeq1.05\times10^{-34}\,\unit{J\,s}$ and ${\mechanical{m}}_e\simeq9.11\times10^{-31}\,\unit{kg}$,

$$
{\energy{\varepsilon}}_F\simeq\frac{(1.05\times10^{-34})^2}{2(9.11\times10^{-31})}(3\pi^2\,8.5\times10^{28})^{2/3}
\simeq7.0\,\unit{eV}.
$$

Then ${\thermal{T}}_F={\energy{\varepsilon}}_F/{\thermal{k}}_B\simeq8.1\times10^4\,\unit{K}$, far above room temperature. The conduction-electron gas is strongly degenerate.

$$
{\mechanical{P}}_0\simeq\frac25(8.5\times10^{28})(7.0)(1.6\times10^{-19})
\simeq3.8\times10^{10}\,\unit{Pa}
\simeq3.8\times10^5\,\unit{atm}.
$$

In a metal, the electron contribution is balanced by the full binding and lattice stresses. It is not the external pressure of a free electron gas.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\mechanical{P}}$ | $\unit{Pa}$ | Pressure | Mechanical pressure of the gas. |
| ${\mechanical{n}}$ | $\unit{m^{-3}}$ | Number density | Number of particles per volume. |
| ${\energy{\varepsilon}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Single-particle energy | Energy of one single-particle state. |
| ${\energy{\hbar}}$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Reduced Planck constant | Equal to $h/(2\pi)$. |
| ${\mechanical{m}}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | Mass of one particle. |
| ${\mechanical{g}}_s$ | $\unit{1}$ | Spin degeneracy | Number of internal spin states at a given orbital state. |
| ${\thermal{T}}_F$ | $\unit{K}$ | Fermi temperature | Fermi energy divided by the Boltzmann constant. |
| ${\thermal{k}}_B$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Boltzmann constant | Converts temperature to energy. |
| ${\thermal{T}}$ | $\unit{K}$ | Temperature | Absolute temperature of the system. |
