> [!TLDR]
> Exchange of identical particles constrains the many-particle wavefunction.

Identical quantum particles cannot be identified by permanent particle labels. Their exchange must preserve the physical state.

- [[06.01.01 The Indistinguishability Problem]] defines identical particles.
- [[06.01.02 Consequences for the Wavefunction]] derives the symmetric and antisymmetric exchange rules used here.
- [[06.01.03 Symmetrization and Antisymmetrization]] constructs the corresponding states.
