> [!TLDR]
> Counting wave-vector states gives the density of states used to replace quantum sums by energy integrals.

The free-particle energy is quadratic in the wave vector. Its density of states depends on spatial dimension.

- [[06.09.01 Motivation and Problem Formulation]] sets periodic boundary conditions.
- [[06.09.02 Density of States in Three Dimensions]] derives the square-root energy law.
- [[06.09.03 Density of States in Two Dimensions]] gives an energy-independent density.
- [[06.09.04 Density of States in One Dimension]] gives an inverse-square-root density.
- [[06.09.05 Transition from a Sum to an Integral]] applies these results.
