**Fig. 6.3** The three mean occupation laws on a common dimensionless energy scale. Fermions saturate at one. The physical Bose branch diverges as x approaches zero from above. All three approach exp(−x) for large positive x. Curves that exceed the displayed range continue above it.

The Maxwell–Boltzmann curve at $x\le0$ is shown as a mathematical comparison. Its large values are outside the dilute approximation. See [[06.10.03 Analysis of the Comparison]].

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\mechanical{n}}_\alpha$ | $\unit{1}$ | Occupation number | Number or mean number of particles in one state. |
| ${\energy{\varepsilon}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Single-particle energy | Energy of one single-particle state. |
| ${\energy{\mu}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Chemical potential | Controls the mean particle number. |
| ${\thermal{k}}_B$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Boltzmann constant | Converts temperature to energy. |
| ${\thermal{T}}$ | $\unit{K}$ | Temperature | Absolute temperature of the system. |
