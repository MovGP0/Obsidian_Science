> [!TLDR]
> The grand partition function of an ideal quantum gas is a product over single-particle states.

Use the grand canonical ensemble to sum over all allowed occupations. The absence of interactions permits exact factorization.

- [[06.04.01 Grand Canonical Ensemble]] gives the trace.
- [[06.04.02 Factorization over Individual Levels]] separates the trace into state contributions.
- [[06.04.03 Grand Partition Function for Fermions]] sums occupations zero and one.
- [[06.04.04 Grand Partition Function for Bosons]] sums all nonnegative occupations.
