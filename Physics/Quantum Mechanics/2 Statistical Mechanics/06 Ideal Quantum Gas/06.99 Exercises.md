For a guided route, begin with Exercises 6.1 and 6.2 to connect state counting to energy and pressure. Exercise 6.3 then reconstructs the grand-potential calculation. Exercise 6.4 changes the thermal regime while keeping particle number fixed. Exercises 6.5 and 6.7 change the dispersion and internal-state count; Exercise 6.6 tests which control parameters a comparison must share.

Before each calculation, predict whether the answer should depend on statistics, dispersion, or both. For example, the pressure-to-energy ratio in Exercise 6.1 is fixed by the quadratic dispersion, while the energy itself still depends on the occupation law.

The supplied screenshots contain the questions but no Chapter 6 answer-key pages. The worked calculations in these exercise files are added derivations, clearly identified as such.
