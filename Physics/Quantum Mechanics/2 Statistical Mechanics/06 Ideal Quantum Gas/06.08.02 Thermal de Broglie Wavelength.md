> [!TLDR]
> The thermal de Broglie wavelength gives the quantum length that relates fugacity to classical particle density.

For a three-dimensional ideal gas with one internal state, determine the chemical potential from the required mean particle number:

$$
\frac {\mechanical{N}}{\mechanical{V}}=\frac1{\mechanical{V}}\sum_\alpha {\mechanical{n}}_{\mathrm{MB}}({\energy{\varepsilon}}_\alpha)
\simeq\int_0^\infty\mathcal D({\energy{\varepsilon}}){\mechanical{n}}_{\mathrm{MB}}({\energy{\varepsilon}})\,d{\energy{\varepsilon}}.
$$

Here $\mathcal D({\energy{\varepsilon}})={\energy{g}}({\energy{\varepsilon}})/{\mechanical{V}}$ is the density of states **per volume**. Insert the free-particle density from [[06.09.02 Density of States in Three Dimensions]] and evaluate the Gaussian integral:

$$
\frac {\mechanical{N}}{\mechanical{V}}={\mechanical{z}}\left(\frac{2\pi {\mechanical{m}} {\thermal{k}}_B{\thermal{T}}}{{\energy{h}}^2}\right)^{3/2}
=\frac {\mechanical{z}}{{\signal{\lambda}}_T^3}.\tag{6.6}
$$

The **thermal de Broglie wavelength** is

$$
{\signal{\lambda}}_T=\frac {\energy{h}}{\sqrt{2\pi {\mechanical{m}} {\thermal{k}}_B{\thermal{T}}}}.
$$

> [!note] Remark 6.3 — Physical scale
> The wavelength is a characteristic thermal quantum length. It scales as the de Broglie wavelength ${\energy{h}}/{\mechanical{p}}$ for a thermal momentum. It is not a uniquely defined wave-packet width. With ${\mechanical{p}}=\sqrt{2{\mechanical{m}}{\thermal{k}}_B{\thermal{T}}}$, ${\energy{h}}/{\mechanical{p}}=\sqrt\pi{\signal{\lambda}}_T$, so the equality asserted in the source is only an order-of-magnitude comparison. > [!note] Density-of-states convention
> The source uses $g$ for a per-volume density here and for the total density in Eq. (6.7). These notes use total $g$ and $\mathcal D=g/{\mechanical{V}}$ throughout. Equation (6.6) assumes one internal state; with spin degeneracy, ${\mechanical{N}}/{\mechanical{V}}={\mechanical{g}}_s {\mechanical{z}}/{\signal{\lambda}}_T^3$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\mechanical{N}}$ | $\unit{1}$ | Particle number | Total or mean number of particles. |
| ${\mechanical{V}}$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Volume | Volume of a three-dimensional system. |
| $\mathcal D({\energy{\varepsilon}})$ | $\unit{J^{-1}\,m^{-3}}$ | Density of states per volume | Total density of states divided by the volume. |
| ${\energy{g}}({\energy{\varepsilon}})$ | $\unit{J^{-1}}$ | Density of states | Total number of states per energy interval, including internal degeneracy. |
| ${\mechanical{n}}_\alpha$ | $\unit{1}$ | Occupation number | Number or mean number of particles in one state. |
| ${\energy{\varepsilon}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Single-particle energy | Energy of one single-particle state. |
| ${\mechanical{m}}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | Mass of one particle. |
| ${\thermal{k}}_B$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Boltzmann constant | Converts temperature to energy. |
| ${\thermal{T}}$ | $\unit{K}$ | Temperature | Absolute temperature of the system. |
| ${\energy{h}}$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Planck constant | Relates frequency to quantum energy. |
| ${\signal{\lambda}}_T$ | $\unit{m}$ | Thermal de Broglie wavelength | Thermal quantum length for a particle of mass m. |
| ${\mechanical{p}}$ | $\unit{kg\,m\,s^{-1}}$ | Momentum | Magnitude of particle momentum. |
| ${\mechanical{g}}_s$ | $\unit{1}$ | Spin degeneracy | Number of internal spin states at a given orbital state. |
| ${\mechanical{z}}$ | $\unit{1}$ | Fugacity | Equal to $e^{\beta\mu}$. |
