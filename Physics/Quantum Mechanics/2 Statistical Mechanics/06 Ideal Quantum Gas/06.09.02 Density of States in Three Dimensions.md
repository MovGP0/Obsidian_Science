> [!TLDR]
> The total density of free-particle states in three dimensions grows as the square root of energy.

For a large box, the number of states in a wave-vector cell is

$$
d\mathcal N={\mechanical{g}}_s\frac {\mechanical{V}}{(2\pi)^3}\,d^3{\signal{k}}.
$$

The factor ${\mechanical{g}}_s=2{\mechanical{s}}+1$ counts the spin states of a massive particle when they have equal energy. Photons instead have two physical polarizations; their dispersion is relativistic and requires a different density of states.

Convert the wave-vector integral to spherical coordinates. Since ${\energy{\varepsilon}}={\energy{\hbar}}^2{\signal{k}}^2/(2{\mechanical{m}})$,

$$
{\signal{k}}=\frac{\sqrt{2{\mechanical{m}}{\energy{\varepsilon}}}}{\energy{\hbar}},
\qquad d{\signal{k}}=\frac1{\energy{\hbar}}\sqrt{\frac {\mechanical{m}}{2{\energy{\varepsilon}}}}\,d{\energy{\varepsilon}}.
$$

The number in $[{\energy{\varepsilon}},{\energy{\varepsilon}}+d{\energy{\varepsilon}}]$ is

$$
\begin{aligned}
{\energy{g}}({\energy{\varepsilon}})d{\energy{\varepsilon}}
&={\mechanical{g}}_s\frac {\mechanical{V}}{(2\pi)^3}\,4\pi {\signal{k}}^2\,d{\signal{k}}\\
&={\mechanical{g}}_s\frac {\mechanical{V}}{(2\pi)^3}\,4\pi\frac{2{\mechanical{m}}{\energy{\varepsilon}}}{{\energy{\hbar}}^2}\frac1{\energy{\hbar}}\sqrt{\frac {\mechanical{m}}{2{\energy{\varepsilon}}}}\,d{\energy{\varepsilon}}.
\end{aligned}
$$

**Proposition 6.2 — Density of states in three dimensions.**

$$
{\energy{g}}({\energy{\varepsilon}})=\frac{{\mechanical{g}}_s {\mechanical{V}}}{4\pi^2}\left(\frac{2{\mechanical{m}}}{{\energy{\hbar}}^2}\right)^{3/2}{\energy{\varepsilon}}^{1/2}
\equiv A_{3D}{\energy{\varepsilon}}^{1/2}.\tag{6.7}
$$

Here $A_{3D}={\mechanical{g}}_s{\mechanical{V}}(2{\mechanical{m}})^{3/2}/(4\pi^2{\energy{\hbar}}^3)$. This is the **total** density, with units of inverse energy. Divide by ${\mechanical{V}}$ for $\mathcal D({\energy{\varepsilon}})$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\energy{g}}({\energy{\varepsilon}})$ | $\unit{J^{-1}}$ | Density of states | Total number of states per energy interval, including internal degeneracy. |
| $\mathcal D({\energy{\varepsilon}})$ | $\unit{J^{-1}\,m^{-3}}$ | Density of states per volume | Total density of states divided by the volume. |
| ${\mechanical{g}}_s$ | $\unit{1}$ | Spin degeneracy | Number of internal spin states at a given orbital state. |
| ${\mechanical{s}}$ | $\unit{1}$ | Spin quantum number | Intrinsic angular-momentum quantum number. |
| ${\mechanical{V}}$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Volume | Volume of a three-dimensional system. |
| ${\signal{k}}$ | $\unit{m^{-1}}$ | Wavenumber | Magnitude or component of a wave vector. |
| ${\energy{\varepsilon}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Single-particle energy | Energy of one single-particle state. |
| ${\mechanical{m}}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | Mass of one particle. |
| ${\energy{\hbar}}$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Reduced Planck constant | Equal to $h/(2\pi)$. |
