> [!TLDR]
> Differentiation of the scaling free energy gives Widom, Rushbrooke, Josephson, and Fisher relations.

The scaling hypothesis gives relations between critical exponents, called **scaling relations**.

## Rushbrooke relation

The susceptibility is obtained from the field derivatives of the singular free energy. The scaling form (12.11) gives

$$
\chi\sim|\thermal{t}|^{2-\alpha-2\Delta}
=|\thermal{t}|^{2-\alpha-2\beta\delta}.
$$

Comparison with $\chi\sim|\thermal{t}|^{-\gamma}$ gives

$$
\gamma=2\Delta-2+\alpha=2\beta\delta-2+\alpha.
$$

With $\Delta=\beta\delta$ and $\delta=\gamma/\beta+1$,

$$
\alpha+2\beta+\gamma=2.
$$

This is the **Rushbrooke relation**.

> [!note] Source correction: susceptibility sign
> The source writes $\chi=\partial^2\energy{f}_{\mathrm s}/\partial h^2$. For the coupling $-h\phi$, the susceptibility is $-\partial^2\energy{f}_{\mathrm s}/\partial h^2$. This sign does not change the critical exponent.

## Widom relation

The scaling equation of state gives

$$
\gamma=\beta(\delta-1).
$$

This is the **Widom relation**, with $\Delta=\beta\delta$.

## Josephson relation: hyperscaling

The RG homogeneity of free-energy density gives

$$
2-\alpha=d\nu.
$$

This is the **Josephson hyperscaling relation**. It contains the spatial dimension and fails in its usual form above the upper critical dimension $d_{\mathrm c}=4$.

## Fisher relation

At criticality,

$$
G(\mechanical{r})\sim\mechanical{r}^{-(d-2+\eta)}.
$$

Up to normalization factors, the susceptibility is the spatial integral of the connected correlation function:

$$
\chi\sim\int d^d\mechanical{r}\,G(\mechanical{r})
\sim\int^{\mechanical{\xi}}
\mechanical{r}^{-(d-2+\eta)}\mechanical{r}^{d-1}\,d\mechanical{r}
\sim\mechanical{\xi}^{2-\eta}.
$$

Since $\chi\sim|\thermal{t}|^{-\gamma}$ and $\mechanical{\xi}\sim|\thermal{t}|^{-\nu}$,

$$
\gamma=\nu(2-\eta).
$$

This is the **Fisher relation**.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\chi$ | — | Susceptibility | The derivative of the order parameter with respect to its conjugate field. |
| $\energy{f}_{\mathrm s}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Singular free energy per site | The singular part of the free energy per lattice site. |
| $\mechanical{\xi}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Correlation length | The spatial range over which fluctuations are correlated. |
| $\mechanical{r}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Separation | The spatial distance between two field points. |
| $\thermal{t}$ | $1$ | Reduced temperature | The temperature difference from the critical point, divided by the critical temperature. |
| $d$ | $1$ | Spatial dimension | The number of spatial dimensions. |
| $G(r)$ | — | Connected correlation | The covariance of the order-parameter field; its unit is the square of the field unit. |
| $h$ | — | Conjugate field | The field coupled to the order parameter; its unit depends on the normalization. |
