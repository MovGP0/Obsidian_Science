> [!TLDR]
> Summing every second spin of the Ising chain produces a new nearest-neighbour coupling for the remaining spins.

The one-dimensional Ising chain gives a simple exact RG transformation. Consider $N$ spins $\magnetic{s}_i=\pm1$ with no external field:

$$
\energy{\mathcal H}=-\energy{J}\sum_{i=1}^{N}\magnetic{s}_i\magnetic{s}_{i+1}.
$$

Define the **reduced coupling** $K=\energy{J}/(\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}\thermal{T})$. The partition function is

$$
\mathcal Z=\sum_{\{s_i\}}
e^{K\sum_i\magnetic{s}_i\magnetic{s}_{i+1}}.
$$

**Decimation** sums over the even-index spins while holding the odd-index spins fixed. For three consecutive spins, eliminate $\magnetic{s}_2$:

$$
\sum_{s_2=\pm1}e^{K(\magnetic{s}_1\magnetic{s}_2+\magnetic{s}_2\magnetic{s}_3)}
=\sum_{s_2=\pm1}e^{K\magnetic{s}_2(\magnetic{s}_1+\magnetic{s}_3)}.
$$

There are two possible bond products:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\magnetic{s}_1=\magnetic{s}_3:\quad&
\sum_{s_2=\pm1}e^{2K\magnetic{s}_2}=2\cosh(2K),\\
\magnetic{s}_1=-\magnetic{s}_3:\quad&
\sum_{s_2=\pm1}e^0=2.
\end{aligned}
$$

The first line also covers two negative parallel spins because the sum is unchanged under $\magnetic{s}_2\to-\magnetic{s}_2$.

To retain the original functional form, require

$$
\sum_{s_2=\pm1}e^{K(\magnetic{s}_1\magnetic{s}_2+\magnetic{s}_2\magnetic{s}_3)}
=A(K)e^{K'\magnetic{s}_1\magnetic{s}_3}.
$$

Here $A(K)$ is a spin-independent factor and $K'$ is the renormalized coupling. Matching the two cases gives

$$
\magnetic{s}_1=\magnetic{s}_3:\qquad Ae^{K'}=2\cosh(2K),
\tag{12.5}
$$

$$
\magnetic{s}_1=-\magnetic{s}_3:\qquad Ae^{-K'}=2.
\tag{12.6}
$$

Divide Equation (12.5) by Equation (12.6):

$$
e^{2K'}=\cosh(2K)
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
K'=\frac12\ln\cosh(2K).
\tag{12.7}
$$

Multiplication instead gives

$$
A^2=4\cosh(2K),
\qquad
A=2\sqrt{\cosh(2K)}.
$$

The factor $A$ changes the overall partition-function normalization; it is not a new spin interaction.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\energy{\mathcal H}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Hamiltonian | The energy of a spin configuration. |
| $\energy{J}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Exchange coupling | The energy scale of the nearest-neighbour interaction. |
| $\magnetic{s}_i$ | $1$ | Spin variable | The normalized spin at site i. |
| $\mathcal Z$ | $1$ | Partition function | The sum of Boltzmann weights. |
| $\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Boltzmann constant | The constant that converts temperature to energy. |
| $\thermal{T}$ | $\unit{K}$ | Temperature | The absolute temperature; subscript c marks the transition temperature. |
| $K$ | $1$ | Reduced coupling | The dimensionless interaction coupling. |
| $N$ | $1$ | Spin count | The number of spin sites. |
