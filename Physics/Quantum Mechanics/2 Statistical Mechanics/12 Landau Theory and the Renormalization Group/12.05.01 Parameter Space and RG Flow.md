> [!TLDR]
> Repeated coarse graining produces a trajectory in the space of interaction couplings.

A general model has many interaction parameters. The coordinates $\{K_1,K_2,\ldots\}$ describe, for example, nearest-neighbour couplings, further-neighbour couplings, and cubic terms.

An RG transformation with scale factor $b$ defines

$$
\mathbf K'=\mathcal R_b(\mathbf K).
$$

The **RG flow** is the trajectory traced by repeated application of this transformation. Figure 12.5 shows a schematic flow in a two-dimensional coupling space.
