> [!TLDR]
> Linearizing the RG map at a fixed point separates coupling changes into scaling eigenvectors.

Let $\mathbf K^*$ be a fixed point. For nearby couplings $\mathbf K=\mathbf K^*+\delta\mathbf K$, linearize:

$$
\delta K'_\alpha=\sum_\beta M_{\alpha\beta}\delta K_\beta,
\qquad
M_{\alpha\beta}
=\left.\frac{\partial\mathcal R_\alpha}{\partial K_\beta}\right|_{\mathbf K^*}.
$$

Use eigenvectors $\phi_i$ and eigenvalues $\lambda_i$ of $M$:

$$
M\phi_i=\lambda_i\phi_i.
$$

Expand $\delta\mathbf K=\sum_i u_i\phi_i$. A transformation by scale factor $b$ gives

$$
u_i'=\lambda_i(b)u_i=b^{y_i}u_i.
$$

Here $\phi_i$ denotes a mathematical eigenvector, not the physical order parameter used earlier.

The RG exponent $y_i$ is defined by $\lambda_i(b)=b^{y_i}$.

**Definition 12.2 (Relevant, irrelevant, and marginal operators).** An operator with coupling $u_i$ is:

- **Relevant** if $y_i>0$: its coupling grows under RG and moves the system away from the critical fixed point.
- **Irrelevant** if $y_i<0$: its coupling decreases under RG and approaches the fixed point along that direction.
- **Marginal** if $y_i=0$: linearization alone cannot determine its behaviour.

After repeated steps, a scaling-field displacement is multiplied by the corresponding eigenvalue at each step. For a blocking factor greater than one, a positive exponent amplifies the displacement and a negative exponent suppresses it. The linear approximation applies only while these displacements remain small; a relevant perturbation eventually leaves this neighborhood.

For a marginal field, the first nonzero nonlinear flow term decides whether the field grows, decays or remains on a line of fixed points. Thus zero linear exponent is not a statement of stability. Also distinguish an eigenvector, which is a direction in coupling space, from the physical operator in the effective Hamiltonian whose coefficient changes in that direction. The scaling fields are generally combinations of microscopic couplings.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mathbf K$ | $1$ | Reduced couplings | The vector of dimensionless interaction parameters. |
| $b$ | $1$ | Scale factor | The spatial blocking and rescaling factor. |
| $u_i$ | $1$ | Scaling field | A coupling displacement along an eigenvector of the linearized RG transformation. |
