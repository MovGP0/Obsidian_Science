> [!TLDR]
> The Landau expansion retains only free-energy terms that obey the symmetry of the order parameter.

Landau's main idea is to construct an effective free energy $\energy{F}(\phi,\thermal{T})$. Expand it in powers of $\phi$ and retain only terms allowed by the system symmetry.

For $\mathbb Z_2$ symmetry, as in the zero-field Ising model,

$$
\energy{F}(\phi)=\energy{F}(-\phi).
$$

Only even powers can occur:

$$
\energy{F}(\phi,\thermal{T})
=\energy{F}_0(\thermal{T})
+a_2(\thermal{T})\phi^2
+a_4(\thermal{T})\phi^4
+a_6(\thermal{T})\phi^6+\cdots.
$$

The coefficients $a_n(\thermal{T})$ are regular functions of temperature. To describe a phase transition, impose the following conditions on the coefficients.

- Require $a_4>0$ for stability at large $|\phi|$ when higher-order terms are neglected.
- Require $a_2(\thermal{T})$ to change sign at $\thermal{T}=\thermal{T}_{\mathrm c}$.

Near the critical temperature, assume

$$
a_2(\thermal{T})
=a'(\thermal{T}-\thermal{T}_{\mathrm c})
=a'\thermal{T}_{\mathrm c}
\frac{\thermal{T}-\thermal{T}_{\mathrm c}}{\thermal{T}_{\mathrm c}}
\equiv a\thermal{t},
\qquad
a=a'\thermal{T}_{\mathrm c}>0.
$$

The reduced temperature is $\thermal{t}=(\thermal{T}-\thermal{T}_{\mathrm c})/\thermal{T}_{\mathrm c}$. Hold $a_4$ at its critical value and write $a_4=b>0$. The fourth-order Landau functional is then

$$
\energy{F}(\phi,\thermal{T})
=\energy{F}_0(\thermal{T})+a\thermal{t}\phi^2+b\phi^4.
$$

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\energy{F}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Free energy | The effective free energy; subscript 0 marks a reference value. |
| $\phi$ | — | Order parameter | The quantity that distinguishes the phases; its unit depends on the system. |
| $\thermal{T}$ | $\unit{K}$ | Temperature | The absolute temperature; subscript c marks the transition temperature. |
| $\thermal{t}$ | $1$ | Reduced temperature | The temperature difference from the critical point, divided by the critical temperature. |
| $a$ | — | Quadratic coefficient scale | The coefficient multiplying reduced temperature and the squared order parameter; its unit depends on field normalization. |
| $a_n$ | — | Landau coefficient | The coefficient of the nth order-parameter power; its unit depends on that power and normalization. |
| $a'$ | — | Temperature slope | The slope of the quadratic coefficient at the critical temperature. |
| $b$ | — | Quartic coefficient | The coefficient of the fourth power of the order parameter; its unit depends on field normalization. |
