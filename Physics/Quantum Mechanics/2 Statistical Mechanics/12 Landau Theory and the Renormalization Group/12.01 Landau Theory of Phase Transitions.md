> [!TLDR]
> Landau theory describes a phase transition through an order parameter and a symmetry-based expansion of the free energy.

The task is to predict which phase is stable without solving every microscopic spin. Start by choosing a quantity that distinguishes the phases, then use its symmetry to restrict the free energy. Equilibrium requires the lowest free-energy minimum, not merely a solution of the stationarity equation.

Read the first three notes as one construction: choose the order parameter, justify the expansion, and test the candidate minima. The exponent note extracts measurable response laws from this construction. The first-order note changes the allowed polynomial terms and shows why a jump can occur before the origin loses local stability. These are phenomenological predictions; the coefficients require microscopic calculation or experimental input.
