> [!TLDR]
> Fixed points and the growth or decay of nearby scaling fields determine universality and critical behaviour.

The one-dimensional example had only one running coupling. A general coarse-grained model needs a space of couplings because eliminating spins can generate new interactions. Read the flow note to locate a critical trajectory, then linearize about its fixed point to find which perturbations grow. The exponent note translates that growth into response laws; the universality note explains which microscopic differences disappear.

Keep the fixed point and its basin distinct. Sharing a symmetry is useful evidence for a class, but the decisive condition is approach to the same critical fixed point. A relevant field must be tuned before irrelevant microscopic differences can decay.
