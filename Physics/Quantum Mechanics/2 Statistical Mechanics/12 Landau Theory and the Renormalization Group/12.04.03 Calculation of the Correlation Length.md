> [!TLDR]
> Each decimation halves the correlation length measured in the current lattice units.

After $n$ decimation steps, the spin count is reduced by $b^n=2^n$. The correlation length transforms as

$$
\xi(K^{(n)})=\frac{\xi(K)}{b^n}=\frac{\xi(K)}{2^n}.
$$

Near the high-temperature fixed point, $K^*=0$, the recursion gives

$$
K^{(n)}\approx K^{2^n}.
$$

The exact correlation length is

$$
\xi(K)=\frac{1}{|\ln\tanh K|}
\approx-\frac{1}{\ln K}
\qquad(K\ll1).
$$

Substitution of $K^{(n)}\approx K^{2^n}$ verifies the same factor $2^{-n}$.

> [!note] Length convention
> Here $\xi$ is measured in lattice spacings. The physical correlation length stays the same when the original system is described with a coarser lattice; its numerical value in the new spacing is smaller.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\xi$ | $1$ | Correlation length in lattice units | The correlation range measured in the lattice spacing at the current RG scale. |
| $b$ | $1$ | Length scale factor | The increase in lattice spacing at one decimation step. |
| $K$ | $1$ | Reduced coupling | The dimensionless interaction coupling. |
