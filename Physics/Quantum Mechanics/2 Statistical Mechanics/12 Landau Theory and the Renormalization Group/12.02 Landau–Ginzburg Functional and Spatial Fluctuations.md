> [!TLDR]
> The Landau–Ginzburg functional includes spatial changes of the order parameter and gives a basis for fluctuation theory.

A uniform order parameter cannot describe a domain wall or the range over which two regions fluctuate together. This section first assigns a free-energy cost to spatial changes, then extracts the correlation length from the quadratic fluctuation spectrum. The Ginzburg criterion finally asks whether fluctuations on that length scale are small enough to justify the uniform solution.

Keep two operations distinct while reading: minimizing the functional gives a mean-field spatial profile; averaging fields with their thermal weights gives fluctuations about that profile. Merely writing a gradient term does not by itself include fluctuation corrections to every exponent.
