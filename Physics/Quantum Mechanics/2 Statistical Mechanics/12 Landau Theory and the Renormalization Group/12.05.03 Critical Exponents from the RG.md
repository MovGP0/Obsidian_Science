> [!TLDR]
> The two relevant RG eigenvalues determine the usual thermodynamic critical exponents.

Near a critical fixed point, the two principal relevant perturbations are usually:

- Reduced temperature $\thermal{t}=(\thermal{T}-\thermal{T}_{\mathrm c})/\thermal{T}_{\mathrm c}$, with $y_t>0$.
- External field $h$, with $y_h>0$.

They transform as

$$
\thermal{t}'=b^{y_t}\thermal{t},
\qquad
h'=b^{y_h}h.
$$

The singular free energy per site obeys

$$
\energy{f}_{\mathrm s}(\thermal{t},h)
=b^{-d}\energy{f}_{\mathrm s}
(b^{y_t}\thermal{t},b^{y_h}h).
\tag{12.8}
$$

The factor $b^{-d}$ accounts for the reduced number of sites after blocking. Choose $b^{y_t}|\thermal{t}|=1$, or $b=|\thermal{t}|^{-1/y_t}$. Then

$$
\energy{f}_{\mathrm s}(\thermal{t},h)
=|\thermal{t}|^{d/y_t}
\energy{f}_{\mathrm s}
(\pm1,h|\thermal{t}|^{-y_h/y_t}).
$$

This is the Widom scaling form. The critical exponents are

$$
\nu=\frac{1}{y_t},
\tag{12.9}
$$

and

$$
\begin{aligned}
\beta&=\frac{d-y_h}{y_t},\\
\gamma&=\frac{2y_h-d}{y_t},\\
\delta&=\frac{y_h}{d-y_h},\\
\alpha&=2-\frac{d}{y_t}=2-d\nu.
\end{aligned}
\tag{12.10}
$$

See [[12.06 Scaling and Relations between Critical Exponents]].

> [!note] Hyperscaling assumption
> The free-energy homogeneity used here gives hyperscaling. Above the upper critical dimension, a dangerously irrelevant coupling can invalidate this form for thermodynamic exponents. The chapter returns to this limitation in Section 12.7.

## Example 12.2: Two-dimensional Ising exponents

For $d=2$, the exact scaling eigenvalues are $y_t=1$ and $y_h=15/8$. Substitution gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
\nu&=1/y_t=1,\\
\beta&=(2-15/8)/1=1/8,\\
\gamma&=(2\cdot15/8-2)/1=7/4,\\
\alpha&=2-2\cdot1=0.
\end{aligned}
$$

These agree with the exact two-dimensional Ising exponents. The heat-capacity singularity is logarithmic.

> [!note] Meaning of α = 0
> The exponent value alone does not establish a logarithmic divergence. That additional information comes from the exact solution; a finite jump can also have $\alpha=0$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\energy{f}_{\mathrm s}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Singular free energy per site | The singular part of the free energy per lattice site. |
| $\thermal{T}$ | $\unit{K}$ | Temperature | The absolute temperature; subscript c marks the transition temperature. |
| $b$ | $1$ | Scale factor | The spatial blocking and rescaling factor. |
| $d$ | $1$ | Spatial dimension | The number of spatial dimensions. |
| $h$ | — | Conjugate field | The field coupled to the order parameter; its unit depends on the normalization. |
