> [!TLDR]
> The grand potential has temperature, volume, and chemical potential as its natural variables.

**Definition 5.3 — Grand potential.** The grand potential, also called the Landau potential, is:

$$
{\energy{\Phi}}({\thermal{T}},{\mechanical{V}},{\energy{\mu}})=-{\thermal{k}_{B}} {\thermal{T}}\ln\Xi({\thermal{T}},{\mechanical{V}},{\energy{\mu}}).
$$

It has the same role here as Helmholtz free energy has in the canonical ensemble. Its thermodynamic form is:

$$
{\energy{\Phi}}={\energy{U}}-{\thermal{T}}{\thermal{S}}-{\energy{\mu}} {\mechanical{N}}={\energy{F}}-{\energy{\mu}} {\mechanical{N}}.
$$

For the ensemble thermodynamic quantities, ${\mechanical{N}}$ in this expression is the mean particle number. Differentiate and use $d{\energy{U}}={\thermal{T}}\,d{\thermal{S}}-{\mechanical{P}}\,d{\mechanical{V}}+{\energy{\mu}}\,d{\mechanical{N}}$:

$$
d{\energy{\Phi}}=-{\thermal{S}}\,d{\thermal{T}}-{\mechanical{P}}\,d{\mechanical{V}}-{\mechanical{N}}\,d{\energy{\mu}}.
$$

The natural derivatives are:

$$
{\thermal{S}}=-\left(\frac{\partial{\energy{\Phi}}}{\partial {\thermal{T}}}\right)_{V,\mu},\qquad
{\mechanical{P}}=-\left(\frac{\partial{\energy{\Phi}}}{\partial {\mechanical{V}}}\right)_{T,\mu},\qquad
\langle {\mechanical{N}}\rangle=-\left(\frac{\partial{\energy{\Phi}}}{\partial{\energy{\mu}}}\right)_{T,V}.
$$

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\energy{\Phi}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Grand potential | Helmholtz free energy minus chemical work. |
| ${\energy{U}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Internal energy | Mean internal energy of the thermodynamic system. |
| ${\energy{F}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Helmholtz free energy | Thermodynamic potential at fixed temperature and volume. |
| ${\thermal{T}}$ | $\unit{K}$ | Temperature | Absolute temperature. |
| ${\thermal{S}}$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Entropy | Thermodynamic or Gibbs entropy. |
| ${\energy{\mu}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Chemical potential | Energy per added particle; subscript or superscript identifies species or reference state. |
| ${\mechanical{N}}$ | $\unit{1}$ | Particle number | Actual or mean number; subscripts identify the subsystem. |
| ${\mechanical{P}}$ | $\unit{Pa}$ | Pressure | Bulk or partial gas pressure, as indicated. |
| ${\mechanical{V}}$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Volume | Volume of the specified system. |
| ${\thermal{k}_{B}}$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Boltzmann constant | Converts temperature to energy. |
