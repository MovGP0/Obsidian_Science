> [!TLDR]
> Eliminating fugacity makes the ideal-gas grand potential reproduce canonical thermodynamics.

For the canonical classical ideal gas:

$$
Z_N=\frac1{{\mechanical{N}}!}\left(\frac {\mechanical{V}}{{\signal{\lambda}}_T^3}\right)^{\mechanical{N}},
\qquad
{\energy{F}}\simeq-{\mechanical{N}}{\thermal{k}_{B}} {\thermal{T}}\left[\ln\left(\frac {\mechanical{V}}{{\mechanical{N}}{\signal{\lambda}}_T^3}\right)+1\right].
$$

The free-energy expression uses Stirling's approximation. The grand canonical calculation gives:

$$
\ln\Xi=\frac{z{\mechanical{V}}}{{\signal{\lambda}}_T^3},\qquad
{\mechanical{P}}=\frac{{\thermal{k}_{B}} {\thermal{T}}z}{{\signal{\lambda}}_T^3},\qquad
\langle {\mechanical{N}}\rangle=\frac{z{\mechanical{V}}}{{\signal{\lambda}}_T^3}.
$$

Eliminate fugacity with $z=\langle {\mechanical{N}}\rangle{\signal{\lambda}}_T^3/{\mechanical{V}}$:

$$
{\mechanical{P}}=\frac{\langle {\mechanical{N}}\rangle {\thermal{k}_{B}} {\thermal{T}}}{{\mechanical{V}}},\qquad
{\energy{\Phi}}=-{\mechanical{P}}{\mechanical{V}}=-\langle {\mechanical{N}}\rangle {\thermal{k}_{B}} {\thermal{T}}.
$$

Since ${\energy{\mu}}={\thermal{k}_{B}} {\thermal{T}}\ln z$:

$$
{\energy{\Phi}}+{\energy{\mu}}\langle {\mechanical{N}}\rangle
=-\langle {\mechanical{N}}\rangle {\thermal{k}_{B}} {\thermal{T}}\left[\ln\left(\frac {\mechanical{V}}{\langle {\mechanical{N}}\rangle{\signal{\lambda}}_T^3}\right)+1\right].
$$

This is the canonical free energy at ${\mechanical{N}}=\langle {\mechanical{N}}\rangle$, to the same thermodynamic accuracy. The equation of state agrees as well.
