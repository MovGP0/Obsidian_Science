> [!TLDR]
> Reservoir state counting and maximum entropy give the same normalized equilibrium distribution.

- [[05.02.01 System in Contact with a Reservoir]]
- [[05.02.02 Derivation of the Grand Canonical Distribution]]
- [[05.02.03 Derivation from the Maximum-Entropy Principle]]

There are two routes to the same equilibrium law. [[05.02.01 System in Contact with a Reservoir]] identifies what is conserved in the isolated composite and what can fluctuate in the subsystem. [[05.02.02 Derivation of the Grand Canonical Distribution]] then counts compatible reservoir states. Track the sign of the particle term: removing a particle from the reservoir changes its entropy differently from removing energy.

[[05.02.03 Derivation from the Maximum-Entropy Principle]] starts instead from specified mean values and a probability model. It reproduces the distribution but does not prove that an arbitrary driven open system reaches equilibrium. In either route, normalization must include all permitted particle numbers, not only the states of one fixed-number system.
