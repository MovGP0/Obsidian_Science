> [!TLDR]
> Equilibrium particle-number variance measures the subsystem response to the reservoir chemical potential.

- [[05.04.01 Calculation of the Variance]]
- [[05.04.02 Relation to Isothermal Compressibility]]
- [[05.04.03 Ideal-Gas Case]]

Can a system have a steady density while its particle count changes? Yes: equilibrium fixes the count distribution, not the count in each observation. [[05.04.01 Calculation of the Variance]] obtains the width by differentiating the normalized probability law. [[05.04.02 Relation to Isothermal Compressibility]] converts that width into a bulk mechanical response, with additional assumptions about a uniform phase.

Use [[05.04.03 Ideal-Gas Case]] as the reference check: independent dilute particles give a variance equal to the mean. Interactions or restricted site occupancy can change this ratio. Compare equilibrium samples of the same observation volume; a sealed total system has fixed total count and is a different fluctuation problem.
