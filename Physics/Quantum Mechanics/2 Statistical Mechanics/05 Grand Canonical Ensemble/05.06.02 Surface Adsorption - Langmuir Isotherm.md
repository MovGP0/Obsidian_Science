> [!TLDR]
> Independent adsorption sites give the Langmuir coverage law and a saturation limit.

The **Langmuir model** describes equilibrium between gas molecules and a solid surface. The surface has ${\mechanical{M}}$ independent, distinguishable sites. A site is either empty, with energy zero, or occupied by one molecule, with energy $-{\energy{\varepsilon}}_b$. Here ${\energy{\varepsilon}}_b=|{\energy{\varepsilon}}_0|>0$ is the binding-energy magnitude. The gas reservoir fixes ${\thermal{T}},{\energy{\mu}}$.

## Partition function of one site

Independence gives $\Xi_{\mathrm{surf}}=\xi_{\mathrm{site}}^{\mechanical{M}}$. Sum the two possible occupations:

$$
\xi_{\mathrm{site}}=1+e^{-\beta(-{\energy{\varepsilon}}_b-{\energy{\mu}})}
=1+e^{\beta({\energy{\mu}}+{\energy{\varepsilon}}_b)}=1+ze^{\beta{\energy{\varepsilon}}_b}.
$$

The probability of occupation is also its mean occupation:

$$
\theta=\langle {\mechanical{N}}_{\mathrm{site}}\rangle
=\frac{ze^{\beta{\energy{\varepsilon}}_b}}{1+ze^{\beta{\energy{\varepsilon}}_b}}.\tag{5.12}
$$

## Pressure form

For the ideal gas in equilibrium with the surface, $z={\mechanical{n}}{\signal{\lambda}}_T^3={\mechanical{P}}{\signal{\lambda}}_T^3/({\thermal{k}_{B}} {\thermal{T}})$. Define:

$$
{\mechanical{P}}_0({\thermal{T}})=\frac{{\thermal{k}_{B}} {\thermal{T}}}{{\signal{\lambda}}_T^3}e^{-\beta{\energy{\varepsilon}}_b}.
$$

Equation (5.12) becomes the **Langmuir isotherm**:

$$
\theta=\frac{{\mechanical{P}}/{\mechanical{P}}_0}{1+{\mechanical{P}}/{\mechanical{P}}_0}=\frac {\mechanical{P}}{{\mechanical{P}}+{\mechanical{P}}_0}.
$$

**Remark 5.4 — Physical limits.**

- For ${\mechanical{P}}\ll {\mechanical{P}}_0$, $\theta\simeq {\mechanical{P}}/{\mechanical{P}}_0$: the Henry regime, with coverage proportional to pressure.
- For ${\mechanical{P}}\gg {\mechanical{P}}_0$, $\theta\to1$: all sites approach occupation.
- For ${\mechanical{P}}={\mechanical{P}}_0$, $\theta=1/2$: half the sites are occupied.

## Adsorption heat

The total mean number of adsorbed molecules and the surface energy are:

$$
\langle {\mechanical{N}}_{\mathrm{ads}}\rangle={\mechanical{M}}\theta,\qquad
\langle {\energy{E}}\rangle=-{\mechanical{M}}\theta{\energy{\varepsilon}}_b.
$$

At fixed coverage, ${\mechanical{P}}/{\mechanical{P}}_0({\thermal{T}})$ is constant. As ${\signal{\lambda}}_T^{-3}\propto {\thermal{T}}^{3/2}$, the pressure prefactor is proportional to ${\thermal{T}}^{5/2}$. The **isosteric adsorption heat** per molecule is therefore:

$$
Q_{\mathrm{st}}=-\left(\frac{\partial\ln({\mechanical{P}}/{\mechanical{P}}_{\mathrm{ref}})}{\partial\beta}\right)_\theta
={\energy{\varepsilon}}_b+\frac52 {\thermal{k}_{B}} {\thermal{T}}\simeq{\energy{\varepsilon}}_b
\quad ({\energy{\varepsilon}}_b\gg {\thermal{k}_{B}} {\thermal{T}}).
$$

The reference pressure in the logarithm is fixed. It does not affect the derivative. See [[05.02 Langmuir Heat Capacity at Fixed Chemical Potential]] for the distinction between heat and energy change when particles are exchanged.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\mechanical{M}}$ | $\unit{1}$ | Site number | Number of independent adsorption sites. |
| ${\mechanical{N}}$ | $\unit{1}$ | Particle number | Actual or mean number; subscripts identify the subsystem. |
| ${\energy{E}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Energy | System, reservoir, microstate, or site energy, as indicated. |
| ${\energy{\varepsilon}}_b$ | $\unit{J}$ | Binding energy | Positive magnitude of the first-layer binding energy; other level energies are identified in the text. |
| ${\energy{\mu}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Chemical potential | Energy per added particle; subscript or superscript identifies species or reference state. |
| $\beta$ | $\unit{J^{-1}}$ | Inverse temperature | Defined as one over Boltzmann constant times temperature. |
| ${\mechanical{P}}$ | $\unit{Pa}$ | Pressure | Bulk or partial gas pressure, as indicated. |
| ${\mechanical{P}}_0$ | $\unit{Pa}$ | Reference pressure | Standard pressure, or Langmuir half-coverage pressure where stated. |
| ${\thermal{k}_{B}}$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Boltzmann constant | Converts temperature to energy. |
| ${\thermal{T}}$ | $\unit{K}$ | Temperature | Absolute temperature. |
| ${\signal{\lambda}}_T$ | $\unit{m}$ | Thermal de Broglie wavelength | Thermal wavelength of a particle; a scale factor of the same letter is not a wavelength. |
| ${\mechanical{n}}$ | $\unit{m^{-3}}$ | Number density | Particle number per volume; a site occupation with this letter is dimensionless. |
