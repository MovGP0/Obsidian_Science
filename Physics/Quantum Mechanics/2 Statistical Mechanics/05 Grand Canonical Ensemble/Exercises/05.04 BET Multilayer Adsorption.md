> [!TLDR]
> Summing the allowed layer occupations gives the BET isotherm and its saturation-pressure divergence.

## Question — Exercise 5.4

Extend the Langmuir model to multilayer adsorption, using the Brunauer–Emmett–Teller model. The first layer has binding energy $|{\energy{\varepsilon}}_0|$; later layers have binding-energy magnitude ${\energy{\varepsilon}}_L$. Show that:

$$
\theta=\frac{c({\mechanical{P}}/{\mechanical{P}}_s)}{(1-{\mechanical{P}}/{\mechanical{P}}_s)[1+(c-1){\mechanical{P}}/{\mechanical{P}}_s]},
\qquad c=e^{\beta(|{\energy{\varepsilon}}_0|-{\energy{\varepsilon}}_L)},
$$

where ${\mechanical{P}}_s$ is the saturation vapor pressure.

## Worked solution

### Assign column weights

Each independent surface column can contain $\ell=0,1,2,\ldots$ molecules. There are no interactions between columns. An occupied column gains binding energy $|{\energy{\varepsilon}}_0|$ for its first molecule and ${\energy{\varepsilon}}_L$ for every later molecule:

$$
{\energy{E}}_\ell=-|{\energy{\varepsilon}}_0|-(\ell-1){\energy{\varepsilon}}_L\quad(\ell\ge1).
$$

Let $x=ze^{\beta{\energy{\varepsilon}}_L}$ and $c=e^{\beta(|{\energy{\varepsilon}}_0|-{\energy{\varepsilon}}_L)}$. The grand weight is then:

$$
w_0=1,\qquad w_\ell=z^\ell e^{-\beta {\energy{E}}_\ell}=c x^\ell\quad(\ell\ge1).
$$

Within this ideal-vapor BET model, define saturation by $z_s e^{\beta{\energy{\varepsilon}}_L}=1$. Since vapor fugacity is proportional to pressure at fixed temperature, $x=z/z_s={\mechanical{P}}/{\mechanical{P}}_s$.

### Sum the column partition function

The geometric series converges for $0\le x<1$:

$$
\xi=1+c\sum_{\ell=1}^{\infty}x^\ell
=1+\frac{cx}{1-x}
=\frac{1+(c-1)x}{1-x}.
$$

### Calculate mean layer number

Differentiate the logarithm because each power of $x$ counts molecules in a column:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\theta=\langle\ell\rangle
&=x\frac{\partial\ln\xi}{\partial x}\\
&=x\left[\frac{c-1}{1+(c-1)x}+\frac1{1-x}\right]\\
&=\frac{cx}{(1-x)[1+(c-1)x]}.
\end{aligned}
$$

## Answer

With $x={\mechanical{P}}/{\mechanical{P}}_s$, this is the requested BET isotherm. Here $\theta$ is the mean number of molecules per column, measured in monolayers. It can exceed one, unlike Langmuir fractional coverage. As ${\mechanical{P}}\to {\mechanical{P}}_s^-$, the ideal model permits arbitrarily thick layers and $\theta$ diverges. This limit signals bulk condensation and the limits of the independent-column approximation.
