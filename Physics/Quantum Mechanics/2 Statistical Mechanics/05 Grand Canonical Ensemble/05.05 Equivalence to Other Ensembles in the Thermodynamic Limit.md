> [!TLDR]
> Matched ensembles can agree on bulk thermodynamics while retaining different global fluctuation constraints.

- [[05.05.01 Thermodynamic Limit]]
- [[05.05.02 Proof for the Ideal Gas]]
- [[05.05.03 When Ensembles Are Not Equivalent]]

Ensemble equivalence asks which predictions survive a change in the imposed constraints. Read [[05.05.01 Thermodynamic Limit]] to match size and density before taking the limit. [[05.05.02 Proof for the Ideal Gas]] compares a canonical free energy with the correctly transformed grand potential, rather than equating two different thermodynamic potentials directly.

Then use [[05.05.03 When Ensembles Are Not Equivalent]] to separate agreement of bulk thermodynamics from agreement of entire probability distributions. A canonical particle count is exact and a grand canonical count fluctuates even when their pressures agree. Small relative fluctuations explain why many bulk measurements can agree; they do not erase the different global constraints.
