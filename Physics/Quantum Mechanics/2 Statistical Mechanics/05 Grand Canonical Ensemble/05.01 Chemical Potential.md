> [!TLDR]
> Chemical potential gives the free-energy cost of matter transfer under stated thermodynamic constraints.

- [[05.01.01 Thermodynamic Definition]]
- [[05.01.02 Gibbs–Duhem Relation]]
- [[05.01.03 Physical Interpretation]]
- [[05.01.04 Chemical Potential of the Classical Ideal Gas]]

The physical question is which way matter moves when two bodies can exchange the same species. Start with [[05.01.01 Thermodynamic Definition]]: adding matter changes a thermodynamic potential, and the variables held fixed determine which derivative to use. [[05.01.02 Gibbs–Duhem Relation]] then shows why pressure, temperature, and chemical potential cannot be chosen independently in a one-component bulk phase.

[[05.01.03 Physical Interpretation]] uses free-energy decrease to determine the transfer direction. Finally, [[05.01.04 Chemical Potential of the Classical Ideal Gas]] supplies a calculable example and a dilute-limit check. Do not infer a transfer direction from density alone when the two bodies have different temperatures or interactions.
