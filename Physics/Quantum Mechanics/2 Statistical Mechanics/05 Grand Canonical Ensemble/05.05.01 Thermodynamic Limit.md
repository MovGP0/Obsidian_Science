> [!TLDR]
> The three ensembles give the same bulk thermodynamics under the usual thermodynamic-limit assumptions.

The **thermodynamic limit** is:

$$
{\mechanical{N}}\to\infty,\qquad {\mechanical{V}}\to\infty,\qquad {\mechanical{n}}=\frac {\mechanical{N}}{\mechanical{V}}=\text{constant}.
$$

For homogeneous systems with short-range interactions and a regular equilibrium state, the ensembles give the same pressure, energy density, and chemical potential. Relative fluctuations usually decrease as ${\mechanical{N}}^{-1/2}$.

**Theorem 5.1 — Equivalence of ensembles.** At matched thermodynamic states, the entropy per particle and the corresponding free energies agree in this limit:

$$
\frac{{\thermal{S}}_{\mathrm{micro}}({\energy{E}},{\mechanical{V}},{\mechanical{N}})}{\mechanical{N}}
\longrightarrow\frac{{\thermal{S}}_{\mathrm{can}}({\thermal{T}},{\mechanical{V}},{\mechanical{N}})}{\mechanical{N}}
\longrightarrow\frac{{\thermal{S}}_{\mathrm{grand}}({\thermal{T}},{\mechanical{V}},{\energy{\mu}})}{\langle {\mechanical{N}}\rangle},
$$

$$
\frac{{\energy{F}}_{\mathrm{can}}({\thermal{T}},{\mechanical{V}},{\mechanical{N}})}{\mechanical{N}}
\longrightarrow\frac{{\energy{\Phi}}({\thermal{T}},{\mechanical{V}},{\energy{\mu}})+{\energy{\mu}}\langle {\mechanical{N}}\rangle}{\langle {\mechanical{N}}\rangle}.
$$

The arrows describe agreement of limiting values, with ${\mechanical{N}}=\langle {\mechanical{N}}\rangle$ for the comparison. They do not equate potentials at unrelated state parameters. See [[05.05.03 When Ensembles Are Not Equivalent]] for qualifications.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\mechanical{N}}$ | $\unit{1}$ | Particle number | Actual or mean number; subscripts identify the subsystem. |
| ${\mechanical{V}}$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Volume | Volume of the specified system. |
| ${\mechanical{n}}$ | $\unit{m^{-3}}$ | Number density | Particle number per volume; a site occupation with this letter is dimensionless. |
| ${\thermal{S}}$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Entropy | Thermodynamic or Gibbs entropy. |
| ${\energy{F}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Helmholtz free energy | Thermodynamic potential at fixed temperature and volume. |
| ${\energy{\Phi}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Grand potential | Helmholtz free energy minus chemical work. |
| ${\energy{\mu}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Chemical potential | Energy per added particle; subscript or superscript identifies species or reference state. |
| ${\thermal{T}}$ | $\unit{K}$ | Temperature | Absolute temperature. |
| ${\energy{E}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Energy | System, reservoir, microstate, or site energy, as indicated. |
