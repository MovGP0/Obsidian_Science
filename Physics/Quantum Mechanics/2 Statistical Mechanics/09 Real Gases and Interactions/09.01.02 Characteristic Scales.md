> [!TLDR]
> Molecular diameter and well depth set the length and energy scales of a real gas.

For a pair potential ${\energy{u}}({\mechanical{r}})$, two scales are useful.

- **Collision distance:** an effective diameter ${\mechanical{\sigma}}\sim2$–$5\,\unit{\mathring A}$. The density scale at which molecules are close is ${\mechanical{n}}\sim{\mechanical{\sigma}}^{-3}$, of order $10^{28}$–$10^{29}\,\unit{m^{-3}}$.
- **Well depth:** an attractive energy scale ${\energy{\varepsilon}}$ from a few $\unit{meV}$ to about $0.1\,\unit{eV}$, depending on the molecule. The corresponding temperature scale is ${\energy{\varepsilon}}/{\thermal{k}}_B$. The source gives about $120\,\unit K$ for argon and $95\,\unit K$ for nitrogen.

The source introduces an interaction measure

$$
{\mechanical{\Gamma}}={\mechanical{n}}{\mechanical{\sigma}}^3\frac{{\energy{\varepsilon}}}{{\thermal{k}}_B{\thermal{T}}}.
$$

It combines a packing measure with the ratio of attraction energy to thermal energy. A small value indicates weak attractive corrections. Values of order one indicate significant corrections.

> [!note] Remark 9.1 and scope
> The source estimates ${\mechanical{\Gamma}}\sim10^{-3}$ for nitrogen and a few times $10^{-3}$ for carbon dioxide at room conditions. It reports percent-level deviations at ${\thermal{T}}=300\,\unit K$ and ${\mechanical{P}}=100\,\unit{atm}$. These are source estimates, not new measurements. A small ${\mechanical{\Gamma}}$ alone is not sufficient at high temperature: excluded-volume effects also require ${\mechanical{n}}{\mechanical{\sigma}}^3\ll1$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\energy{u}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Pair potential | Interaction energy of two molecules at separation r. |
| ${\mechanical{r}}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Separation | Distance between the centers of two molecules. |
| ${\mechanical{\sigma}}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Molecular length | Hard-sphere diameter or Lennard–Jones zero-crossing distance, as stated. |
| ${\mechanical{n}}$ | $\unit{m^{-3}}$ | Number density | Particle number divided by volume. |
| ${\energy{\varepsilon}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Well depth | Positive energy scale of the attractive potential well. |
| ${\thermal{k}}_B$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Boltzmann constant | Converts temperature to energy per particle. |
| ${\thermal{T}}$ | $\unit{K}$ | Temperature | Absolute temperature of the gas. |
| ${\mechanical{\Gamma}}$ | $\unit{1}$ | Interaction parameter | Packing measure times the ratio of interaction energy to thermal energy. |
| ${\mechanical{P}}$ | $\unit{Pa}$ | Pressure | Mechanical pressure of the gas. |
