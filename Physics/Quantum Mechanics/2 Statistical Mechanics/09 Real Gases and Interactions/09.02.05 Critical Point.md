> [!TLDR]
> The critical point is a horizontal inflection of the pressure–volume isotherm.

At the critical point, both the first and second volume derivatives of pressure vanish. Use volume per molecule ${\mechanical{v}}={\mechanical{V}}/{\mechanical{N}}$:

$$
{\mechanical{P}}=\frac{{\thermal{k}}_B{\thermal{T}}}{{\mechanical{v}}-{\mechanical{b}}}-\frac{{\energy{a}}}{{\mechanical{v}}^2}.
$$

Thus

$$
\left(\frac{\partial {\mechanical{P}}}{\partial {\mechanical{v}}}\right)_T
=-\frac{{\thermal{k}}_B{\thermal{T}}}{({\mechanical{v}}-{\mechanical{b}})^2}+\frac{2{\energy{a}}}{{\mechanical{v}}^3}=0, \tag{9.4}
$$

$$
\left(\frac{\partial^2{\mechanical{P}}}{\partial {\mechanical{v}}^2}\right)_T
=\frac{2{\thermal{k}}_B{\thermal{T}}}{({\mechanical{v}}-{\mechanical{b}})^3}-\frac{6{\energy{a}}}{{\mechanical{v}}^4}=0. \tag{9.5}
$$

Divide the equality between the positive terms in the second equation by that in the first. This avoids division by a derivative that is zero:

$$
\frac{2}{{\mechanical{v}}_c-{\mechanical{b}}}=\frac{3}{{\mechanical{v}}_c}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad {\mechanical{v}}_c=3{\mechanical{b}}.
$$

Substitute into (9.4):

$$
{\thermal{k}}_B{\thermal{T}}_c=\frac{2{\energy{a}}({\mechanical{v}}_c-{\mechanical{b}})^2}{{\mechanical{v}}_c^3}=\frac{8{\energy{a}}}{27{\mechanical{b}}}.
$$

The critical constants are

$$
{\mechanical{V}}_c=3{\mechanical{N}}{\mechanical{b}},\qquad {\thermal{T}}_c=\frac{8{\energy{a}}}{27{\thermal{k}}_B{\mechanical{b}}},\qquad {\mechanical{P}}_c=\frac{{\energy{a}}}{27{\mechanical{b}}^2}. \tag{9.6}
$$

The model predicts the universal critical compressibility factor

$$
{\mechanical{Z}}_c=\frac{{\mechanical{P}}_c{\mechanical{V}}_c}{{\mechanical{N}}{\thermal{k}}_B{\thermal{T}}_c}=\frac38=0.375.
$$

The source lists typical measured values around 0.23–0.29. This difference shows the quantitative limits of the model.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\mechanical{P}}$ | $\unit{Pa}$ | Pressure | Mechanical pressure of the gas. |
| ${\mechanical{v}}$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Volume per particle | Total volume divided by particle number. |
| ${\mechanical{V}}$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Volume | Total volume occupied by the gas. |
| ${\mechanical{N}}$ | $\unit{1}$ | Particle number | Number of molecules. |

| ${\thermal{T}}_c$ | $\unit{K}$ | Critical temperature | Temperature at the liquid–vapor critical point. |
| ${\mechanical{V}}_c$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Critical volume | Total volume at the critical point for the stated particle number. |
| ${\mechanical{P}}_c$ | $\unit{Pa}$ | Critical pressure | Pressure at the liquid–vapor critical point. |
| ${\mechanical{Z}}$ | $\unit{1}$ | Compressibility factor | Pressure times volume divided by ideal-gas thermal energy. |
