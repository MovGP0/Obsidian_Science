> [!TLDR]
> Excluded volume and attraction give a simple model of liquid–vapor coexistence.

The van der Waals model combines a geometric correction with an attractive mean field.

Read the two corrections separately before combining them. Excluded volume changes the entropy of accessible positions. The attractive mean field changes the interaction energy. Their combination is a model assumption; it is not an exact solution for arbitrary pair forces or dense packing.

The later sections ask a second question: which solutions can represent equilibrium? The pressure slope tests local mechanical stability, and the Maxwell construction compares coexisting phases. The critical constants and reduced variables then test what this two-parameter model predicts for different gases. Recovery of the ideal-gas law at large volume is a necessary check; it does not establish accuracy near the critical point.
