> [!TLDR]
> The conversion from cluster integrals to virial coefficients cancels reducible chains.

The Mayer–Montroll relations give

$$
{\mechanical{B}}_2=-{\mechanical{b}}_2=-\frac12\int_0^\infty f({\mechanical{r}})4\pi {\mechanical{r}}^2\,d{\mechanical{r}},
$$

$$
{\mechanical{B}}_3=-2{\mechanical{b}}_3+4{\mechanical{b}}_2^2.
$$

To see the cancellation, fix molecule 1. Each chain integrates to the square of the single-bond integral. The sum of the three chains contributes $2{\mechanical{b}}_2^2$ to ${\mechanical{b}}_3$. Consequently,

$$
{\mechanical{b}}_3=2{\mechanical{b}}_2^2+\frac16\int\!\int f_{12}f_{13}f_{23}\,d^3{\mechanical{\mathbf r}}_2\,d^3{\mechanical{\mathbf r}}_3,
$$

and therefore

$$
{\mechanical{B}}_3=-\frac13\int\!\int f_{12}f_{13}f_{23}\,d^3{\mechanical{\mathbf r}}_2\,d^3{\mechanical{\mathbf r}}_3.
$$

This agrees with [[09.03.02 Pressure and Coefficients]].

> [!note] Remark 9.2 — graph structure
> Disconnected graphs factor into independent components. Connected graphs determine the logarithm of the partition function. After conversion to a series in density, the irreducible correlations remain. Thus connectedness alone is not enough to identify a graph that contributes to a virial coefficient.
