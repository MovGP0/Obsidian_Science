> [!TLDR]
> Below the critical temperature, the van der Waals isotherm contains a mechanically unstable section.

Solve (9.2) for pressure:

$$
{\mechanical{P}}=\frac{{\mechanical{N}}{\thermal{k}}_B{\thermal{T}}}{{\mechanical{V}}-{\mechanical{N}}{\mechanical{b}}}-\frac{{\energy{a}}{\mechanical{N}}^2}{{\mechanical{V}}^2}. \tag{9.3}
$$

Above ${\thermal{T}}_c$, the isotherms decrease monotonically. Below ${\thermal{T}}_c$, a range of pressures gives three volume roots. The middle branch has $(\partial {\mechanical{P}}/\partial {\mechanical{V}})_T>0$ and is mechanically unstable. Stable equilibrium replaces part of the loop by liquid–vapor coexistence.

**Fig. 9.1** Calculated van der Waals isotherms in reduced variables. The critical isotherm has a horizontal inflection at (1, 1). Below the critical temperature, a positive-slope branch is unstable. The dashed segment is the Maxwell coexistence pressure for T/T꜀ = 0.85: P₀/P꜀ ≈ 0.50449, with liquid and vapor volumes V/V꜀ ≈ 0.55336 and 3.12764. Negative-pressure parts of the lowest curve extend below the displayed range.

The coexistence pressure in Fig. 9.1 is calculated by the equal-area condition for the ${\thermal{T}}=0.85{\thermal{T}}_c$ curve. It is not a common coexistence pressure for all the plotted temperatures.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\mechanical{P}}$ | $\unit{Pa}$ | Pressure | Mechanical pressure of the gas. |
| ${\mechanical{N}}$ | $\unit{1}$ | Particle number | Number of molecules. |
| ${\thermal{k}}_B$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Boltzmann constant | Converts temperature to energy per particle. |
| ${\thermal{T}}$ | $\unit{K}$ | Temperature | Absolute temperature of the gas. |
| ${\mechanical{V}}$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Volume | Total volume occupied by the gas. |
| ${\mechanical{b}}$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Excluded-volume parameter | Molecular van der Waals covolume per particle. |
| ${\energy{a}}$ | $\unit{J\,m^3}$ | Attraction parameter | Molecular van der Waals parameter for the attractive interaction. |
| ${\thermal{T}}_c$ | $\unit{K}$ | Critical temperature | Temperature at the liquid–vapor critical point. |
| ${\mechanical{P}}/{\mechanical{P}}_c$ | $\unit{1}$ | Reduced pressure | Pressure divided by critical pressure. |
| ${\mechanical{V}}/{\mechanical{V}}_c$ | $\unit{1}$ | Reduced volume | Volume divided by critical volume at fixed particle number. |
| ${\thermal{T}}/{\thermal{T}}_c$ | $\unit{1}$ | Reduced temperature | Temperature divided by critical temperature. |
| ${\mechanical{P}}_c$ | $\unit{Pa}$ | Critical pressure | Pressure at the critical point. |
| ${\mechanical{V}}_c$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Critical volume | Volume at the critical point for the stated particle number. |
