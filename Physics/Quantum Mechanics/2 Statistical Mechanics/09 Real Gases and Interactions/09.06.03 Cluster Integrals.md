> [!TLDR]
> A cluster integral sums the position integrals of connected graphs with a fixed number of molecules.

Use the following convention, without thermal-wavelength factors:

$$
{\mechanical{b}}_\ell=\frac1{\ell!{\mechanical{V}}}\int
\left(\sum_{\text{connected graphs}}\prod_{\text{edges }ij}f_{ij}\right)
\,d^3{\mechanical{\mathbf r}}_1\cdots d^3{\mechanical{\mathbf r}}_\ell.
$$

The first integrals are

$$
{\mechanical{b}}_1=1,
$$

$$
{\mechanical{b}}_2=\frac1{2{\mechanical{V}}}\int\!\int f_{12}\,d^3{\mechanical{\mathbf r}}_1\,d^3{\mechanical{\mathbf r}}_2
=\frac12\int_0^\infty f({\mechanical{r}})4\pi {\mechanical{r}}^2\,d{\mechanical{r}},
$$

$$
{\mechanical{b}}_3=\frac1{6{\mechanical{V}}}\int\!\int\!\int
\left(f_{12}f_{23}+f_{12}f_{13}+f_{13}f_{23}+f_{12}f_{13}f_{23}\right)
\,d^3{\mechanical{\mathbf r}}_1\,d^3{\mechanical{\mathbf r}}_2\,d^3{\mechanical{\mathbf r}}_3.
$$

There are three chains and one triangle in the last expression. Translation invariance supplies one overall volume factor, which cancels the denominator volume. Boundary corrections are neglected in the thermodynamic limit.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\mechanical{b}}_\ell$ | $\unit{m^{3(\ell-1)}}$ | Cluster integral | Connected integral for the stated number of particles. |
| ${\mechanical{V}}$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Volume | Total volume occupied by the gas. |
| ${\mechanical{\mathbf r}}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Position | Position vector of a molecule. |
| ${\mechanical{b}}_2$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Two-particle cluster integral | Connected two-particle integral in the stated volume convention. |
| ${\mechanical{b}}_3$ | $\unit{m^6}$ | Three-particle cluster integral | Sum of connected three-particle integrals in the stated volume convention. |
| ${\mechanical{r}}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Separation | Distance between the centers of two molecules. |
