> [!TLDR]
> Pressure derivatives of the partition function give virial coefficients as integrals of Mayer functions.

At fixed temperature and particle number,

$$
{\mechanical{P}}={\thermal{k}}_B{\thermal{T}}\left(\frac{\partial\ln\mathcal Z}{\partial {\mechanical{V}}}\right)_{T,N}.
$$

Expand the configurational integral in clusters, take its logarithm, and differentiate. The second coefficient is

$$
{\mechanical{B}}_2({\thermal{T}})=-\frac12\int_{\mathbb R^3}f({\mechanical{r}})\,d^3{\mechanical{\mathbf r}}
=-\frac12\int_0^\infty\left(e^{-{\energy{u}}({\mechanical{r}})/({\thermal{k}}_B{\thermal{T}})}-1\right)4\pi {\mechanical{r}}^2\,d{\mechanical{r}}.\tag{9.10}
$$

For a system with pair interactions, the third coefficient is

$$
{\mechanical{B}}_3({\thermal{T}})=-\frac13\int\!\int f_{12}f_{13}f_{23}\,d^3{\mechanical{\mathbf r}}_2\,d^3{\mechanical{\mathbf r}}_3.
$$

Here molecule 1 is fixed at the origin. Translational invariance removes its position integral. The remaining triangle connects all three molecules.

See [[09.06.04 Relation between Cluster Integrals and Virial Coefficients]] for the cancellation of the chain contributions.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\mechanical{P}}$ | $\unit{Pa}$ | Pressure | Mechanical pressure of the gas. |
| ${\thermal{k}}_B$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Boltzmann constant | Converts temperature to energy per particle. |
| ${\thermal{T}}$ | $\unit{K}$ | Temperature | Absolute temperature of the gas. |
| ${\mechanical{V}}$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Volume | Total volume occupied by the gas. |
| ${\mechanical{N}}$ | $\unit{1}$ | Particle number | Number of molecules. |
| ${\mechanical{B}}_2$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Second virial coefficient | Coefficient of number density in the compressibility-factor expansion. |
| ${\mechanical{B}}_3$ | $\unit{m^6}$ | Third virial coefficient | Coefficient of squared number density in the compressibility-factor expansion. |
| ${\mechanical{r}}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Separation | Distance between the centers of two molecules. |
| ${\mechanical{\mathbf r}}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Position | Position vector of a molecule. |
| ${\energy{u}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Pair potential | Interaction energy of two molecules at separation r. |
