> [!TLDR]
> The latent heat vanishes as the liquid and vapor become identical at the critical point.

## Question — Exercise 9.4

Calculate the latent heat of vaporization of a van der Waals gas from the Clausius–Clapeyron relation and show that it approaches zero at the critical temperature:

$$
\frac{d{\mechanical{P}}_0}{d{\thermal{T}}}=\frac{{\energy{L}}}{{\thermal{T}}({\mechanical{V}}_g-{\mechanical{V}}_\ell)}.
$$

Use the same fixed particle number in the liquid and vapor volumes and in the latent heat.

## Calculation and solution

### Express latent heat through the coexistence curve

**Why:** The Maxwell construction supplies the two equilibrium volumes and the coexistence pressure for each temperature.

Rearrange the relation:

$$
\boxed{{\energy{L}}({\thermal{T}})={\thermal{T}}\,[{\mechanical{V}}_g({\thermal{T}})-{\mechanical{V}}_\ell({\thermal{T}})]\frac{d{\mechanical{P}}_0}{d{\thermal{T}}}.}
$$

For temperatures away from the critical point, the coexistence volumes are found numerically. An equivalent explicit expression in terms of those volumes follows from entropy.

### Calculate the entropy difference

The Maxwell relation gives

$$
\left(\frac{\partial {\thermal{S}}}{\partial {\mechanical{V}}}\right)_T
=\left(\frac{\partial {\mechanical{P}}}{\partial {\thermal{T}}}\right)_V
=\frac{{\mechanical{N}}{\thermal{k}}_B}{{\mechanical{V}}-{\mechanical{N}}{\mechanical{b}}}.
$$

Integrate at fixed temperature between the two equilibrium volumes:

$$
{\thermal{S}}_g-{\thermal{S}}_\ell={\mechanical{N}}{\thermal{k}}_B\ln\left(\frac{{\mechanical{V}}_g-{\mechanical{N}}{\mechanical{b}}}{{\mechanical{V}}_\ell-{\mechanical{N}}{\mechanical{b}}}\right).
$$

Vaporization is reversible along coexistence, so

$$
\boxed{{\energy{L}}({\thermal{T}})={\mechanical{N}}{\thermal{k}}_B{\thermal{T}}\ln\left(\frac{{\mechanical{V}}_g-{\mechanical{N}}{\mechanical{b}}}{{\mechanical{V}}_\ell-{\mechanical{N}}{\mechanical{b}}}\right).}
$$

This is the latent heat for the stated amount of gas. Divide by particle number for the value per molecule, or by amount of substance for the molar value.

### Evaluate the critical limit

Set ${\thermal{\delta}}=1-{\thermal{T}}/{\thermal{T}}_c>0$. From the Maxwell expansion,

$$
{\mechanical{V}}_g={\mechanical{V}}_c[1+2\sqrt{\thermal{\delta}}+O({\thermal{\delta}})],\qquad
{\mechanical{V}}_\ell={\mechanical{V}}_c[1-2\sqrt{\thermal{\delta}}+O({\thermal{\delta}})].
$$

Since ${\mechanical{V}}_c=3{\mechanical{N}}{\mechanical{b}}$, their difference is $12{\mechanical{N}}{\mechanical{b}}\sqrt{\thermal{\delta}}$ to leading order. The coexistence slope approaches ${\thermal{k}}_B/(2{\mechanical{b}})$. Thus

$$
{\energy{L}}({\thermal{T}})\sim {\thermal{T}}_c\,(12{\mechanical{N}}{\mechanical{b}}\sqrt{\thermal{\delta}})\frac{{\thermal{k}}_B}{2{\mechanical{b}}}
=6{\mechanical{N}}{\thermal{k}}_B{\thermal{T}}_c\sqrt{\thermal{\delta}}.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{{\energy{L}}({\thermal{T}})\to0\quad\text{as }{\thermal{T}}\to {\thermal{T}}_c^-.}
$$

**Check:** In the logarithmic expression, both free volumes approach the same value. Its logarithm approaches zero. This independently confirms the limit.

See [[09.03 Coexistence-Curve Slope]].

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\mechanical{P}}_0$ | $\unit{Pa}$ | Coexistence pressure | Pressure at which liquid and vapor coexist at the specified temperature. |
| ${\thermal{T}}$ | $\unit{K}$ | Temperature | Absolute temperature of the gas. |
| ${\energy{L}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Latent heat | Enthalpy difference between vapor and liquid for the same particle number. |
| ${\mechanical{V}}$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Volume | Total volume occupied by the gas. |
| ${\mechanical{N}}$ | $\unit{1}$ | Particle number | Number of molecules. |
| ${\mechanical{b}}$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Excluded-volume parameter | Molecular van der Waals covolume per particle. |
| ${\thermal{S}}$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Entropy | Thermodynamic entropy of the gas. |
| ${\mechanical{P}}$ | $\unit{Pa}$ | Pressure | Mechanical pressure of the gas. |
| ${\thermal{k}}_B$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Boltzmann constant | Converts temperature to energy per particle. |
| ${\thermal{T}}_c$ | $\unit{K}$ | Critical temperature | Temperature at the liquid–vapor critical point. |
| ${\thermal{\delta}}$ | $\unit{1}$ | Distance below the critical temperature | One minus reduced temperature, taken positive below the critical point. |
| ${\mechanical{V}}_c$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Critical volume | Total volume at the critical point for the stated particle number. |
