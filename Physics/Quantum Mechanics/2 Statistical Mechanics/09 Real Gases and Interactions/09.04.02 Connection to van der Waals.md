> [!TLDR]
> The van der Waals model gives a constant repulsive term and an attractive term proportional to inverse temperature.

At low number density, expand the excluded-volume denominator:

$$
\begin{aligned}
{\mechanical{P}}&=\frac{{\mechanical{N}}{\thermal{k}}_B{\thermal{T}}}{{\mechanical{V}}-{\mechanical{N}}{\mechanical{b}}}-\frac{{\energy{a}}{\mechanical{N}}^2}{{\mechanical{V}}^2}\\
&=\frac{{\mechanical{N}}{\thermal{k}}_B{\thermal{T}}}{{\mechanical{V}}}\frac1{1-{\mechanical{N}}{\mechanical{b}}/{\mechanical{V}}}-\frac{{\energy{a}}{\mechanical{N}}^2}{{\mechanical{V}}^2}\\
&=\frac{{\mechanical{N}}{\thermal{k}}_B{\thermal{T}}}{{\mechanical{V}}}\left(1+\frac{{\mechanical{N}}{\mechanical{b}}}{{\mechanical{V}}}+\cdots\right)-\frac{{\energy{a}}{\mechanical{N}}^2}{{\mechanical{V}}^2}.
\end{aligned}
$$

Thus

$$
\frac{{\mechanical{P}}{\mechanical{V}}}{{\mechanical{N}}{\thermal{k}}_B{\thermal{T}}}=1+\left({\mechanical{b}}-\frac{{\energy{a}}}{{\thermal{k}}_B{\thermal{T}}}\right)\frac {\mechanical{N}}{\mechanical{V}}+\cdots.
$$

Comparison with Eq. (9.9) gives

$$
{\mechanical{B}}_2^{\mathrm{vdW}}({\thermal{T}})={\mechanical{b}}-\frac{{\energy{a}}}{{\thermal{k}}_B{\thermal{T}}}.\tag{9.12}
$$

The excluded-volume term is positive. The attraction term is negative. In a molar convention, use

$$
{\mechanical{B}}_{2,m}^{\mathrm{vdW}}={\mechanical{b}}_m-\frac{{\energy{a}}_m}{{\thermal{R}}{\thermal{T}}}.
$$

Do not combine molecular parameters with the molar gas constant.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\mechanical{P}}$ | $\unit{Pa}$ | Pressure | Mechanical pressure of the gas. |
| ${\mechanical{N}}$ | $\unit{1}$ | Particle number | Number of molecules. |
| ${\thermal{k}}_B$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Boltzmann constant | Converts temperature to energy per particle. |
| ${\thermal{T}}$ | $\unit{K}$ | Temperature | Absolute temperature of the gas. |
| ${\mechanical{V}}$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Volume | Total volume occupied by the gas. |
| ${\mechanical{b}}$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Excluded-volume parameter | Molecular van der Waals covolume per particle. |
| ${\energy{a}}$ | $\unit{J\,m^3}$ | Attraction parameter | Molecular van der Waals parameter for the attractive interaction. |
| ${\mechanical{B}}_2$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Second virial coefficient | Coefficient of number density in the compressibility-factor expansion. |
| ${\mechanical{B}}_{2,m}$ | $\unit{m^3\,mol^{-1}}$ | Molar second virial coefficient | Coefficient used with molar density. |
| ${\mechanical{b}}_m$ | $\unit{m^3\,mol^{-1}}$ | Molar excluded-volume parameter | Molar van der Waals covolume. |
| ${\energy{a}}_m$ | $\unit{Pa\,m^6\,mol^{-2}}$ | Molar attraction parameter | Molar van der Waals attraction parameter. |
| ${\thermal{R}}$ | $\unit{J\,mol^{-1}\,K^{-1}}$ | Molar gas constant | Boltzmann constant times the Avogadro constant. |
