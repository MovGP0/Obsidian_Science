> [!TLDR]
> To first order in density, the Joule–Thomson coefficient follows from the second virial coefficient.

Let ${\thermal{c}}_P={\thermal{C}}_P/{\mechanical{N}}$ be the heat capacity per molecule. The leading virial approximation gives

$$
{\thermal{\mu}}_{JT}=\frac1{{\thermal{c}}_P}\left({\thermal{T}}\frac{d{\mechanical{B}}_2}{d{\thermal{T}}}-{\mechanical{B}}_2\right).\tag{9.14}
$$

For the van der Waals coefficient,

$$
{\mechanical{B}}_2={\mechanical{b}}-\frac{{\energy{a}}}{{\thermal{k}}_B{\thermal{T}}},\qquad \frac{d{\mechanical{B}}_2}{d{\thermal{T}}}=\frac{{\energy{a}}}{{\thermal{k}}_B{\thermal{T}}^2}.
$$

Therefore

$$
{\thermal{\mu}}_{JT}\approx\frac1{{\thermal{c}}_P}\left(\frac{2{\energy{a}}}{{\thermal{k}}_B{\thermal{T}}}-{\mechanical{b}}\right).
$$

The attraction and excluded-volume terms have opposite signs. A pressure decrease cools the gas when the bracket is positive. See [[09.06 Joule–Thomson Coefficient from the Virial Expansion]] for the complete derivation.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\thermal{c}}_P$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Heat capacity per particle | Heat capacity at constant pressure divided by particle number. |
| ${\thermal{C}}_P$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Heat capacity at constant pressure | Total heat capacity of the stated amount of gas. |
| ${\mechanical{N}}$ | $\unit{1}$ | Particle number | Number of molecules. |
| ${\thermal{\mu}}_{JT}$ | $\unit{K\,Pa^{-1}}$ | Joule–Thomson coefficient | Temperature derivative with respect to pressure at constant enthalpy. |
| ${\thermal{T}}$ | $\unit{K}$ | Temperature | Absolute temperature of the gas. |
| ${\mechanical{B}}_2$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Second virial coefficient | Coefficient of number density in the compressibility-factor expansion. |
| ${\mechanical{b}}$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Excluded-volume parameter | Molecular van der Waals covolume per particle. |
| ${\energy{a}}$ | $\unit{J\,m^3}$ | Attraction parameter | Molecular van der Waals parameter for the attractive interaction. |
| ${\thermal{k}}_B$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Boltzmann constant | Converts temperature to energy per particle. |
