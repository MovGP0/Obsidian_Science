> [!TLDR]
> The van der Waals equation combines excluded volume with a mean-field attractive pressure.

Combine the corrections from [[09.02.01 Excluded Volume]] and [[09.02.02 Long-Range Attraction]]:

$$
\left({\mechanical{P}}+\frac{{\energy{a}}{\mechanical{N}}^2}{{\mechanical{V}}^2}\right)({\mechanical{V}}-{\mechanical{N}}{\mechanical{b}})={\mechanical{N}}{\thermal{k}}_B{\thermal{T}}. \tag{9.2}
$$

Here ${\energy{a}}$ and ${\mechanical{b}}$ are **molecular** parameters. With amount ${\mechanical{n}}_{\mathrm{mol}}={\mechanical{N}}/{\mechanical{N}}_A$ and molar volume ${\mechanical{v}}_m={\mechanical{V}}/{\mechanical{n}}_{\mathrm{mol}}$, define

$$
{\energy{a}}_m={\mechanical{N}}_A^2{\energy{a}},\qquad {\mechanical{b}}_m={\mechanical{N}}_A{\mechanical{b}},\qquad {\thermal{R}}={\mechanical{N}}_A{\thermal{k}}_B.
$$

The molar equation is

$$
\left({\mechanical{P}}+\frac{{\energy{a}}_m}{{\mechanical{v}}_m^2}\right)({\mechanical{v}}_m-{\mechanical{b}}_m)={\thermal{R}}{\thermal{T}}.
$$

> [!note] Notation correction
> The source uses the same letters ${\energy{a}},{\mechanical{b}}$ for molecular and molar parameters. Their units differ. These notes use ${\energy{a}}_m,{\mechanical{b}}_m$ for molar values to prevent a unit error.

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
