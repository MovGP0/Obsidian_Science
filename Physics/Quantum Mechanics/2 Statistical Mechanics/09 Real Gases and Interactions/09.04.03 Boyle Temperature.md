> [!TLDR]
> At the Boyle temperature, the leading virial correction is zero.

The **Boyle temperature** satisfies ${\mechanical{B}}_2({\thermal{T}}_B)=0$. The gas then obeys the ideal-gas law to first order in density, although higher virial terms can remain.

For the van der Waals model,

$$
{\thermal{T}}_B=\frac{{\energy{a}}}{{\thermal{k}}_B{\mechanical{b}}}=\frac{27}{8}{\thermal{T}}_c=3.375{\thermal{T}}_c.\tag{9.13}
$$

Above this temperature, ${\mechanical{B}}_2>0$ and repulsion dominates the leading correction. Below it, ${\mechanical{B}}_2<0$ and attraction dominates. Relative to the ideal-gas value, the pressure at fixed density and temperature is respectively higher or lower.

At this temperature the initial density slope of the compressibility factor is zero. This is the operational meaning of the cancellation. In the van der Waals model, the next term is the positive covolume-squared contribution, so a gas at the Boyle temperature can still have pressure above the ideal value at finite density. The cancellation is exact for the second coefficient, not for the full equation of state.

The ratio to critical temperature in Eq. (9.13) uses the same van der Waals parameters for both temperatures. It is therefore a model relation. Do not combine a measured critical temperature and an independently fitted coefficient and assume that their ratio must obey it. Also keep Boyle cancellation separate from Joule–Thomson inversion: the latter includes a temperature derivative of the coefficient.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\mechanical{B}}_2$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Second virial coefficient | Coefficient of number density in the compressibility-factor expansion. |
| ${\thermal{T}}_B$ | $\unit{K}$ | Boyle temperature | Temperature at which the second virial coefficient is zero. |
| ${\thermal{T}}$ | $\unit{K}$ | Temperature | Absolute temperature of the gas. |
| ${\energy{a}}$ | $\unit{J\,m^3}$ | Attraction parameter | Molecular van der Waals parameter for the attractive interaction. |
| ${\mechanical{b}}$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Excluded-volume parameter | Molecular van der Waals covolume per particle. |
| ${\thermal{k}}_B$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Boltzmann constant | Converts temperature to energy per particle. |
| ${\thermal{T}}_c$ | $\unit{K}$ | Critical temperature | Temperature at the liquid–vapor critical point. |
