**Fig. 9.4** First Mayer graphs. A single edge contributes to the second virial coefficient. The two disconnected bonds factor into separate position integrals. The chain is connected and enters the three-particle cluster integral, but its contribution cancels when the third virial coefficient is formed. The triangle remains.

> [!note] Source clarification
> Connected graphs contribute to cluster integrals. For a pair potential, the density virial coefficients retain graphs that remain connected when any single node is removed. The chain is connected but does not satisfy this stronger condition.
