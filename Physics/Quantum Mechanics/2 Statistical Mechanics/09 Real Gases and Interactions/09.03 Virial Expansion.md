> [!TLDR]
> The virial expansion expresses departures from ideal-gas behavior as powers of number density.

The **virial expansion** is an expansion of the equation of state in the number density ${\mechanical{n}}={\mechanical{N}}/{\mechanical{V}}$:

$$
{\mechanical{Z}}=\frac{{\mechanical{P}}{\mechanical{V}}}{{\mechanical{N}}{\thermal{k}}_B{\thermal{T}}}
=1+\frac{{\mechanical{B}}_2({\thermal{T}})}{{\mechanical{V}}/{\mechanical{N}}}+\frac{{\mechanical{B}}_3({\thermal{T}})}{({\mechanical{V}}/{\mechanical{N}})^2}+\cdots
=1+\sum_{k=2}^{\infty}{\mechanical{B}}_k({\thermal{T}})\left(\frac {\mechanical{N}}{\mechanical{V}}\right)^{{\thermal{k}}-1}.\tag{9.9}
$$

The coefficients describe the effects of molecular interactions. A coefficient has the dimensions needed to make its term dimensionless.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\mechanical{Z}}$ | $\unit{1}$ | Compressibility factor | Pressure times volume divided by ideal-gas thermal energy. |
| ${\mechanical{P}}$ | $\unit{Pa}$ | Pressure | Mechanical pressure of the gas. |
| ${\mechanical{V}}$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Volume | Total volume occupied by the gas. |
| ${\mechanical{N}}$ | $\unit{1}$ | Particle number | Number of molecules. |
| ${\thermal{k}}_B$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Boltzmann constant | Converts temperature to energy per particle. |
| ${\thermal{T}}$ | $\unit{K}$ | Temperature | Absolute temperature of the gas. |
| ${\mechanical{n}}$ | $\unit{m^{-3}}$ | Number density | Particle number divided by volume. |
| ${\mechanical{B}}_2$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Second virial coefficient | Coefficient of number density in the compressibility-factor expansion. |
| ${\mechanical{B}}_3$ | $\unit{m^6}$ | Third virial coefficient | Coefficient of squared number density in the compressibility-factor expansion. |
| ${\mechanical{B}}_k$ | $\unit{m^{3(k-1)}}$ | Virial coefficient | Coefficient multiplying the corresponding power of number density. |
