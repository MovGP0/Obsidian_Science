> [!TLDR]
> The Joule–Thomson coefficient compares thermal expansion with the ideal-gas value.

Define

$$
{\thermal{\mu}}_{JT}=\left(\frac{\partial {\thermal{T}}}{\partial {\mechanical{P}}}\right)_H.
$$

Start with $d{\energy{H}}={\thermal{T}}\,d{\thermal{S}}+{\mechanical{V}}\,d{\mechanical{P}}$. Write entropy as a function of temperature and pressure and use the Maxwell relation:

$$
\left(\frac{\partial {\thermal{S}}}{\partial {\mechanical{P}}}\right)_T
=-\left(\frac{\partial {\mechanical{V}}}{\partial {\thermal{T}}}\right)_P.
$$

This gives

$$
d{\energy{H}}={\thermal{C}}_P\,d{\thermal{T}}+\left[{\mechanical{V}}-{\thermal{T}}\left(\frac{\partial {\mechanical{V}}}{\partial {\thermal{T}}}\right)_P\right]d{\mechanical{P}}.
$$

Set this differential to zero and solve for the temperature derivative:

$$
{\thermal{\mu}}_{JT}=-\frac1{{\thermal{C}}_P}\left(\frac{\partial {\energy{H}}}{\partial {\mechanical{P}}}\right)_T
=\frac1{{\thermal{C}}_P}\left[{\thermal{T}}\left(\frac{\partial {\mechanical{V}}}{\partial {\thermal{T}}}\right)_P-{\mechanical{V}}\right]
=\frac{{\mechanical{V}}}{{\thermal{C}}_P}({\thermal{T}}{\mechanical{\alpha}}_P-1).
$$

> [!note] Correction to the source equation
> A minus sign is required before the pressure derivative of enthalpy. The source omits it in the first expression, although its final expression is correct.

For an ideal gas, ${\mechanical{P}}{\mechanical{V}}={\mechanical{N}}{\thermal{k}}_B{\thermal{T}}$ and ${\thermal{T}}(\partial {\mechanical{V}}/\partial {\thermal{T}})_P={\mechanical{V}}$. Consequently ${\thermal{\mu}}_{JT}=0$: ideal-gas throttling leaves the temperature unchanged.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\thermal{\mu}}_{JT}$ | $\unit{K\,Pa^{-1}}$ | Joule–Thomson coefficient | Temperature derivative with respect to pressure at constant enthalpy. |
| ${\thermal{T}}$ | $\unit{K}$ | Temperature | Absolute temperature of the gas. |
| ${\mechanical{P}}$ | $\unit{Pa}$ | Pressure | Mechanical pressure of the gas. |
| ${\energy{H}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Enthalpy | Internal energy plus pressure times volume. |
| ${\thermal{S}}$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Entropy | Thermodynamic entropy of the gas. |
| ${\mechanical{V}}$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Volume | Total volume occupied by the gas. |
| ${\thermal{C}}_P$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Heat capacity at constant pressure | Total heat capacity of the stated amount of gas. |
| ${\mechanical{\alpha}}_P$ | $\unit{K^{-1}}$ | Thermal expansion coefficient | Relative volume derivative with respect to temperature at fixed pressure. |
| ${\mechanical{N}}$ | $\unit{1}$ | Particle number | Number of molecules. |
| ${\thermal{k}}_B$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Boltzmann constant | Converts temperature to energy per particle. |
