> [!TLDR]
> The dilute van der Waals gas changes from cooling to heating at twice its Boyle temperature.

At inversion, the thermal expansion satisfies

$$
{\thermal{T}}\left(\frac{\partial {\mechanical{V}}}{\partial {\thermal{T}}}\right)_P={\mechanical{V}}.
$$

In the dilute van der Waals approximation,

$$
{\thermal{T}}_{\mathrm{inv}}=\frac{2{\energy{a}}}{{\mechanical{b}}{\thermal{k}}_B}=2{\thermal{T}}_B=\frac{27}{4}{\thermal{T}}_c.\tag{9.15}
$$

Below this inversion temperature, ${\thermal{\mu}}_{JT}>0$ and a pressure decrease cools the gas. Above it, ${\thermal{\mu}}_{JT}<0$ and a pressure decrease heats the gas.

> [!note] Range of Eq. (9.15)
> This is the upper inversion temperature in the zero-pressure limit of the model. At finite pressure, inversion occurs on a curve. The exact van der Waals condition, in volume per molecule, is
> $$
> {\thermal{T}}_{\mathrm{inv}}({\mechanical{v}})=\frac{2{\energy{a}}}{{\thermal{k}}_B{\mechanical{b}}}\left(1-\frac {\mechanical{b}}{\mechanical{v}}\right)^2.
> $$
> Thus Eq. (9.15) must not be used as the full finite-pressure inversion boundary.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\thermal{T}}$ | $\unit{K}$ | Temperature | Absolute temperature of the gas. |
| ${\mechanical{V}}$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Volume | Total volume occupied by the gas. |
| ${\mechanical{P}}$ | $\unit{Pa}$ | Pressure | Mechanical pressure of the gas. |
| ${\thermal{T}}_{\mathrm{inv}}$ | $\unit{K}$ | Inversion temperature | Temperature at which the Joule–Thomson coefficient changes sign. |
| ${\energy{a}}$ | $\unit{J\,m^3}$ | Attraction parameter | Molecular van der Waals parameter for the attractive interaction. |
| ${\mechanical{b}}$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Excluded-volume parameter | Molecular van der Waals covolume per particle. |
| ${\thermal{k}}_B$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Boltzmann constant | Converts temperature to energy per particle. |
| ${\thermal{T}}_B$ | $\unit{K}$ | Boyle temperature | Temperature at which the second virial coefficient is zero. |
| ${\thermal{T}}_c$ | $\unit{K}$ | Critical temperature | Temperature at the liquid–vapor critical point. |
| ${\thermal{\mu}}_{JT}$ | $\unit{K\,Pa^{-1}}$ | Joule–Thomson coefficient | Temperature derivative with respect to pressure at constant enthalpy. |
| ${\mechanical{v}}$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Volume per particle | Total volume divided by particle number. |
