> [!TLDR]
> Maxwell relations connect derivatives of measurable state variables with entropy derivatives. This section derives the four relations and applies them to internal pressure, heat capacities, and throttling.

The practical question is how to obtain an entropy response from pressure, volume, and temperature data. Start by identifying a potential whose natural variables match the desired derivatives. Equality of its mixed derivatives then replaces an inaccessible entropy derivative with a measurable mechanical or thermal response.

Read the derivation before the applications so that the signs and held-fixed variables remain traceable. The relations require a smooth equilibrium description. They do not supply an equation of state, and a phase boundary must be treated with its own coexistence conditions rather than by differentiating across a jump.
