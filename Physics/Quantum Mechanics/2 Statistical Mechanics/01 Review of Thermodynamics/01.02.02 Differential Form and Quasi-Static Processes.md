> [!TLDR]
> The differential first law distinguishes exact changes in internal energy from heat and work transfers. In a reversible pressure–volume process, entropy and volume changes determine the internal-energy change.

For an infinitesimal process, the first law is:

$$
\mathrm d\energy{U}=\delta\thermal{Q}-\delta\energy{W}.
$$

The notation $\mathrm d\energy{U}$ denotes an exact differential of a state function. The symbols $\delta\thermal{Q}$ and $\delta\energy{W}$ denote elementary heat and work transfers. Their differential forms are generally not exact.

For a pressure–volume–temperature system whose only work is pressure–volume work, the elementary work performed by the system is:

$$
\delta\energy{W}=\mechanical{P}\,\mathrm d\mechanical{V}.
$$

This expression applies to quasi-static processes. Such processes are sufficiently slow to be described as a continuous sequence of equilibrium states.

## Definition 1.5 — Quasi-static, reversible, and irreversible processes

A process is **quasi-static** if it passes through a sequence of equilibrium states. It is **reversible** if it is quasi-static and produces no entropy. Mathematically:

$$
\delta\thermal{Q}_{\mathrm{rev}}=\thermal{T}\,\mathrm d\thermal{S}.
$$

A process is **irreversible** if it produces entropy. Spontaneous processes in an isolated system are irreversible.

For a quasi-static reversible process, the first law becomes:

$$
\mathrm d\energy{U}=\thermal{T}\,\mathrm d\thermal{S}
-\mechanical{P}\,\mathrm d\mechanical{V}. \tag{1.2}
$$

Here, $\thermal{T}\,\mathrm d\thermal{S}$ is heat absorbed reversibly and $\mechanical{P}\,\mathrm d\mechanical{V}$ is work performed by the system. This fundamental expression applies to a closed system with only pressure–volume work.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\energy{U}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Internal energy | State function whose differential is exact. |
| $\thermal{Q},\thermal{Q}_{\mathrm{rev}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Heat | General and reversible heat transfer into the system. |
| $\energy{W}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Work | Pressure–volume work performed by the system. |
| $\mechanical{P}$ | $\unit{Pa}$ | Pressure | Equilibrium pressure during a quasi-static process. |
| $\mechanical{V}$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Volume | Variable changed by expansion or compression. |
| $\thermal{T}$ | $\unit{K}$ | Temperature | Absolute temperature during reversible heat transfer. |
| $\thermal{S}$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Entropy | State function whose change determines reversible heat transfer. |
