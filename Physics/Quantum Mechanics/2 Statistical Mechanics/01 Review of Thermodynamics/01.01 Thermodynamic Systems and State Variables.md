> [!TLDR]
> This section defines thermodynamic systems, equilibrium states, state variables, and equations of state.

Before assigning a temperature or pressure, decide what is included in the system and whether it has reached equilibrium. A sealed cylinder can exchange energy while its particle number stays fixed. If a valve opens, the same region becomes an open system and needs a different energy account.

Read the three notes in order: classify the boundary, identify independent state variables, then impose an equation of state. As a check, ask whether a stationary fluid with a sustained heat current is in equilibrium. Its properties can be constant in time, but the current distinguishes a steady state from thermodynamic equilibrium.
