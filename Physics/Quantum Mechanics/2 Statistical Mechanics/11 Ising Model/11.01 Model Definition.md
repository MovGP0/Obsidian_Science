> [!TLDR]
> Each lattice site carries a binary spin that interacts with nearby spins and an applied field.

The model uses a regular lattice with discrete spin variables. The lattice geometry sets the number of nearest neighbours. Without an applied field, global spin inversion leaves the energy unchanged.
