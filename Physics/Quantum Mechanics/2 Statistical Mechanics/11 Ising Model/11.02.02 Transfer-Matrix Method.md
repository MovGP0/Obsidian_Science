> [!TLDR]
> The partition function of a periodic chain is the trace of a power of its transfer matrix.

**Definition 11.2 (Transfer matrix).** In the row and column order $(+1,-1)$, the transfer matrix is

$$
\mathbf T=
\begin{pmatrix}
T(+,+)&T(+,-)\\
T(-,+)&T(-,-)
\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
e^{\thermal{\beta}(\energy{J}+\energy{h})}&e^{-\thermal{\beta}\energy{J}}\\
e^{-\thermal{\beta}\energy{J}}&e^{\thermal{\beta}(\energy{J}-\energy{h})}
\end{pmatrix}.
\tag{11.2}
$$

For periodic boundaries,

$$
\mathcal Z=\operatorname{Tr}(\mathbf T^N)=\lambda_+^N+\lambda_-^N,
$$

where $\lambda_\pm$ are the eigenvalues of $\mathbf T$.

## Calculation of the eigenvalues

The matrix is real and symmetric, so its eigenvalues are real. Its characteristic equation is

$$
\det(\mathbf T-\lambda\mathbf I)=
\left(e^{\thermal{\beta}(\energy{J}+\energy{h})}-\lambda\right)
\left(e^{\thermal{\beta}(\energy{J}-\energy{h})}-\lambda\right)
-e^{-2\thermal{\beta}\energy{J}}=0.
$$

Expansion gives

$$
\lambda^2-\lambda
\left(e^{\thermal{\beta}(\energy{J}+\energy{h})}
+e^{\thermal{\beta}(\energy{J}-\energy{h})}\right)
+e^{2\thermal{\beta}\energy{J}}-e^{-2\thermal{\beta}\energy{J}}=0.
$$

Use the identities

$$
\begin{aligned}
e^{\thermal{\beta}(\energy{J}+\energy{h})}
+e^{\thermal{\beta}(\energy{J}-\energy{h})}
&=2e^{\thermal{\beta}\energy{J}}\cosh(\thermal{\beta}\energy{h}),\\
e^{2\thermal{\beta}\energy{J}}-e^{-2\thermal{\beta}\energy{J}}
&=2\sinh(2\thermal{\beta}\energy{J}).
\end{aligned}
$$

The quadratic formula gives

$$
\lambda_\pm=e^{\thermal{\beta}\energy{J}}\cosh(\thermal{\beta}\energy{h})
\pm\sqrt{e^{2\thermal{\beta}\energy{J}}\sinh^2(\thermal{\beta}\energy{h})
+e^{-2\thermal{\beta}\energy{J}}}.
\tag{11.3}
$$

For $\energy{J}>0$ and $\thermal{T}>0$, $\lambda_+>\lambda_->0$. For $\energy{J}<0$, $\lambda_-<0$, but $|\lambda_-|<\lambda_+$ still holds.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $N$ | $1$ | Spin count | The number of lattice sites or spins. |
| $\magnetic{\sigma}_i$ | $1$ | Ising spin | The normalized spin at site i. |
| $\energy{J}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Exchange coupling | The energy scale of a nearest-neighbour interaction. |
| $\energy{h}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Field parameter | The applied field multiplied by the spin magnetic moment. |
| $\thermal{\beta}$ | $\unit{J^{-1}}$ | Inverse temperature | The inverse of the thermal energy. |
| $\thermal{T}$ | $\unit{K}$ | Temperature | The absolute temperature; a c subscript marks a critical value. |
| $\mathcal Z$ | $1$ | Partition function | The sum of Boltzmann weights of the configurations. |
