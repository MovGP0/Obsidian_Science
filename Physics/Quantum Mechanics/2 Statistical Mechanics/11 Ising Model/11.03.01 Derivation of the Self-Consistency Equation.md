> [!TLDR]
> Neglect of products of spin fluctuations reduces the model to independent spins in an effective field.

Assume a homogeneous phase with the same mean spin at each site:

$$
\langle\magnetic{\sigma}_i\rangle=\magnetic{m}.
$$

This is the assumption of translation invariance. Write each neighbouring spin as

$$
\magnetic{\sigma}_j=\langle\magnetic{\sigma}_j\rangle+\delta\magnetic{\sigma}_j
=\magnetic{m}+\delta\magnetic{\sigma}_j,
\qquad
\delta\magnetic{\sigma}_j=\magnetic{\sigma}_j-\magnetic{m}.
$$

Substitution gives

$$
\magnetic{\sigma}_i\magnetic{\sigma}_j
=\magnetic{m}\magnetic{\sigma}_i+\magnetic{\sigma}_i\delta\magnetic{\sigma}_j.
$$

The mean-field approximation neglects the product of fluctuations $\delta\magnetic{\sigma}_i\delta\magnetic{\sigma}_j$. Thus

$$
\magnetic{\sigma}_i\magnetic{\sigma}_j
\approx\magnetic{m}\magnetic{\sigma}_i+\magnetic{m}\magnetic{\sigma}_j-\magnetic{m}^2.
$$

The resulting Hamiltonian is

$$
\begin{aligned}
\energy{\mathcal H}_{\mathrm{MF}}
&=-\energy{J}\sum_{\langle i,j\rangle}
(\magnetic{m}\magnetic{\sigma}_i+\magnetic{m}\magnetic{\sigma}_j-\magnetic{m}^2)
-\energy{h}\sum_i\magnetic{\sigma}_i\\
&=\frac{Nz\energy{J}\magnetic{m}^2}{2}
-(z\energy{J}\magnetic{m}+\energy{h})\sum_i\magnetic{\sigma}_i.
\end{aligned}
$$

The **effective field**, or Weiss field, is

$$
\energy{h}_{\mathrm{eff}}=z\energy{J}\magnetic{m}+\energy{h}.
$$

The system now contains $N$ independent spins in the effective field. Its partition function is

$$
\mathcal Z^{\mathrm{MF}}=
e^{-\thermal{\beta}Nz\energy{J}\magnetic{m}^2/2}
\left[2\cosh(\thermal{\beta}\energy{h}_{\mathrm{eff}})\right]^N.
$$

The requirement $\magnetic{m}=\langle\magnetic{\sigma}_i\rangle_{\mathrm{MF}}$ gives

$$
\magnetic{m}=\tanh\!\left[\thermal{\beta}
(z\energy{J}\magnetic{m}+\energy{h})\right].
\tag{11.5}
$$

This is the **mean-field self-consistency equation**, also called the Weiss equation.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\magnetic{\sigma}_i$ | $1$ | Ising spin | The normalized spin at site i. |
| $\magnetic{m}$ | $1$ | Magnetization per spin | The mean normalized spin; subscripts identify particular or spontaneous values. |
| $N$ | $1$ | Spin count | The number of lattice sites or spins. |
| $z$ | $1$ | Coordination number | The number of nearest neighbours at a lattice site. |
| $\energy{\mathcal H}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Hamiltonian | The energy of a spin configuration. |
| $\energy{J}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Exchange coupling | The energy scale of a nearest-neighbour interaction. |
| $\energy{h}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Field parameter | The applied field multiplied by the spin magnetic moment. |
| $\energy{h}_{\mathrm{eff}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Effective field parameter | The mean field and applied field in energy units. |
| $\thermal{\beta}$ | $\unit{J^{-1}}$ | Inverse temperature | The inverse of the thermal energy. |
| $\mathcal Z$ | $1$ | Partition function | The sum of Boltzmann weights of the configurations. |
