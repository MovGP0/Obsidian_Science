> [!TLDR]
> A reduced partition sum for a chain satisfies a recursion with a two-spin transfer weight.

Consider a chain of $N$ spins with periodic boundary conditions, $\magnetic{\sigma}_{N+1}=\magnetic{\sigma}_1$. The Hamiltonian is

$$
\energy{\mathcal H}=-\energy{J}\sum_{i=1}^{N}\magnetic{\sigma}_i\magnetic{\sigma}_{i+1}
-\energy{h}\sum_{i=1}^{N}\magnetic{\sigma}_i.
$$

The canonical partition function is

$$
\mathcal Z=\sum_{\{\sigma_i\}}e^{-\thermal{\beta}\energy{\mathcal H}}
=\sum_{\sigma_1=\pm1}\cdots\sum_{\sigma_N=\pm1}
\prod_{i=1}^{N}
e^{\thermal{\beta}\energy{J}\magnetic{\sigma}_i\magnetic{\sigma}_{i+1}
+\frac{\thermal{\beta}\energy{h}}{2}(\magnetic{\sigma}_i+\magnetic{\sigma}_{i+1})},
\qquad
\thermal{\beta}=\frac{1}{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}\thermal{T}}.
$$

Define a reduced partition sum for an open segment with its end spin fixed at $\magnetic{\sigma}_n=s$:

$$
Z_n(s)=
\sum_{\sigma_1,\ldots,\sigma_{n-1}}
\prod_{i=1}^{n-1}
e^{\thermal{\beta}\energy{J}\magnetic{\sigma}_i\magnetic{\sigma}_{i+1}
+\frac{\thermal{\beta}\energy{h}}{2}(\magnetic{\sigma}_i+\magnetic{\sigma}_{i+1})}.
$$

It satisfies the recursion

$$
Z_{n+1}(s)=\sum_{s'=\pm1}T(s',s)\,Z_n(s'),
$$

where the transfer weight is

$$
T(s',s)=e^{\thermal{\beta}\energy{J}s's+\frac{\thermal{\beta}\energy{h}}{2}(s'+s)}.
$$

The product structure leads to the $2\times2$ matrix method in [[11.02.02 Transfer-Matrix Method]].

> [!note] Boundary weights
> The reduced sum above uses half of the field energy at each open end. It is a recursion weight, rather than the full open-chain partition function with complete endpoint field terms. Closing the chain gives the periodic partition function.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $N$ | $1$ | Spin count | The number of lattice sites or spins. |
| $\magnetic{\sigma}_i$ | $1$ | Ising spin | The normalized spin at site i. |
| $\energy{\mathcal H}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Hamiltonian | The energy of a spin configuration. |
| $\energy{J}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Exchange coupling | The energy scale of a nearest-neighbour interaction. |
| $\energy{h}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Field parameter | The applied field multiplied by the spin magnetic moment. |
| $\mathcal Z$ | $1$ | Partition function | The sum of Boltzmann weights of the configurations. |
| $\thermal{\beta}$ | $\unit{J^{-1}}$ | Inverse temperature | The inverse of the thermal energy. |
| $\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Boltzmann constant | The constant that converts temperature to energy. |
| $\thermal{T}$ | $\unit{K}$ | Temperature | The absolute temperature; a c subscript marks a critical value. |
