> [!TLDR]
> The zero-field self-consistency equation develops two stable magnetized solutions below the mean-field critical temperature.

## Zero external field

For $\energy{h}=0$, Equation (11.5) becomes

$$
\magnetic{m}=\tanh(\thermal{\beta}z\energy{J}\magnetic{m}).
$$

The solutions are the intersections of $y=\magnetic{m}$ and $y=\tanh(\thermal{K}\magnetic{m})$, with $\thermal{K}=\thermal{\beta}z\energy{J}$. The slope of the hyperbolic tangent at the origin is $\thermal{K}$.

- If $\thermal{K}<1$ (high temperature), the only solution is $\magnetic{m}=0$: the **paramagnetic phase**.
- If $\thermal{K}>1$ (low temperature), there are three solutions. The solution $\magnetic{m}=0$ is unstable; the solutions $\magnetic{m}=\pm\magnetic{m}_0$, with $\magnetic{m}_0>0$, are stable: the **ferromagnetic phase**.
- The transition occurs at the **mean-field critical temperature**:

$$
\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}\thermal{T}_{\mathrm c}=z\energy{J}.
$$

## Spontaneous magnetization near the critical temperature

For $\thermal{T}\lesssim\thermal{T}_{\mathrm c}$ at zero field, expand $\tanh x=x-x^3/3+\cdots$:

$$
\magnetic{m}
=\tanh\!\left(\frac{\thermal{T}_{\mathrm c}}{\thermal{T}}\magnetic{m}\right)
\approx
\frac{\thermal{T}_{\mathrm c}}{\thermal{T}}\magnetic{m}
-\frac13\left(\frac{\thermal{T}_{\mathrm c}}{\thermal{T}}\right)^3\magnetic{m}^3.
$$

For $\magnetic{m}\ne0$, divide by $\magnetic{m}$:

$$
1=\frac{\thermal{T}_{\mathrm c}}{\thermal{T}}
-\frac13\left(\frac{\thermal{T}_{\mathrm c}}{\thermal{T}}\right)^3\magnetic{m}^2.
$$

Thus

$$
\magnetic{m}^2=
\frac{3(\thermal{T}_{\mathrm c}/\thermal{T}-1)}
{(\thermal{T}_{\mathrm c}/\thermal{T})^3}
\approx3\left(1-\frac{\thermal{T}}{\thermal{T}_{\mathrm c}}\right).
$$

The spontaneous magnetization has the critical behaviour

$$
\magnetic{m}\sim
\left(1-\frac{\thermal{T}}{\thermal{T}_{\mathrm c}}\right)^{\beta_{\mathrm c}},
\qquad
\beta_{\mathrm c}=\frac12.
\tag{11.6}
$$

The exponent $\beta_{\mathrm c}$ is distinct from the inverse temperature $\thermal{\beta}$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\magnetic{m}$ | $1$ | Magnetization per spin | The mean normalized spin; subscripts identify particular or spontaneous values. |
| $\energy{h}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Field parameter | The applied field multiplied by the spin magnetic moment. |
| $\thermal{\beta}$ | $\unit{J^{-1}}$ | Inverse temperature | The inverse of the thermal energy. |
| $\thermal{K}$ | $1$ | Reduced coupling | The dimensionless coupling defined in this article; star and c mark dual and critical values. |
| $z$ | $1$ | Coordination number | The number of nearest neighbours at a lattice site. |
| $\energy{J}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Exchange coupling | The energy scale of a nearest-neighbour interaction. |
| $\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Boltzmann constant | The constant that converts temperature to energy. |
| $\thermal{T}$ | $\unit{K}$ | Temperature | The absolute temperature; a c subscript marks a critical value. |
