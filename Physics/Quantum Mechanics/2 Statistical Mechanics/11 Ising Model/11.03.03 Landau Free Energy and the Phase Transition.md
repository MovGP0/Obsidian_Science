> [!TLDR]
> The mean-field free energy changes from one minimum to two equal minima at the critical temperature.

The mean-field free energy per spin can be written as a function of the trial magnetization:

$$
\energy{f}(\magnetic{m},\thermal{T})
=\frac{z\energy{J}\magnetic{m}^2}{2}
-\frac{1}{\thermal{\beta}}
\ln\!\left[2\cosh(\thermal{\beta}\energy{h}_{\mathrm{eff}})\right].
$$

At $\energy{h}=0$, expand in powers of $\magnetic{m}$:

$$
\energy{f}(\magnetic{m},\thermal{T})
=\energy{f}_0(\thermal{T})
+\energy{a}(\thermal{T})\magnetic{m}^2
+\energy{b}(\thermal{T})\magnetic{m}^4
+O(\magnetic{m}^6),
$$

where

$$
\energy{a}(\thermal{T})
=\frac{z\energy{J}}{2}
\left(1-\frac{z\energy{J}}{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}\thermal{T}}\right)
=\frac{z\energy{J}}{2}
\left(1-\frac{\thermal{T}_{\mathrm c}}{\thermal{T}}\right),
\qquad
\energy{b}(\thermal{T})
=\frac{(z\energy{J})^4}{12(\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}\thermal{T})^3}>0.
$$

- For $\thermal{T}>\thermal{T}_{\mathrm c}$, $\energy{a}>0$. The minimum is at $\magnetic{m}=0$: a single well.
- For $\thermal{T}<\thermal{T}_{\mathrm c}$, $\energy{a}<0$. Two equal minima occur at nonzero magnetization: a double well.
- At $\thermal{T}=\thermal{T}_{\mathrm c}$, $\energy{a}=0$. The quadratic coefficient changes sign: the Landau bifurcation.

**Fig. 11.4** The Landau free energy $\energy{f}-\energy{f}_0=\energy{a}\magnetic{m}^2+\energy{b}\magnetic{m}^4$ in mean-field theory. Above the critical temperature there is one minimum. Below it there are two equal minima.

> [!note] Source correction: the critical minimum
> The source calls the point at $\thermal{T}_{\mathrm c}$ a “flat inflection point”. With a positive quartic coefficient it is a flat minimum. The curvature is zero at the minimum, but it does not change sign across $\magnetic{m}=0$. The figure label uses the corrected term.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\energy{f}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Free energy per spin | The Helmholtz free energy per spin; subscript 0 marks a reference value. |
| $\magnetic{m}$ | $1$ | Magnetization per spin | The mean normalized spin; subscripts identify particular or spontaneous values. |
| $\thermal{T}$ | $\unit{K}$ | Temperature | The absolute temperature; a c subscript marks a critical value. |
| $z$ | $1$ | Coordination number | The number of nearest neighbours at a lattice site. |
| $\energy{J}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Exchange coupling | The energy scale of a nearest-neighbour interaction. |
| $\thermal{\beta}$ | $\unit{J^{-1}}$ | Inverse temperature | The inverse of the thermal energy. |
| $\energy{h}_{\mathrm{eff}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Effective field parameter | The mean field and applied field in energy units. |
| $\energy{h}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Field parameter | The applied field multiplied by the spin magnetic moment. |
| $\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Boltzmann constant | The constant that converts temperature to energy. |
| $\energy{a},\ \energy{b}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Landau coefficients | The quadratic and quartic coefficients for dimensionless magnetization. |
