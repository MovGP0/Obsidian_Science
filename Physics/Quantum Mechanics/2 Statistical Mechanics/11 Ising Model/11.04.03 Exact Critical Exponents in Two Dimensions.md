> [!TLDR]
> The exact two-dimensional critical exponents differ from their mean-field values.

Onsager's solution and later analyses, including Yang (1952) and McCoy–Wu (1973), determine the critical exponents of the two-dimensional Ising model.

| Exponent | Definition | Ising 2D (exact) | Mean field |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\alpha$ | $\thermal{C}\sim\lvert\thermal{t}\rvert^{-\alpha}$ | $0$ (logarithmic) | $0$ (jump) |
| $\beta$ | $\magnetic{m}\sim(-\thermal{t})^\beta$ | $1/8$ | $1/2$ |
| $\gamma$ | $\magnetic{\chi}\sim\lvert\thermal{t}\rvert^{-\gamma}$ | $7/4$ | $1$ |
| $\delta$ | $\magnetic{m}\sim\energy{h}^{1/\delta}$ at $\thermal{T}_{\mathrm c}$ | $15$ | $3$ |
| $\nu$ | $\xi\sim\lvert\thermal{t}\rvert^{-\nu}$ | $1$ | $1/2$ |
| $\eta$ | $G(r)\sim r^{-(d-2+\eta)}$ at criticality | $1/4$ | $0$ |

The **reduced temperature** is

$$
\thermal{t}=\frac{\thermal{T}-\thermal{T}_{\mathrm c}}{\thermal{T}_{\mathrm c}}.
$$

The symbols $\alpha,\beta,\gamma,\delta,\nu,\eta$ denote critical exponents. In particular, the exponent $\beta$ is distinct from inverse temperature.

> [!note] Remark 11.4 (Logarithmic heat-capacity divergence)
> Near the critical temperature, the heat capacity diverges as
>
> $$
> \thermal{C}\sim-\thermal{A}\ln\left|1-\frac{\thermal{T}}{\thermal{T}_{\mathrm c}}\right|+\thermal{B}.
> $$
>
> This is the logarithmic case $\alpha=0$. It differs from the finite mean-field jump.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\thermal{C},\ \Delta\thermal{C}$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Heat capacity per spin | The heat capacity and its jump at the transition. |
| $\thermal{t}$ | $1$ | Reduced temperature | The temperature difference from the critical point, divided by that temperature. |
| $\magnetic{m}$ | $1$ | Magnetization per spin | The mean normalized spin; subscripts identify particular or spontaneous values. |
| $\magnetic{\chi}$ | $\unit{J^{-1}}$ | Susceptibility per spin | The derivative of magnetization with respect to the field energy. |
| $\energy{h}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Field parameter | The applied field multiplied by the spin magnetic moment. |
| $\thermal{T}$ | $\unit{K}$ | Temperature | The absolute temperature; a c subscript marks a critical value. |
| $\xi$ | $1$ | Correlation length | The correlation range, measured in lattice spacings. |
| $G(r)$ | $1$ | Spin correlation | The connected correlation of normalized spins. |
| $r$ | $1$ | Lattice separation | The distance between sites, measured in lattice spacings. |
| $d,\ d_{\mathrm c}$ | $1$ | Spatial dimension | The lattice dimension and its upper critical value. |
| $\thermal{A},\ \thermal{B}$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Heat-capacity amplitudes | The logarithmic amplitude and regular background. |
