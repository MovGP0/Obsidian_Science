> [!TLDR]
> Maximizing Gibbs entropy gives a uniform microcanonical distribution or a Boltzmann distribution, depending on the constraints.

The **maximum-entropy principle**, associated with Jaynes, selects the equilibrium probability distribution by maximizing Gibbs entropy under specified physical constraints.

## Microcanonical ensemble

Only states with the fixed allowed energy ${\energy{E}}$ are included. The remaining constraint is normalization:

$$
\sum_i p_i=1.
$$

Introduce a Lagrange multiplier $\lambda$:

$$
\delta\left[
-{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}\sum_i p_i\ln p_i
-\lambda\left(\sum_i p_i-1\right)
\right]=0.
$$

Differentiate with respect to each probability:

$$
-{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}(\ln p_i+1)-\lambda=0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad p_i=\text{constant}.
$$

Normalization then gives $p_i=1/\Omega$. Equal probabilities maximize entropy when normalization is the only remaining constraint.

## Canonical ensemble

Now fix normalization and mean energy:

$$
\sum_i p_i=1,
\qquad
\sum_i p_i{\energy{E}}_i=\langle {\energy{E}}\rangle={\energy{U}}.
$$

Introduce two multipliers, $\lambda$ and $\beta$:

$$
\delta\left[
-{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}\sum_i p_i\ln p_i
-\lambda\left(\sum_i p_i-1\right)
-{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}\beta\left(\sum_i p_i{\energy{E}}_i-{\energy{U}}\right)
\right]=0.
$$

The stationary equation is:

$$
-{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}(\ln p_i+1)-\lambda-{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}\beta {\energy{E}}_i=0.
$$

Therefore:

$$
p_i\propto e^{-\beta {\energy{E}}_i},
\qquad
\beta=\frac1{{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}{\thermal{T}}}.
$$

This is the **Boltzmann distribution**. Normalization gives $p_i=e^{-\beta {\energy{E}}_i}/Z$, where $Z=\sum_i e^{-\beta {\energy{E}}_i}$.

> [!note] Logical status
> The source calls the uniform result a derivation of the fundamental postulate. It is a derivation **conditional on the maximum-entropy principle and the chosen allowed states**, rather than a proof from mechanical equations alone. The entropy-maximization construction is also given in [David Tong's statistical physics notes](https://www.damtp.cam.ac.uk/user/tong/statphys/statmechhtml/S1.html).

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\thermal{S}}$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Entropy | Boltzmann or Gibbs entropy, as specified by the context or subscript. |
| ${\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Boltzmann constant | The conversion factor between microscopic counting and thermodynamic entropy. |
| ${\energy{E}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Energy | The system or level energy; ΔE is an energy interval. |
| ${\energy{U}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Internal energy | The thermodynamic internal energy or ensemble mean energy. |
| ${\thermal{T}}$ | $\unit{K}$ | Temperature | The thermodynamic temperature. |
| $\beta$ | $\unit{J^{-1}}$ | Inverse thermal energy | The canonical parameter 1/(kᴮ T). |
| $p_i$ | $1$ | Microstate probability | The probability assigned to accessible microstate i. |
