> [!TLDR]
> Liouville's theorem states that phase-space density is constant along Hamiltonian trajectories.

Liouville's theorem is a central result of analytical mechanics for statistical mechanics. In simple terms, the density of an ensemble of phase-space points is conserved as those points move along Hamiltonian trajectories.
