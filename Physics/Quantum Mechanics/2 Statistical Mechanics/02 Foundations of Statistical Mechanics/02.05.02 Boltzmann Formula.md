> [!TLDR]
> Entropy is proportional to the logarithm of the number of accessible microstates.

## Definition 2.7 — Boltzmann entropy

$$
{\thermal{S}}={\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}\ln\Omega.
\tag{2.2}
$$

The formula is engraved on Boltzmann's grave in Vienna's Central Cemetery. It relates entropy to the number of microscopic configurations compatible with a macrostate. A larger count gives a larger entropy. In this sense, the source uses the word *disorder*.

## Remark 2.4 — Why the logarithm?

Two requirements explain the form of the formula.

1. **Additivity.** For two independent systems, the numbers of states multiply:

   $$
   \Omega_{AB}=\Omega_A\Omega_B.
   $$

   Their entropies must add:

   $$
   {\thermal{S}}_{AB}={\thermal{S}}_A+{\thermal{S}}_B.
   $$

   A logarithm converts a product into a sum.

2. **Units.** Boltzmann's constant, approximately ${\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}=1.380\times10^{-23}\,\unit{J/K}$ at the precision used in the source, gives entropy its unit of joules per kelvin. It connects the microscopic count with thermodynamic entropy.
