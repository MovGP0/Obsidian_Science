> [!TLDR]
> Gibbs entropy equals Boltzmann entropy when all accessible microstates have equal probability.

If all $\Omega$ microstates are equally likely, then $p_i=1/\Omega$. Substitute this value into the Gibbs formula:

$$
\begin{aligned}
{\thermal{S}}_G
&=-{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}\sum_{i=1}^{\Omega}\frac1\Omega\ln\frac1\Omega\\
&=-{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}\,\Omega\,\frac1\Omega(-\ln\Omega)\\
&={\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}\ln\Omega={\thermal{S}}.
\end{aligned}
$$

The two definitions agree exactly in this case.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\thermal{S}}$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Entropy | Boltzmann or Gibbs entropy, as specified by the context or subscript. |
| ${\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Boltzmann constant | The conversion factor between microscopic counting and thermodynamic entropy. |
| $p_i$ | $1$ | Microstate probability | The probability assigned to accessible microstate i. |
