> [!TLDR]
> A bounded spin system has negative temperature when its higher-energy level has the larger population.

## Example 2.3 — Spins in a magnetic field

Consider ${\mechanical{N}}$ distinguishable spin-½ degrees of freedom in a magnetic field ${\magnetic{B}}$. The two energy values are:

$$
{\energy{\varepsilon}}_k=\pm{\magnetic{\mu}_{\mathrm B}}{\magnetic{B}}.
$$

Use the source's convention: the level called “+” has energy $+{\magnetic{\mu}_{\mathrm B}}{\magnetic{B}}$, and the “−” level has energy $-{\magnetic{\mu}_{\mathrm B}}{\magnetic{B}}$. If their populations are ${\mechanical{n}}_+$ and ${\mechanical{n}}_-={\mechanical{N}}-{\mechanical{n}}_+$:

$$
{\energy{E}}=({\mechanical{n}}_+-{\mechanical{n}}_-){\magnetic{\mu}_{\mathrm B}}{\magnetic{B}}=(2{\mechanical{n}}_+-{\mechanical{N}}){\magnetic{\mu}_{\mathrm B}}{\magnetic{B}}.
$$

The number of microstates with this population is:

$$
\Omega({\mechanical{n}}_+)=\binom {\mechanical{N}}{{\mechanical{n}}_+}
=\frac{{\mechanical{N}}!}{{\mechanical{n}}_+!({\mechanical{N}}-{\mechanical{n}}_+)!}.
$$

The entropy is ${\thermal{S}}={\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}\ln\binom {\mechanical{N}}{{\mechanical{n}}_+}$. For ${\mechanical{N}}\gg1$, Stirling's formula gives:

$$
{\thermal{S}}\approx-{\mechanical{N}}{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}\bigl[x\ln x+(1-x)\ln(1-x)\bigr],
\qquad x=\frac{{\mechanical{n}}_+}{\mechanical{N}}.
$$

## Temperature and population inversion

Since ${\energy{E}}={\mechanical{N}}(2x-1){\magnetic{\mu}_{\mathrm B}}{\magnetic{B}}$:

$$
\frac1{\thermal{T}}
=\frac{\partial {\thermal{S}}}{\partial x}\frac1{2{\mechanical{N}}{\magnetic{\mu}_{\mathrm B}}{\magnetic{B}}}
=\frac{{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}}{2{\magnetic{\mu}_{\mathrm B}}{\magnetic{B}}}\ln\frac{{\mechanical{n}}_-}{{\mechanical{n}}_+}.
$$

Rearrangement gives:

$$
\frac{{\mechanical{n}}_+}{{\mechanical{n}}_-}
=\exp\!\left(-\frac{2{\magnetic{\mu}_{\mathrm B}}{\magnetic{B}}}{{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}{\thermal{T}}}\right).
$$

For ${\energy{E}}>0$, the upper level has the larger population: ${\mechanical{n}}_+>{\mechanical{n}}_-$. This is a **population inversion**. The logarithm in the temperature formula is negative, so ${\thermal{T}}<0$.

Negative temperature is not colder than absolute zero. It is hotter than any positive temperature: when suitable systems exchange energy, the negative-temperature system gives heat to the positive-temperature system. This requires a bounded energy spectrum and suitable isolation of the spin degrees of freedom.

The entropy has its maximum at ${\energy{E}}=0$, where ${\mechanical{n}}_+={\mechanical{n}}_-={\mechanical{N}}/2$ and the multiplicity is greatest.
