> [!TLDR]
> Continuous Gibbs entropy integrates over phase-space density and requires a fixed reference measure.

For a continuous distribution, the sum becomes an integral:

$$
{\thermal{S}}_G=-{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}\int_\Gamma\rho({\mechanical{q}},{\mechanical{p}})\ln\rho({\mechanical{q}},{\mechanical{p}})\,\mathrm d\Gamma.
$$

> [!note] Reference-measure clarification
> This is the source's continuous formula. If $\rho$ is a density with respect to dimensional phase-space volume, its logarithm needs a reference density $\rho_0$ with the same unit. The dimensionally explicit form is ${\thermal{S}}_G=-{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}\int\rho\ln(\rho/\rho_0)\,\mathrm d\Gamma$. Alternatively, use a dimensionless phase-space measure based on elementary cells. A fixed constant reference changes the entropy by an additive constant and does not change the Liouville conservation result.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\thermal{S}}$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Entropy | Boltzmann or Gibbs entropy, as specified by the context or subscript. |
| ${\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Boltzmann constant | The conversion factor between microscopic counting and thermodynamic entropy. |
| ${\mechanical{q}}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Position coordinate | A Cartesian coordinate; generalized coordinates can have different units. |
| ${\mechanical{p}}$ | $\unit{kg\,m\,s^{-1}}$ | Momentum | Momentum conjugate to a Cartesian position coordinate. |
| $\rho$ | $\unit{(J\,s)^{-3N}}$ | Phase-space probability density | Density relative to dΓ for N point particles in three dimensions. |
| $\mathrm d\Gamma$ | $\unit{(J\,s)^{3N}}$ | Phase-space volume element | The product of all coordinate and conjugate-momentum intervals in three dimensions. |
| ${\mechanical{N}}$ | $1$ | Particle or oscillator count | The number of particles or distinguishable oscillators. |
