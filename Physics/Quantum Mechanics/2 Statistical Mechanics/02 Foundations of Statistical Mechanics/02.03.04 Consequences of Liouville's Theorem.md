> [!TLDR]
> Hamiltonian evolution preserves phase-space measure and fine-grained Gibbs entropy.

## Corollary 2.1 — Invariant measure

The Lebesgue measure:

$$
\mathrm d\Gamma=\prod_i\mathrm d{\mechanical{q}}_i\,\mathrm d{\mechanical{p}}_i
$$

is invariant under Hamiltonian flow.

> [!note] Source correction
> The printed corollary omits the momentum differentials. Both coordinate and momentum differentials are required in the phase-space measure.

## Corollary 2.2 — Gibbs entropy outside equilibrium

The fine-grained Gibbs entropy calculated from the evolving density:

$$
{\thermal{S}}_G({\signal{t}})=-{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}\int_\Gamma\rho\ln\rho\,\mathrm d\Gamma
$$

is constant during Hamiltonian evolution. The continuous formula uses a fixed reference measure, as explained in [[02.06.02 Continuous Form]].

This can seem to conflict with the increase of thermodynamic entropy. A resolution requires **coarse-graining**: a description that does not retain arbitrarily fine phase-space detail. See [[02.09 Comparison of the Two Entropy Definitions]].

> [!note] Remark 2.2
> Liouville's theorem concerns classical Hamiltonian mechanics. Its quantum analogue is the von Neumann equation for the density operator:
>
> $i{\energy{\hbar}}\,\partial_{\signal{t}}\hat\rho=[\hat H,\hat\rho]$.
>
> The two equations have a corresponding evolution structure.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\mechanical{q}}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Position coordinate | A Cartesian coordinate; generalized coordinates can have different units. |
| ${\mechanical{p}}$ | $\unit{kg\,m\,s^{-1}}$ | Momentum | Momentum conjugate to a Cartesian position coordinate. |
| ${\signal{t}}$ | $\unit{s}$ | Time | Elapsed time; starred or subscripted forms specify prediction or recurrence times. |
| $\rho$ | $\unit{(J\,s)^{-3N}}$ | Phase-space probability density | Density relative to dΓ for N point particles in three dimensions. |
| ${\thermal{S}}$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Entropy | Boltzmann or Gibbs entropy, as specified by the context or subscript. |
| ${\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Boltzmann constant | The conversion factor between microscopic counting and thermodynamic entropy. |
| ${\energy{\hbar}}$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Reduced Planck constant | Planck constant divided by 2π. |
| $\mathrm d\Gamma$ | $\unit{(J\,s)^{3N}}$ | Phase-space volume element | The product of all coordinate and conjugate-momentum intervals in three dimensions. |
| ${\mechanical{N}}$ | $1$ | Particle or oscillator count | The number of particles or distinguishable oscillators. |
