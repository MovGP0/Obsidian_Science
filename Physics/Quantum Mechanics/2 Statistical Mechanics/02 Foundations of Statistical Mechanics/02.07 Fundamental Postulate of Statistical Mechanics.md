## Definition 2.9 — Fundamental postulate: equal a priori probabilities

For an isolated system in thermodynamic equilibrium with energy ${\energy{E}}$, volume ${\mechanical{V}}$, and particle count ${\mechanical{N}}$, all accessible microstates are equally likely:

$$
p_i=\frac1{\Omega({\energy{E}},{\mechanical{V}},{\mechanical{N}})}
\qquad\text{for every accessible microstate }i.
$$

This is the fundamental postulate on which equilibrium statistical mechanics is built.

See [[02.07.01 Justification and Status of the Postulate]].

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\energy{E}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Energy | The system or level energy; ΔE is an energy interval. |
| ${\mechanical{V}}$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Volume | The physical volume occupied by the system. |
| ${\mechanical{N}}$ | $1$ | Particle or oscillator count | The number of particles or distinguishable oscillators. |
| $p_i$ | $1$ | Microstate probability | The probability assigned to accessible microstate i. |
