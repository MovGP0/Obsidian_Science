> [!TLDR]
> Count the microscopic states compatible with a macrostate by measuring its accessible phase-space region in elementary cells.

Boltzmann's formulation starts with the number of microstates compatible with a specified macrostate.

## Definition 2.6 — Macrostate and microstate

A **macrostate** is specified by measurable macroscopic quantities such as $({\energy{E}},{\mechanical{V}},{\mechanical{N}},\ldots)$. A **microstate** is one precise phase-space point compatible with that macrostate.

The accessible microstate count $\Omega({\energy{E}},{\mechanical{V}},{\mechanical{N}};\Delta {\energy{E}})$ is also called the **multiplicity** or degeneracy. In the semiclassical description of identical monatomic particles, it is:

$$
\Omega({\energy{E}},{\mechanical{V}},{\mechanical{N}};\Delta {\energy{E}})
=\frac1{{\energy{h}}^{3{\mechanical{N}}}{\mechanical{N}}!}
\int_{{\energy{\mathcal H}}\in[{\energy{E}},{\energy{E}}+\Delta {\energy{E}}]}\mathrm d\Gamma.
$$

The integral measures the phase-space region within an energy interval. Division by ${\energy{h}}^{3{\mechanical{N}}}$ expresses its volume in elementary phase-space cells.

The additional factor ${\mechanical{N}}!$ accounts for the indistinguishability of identical particles in classical Boltzmann statistics. Permutations do not produce new physical states. This factor is needed to avoid the Gibbs paradox, discussed in Chapter 3.

> [!note]
> This formula is a semiclassical counting rule. The correction for particle permutations is not a substitute for the full Bose or Fermi counting required in a quantum-degenerate gas.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\energy{E}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Energy | The system or level energy; ΔE is an energy interval. |
| ${\mechanical{V}}$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Volume | The physical volume occupied by the system. |
| ${\mechanical{N}}$ | $1$ | Particle or oscillator count | The number of particles or distinguishable oscillators. |
| ${\energy{h}}$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Planck constant | The action scale in semiclassical state counting. |
| ${\energy{\mathcal H}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Hamiltonian | The energy function of the system or individual oscillator. |
| $p_i$ | $1$ | Microstate probability | The probability assigned to accessible microstate i. |
| $\mathrm d\Gamma$ | $\unit{(J\,s)^{3N}}$ | Phase-space volume element | The product of all coordinate and conjugate-momentum intervals in three dimensions. |
