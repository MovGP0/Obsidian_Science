> [!TLDR]
> Hamiltonian flow can change the shape of a phase-space region, but it preserves its volume.

The proof of Liouville's theorem gives:

$$
\sum_i\left(
\frac{\partial\dot {\mechanical{q}}_i}{\partial {\mechanical{q}}_i}
+\frac{\partial\dot {\mechanical{p}}_i}{\partial {\mechanical{p}}_i}
\right)=0.
$$

This equation has a direct geometric meaning: **Hamiltonian phase-space flow is incompressible**. The ensemble cloud behaves like an incompressible fluid. Its shape can stretch, shear, or become more complex, but its volume stays constant.
