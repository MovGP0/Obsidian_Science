> [!TLDR]
> A weak magnetic field slightly shifts the two electron spin populations and produces Pauli paramagnetism.

- [[07.05.01 Magnetization of the Electron Gas]] defines the two spin populations.
- [[07.05.02 Pauli Susceptibility]] derives the leading response.
- [[07.05.03 Finite-Temperature Corrections]] gives the low-temperature correction.
