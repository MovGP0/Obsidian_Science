> [!TLDR]
> The Sirius B exercise combines density, Fermi energy, relativity, and degeneracy pressure in one worked note.

The supplied Chapter 7 has one numbered exercise. Its subquestions remain together in one file:

- [[07.01 Sirius B - Degenerate Electrons]] — Exercise 7.1, with question, calculation steps, comments, and solution.

Before reading the solution, predict which tests are independent. A small temperature relative to the Fermi temperature tests degeneracy; a large Fermi momentum relative to mass times the speed of light tests relativistic motion. Passing the first does not determine the second. Find the electron density and Fermi momentum before selecting the energy and pressure approximations.

After the calculation, explain why a mean stellar density gives only a scale estimate for the local pressure. Also identify which quoted temperatures refer to the surface and which would be needed for the interior. The worked note keeps the source question and its solution together so that these limits can be checked without replacing the supplied problem.

The other supplied calculations are Examples 7.1–7.3 in [[07.08 Worked Examples]].
