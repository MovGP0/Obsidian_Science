> [!TLDR]
> The Sommerfeld expansion gives controlled low-temperature corrections to a degenerate Fermi gas.

At finite temperature, the Fermi surface is broadened over an energy interval of order ${\thermal{k}}_B{\thermal{T}}$.

- [[07.03.01 Fermi–Dirac Distribution]] gives the occupation and normalization.
- [[07.03.02 Fermi Integrals]] defines the relevant integrals.
- [[07.03.03 Sommerfeld Expansion]] develops the low-temperature method.
- [[07.03.04 Chemical Potential at Low Temperature]] applies it at fixed density.
- [[07.03.05 Total Energy at Finite Temperature]] obtains the energy correction.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\thermal{T}}$ | $\unit{K}$ | Temperature | Absolute temperature of the system. |
| ${\thermal{k}}_B$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Boltzmann constant | Converts temperature to energy. |
