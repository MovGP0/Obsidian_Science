> [!TLDR]
> In three dimensions, the density of states of a nonrelativistic free particle is proportional to the square root of energy.

In this chapter, ${\energy{g}}({\energy{\varepsilon}})$ counts states **per volume and per energy**, including spin:

$$
{\energy{g}}({\energy{\varepsilon}})\,d{\energy{\varepsilon}}
=\frac{{\mechanical{g}}_s}{(2\pi)^3}\,4\pi {\signal{k}}^2d{\signal{k}}.
$$

From Equation (7.1),

$$
{\signal{k}}=\frac{\sqrt{2{\mechanical{m}}{\energy{\varepsilon}}}}{{\energy{\hbar}}},\qquad
d{\signal{k}}=\frac1{\energy{\hbar}}\sqrt{\frac{{\mechanical{m}}}{2{\energy{\varepsilon}}}}\,d{\energy{\varepsilon}}.
$$

Therefore

$$
{\energy{g}}({\energy{\varepsilon}})=\frac{{\mechanical{g}}_s}{4\pi^2}\left(\frac{2{\mechanical{m}}}{{\energy{\hbar}}^2}\right)^{3/2}{\energy{\varepsilon}}^{1/2}
\equiv C{\energy{\varepsilon}}^{1/2},
\qquad C=\frac{{\mechanical{g}}_s}{4\pi^2}\left(\frac{2{\mechanical{m}}}{{\energy{\hbar}}^2}\right)^{3/2}.
$$

**Remark 7.1.** In $d$ spatial dimensions and for quadratic dispersion, ${\energy{g}}({\energy{\varepsilon}})\propto{\energy{\varepsilon}}^{d/2-1}$. It is constant in two dimensions and diverges as ${\energy{\varepsilon}}^{-1/2}$ in one dimension.

> [!note] Density-of-states convention
> Chapter 6 uses a total density of states in its state-counting formulas. Here, $g$ is that total divided by ${\mechanical{V}}$. Thus ${\mechanical{N}}={\mechanical{V}}\int {\energy{g}}({\energy{\varepsilon}}){\mechanical{f}}({\energy{\varepsilon}},{\thermal{T}})\,d{\energy{\varepsilon}}$. Keep the volume factor when changing conventions.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\energy{g}}({\energy{\varepsilon}})$ | $\unit{J^{-1}\,m^{-3}}$ | Density of states per volume | Number of states per energy and volume, including spin. |
| ${\mechanical{g}}_s$ | $\unit{1}$ | Spin degeneracy | Number of internal spin states at a given orbital state. |
| ${\signal{k}}$ | $\unit{m^{-1}}$ | Wavenumber | Magnitude or component of a wave vector. |
| ${\energy{\varepsilon}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Single-particle energy | Energy of one single-particle state. |
| ${\mechanical{m}}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | Mass of one particle. |
| ${\energy{\hbar}}$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Reduced Planck constant | Equal to $h/(2\pi)$. |
| ${\mechanical{N}}$ | $\unit{1}$ | Particle number | Total or mean number of particles. |
| ${\mechanical{V}}$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Volume | Volume of a three-dimensional system. |
| ${\thermal{T}}$ | $\unit{K}$ | Temperature | Absolute temperature of the system. |
| ${\mechanical{f}}$ | $\unit{1}$ | Mean occupation | Fermi–Dirac occupation of one complete single-particle state. |
