> [!TLDR]
> Electron degeneracy pressure supports white dwarfs, but relativistic electrons produce a limiting mass.

This application combines two models. The electron gas supplies a relation between density and pressure; gravity and the mass distribution determine how much pressure support the star needs. The nuclei carry most of the mass, while electrons supply the degeneracy pressure in this approximation. Composition is needed to convert the mass density into electron number density.

Read the nonrelativistic energy balance before the limiting-mass argument. The decisive change is that relativistic kinetic energy and gravitational energy acquire the same inverse-radius scaling, so reducing the radius cannot always restore balance. This is a scaling argument for a mass limit, not a derivation of its exact coefficient. [Tong, Statistical Physics, §3.6.5](https://www.damtp.cam.ac.uk/user/tong/statphys/statmechhtml/S3.html#S3.SS6.SSS5) gives the same uniform-density comparison and distinguishes the nonrelativistic and relativistic limits.
