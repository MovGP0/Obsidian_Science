> [!TLDR]
> Counting states inside the Fermi sphere fixes the characteristic momentum and energy scales.

The number of states in a wave-vector volume element is

$$
d\mathcal N={\mechanical{g}}_s\frac{{\mechanical{V}}}{(2\pi)^3}\,d^3{\signal{k}}.
$$

Integrate over the sphere of radius ${\signal{k}}_F$:

$$
{\mechanical{N}}={\mechanical{g}}_s\frac{{\mechanical{V}}}{(2\pi)^3}\int_{|\mathbf k|\le k_F}d^3{\signal{k}}
={\mechanical{g}}_s\frac{{\mechanical{V}}}{(2\pi)^3}\frac{4\pi}{3}{\signal{k}}_F^3.
$$

With number density ${\mechanical{n}}={\mechanical{N}}/{\mechanical{V}}$, this gives

$$
{\signal{k}}_F=\left(\frac{6\pi^2{\mechanical{n}}}{{\mechanical{g}}_s}\right)^{1/3}.
$$

For electrons, ${\mechanical{g}}_s=2$ and ${\signal{k}}_F=(3\pi^2{\mechanical{n}})^{1/3}$. The Fermi energy and momentum are

$$
{\energy{\varepsilon}}_F=\frac{{\energy{\hbar}}^2 {\signal{k}}_F^2}{2{\mechanical{m}}}
=\frac{{\energy{\hbar}}^2}{2{\mechanical{m}}}\left(\frac{6\pi^2{\mechanical{n}}}{{\mechanical{g}}_s}\right)^{2/3},
\qquad
{\mechanical{p}}_F={\energy{\hbar}} {\signal{k}}_F={\energy{\hbar}}\left(\frac{6\pi^2{\mechanical{n}}}{{\mechanical{g}}_s}\right)^{1/3}.
$$

Define the Fermi temperature by ${\thermal{T}}_F={\energy{\varepsilon}}_F/{\thermal{k}}_B$. A gas is strongly degenerate when ${\thermal{T}}\ll {\thermal{T}}_F$. Typical metallic Fermi temperatures are of order $10^4$–$10^5\,\unit{K}$, so conduction electrons remain strongly degenerate at room temperature.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\mechanical{N}}$ | $\unit{1}$ | Particle number | Total or mean number of particles. |
| ${\mechanical{V}}$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Volume | Volume of a three-dimensional system. |
| ${\mechanical{n}}$ | $\unit{m^{-3}}$ | Number density | Number of particles per volume. |
| ${\mechanical{g}}_s$ | $\unit{1}$ | Spin degeneracy | Number of internal spin states at a given orbital state. |
| ${\signal{k}}$ | $\unit{m^{-1}}$ | Wavenumber | Magnitude or component of a wave vector. |
| ${\energy{\varepsilon}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Single-particle energy | Energy of one single-particle state. |
| ${\energy{\hbar}}$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Reduced Planck constant | Equal to $h/(2\pi)$. |
| ${\mechanical{m}}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | Mass of one particle. |
| ${\mechanical{p}}$ | $\unit{kg\,m\,s^{-1}}$ | Momentum | Magnitude of particle momentum. |
| ${\thermal{T}}$ | $\unit{K}$ | Temperature | Absolute temperature of the system. |
| ${\thermal{T}}_F$ | $\unit{K}$ | Fermi temperature | Fermi energy divided by the Boltzmann constant. |
| ${\thermal{k}}_B$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Boltzmann constant | Converts temperature to energy. |
