> [!TLDR]
> The energy variance equals Boltzmann constant times squared temperature times heat capacity.

The subsystem energy is a random variable because it exchanges energy with the bath:

$$
{\energy{\sigma}}_E^2=\langle {\energy{E}}^2\rangle-\langle {\energy{E}}\rangle^2
=\left.\frac{\partial^2\ln\mathcal Z}{\partial{\beta}^2}\right|_{V,N}.
$$

Equation (4.9) gives:

$$
{\energy{\sigma}}_E^2=\langle({\energy{E}}-\langle {\energy{E}}\rangle)^2\rangle
={\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}{\thermal{T}}^2{\thermal{C}_V}.
$$

**Proof.** Since ${\energy{U}}=-\partial_{\beta}\ln\mathcal Z$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
{\thermal{C}_V}&=\frac{\partial {\energy{U}}}{\partial{\beta}}\frac{\mathrm d{\beta}}{\mathrm d{\thermal{T}}}\\
&=-\frac{\partial^2\ln\mathcal Z}{\partial{\beta}^2}
\left(-\frac{1}{{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}{\thermal{T}}^2}\right)\\
&=\frac{1}{{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}{\thermal{T}}^2}
\frac{\partial^2\ln\mathcal Z}{\partial{\beta}^2}
=\frac{{\energy{\sigma}}_E^2}{{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}{\thermal{T}}^2}.
\end{aligned}
$$

This proves the result. $\square$

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\energy{E}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Energy | The system, level, bath, or reference energy, as indicated by the subscript. |
| ${\energy{U}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Internal energy | The canonical mean energy. |
| ${\thermal{T}}$ | $\unit{K}$ | Temperature | The positive absolute temperature unless a limit is stated. |
| ${\thermal{C}_V}$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Heat capacity at constant volume | The temperature derivative of internal energy at fixed volume and particle count. |
| ${\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Boltzmann constant | The conversion between temperature and thermal energy. |
| ${\energy{\sigma}}_E$ | $\unit{J}$ | Energy standard deviation | The square root of the canonical energy variance. |
| ${\beta}$ | $\unit{J^{-1}}$ | Inverse thermal energy | The parameter 1/(kᴮT). |
| $\mathcal Z$ | 1 | Partition function | The dimensionless sum or integral of Boltzmann weights. |
