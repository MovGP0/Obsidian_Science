> [!TLDR]
> Gaussian momentum integrals give the ideal-gas partition function and equation of state.

This model asks whether state counting can reproduce gas pressure and thermal energy. The atoms are nonrelativistic, their mutual interactions are neglected, and only translational motion contributes. A rigid container fixes the volume and particle count; a bath fixes temperature. The classical approximation also requires low occupation of quantum translational states.

The calculation separates position counting from momentum counting. The next note differentiates the result to obtain pressure, energy, and entropy. The pressure law alone does not test all of that counting: the Gibbs factorial is independent of volume but is essential for the entropy and chemical potential.
