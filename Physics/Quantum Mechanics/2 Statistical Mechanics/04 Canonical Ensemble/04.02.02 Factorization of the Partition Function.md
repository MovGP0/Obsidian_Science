> [!TLDR]
> Independent classical identical particles give a product partition function with a Gibbs correction.

For ${\mechanical{N}}$ noninteracting, identical classical particles with one-particle partition function $\mathcal Z_1$:

$$
\mathcal Z_N=\frac{\mathcal Z_1^{\mechanical{N}}}{{\mechanical{N}}!}. \tag{4.6}
$$

The Gibbs factor removes repeated counting of permutations of identical particles. This result is central to the classical ideal gas.

> [!note] Scope of the factorization
> For distinguishable independent subsystems, the partition function is a product with no ${\mechanical{N}}!$. Independent localized spins and oscillators are examples. Equation (4.6) applies to classical identical particles; it is not the general quantum Bose or Fermi partition function.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\mechanical{N}}$ | 1 | Particle or subsystem count | The number of particles, localized spins, or independent oscillators. |
| $\mathcal Z$ | 1 | Partition function | The dimensionless sum or integral of Boltzmann weights. |
