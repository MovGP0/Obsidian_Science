> [!TLDR]
> Helmholtz free energy connects the canonical partition function to thermodynamics.

Microscopic probabilities are not yet a thermodynamic description. Free energy provides the bridge: it includes both the energetic cost of a state and the entropy gained by having many accessible states. The reservoir fixes temperature, so this energy–entropy balance uses Helmholtz free energy under the fixed-volume, fixed-particle constraints of this chapter.

The first note derives this connection from Gibbs entropy. The second shows how changes in temperature, volume, and particle count extract distinct responses. Keep the held-fixed variables with each derivative; the same free-energy expression can give a different result along a different thermodynamic path.
