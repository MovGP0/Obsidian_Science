> [!TLDR]
> Derivatives of the partition function give energy moments; derivatives of its logarithm give cumulants.

## Definition 4.2 — Canonical partition function

$$
\mathcal Z({\thermal{T}},{\mechanical{V}},{\mechanical{N}})=\sum_i e^{-{\beta} {\energy{E}}_i}
=\operatorname{Tr}\left(e^{-{\beta}\hat{{\energy{\mathcal H}}}}\right). \tag{4.4}
$$

The sum, or the quantum trace, includes all microstates.

For a classical continuous system of identical particles in the Maxwell–Boltzmann regime:

$$
\mathcal Z=\frac{1}{{\energy{h}}^{3{\mechanical{N}}}{\mechanical{N}}!}
\int \mathrm d^{3{\mechanical{N}}}{\mechanical{q}}\,\mathrm d^{3{\mechanical{N}}}{\mechanical{p}}\,
e^{-{\beta}{\energy{\mathcal H}}({\mechanical{q}},{\mechanical{p}})}.
$$

The phase-space cell factor is ${\energy{h}}^{3{\mechanical{N}}}$; the Gibbs factor is ${\mechanical{N}}!$.

## Proposition 4.1 — Derivatives of the partition function

$$
\langle {\energy{E}}\rangle=-\frac{\partial\ln\mathcal Z}{\partial{\beta}}, \tag{4.5}
$$

$$
\langle {\energy{E}}^2\rangle
=\frac{1}{\mathcal Z}\frac{\partial^2\mathcal Z}{\partial{\beta}^2}
=\frac{\partial^2\ln\mathcal Z}{\partial{\beta}^2}+\langle {\energy{E}}\rangle^2.
$$

**Proof.** Direct differentiation gives:

$$
-\frac{\partial\ln\mathcal Z}{\partial{\beta}}
=-\frac{1}{\mathcal Z}\frac{\partial\mathcal Z}{\partial{\beta}}
=-\frac{1}{\mathcal Z}\sum_i(-{\energy{E}}_i)e^{-{\beta} {\energy{E}}_i}
=\frac{\sum_i {\energy{E}}_i e^{-{\beta} {\energy{E}}_i}}{\mathcal Z}
=\langle {\energy{E}}\rangle.
$$

A second derivative gives $\langle {\energy{E}}^2\rangle-\langle {\energy{E}}\rangle^2$, the energy variance. $\square$

> [!note] Terminology clarification
> The source calls logarithmic derivatives “moments.” Their first two results are the mean and variance. In general, derivatives of $\mathcal Z$ generate raw moments; derivatives of $\ln\mathcal Z$ generate cumulants. The Hamiltonian is assumed independent of temperature.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\energy{E}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Energy | The system, level, bath, or reference energy, as indicated by the subscript. |
| ${\thermal{T}}$ | $\unit{K}$ | Temperature | The positive absolute temperature unless a limit is stated. |
| ${\mechanical{V}}$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Volume | The volume available to the particles. |
| ${\mechanical{N}}$ | 1 | Particle or subsystem count | The number of particles, localized spins, or independent oscillators. |
| ${\mechanical{p}}$ | $\unit{kg\,m\,s^{-1}}$ | Momentum | A Cartesian canonical momentum component. |
| ${\mechanical{q}}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Position | A Cartesian position coordinate. |
| ${\energy{h}}$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Planck constant | The classical phase-space cell constant. |
| ${\energy{\mathcal H}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Hamiltonian | The classical energy function or quantum energy operator. |
| ${\beta}$ | $\unit{J^{-1}}$ | Inverse thermal energy | The parameter 1/(kᴮT). |
| $\mathcal Z$ | 1 | Partition function | The dimensionless sum or integral of Boltzmann weights. |
