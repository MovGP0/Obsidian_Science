> [!TLDR]
> The classical ideal-gas partition function is the available volume per thermal wavelength cubed, raised to particle count and divided by N factorial.

Consider ${\mechanical{N}}$ noninteracting monatomic atoms of mass ${\mechanical{m}}$ in volume ${\mechanical{V}}$. The Hamiltonian is kinetic:

$$
{\energy{\mathcal H}}=\sum_{i=1}^{\mechanical{N}}\frac{\mathbf {\mechanical{p}}_i^2}{2{\mechanical{m}}}.
$$

It is sufficient to calculate the one-particle partition function:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal Z_1
&=\frac{1}{{\energy{h}}^3}\int_V\mathrm d^3{\mechanical{q}}\int\mathrm d^3{\mechanical{p}}\,
e^{-{\beta}\mathbf {\mechanical{p}}^2/(2{\mechanical{m}})}\\
&=\frac {\mechanical{V}}{{\energy{h}}^3}
\left(\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}e^{-{\beta} {\mechanical{p}}_x^2/(2{\mechanical{m}})}\,\mathrm d{\mechanical{p}}_x\right)^3.
\end{aligned}
$$

The Gaussian integral is:

$$
\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}e^{-{\beta} {\mechanical{p}}^2/(2{\mechanical{m}})}\,\mathrm d{\mechanical{p}}
=\sqrt{\frac{2\pi {\mechanical{m}}}{{\beta}}}
=\sqrt{2\pi {\mechanical{m}} {\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}{\thermal{T}}}.
$$

With the thermal de Broglie wavelength:

$$
{\signal{\lambda}}_{\mathrm{th}}=\frac{{\energy{h}}}{\sqrt{2\pi {\mechanical{m}} {\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}{\thermal{T}}}},
$$

we obtain $\mathcal Z_1={\mechanical{V}}/{\signal{\lambda}}_{\mathrm{th}}^3$. For identical particles:

$$
\mathcal Z_N=\frac{\mathcal Z_1^{\mechanical{N}}}{{\mechanical{N}}!}
=\frac{1}{{\mechanical{N}}!}\left(\frac {\mechanical{V}}{{\signal{\lambda}}_{\mathrm{th}}^3}\right)^{\mechanical{N}}. \tag{4.11}
$$

This is the Maxwell–Boltzmann limit; it requires a dilute gas with ${\mechanical{N}}{\signal{\lambda}}_{\mathrm{th}}^3/{\mechanical{V}}\ll1$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\thermal{T}}$ | $\unit{K}$ | Temperature | The positive absolute temperature unless a limit is stated. |
| ${\mechanical{V}}$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Volume | The volume available to the particles. |
| ${\mechanical{N}}$ | 1 | Particle or subsystem count | The number of particles, localized spins, or independent oscillators. |
| ${\mechanical{m}}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Particle mass | The mass of one atom or molecule. |
| ${\mechanical{p}}$ | $\unit{kg\,m\,s^{-1}}$ | Momentum | A Cartesian canonical momentum component. |
| ${\mechanical{q}}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Position | A Cartesian position coordinate. |
| ${\energy{h}}$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Planck constant | The classical phase-space cell constant. |
| ${\signal{\lambda}}_{\mathrm{th}}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Thermal wavelength | The thermal de Broglie wavelength. |
| ${\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Boltzmann constant | The conversion between temperature and thermal energy. |
| ${\energy{\mathcal H}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Hamiltonian | The classical energy function or quantum energy operator. |
| ${\beta}$ | $\unit{J^{-1}}$ | Inverse thermal energy | The parameter 1/(kᴮT). |
| $\mathcal Z$ | 1 | Partition function | The dimensionless sum or integral of Boltzmann weights. |
