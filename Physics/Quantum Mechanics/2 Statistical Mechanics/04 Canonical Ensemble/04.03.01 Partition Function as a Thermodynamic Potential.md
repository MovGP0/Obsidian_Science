> [!TLDR]
> Helmholtz free energy is minus thermal energy times the logarithm of the partition function.

## Theorem 4.1 — Helmholtz free energy

The Helmholtz free energy is:

$$
{\energy{F}}({\thermal{T}},{\mechanical{V}},{\mechanical{N}})=-{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}{\thermal{T}}\ln\mathcal Z.
$$

**Proof.** Start from the Gibbs entropy:

$$
\begin{aligned}
{\thermal{S}}&=-{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}\sum_i P_i\ln P_i\\
&=-{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}\sum_iP_i(-{\beta} {\energy{E}}_i-\ln\mathcal Z)\\
&={\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}{\beta}\langle {\energy{E}}\rangle+{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}\ln\mathcal Z.
\end{aligned}
$$

Thus:

$$
{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}\ln\mathcal Z={\thermal{S}}-\frac{\langle {\energy{E}}\rangle}{{\thermal{T}}},
$$

and:

$$
-{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}{\thermal{T}}\ln\mathcal Z=\langle {\energy{E}}\rangle-{\thermal{T}}{\thermal{S}}={\energy{F}}.
$$

This proves the relation. $\square$

> [!note] Remark 4.2 — Statistical bridge
> ${\energy{F}}=-{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}{\thermal{T}}\ln\mathcal Z$ plays a role similar to ${\thermal{S}}={\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}\ln\Omega$ in the microcanonical ensemble. Both connect a thermodynamic quantity with a statistical state function.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\energy{E}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Energy | The system, level, bath, or reference energy, as indicated by the subscript. |
| ${\energy{F}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Helmholtz free energy | The equilibrium potential at fixed temperature, volume, and particle count. |
| ${\thermal{S}}$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Entropy | The thermodynamic or Gibbs entropy. |
| ${\thermal{T}}$ | $\unit{K}$ | Temperature | The positive absolute temperature unless a limit is stated. |
| ${\mechanical{V}}$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Volume | The volume available to the particles. |
| ${\mechanical{N}}$ | 1 | Particle or subsystem count | The number of particles, localized spins, or independent oscillators. |
| ${\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Boltzmann constant | The conversion between temperature and thermal energy. |
| ${\beta}$ | $\unit{J^{-1}}$ | Inverse thermal energy | The parameter 1/(kᴮT). |
| $\mathcal Z$ | 1 | Partition function | The dimensionless sum or integral of Boltzmann weights. |
| $\Omega$ | 1 | State count | The number of compatible microscopic states. |
