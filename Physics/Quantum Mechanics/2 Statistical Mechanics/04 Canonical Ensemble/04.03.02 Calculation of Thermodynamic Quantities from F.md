> [!TLDR]
> Derivatives of free energy give entropy, pressure, chemical potential, internal energy, and heat capacity.

Once ${\energy{F}}({\thermal{T}},{\mechanical{V}},{\mechanical{N}})$ is known, the equilibrium quantities follow by differentiation:

$$
{\thermal{S}}=-\left(\frac{\partial {\energy{F}}}{\partial {\thermal{T}}}\right)_{V,N}
={\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}\ln\mathcal Z
+{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}{\thermal{T}}\left(\frac{\partial\ln\mathcal Z}{\partial {\thermal{T}}}\right)_{V,N},
$$

$$
{\mechanical{P}}=-\left(\frac{\partial {\energy{F}}}{\partial {\mechanical{V}}}\right)_{T,N}
={\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}{\thermal{T}}\left(\frac{\partial\ln\mathcal Z}{\partial {\mechanical{V}}}\right)_{T,N}. \tag{4.7}
$$

$$
{\energy{\mu}}=\left(\frac{\partial {\energy{F}}}{\partial {\mechanical{N}}}\right)_{T,V}
=-{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}{\thermal{T}}\left(\frac{\partial\ln\mathcal Z}{\partial {\mechanical{N}}}\right)_{T,V},
$$

$$
{\energy{U}}={\energy{F}}+{\thermal{T}}{\thermal{S}}
={\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}{\thermal{T}}^2\left(\frac{\partial\ln\mathcal Z}{\partial {\thermal{T}}}\right)_{V,N}
=-\left.\frac{\partial\ln\mathcal Z}{\partial{\beta}}\right|_{V,N}. \tag{4.8}
$$

> [!note] Notation
> The source uses lowercase p for pressure. These notes use ${\mechanical{P}}$ for pressure and reserve $p$ for momentum. Equation (4.8) agrees with Eq. (4.5), as required by the consistency of the two derivations.

The heat capacity at constant volume is:

$$
{\thermal{C}_V}=\left(\frac{\partial {\energy{U}}}{\partial {\thermal{T}}}\right)_{V,N}
={\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}{\beta}^2
\left.\frac{\partial^2\ln\mathcal Z}{\partial{\beta}^2}\right|_{V,N}. \tag{4.9}
$$

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\energy{U}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Internal energy | The canonical mean energy. |
| ${\energy{F}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Helmholtz free energy | The equilibrium potential at fixed temperature, volume, and particle count. |
| ${\thermal{S}}$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Entropy | The thermodynamic or Gibbs entropy. |
| ${\thermal{T}}$ | $\unit{K}$ | Temperature | The positive absolute temperature unless a limit is stated. |
| ${\mechanical{V}}$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Volume | The volume available to the particles. |
| ${\mechanical{N}}$ | 1 | Particle or subsystem count | The number of particles, localized spins, or independent oscillators. |
| ${\mechanical{P}}$ | $\unit{Pa}$ | Pressure | The force per area conjugate to volume. |
| ${\energy{\mu}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Chemical potential | The free-energy change per added particle. |
| ${\thermal{C}_V}$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Heat capacity at constant volume | The temperature derivative of internal energy at fixed volume and particle count. |
| ${\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Boltzmann constant | The conversion between temperature and thermal energy. |
| ${\beta}$ | $\unit{J^{-1}}$ | Inverse thermal energy | The parameter 1/(kᴮT). |
| $\mathcal Z$ | 1 | Partition function | The dimensionless sum or integral of Boltzmann weights. |
