> [!TLDR]
> Spin entropy decreases as field alignment increases, and heat capacity has a Schottky form.

The free energy is:

$$
{\energy{F}}=-{\mechanical{N}}{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}{\thermal{T}}\ln\left[2\cosh\left(\frac{{\magnetic{\mu}_0}{\magnetic{B}}}{{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}{\thermal{T}}}\right)\right].
$$

The entropy is:

$$
{\thermal{S}}=-\left(\frac{\partial {\energy{F}}}{\partial {\thermal{T}}}\right)_{B,N}
={\mechanical{N}}{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}[\ln(2\cosh x)-x\tanh x],
\qquad x=\frac{{\magnetic{\mu}_0}{\magnetic{B}}}{{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}{\thermal{T}}}. \tag{4.17}
$$

As ${\magnetic{B}}\to0$ at fixed positive temperature, $x\to0$ and ${\thermal{S}}\to {\mechanical{N}}{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}\ln2$. Each of the $2^{\mechanical{N}}$ configurations is equally probable, so entropy is maximal. As $x\to+\infty$, ${\thermal{S}}\to0$ and the moments become fully ordered.

> [!note] Added heat-capacity relation
> The source heading includes heat capacity but its body stops after the entropy limits. Differentiating $U=-{\mechanical{N}}{\magnetic{\mu}_0}{\magnetic{B}}\tanh x$ at fixed field gives:
> ${\thermal{C}_B}={\mechanical{N}}{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}x^2\operatorname{sech}^2x$.
> This is the Schottky form for a level gap $2{\magnetic{\mu}_0}{\magnetic{B}}$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\energy{F}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Helmholtz free energy | The equilibrium potential at fixed temperature, volume, and particle count. |
| ${\thermal{S}}$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Entropy | The thermodynamic or Gibbs entropy. |
| ${\thermal{T}}$ | $\unit{K}$ | Temperature | The positive absolute temperature unless a limit is stated. |
| ${\mechanical{N}}$ | 1 | Particle or subsystem count | The number of particles, localized spins, or independent oscillators. |
| ${\magnetic{B}}$ | $\unit{T}$ | Magnetic flux density | The applied magnetic field in the source convention. |
| ${\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Boltzmann constant | The conversion between temperature and thermal energy. |
| ${\magnetic{\mu}_0}$ | $\unit{J\,T^{-1}}$ | Effective spin moment | The moment magnitude used in this chapter, not vacuum permeability. |
| ${\thermal{C}_B}$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Heat capacity at fixed field | The spin energy derivative at fixed applied magnetic field. |
