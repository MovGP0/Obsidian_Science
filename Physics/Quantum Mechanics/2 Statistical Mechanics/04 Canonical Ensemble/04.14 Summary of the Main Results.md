> [!TLDR]
> The partition function contains all canonical equilibrium information.

**Table 4.1** Thermodynamic quantities in the canonical ensemble.

| Quantity | Symbol | Formula |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Partition function | $\mathcal Z$ | $\sum_i e^{-{\beta} {\energy{E}}_i}$ |
| Helmholtz free energy | ${\energy{F}}$ | $-{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}{\thermal{T}}\ln\mathcal Z$ |
| Internal energy | ${\energy{U}}$ | $-(\partial\ln\mathcal Z/\partial{\beta})_{V,N}$ |
| Entropy | ${\thermal{S}}$ | $-(\partial {\energy{F}}/\partial {\thermal{T}})_{V,N}$ |
| Pressure | ${\mechanical{P}}$ | $-(\partial {\energy{F}}/\partial {\mechanical{V}})_{T,N}$ |
| Chemical potential | ${\energy{\mu}}$ | $(\partial {\energy{F}}/\partial {\mechanical{N}})_{T,V}$ |
| Heat capacity | ${\thermal{C}_V}$ | ${\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}{\beta}^2(\partial^2\ln\mathcal Z/\partial{\beta}^2)_{V,N}$ |
| Energy variance | ${\energy{\sigma}}_E^2$ | ${\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}{\thermal{T}}^2{\thermal{C}_V}$ |

The fixed variables are made explicit here. The source's summary table abbreviates these subscripts.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\energy{E}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Energy | The system, level, bath, or reference energy, as indicated by the subscript. |
| ${\energy{U}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Internal energy | The canonical mean energy. |
| ${\energy{F}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Helmholtz free energy | The equilibrium potential at fixed temperature, volume, and particle count. |
| ${\thermal{S}}$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Entropy | The thermodynamic or Gibbs entropy. |
| ${\thermal{T}}$ | $\unit{K}$ | Temperature | The positive absolute temperature unless a limit is stated. |
| ${\mechanical{V}}$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Volume | The volume available to the particles. |
| ${\mechanical{N}}$ | 1 | Particle or subsystem count | The number of particles, localized spins, or independent oscillators. |
| ${\mechanical{P}}$ | $\unit{Pa}$ | Pressure | The force per area conjugate to volume. |
| ${\energy{\mu}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Chemical potential | The free-energy change per added particle. |
| ${\thermal{C}_V}$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Heat capacity at constant volume | The temperature derivative of internal energy at fixed volume and particle count. |
| ${\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Boltzmann constant | The conversion between temperature and thermal energy. |
| ${\energy{\sigma}}_E$ | $\unit{J}$ | Energy standard deviation | The square root of the canonical energy variance. |
| ${\beta}$ | $\unit{J^{-1}}$ | Inverse thermal energy | The parameter 1/(kᴮT). |
| $\mathcal Z$ | 1 | Partition function | The dimensionless sum or integral of Boltzmann weights. |
