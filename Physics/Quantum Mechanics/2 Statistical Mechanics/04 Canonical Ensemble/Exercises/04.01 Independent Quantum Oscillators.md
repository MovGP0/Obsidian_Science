> [!TLDR]
> Independent oscillators have a product partition function and additive free energy, energy, entropy, and heat capacity.

## Question

Calculate the partition function and free energy of ${\mechanical{N}}$ quantum harmonic oscillators with frequencies ${\signal{\omega}}_1,{\signal{\omega}}_2,\ldots,{\signal{\omega}}_N$. Show that ${\energy{F}}=\sum_{i=1}^{\mechanical{N}}{\energy{F}}_i$, where ${\energy{F}}_i$ is the free energy of oscillator $i$. Which thermodynamic quantities are additive?

## Worked solution

> [!note] Added solution
> The question is transcribed from Exercise 4.1. The calculations and explanations below are added.

### Separate the independent degrees of freedom

The oscillators are independent and labeled by their frequencies or sites. The total energy is a sum:

$$
{\energy{E}}_{\{n_i\}}=\sum_{i=1}^{\mechanical{N}}{\energy{\hbar}}{\signal{\omega}}_i(n_i+1/2).
$$

Because the exponential of a sum factors, the sums over occupation numbers also factor:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal Z_N
&=\sum_{n_1=0}^\infty\cdots\sum_{n_N=0}^\infty
\prod_{i=1}^{\mechanical{N}} e^{-{\beta}{\energy{\hbar}}{\signal{\omega}}_i(n_i+1/2)}\\
&=\prod_{i=1}^{\mechanical{N}}\mathcal Z_i,\qquad
\mathcal Z_i=\frac1{2\sinh({\beta}{\energy{\hbar}}{\signal{\omega}}_i/2)}.
\end{aligned}
$$

There is no Gibbs factorial: permutations of these labeled oscillator degrees of freedom are not repeated counts of gas particles.

### Take the logarithm to find free energy

A logarithm turns the product into a sum:

$$
{\energy{F}}=-{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}{\thermal{T}}\ln\mathcal Z_N
=\sum_{i=1}^{\mechanical{N}} {\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}{\thermal{T}}\ln[2\sinh({\beta}{\energy{\hbar}}{\signal{\omega}}_i/2)]
=\sum_i{\energy{F}}_i.
$$

Equivalently:

$$
{\energy{F}}_i=\frac{{\energy{\hbar}}{\signal{\omega}}_i}{2}
+{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}{\thermal{T}}\ln(1-e^{-{\beta}{\energy{\hbar}}{\signal{\omega}}_i}).
$$

### Differentiate the sum

For temperature-independent frequencies:

$$
{\energy{U}}=\sum_i{\energy{\hbar}}{\signal{\omega}}_i\left[\frac12+\frac1{e^{{\beta}{\energy{\hbar}}{\signal{\omega}}_i}-1}\right]
=\sum_i{\energy{U}}_i,
$$

$$
{\thermal{S}}=-\left(\frac{\partial {\energy{F}}}{\partial {\thermal{T}}}\right)=\sum_i{\thermal{S}}_i,
\qquad
C=\left(\frac{\partial {\energy{U}}}{\partial {\thermal{T}}}\right)=\sum_iC_i.
$$

The energy variances also add because the independent energy cross-covariances vanish. A generalized force is additive when all terms are differentiated with respect to the same external parameter.

## Answer

The partition function is $\prod_i[2\sinh({\beta}{\energy{\hbar}}{\signal{\omega}}_i/2)]^{-1}$. Free energy, mean energy, entropy, and heat capacity are additive. Temperature and the individual oscillator frequencies are not additive quantities.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\energy{E}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Energy | The system, level, bath, or reference energy, as indicated by the subscript. |
| ${\energy{U}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Internal energy | The canonical mean energy. |
| ${\energy{F}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Helmholtz free energy | The equilibrium potential at fixed temperature, volume, and particle count. |
| ${\thermal{S}}$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Entropy | The thermodynamic or Gibbs entropy. |
| ${\thermal{T}}$ | $\unit{K}$ | Temperature | The positive absolute temperature unless a limit is stated. |
| ${\mechanical{N}}$ | 1 | Particle or subsystem count | The number of particles, localized spins, or independent oscillators. |
| ${\energy{\hbar}}$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Reduced Planck constant | Planck constant divided by 2π. |
| ${\signal{\omega}}$ | $\unit{s^{-1}}$ | Angular frequency | The oscillator angular frequency. |
| ${\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Boltzmann constant | The conversion between temperature and thermal energy. |
| ${\beta}$ | $\unit{J^{-1}}$ | Inverse thermal energy | The parameter 1/(kᴮT). |
| $\mathcal Z$ | 1 | Partition function | The dimensionless sum or integral of Boltzmann weights. |
