> [!TLDR]
> A spin in a magnetic field has two Boltzmann weights, whose sum is a hyperbolic cosine.

Consider ${\mechanical{N}}$ noninteracting spin-½ magnetic moments in a field $\mathbf {\magnetic{B}}={\magnetic{B}}\hat{\mathbf z}$. The source models the moment as $\boldsymbol\mu_i={g}{\magnetic{\mu}_{\mathrm B}}\mathbf S_i/{\energy{\hbar}}$. Then:

$$
{\energy{\mathcal H}}=-\sum_{i=1}^{\mechanical{N}}\boldsymbol\mu_i\cdot\mathbf {\magnetic{B}}
=-{\magnetic{\mu}_0}{\magnetic{B}}\sum_{i=1}^{\mechanical{N}}\sigma_i,
\qquad \sigma_i=\pm1,\qquad {\magnetic{\mu}_0}=\frac{{g}{\magnetic{\mu}_{\mathrm B}}}2.
$$

> [!note] Magnetic notation and sign
> Here ${\magnetic{\mu}_0}$ means the effective spin magnetic moment, not vacuum permeability. For the source's ${g}=2$ model, ${\magnetic{\mu}_0}={\magnetic{\mu}_{\mathrm B}}$. This is the effective two-state convention also used in Chapter 3. For an actual electron, the magnetic moment points opposite to its angular momentum; the sign must be included when identifying angular-momentum “up” with a physical magnetic moment.

The single-spin partition function is:

$$
\mathcal Z_1=\sum_{\sigma=\pm1}e^{{\beta}{\magnetic{\mu}_0}{\magnetic{B}}\sigma}
=e^{{\beta}{\magnetic{\mu}_0}{\magnetic{B}}}+e^{-{\beta}{\magnetic{\mu}_0}{\magnetic{B}}}
=2\cosh({\beta}{\magnetic{\mu}_0}{\magnetic{B}}).
$$

For independent distinguishable spin sites:

$$
\mathcal Z_N=\mathcal Z_1^{\mechanical{N}}=[2\cosh({\beta}{\magnetic{\mu}_0}{\magnetic{B}})]^{\mechanical{N}}. \tag{4.15}
$$

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\mechanical{N}}$ | 1 | Particle or subsystem count | The number of particles, localized spins, or independent oscillators. |
| ${\energy{\hbar}}$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Reduced Planck constant | Planck constant divided by 2π. |
| ${\magnetic{B}}$ | $\unit{T}$ | Magnetic flux density | The applied magnetic field in the source convention. |
| ${\magnetic{\mu}_0}$ | $\unit{J\,T^{-1}}$ | Effective spin moment | The moment magnitude used in this chapter, not vacuum permeability. |
| ${\magnetic{\mu}_{\mathrm B}}$ | $\unit{J\,T^{-1}}$ | Bohr magneton | The atomic magnetic-moment scale. |
| ${\energy{\mathcal H}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Hamiltonian | The classical energy function or quantum energy operator. |
| ${\beta}$ | $\unit{J^{-1}}$ | Inverse thermal energy | The parameter 1/(kᴮT). |
| ${g}$ | 1 | Magnetic g factor | The dimensionless factor that relates angular momentum to magnetic moment. |
| $\mathcal Z$ | 1 | Partition function | The dimensionless sum or integral of Boltzmann weights. |
| $\mathbf S_i$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Spin angular momentum | The angular momentum of spin i. |
| $\boldsymbol\mu_i$ | $\unit{J\,T^{-1}}$ | Magnetic moment | The vector moment coupled to the applied field. |
