> [!TLDR]
> The partition function normalizes probabilities and generates energy statistics.

The partition function answers a counting question: how much total statistical weight is available at the chosen temperature? Its normalization role fixes probabilities; its dependence on temperature and external constraints gives energy and response functions. Both uses require the same state space and Hamiltonian.

Read the definition before using factorization. A sum over interacting many-particle states usually cannot be replaced by a product of one-particle sums. The ideal gas will supply a case where that replacement is justified; quantum exchange statistics will set an additional boundary.
