> [!TLDR]
> Sackur–Tetrode entropy is extensive and gives the classical heat capacity, but fails at low temperature.

## Extensivity

Scale ${\energy{E}},{\mechanical{V}},{\mechanical{N}}$ by the same factor $a$. Since the ratio ${\energy{E}}/{\mechanical{N}}$ and thus temperature remain fixed:

$$
{\thermal{S}}(a{\energy{E}},a{\mechanical{V}},a{\mechanical{N}})
=a{\mechanical{N}}{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}\left[
\ln\frac{a{\mechanical{V}}}{a{\mechanical{N}}{\signal{\lambda}}_{\mathrm{th}}^3}+\frac52
\right]
=a{\thermal{S}}({\energy{E}},{\mechanical{V}},{\mechanical{N}}).
$$

Here $a$ is a mathematical scale factor. The source uses ${\signal{\lambda}}$ for this factor as well as for the thermal wavelength; a different symbol avoids ambiguity.

## Third law and the classical limit

At fixed density, ${\thermal{T}}\to0$ gives ${\signal{\lambda}}_{\mathrm{th}}\to\infty$ and the classical formula predicts ${\thermal{S}}\to-\infty$. This is a failure of the approximation, not a physical negative-infinite entropy. The degeneracy parameter becomes of order one, so quantum statistics must replace the classical formula.

## Heat capacity at constant volume

$$
{\thermal{C}}_V={\thermal{T}}\left(\frac{\partial {\thermal{S}}}{\partial {\thermal{T}}}\right)_{V,N}
=\frac32{\mechanical{N}}{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}.
$$

This agrees with the classical monatomic-gas result. The source calls this “specific heat”; ${\thermal{C}}_V$ here is the **total heat capacity**, not heat capacity per unit mass.

## Euler relation and chemical potential

Extensivity gives:

$$
{\thermal{T}}{\thermal{S}}={\energy{E}}+{\mechanical{P}}{\mechanical{V}}-{\energy{\mu}} {\mechanical{N}}.
$$

The chemical potential is:

$$
{\energy{\mu}}=-{\thermal{T}}\left(\frac{\partial {\thermal{S}}}{\partial {\mechanical{N}}}\right)_{E,V}
={\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}{\thermal{T}}\ln({\mechanical{n}}{\signal{\lambda}}_{\mathrm{th}}^3).
$$

In the classical regime, ${\mechanical{n}}{\signal{\lambda}}_{\mathrm{th}}^3\ll1$, so ${\energy{\mu}}<0$ for the energy reference used here.
