> [!TLDR]
> The ideal-gas energy constraint is a ball in momentum space.

Consider ${\mechanical{N}}$ noninteracting point particles of mass ${\mechanical{m}}$ in volume ${\mechanical{V}}$:

$$
{\energy{\mathcal H}}=\sum_{i=1}^{\mechanical{N}}\frac{\mathbf {\mechanical{p}}_i^2}{2{\mechanical{m}}}
=\frac1{2{\mechanical{m}}}\sum_{i=1}^{\mechanical{N}}\mathbf {\mechanical{p}}_i^2.
$$

The condition ${\energy{\mathcal H}}\leq {\energy{E}}$ becomes:

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{\mechanical{N}}\mathbf {\mechanical{p}}_i^2\leq2{\mechanical{m}}{\energy{E}}.
$$

Thus the $3{\mechanical{N}}$ momentum components lie in a ball of radius $\sqrt{2{\mechanical{m}}{\energy{E}}}$ in $\mathbb R^{3{\mechanical{N}}}$. The positions are free throughout the container.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\mechanical{N}}$ | $1$ | Particle count | The number of particles, spins, or distinguishable oscillators. |
| ${\mechanical{m}}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Particle mass | The mass of an atom, molecule, or electron. |
| ${\mechanical{V}}$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Volume | The physical volume of the gas or system. |
| ${\energy{\mathcal H}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Hamiltonian | The energy function of the system. |
| ${\mechanical{p}}$ | $\unit{kg\,m\,s^{-1}}$ | Translational momentum | The momentum conjugate to a Cartesian position. |
| ${\energy{E}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Energy | The system energy; subscripts identify levels or allocated energy contributions. |
