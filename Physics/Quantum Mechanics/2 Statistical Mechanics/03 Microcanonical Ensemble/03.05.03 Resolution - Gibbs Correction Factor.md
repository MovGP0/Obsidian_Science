> [!TLDR]
> The Gibbs factor removes particle-label overcounting and restores extensive entropy in the thermodynamic limit.

Gibbs proposed the correction before quantum mechanics. Divide the cumulative count by ${\mechanical{N}}!$:

$$
\Phi_{\mathrm{corr}}({\energy{E}},{\mechanical{V}},{\mechanical{N}})
=\frac{\Phi({\energy{E}},{\mechanical{V}},{\mechanical{N}})}{{\mechanical{N}}!}
=\frac{{\mechanical{V}}^{\mechanical{N}}}{{\mechanical{N}}!{\energy{h}}^{3{\mechanical{N}}}}
\frac{\pi^{3{\mechanical{N}}/2}}{\Gamma(3{\mechanical{N}}/2+1)}(2{\mechanical{m}}{\energy{E}})^{3{\mechanical{N}}/2}.
$$

The corrected entropy is:

$$
{\thermal{S}}_{\mathrm{corr}}
={\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}\ln\Phi_{\mathrm{corr}}
={\thermal{S}}_{\mathrm{nonext}}-{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}\ln({\mechanical{N}}!).
\tag{3.9}
$$

Here ${\thermal{S}}_{\mathrm{nonext}}$ is the entropy from Eq. (3.7). With $\ln {\mechanical{N}}!\approx {\mechanical{N}}\ln {\mechanical{N}}-{\mechanical{N}}$:

$$
{\thermal{S}}_{\mathrm{corr}}\approx {\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}\left[
{\mechanical{N}}\ln {\mechanical{V}}+\frac{3{\mechanical{N}}}{2}\ln\!\left(\frac{2\pi {\mechanical{m}}{\energy{E}}}{{\energy{h}}^2}\right)
-\ln\Gamma\!\left(\frac{3{\mechanical{N}}}{2}+1\right)
-{\mechanical{N}}\ln {\mechanical{N}}+{\mechanical{N}}
\right].
\tag{3.10}
$$

Substitute ${\energy{E}}=(3/2){\mechanical{N}}{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}{\thermal{T}}$ and simplify to obtain the Sackur–Tetrode formula in the next section.

> [!note] Source layout
> The printed label (3.10) is displaced onto the page after Fig. 3.3. It is attached here to the expanded corrected-entropy expression.

The quantum description treats identical particles as indistinguishable. In the dilute Maxwell–Boltzmann regime, available one-particle states are much more numerous than particles. Quantum exchange effects are then small, and the classical count with $1/{\mechanical{N}}!$ applies. Quantum-degenerate gases require Bose or Fermi statistics.

The same correction and Sackur–Tetrode form are discussed in [David Tong's classical-gas notes](https://www.damtp.cam.ac.uk/user/tong/statphys/statmechhtml/S2.html).
