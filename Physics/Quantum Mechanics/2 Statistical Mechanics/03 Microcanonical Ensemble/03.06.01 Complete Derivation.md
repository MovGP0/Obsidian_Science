> [!TLDR]
> Stirling's approximation and the thermal de Broglie wavelength reduce the corrected count to the Sackur–Tetrode equation.

Start from Eq. (3.10). Apply Stirling's approximation to both factorial factors:

$$
\ln\Gamma\!\left(\frac{3{\mechanical{N}}}{2}+1\right)
\approx\frac{3{\mechanical{N}}}{2}\ln\frac{3{\mechanical{N}}}{2}-\frac{3{\mechanical{N}}}{2}
+\frac12\ln(3\pi {\mechanical{N}}).
$$

Neglect the logarithmic term relative to terms of order ${\mechanical{N}}$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
{\thermal{S}}
&\approx {\mechanical{N}}{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}\ln {\mechanical{V}}
+\frac{3{\mechanical{N}}}{2}{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}\ln\!\left(\frac{2\pi {\mechanical{m}}{\energy{E}}}{{\energy{h}}^2}\right)
-\frac{3{\mechanical{N}}}{2}{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}\ln\frac{3{\mechanical{N}}}{2}
+\frac{3{\mechanical{N}}}{2}{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}
-{\mechanical{N}}{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}\ln {\mechanical{N}}+{\mechanical{N}}{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}\\
&={\mechanical{N}}{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}\ln\frac {\mechanical{V}}{\mechanical{N}}
+\frac{3{\mechanical{N}}}{2}{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}\ln\!\left(\frac{2\pi {\mechanical{m}}{\energy{E}}}{{\energy{h}}^2}\right)
-\frac{3{\mechanical{N}}}{2}{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}\ln\frac{3{\mechanical{N}}}{2}
+\frac{5{\mechanical{N}}}{2}{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{3.11}
$$

Combine the logarithms and substitute ${\energy{E}}=(3/2){\mechanical{N}}{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}{\thermal{T}}$:

$$
\frac{2\pi {\mechanical{m}}{\energy{E}}}{{\energy{h}}^2}\frac1{3{\mechanical{N}}/2}
=\frac{2\pi {\mechanical{m}}(3/2){\mechanical{N}}{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}{\thermal{T}}}{{\energy{h}}^2}\frac2{3{\mechanical{N}}}
=\frac{2\pi {\mechanical{m}} {\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}{\thermal{T}}}{{\energy{h}}^2}.
$$

Define the **thermal de Broglie wavelength**:

$$
{\signal{\lambda}}_{\mathrm{th}}=\frac {\energy{h}}{\sqrt{2\pi {\mechanical{m}} {\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}{\thermal{T}}}}.
\tag{3.12}
$$

Then $2\pi {\mechanical{m}} {\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}{\thermal{T}}/{\energy{h}}^2=1/{\signal{\lambda}}_{\mathrm{th}}^2$. Equation (3.11) becomes:

$$
{\thermal{S}}={\mechanical{N}}{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}\left[
\ln\!\left(\frac {\mechanical{V}}{{\mechanical{N}}{\signal{\lambda}}_{\mathrm{th}}^3}\right)+\frac52
\right].
\tag{3.13}
$$

This is the **Sackur–Tetrode equation**, independently developed by Hugo Martin Tetrode and Otto Sackur in 1912.

> [!note] Remark 3.5 — Degeneracy parameter
> The number density is ${\mechanical{n}}={\mechanical{N}}/{\mechanical{V}}$. The scale ${\signal{\lambda}}_{\mathrm{th}}^3$ is a quantum volume per particle. Classical dilute-gas statistics require ${\mechanical{n}}{\signal{\lambda}}_{\mathrm{th}}^3\ll1$: this quantum volume must be much smaller than the available volume per particle.
