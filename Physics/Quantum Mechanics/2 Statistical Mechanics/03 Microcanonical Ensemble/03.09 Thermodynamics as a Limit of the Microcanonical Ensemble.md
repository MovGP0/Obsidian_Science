> [!TLDR]
> Thermodynamics follows when a large-system limit suppresses relative fluctuations and removes leading ensemble differences.

A large particle count alone does not remove every fluctuation. One must state what grows, what remains fixed, and which observable is averaged. A total energy fixed by the ensemble has zero variance even before any limit, whereas a local observable can fluctuate.

Use the limit note for the scaling assumptions, the fluctuation note for the difference between global constraints and local measurements, and the information note for the probability expression behind the entropy.
