> [!TLDR]
> Permuting labels on identical particles does not create a different physical configuration.

The count used in Eq. (3.4) treats particles as distinguishable. It counts configurations that differ only by an exchange of labels as different states.

For identical particles, these permutations do not give new physical configurations. The classical dilute-gas count must therefore be divided by ${\mechanical{N}}!$, the number of label permutations.
