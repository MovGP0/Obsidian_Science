> [!TLDR]
> A macrostate uses a few bulk quantities; a microstate specifies every position and momentum.

Consider ${\mechanical{N}}$ point particles in volume ${\mechanical{V}}$, with total energy ${\energy{E}}$. The triple $({\energy{E}},{\mechanical{V}},{\mechanical{N}})$ specifies the macrostate. A microscopic state is one point in a $6{\mechanical{N}}$-dimensional phase space:

$$
\Gamma=(\mathbf {\mechanical{q}}_1,\ldots,\mathbf {\mechanical{q}}_N;\mathbf {\mechanical{p}}_1,\ldots,\mathbf {\mechanical{p}}_N)
\in\text{phase space}\subset\mathbb R^{6{\mechanical{N}}}.
$$

Here the source uses $\Gamma$ for a phase-space point as well as for phase space in other sections. Its meaning follows from the context.

The point $\Gamma({\signal{t}})$ evolves according to Hamilton's equations:

$$
\dot {\mechanical{q}}_i^\alpha=\frac{\partial{\energy{\mathcal H}}}{\partial {\mechanical{p}}_i^\alpha},
\qquad
\dot {\mechanical{p}}_i^\alpha=-\frac{\partial{\energy{\mathcal H}}}{\partial {\mechanical{q}}_i^\alpha},
\qquad i=1,\ldots,{\mechanical{N}},\quad\alpha=x,y,z.
$$

## Definition 3.1 — Gibbs ensemble

An **ensemble** is an imagined collection of infinitely many systems. They have the same macroscopic values $({\energy{E}},{\mechanical{V}},{\mechanical{N}})$, but different microscopic initial states.

A probability density $\rho(\Gamma,{\signal{t}})$ describes the ensemble:

$$
\int\rho(\Gamma,{\signal{t}})\,\mathrm d^{6{\mechanical{N}}}\Gamma=1.
$$

The ensemble mean of an observable $A(\Gamma)$ is:

$$
\langle A\rangle({\signal{t}})=\int A(\Gamma)\rho(\Gamma,{\signal{t}})\,\mathrm d^{6{\mechanical{N}}}\Gamma.
$$

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\mechanical{N}}$ | $1$ | Particle count | The number of particles, spins, or distinguishable oscillators. |
| ${\mechanical{V}}$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Volume | The physical volume of the gas or system. |
| ${\energy{E}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Energy | The system energy; subscripts identify levels or allocated energy contributions. |
| ${\mechanical{q}}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Position coordinate | A Cartesian position; generalized angles are listed separately. |
| ${\mechanical{p}}$ | $\unit{kg\,m\,s^{-1}}$ | Translational momentum | The momentum conjugate to a Cartesian position. |
| ${\signal{t}}$ | $\unit{s}$ | Time | The time along a microscopic trajectory. |
| ${\energy{\mathcal H}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Hamiltonian | The energy function of the system. |
| $\rho$ | $\unit{(J\,s)^{-3N}}$ | Phase-space probability density | The unit depends on whether raw or reduced phase-space volume is used. |
| $A$ | $—$ | Observable | A measured quantity with a unit determined by the chosen observable. |
