> [!TLDR]
> Position integration gives a factor of volume per particle; momentum integration gives a high-dimensional ball volume.

Initially omit $1/{\mechanical{N}}!$. We will add that correction when particle indistinguishability is discussed.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Phi({\energy{E}})
&=\frac1{{\energy{h}}^{3{\mechanical{N}}}}\int_{\mathcal H\leq E}\mathrm d^{3{\mechanical{N}}}{\mechanical{q}}\,\mathrm d^{3{\mechanical{N}}}{\mechanical{p}}\\
&=\frac1{{\energy{h}}^{3{\mechanical{N}}}}
\left(\int_V\mathrm d^3{\mechanical{q}}\right)^{\mechanical{N}}
\int_{\sum_i\mathbf p_i^2\leq2mE}\mathrm d^{3{\mechanical{N}}}{\mechanical{p}}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{3.4}
$$

Each position integral gives ${\mechanical{V}}$. The remaining momentum integral is:

$$
\int_{\sum_i\mathbf p_i^2\leq2mE}\mathrm d^{3{\mechanical{N}}}{\mechanical{p}}
={\mechanical{V}}_{3N}\!\left(\sqrt{2{\mechanical{m}}{\energy{E}}}\right)
=\frac{\pi^{3{\mechanical{N}}/2}}{\Gamma(3{\mechanical{N}}/2+1)}(2{\mechanical{m}}{\energy{E}})^{3{\mechanical{N}}/2}.
$$

Therefore:

$$
\Phi({\energy{E}})=\frac{{\mechanical{V}}^{\mechanical{N}}}{{\energy{h}}^{3{\mechanical{N}}}}\,
\frac{\pi^{3{\mechanical{N}}/2}}{\Gamma(3{\mechanical{N}}/2+1)}(2{\mechanical{m}}{\energy{E}})^{3{\mechanical{N}}/2}.
\tag{3.5}
$$

The function ${\mechanical{V}}_{3N}$ in the intermediate step denotes a mathematical ball volume, not the container volume.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\energy{E}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Energy | The system energy; subscripts identify levels or allocated energy contributions. |
| ${\mechanical{V}}$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Volume | The physical volume of the gas or system. |
| ${\mechanical{N}}$ | $1$ | Particle count | The number of particles, spins, or distinguishable oscillators. |
| ${\mechanical{m}}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Particle mass | The mass of an atom, molecule, or electron. |
| ${\energy{h}}$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Planck constant | The elementary action scale in the state-counting measure. |
| ${\energy{\mathcal H}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Hamiltonian | The energy function of the system. |
| ${\mechanical{q}}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Position coordinate | A Cartesian position; generalized angles are listed separately. |
| ${\mechanical{p}}$ | $\unit{kg\,m\,s^{-1}}$ | Translational momentum | The momentum conjugate to a Cartesian position. |
