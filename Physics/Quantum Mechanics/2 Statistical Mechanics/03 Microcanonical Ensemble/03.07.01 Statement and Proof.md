> [!TLDR]
> Gaussian integration gives a mean energy of kᴮT/2 for each unrestricted quadratic term.

## Theorem 3.1 — Equipartition of energy

Let ${\energy{\mathcal H}}={\energy{\mathcal H}}_0+a {\mechanical{x}}_i^2$, where ${\mechanical{x}}_i$ is a phase-space variable, $a>0$, and ${\energy{\mathcal H}}_0$ is independent of ${\mechanical{x}}_i$. In the canonical ensemble:

$$
\langle a {\mechanical{x}}_i^2\rangle=\frac12{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}{\thermal{T}}.
$$

The result also applies to macroscopic microcanonical systems when the ensembles are equivalent.

## Proof

The canonical mean of an observable is:

$$
\langle A\rangle
=\frac{\int A e^{-\beta{\energy{\mathcal H}}}\,\mathrm d\Gamma}{Z},
\qquad \beta=\frac1{{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}{\thermal{T}}}.
$$

Integrations over the variables in ${\energy{\mathcal H}}_0$ cancel between numerator and denominator. The remaining Gaussian integrals give:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\langle a {\mechanical{x}}_i^2\rangle
&=\frac{\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}a {\mechanical{x}}_i^2e^{-\beta a {\mechanical{x}}_i^2}\,\mathrm d{\mechanical{x}}_i}
{\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}e^{-\beta a {\mechanical{x}}_i^2}\,\mathrm d{\mechanical{x}}_i}\\
&=\frac{\displaystyle\frac a2\sqrt{\frac{\pi}{(\beta a)^3}}}
{\displaystyle\sqrt{\frac\pi{\beta a}}}
=\frac a{2\beta a}
=\frac1{2\beta}
=\frac12{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}{\thermal{T}}.
\end{aligned}
$$

This proves the result. $\square$

> [!note] Remark 3.6 — Integration domain
> The Gaussian proof assumes that the quadratic variable ranges over the full real line, or that physical boundaries lie far enough into the Gaussian tails to be negligible. A bounded variable does not automatically satisfy this result.
>
> Here ${\mechanical{x}}_i$ can be a position or momentum, so its unit depends on the case. The coefficient has the reciprocal squared-variable unit needed to make $a {\mechanical{x}}_i^2$ an energy.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\energy{\mathcal H}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Hamiltonian | The energy function of the system. |
| ${\thermal{T}}$ | $\unit{K}$ | Temperature | The thermodynamic temperature. |
| ${\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Boltzmann constant | The conversion between microscopic counting and thermodynamic entropy. |
| $\beta$ | $\unit{J^{-1}}$ | Inverse thermal energy | The canonical parameter 1/(kᴮT). |
| $A$ | $—$ | Observable | A measured quantity with a unit determined by the chosen observable. |
| ${\mechanical{x}}_i$ | $—$ | Quadratic phase-space variable | A position or momentum variable; its unit depends on the example. |
| $a$ | $—$ | Quadratic energy coefficient | Its unit makes a times xᵢ² an energy. |
