## Question — Exercise 3.2

Starting from Sackur–Tetrode Eq. (3.13), calculate pressure and chemical potential. Verify ${\energy{E}}={\thermal{T}}{\thermal{S}}-{\mechanical{P}}{\mechanical{V}}+{\energy{\mu}} {\mechanical{N}}$.

> [!note] Solution origin
> The question is translated from the supplied notes. The worked derivation is added.

## Calculation

Write entropy in its energy form:

$$
{\thermal{S}}={\mechanical{N}}{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}\left[
\ln\frac {\mechanical{V}}{\mechanical{N}}+\frac32\ln\!\left(\frac{4\pi {\mechanical{m}}{\energy{E}}}{3{\mechanical{N}}{\energy{h}}^2}\right)+\frac52
\right].
$$

**Why use this form?** The chemical-potential derivative holds energy, not temperature, fixed. This expression makes that condition explicit.

### Pressure

Only $\ln {\mechanical{V}}$ depends on volume at fixed energy and count:

$$
\frac {\mechanical{P}}{\thermal{T}}=\left(\frac{\partial {\thermal{S}}}{\partial {\mechanical{V}}}\right)_{E,N}
=\frac{{\mechanical{N}}{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}}{\mechanical{V}},
\qquad {\mechanical{P}}=\frac{{\mechanical{N}}{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}{\thermal{T}}}{\mechanical{V}}.
$$

### Chemical potential

Let the square bracket be $b$. At fixed energy and volume, its derivative is $-5/(2{\mechanical{N}})$:

$$
\left(\frac{\partial {\thermal{S}}}{\partial {\mechanical{N}}}\right)_{E,V}
={\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}b+{\mechanical{N}}{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}\left(-\frac5{2{\mechanical{N}}}\right)
={\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}\left[
\ln\frac {\mechanical{V}}{\mechanical{N}}+\frac32\ln\!\left(\frac{4\pi {\mechanical{m}}{\energy{E}}}{3{\mechanical{N}}{\energy{h}}^2}\right)
\right].
$$

Use ${\energy{E}}=(3/2){\mechanical{N}}{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}{\thermal{T}}$ and ${\signal{\lambda}}_{\mathrm{th}}={\energy{h}}/\sqrt{2\pi {\mechanical{m}} {\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}{\thermal{T}}}$:

$$
{\energy{\mu}}=-{\thermal{T}}\left(\frac{\partial {\thermal{S}}}{\partial {\mechanical{N}}}\right)_{E,V}
=-{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}{\thermal{T}}\ln\frac {\mechanical{V}}{{\mechanical{N}}{\signal{\lambda}}_{\mathrm{th}}^3}
={\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}{\thermal{T}}\ln({\mechanical{n}}{\signal{\lambda}}_{\mathrm{th}}^3),
\qquad {\mechanical{n}}=\frac {\mechanical{N}}{\mechanical{V}}.
$$

### Euler relation

Set $b_0=\ln[{\mechanical{V}}/({\mechanical{N}}{\signal{\lambda}}_{\mathrm{th}}^3)]$. Then:

$$
\begin{aligned}
{\thermal{T}}{\thermal{S}}-{\mechanical{P}}{\mechanical{V}}+{\energy{\mu}} {\mechanical{N}}
&={\mechanical{N}}{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}{\thermal{T}}(b_0+5/2)-{\mechanical{N}}{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}{\thermal{T}}-{\mechanical{N}}{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}{\thermal{T}} b_0\\
&=\frac32{\mechanical{N}}{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}{\thermal{T}}={\energy{E}}.
\end{aligned}
$$

## Answer

$$
{\mechanical{P}}=\frac{{\mechanical{N}}{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}{\thermal{T}}}{\mechanical{V}},\qquad
{\energy{\mu}}={\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}{\thermal{T}}\ln({\mechanical{n}}{\signal{\lambda}}_{\mathrm{th}}^3),
\qquad {\energy{E}}={\thermal{T}}{\thermal{S}}-{\mechanical{P}}{\mechanical{V}}+{\energy{\mu}} {\mechanical{N}}.
$$
