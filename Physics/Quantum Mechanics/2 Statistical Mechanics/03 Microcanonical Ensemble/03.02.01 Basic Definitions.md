> [!TLDR]
> The density of states is the derivative of the cumulative state count with respect to energy.

## Definition 3.3 — Cumulative volume and density of states

The dimensionless cumulative phase-space volume counts states with energy at most ${\energy{E}}$:

$$
\Phi({\energy{E}})=\int_{\mathcal H(\Gamma)\leq E}\frac{\mathrm d^{6{\mechanical{N}}}\Gamma}{{\energy{h}}^{3{\mechanical{N}}}}.
$$

The **density of states**, also called the level density, is:

$$
\omega({\energy{E}})=\frac{\mathrm d\Phi}{\mathrm d{\energy{E}}}
=\int\frac{\mathrm d^{6{\mechanical{N}}}\Gamma}{{\energy{h}}^{3{\mechanical{N}}}}\,
\delta_{\mathrm D}({\energy{\mathcal H}}(\Gamma)-{\energy{E}}).
$$

The notation $\delta_{\mathrm D}$ identifies the Dirac distribution and distinguishes it from the shell width ${\energy{\delta}}$.

The exact shell count is:

$$
\Omega({\energy{E}},{\mechanical{V}},{\mechanical{N}};{\energy{\delta}})=\Phi({\energy{E}})-\Phi({\energy{E}}-{\energy{\delta}}).
$$

If the density changes little over the chosen interval:

$$
\Omega({\energy{E}},{\mechanical{V}},{\mechanical{N}};{\energy{\delta}})\approx\omega({\energy{E}}){\energy{\delta}}.
$$

> [!note] Remark 3.2
> For a macroscopic system, these functions grow very rapidly with energy. An ideal monatomic gas has $\Phi({\energy{E}})\propto {\energy{E}}^{3{\mechanical{N}}/2}$ and $\Phi({\energy{E}})=[2{\energy{E}}/(3{\mechanical{N}})]\omega({\energy{E}})$. Most of a high-dimensional volume lies near its boundary. With a suitably narrow energy window, the entropy definitions differ only by subextensive terms, usually logarithmic in system size.
>
> The linear approximation to the shell count additionally requires the window to be small on the variation scale of the density. The condition ${\energy{\delta}}\ll {\energy{E}}$ alone does not guarantee this when ${\mechanical{N}}$ is large.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\energy{E}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Energy | The system energy; subscripts identify levels or allocated energy contributions. |
| ${\mechanical{V}}$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Volume | The physical volume of the gas or system. |
| ${\mechanical{N}}$ | $1$ | Particle count | The number of particles, spins, or distinguishable oscillators. |
| ${\energy{\mathcal H}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Hamiltonian | The energy function of the system. |
| ${\energy{h}}$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Planck constant | The elementary action scale in the state-counting measure. |
| ${\energy{\delta}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Energy-window width | The small energy interval that defines the shell. |
| $\omega({\energy{E}})$ | $\unit{J^{-1}}$ | Density of states | The energy derivative of the dimensionless cumulative count. |
