> [!TLDR]
> Counting the ideal gas's accessible momenta gives its energy–temperature relation and equation of state.

The model is a dilute monatomic gas with translational kinetic energy only. The walls confine the particles, while particle interactions do not contribute to the count. Classical continuum counting and the large-particle-number approximation are separate assumptions.

First factor the position and momentum integrations. Then differentiate the exact count before taking logarithms. The final two notes show why correct pressure and temperature alone do not prove that the entropy has the correct particle-count dependence: the omitted factorial is tested in the Gibbs-paradox section.
