> [!TLDR]
> Uniform probabilities reduce Gibbs information entropy to the Boltzmann formula.

The general discrete Gibbs, or thermodynamic information-entropy, expression is:

$$
{\thermal{S}}=-{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}\sum_i p_i\ln p_i.
\tag{3.18}
$$

The sum runs over microstates. In the microcanonical ensemble, $p_i=1/\Omega$ for each accessible state:

$$
{\thermal{S}}=-{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}\Omega\frac1\Omega\ln\frac1\Omega
={\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}\ln\Omega.
$$

Shannon independently developed the related information-theory formula in 1948. These expressions quantify missing information about the microscopic state.

> [!note] Units and interpretation
> Shannon entropy written with base-two logarithms is measured in bits. The thermodynamic expression uses natural logarithms and the factor ${\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}$. Equating them requires the probability distribution, state description, and logarithm convention to be specified.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\thermal{S}}$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Entropy | The total thermodynamic entropy or its stated statistical approximation. |
| ${\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Boltzmann constant | The conversion between microscopic counting and thermodynamic entropy. |
| $p_i$ | $1$ | Microstate probability | The probability assigned to an accessible microstate. |
