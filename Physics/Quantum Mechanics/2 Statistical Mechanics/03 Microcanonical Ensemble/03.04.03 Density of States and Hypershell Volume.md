> [!TLDR]
> Differentiating the ideal-gas state count gives a density proportional to energy raised to one lower power.

Differentiate Eq. (3.5):

$$
\omega({\energy{E}})=\frac{\mathrm d\Phi}{\mathrm d{\energy{E}}}
=\frac{{\mechanical{V}}^{\mechanical{N}}}{{\energy{h}}^{3{\mechanical{N}}}}\frac{\pi^{3{\mechanical{N}}/2}}{\Gamma(3{\mechanical{N}}/2+1)}
\frac{3{\mechanical{N}}}{2}(2{\mechanical{m}})^{3{\mechanical{N}}/2}{\energy{E}}^{3{\mechanical{N}}/2-1}.
\tag{3.6}
$$

Thus:

$$
\omega({\energy{E}})=\frac{3{\mechanical{N}}}{2{\energy{E}}}\Phi({\energy{E}}).
$$

The cumulative count and density differ by an energy-dependent prefactor. After multiplication of the density by an energy interval, the resulting entropy differs from the volume entropy only by subextensive terms in the appropriate macroscopic limit.

For a narrow shell, $\Omega=\Phi({\energy{E}})-\Phi({\energy{E}}-{\energy{\delta}})\approx\omega({\energy{E}}){\energy{\delta}}$, subject to the small-window condition in [[03.02.01 Basic Definitions]].
