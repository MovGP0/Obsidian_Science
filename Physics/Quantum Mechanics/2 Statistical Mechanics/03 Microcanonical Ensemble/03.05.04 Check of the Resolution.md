> [!TLDR]
> With the Gibbs correction, combining identical gases at equal conditions gives zero macroscopic mixing entropy.

Repeat the mixing calculation. In the corrected entropy, the volume and particle-count terms combine as:

$$
{\mechanical{N}}\ln {\mechanical{V}}-{\mechanical{N}}\ln {\mechanical{N}}={\mechanical{N}}\ln({\mechanical{V}}/{\mechanical{N}}).
$$

The large-system expression gives:

$$
\begin{aligned}
{\thermal{S}}(2{\energy{E}},2{\mechanical{V}},2{\mechanical{N}})
&=2{\mechanical{N}}{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}\left[
\ln\frac{2{\mechanical{V}}}{2{\mechanical{N}}}
+\frac32\ln\!\left(\frac{4\pi {\mechanical{m}}{\energy{E}}}{3{\mechanical{N}}{\energy{h}}^2}\right)+\frac52
\right]\\
&=2{\mechanical{N}}{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}\left[
\ln\frac {\mechanical{V}}{\mechanical{N}}+\frac32\ln\!\left(\frac{4\pi {\mechanical{m}}{\energy{E}}}{3{\mechanical{N}}{\energy{h}}^2}\right)+\frac52
\right]\\
&=2{\thermal{S}}({\energy{E}},{\mechanical{V}},{\mechanical{N}}).
\end{aligned}
$$

Entropy is extensive, and $\Delta {\thermal{S}}=0$ for mixing identical gases at the same macroscopic conditions.

> [!note] Thermodynamic-limit qualification
> This equality concerns the extensive thermodynamic entropy. Exact finite-system factorials and imposed particle-number constraints can leave subextensive corrections. The false extensive term $2{\mechanical{N}}{\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}\ln2$ is removed.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\thermal{S}}$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Entropy | The total thermodynamic entropy or its stated statistical approximation. |
| ${\energy{E}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Energy | The system energy; subscripts identify levels or allocated energy contributions. |
| ${\mechanical{V}}$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Volume | The physical volume of the gas or system. |
| ${\mechanical{N}}$ | $1$ | Particle count | The number of particles, spins, or distinguishable oscillators. |
| ${\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Boltzmann constant | The conversion between microscopic counting and thermodynamic entropy. |
| ${\mechanical{m}}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Particle mass | The mass of an atom, molecule, or electron. |
| ${\energy{h}}$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Planck constant | The elementary action scale in the state-counting measure. |
