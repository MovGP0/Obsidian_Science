> [!TLDR]
> The exact two-dimensional Ising exponents satisfy the Rushbrooke, Fisher, Josephson, and Widom scaling relations.

## Question

**Exercise 10.4 — Check the scaling relations.** Verify the Rushbrooke and Fisher relations for the exact two-dimensional Ising model:

$$\alpha=0,\quad\beta=\frac18,\quad\gamma=\frac74,\quad\nu=1,\quad\eta=\frac14.$$

## Calculations and explanation

These exponents are dimensionless numbers. Substitute them directly into each relation. The value $\alpha=0$ denotes a logarithmic heat-capacity singularity for this model.

### Rushbrooke relation

$$\alpha+2\beta+\gamma=0+2\left(\frac18\right)+\frac74
=\frac14+\frac74=2.$$

Thus, the scaling equality is satisfied.

### Fisher relation

$$\nu(2-\eta)=1\left(2-\frac14\right)=\frac74=\gamma.$$

This checks the link between the susceptibility and the spatial correlations.

### Josephson relation — additional source check

With spatial dimension $d=2$:

$$\nu d=1\cdot2=2=2-\alpha.$$

The model lies below its upper critical dimension, so this hyperscaling check applies.

### Widom relation — additional check

The other exponents predict

$$\delta=1+\frac{\gamma}{\beta}=1+\frac{7/4}{1/8}=15,$$

which is the exact critical-isotherm exponent.

## Solution

All checked scaling relations hold. The first two answer the question; the Josephson check is also given in the source solution.
