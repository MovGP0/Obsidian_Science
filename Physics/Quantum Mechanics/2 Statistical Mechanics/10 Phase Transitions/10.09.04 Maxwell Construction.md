> [!TLDR]
> The Maxwell construction replaces the unstable loop by liquid–gas coexistence at constant pressure.

For ${\thermal{T}}<{\thermal{T}}_c$, the uncorrected van der Waals isotherm is not monotonic. Its segment with positive pressure–volume slope has negative isothermal compressibility and is mechanically unstable.

The equilibrium isotherm contains a horizontal coexistence segment. Its pressure satisfies the equal-area condition

$$\int_{v_L}^{{\mechanical{v}}_G}{\mechanical{P}}({\mechanical{v}},{\thermal{T}})\,d{\mechanical{v}}={\mechanical{P}}_{\mathrm{coex}}({\mechanical{v}}_G-{\mechanical{v}}_L).$$

The endpoints are the coexisting liquid and gas molar volumes. This construction is equivalent to equality of the chemical potentials:

$${\energy{\mu}}_L({\thermal{T}},{\mechanical{P}}_{\mathrm{coex}})={\energy{\mu}}_G({\thermal{T}},{\mechanical{P}}_{\mathrm{coex}}).$$

See [[09.02.06 Maxwell Construction]] for the thermodynamic proof, and [[10.04.04 P–V Diagram with Isotherms]] for the equilibrium isotherms.
