> [!TLDR]
> A negative quartic term and a positive sixth-order term can produce a discontinuous transition.

Consider

$${\energy{F}}={\energy{F}}_0+{\energy{a}}{\mechanical{\phi}}^2+{\energy{b}}{\mechanical{\phi}}^4+{\energy{c}}{\mechanical{\phi}}^6,\qquad {\energy{b}}<0,\quad {\energy{c}}>0.$$

The sixth-order term keeps the free energy bounded below. Let $x={\mechanical{\phi}}^2\geq0$. A nonzero stationary point must satisfy

$${\energy{a}}+2{\energy{b}}x+3{\energy{c}}x^2=0.$$

Nonzero stationary points first appear when the discriminant vanishes:

$${\energy{a}}_{\mathrm{sp,upper}}=\frac{{\energy{b}}^2}{3{\energy{c}}}.$$

One of the resulting branches is a local minimum. This ordered minimum is initially metastable: its free energy exceeds that at the origin.

At coexistence, the ordered and disordered minima have equal free energy. Therefore solve both

$${\energy{a}}+2{\energy{b}}x+3{\energy{c}}x^2=0,\qquad {\energy{a}}+{\energy{b}}x+{\energy{c}}x^2=0.$$

Subtract the second equation from the first. For $x\ne0$, this gives ${\energy{b}}+2{\energy{c}}x=0$. Consequently,

$${\mechanical{\phi}}_0^2=-\frac{{\energy{b}}}{2{\energy{c}}},\qquad {\energy{a}}_{\mathrm{coex}}=\frac{{\energy{b}}^2}{4{\energy{c}}}.$$

The order parameter jumps from zero to a nonzero magnitude at coexistence. The origin remains a local minimum until ${\energy{a}}=0$, its lower spinodal.

> [!warning] Source correction — temperature order
> The source states that coexistence occurs above the temperature where the metastable ordered minima appear. For ${\energy{a}}={\energy{a}}_0({\thermal{T}}-{\thermal{T}}_{\mathrm{ref}})$ with ${\energy{a}}_0>0$, the equations give the reverse order during cooling:
> $${\thermal{T}}_{\mathrm{sp,upper}}>{\thermal{T}}_{\mathrm{coex}}>{\thermal{T}}_{\mathrm{sp,lower}}.$$
> The source's symbol ${\thermal{T}}^*$ is ambiguous unless the specific spinodal is identified.

Metastability permits hysteresis. A small disturbance can leave the system in a local minimum until nucleation or loss of local stability causes the transition.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\energy{F}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Free energy | Thermodynamic or Landau free energy as specified in the text. |
| ${\thermal{T}}$ | $\unit{K}$ | Temperature | Absolute temperature of the system. |
| ${\mechanical{\phi}}$ | $\unit{1}$ | Order parameter | Dimensionless normalized order parameter used in the generic Landau model. |
| ${\energy{a}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Quadratic Landau coefficient | Coefficient of the squared dimensionless order parameter. |
| ${\energy{a}}_0$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Landau temperature coefficient | Slope of the quadratic coefficient with temperature. |
| ${\energy{b}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Quartic Landau coefficient | Coefficient of the fourth power of the dimensionless order parameter. |
| ${\energy{c}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Sixth-order Landau coefficient | Positive stabilizing coefficient when the quartic coefficient is negative. |
