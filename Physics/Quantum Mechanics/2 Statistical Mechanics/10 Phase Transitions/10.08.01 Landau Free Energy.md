> [!TLDR]
> Expand the free energy in powers of a small order parameter. Symmetry restricts the allowed terms.

Landau proposed a phenomenological theory based on a free-energy expansion near the transition, where the order parameter is small:

$$
{\energy{F}}({\thermal{T}},{\mechanical{\phi}})={\energy{F}}_0({\thermal{T}})+{\energy{a}}({\thermal{T}}){\mechanical{\phi}}^2+{\energy{b}}({\thermal{T}}){\mechanical{\phi}}^4+{\energy{c}}({\thermal{T}}){\mechanical{\phi}}^6+\cdots .
\tag{10.2}
$$

Odd powers are absent when the system is invariant under ${\mechanical{\phi}}\mapsto-{\mechanical{\phi}}$. For stability at large $|{\mechanical{\phi}}|$, the highest retained even power must have a positive coefficient.

- If the expansion stops at fourth order, require ${\energy{b}}>0$.
- If ${\energy{b}}<0$, retain the sixth-order term with ${\energy{c}}>0$.

The equilibrium state minimizes ${\energy{F}}$ with respect to ${\mechanical{\phi}}$, at the imposed temperature and other control variables. The coefficients contain the microscopic information that this description does not resolve.

> [!note] Scope of the model
> Here ${\mechanical{\phi}}$ is dimensionless and ${\energy{F}}$ is a total free energy. Thus ${\energy{a}}$, ${\energy{b}}$, and ${\energy{c}}$ have energy units. A free-energy density convention gives different coefficient units. Use one convention throughout a calculation.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\energy{F}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Free energy | Thermodynamic or Landau free energy as specified in the text. |
| ${\thermal{T}}$ | $\unit{K}$ | Temperature | Absolute temperature of the system. |
| ${\mechanical{\phi}}$ | $\unit{1}$ | Order parameter | Dimensionless normalized order parameter used in the generic Landau model. |
| ${\energy{a}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Quadratic Landau coefficient | Coefficient of the squared dimensionless order parameter. |
| ${\energy{b}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Quartic Landau coefficient | Coefficient of the fourth power of the dimensionless order parameter. |
| ${\energy{c}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Sixth-order Landau coefficient | Positive stabilizing coefficient when the quartic coefficient is negative. |
