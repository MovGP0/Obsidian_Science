> [!TLDR]
> All van der Waals fluids obey the same equation in reduced variables.

Equation (10.4) contains no parameters specific to a gas. Fluids at the same reduced pressure, volume, and temperature are in corresponding states. This is the **law of corresponding states** within the van der Waals model.

The model predicts a universal reduced equation of state. For real substances, corresponding-states behavior is approximate. Microscopic details and additional molecular parameters can cause deviations.

See [[10.09.01 van der Waals Equation]] for the reduced variables, and [[10.07.03 Universality and Universality Classes]] for critical universality.
