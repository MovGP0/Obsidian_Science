> [!TLDR]
> The renormalization group connects scale invariance, universality, and critical exponents.

Wilson developed the modern renormalization-group framework in the 1970s. It explains why systems with different microscopic structures can have the same critical behavior. It also gives a systematic method to calculate critical exponents.
