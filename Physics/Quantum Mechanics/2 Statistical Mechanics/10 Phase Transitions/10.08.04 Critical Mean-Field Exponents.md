> [!TLDR]
> The quartic Landau model gives β = 1/2, γ = 1, δ = 3, and α = 0.

## Example 10.4 — Critical mean-field exponents

Use the Landau expansion (10.2), with ${\energy{a}}={\energy{a}}_0({\thermal{T}}-{\thermal{T}}_c)={\energy{a}}_0{\thermal{T}}_ct$, ${\energy{b}}>0$, and $t=({\thermal{T}}-{\thermal{T}}_c)/{\thermal{T}}_c$.

**Order parameter.** Equation (10.3) gives $|{\mechanical{\phi}}|\propto(-t)^{1/2}$ below the transition. Therefore $\beta_{\mathrm{MF}}=1/2$.

**Susceptibility.** Add a conjugate field term $-{\energy{h}}{\mechanical{\phi}}$. Invert the curvature of the zero-field free energy at the selected minimum:

$$
{\magnetic{\chi}}=\begin{cases}
\displaystyle\frac{1}{2{\energy{a}}_0{\thermal{T}}_ct},&t>0,\\
\displaystyle\frac{1}{4{\energy{a}}_0{\thermal{T}}_c|t|},&t<0.
\end{cases}
$$

The amplitudes differ on the two sides. Both branches scale as $|t|^{-1}$, so $\gamma_{\mathrm{MF}}=1$.

**Critical isotherm.** At ${\thermal{T}}={\thermal{T}}_c$, the quadratic coefficient is zero. Minimization of ${\energy{F}}-{\energy{F}}_0={\energy{b}}{\mechanical{\phi}}^4-{\energy{h}}{\mechanical{\phi}}$ gives $4{\energy{b}}{\mechanical{\phi}}^3={\energy{h}}$. Thus ${\mechanical{\phi}}\propto {\energy{h}}^{1/3}$ and $\delta_{\mathrm{MF}}=3$.

**Heat capacity.** The heat capacity has the finite jump derived in [[10.08.02 Second-Order Transition]]. In exponent notation, $\alpha_{\mathrm{MF}}=0$.

**Rushbrooke check:** $\alpha+2\beta+\gamma=0+2(1/2)+1=2$.

**Widom check:** $\delta=1+\gamma/\beta=1+1/(1/2)=3$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\energy{F}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Free energy | Thermodynamic or Landau free energy as specified in the text. |
| ${\thermal{T}}$ | $\unit{K}$ | Temperature | Absolute temperature of the system. |
| ${\thermal{T}}_c$ | $\unit{K}$ | Critical temperature | Temperature of the stated phase transition. |
| ${\mechanical{\phi}}$ | $\unit{1}$ | Order parameter | Dimensionless normalized order parameter used in the generic Landau model. |
| ${\energy{a}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Quadratic Landau coefficient | Coefficient of the squared dimensionless order parameter. |
| ${\energy{a}}_0$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Landau temperature coefficient | Slope of the quadratic coefficient with temperature. |
| ${\energy{b}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Quartic Landau coefficient | Coefficient of the fourth power of the dimensionless order parameter. |
| ${\energy{h}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Conjugate field | Field coupled linearly to a dimensionless order parameter. |
| ${\magnetic{\chi}}$ | $\unit{J^{-1}}$ | Reduced susceptibility | Response of a dimensionless order parameter to an energy-like field. |
| ${\thermal{C}}$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Heat capacity | Total heat capacity under the stated constraint. |
