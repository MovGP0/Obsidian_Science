> [!TLDR]
> The Ehrenfest classification uses the first thermodynamic derivative that becomes nonanalytic.

Paul Ehrenfest proposed this classification in 1933.

**Definition 10.1 — Ehrenfest classification.** A transition has order $n$ if derivatives of a thermodynamic potential, such as the Gibbs free energy, are continuous below order $n$, while a derivative of order $n$ is discontinuous or singular.
