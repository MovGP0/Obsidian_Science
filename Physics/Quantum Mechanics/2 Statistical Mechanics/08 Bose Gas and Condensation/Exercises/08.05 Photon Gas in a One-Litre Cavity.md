## Question — Exercise 8.5

A photon gas is in equilibrium at ${\thermal{T}}=3000\,\unit{K}$ in a cavity with volume ${\mechanical{V}}=1\,\unit{L}$. Calculate:

1. The total radiation energy ${\energy{U}}$.
2. The radiation pressure ${\mechanical{P}}_{\mathrm{rad}}$.
3. The mean photon number $\langle {\mechanical{N}}_\gamma\rangle$. Use

$$
\langle {\mechanical{N}}_\gamma\rangle=\frac{2\zeta(3)}{\pi^2}
{\mechanical{V}}\left(\frac{{\thermal{k}}_B{\thermal{T}}}{{\energy{\hbar}} {\signal{c}}}\right)^3.
$$

## Calculation

### Prepare the SI values

We convert the cavity volume before substitution:

$$
{\mechanical{V}}=1\,\unit{L}=10^{-3}\,\unit{m^3}.
$$

Use ${\thermal{k}}_B=1.380649\times10^{-23}\,\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$, ${\energy{\hbar}}=1.05457182\times10^{-34}\,\unit{J\,s}$, ${\signal{c}}=2.99792458\times10^8\,\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$, and $\zeta(3)=1.20205690$.

### Part (a) — Total energy

Integration of the Planck spectrum gives the radiation constant

$$
{\energy{a}}_R=\frac{\pi^2{\thermal{k}}_B^4}{15{\energy{\hbar}}^3{\signal{c}}^3}
\approx7.56573\times10^{-16}\,\unit{J\,m^{-3}\,K^{-4}}.
$$

The energy density is

$$
{\energy{u}}={\energy{a}}_R{\thermal{T}}^4
=(7.56573\times10^{-16})(3000)^4\,\unit{J\,m^{-3}}
\approx0.0612824\,\unit{J\,m^{-3}}.
$$

Multiply by the volume:

$$
\boxed{{\energy{U}}={\energy{u}}{\mechanical{V}}\approx6.12824\times10^{-5}\,\unit{J}.}
$$

### Part (b) — Radiation pressure

For isotropic radiation, the pressure is one third of the energy density. The factor accounts for the distribution of photon momentum over the three spatial directions:

$$
\boxed{{\mechanical{P}}_{\mathrm{rad}}=\frac{{\energy{u}}}{3}
\approx2.04275\times10^{-2}\,\unit{Pa}.}
$$

This is the photon contribution to pressure. It does not include any material gas in the cavity.

### Part (c) — Mean photon number

Substitute the temperature and volume into the supplied formula:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\langle {\mechanical{N}}_\gamma\rangle
&=\frac{2(1.20205690)}{\pi^2}(10^{-3})
\left[\frac{(1.380649\times10^{-23})(3000)}
{(1.05457182\times10^{-34})(2.99792458\times10^8)}\right]^3\\
&\approx5.47745\times10^{14}.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\boxed{\langle {\mechanical{N}}_\gamma\rangle\approx5.48\times10^{14}.}
$$

## Check

The mean photon energy is

$$
\frac{{\energy{U}}}{\langle {\mechanical{N}}_\gamma\rangle}
=\frac{\pi^4}{30\zeta(3)}{\thermal{k}}_B{\thermal{T}}
\approx2.70118{\thermal{k}}_B{\thermal{T}}.
$$

This is the expected mean energy for equilibrium blackbody photons. It checks the relative normalization of the energy and particle-number formulas.
