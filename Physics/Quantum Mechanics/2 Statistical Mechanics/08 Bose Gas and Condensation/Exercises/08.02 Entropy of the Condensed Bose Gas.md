## Question — Exercise 8.2

Show that the entropy of the Bose gas in the condensed phase is

$$
{\thermal{S}}({\thermal{T}}<{\thermal{T}}_c)=\frac52\frac{{\mechanical{V}} {\thermal{k}}_B}{{\signal{\lambda}}^3}\zeta(5/2),
$$

and verify that ${\thermal{S}}\to0$ for ${\thermal{T}}\to0$, as required by the third law of thermodynamics.

## Calculation

### Write the energy and pressure in the condensed phase

We consider a homogeneous three-dimensional ideal gas at fixed volume and particle number. Set the ground-state energy to zero. In the thermodynamic limit, the condensed phase has ${\mechanical{z}}=1$ and ${\energy{\mu}}=0$. The excited particles give

$$
{\mechanical{P}}=\frac{{\thermal{k}}_B{\thermal{T}}}{{\signal{\lambda}}^3}\zeta(5/2),\qquad
{\energy{U}}=\frac32{\mechanical{P}}{\mechanical{V}},
$$

where

$$
{\signal{\lambda}}=\frac{{\energy{h}}}{\sqrt{2\pi {\mechanical{m}} {\thermal{k}}_B{\thermal{T}}}}.
$$

The particles in the zero-energy ground state add no energy or pressure in this ideal model.

### Use the thermodynamic identity

The grand potential satisfies

$$
{\energy{\Phi}}={\energy{U}}-{\thermal{T}}{\thermal{S}}-{\energy{\mu}} {\mechanical{N}}=-{\mechanical{P}}{\mechanical{V}}.
$$

We use this identity because all its other terms are already known. With ${\energy{\mu}}=0$:

$$
{\thermal{T}}{\thermal{S}}={\energy{U}}+{\mechanical{P}}{\mechanical{V}}=\frac52{\mechanical{P}}{\mechanical{V}}.
$$

Substitute the pressure:

$$
\boxed{{\thermal{S}}=\frac52\frac{{\mechanical{V}} {\thermal{k}}_B}{{\signal{\lambda}}^3}\zeta(5/2).}
$$

### Check the temperature dependence independently

Since ${\signal{\lambda}}^{-3}\propto {\thermal{T}}^{3/2}$ at fixed mass,

$$
{\thermal{S}}=\frac52{\mechanical{V}} {\thermal{k}}_B\zeta(5/2)
\left(\frac{2\pi {\mechanical{m}} {\thermal{k}}_B}{{\energy{h}}^2}\right)^{3/2}{\thermal{T}}^{3/2}.
$$

An independent check follows from the heat capacity. Write

$$
{\energy{U}}=\frac32{\mechanical{V}} {\thermal{k}}_B\zeta(5/2)
\left(\frac{2\pi {\mechanical{m}} {\thermal{k}}_B}{{\energy{h}}^2}\right)^{3/2}{\thermal{T}}^{5/2}.
$$

Differentiation gives ${\thermal{C}}_V=(\partial {\energy{U}}/\partial {\thermal{T}})_{V,N}$. Then $\mathrm d{\thermal{S}}={\thermal{C}}_V\,\mathrm d{\thermal{T}}/{\thermal{T}}$ integrates to the same entropy if the zero-temperature ground state has zero entropy.

## Solution and limiting case

At the transition, ${\mechanical{N}}={\mechanical{V}}\zeta(3/2)/{\signal{\lambda}}_c^3$. Therefore, an equivalent form is

$$
{\thermal{S}}=\frac52{\mechanical{N}} {\thermal{k}}_B\frac{\zeta(5/2)}{\zeta(3/2)}
\left(\frac{{\thermal{T}}}{{\thermal{T}}_c}\right)^{3/2}.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{\lim_{T\to0}{\thermal{S}}=0.}
$$

In this limit all particles occupy the same nondegenerate ground state. The number of thermally accessible configurations tends to one, so the entropy tends to zero.
