> [!TLDR]
> Integration of the phonon mode energy gives the Debye internal energy and constant-volume heat capacity.

The total vibrational energy includes phonon excitation energy and zero-point energy:

$$
\begin{aligned}
{\energy{U}}
&=\int_0^{{\signal{\omega}}_D}{\energy{\hbar}}{\signal{\omega}}
\left(\langle {\mechanical{n}}({\signal{\omega}})\rangle+\frac12\right)
\signal{g}_D({\signal{\omega}})\,\mathrm d{\signal{\omega}}\\
&={\energy{U}}_0+\frac{9{\mechanical{N}}{\energy{\hbar}}}{{\signal{\omega}}_D^3}
\int_0^{{\signal{\omega}}_D}
\frac{{\signal{\omega}}^3}{e^{{\energy{\hbar}}{\signal{\omega}}/({\thermal{k}}_B{\thermal{T}})}-1}\,\mathrm d{\signal{\omega}}.
\end{aligned}
$$

Here the Bose–Einstein occupation and zero-point energy are

$$
\langle {\mechanical{n}}({\signal{\omega}})\rangle=\frac{1}{e^{{\energy{\hbar}}{\signal{\omega}}/({\thermal{k}}_B{\thermal{T}})}-1},
\qquad
{\energy{U}}_0=\frac12\int_0^{{\signal{\omega}}_D}{\energy{\hbar}}{\signal{\omega}}\signal{g}_D({\signal{\omega}})\,\mathrm d{\signal{\omega}}
=\frac98{\mechanical{N}}{\thermal{k}}_B{\thermal{\Theta}}_D.
$$

Introduce the dimensionless integration variable $x={\energy{\hbar}}{\signal{\omega}}/({\thermal{k}}_B{\thermal{T}})$ and the upper limit $x_D={\thermal{\Theta}}_D/{\thermal{T}}$. The thermal part becomes

$$
\begin{aligned}
{\energy{U}}-{\energy{U}}_0
&=9{\mechanical{N}}{\thermal{k}}_B{\thermal{T}}\left(\frac{{\thermal{T}}}{{\thermal{\Theta}}_D}\right)^3
\int_0^{{\thermal{\Theta}}_D/{\thermal{T}}}\frac{x^3}{e^x-1}\,\mathrm dx\\
&=3{\mechanical{N}}{\thermal{k}}_B{\thermal{T}}\,D\left(\frac{{\thermal{\Theta}}_D}{{\thermal{T}}}\right).
\end{aligned}
$$

The **Debye function** is the dimensionless mathematical function

$$
D(y)=\frac{3}{y^3}\int_0^y\frac{x^3}{e^x-1}\,\mathrm dx.
$$

## Heat capacity

At fixed volume and atom number, the mode frequencies and the zero-point energy are constant in this model. Differentiate the excitation energy of each mode:

$$
\frac{\partial}{\partial {\thermal{T}}}
\left[\frac{{\energy{\hbar}}{\signal{\omega}}}{e^{{\energy{\hbar}}{\signal{\omega}}/({\thermal{k}}_B{\thermal{T}})}-1}\right]
={\thermal{k}}_B\frac{x^2e^x}{(e^x-1)^2}.
$$

Insert the Debye mode density and use the same change of variable. This gives the total constant-volume heat capacity:

$$
{\thermal{C}}_V=\left(\frac{\partial {\energy{U}}}{\partial {\thermal{T}}}\right)_{V,N}
=9{\mechanical{N}}{\thermal{k}}_B\left(\frac{{\thermal{T}}}{{\thermal{\Theta}}_D}\right)^3
\int_0^{{\thermal{\Theta}}_D/{\thermal{T}}}\frac{x^4e^x}{(e^x-1)^2}\,\mathrm dx.
\tag{8.11}
$$

> [!note] Correction to the source — total and molar heat capacity
> The source calls Eq. (8.11) a molar heat capacity. Its factor ${\mechanical{N}}{\thermal{k}}_B$ gives the **total** heat capacity, in $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$. For the heat capacity per mole, replace ${\mechanical{N}}{\thermal{k}}_B$ with the molar gas constant ${\thermal{R}}_{\mathrm{gas}}={\mechanical{N}}_A{\thermal{k}}_B$:
>
> $$
> {\thermal{C}}_{V,\mathrm{mol}}
> =9{\thermal{R}}_{\mathrm{gas}}\left(\frac{{\thermal{T}}}{{\thermal{\Theta}}_D}\right)^3
> \int_0^{{\thermal{\Theta}}_D/{\thermal{T}}}\frac{x^4e^x}{(e^x-1)^2}\,\mathrm dx.
> $$

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\energy{U}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Internal energy | Total vibrational energy, including zero-point energy. |
| ${\energy{U}}_0$ | $\unit{J}$ | Zero-point energy | Temperature-independent energy of the phonon vacuum. |
| ${\mechanical{N}}$ | $\unit{1}$ | Atom number | Number of atoms in the crystal. |
| ${\signal{\omega}}$ | $\unit{s^{-1}}$ | Angular frequency | Angular frequency of a mode or trap. |
| ${\mechanical{n(\omega)}}$ | $\unit{1}$ | Phonon occupation | Mean number of phonons in a mode of angular frequency omega. |
| ${\signal{g}}_D(\omega)$ | $\unit{s}$ | Debye mode density | Number of vibrational modes per angular-frequency interval. |
| ${\energy{\hbar}}$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Reduced Planck constant | Equal to $h/(2\pi)$. |
| ${\thermal{k}}_B$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Boltzmann constant | Converts temperature to energy. |
| ${\thermal{T}}$ | $\unit{K}$ | Temperature | Absolute temperature of the system. |
| ${\thermal{\Theta}}_D$ | $\unit{K}$ | Debye temperature | Debye cutoff energy divided by the Boltzmann constant. |
| ${\thermal{C}}_V$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Heat capacity | Temperature derivative of energy at fixed volume and particle number. |
| ${\mechanical{V}}$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Volume | Volume of a three-dimensional system. |
| ${\thermal{C}}_{V,\mathrm{mol}}$ | $\unit{J\,mol^{-1}\,K^{-1}}$ | Molar heat capacity | Heat capacity per mole of material. |
| ${\thermal{R}}_{\mathrm{gas}}$ | $\unit{J\,mol^{-1}\,K^{-1}}$ | Molar gas constant | Avogadro constant times the Boltzmann constant. |
| ${\mechanical{N}}_A$ | $\unit{mol^{-1}}$ | Avogadro constant | Particle number per mole. |
