> [!TLDR]
> A photon gas gives the Planck spectrum, blackbody emission, and radiation pressure.

Photons are bosons whose number can change when the cavity walls emit or absorb radiation. Their equilibrium distribution gives blackbody radiation.

- [[08.04.01 Photons as Relativistic Bosons]] defines the photon gas and its mean mode occupation.
- [[08.04.02 Density of States and the Planck Spectrum]] calculates the energy spectrum.
- [[08.04.03 Stefan–Boltzmann Law]] integrates the spectrum and calculates emitted power.
- [[08.04.04 Wien Displacement Law]] locates the spectral maximum.
- [[08.04.05 Radiation Pressure]] connects radiation energy to mechanical pressure.
