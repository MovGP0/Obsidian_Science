> [!TLDR]
> The total radiation energy density and blackbody power per area are proportional to the fourth power of temperature.

Integrate the Planck spectrum over all angular frequencies to obtain the total radiation energy per volume:

$$
{\energy{u}}_{\mathrm{tot}}=\int_0^\infty {\energy{u}}({\signal{\omega}},{\thermal{T}})\,d{\signal{\omega}}.
$$

Use the dimensionless variable $x={\energy{\hbar}}{\signal{\omega}}/({\thermal{k}}_B{\thermal{T}})$. Then

$$
{\energy{u}}_{\mathrm{tot}}
=\frac{({\thermal{k}}_B{\thermal{T}})^4}{\pi^2{\energy{\hbar}}^3{\signal{c}}^3}
\int_0^\infty\frac{x^3}{e^x-1}\,dx.
$$

The integral is

$$
\int_0^\infty\frac{x^3}{e^x-1}\,dx
=\Gamma(4)\zeta(4)
=6\frac{\pi^4}{90}
=\frac{\pi^4}{15}.
$$

Therefore,

$$
{\energy{u}}_{\mathrm{tot}}=
\frac{\pi^2{\thermal{k}}_B^4}{15{\energy{\hbar}}^3{\signal{c}}^3}{\thermal{T}}^4
\equiv {\energy{a}}_R{\thermal{T}}^4.
\tag{8.10}
$$

The power emitted by a blackbody per surface area is its **radiant exitance**:

$$
{\energy{j^*}}=\frac{{\signal{c}}}{4}{\energy{u}}_{\mathrm{tot}}={\energy{\sigma}} {\thermal{T}}^4,
\qquad
{\energy{\sigma}}=\frac{\pi^2{\thermal{k}}_B^4}{60{\energy{\hbar}}^3{\signal{c}}^2}.
$$

The Stefan–Boltzmann constant is approximately

$$
{\energy{\sigma}}\approx5.67\times10^{-8}\,\unit{W\,m^{-2}\,K^{-4}}.
$$

> [!note] Energy density and emitted power
> The total energy density is energy per volume. Radiant exitance is power per surface area. The factor ${\signal{c}}/4$ connects these quantities for isotropic equilibrium radiation incident on a surface. It includes the angular average over the outward hemisphere.

The peak of the spectrum is given by [[08.04.04 Wien Displacement Law]].

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\energy{u}}$ | $\unit{J\,m^{-3}}$ | Energy density | Energy per volume. |
| ${\energy{u}}({\signal{\omega}},{\thermal{T}})$ | $\unit{J\,s\,m^{-3}}$ | Angular-frequency spectrum | Energy per volume and angular-frequency interval. |
| ${\signal{\omega}}$ | $\unit{s^{-1}}$ | Angular frequency | Angular frequency of a mode or trap. |
| ${\thermal{T}}$ | $\unit{K}$ | Temperature | Absolute temperature of the system. |
| ${\thermal{k}}_B$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Boltzmann constant | Converts temperature to energy. |
| ${\energy{\hbar}}$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Reduced Planck constant | Equal to $h/(2\pi)$. |
| ${\signal{c}}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Speed of light | Propagation speed of light in vacuum. |
| ${\energy{a}}_R$ | $\unit{J\,m^{-3}\,K^{-4}}$ | Radiation constant | Relates total radiation energy density to the fourth power of temperature. |
| ${\energy{j^*}}$ | $\unit{W\,m^{-2}}$ | Radiant exitance | Power emitted per surface area. |
| ${\energy{\sigma}}$ | $\unit{W\,m^{-2}\,K^{-4}}$ | Stefan–Boltzmann constant | Relates emitted radiant power per area to the fourth power of temperature. |
