> [!TLDR]
> The Debye cutoff fixes the total number of vibration modes at three times the atom number.

Debye proposed a simplified model in 1912 with these assumptions:

1. The crystal is a continuous, isotropic elastic medium.
2. The dispersion relation is linear, ${\signal{\omega}}={\mechanical{v}}_s {\signal{k}}$, for all modes in the model.
3. The total number of modes is exactly $3{\mechanical{N}}$. An upper frequency limit ${\signal{\omega}}_D$ enforces this condition.

## Definition 8.3 — Debye temperature

The **Debye frequency** ${\signal{\omega}}_D$ is determined by the normalization condition

$$
\int_0^{{\signal{\omega}}_D}\signal{g}_D({\signal{\omega}})\,\mathrm d{\signal{\omega}}=3{\mechanical{N}}.
$$

The **Debye temperature** is

$$
{\thermal{\Theta}}_D=\frac{{\energy{\hbar}}{\signal{\omega}}_D}{{\thermal{k}}_B}.
$$

The mode density includes three polarizations: two transverse and one longitudinal. A single effective sound speed ${\mechanical{v}}_s$ represents these branches. The density is

$$
\signal{g}_D({\signal{\omega}})
=\frac{9{\mechanical{N}}}{{\signal{\omega}}_D^3}{\signal{\omega}}^2
=\frac{3{\mechanical{V}}}{2\pi^2{\mechanical{v}}_s^3}{\signal{\omega}}^2,
\qquad 0\leq{\signal{\omega}}\leq{\signal{\omega}}_D.
$$

Outside this frequency interval, the model mode density is zero. Integration gives

$$
\int_0^{{\signal{\omega}}_D}\frac{3{\mechanical{V}}}{2\pi^2{\mechanical{v}}_s^3}{\signal{\omega}}^2\,\mathrm d{\signal{\omega}}
=\frac{{\mechanical{V}}{\signal{\omega}}_D^3}{2\pi^2{\mechanical{v}}_s^3}=3{\mechanical{N}}.
$$

Thus,

$$
{\signal{\omega}}_D={\mechanical{v}}_s\left(\frac{6\pi^2{\mechanical{N}}}{{\mechanical{V}}}\right)^{1/3}.
$$

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\mechanical{N}}$ | $\unit{1}$ | Atom number | Number of atoms in the crystal. |
| ${\signal{\omega}}$ | $\unit{s^{-1}}$ | Angular frequency | Angular frequency of a mode or trap. |
| ${\thermal{\Theta}}_D$ | $\unit{K}$ | Debye temperature | Debye cutoff energy divided by the Boltzmann constant. |
| ${\energy{\hbar}}$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Reduced Planck constant | Equal to $h/(2\pi)$. |
| ${\thermal{k}}_B$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Boltzmann constant | Converts temperature to energy. |
| ${\mechanical{v}}_s$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Sound speed | Effective acoustic speed in the Debye model. |
| ${\signal{k}}$ | $\unit{m^{-1}}$ | Wavenumber | Magnitude or component of a wave vector. |
| ${\mechanical{V}}$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Volume | Volume of a three-dimensional system. |
| ${\signal{g}}_D(\omega)$ | $\unit{s}$ | Debye mode density | Number of vibrational modes per angular-frequency interval. |
