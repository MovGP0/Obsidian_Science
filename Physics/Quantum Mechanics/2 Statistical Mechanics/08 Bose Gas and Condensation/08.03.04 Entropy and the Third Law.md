> [!TLDR]
> The entropy below condensation is proportional to T³ᐟ² and tends to zero as temperature tends to zero.

For the condensed phase, integrate ${\thermal{C}}_V/{\thermal{T}}$ with the zero-temperature entropy set to zero:

$$
{\thermal{S}}({\thermal{T}}<{\thermal{T}}_c)=\int_0^{\thermal{T}}\frac{{\thermal{C}}_V^{(<)}({\thermal{T}}')}{{\thermal{T}}'}\,d{\thermal{T}}'
=\frac52{\mechanical{N}}{\thermal{k}}_B\frac{\zeta(5/2)}{\zeta(3/2)}\left(\frac{{\thermal{T}}}{{\thermal{T}}_c}\right)^{3/2}.
\tag{8.8}
$$

Thus ${\thermal{S}}\to0$ as ${\thermal{T}}\to0$, in agreement with the third law for the nondegenerate many-body ground state. At zero temperature all particles occupy the same single-particle state. There is **one** many-body occupation configuration, so its entropy is ${\thermal{k}}_B\ln1=0$.

> [!note] Source clarification
> The source says that microstate counting is reduced to zero. The number of accessible ground-state configurations is one, not zero. Its logarithm is zero. At positive temperature below ${\thermal{T}}_c$, the excited cloud still carries nonzero entropy.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\thermal{S}}$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Entropy | Thermodynamic entropy of the gas. |
| ${\thermal{T}}$ | $\unit{K}$ | Temperature | Absolute temperature of the system. |
| ${\thermal{T}}_c$ | $\unit{K}$ | Critical temperature | Temperature at which macroscopic ground-state occupation begins. |
| ${\thermal{C}}_V$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Heat capacity | Temperature derivative of energy at fixed volume and particle number. |
| ${\mechanical{N}}$ | $\unit{1}$ | Particle number | Total or mean number of particles. |
| ${\thermal{k}}_B$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Boltzmann constant | Converts temperature to energy. |
