> [!TLDR]
> In the condensed ideal gas, pressure depends on temperature but not on total particle density.

For ${\thermal{T}}<{\thermal{T}}_c$,

$$
{\mechanical{P}}=\frac{{\thermal{k}}_B{\thermal{T}}}{{\signal{\lambda}}^3}\zeta(5/2)\propto {\thermal{T}}^{5/2}.
$$

At fixed temperature, this pressure is independent of ${\mechanical{V}}$ and ${\mechanical{N}}$ as long as the system remains condensed. Ground-state particles have ${\energy{\varepsilon}}_0=0$ in the bulk convention and do not exert pressure. Only the excited particles contribute.

The isothermal compressibility is

$$
{\mechanical{\kappa}}_T=-\frac1{\mechanical{V}}\left(\frac{\partial {\mechanical{V}}}{\partial {\mechanical{P}}}\right)_{T,N}.
$$

Since $(\partial {\mechanical{P}}/\partial {\mechanical{V}})_{T,N}=0$ in the condensed phase, ${\mechanical{\kappa}}_T$ diverges. This is a property of the ideal uniform-gas limit. Repulsive interactions and finite-size effects change the mechanical response of real condensates.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\mechanical{P}}$ | $\unit{Pa}$ | Pressure | Mechanical pressure of the gas. |
| ${\thermal{k}}_B$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Boltzmann constant | Converts temperature to energy. |
| ${\thermal{T}}$ | $\unit{K}$ | Temperature | Absolute temperature of the system. |
| ${\signal{\lambda}}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Thermal wavelength | Thermal de Broglie wavelength of one massive particle. |
| ${\thermal{T}}_c$ | $\unit{K}$ | Critical temperature | Temperature at which macroscopic ground-state occupation begins. |
| ${\mechanical{V}}$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Volume | Volume of a three-dimensional system. |
| ${\mechanical{N}}$ | $\unit{1}$ | Particle number | Total or mean number of particles. |
| ${\energy{\varepsilon}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Single-particle energy | Energy of one single-particle state. |
| ${\mechanical{\kappa}}_T$ | $\unit{Pa^{-1}}$ | Isothermal compressibility | Relative volume response to pressure at fixed temperature. |
