> [!TLDR]
> The equilibrium photon gas has zero chemical potential and follows the Planck distribution.

Photons are bosons with spin ${\mechanical{s}}=1$ and two physical polarization states. Their rest mass is zero. The energy of a photon in mode ${\signal{\mathbf k}}$ is

$$
{\energy{\varepsilon}}_{\mathbf k}={\energy{\hbar}}{\signal{\omega}}_{\mathbf k}={\energy{\hbar}} {\signal{c}}|{\signal{\mathbf k}}|.
$$

The photon number is not conserved: the cavity walls can emit and absorb photons. Therefore, the chemical potential is fixed at zero,

$$
{\energy{\mu}}=0,\qquad {\mechanical{z}}=1.
$$

## Definition 8.2 — Photon gas

A system of photons in thermal equilibrium in a cavity of volume ${\mechanical{V}}$ at temperature ${\thermal{T}}$ follows the **Planck distribution**:

$$
\langle {\mechanical{n}}({\signal{\omega}})\rangle=
\frac{1}{e^{{\energy{\hbar}}{\signal{\omega}}/({\thermal{k}}_B{\thermal{T}})}-1}.
$$

This is the mean number of photons in a mode $({\signal{\mathbf k}},\sigma)$ with angular frequency

$$
{\signal{\omega}}={\signal{c}}|{\signal{\mathbf k}}|.
$$

Here, $\sigma$ labels the polarization. It is an index, not the Stefan–Boltzmann constant used later.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\mechanical{s}}$ | $\unit{1}$ | Spin quantum number | Intrinsic angular-momentum quantum number. |
| ${\energy{\varepsilon}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Single-particle energy | Energy of one single-particle state. |
| ${\energy{\hbar}}$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Reduced Planck constant | Equal to ${\energy{h}}/(2\pi)$. |
| ${\signal{\omega}}$ | $\unit{s^{-1}}$ | Angular frequency | Angular frequency of a mode or trap. |
| ${\signal{c}}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Speed of light | Propagation speed of light in vacuum. |
| ${\signal{k}}$ | $\unit{m^{-1}}$ | Wavenumber | Magnitude or component of a wave vector. |
| ${\energy{\mu}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Chemical potential | Controls the mean particle number. |
| ${\mechanical{z}}$ | $\unit{1}$ | Fugacity | Equal to $e^{\beta{\energy{\mu}}}$. |
| ${\mechanical{V}}$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Volume | Volume of a three-dimensional system. |
| ${\thermal{T}}$ | $\unit{K}$ | Temperature | Absolute temperature of the system. |
| ${\thermal{k}}_B$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Boltzmann constant | Converts temperature to energy. |
| ${\mechanical{n}}$ | $\unit{1}$ | Mode occupation | Number or mean number of photons in one electromagnetic mode. |

| ${\energy{h}}$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Planck constant | Relates frequency to quantum energy. |
