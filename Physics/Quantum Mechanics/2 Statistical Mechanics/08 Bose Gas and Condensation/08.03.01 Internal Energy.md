> [!TLDR]
> The energy of a nonrelativistic ideal Bose gas is three halves of pressure times volume.

The mean energy is

$$
{\energy{U}}=\int_0^\infty{\energy{\varepsilon}} {\energy{g}}({\energy{\varepsilon}})\langle {\mechanical{n}}({\energy{\varepsilon}})\rangle\,d{\energy{\varepsilon}}.
$$

The same integral steps used for pressure give

$$
{\energy{U}}=\frac32{\mechanical{P}}{\mechanical{V}}=\frac32\frac{{\mechanical{V}}{\thermal{k}}_B{\thermal{T}}}{{\signal{\lambda}}^3}g_{5/2}({\mechanical{z}}).
\tag{8.5}
$$

The relation ${\energy{U}}=3{\mechanical{P}}{\mechanical{V}}/2$ holds for a nonrelativistic ideal three-dimensional gas with quadratic dispersion. It is not limited to the classical regime.

## Condensed phase

For ${\thermal{T}}<{\thermal{T}}_c$ and ${\mechanical{z}}=1$,

$$
{\energy{U}}=\frac32\frac{{\mechanical{V}}{\thermal{k}}_B{\thermal{T}}}{{\signal{\lambda}}^3}\zeta(5/2)
=\frac32{\mechanical{N}}{\thermal{k}}_B{\thermal{T}}\frac{\zeta(5/2)}{\zeta(3/2)}\left(\frac{{\thermal{T}}}{{\thermal{T}}_c}\right)^{3/2}.
\tag{8.6}
$$

Here $\zeta(5/2)\approx1.341$. The chosen zero-energy condensate does not contribute to ${\energy{U}}$.

## Normal phase

For ${\thermal{T}}>{\thermal{T}}_c$, determine ${\mechanical{z}}<1$ from ${\mechanical{n}}{\signal{\lambda}}^3=g_{3/2}({\mechanical{z}})$ and use that value in Equation (8.5).

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\energy{U}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Internal energy | Total energy of the gas. |
| ${\energy{\varepsilon}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Single-particle energy | Energy of one single-particle state. |
| ${\energy{g(\varepsilon)}}$ | $\unit{J^{-1}}$ | Density of states | Total number of states per energy interval, including internal degeneracy. |
| ${\mechanical{n}}_\alpha$ | $\unit{1}$ | Occupation number | Number or mean number of particles in one state. |
| ${\mechanical{P}}$ | $\unit{Pa}$ | Pressure | Mechanical pressure of the gas. |
| ${\mechanical{V}}$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Volume | Volume of a three-dimensional system. |
| ${\thermal{k}}_B$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Boltzmann constant | Converts temperature to energy. |
| ${\thermal{T}}$ | $\unit{K}$ | Temperature | Absolute temperature of the system. |
| ${\signal{\lambda}}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Thermal wavelength | Thermal de Broglie wavelength of one massive particle. |
| ${\mechanical{z}}$ | $\unit{1}$ | Fugacity | Equal to $e^{\beta\mu}$. |
| ${\thermal{T}}_c$ | $\unit{K}$ | Critical temperature | Temperature at which macroscopic ground-state occupation begins. |
| ${\mechanical{N}}$ | $\unit{1}$ | Particle number | Total or mean number of particles. |
| ${\mechanical{n}}$ | $\unit{m^{-3}}$ | Number density | Number of particles per volume. |
