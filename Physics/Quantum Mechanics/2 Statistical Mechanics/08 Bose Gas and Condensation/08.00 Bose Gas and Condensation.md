> [!TLDR]
> Bose statistics explains macroscopic ground-state occupation, blackbody radiation, and the thermal response of lattice vibrations.

Bosons have integer spin and do not obey the Pauli exclusion principle. Any number of identical bosons can occupy the same complete quantum state. At low temperature this permits **Bose–Einstein condensation (BEC)**: a macroscopic number of particles occupies the ground state.

This chapter examines the ideal Bose gas, the condensation transition, and two important applications of Bose statistics: blackbody radiation from photons and the Debye model of phonons in crystals.
