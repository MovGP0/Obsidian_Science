> [!TLDR]
> The Debye heat capacity approaches the Dulong–Petit value at high temperature and varies as the cube of temperature at low temperature.

## High temperature

For ${\thermal{T}}\gg{\thermal{\Theta}}_D$, set $y={\thermal{\Theta}}_D/{\thermal{T}}\ll1$. The small-argument expansion is

$$
\frac{x}{e^x-1}=1-\frac{x}{2}+\cdots.
$$

At leading order,

$$
\int_0^y\frac{x^3}{e^x-1}\,\mathrm dx
\approx\int_0^y x^2\,\mathrm dx=\frac{y^3}{3}.
$$

Thus $D(y)\to1$ and the leading thermal energy is ${\energy{U}}-{\energy{U}}_0\sim3{\mechanical{N}}{\thermal{k}}_B{\thermal{T}}$. Differentiation gives

$$
{\thermal{C}}_V\longrightarrow3{\mechanical{N}}{\thermal{k}}_B.
$$

This is the **Dulong–Petit law**. For one mole, the limit is ${\thermal{C}}_{V,\mathrm{mol}}\to3{\thermal{R}}_{\mathrm{gas}}$.

## Low temperature

For ${\thermal{T}}\ll{\thermal{\Theta}}_D$, the upper limit $y={\thermal{\Theta}}_D/{\thermal{T}}$ is large. Replace it by infinity in the thermal energy integral:

$$
\int_0^\infty\frac{x^3}{e^x-1}\,\mathrm dx=\frac{\pi^4}{15}.
$$

Therefore,

$$
\begin{aligned}
{\energy{U}}-{\energy{U}}_0
&\approx3{\mechanical{N}}{\thermal{k}}_B{\thermal{T}}\frac{3}{y^3}\frac{\pi^4}{15}\\
&=\frac{3\pi^4}{5}{\mechanical{N}}{\thermal{k}}_B{\thermal{T}}\left(\frac{{\thermal{T}}}{{\thermal{\Theta}}_D}\right)^3.
\end{aligned}
$$

Differentiate the ${\thermal{T}}^4$ dependence at fixed volume:

$$
{\thermal{C}}_V\approx\frac{12\pi^4}{5}{\mechanical{N}}{\thermal{k}}_B
\left(\frac{{\thermal{T}}}{{\thermal{\Theta}}_D}\right)^3\propto {\thermal{T}}^3.
$$

This is the **Debye cubic law**. It agrees well with the phonon heat capacity of many crystals at low temperature. Only long-wavelength acoustic modes are thermally excited in this limit.

> [!note] Correction to the source — energy reference
> The source labels the low-temperature expression as ${\energy{U}}$. It is the thermal energy ${\energy{U}}-{\energy{U}}_0$. The total vibrational energy approaches the nonzero value ${\energy{U}}_0$ as ${\thermal{T}}\to0$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\thermal{T}}$ | $\unit{K}$ | Temperature | Absolute temperature of the system. |
| ${\thermal{\Theta}}_D$ | $\unit{K}$ | Debye temperature | Debye cutoff energy divided by the Boltzmann constant. |
| ${\energy{U}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Internal energy | Total vibrational energy, including zero-point energy. |
| ${\energy{U}}_0$ | $\unit{J}$ | Zero-point energy | Temperature-independent energy of the phonon vacuum. |
| ${\mechanical{N}}$ | $\unit{1}$ | Atom number | Number of atoms in the crystal. |
| ${\thermal{k}}_B$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Boltzmann constant | Converts temperature to energy. |
| ${\thermal{C}}_V$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Heat capacity | Temperature derivative of energy at fixed volume and particle number. |
| ${\thermal{C}}_{V,\mathrm{mol}}$ | $\unit{J\,mol^{-1}\,K^{-1}}$ | Molar heat capacity | Heat capacity per mole of material. |
| ${\thermal{R}}_{\mathrm{gas}}$ | $\unit{J\,mol^{-1}\,K^{-1}}$ | Molar gas constant | Avogadro constant times the Boltzmann constant. |
