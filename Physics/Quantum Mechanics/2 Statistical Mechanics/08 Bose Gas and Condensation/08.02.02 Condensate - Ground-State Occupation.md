> [!TLDR]
> Below the critical temperature, the excited-state fraction falls as T³ᐟ² and the remaining particles occupy the ground state.

For ${\thermal{T}}<{\thermal{T}}_c$, the bulk fugacity is fixed at ${\mechanical{z}}=1$ and ${\energy{\mu}}=0$. The particles that cannot fit in the excited states occupy the ground state with ${\energy{\varepsilon}}_0=0$.

$$
{\mechanical{N}}_{\mathrm{ex}}({\thermal{T}})=\frac{{\mechanical{V}}}{{\signal{\lambda}}^3}\zeta(3/2)
={\mechanical{N}}\left(\frac{{\thermal{T}}}{{\thermal{T}}_c}\right)^{3/2}.
$$

Here ${\mechanical{N}}={\mechanical{V}}\zeta(3/2)/{\signal{\lambda}}_c^3$ and ${\signal{\lambda}}^3\propto {\thermal{T}}^{-3/2}$. Thus

$$
\frac{{\mechanical{N}}_0}{{\mechanical{N}}}=1-\left(\frac{{\thermal{T}}}{{\thermal{T}}_c}\right)^{3/2},\qquad {\thermal{T}}\le {\thermal{T}}_c.
$$

For ${\thermal{T}}>{\thermal{T}}_c$, the condensate fraction is zero in the thermodynamic limit.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\thermal{T}}$ | $\unit{K}$ | Temperature | Absolute temperature of the system. |
| ${\thermal{T}}_c$ | $\unit{K}$ | Critical temperature | Temperature at which macroscopic ground-state occupation begins. |
| ${\mechanical{z}}$ | $\unit{1}$ | Fugacity | Equal to $e^{\beta{\energy{\mu}}}$. |
| ${\energy{\mu}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Chemical potential | Controls the mean particle number. |
| ${\energy{\varepsilon}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Single-particle energy | Energy of one single-particle state. |
| ${\mechanical{N}}$ | $\unit{1}$ | Particle number | Total or mean number of particles. |
| ${\mechanical{V}}$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Volume | Volume of a three-dimensional system. |
| ${\signal{\lambda}}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Thermal wavelength | Thermal de Broglie wavelength of one massive particle. |
