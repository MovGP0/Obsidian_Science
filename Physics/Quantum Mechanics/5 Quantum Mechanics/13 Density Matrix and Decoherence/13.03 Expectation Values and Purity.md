title: "Expectation Values and Purity"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The expectation value of an observable is its trace against the density operator. The purity is the sum of squared density-operator eigenvalues.

For a preparation ensemble, average the quantum expectation values with the preparation probabilities.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\langle\hat O\rangle
&=\sum_i p_i\langle\signal{\psi}_i\lvert\hat O\rvert\signal{\psi}_i\rangle\\
&=\sum_i p_i\operatorname{Tr}(\lvert\signal{\psi}_i\rangle\langle\signal{\psi}_i\rvert\hat O)
=\operatorname{Tr}(\hat\rho\hat O).
\end{aligned}
$$

For $\hat\rho=\lvert\signal{\psi}\rangle\langle\signal{\psi}\rvert$, this is the pure-state expectation value $\langle\signal{\psi}\lvert\hat O\rvert\signal{\psi}\rangle$.

A density operator represents a pure state if and only if $\hat\rho^2=\hat\rho$. Its trace is one, so a projector of this kind has one eigenvalue equal to one and the others equal to zero. The **purity** is

$$
\mathcal P(\hat\rho)=\operatorname{Tr}(\hat\rho^2)=\sum_n\lambda_n^2.
$$

It is one for a pure state. In finite dimension $d$, the quadratic-mean inequality gives $\sum_n\lambda_n^2\ge1/d$ for $\sum_n\lambda_n=1$. Equality requires all eigenvalues to be $1/d$, so $\hat\rho_*=\hat I/d$ is the **maximally mixed state**. Thus $\mathcal P\in[1/d,1]$. Purity measures how far the state is from maximal mixing.

> [!theorem] Proposition 13.1. Qubit purity
> Every spin-$1/2$ density operator has the form
>
> $$
> \hat\rho=\frac12(\hat I+\mathbf a\cdot\boldsymbol{\sigma}),\qquad
> \lvert\mathbf a\rvert\le1,
> $$
>
> where $\mathbf a$ is the Bloch vector. Its purity is $\mathcal P=(1+\lvert\mathbf a\rvert^2)/2$. Pure states lie on the surface, and the maximally mixed state is at the center.

The Bloch representation puts the qubit states in one-to-one correspondence with the points of the closed unit ball in $\mathbb R^3$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\hat\rho,\hat\rho_*$ | $1$ | Density operators | The general state and the maximally mixed state. |
| $\hat O,\langle\hat O\rangle$ | Depends on observable | Observable and expectation | The measured quantity and its statistical mean. |
| $p_i,\lambda_n$ | $1$ | Weights and eigenvalues | Ensemble probabilities and the density-operator spectrum. |
| $\mathcal P$ | $1$ | Purity | The trace of the squared density operator. |
| $d$ | $1$ | Dimension | The number of basis states for the finite-dimensional purity bound. |
| $\mathbf a,\boldsymbol{\sigma}$ | $1$ | Bloch vector and Pauli matrices | The vector that specifies the qubit state and its operator basis. |
