---
title: "Definition and Properties of the Density Matrix"
---

> [!TLDR]
> A density operator is a positive Hermitian operator of trace one. For a preparation ensemble it is the weighted sum of the pure-state projectors.

For an ensemble $\{p_i,\lvert\signal{\psi}_i\rangle\}$, the **density operator** is

$$
\hat\rho=\sum_i p_i\lvert\signal{\psi}_i\rangle\langle\signal{\psi}_i\rvert.
$$

The weights record the classical preparation probabilities. Each projector describes the quantum state for that preparation.

1. **Hermiticity.** $\hat\rho^\dagger=\hat\rho$ because each projector is Hermitian and the weights are real.
2. **Normalization.** $\operatorname{Tr}\hat\rho=1$ because each normalized projector has trace one and $\sum_i p_i=1$.
3. **Positivity.** $\hat\rho\ge0$ because $\langle\signal{\varphi}\lvert\hat\rho\rvert\signal{\varphi}\rangle=\sum_i p_i\lvert\langle\signal{\varphi}\mid\signal{\psi}_i\rangle\rvert^2\ge0$ for every $\lvert\signal{\varphi}\rangle$.

> [!theorem] Theorem 13.1. Characterization of density operators
> An operator on a Hilbert space is the density operator of a statistical ensemble if and only if it is Hermitian, positive, and has trace one.

**Proof.** The three properties follow from the ensemble formula. Conversely, a positive operator of trace one is trace class and has a spectral decomposition $\hat\rho=\sum_n\lambda_n\lvert n\rangle\langle n\rvert$. The eigenvectors can be chosen orthonormal. Positivity gives $\lambda_n\ge0$ and the trace gives $\sum_n\lambda_n=1$. Thus the eigenvalues and eigenvectors specify one ensemble.

> [!note] Remark 13.1. Ensembles for the same state
> A mixed density operator admits many ensemble decompositions, including decompositions with nonorthogonal states. Two ensembles that give the same $\hat\rho$ give the same measurement statistics on the system. The density operator specifies the operational state.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\hat\rho$ | $1$ | Density operator | The positive trace-one operator specifying the state. |
| $p_i,\lambda_n$ | $1$ | Weights and eigenvalues | Preparation probabilities and the weights of the spectral decomposition. |
| $\lvert\signal{\psi}_i\rangle,\lvert n\rangle$ | $1$ | State vectors | Ensemble members and orthonormal eigenvectors of the density operator. |
