title: "Wigner–Eckart Theorem"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The Wigner–Eckart theorem factors a spherical tensor matrix element into a Clebsch–Gordan coefficient and one reduced matrix element.

> [!theorem] Theorem 11.1. Wigner–Eckart theorem
> For a rank-$k$ spherical tensor $\hat T_q^k$ and a pair of angular momentum multiplets,
>
> $$
> \langle j'm'\lvert\hat T_q^k\rvert jm\rangle
> =\frac{\langle jm;kq\mid j'm'\rangle}{\sqrt{2j'+1}}
> \langle j'\lVert\hat T^k\rVert j\rangle.
> $$

The **reduced matrix element** $\langle j'\lVert\hat T^k\rVert j\rangle$ is independent of $m,m',q$. The Clebsch–Gordan coefficient contains all the dependence on these three indices. At fixed multiplets, calculate one matrix element with a nonzero coefficient to extract the reduced element. The other matrix elements then follow from their coefficients.

A matrix element can be nonzero only if $\lvert j-k\rvert\le j'\le j+k$ and $m'=m+q$.

> [!theorem] Proposition 11.1. Scalar case
> For $\hat S=\hat T_0^0$,
>
> $$
> \langle j'm'\lvert\hat S\rvert jm\rangle
> =\delta_{jj'}\delta_{mm'}\frac{\langle j\lVert\hat S\rVert j\rangle}{\sqrt{2j+1}}.
> $$

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\hat T_q^k,\hat S$ | Depends on operator | Tensor component and scalar | The operator in the general theorem and its rank-zero case. |
| $\langle j'\lVert\hat T^k\rVert j\rangle$ | Same as $\hat T_q^k$ | Reduced matrix element | The factor independent of the projection indices. |
| $\langle jm;kq\mid j'm'\rangle$ | $1$ | Clebsch–Gordan coefficient | The geometric factor that gives the projection dependence. |
| $j,j',m,m',k,q$ | $1$ | Angular momentum and tensor labels | The state representation, state projection, rank, and component indices. |
