---
title: "Statement of the Problem"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Operators on different angular momentum factors commute. Their component-wise sum satisfies the same angular momentum algebra.

Consider independent subsystems with angular momenta $\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf J}}_1$ and $\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf J}}_2$. Each satisfies the angular momentum commutators.

$$
[\mechanical{\hat J}_{1i},\mechanical{\hat J}_{1j}]=i\mechanical{\hbar}\epsilon_{ijk}\mechanical{\hat J}_{1k},
\qquad
[\mechanical{\hat J}_{2i},\mechanical{\hat J}_{2j}]=i\mechanical{\hbar}\epsilon_{ijk}\mechanical{\hat J}_{2k}.
\tag{11.1}
$$

Operators on the two factors commute.

$$
[\mechanical{\hat J}_{1i},\mechanical{\hat J}_{2j}]=0,\qquad i,j\in\{x,y,z\}.
\tag{11.2}
$$

Define the total angular momentum by $\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf J}}=\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf J}}_1+\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf J}}_2$. Equations (11.1) and (11.2) give

$$
[\mechanical{\hat J}_i,\mechanical{\hat J}_j]=i\mechanical{\hbar}\epsilon_{ijk}\mechanical{\hat J}_k.
\tag{11.3}
$$

Thus the total operator has the angular momentum algebra. The eigenvalues of its square are $\mechanical{\hbar}^{\,2}J(J+1)$ with integer or half-integer $J$. The axial projection has eigenvalues $\mechanical{\hbar}M$, where $M=-J,-J+1,\ldots,J$.

> [!note] Remark 11.1. Compatible observables
> The squared operators $\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf J}}_1^{\,2}$ and $\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf J}}_2^{\,2}$ commute with the total rotation generators and are scalars under total rotations. The individual axial projections generally do not commute with $\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf J}}^{\,2}$. This leads to two choices of commuting observables.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf J}}_1,\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf J}}_2,\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf J}}$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Angular momentum operators | The two individual operators and their total. |
| $\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf J}}^{\,2}$ | $\unit{J^2\,s^2}$ | Squared total angular momentum | The operator with eigenvalue $\mechanical{\hbar}^{\,2}J(J+1)$. |
| $J,M$ | $1$ | Total angular momentum labels | The representation and its axial projection index. |
| $\mechanical{\hbar}$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Reduced Planck constant | The angular momentum scale. |
