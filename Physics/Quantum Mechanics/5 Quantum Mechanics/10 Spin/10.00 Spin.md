> [!TLDR]
> Spin is intrinsic angular momentum. It follows the same commutation relations as orbital angular momentum, and its quantum number can be half-integer.

Uhlenbeck and Goudsmit proposed spin in 1925 to explain spectral fine structure and the anomalous Zeeman effect. The result of the [[01.07 Stern–Gerlach Experiment|Stern–Gerlach experiment]] was later understood in terms of spin.

Spin is an additional degree of freedom independent of orbital motion. Its algebra is the angular momentum algebra, and its spin quantum number is integer or half-integer. The spin state space enters the full state space as a tensor factor together with the spatial state space.

This chapter concentrates on spin $1/2$, which occurs for electrons, protons, neutrons, and quarks and is central to atomic and particle physics.

## Chapter summary

- Spin is intrinsic angular momentum. Its components obey the same algebra as orbital angular momentum and admit half-integer $s$.
- For spin $1/2$, $\mechanical{\hat S}_i=(\mechanical{\hbar}/2)\sigma_i$. The Pauli matrices satisfy $\sigma_i^2=\hat I$ and $[\sigma_i,\sigma_j]=2i\epsilon_{ijk}\sigma_k$.
- Each pure qubit state corresponds to a unit Bloch vector $\langle\boldsymbol{\sigma}\rangle=\hat{\mathbf n}$.
- A spinor rotation is $\hat R_{\hat{\mathbf n}}(\alpha)=\cos(\alpha/2)\hat I-i\sin(\alpha/2)\boldsymbol{\sigma}\cdot\hat{\mathbf n}$. A rotation by $2\pi$ changes the spinor sign.
- In $\magnetic{\mathbf B}=\magnetic B\hat{\mathbf z}$, the signed Larmor angular frequency is $\mechanical{\omega}_L=\magnetic{\gamma}\magnetic B$.
- A Stern–Gerlach analyzer measures a spin component and separates the beam into two discrete parts for spin $1/2$.
- Two spin-$1/2$ spaces decompose into a triplet with $s=1$ and a singlet with $s=0$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{\hat S}_i$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Spin component | The intrinsic angular momentum operator along axis $i$. |
| $\sigma_i,\langle\boldsymbol{\sigma}\rangle$ | $1$ | Pauli matrices and Bloch vector | The spin matrices and their expectation vector. |
| $s$ | $1$ | Spin quantum number | The representation label for squared spin. |
| $\alpha$ | $\unit{rad}$ | Rotation angle | The angle through which the spinor is rotated. |
| $\magnetic B$ | $\unit T$ | Magnetic flux density | The axial field for Larmor precession. |
| $\magnetic{\gamma}$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}\,T^{-1}}$ | Gyromagnetic ratio | The proportionality between the signed Larmor frequency and the field. |
| $\mechanical{\omega}_L$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Larmor angular frequency | The signed frequency parameter of the spin dynamics. |
| $\mechanical{\hbar}$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Reduced Planck constant | The angular momentum scale. |
