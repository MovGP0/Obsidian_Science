> [!TLDR]
> Spin components satisfy the angular momentum algebra and commute with the spatial operators. The spin quantum number can be integer or half-integer.

> [!definition] Definition 10.1. Spin operators
> The components $\mechanical{\hat S}_x,\mechanical{\hat S}_y,\mechanical{\hat S}_z$ are Hermitian operators with
>
> $$
> [\mechanical{\hat S}_i,\mechanical{\hat S}_j]
> =i\mechanical{\hbar}\epsilon_{ijk}\mechanical{\hat S}_k,
> $$
>
> with a sum over $k$. The spin components commute with position and momentum because they act on a separate factor of the state space.

The [[09.00 Orbital Angular Momentum|angular momentum algebra]] gives squared spin $\mechanical{\hbar}^{\,2}s(s+1)$ and axial projection $m_s\mechanical{\hbar}$, where $m_s=-s,-s+1,\ldots,s$. Orbital motion requires a single-valued azimuthal wave function and thus integer $\ell$. Spin representations also permit half-integer $s$.

| Particle | Spin $s$ |
| --- | --- |
| Photon, W boson, Z boson, gluon | $1$ |
| Electron, proton, neutron, quark, neutrino | $1/2$ |
| Pion, kaon | $0$ |
| Graviton (hypothetical) | $2$ |

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{\hat S}_i$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Spin component | The intrinsic angular momentum operator along axis $i$. |
| $\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf S}}^{\,2}$ | $\unit{J^2\,s^2}$ | Squared spin | The sum of squared spin components. |
| $s,m_s$ | $1$ | Spin quantum numbers | The labels of squared spin and its $z$ projection. |
| $\hat{\mathbf r},\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf p}}$ | $\unit m,\unit{kg\,m\,s^{-1}}$ | Position and momentum | The spatial operators that commute with spin. |
| $\mechanical{\hbar}$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Reduced Planck constant | The angular momentum scale. |
