---
title: "Spin Exercises"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Apply the Pauli matrices, Bloch sphere, and spinor rotations to measurements and spin dynamics.

1. Verify $\sigma_x\sigma_y=i\sigma_z$ and $\sigma_y\sigma_z=i\sigma_x$.
2. Calculate the probabilities of measuring $\pm\mechanical{\hbar}/2$ for $\mechanical{\hat S}_z$ in the state $\lvert+y\rangle$.  
   **Result.** $1/2$ and $1/2$.
3. Show that $\mechanical{\hat S}_x^{\,2}=\mechanical{\hat S}_y^{\,2}=\mechanical{\hat S}_z^{\,2}=(\mechanical{\hbar}^{\,2}/4)\hat I$ for spin $1/2$.
4. Write $\lvert\signal{\psi}\rangle=\lvert+z\rangle$ in terms of $\theta,\phi$ and give the corresponding point on the Bloch sphere.  
   **Result.** $\theta=0$ at the north pole, where $\phi$ is arbitrary.
5. Calculate $\langle\boldsymbol{\sigma}\rangle$ for $\lvert\signal{\psi}\rangle=(3\lvert+\rangle+4i\lvert-\rangle)/5$.  
   **Result.** $\hat{\mathbf n}=(0,24/25,-7/25)$.
6. Show explicitly that $e^{-i\sigma_z\alpha/2}\lvert+\rangle=e^{-i\alpha/2}\lvert+\rangle$.
7. At $t=0$, a spin in the field $\magnetic{\mathbf B}=\magnetic B\hat{\mathbf z}$ is in $\lvert+x\rangle$. Calculate its mean spin components.  
   **Result.** $\langle\mechanical{\hat S}_x(t)\rangle=(\mechanical{\hbar}/2)\cos(\mechanical{\omega}_L t)$, $\langle\mechanical{\hat S}_y(t)\rangle=-(\mechanical{\hbar}/2)\sin(\mechanical{\omega}_L t)$, and $\langle\mechanical{\hat S}_z(t)\rangle=0$.
8. Rotate $\lvert+z\rangle$ by $\pi/2$ about the positive $y$ axis. Calculate the final state.  
   **Result.** $\lvert+x\rangle$.
9. Calculate the probability of measuring $\lvert-z\rangle$ after rotating $\lvert+z\rangle$ by $\pi$ about the $x$ axis.  
   **Result.** One.
10. Apply $\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf S}}^{\,2}$ to the three triplet states and the singlet and verify the eigenvalues.  
    **Result.** $2\mechanical{\hbar}^{\,2}$ for the triplet and zero for the singlet.
11. **Berry phase.** The direction of a magnetic field $\magnetic{\mathbf B}(t)$ for a spin-$1/2$ system traces a closed path on the parameter sphere. In addition to the dynamical phase, the eigenstate with spin projection $+\mechanical{\hbar}/2$ along the field acquires a Berry phase equal to minus half the oriented solid angle. Prove this for an adiabatic conical path.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\sigma_i,\langle\boldsymbol{\sigma}\rangle$ | $1$ | Pauli matrices and Bloch vector | The component matrices and their expectation values. |
| $\mechanical{\hat S}_i,\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf S}}^{\,2}$ | $\unit{J\,s},\unit{J^2\,s^2}$ | Spin component and squared spin | The single-axis observables and the squared total spin. |
| $\theta,\phi,\alpha$ | $\unit{rad}$ | Angles | The Bloch polar angle, azimuth, and rotation angle. |
| $\magnetic{\mathbf B}$ | $\unit T$ | Magnetic flux density | The static or time-varying field in the exercises. |
| $\mechanical{\omega}_L$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Signed Larmor angular frequency | The frequency parameter of spin precession. |
| $\mechanical{\hbar}$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Reduced Planck constant | The spin angular momentum scale. |
| $t$ | $\unit s$ | Time | The time after preparation. |
