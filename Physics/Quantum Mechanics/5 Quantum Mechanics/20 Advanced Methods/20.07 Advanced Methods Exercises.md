title: "Advanced Methods Exercises"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Apply variational estimates, WKB quantization, and scattering theory to specific potentials.

1. For the harmonic oscillator with trial function $\signal\phi\propto e^{-\alpha x^2}$, calculate $\langle\energy{\hat H}\rangle$ as a function of $\alpha$. Verify that $\alpha^*=\mechanical m\omega/(2\mechanical{\hbar})$ in this exponent convention.
2. For the infinite well on $[0,L]$, use the trial function $\signal\phi(x)=Nx(L-x)$. Calculate $\langle\energy{\hat H}\rangle$ and compare it with the exact $\energy E_1$.  
   **Result.** $\langle\energy{\hat H}\rangle=5\mechanical{\hbar}^2/(\mechanical m L^2)=(10/\pi^2)\energy E_1\simeq1.013\energy E_1$.
3. Apply the hard-wall Bohr–Sommerfeld rule to an infinite well of width $L$ and recover $\energy E_n=n^2\pi^2\mechanical{\hbar}^2/(2\mechanical m L^2)$.
4. Apply WKB to the quartic oscillator $\energy V(x)=\lambda x^4$. Show that $\energy E_n\propto\lambda^{1/3}(n+1/2)^{4/3}$ for fixed $\mechanical m$ and $\mechanical{\hbar}$.
5. For scattering by a hard sphere of radius $a$, find the low-energy $s$-wave phase shift.  
   **Result.** $\delta_0=-ka$ on the branch continuous at $k=0$. The scattering length is $a$.
6. Show that the Gaussian potential gives a Born amplitude proportional to $e^{-q^2b^2/4}$. Use $\energy V(\mathbf r)=\energy V_0e^{-r^2/b^2}$ for the central three-dimensional problem. The source writes $\energy V(x)=\energy V_0e^{-x^2/b^2}$. Its one-dimensional Fourier transform has the same Gaussian dependence on the scalar wave-vector transfer.
7. For helium, use a product of two hydrogen-like $1s$ orbitals of effective charge number $Z_{\mathrm{eff}}$ and the spin singlet. Derive $\energy E(Z_{\mathrm{eff}})=2\unit{Ry}[Z_{\mathrm{eff}}^2-4Z_{\mathrm{eff}}+(5/8)Z_{\mathrm{eff}}]$ and minimize it.
8. For a spherical well with $\energy V(r)=-\energy V_0$ at $r<a$ and zero at $r>a$, derive the bound-state matching condition for $s$ states. Find the criterion for at least one bound state.
9. Integrate the first Born differential cross section for $\energy V(r)=V_0e^{-\mu r}/r$ to find the total cross section.  
   **Result.** $\sigma=16\pi\mechanical m^2 V_0^2/[\mechanical{\hbar}^4\mu^2(\mu^2+4k^2)]$.
10. Use WKB to estimate the first two levels for $\energy V(x)=\energy V_0(x/a)^6$. Compare the results for $n=0,1$ with a numerical solution.
11. **Regge trajectories.** Discuss the idea of analytically continuing the partial-wave angular momentum label $\ell$ to complex values.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\alpha$ | $\unit{m^{-2}}$ | Trial Gaussian parameter | Controls the width of the oscillator trial state. |
| $\lambda$ | $\unit{J\,m^{-4}}$ | Quartic coefficient | Sets the strength of the quartic potential. |
| $L,a,b$ | $\unit{m}$ | Lengths | The well width, sphere radius or potential scale, and Gaussian width. |
| $\energy V_0,V_0$ | $\unit{J}$ or $\unit{J\,m}$ | Potential strength | An energy for the Gaussian and well. The Yukawa convention uses energy times length. |
| $k,q,\mu$ | $\unit{m^{-1}}$ | Wave numbers and screening parameter | The incident wave number, transferred wave number, and inverse Yukawa range. |
| $\delta_0,\sigma$ | $\unit{rad},\unit{m^2}$ | Phase shift and cross section | The $s$-wave phase and the angle-integrated scattering cross section. |
| $Z_{\mathrm{eff}}$ | $1$ | Effective charge number | The adjustable parameter of the helium orbitals. |
