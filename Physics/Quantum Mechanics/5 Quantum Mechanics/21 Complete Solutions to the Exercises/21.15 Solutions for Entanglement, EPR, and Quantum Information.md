## Exercise 1 · Separability test

The state $(\lvert00\rangle+\lvert01\rangle+\lvert10\rangle+\lvert11\rangle)/2$ factors as $\lvert+\rangle\otimes\lvert+\rangle$. Its coefficient matrix in the computational basis is $\frac12\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix}$ and has rank one. This rank is necessary and sufficient for a pure bipartite state to be separable.

## Exercise 2 · Entangled state

The coefficients in the order $00,01,10,11$ are $(1,1,0,1)/\sqrt3$. The corresponding matrix is $\frac1{\sqrt3}\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$ with determinant $1/3$, so it has rank two. Equivalently, the conditions $ac=ad=bd=1/\sqrt3$ and $bc=0$ for a product state imply both $b=0$ and $b\ne0$. The state is entangled.

## Exercise 3 · z correlation of Φ⁺

Both $\lvert00\rangle$ and $\lvert11\rangle$ are eigenstates of $\sigma_z\otimes\sigma_z$ with eigenvalue $+1$. Their symmetric superposition $\lvert\signal\Phi^+\rangle$ also has eigenvalue and expectation $+1$. The outcomes along $z$ are perfectly correlated.

## Exercise 4 · x correlation of Φ⁺

In the $x$ basis, $\lvert\signal\Phi^+\rangle=(\lvert++\rangle+\lvert--\rangle)/\sqrt2$. Both terms have eigenvalue $+1$ under $\sigma_x\otimes\sigma_x$, so its expectation is $+1$.

## Exercise 5 · y correlation of Ψ⁻

Use $\sigma_y\lvert0\rangle=i\lvert1\rangle$ and $\sigma_y\lvert1\rangle=-i\lvert0\rangle$. The tensor product interchanges $\lvert01\rangle$ and $\lvert10\rangle$ with factor one. Thus it takes $\lvert\signal\Psi^-\rangle$ to $-\lvert\signal\Psi^-\rangle$ and has expectation $-1$.

## Exercise 6 · Singlet correlation for arbitrary axes

In the singlet, $\langle\signal\Psi^-\rvert\sigma_i\otimes\sigma_j\lvert\signal\Psi^-\rangle=-\delta_{ij}$ for $i,j=x,y,z$. Expanding the directional operators gives

$$
\langle(\mathbf a\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma)\otimes(\mathbf b\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma)\rangle_{\Psi^-}
=\sum_{i,j}a_i b_j(-\delta_{ij})=-\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b.
$$

## Exercise 7 · Coplanar CHSH settings

Use angles $0,\pi/2$ for $\mathbf a,\mathbf a'$ and $\pi/4,3\pi/4$ for $\mathbf b,\mathbf b'$. Since the correlation is minus the cosine of the angle difference,

$$
E(\mathbf a,\mathbf b)=-1/\sqrt2,\quad
E(\mathbf a,\mathbf b')=+1/\sqrt2,\quad
E(\mathbf a',\mathbf b)=E(\mathbf a',\mathbf b')=-1/\sqrt2.
$$

The combination $S=E(\mathbf a,\mathbf b)-E(\mathbf a,\mathbf b')+E(\mathbf a',\mathbf b)+E(\mathbf a',\mathbf b')$ is $-2\sqrt2$. Its magnitude saturates the Tsirelson bound.

## Exercise 8 · Hadamard gates on the target of a CNOT

On $\lvert00\rangle$, the first $\hat I\otimes H$ gives $\lvert0+\rangle$. The control is zero, so the CNOT leaves this state fixed. The last $\hat I\otimes H$ gives $\lvert00\rangle$ again. More generally, conjugation by $H$ on the target turns $\lvert c\rangle\langle c\rvert\otimes X^c$ into $\lvert c\rangle\langle c\rvert\otimes Z^c$. Summing over the two control values proves $(\hat I\otimes H)\operatorname{CNOT}(\hat I\otimes H)=\operatorname{CZ}$.

## Exercise 9 · Teleportation with Bell outcome Ψ⁺

Start with $\lvert\signal\psi\rangle_C\otimes\lvert\signal\Phi^+\rangle_{AB}$ for $\lvert\signal\psi\rangle=\alpha\lvert0\rangle+\beta\lvert1\rangle$. In the Bell basis of $CA$, the $\lvert\signal\Phi^+\rangle$ and $\lvert\signal\Phi^-\rangle$ components carry $\alpha\lvert0\rangle+\beta\lvert1\rangle$ and $\alpha\lvert0\rangle-\beta\lvert1\rangle$ on $B$, each with overall coefficient $1/2$. The $\lvert\signal\Psi^+\rangle$ and $\lvert\signal\Psi^-\rangle$ components carry $\beta\lvert0\rangle+\alpha\lvert1\rangle$ and $-\beta\lvert0\rangle+\alpha\lvert1\rangle$ on $B$, with the same overall coefficient. On outcome $\lvert\signal\Psi^+\rangle$, Bob's state is $X\lvert\signal\psi\rangle$. Applying $X$ recovers $\lvert\signal\psi\rangle$ with fidelity one in the ideal protocol.

## Exercise 10 · Reduced state of the singlet

In the projector onto $(\lvert01\rangle-\lvert10\rangle)/\sqrt2$, the off-diagonal terms vanish under either partial trace because $\langle0\mid1\rangle=0$. The remaining terms give $\hat\rho_A=(\lvert0\rangle\langle0\rvert+\lvert1\rangle\langle1\rvert)/2=\hat I/2$. The same holds for $\hat\rho_B$, so both local states are maximally mixed.

## Exercise 11 · Superdense coding

Start with $\lvert\signal\Phi^+\rangle=(\lvert00\rangle+\lvert11\rangle)/\sqrt2$. On Alice's qubit, $\hat I$ leaves it unchanged. The gate $X$ gives $(\lvert10\rangle+\lvert01\rangle)/\sqrt2=\lvert\signal\Psi^+\rangle$. The gate $Z$ gives $(\lvert00\rangle-\lvert11\rangle)/\sqrt2=\lvert\signal\Phi^-\rangle$. The gate $XZ$ gives $(\lvert10\rangle-\lvert01\rangle)/\sqrt2=-\lvert\signal\Psi^-\rangle$. Bob distinguishes the four outcomes in the Bell basis. A shared Bell pair and transmission of one qubit convey two classical bits.
