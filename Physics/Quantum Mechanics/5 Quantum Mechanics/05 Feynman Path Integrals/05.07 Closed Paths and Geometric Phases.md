---
title: "Closed Paths and Geometric Phases"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Two paths with the same endpoints can acquire a relative phase from the magnetic flux through their closed loop. This is the basis of the Aharonov–Bohm effect.

In a multiply connected region with a flat vector potential along the accessible paths, the phase can depend on their homotopy class. For a particle of charge $\electric q$ in a vector potential $\magnetic{\mathbf A}$, the action contains $\electric q\int\magnetic{\mathbf A}\cdot d\mathbf x$. Two paths $\gamma_1$ and $\gamma_2$ with the same endpoints have a relative phase factor

$$
e^{i\electric q\oint_{\gamma_1-\gamma_2}\magnetic{\mathbf A}\cdot d\mathbf x/\mechanical{\hbar}}
=e^{i\electric q\magnetic{\Phi}/\mechanical{\hbar}},
$$

where $\magnetic{\Phi}$ is the oriented magnetic flux through the loop $\gamma_1-\gamma_2$. This is the basis of the Aharonov–Bohm effect, which [[18.00 Geometric Phases]] treats in more detail.

> [!note] Homotopy and magnetic field
> The source describes the phase as depending only on the path's homotopy class. This holds for the loop phase when the magnetic field vanishes in the accessible region. If the field is nonzero there, deforming a loop can change the flux and therefore the phase.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\electric q$ | $\unit{C}$ | Electric charge | The charge of the propagating particle. |
| $\magnetic{\mathbf A}$ | $\unit{Wb\,m^{-1}}$ | Magnetic vector potential | The field whose line integral gives the magnetic contribution to the action. |
| $\magnetic{\Phi}$ | $\unit{Wb}$ | Magnetic flux | The oriented flux through the loop formed by the two paths. |
| $\mathbf x$ | $\unit{m}$ | Position | The coordinate along a path. |
| $\mechanical{\hbar}$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Reduced Planck constant | The constant that converts the action contribution into a phase. |
