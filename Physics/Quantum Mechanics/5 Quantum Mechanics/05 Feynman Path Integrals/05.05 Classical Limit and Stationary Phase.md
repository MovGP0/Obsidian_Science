---
title: "Classical Limit and Stationary Phase"
---

> [!TLDR]
> When the action scale is large compared with the reduced Planck constant, stationary-phase paths dominate the propagator. They satisfy the classical equations of motion.

When the typical action is large compared with $\mechanical{\hbar}$, the phase $\mechanical S[x]/\mechanical{\hbar}$ varies rapidly away from stationary paths in Eq. (5.3) of [[05.02 Time Discretization and the Feynman Formula]]. There, contributions largely cancel. With fixed endpoints, the condition $\delta\mechanical S=0$ is equivalent to the Euler–Lagrange equation

$$
\frac{d}{dt}\frac{\partial\energy L}{\partial\dot x}-\frac{\partial\energy L}{\partial x}=0.
$$

This is Hamilton's principle. The classical trajectory emerges as a path of stationary phase.

> [!theorem] Theorem 5.1. Semiclassical approximation
> For an isolated, nondegenerate classical trajectory, the source gives the approximation
>
> $$
> \signal K(x_f,T;x_i,0)\simeq
> \sqrt{\frac{1}{2\pi\mechanical{\hbar}}\left\lvert\det\left(-\frac{\partial^2\mechanical S_{\mathrm{cl}}}{\partial x_f\,\partial x_i}\right)\right\rvert}
> e^{i\mechanical S_{\mathrm{cl}}/\mechanical{\hbar}-i\pi\mu/2}.
> \tag{5.7}
> $$
>
> The Maslov index $\mu$ counts conjugate points along the classical trajectory.

> [!note] Phase convention in the source
> With $\mu$ defined as a count of conjugate points, Eq. (5.7) also needs the one-dimensional phase factor $e^{-i\pi/4}$ for positive propagation time. This follows by matching the free kernel in Eq. (5.4), where $\mu=0$ and the prefactor contains $1/\sqrt i$.

With that phase convention the formula is exact for quadratic Lagrangians, including the free particle and harmonic oscillator. The [[04.06 Ehrenfest Theorem|Ehrenfest theorem]] gives the differential counterpart. To first approximation, the mean position and momentum of a strongly localized packet follow the classical equations.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal K$ | $\unit{m^{-1}}$ | Propagator | The position-space kernel approximated by stationary phase. |
| $\mechanical S[x],\mechanical S_{\mathrm{cl}}$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Action | The action functional and its value on the classical path. |
| $\energy L$ | $\unit{J}$ | Lagrangian | The function integrated along a path to compute its action. |
| $x,x_i,x_f$ | $\unit{m}$ | Positions | The position along a path and the initial and final positions. |
| $t,T$ | $\unit{s}$ | Time | The time along a path and the full propagation time. |
| $\mechanical{\hbar}$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Reduced Planck constant | The reference scale for action. |
| $\mu$ | $1$ | Maslov index | The number of conjugate points on the classical trajectory. |
