---
title: "Conversions"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Use these rounded factors to convert between energy, spectroscopic wavenumber, frequency, wavelength, and temperature.

| Conversion | Rounded value |
| --- | --- |
| Electronvolt to joules | $\quantity{1}{eV}\approx\quantity{1.602\times10^{-19}}{J}$ |
| Energy to spectroscopic wavenumber | $\quantity{1}{eV}/(\mechanical h\mechanical c)\approx\quantity{8066}{cm^{-1}}$ |
| Energy to frequency | $\quantity{1}{eV}/\mechanical h\approx\quantity{2.418\times10^{14}}{Hz}$ |
| Thermal energy at room temperature | $\thermal{k_B}\thermal T\approx\quantity{25.7}{meV}$ at $\thermal T=\quantity{298}{K}$ |
| Wavelength-energy product | $\mechanical h\mechanical c\approx\quantity{1.240}{\mu m\,eV}$ |
| Reduced wavelength-energy product | $\mechanical{\hbar}\mechanical c\approx\quantity{197.3}{eV\,nm}$ |

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical h$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Planck constant | Relates photon energy to frequency. |
| $\mechanical{\hbar}$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Reduced Planck constant | Planck constant divided by $2\pi$. |
| $\mechanical c$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Speed of light | Speed of light in vacuum. |
| $\thermal{k_B}$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Boltzmann constant | Relates temperature to thermal energy. |
| $\thermal T$ | $\unit{K}$ | Temperature | Absolute temperature. |
