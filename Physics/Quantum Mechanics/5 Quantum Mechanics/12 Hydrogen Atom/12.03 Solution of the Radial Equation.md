---
title: "Solution of the Radial Equation"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The radial equation becomes an associated Laguerre equation. A normalizable solution requires the series to terminate and gives the hydrogen energies proportional to $-1/n^2$.

The **Bohr radius** is

$$
a_0=\frac{4\pi\electric{\varepsilon}_0\mechanical{\hbar}^{\,2}}
{\mechanical m_e\electric e^{\,2}}\simeq5.29\times10^{-11}\,\unit m.
$$

The Hartree energy is $\energy{E_{\mathrm h}}=\electric e^{\,2}/(4\pi\electric{\varepsilon}_0a_0)=\alpha^2\mechanical m_e\signal c^2\simeq27.2\,\unit{eV}$. The Rydberg energy is $\energy{E_{\mathrm{Ry}}}=\energy{E_{\mathrm h}}/2\simeq13.6\,\unit{eV}$.

For $\energy E<0$, set $\signal{\kappa}=\sqrt{-2\mechanical m_e\energy E}/\mechanical{\hbar}$ and $\rho=\signal{\kappa}r$. The reduced radial equation is

$$
u''(\rho)-\left[\frac{\ell(\ell+1)}{\rho^2}-\frac{\rho_0}{\rho}+1\right]u(\rho)=0,
\qquad
\rho_0=\frac{2}{a_0\signal{\kappa}}.
$$

## Asymptotic behavior and quantization

At large $\rho$, $u''\simeq u$ and the decaying solution behaves as $e^{-\rho}$. At small $\rho$, the centrifugal term gives powers $\rho^{\ell+1}$ and $\rho^{-\ell}$. The first satisfies the regular boundary condition. The second either diverges for $\ell>0$ or fails $u(0)=0$ for $\ell=0$.

Put $u(\rho)=\rho^{\ell+1}e^{-\rho}L(\rho)$. Substitution gives

$$
\rho L''+[2(\ell+1)-2\rho]L'+[\rho_0-2(\ell+1)]L=0.
$$

This is the associated Laguerre equation after rescaling the argument to $2\rho$. The series must terminate, because otherwise the regular solution has an $e^{2\rho}$ factor at large $\rho$ and the full radial function grows. Termination requires $\rho_0=2n$ with $n=n_r+\ell+1$ and $n_r=0,1,2,\ldots$ the degree of the radial polynomial. Hence $\signal{\kappa}=1/(na_0)$ and

$$
\energy E_n=-\frac{\mechanical{\hbar}^{\,2}}{2\mechanical m_ea_0^2n^2}
\simeq-\frac{13.6\,\unit{eV}}{n^2}.
$$

At fixed $n$, the orbital label is $\ell=0,1,\ldots,n-1$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $a_0,r$ | $\unit m$ | Bohr radius and radial distance | The natural hydrogen length and the electron–proton separation. |
| $\energy{E_{\mathrm h}},\energy{E_{\mathrm{Ry}}},\energy E_n$ | $\unit{J}$ | Energy scales and levels | The Hartree, Rydberg, and bound-state energy. |

| $\electric e,\electric{\varepsilon}_0$ | $\unit C,\unit{F\,m^{-1}}$ | Charge and permittivity | The constants of the Coulomb interaction. |
| $\alpha,\signal c$ | $1,\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Fine-structure constant and speed of light | The coupling and speed in the Hartree expression. |
