---
title: "Separation of Radial and Angular Variables"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Separate the spherical harmonics from the radial factor. Multiplying the radial function by $r$ gives a one-dimensional equation on the positive half-line.

Since the Coulomb potential depends only on $r$, use $\signal{\psi}(\mathbf r)=R(r)Y_{\ell m}(\theta,\phi)$. The [[09.05 L̂² as the Angular Laplace Operator|angular Laplacian]] reduces the stationary equation to

$$
-\frac{\mechanical{\hbar}^{\,2}}{2\mechanical m_e}
\left[\frac1{r^2}\partial_r(r^2\partial_rR)-\frac{\ell(\ell+1)}{r^2}R\right]
-\frac{\electric e^{\,2}}{4\pi\electric{\varepsilon}_0r}R=\energy E R.
$$

Put $u(r)=rR(r)$. The radial equation becomes

$$
-\frac{\mechanical{\hbar}^{\,2}}{2\mechanical m_e}u''
+\left[\frac{\mechanical{\hbar}^{\,2}\ell(\ell+1)}{2\mechanical m_e r^2}
-\frac{\electric e^{\,2}}{4\pi\electric{\varepsilon}_0r}\right]u
=\energy E u.
$$

On $0\le r<\infty$ the effective potential is

$$
\energy V_{\mathrm{eff}}(r)=-\frac{\electric e^{\,2}}{4\pi\electric{\varepsilon}_0r}
+\frac{\mechanical{\hbar}^{\,2}\ell(\ell+1)}{2\mechanical m_e r^2}.
$$

The second term is the **centrifugal barrier**. For $\ell>0$, it is repulsive and dominates at small $r$. The Coulomb term dominates at large $r$. For $\ell=0$, the effective potential is purely Coulombic and tends to $-\infty$ at the origin.

Regularity of $R$ at $r=0$ gives $u(0)=0$. A bound state also has $u(r)\to0$ as $r\to\infty$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $r,\theta,\phi$ | $\unit m,\unit{rad},\unit{rad}$ | Spherical coordinates | The radius, polar angle, and azimuth of the relative position. |
| $\signal{\psi},R,u$ | $\unit{m^{-3/2}},\unit{m^{-3/2}},\unit{m^{-1/2}}$ | Wave function and radial factors | The position amplitude, radial factor, and reduced radial amplitude. |
| $Y_{\ell m},\ell,m$ | $1$ | Angular function and indices | The spherical harmonic and its quantum numbers. |
| $\energy V_{\mathrm{eff}},\energy E$ | $\unit{J}$ | Effective potential and energy | The potential and eigenvalue of the reduced radial equation. |
| $\mechanical m_e$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Electron mass | The mass used in the approximate relative equation. |
| $\electric e,\electric{\varepsilon}_0$ | $\unit C,\unit{F\,m^{-1}}$ | Charge and permittivity | The constants in the Coulomb term. |
| $\mechanical{\hbar}$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Reduced Planck constant | The action scale in the radial kinetic and centrifugal terms. |
