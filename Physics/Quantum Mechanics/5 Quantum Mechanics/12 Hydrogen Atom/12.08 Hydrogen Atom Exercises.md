---
title: "Hydrogen Atom Exercises"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Calculate hydrogen expectation values, transitions, and energy corrections.

1. Verify that $\signal{\psi}_{100}$ is normalized.
2. Calculate $\langle r\rangle_{100}$ and $\langle r^2\rangle_{100}$.  
   **Result.** $3a_0/2$ and $3a_0^2$.
3. Calculate $\langle1/r\rangle_{100}$ and $\langle1/r^2\rangle_{100}$.  
   **Result.** $1/a_0$ and $2/a_0^2$.
4. Calculate the energy of the Lyman-$\alpha$ transition $n=2\to n=1$.  
   **Result.** $10.2\,\unit{eV}$.
5. Calculate the wavelength of the $2p\to1s$ transition.  
   **Result.** $\signal{\lambda}\simeq121.6\,\unit{nm}$.
6. Count the states at $n=2$, both without and with electron spin.  
   **Result.** Four without spin and eight with spin.
7. For $\mathrm{He}^+$ with $Z=2$, calculate the ground energy and the scaled Bohr radius $a_0'$.  
   **Result.** $\energy E_1\simeq-4(13.6\,\unit{eV})=-54.4\,\unit{eV}$ and $a_0'=a_0/2$.
8. Verify the virial theorem $2\langle\energy T\rangle+\langle\energy V\rangle=0$ for $\signal{\psi}_{100}$.
9. For $\signal{\psi}_{210}$, calculate the probability of finding the electron in $0<r<a_0$.  
   **Result.** $P=(24-65/\mathrm e)/24\simeq3.7\times10^{-3}$.
10. Estimate the ionization energy of ground-state $\mathrm{Li}^{2+}$.  
    **Result.** $Z^2(13.6\,\unit{eV})=122.4\,\unit{eV}$.
11. **Relativistic corrections.** Calculate the first-order perturbation due to $-\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf p}}^{\,4}/(8\mechanical m_e^{\,3}\signal c^2)$ for $\signal{\psi}_{100}$. It is of relative order $\alpha^2$ compared with the nonrelativistic binding energy.  
    **Hint.** Use $\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf p}}^{\,2}\signal{\psi}_{100}=2\mechanical m_e(\energy E-\energy V)\signal{\psi}_{100}$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{\psi}_{100},\signal{\psi}_{210}$ | $\unit{m^{-3/2}}$ | Position wave functions | The $1s$ and $2p$ amplitudes used in the exercises. |
| $r,a_0,a_0'$ | $\unit m$ | Radial lengths | The separation, Bohr radius, and radius scale for a hydrogen-like ion. |
| $\energy E_1,\energy E,\energy T,\energy V$ | $\unit{J}$ | Energy quantities | Ground-state or general total energy and the kinetic and potential energies. |
| $\signal{\lambda}$ | $\unit m$ | Wavelength | The photon wavelength of an emission line. |
| $Z,n$ | $1$ | Nuclear charge number and level index | The number of protons and the principal quantum number. |
| $P$ | $1$ | Probability | The chance of finding the electron in the stated radial interval. |
| $\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf p}}$ | $\unit{kg\,m\,s^{-1}}$ | Momentum operator | The operator in the relativistic kinetic correction. |
| $\alpha$ | $1$ | Fine-structure constant | The coupling used to compare the relativistic and nonrelativistic energy scales. |
| $\mechanical m_e,\signal c$ | $\unit{kg},\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Electron mass and speed of light | The constants in the kinetic correction. |
