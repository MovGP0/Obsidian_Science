> [!TLDR]
> Look up well levels, barrier transmission factors, and one-dimensional quantization rules.

| Model | Formula |
| --- | --- |
| Infinite well on $[0,L]$ | $\energy E_n=n^2\pi^2\mechanical{\hbar}^2/(2\mechanical mL^2)$, $\signal\psi_n=\sqrt{2/L}\sin(n\pi x/L)$ for $n\ge1$ |
| Thick rectangular barrier with $\energy E<\energy V_0$ | $T\simeq[16\energy E(\energy V_0-\energy E)/\energy V_0^2]e^{-2\kappa a}$ |
| WKB tunneling | $T\simeq\exp[-2\int_{x_1}^{x_2}\kappa(x)\,\mathrm dx]$ |
| Attractive delta $\energy V=-\alpha\delta(x)$ | $\energy E_0=-\mechanical m\alpha^2/(2\mechanical{\hbar}^2)$ |
| Bohr–Sommerfeld rule for two simple turning points | $\oint\mechanical p\,\mathrm dx=2\pi\mechanical{\hbar}(n+1/2)$ |

In the forbidden region, $\kappa(x)=\sqrt{2\mechanical m(\energy V(x)-\energy E)}/\mechanical{\hbar}$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\energy E_n,\energy E,\energy V_0$ | $\unit J$ | Energies | The well level, particle energy, and barrier height. |
| $L,a$ | $\unit m$ | Widths | The width of the well and the rectangular barrier. |
| $T,\kappa$ | $1,\unit{m^{-1}}$ | Transmission and decay constant | The flux ratio and the local forbidden-region decay scale. |
| $\alpha$ | $\unit{J\,m}$ | Delta strength | The coefficient of the attractive delta. |
