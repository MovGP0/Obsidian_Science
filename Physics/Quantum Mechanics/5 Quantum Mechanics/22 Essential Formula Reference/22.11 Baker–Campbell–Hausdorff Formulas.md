---
title: "Baker–Campbell–Hausdorff Formulas"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Commutators give the correction when you factor operator exponentials or conjugate an operator by an exponential.

If $[\hat A,[\hat A,\hat B]]=[\hat B,[\hat A,\hat B]]=0$, then

$$
e^{\hat A+\hat B}=e^{\hat A}e^{\hat B}e^{-[\hat A,\hat B]/2}.
$$

In general, conjugation gives

$$
e^{\hat A}\hat B e^{-\hat A}=\hat B+[\hat A,\hat B]+\frac{1}{2!}[\hat A,[\hat A,\hat B]]+\cdots.
$$
