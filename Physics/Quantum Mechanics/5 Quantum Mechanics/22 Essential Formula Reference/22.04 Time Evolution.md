---
title: "Time Evolution"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The unitary propagator supplies energy phases. The probability current and Ehrenfest equations describe the corresponding flow and mean motion.

| Result | Formula |
| --- | --- |
| Time-independent Hamiltonian | $\hat U(t,t_0)=e^{-i\energy{\hat H}(t-t_0)/\mechanical{\hbar}}$ |
| Energy-basis evolution from $t_0=0$ | $\lvert\signal\psi(t)\rangle=\sum_n c_n e^{-i\energy E_n t/\mechanical{\hbar}}\lvert E_n\rangle$ |
| Probability current | $\mathbf J=\frac{\mechanical{\hbar}}{2\mechanical m i}(\signal\psi^*\nabla\signal\psi-\signal\psi\nabla\signal\psi^*)$ |
| Continuity equation | $\partial_t\rho+\nabla\cdot\mathbf J=0$, with $\rho=\lvert\signal\psi\rvert^2$ |
| Ehrenfest equations in one dimension | $\mathrm d\langle\hat x\rangle/\mathrm dt=\langle\mechanical{\hat p}\rangle/\mechanical m$, $\mathrm d\langle\mechanical{\hat p}\rangle/\mathrm dt=-\langle\energy V'(\hat x)\rangle$ |

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\energy{\hat H},\energy E_n$ | $\unit J$ | Hamiltonian and eigenenergy | The generator and its spectral values. |
| $\rho,\mathbf J$ | $\unit{m^{-3}},\unit{m^{-2}\,s^{-1}}$ in three dimensions | Probability density and current | The local probability and its flux density. |
| $\hat x,\mechanical{\hat p}$ | $\unit m,\unit{kg\,m\,s^{-1}}$ | Position and momentum | Their mean motion follows the Ehrenfest equations. |
