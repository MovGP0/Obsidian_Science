title: "Uncertainty"

> [!TLDR]
> The Robertson, Heisenberg, and Mandelstam–Tamm inequalities bound spreads or characteristic evolution times.

| Relation | Formula |
| --- | --- |
| Robertson | $\Delta A\,\Delta B\ge\frac12\lvert\langle[\hat A,\hat B]\rangle\rvert$ |
| Heisenberg position and momentum | $\Delta x\,\Delta\mechanical p\ge\mechanical{\hbar}/2$ |
| Mandelstam–Tamm | $\Delta\energy E\,\tau_B\ge\mechanical{\hbar}/2$ |

Here $\tau_B=\Delta B/\lvert\mathrm d\langle\hat B\rangle/\mathrm dt\rvert$ for an observable with no explicit time dependence and nonzero rate of change.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\Delta x,\Delta\mechanical p$ | $\unit m,\unit{kg\,m\,s^{-1}}$ | Position and momentum spreads | The standard deviations of conjugate observables. |
| $\Delta\energy E,\tau_B$ | $\unit J,\unit s$ | Energy spread and evolution time | Enter the Mandelstam–Tamm bound. |
