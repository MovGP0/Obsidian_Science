---
title: "Harmonic Oscillator"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Use ladder operators to express the oscillator Hamiltonian and generate its eigenstates.

| Result | Formula |
| --- | --- |
| Annihilation operator | $\hat a=\sqrt{\mechanical m\omega/(2\mechanical{\hbar})}\,[\hat x+i\mechanical{\hat p}/(\mechanical m\omega)]$ |
| Algebra and number operator | $[\hat a,\hat a^\dagger]=\hat I$, $\hat N=\hat a^\dagger\hat a$ |
| Hamiltonian and energies | $\energy{\hat H}=\mechanical{\hbar}\omega(\hat N+1/2)$, $\energy E_n=\mechanical{\hbar}\omega(n+1/2)$ |
| Ladder action | $\hat a\lvert n\rangle=\sqrt n\lvert n-1\rangle$, $\hat a^\dagger\lvert n\rangle=\sqrt{n+1}\lvert n+1\rangle$ |
| Position eigenfunctions | $\psi_n(x)\propto H_n(x/\mechanical{x_0})e^{-x^2/(2\mechanical{x_0}^2)}$, $\mechanical{x_0}=\sqrt{\mechanical{\hbar}/(\mechanical m\omega)}$ |
| Coherent states | $\lvert\alpha\rangle=e^{-\lvert\alpha\rvert^2/2}\sum_{n=0}^\infty\frac{\alpha^n}{\sqrt{n!}}\lvert n\rangle$, $\hat a\lvert\alpha\rangle=\alpha\lvert\alpha\rangle$ |

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\energy E_n$ | $\unit{J}$ | Energy | Oscillator level $n$. |
| $\mechanical m$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | Oscillating particle mass. |
| $\omega$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Angular frequency | Oscillator frequency in radians per second. |
| $\mechanical{x_0}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Oscillator length | Scale of the position wavefunctions. |
| $\mechanical{\hbar}$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Reduced Planck constant | Quantum of action. |
