---
title: "Hydrogen Atom"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The principal quantum number sets the hydrogen energy. Orbital quantum numbers set the degeneracy, radial averages, and electric dipole selection rules.

| Result | Formula |
| --- | --- |
| Energy spectrum | $\energy E_n=-\energy{\mathrm{Ry}}/n^2$, $\energy{\mathrm{Ry}}=\quantity{13.606}{eV}$ |
| Bohr radius | $\mechanical{a_0}=4\pi\electric{\varepsilon_0}\mechanical{\hbar}^2/(\mechanical{m_e}\electric e^2)\approx\quantity{5.292\times10^{-11}}{m}$ |
| Quantum numbers | $n\ge 1$, $\ell=0,\ldots,n-1$, $m=-\ell,\ldots,\ell$ |
| Degeneracy without spin | $n^2$ |
| Radial averages | $\langle\mechanical r\rangle_{n\ell}=(\mechanical{a_0}/2)[3n^2-\ell(\ell+1)]$, $\langle1/\mechanical r\rangle_{n\ell}=1/(n^2\mechanical{a_0})$ |
| Electric dipole (E1) selection rules | $\Delta\ell=\pm1$, $\Delta m=0,\pm1$ |

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\energy E_n$ | $\unit{eV}$ | Energy | Bound-state energy at principal quantum number $n$. |
| $\energy{\mathrm{Ry}}$ | $\unit{eV}$ | Rydberg energy | Energy scale of the hydrogen spectrum. |
| $\mechanical{a_0}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Bohr radius | Atomic length scale. |
| $\mechanical r$ | $\unit{m}$ | Radius | Distance from the nucleus. |
| $\electric{\varepsilon_0}$ | $\unit{F\,m^{-1}}$ | Vacuum permittivity | Electric constant in SI. |
| $\mechanical{\hbar}$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Reduced Planck constant | Quantum of action. |
| $\mechanical{m_e}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Electron mass | Rest mass of the electron. |
| $\electric e$ | $\unit{C}$ | Elementary charge | Magnitude of the electron charge. |
