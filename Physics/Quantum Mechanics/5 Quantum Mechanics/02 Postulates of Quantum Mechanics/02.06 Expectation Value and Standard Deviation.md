---
title: "Expectation Value and Standard Deviation"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The expectation value is the probability-weighted mean of measurement results. The standard deviation gives their spread.

For an observable $\hat A$ in a normalized state $\lvert\psi\rangle$, the expectation value over repeated measurements on identically prepared systems is

$$
\langle\hat A\rangle_\psi=\langle\psi\mid\hat A\mid\psi\rangle.
$$

For a discrete spectrum, use $\hat A=\sum_n a_n\lvert a_n\rangle\langle a_n\rvert$. Then $\langle\psi\mid\hat A\mid\psi\rangle=\sum_n a_n\lvert\langle a_n\mid\psi\rangle\rvert^2=\sum_n a_nP_n$. This is the mean of the possible outcomes weighted by their probabilities.

The **standard deviation** measures the spread of the outcomes.

$$
\Delta A=\sqrt{\langle\hat A^2\rangle-\langle\hat A\rangle^2}
=\sqrt{\langle\psi\mid(\hat A-\langle\hat A\rangle\hat I)^2\mid\psi\rangle}.
$$

An eigenstate of $\hat A$ has $\Delta A=0$, so its measurement result is certain. A superposition with nonzero components at different eigenvalues has $\Delta A>0$.

## Example. Two eigenstates

Let $\lvert\psi\rangle=\cos\alpha\lvert u\rangle+e^{i\phi}\sin\alpha\lvert d\rangle$, where $\lvert u\rangle$ and $\lvert d\rangle$ are orthonormal eigenstates of $\hat A$ with eigenvalues $a_u$ and $a_d$. Then

$$
\begin{aligned}
\langle\hat A\rangle&=a_u\cos^2\alpha+a_d\sin^2\alpha,\\
\langle\hat A^2\rangle&=a_u^2\cos^2\alpha+a_d^2\sin^2\alpha,\\
(\Delta A)^2&=(a_u-a_d)^2\cos^2\alpha\sin^2\alpha
=\tfrac14(a_u-a_d)^2\sin^2(2\alpha).
\end{aligned}
$$

For $0\le\alpha\le\pi/2$, the spread is greatest at $\alpha=\pi/4$ and zero at the two endpoints. The relative phase $\phi$ does not affect the moments of $\hat A$ because these states are expanded in its eigenbasis.

> [!note] Source wording
> The source generalizes the phase independence to any single observable. A single observable with nonzero off-diagonal elements in the preparation basis can detect the relative phase.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\hat A,a_n,a_u,a_d,\langle\hat A\rangle,\Delta A$ | — | Observable and measurement statistics | The observable, outcomes, mean, and standard deviation have the same unit, which depends on the observable. |
| $P_n$ | $1$ | Outcome probability | The probability of eigenvalue $a_n$. |
| $\alpha,\phi$ | $\unit{rad}$ | Preparation angles | The angle that controls the weights and the relative phase of the two components. |
