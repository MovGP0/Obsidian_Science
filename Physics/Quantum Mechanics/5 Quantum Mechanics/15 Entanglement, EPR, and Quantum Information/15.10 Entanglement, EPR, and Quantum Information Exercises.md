---
title: "Entanglement, EPR, and Quantum Information Exercises"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Use Bell states, CHSH correlations, and elementary gates to analyze quantum information protocols.

1. Determine whether $\lvert\signal{\psi}\rangle=(\lvert00\rangle+\lvert01\rangle+\lvert10\rangle+\lvert11\rangle)/2$ is separable or entangled.  
   **Result.** It is separable, with $\lvert\signal{\psi}\rangle=\frac{\lvert0\rangle+\lvert1\rangle}{\sqrt2}\otimes\frac{\lvert0\rangle+\lvert1\rangle}{\sqrt2}$.
2. Determine whether $\lvert\signal{\psi}\rangle=(\lvert00\rangle+\lvert11\rangle+\lvert01\rangle)/\sqrt3$ is separable or entangled.  
   **Result.** It is entangled. A product would require $ac=ad=bd=1/\sqrt3$ and $bc=0$, which are inconsistent.
3. Calculate $\langle\signal{\Phi}^+\rvert\hat\sigma_z\otimes\hat\sigma_z\lvert\signal{\Phi}^+\rangle$.  
   **Result.** $+1$.
4. Calculate $\langle\signal{\Phi}^+\rvert\hat\sigma_x\otimes\hat\sigma_x\lvert\signal{\Phi}^+\rangle$.  
   **Result.** $+1$.
5. Calculate $\langle\signal{\Psi}^-\rvert\hat\sigma_y\otimes\hat\sigma_y\lvert\signal{\Psi}^-\rangle$.  
   **Result.** $-1$.
6. Use the identity $(\mathbf a\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma)(\mathbf b\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma)=(\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b)\hat I+i(\mathbf a\times\mathbf b)\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma$ to verify the singlet correlation.  
   **Result.** $\langle\signal{\Psi}^-\rvert\sigma_{\mathbf a}\otimes\sigma_{\mathbf b}\lvert\signal{\Psi}^-\rangle=-\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b$, where $\sigma_{\mathbf a}=\mathbf a\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma$ and $\sigma_{\mathbf b}=\mathbf b\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma$.
7. Calculate the CHSH combination $S$ on the singlet for coplanar axes $\mathbf a,\mathbf a',\mathbf b,\mathbf b'$ at angles $0,\pi/2,\pi/4,3\pi/4$ respectively.  
   **Result.** $S=-2\sqrt2$.
8. Apply $(\hat I\otimes H)\operatorname{CNOT}(\hat I\otimes H)$ to $\lvert00\rangle$ and identify the circuit.  
   **Result.** A Hadamard on the target conjugates CNOT to CZ. The output for $\lvert00\rangle$ is $\lvert00\rangle$.
9. Work through teleportation of $\lvert\signal{\psi}\rangle=\alpha\lvert0\rangle+\beta\lvert1\rangle$ when Alice measures $\lvert\signal{\Psi}^+\rangle$ in the Bell basis.  
   **Result.** Bob has $\beta\lvert0\rangle+\alpha\lvert1\rangle$. The $X$ gate recovers $\alpha\lvert0\rangle+\beta\lvert1\rangle$.
10. Show that tracing out either qubit of $\lvert\signal{\Psi}^-\rangle$ gives $\hat I/2$.  
    **Result.** $\rho_A=(\lvert0\rangle\langle0\rvert+\lvert1\rangle\langle1\rvert)/2=\hat I/2$, and the same result holds for $\rho_B$.
11. Apply Alice's four superdense coding operations to her qubit of $\lvert\signal{\Phi}^+\rangle$. Check the correspondence with the Bell states.  
    **Result.** $\hat I$ gives $\lvert\signal{\Phi}^+\rangle$, $X$ gives $\lvert\signal{\Psi}^+\rangle$, $Z$ gives $\lvert\signal{\Phi}^-\rangle$, and $XZ$ gives $-\lvert\signal{\Psi}^-\rangle$. The global phase leaves the measurement probabilities unchanged.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\alpha,\beta$ | $1$ | State amplitudes | The coefficients of the input qubit in the computational basis. |
| $\mathbf a,\mathbf a',\mathbf b,\mathbf b'$ | $1$ | Measurement axes | Unit vectors that specify the directions in the CHSH test. |
| $E,S$ | $1$ | Correlation and CHSH combination | The singlet correlation and the combination of four correlations. |
| $\rho_A,\rho_B$ | $1$ | Reduced density operators | The individual qubit states obtained by a partial trace. |
