---
title: "Quantum Teleportation"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Quantum teleportation transfers a qubit state using a shared Bell pair and two classical bits. The bits tell the receiver which gate to apply.

Alice has qubit $C$ in an unknown state

$$
\lvert\signal{\psi}\rangle=\alpha\lvert0\rangle+\beta\lvert1\rangle,\qquad
\lvert\alpha\rvert^2+\lvert\beta\rvert^2=1.
$$

Alice and Bob also share a Bell pair in state $\lvert\signal{\Phi}^+\rangle_{AB}$. Alice holds $A$ and Bob holds $B$. The joint state is

$$
\lvert\signal{\Psi}\rangle
=\lvert\signal{\psi}\rangle_C\otimes\lvert\signal{\Phi}^+\rangle_{AB}
=\frac{1}{\sqrt2}(\alpha\lvert0\rangle+\beta\lvert1\rangle)_C\otimes(\lvert00\rangle+\lvert11\rangle)_{AB}.
$$

Group Alice's qubits $C$ and $A$ in the Bell basis.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\lvert\signal{\Psi}\rangle=\tfrac12\big[&
\lvert\signal{\Phi}^+\rangle_{CA}(\alpha\lvert0\rangle+\beta\lvert1\rangle)_B
+\lvert\signal{\Phi}^-\rangle_{CA}(\alpha\lvert0\rangle-\beta\lvert1\rangle)_B\\
&+\lvert\signal{\Psi}^+\rangle_{CA}(\beta\lvert0\rangle+\alpha\lvert1\rangle)_B
+\lvert\signal{\Psi}^-\rangle_{CA}(-\beta\lvert0\rangle+\alpha\lvert1\rangle)_B\big].
\end{aligned}
\tag{15.3}
$$

Alice measures $C$ and $A$ in the Bell basis. Each of the four outcomes has probability $1/4$. The outcome selects one of the four corresponding states of $B$ in Eq. (15.3). Alice sends two classical bits to Bob. In the circuit below, $a$ is the measurement bit from $C$ and $b$ is the bit from $A$.

| Bell outcome | Bits $ab$ | Bob's correction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $\lvert\signal{\Phi}^+\rangle$ | $00$ | $\hat I$ |
| $\lvert\signal{\Phi}^-\rangle$ | $10$ | $Z$ |
| $\lvert\signal{\Psi}^+\rangle$ | $01$ | $X$ |
| $\lvert\signal{\Psi}^-\rangle$ | $11$ | $ZX$ |

Bob applies $Z^aX^b$ to recover $\alpha\lvert0\rangle+\beta\lvert1\rangle$.

> [!note] Bit order in the source
> The source prose lists $00,01,10,11$ in the table's Bell-state order. That list corresponds to bit order $ba$ with the circuit's labels. For outcome $11$, the source prose gives $XZ$ and the circuit gives $ZX$. These differ by a global phase.

![[quantum-mechanics-fig-15-3-teleportation-circuit.svg|**Fig. 15.3** Quantum teleportation circuit. The first Hadamard and CNOT prepare the shared Bell pair $AB$. Alice's CNOT and Hadamard map the Bell basis of $CA$ to the computational basis. The measurements give bits $a$ from $C$ and $b$ from $A$. The dashed double lines carry these bits to Bob. He applies $X^b$ and then $Z^a$ to recover the input state.]]

Alice's measurement consumes the original state, in agreement with the [[15.06 No-Cloning Theorem|no-cloning theorem]]. Before receiving the classical bits, Bob has the maximally mixed state $\hat I/2$, so the protocol respects relativistic causality. The protocol transfers a state described by the continuous Bloch angles $(\theta,\varphi)$ by using two classical bits and a shared Bell pair. The classical bits and the pre-existing correlations jointly encode the transferred state.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\alpha,\beta$ | $1$ | State amplitudes | The coefficients of the two computational basis vectors. |
| $a,b$ | $1$ | Classical measurement bits | The measurement results from Alice's qubits $C$ and $A$. |
| $\theta,\varphi$ | $\unit{rad}$ | Bloch angles | The two angles that specify the input state up to a global phase. |
