---
title: "Particle on a Sphere"
---

> [!TLDR]
> A particle constrained to a sphere is a quantum rigid rotor. Its level energies are proportional to $\ell(\ell+1)$ and have degeneracy $2\ell+1$.

Constrain a particle to the surface $r=R$. The Hamiltonian is purely kinetic, and only the angular term $\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf L}}^{\,2}/(2\mechanical mR^2)$ remains. The energies are

$$
\energy E_\ell=\frac{\mechanical{\hbar}^{\,2}\ell(\ell+1)}{2\mechanical mR^2},
\qquad \ell=0,1,2,\ldots.
$$

The degeneracy of each level is $2\ell+1$. The ground state is the uniform harmonic $Y_{00}$ and has zero energy. The first excited level contains $Y_{1,-1},Y_{10},Y_{11}$ and has $\energy E_1=\mechanical{\hbar}^{\,2}/(\mechanical mR^2)$. The ratio is $\energy E_\ell/\energy E_1=\ell(\ell+1)/2$.

## Example. Rotational absorption

The same rigid-rotor spectrum describes rotational transitions of linear molecules. Far-infrared absorption spectra of CO, HCl, and $\mathrm{N_2O}$ show approximately equally spaced lines in spectroscopic wavenumber $\signal{\tilde\nu}$. The spacing $2\signal B$, with $\signal B=\mechanical{\hbar}/(4\pi\signal c\mechanical I)$, follows from $\ell\mapsto\ell+1$. Here $\mechanical I$ is the moment of inertia.

> [!note] Molecular examples
> The source groups all three examples under diatomic molecules. $\mathrm{N_2O}$ is a linear triatomic molecule and also fits the linear-rotor model.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $R$ | $\unit m$ | Sphere radius | The fixed distance from the origin. |
| $\energy E_\ell$ | $\unit{J}$ | Rotor energy | The eigenvalue of the angular kinetic term. |
| $\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf L}}^{\,2}$ | $\unit{J^2\,s^2}$ | Squared angular momentum | The operator that gives the rotor spectrum. |
| $\ell,m$ | $1$ | Angular quantum numbers | The rotor level and its axial projection label. |
| $\mechanical m,\mechanical I$ | $\unit{kg},\unit{kg\,m^2}$ | Mass and moment of inertia | The particle mass and the moment of inertia about a perpendicular axis. |
| $\signal{\tilde\nu},\signal B$ | $\unit{m^{-1}}$ | Wavenumber and rotational constant | The spectral coordinate and half the rigid-rotor line spacing. |
| $\signal c$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Speed of light | The conversion factor between frequency and spectroscopic wavenumber. |
| $\mechanical{\hbar}$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Reduced Planck constant | The constant in the rotor energy levels. |
