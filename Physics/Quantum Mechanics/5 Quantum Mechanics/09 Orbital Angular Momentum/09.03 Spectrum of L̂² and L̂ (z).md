title: "Spectrum of L̂² and L̂ (z)"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Orbital angular momentum has integer $\ell$. At fixed $\ell$, its $z$ component takes $2\ell+1$ values from $-\ell\mechanical{\hbar}$ to $\ell\mechanical{\hbar}$.

> [!theorem] Theorem 9.1. Algebraic spectrum of orbital angular momentum
> The eigenvalues of $\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf L}}^{\,2}$ are $\mechanical{\hbar}^{\,2}\ell(\ell+1)$ for $\ell=0,1,2,\ldots$. At each $\ell$, the eigenvalues of $\mechanical{\hat L}_z$ are $m\mechanical{\hbar}$ for $m=-\ell,-\ell+1,\ldots,\ell$.

**Proof.** In a subspace with squared angular momentum $\lambda\mechanical{\hbar}^{\,2}$, the inequality $\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf L}}^{\,2}\ge\mechanical{\hat L}_z^{\,2}$ bounds the ladder. Raising the top state gives zero. Apply the identity from the preceding section to it.

$$
\begin{aligned}
0&=\mechanical{\hat L}_-\mechanical{\hat L}_+\lvert\ell,m_{\max}\rangle\\
&=(\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf L}}^{\,2}-\mechanical{\hat L}_z^{\,2}-\mechanical{\hbar}\mechanical{\hat L}_z)\lvert\ell,m_{\max}\rangle\\
&=\mechanical{\hbar}^{\,2}[\lambda-m_{\max}(m_{\max}+1)]\lvert\ell,m_{\max}\rangle.
\end{aligned}
$$

Thus $\lambda=m_{\max}(m_{\max}+1)$. Similarly, the bottom state gives $\lambda=m_{\min}(m_{\min}-1)$. Equating the expressions yields $m_{\min}=-m_{\max}$. The difference $2m_{\max}$ is an integer because the ladder has unit steps, so $m_{\max}$ is an integer or a half-integer.

For orbital angular momentum the azimuthal wave function is single-valued. The eigenvalue equation for $\mechanical{\hat L}_z=-i\mechanical{\hbar}\partial_\phi$ gives a factor $e^{im\phi}$, and periodicity under $\phi\mapsto\phi+2\pi$ requires integer $m$. Thus $\ell=m_{\max}$ is a nonnegative integer.

$$
\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf L}}^{\,2}\lvert\ell,m\rangle
=\mechanical{\hbar}^{\,2}\ell(\ell+1)\lvert\ell,m\rangle,\qquad
\mechanical{\hat L}_z\lvert\ell,m\rangle
=m\mechanical{\hbar}\lvert\ell,m\rangle.
$$

## Geometric interpretation

For $\ell>0$, the maximum projection $\ell\mechanical{\hbar}$ is smaller than the magnitude $\mechanical{\hbar}\sqrt{\ell(\ell+1)}$. Even in $\lvert\ell,\ell\rangle$, the sum $\langle\mechanical{\hat L}_x^{\,2}\rangle+\langle\mechanical{\hat L}_y^{\,2}\rangle=\mechanical{\hbar}^{\,2}\ell$ is positive. ## Explicit ladder action

Normalize the raised and lowered states to obtain

$$
\mechanical{\hat L}_\pm\lvert\ell,m\rangle
=\mechanical{\hbar}\sqrt{\ell(\ell+1)-m(m\pm1)}\,\lvert\ell,m\pm1\rangle.
$$

At the upper and lower ends of the ladder, the corresponding operator gives zero.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf L}}^{\,2}$ | $\unit{J^2\,s^2}$ | Squared orbital angular momentum | The operator with eigenvalues $\mechanical{\hbar}^{\,2}\ell(\ell+1)$. |
| $\mechanical{\hat L}_x,\mechanical{\hat L}_y,\mechanical{\hat L}_z$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Angular momentum components | The operators along the Cartesian axes. |
| $\mechanical{\hat L}_\pm$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Ladder operators | The operators that raise or lower $m$ by one. |
| $\ell,m,\lambda$ | $1$ | Quantum numbers and scaled eigenvalue | The angular labels and the squared magnitude in units of $\mechanical{\hbar}^{\,2}$. |
| $\phi,\theta$ | $\unit{rad}$ | Angles | The azimuthal coordinate and the inclination in the vector model. |
| $\mechanical{\hbar}$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Reduced Planck constant | The unit of angular momentum. |
