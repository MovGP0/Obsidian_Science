---
title: "A Structural Example – the Angular Momentum Components"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The components of orbital angular momentum do not commute, but the squared magnitude commutes with every component.

Orbital angular momentum obeys $[\mechanical{\hat L}_i,\mechanical{\hat L}_j]=i\hbar\sum_k\varepsilon_{ijk}\mechanical{\hat L}_k$. The three components do not commute pairwise, so they do not have a common eigenbasis. The squared magnitude $\mechanical{\hat{\vec L}}^{\,2}=\mechanical{\hat L}_x^2+\mechanical{\hat L}_y^2+\mechanical{\hat L}_z^2$ commutes with each component. For example,

$$
[\mechanical{\hat{\vec L}}^{\,2},\mechanical{\hat L}_z]=0.
$$

The common eigenstates $\lvert\ell,m\rangle$ of $\mechanical{\hat{\vec L}}^{\,2}$ and $\mechanical{\hat L}_z$ have eigenvalues $\ell(\ell+1)\hbar^2$ and $m\hbar$, respectively. See [[09.00 Orbital Angular Momentum]].

Apply the Robertson inequality to $\mechanical{\hat L}_x$ and $\mechanical{\hat L}_y$.

$$
\Delta\mechanical{L}_x\,\Delta\mechanical{L}_y
\ge\frac{\hbar}{2}\lvert\langle\mechanical{\hat L}_z\rangle\rvert.
$$

In $\lvert\ell,m\rangle$, symmetry gives $\langle\mechanical{\hat L}_x\rangle=\langle\mechanical{\hat L}_y\rangle=0$. Since $\langle\mechanical{\hat L}_z\rangle=m\hbar$, the bound is $\lvert m\rvert\hbar^2/2$. At fixed $\ell$, this bound is greatest for $m=\pm\ell$, and the product then equals $\ell\hbar^2/2$. For $\ell>0$, transverse fluctuations remain even at maximum alignment. The geometric picture represents the angular momentum as precessing about the $z$ axis.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{\hat L}_i,\Delta\mechanical{L}_x,\Delta\mechanical{L}_y$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Orbital angular momentum components and spreads | The Cartesian observables and the standard deviations of the transverse components. |
| $\mechanical{\hat{\vec L}}^{\,2}$ | $\unit{J^2\,s^2}$ | Squared orbital angular momentum | The sum of the squared components. |
| $\hbar$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Reduced Planck constant | The scale of the commutators and eigenvalues. |
| $\ell,m$ | $1$ | Orbital and magnetic quantum numbers | The labels of the common eigenstates. |
