---
title: "Geometric Phases Exercises"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Calculate geometric phases, adiabatic time scales, and flux periods for specific paths.

1. Calculate the solid angle of a cone with half-angle $\theta=\pi/3$. Find the Berry phase $\gamma_+$ for spin $1/2$ after one positive traversal.  
   **Result.** $\Omega=2\pi(1-\cos(\pi/3))=\pi$ and $\gamma_+=-\pi/2$.
2. Calculate the Berry phase $\gamma_-$ for a spin-$1/2$ eigenstate that follows the equator of the field sphere in the positive direction.  
   **Result.** $\theta=\pi/2$, $\Omega=2\pi$, and $\gamma_-=+\pi$.
3. A solenoid of radius $a=\quantity{1}{mm}$ contains a field $\magnetic B=\quantity{0.1}{T}$. Find the flux and express it in units of the electron flux period $h/\electric e$.  
   **Result.** $\magnetic\Phi_B=\pi a^2\magnetic B\approx\quantity{3.14\times10^{-7}}{Wb}$, about $7.6\times10^7$ flux quanta.
4. Estimate the adiabatic traversal time for a two-level system with gap $\energy\Delta=\quantity{10^{-3}}{eV}$ and $\lVert\partial_t\energy{\hat H}\rVert\sim\energy\Delta/T$.  
   **Result.** $T\gg\mechanical{\hbar}/\energy\Delta\approx\quantity{6.6\times10^{-13}}{s}$.
5. Calculate the Aharonov–Bohm phase for an electron that encloses solenoid flux $\magnetic\Phi_B=h/(2\electric e)$ in the positive direction.  
   **Result.** $\phi_{\mathrm{AB}}=-2\pi(1/2)=-\pi$, equivalent to $\pi$ modulo $2\pi$.
6. Consider spin $1/2$ in $\magnetic{\mathbf B}(t)=\magnetic B_0(\sin\theta(t)\cos\varphi(t),\sin\theta(t)\sin\varphi(t),\cos\theta(t))$ for a smooth path of period $T$. Show that $\gamma_+=-\frac12\oint(1-\cos\theta)\,\mathrm d\varphi$ in the stated angular gauge.  
   **Result.** Integrate $A_\varphi=-\sin^2(\theta/2)$ along the path in $(\theta,\varphi)$.
7. For the path $\theta(\varphi)=\theta_0+\alpha\cos\varphi$ with $\lvert\alpha\rvert\ll1$, find the first-order change in $\gamma_+$ relative to $\alpha=0$.  
   **Result.** The linear correction vanishes by symmetry. The leading correction is $-(\pi\alpha^2/4)\cos\theta_0$ at second order.
8. In an Aharonov–Bohm double-slit experiment, find the flux shift that moves the pattern by half a fringe.  
   **Result.** $\magnetic\Phi_B=\magnetic\Phi_0/2=h/(2\electric q)$ modulo one flux period.
9. A free particle of charge $\electric q$ and mass $\mechanical m$ moves on a ring of radius $R$ threaded by flux $\magnetic\Phi$. Find the energy levels as functions of the flux.  
   **Result.** $\energy{E}_n(\magnetic\Phi)=\frac{\mechanical{\hbar}^2}{2\mechanical m R^2}(n-\electric q\magnetic\Phi/h)^2$ for $n\in\mathbb Z$. The set of levels is periodic because a flux shift by $\magnetic\Phi_0$ relabels $n$.
10. For the monopole curvature $\mathbf F_+(\magnetic{\mathbf B})=-\magnetic{\mathbf B}/(2\lvert\magnetic{\mathbf B}\rvert^3)$, show that the flux through an outward-oriented sphere centered at $\magnetic{\mathbf B}=0$ is $\int_{S^2}\mathbf F_+\cdot\mathrm d\mathbf S=-2\pi$. Interpret the result.  
    **Result.** The effective Berry charge is $-1/2$. The hemisphere associated with an equatorial loop has flux $-\pi$.
11. Consider a particle of charge $\electric q$ on a ring threaded by flux $\magnetic\Phi$ and coupled to a slowly varying tangential electric field $\electric{\mathcal E}(t)$. Discuss the geometric and dynamical phases over one period and the conditions under which the geometric term dominates.  
    **Result.** For the source's assumed single winding at fixed flux, $\phi_{\mathrm{tot}}=\phi_{\mathrm{AB}}+\phi_{\mathrm{dyn}}$. The term $\phi_{\mathrm{AB}}=2\pi\magnetic\Phi/\magnetic\Phi_0$ is independent of $T$. For a fixed energy profile as a function of $t/T$, the dynamical phase $\phi_{\mathrm{dyn}}=-\int_0^T\energy{E}_n(t)\,\mathrm dt/\mechanical{\hbar}$ scales with $T$. To isolate the geometric term, use a shorter traversal compatible with the adiabatic condition, compare paths with small energy differences, or cancel the dynamical contribution with an echo sequence.

> [!note] Dynamical phase sign in Exercise 11
> The source gives a positive sign for the dynamical integral here. The sign above follows Eq. (18.2). A numerical phase would require the Hamiltonian and a specified driving cycle.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\theta,\varphi,\alpha$ | $\unit{rad}$ | Path angles | Polar and azimuthal angles and the size of a polar-angle modulation. |
| $\Omega$ | $\unit{sr}$ | Solid angle | The oriented area bounded by the loop on a unit sphere. |
| $\gamma_\pm,\phi_{\mathrm{AB}},\phi_{\mathrm{dyn}}$ | $\unit{rad}$ | Phases | The Berry phases, Aharonov–Bohm phase, and dynamical phase. |
| $\magnetic B,\magnetic B_0$ | $\unit{T}$ | Magnetic fields | The field inside the solenoid and the magnitude of the rotating field. |
| $\magnetic\Phi,\magnetic\Phi_B,\magnetic\Phi_0$ | $\unit{Wb}$ | Fluxes | Threaded flux, solenoid flux, and the signed flux increment. |
| $a,R$ | $\unit{m}$ | Radii | The radii of the solenoid and the ring. |
| $\energy\Delta,\energy{E}_n$ | $\unit{J}$ | Energy gap and levels | The gap for the adiabatic estimate and the energy of level $n$. |
| $T$ | $\unit{s}$ | Period | The duration of one traversal. |
