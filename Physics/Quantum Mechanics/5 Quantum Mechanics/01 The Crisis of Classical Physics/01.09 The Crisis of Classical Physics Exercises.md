---
title: "The Crisis of Classical Physics Exercises"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Practice calculations for photons, atomic spectra, matter waves, and early quantum experiments.

1. Calculate the energy in electronvolts of a visible photon with wavelength $\signal{\lambda}=\quantity{550}{nm}$ and an X-ray photon with wavelength $\signal{\lambda}=\quantity{0.1}{nm}$.
   **Result.** About $\quantity{2.25}{eV}$ and $\quantity{1.24\times10^4}{eV}$.
2. A metal has work function $\energy{W}=\quantity{4.5}{eV}$. Find the threshold frequency and the maximum kinetic energy of electrons ejected by light with wavelength $\signal{\lambda}=\quantity{200}{nm}$.
   **Result.** $\signal{\nu}_0\simeq\quantity{1.09\times10^{15}}{Hz}$ and $\energy{K}_{\max}\simeq\quantity{1.7}{eV}$.
3. Calculate the de Broglie wavelength of (a) an electron with kinetic energy $\quantity{1}{eV}$, (b) a proton with the same kinetic energy, and (c) a tennis ball of mass $\quantity{50}{g}$ at $\quantity{30}{m\,s^{-1}}$.
   **Result.** (a) About $\quantity{1.23}{nm}$, (b) $\quantity{2.87\times10^{-11}}{m}$, and (c) $\quantity{4.4\times10^{-34}}{m}$.
4. Find the frequency and wavelength of the photon emitted in the hydrogen transition $n=3\to n=2$ (the Hα line).
   **Result.** $\signal{\nu}\simeq\quantity{4.57\times10^{14}}{Hz}$ and $\signal{\lambda}\simeq\quantity{656}{nm}$.
5. Derive the first Bohr orbit radius $\mechanical{a}_0$ and the ground-state energy from $\mechanical{m}_e\mechanical{v}\mechanical{r}=\hbar$ and the Coulomb attraction.
6. A photon of energy $\quantity{50}{keV}$ undergoes Compton scattering through $90^\circ$. Find (a) the wavelength shift, (b) the scattered photon energy, and (c) the recoil electron's kinetic energy.
   **Result.** (a) $\quantity{2.43\times10^{-12}}{m}$, (b) about $\quantity{45.5}{keV}$, and (c) about $\quantity{4.5}{keV}$.
7. For hydrogen, compare the classical radiative collapse time estimated from the Larmor formula with the observed lifetime $\tau\sim\quantity{10^{-8}}{s}$ of a typical excited state. Explain the discrepancy.
8. A monoenergetic electron beam at $\mechanical{v}=\quantity{10^6}{m\,s^{-1}}$ passes through a slit of width $\mechanical{a}=\quantity{1}{\mu m}$. Use the uncertainty relation to estimate the half-angle divergence.
   **Result.** $\Delta\theta\sim\hbar/(\mechanical{p}\mechanical{a})\simeq\quantity{1.2\times10^{-4}}{rad}$.
9. Derive Wien's displacement law in wavelength form, $\signal{\lambda}_{\max}\thermal{T}=b$ with $b\simeq\quantity{2.90\times10^{-3}}{m\,K}$. Compare it with the frequency form and explain why $\signal{\nu}_{\max}\signal{\lambda}_{\max}\ne c$.
10. In electron diffraction through a slit of width $\mechanical{a}$, the first minimum occurs at angle $\theta_1$ with $\mechanical{a}\sin\theta_1=\signal{\lambda}$. Find the width for $\signal{\lambda}_e=\quantity{0.15}{nm}$ at $\electric{V}=\quantity{67}{V}$. and $\theta_1=3^\circ$.
    **Result.** $\mechanical{a}\simeq\quantity{2.9}{nm}$.
11. **Planck integral.** Show that the total blackbody radiation energy per unit volume is

    $$
    \energy{u}(\thermal{T})=\int_0^\infty \energy{u}(\signal{\nu},\thermal{T})\,d\signal{\nu}
    =\frac{8\pi^5(\thermal{k}_B\thermal{T})^4}{15h^3c^3}.
    $$

    Derive the $\energy{u}\propto\thermal{T}^4$ dependence in the Stefan–Boltzmann law. Use $x=h\signal{\nu}/(\thermal{k}_B\thermal{T})$ and $\int_0^\infty x^3/(e^x-1)\,dx=\pi^4/15$.

## Physical quantities

| $\signal{\lambda},\signal{\lambda}_e,\signal{\lambda}_{\max}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Wavelengths | The photon or matter wavelength, electron diffraction wavelength, and wavelength-spectrum peak. |
| $\signal{\nu},\signal{\nu}_0,\signal{\nu}_{\max}$ | $\unit{Hz}$ | Frequencies | The radiation frequency, photoelectric threshold, and frequency-spectrum peak. |
| $\energy{W},\energy{K}_{\max}$ | $\unit{eV}$ | Work function and kinetic energy | The surface emission threshold energy and maximum photoelectron energy. |
| — | $\unit{eV}$ | Particle or photon energy | The given or calculated energies in the wavelength and scattering exercises. |
| $\mechanical{m}_e$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Electron mass | The electron mass in the Bohr quantization condition. |
| — | $\unit{g}$ | Macroscopic mass | The tennis ball mass in the matter-wave comparison. |
| $\mechanical{v}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Speed | The electron speed in orbit or in a beam. |
| — | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Macroscopic speed | The tennis ball speed in the matter-wave comparison. |
| $\mechanical{r},\mechanical{a}_0,\mechanical{a}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Radius and width | The orbital radius, first Bohr orbit radius, and slit width. |
| $\mechanical{p}$ | $\unit{kg\,m\,s^{-1}}$ | Momentum | The magnitude of electron momentum in the slit experiment. |
| $\theta_1,\Delta\theta$ | $\unit{rad}$ | Diffraction angles | The first minimum angle and estimated half-angle divergence. |
| $\tau$ | $\unit{s}$ | Lifetime | The observed lifetime of a typical excited hydrogen state. |

| $\energy{u}(\thermal{T})$ | $\unit{J\,m^{-3}}$ | Radiation energy density | The integral over all frequencies. |

| $c$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Speed of light | The speed of light in vacuum. |
