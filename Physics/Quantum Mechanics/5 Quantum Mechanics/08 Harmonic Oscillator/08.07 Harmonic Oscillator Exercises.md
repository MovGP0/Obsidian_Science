---
title: "Harmonic Oscillator Exercises"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Use the oscillator spectrum, ladder operators, and coherent states to calculate energies and motion.

1. Calculate the angular frequency $\mechanical{\omega}$ for a carbon-12 atom in a harmonic trap with ground-state energy $\energy E_0=0.1\,\unit{eV}$.  
   **Result.** $\mechanical{\omega}\simeq3.04\times10^{14}\,\unit{s^{-1}}$.
2. Calculate $\langle n\lvert\hat x^2\rvert n\rangle$ using $\hat x=(x_0/\sqrt2)(\hat a+\hat a^\dagger)$.  
   **Result.** $x_0^2(n+1/2)=\mechanical{\hbar}(n+1/2)/(\mechanical m\mechanical{\omega})$.
3. Verify explicitly that $\Delta x\,\Delta\mechanical p=\mechanical{\hbar}/2$ for $\signal{\psi}_0$.
4. Write $\energy{\hat H}^{\,2}$ in terms of $\hat N$. Calculate $\langle\energy{\hat H}^{\,2}\rangle$ and $\Delta\energy H$ in $\lvert n\rangle$.  
   **Result.** $\Delta\energy H=0$ in energy eigenstates.
5. Verify explicitly that $\langle\hat x\rangle=x_0\sqrt2\operatorname{Re}\alpha$ and $\Delta x=x_0/\sqrt2$ in $\lvert\alpha\rangle$.
6. Show that the displacement operator $\hat D(\alpha)=e^{\alpha\hat a^\dagger-\alpha^*\hat a}$ gives $\hat D(\alpha)\lvert0\rangle=\lvert\alpha\rangle$.  
   **Hint.** Use the Baker–Campbell–Hausdorff formula to factor the exponential.
7. Calculate $\langle\hat N\rangle$ and $\Delta N$ in $\lvert\alpha\rangle$. Show that $\Delta N/\langle\hat N\rangle=1/\sqrt{\langle\hat N\rangle}$ for nonzero mean, as for a Poisson count.  
   **Result.** $\langle\hat N\rangle=\lvert\alpha\rvert^2$ and $\Delta N=\lvert\alpha\rvert$.
8. Show that level $n$ of an isotropic two-dimensional oscillator has degeneracy $n+1$. Count the pairs $(n_1,n_2)$ with $n_1+n_2=n$.
9. **Oscillator propagator.** Show that $\signal K(x,t;x',0)=\langle x\lvert e^{-i\energy{\hat H}t/\mechanical{\hbar}}\rvert x'\rangle$ is
   $$
   \signal K=\sqrt{\frac{\mechanical m\mechanical{\omega}}{2\pi i\mechanical{\hbar}\sin(\mechanical{\omega}t)}}\,
   e^{i\mechanical S_{\mathrm{cl}}(x,t;x',0)/\mechanical{\hbar}},
   $$
   where $\mechanical S_{\mathrm{cl}}=\frac{\mechanical m\mechanical{\omega}}{2\sin(\mechanical{\omega}t)}[(x^2+x'^2)\cos(\mechanical{\omega}t)-2xx']$.  
   **Hint.** Use Mehler's formula.
10. **Squeezed states.** The state $\hat S(r)\lvert0\rangle$ with $\hat S(r)=\exp[(r/2)(\hat a^2-(\hat a^\dagger)^2)]$ and $r\in\mathbb R$ satisfies $\Delta x\,\Delta\mechanical p=\mechanical{\hbar}/2$. Calculate both uncertainties. For $r>0$, position narrows and momentum broadens.  
    **Result.** $\Delta x=(x_0/\sqrt2)e^{-r}$ and $\Delta\mechanical p=[\mechanical{\hbar}/(x_0\sqrt2)]e^r$.
11. **Thermal ensemble.** At temperature $\thermal T$, the occupation probability of $\lvert n\rangle$ is $p_n\propto e^{-\energy E_n/(\thermal{k_B}\thermal T)}$. Calculate $\langle\hat N\rangle_T$ and compare it with the Bose–Einstein mean $1/[e^{\mechanical{\hbar}\mechanical{\omega}/(\thermal{k_B}\thermal T)}-1]$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{\omega}$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Angular frequency | The natural oscillator frequency. |
| $\energy{\hat H},\energy E_n,\Delta\energy H$ | $\unit{J}$ | Energy quantities | The Hamiltonian, its eigenvalue, and its standard deviation. |
| $\hat a,\hat a^\dagger,\hat N,\hat D(\alpha),\hat S(r)$ | $1$ | Oscillator operators | The lowering, raising, number, displacement, and squeezing operators. |
| $n,\Delta N,\alpha,r,p_n$ | $1$ | Occupation and state parameters | The excitation index, number uncertainty, coherent parameter, squeezing parameter, and thermal probability. |
| $\hat x,x_0,\Delta x$ | $\unit m$ | Position quantities | The position operator, oscillator length, and position uncertainty. |
| $\Delta\mechanical p$ | $\unit{kg\,m\,s^{-1}}$ | Momentum uncertainty | The momentum standard deviation. |
| $\signal K$ | $\unit{m^{-1}}$ | Oscillator propagator | The kernel of position-space evolution. |
| $\mechanical S_{\mathrm{cl}},\mechanical{\hbar}$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Classical action and reduced Planck constant | Their ratio gives the propagator phase. |
| $\mechanical m,t$ | $\unit{kg},\unit s$ | Mass and time | The oscillator mass and elapsed time. |
| $\thermal T,\thermal{k_B}$ | $\unit K,\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Temperature and Boltzmann constant | Their product sets the thermal energy scale. |
