> [!TLDR]
> The quantum harmonic oscillator has equally spaced energy levels and exact eigenfunctions. It also models small oscillations near a stable equilibrium.

The harmonic oscillator is a central model in quantum mechanics. Its Hamiltonian can be solved in closed form. Its energy levels are equally spaced, and its eigenfunctions are Hermite polynomials multiplied by a Gaussian. Near a stable minimum of the potential, a quadratic expansion gives a harmonic oscillator if the curvature is nonzero. This approach describes molecular vibrations, phonons in crystals, electromagnetic field modes, and collective excitations in Bose–Einstein condensates.

This chapter treats the oscillator in two ways. The algebraic method uses creation and annihilation operators. The analytic method uses Hermite polynomials in the position representation.

The Hamiltonian is

$$
\energy{\hat H}=\frac{\mechanical{\hat p}^{\,2}}{2\mechanical m}+\frac12\mechanical m\mechanical{\omega}^{\,2}\hat x^2.
$$

## Chapter summary

- The ladder operators satisfy $[\hat a,\hat a^\dagger]=\hat I$. With $\hat N=\hat a^\dagger\hat a$, $\energy{\hat H}=\mechanical{\hbar}\mechanical{\omega}(\hat N+\hat I/2)$.
- The one-dimensional levels $\energy E_n=\mechanical{\hbar}\mechanical{\omega}(n+1/2)$ are equally spaced and nondegenerate.
- The states are $\lvert n\rangle=(\hat a^\dagger)^n\lvert0\rangle/\sqrt{n!}$. In position space, they are Gaussians multiplied by Hermite polynomials $H_n$.
- The matrix elements of $\hat x$ and $\mechanical{\hat p}$ connect $\lvert n\rangle$ to $\lvert n\pm1\rangle$.
- The uncertainty product is $\Delta x\,\Delta\mechanical p=\mechanical{\hbar}(n+1/2)$. The ground state saturates the Heisenberg bound.
- Coherent states are eigenstates of $\hat a$. Their level probabilities are Poisson, their uncertainties are minimal, and the packet moves with constant width.
- In three dimensions, the isotropic oscillator has degeneracy $(n+1)(n+2)/2$ at total excitation $n$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\energy{\hat H},\energy E_n$ | $\unit{J}$ | Hamiltonian and energy levels | The oscillator energy operator and its eigenvalues. |
| $\hat a,\hat a^\dagger,\hat N,n$ | $1$ | Ladder and number operators and index | The algebraic operators and the number of quanta. |
| $\hat x,\Delta x$ | $\unit m$ | Position operator and uncertainty | The coordinate observable and its standard deviation. |
| $\mechanical{\hat p},\Delta\mechanical p$ | $\unit{kg\,m\,s^{-1}}$ | Momentum operator and uncertainty | The momentum observable and its standard deviation. |
| $\mechanical m$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | The oscillator mass. |
| $\mechanical{\omega}$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Angular frequency | The natural frequency and the energy spacing divided by $\mechanical{\hbar}$. |
| $\mechanical{\hbar}$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Reduced Planck constant | The constant in the energy spacing and uncertainty product. |
