> [!TLDR]
> Practice matrix eigenvalues, spectral decompositions, spin measurements, two-level dynamics, and the harmonic-oscillator spectrum with complete worked solutions.

Chapter 7 — Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors. Each linked note contains the complete question and all of its parts, a worked solution, and the final answer.

For Exercises 1–3, verify each result in two representations: direct matrix action and spectral invariants. In Exercise 4, use the symmetry between the two basis states to predict symmetric and antisymmetric eigenvectors before calculating a determinant. Then retain their relative phases in Exercise 6; unequal energy phases produce population transfer.

Exercise 5 distinguishes a mean, a second moment, and a variance. Do not infer an individual outcome from the mean. In Exercise 7, the decisive step is positivity of the number operator, not the lowering equation alone. Check what happens when lowering reaches zero: the zero vector is not another eigenstate.
