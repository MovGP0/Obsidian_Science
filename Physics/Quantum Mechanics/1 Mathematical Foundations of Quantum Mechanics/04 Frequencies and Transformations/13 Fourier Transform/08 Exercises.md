> [!TLDR]
> Practice Fourier transforms, Gaussian momentum distributions, free propagation, and sampling with complete worked solutions.

Chapter 13: [[00 Fourier Transform|Fourier transform]]. Each note contains the question, hints, source qualifications, and a complete worked solution.

Exercises 1 and 2 build a transform toolkit: use parity, differentiation, and shifts before direct integration. Exercise 3 checks the momentum integration measure. Exercises 4 and 5 then ask different questions: a distributional differential equation versus ordinary probability moments.

Complete Exercise 6 before Exercise 8 so that the Gaussian evolved by a kernel can be compared with an independent momentum-space result. Exercise 7 returns to Fourier series, now on a finite spectral interval, to prove sampling reconstruction.

Before each calculation, state whether the output is an ordinary function, an $L^2$ equivalence class, or a distribution. For a probability question, specify the integration variable and normalization. For a propagation question, check the initial state and norm as well as the differential equation. These choices are part of the solution, not just notation.
