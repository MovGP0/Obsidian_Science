---
title: "Large-N_c Expansion"
---

> [!TLDR]
> At large N_c with g_s²N_c fixed, planar diagrams dominate the color expansion. This limit organizes gauge theory by powers of 1/N_c.

Take
$$
N_c\to\infty,
\qquad
\signal{g}_s^2N_c=\signal{\lambda}\text{ fixed}.
$$
This is the **’t Hooft limit**.
QCD simplifies because planar diagrams dominate at leading order in $1/N_c$.
The source connects this regime to AdS/CFT duality and to gauge-theory/string-theory correspondence.

> [!note]
> Large $N_c$ supplies an expansion, but does not make ordinary QCD exactly solvable. Established AdS/CFT examples involve particular gauge theories, such as supersymmetric conformal theories; a direct exact AdS/CFT dual of physical QCD is not supplied by the large-color limit alone.

Holding the gauge coupling itself fixed would make a typical gauge-loop color factor grow without bound as the number of colors increases. The displayed limit compensates this growth: the squared coupling decreases inversely with the color count, so its product with the adjoint Casimir remains fixed. This is why the fixed product is the relevant interaction parameter.

A large number of colors is a different approximation from weak coupling. The fixed product can be large, so leading planar diagrams can still require difficult dynamics rather than a few low-order graphs. Specify how the number of flavors scales as well; keeping it fixed is a different limit from increasing it with the number of colors. Returning to three colors requires an assessment of omitted terms, not an assertion of exact agreement.

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
