---
title: "Large-N_c Expansion"
---

> [!TLDR]
> At large N_c with g_s²N_c fixed, planar diagrams dominate the color expansion. This limit organizes gauge theory by powers of 1/N_c.

Take
$$
N_c\to\infty,
\qquad
\signal{g}_s^2N_c=\signal{\lambda}\text{ fixed}.
$$
This is the **’t Hooft limit**.
QCD simplifies because planar diagrams dominate at leading order in $1/N_c$.
The source connects this regime to AdS/CFT duality and to gauge-theory/string-theory correspondence.

> [!note]
> Large $N_c$ supplies an expansion, but does not make ordinary QCD exactly solvable. Established AdS/CFT examples involve particular gauge theories, such as supersymmetric conformal theories; a direct exact AdS/CFT dual of physical QCD is not supplied by the large-color limit alone.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{g}_s$ | $1$ | Gauge coupling | The coupling scaled as the number of colors increases. |
| $\signal{\lambda}$ | $1$ | ’t Hooft coupling | The fixed product g_s²N_c in the large-color limit. |
