---
title: "Additional Rules"
---

> [!TLDR]
> QCD diagrams require fermion-loop signs, Dirac and color traces, graph symmetry factors, and one momentum integral per independent loop.

The source lists these additional QCD rules:

- Each quark loop gives a factor $-1$ and a trace over Dirac and color indices.
- Each ghost loop gives a factor $-1$ from Fermi statistics.
- Apply symmetry factors for diagrams with identical external lines.
- Include a factor $1/(2!)$ for identical internal gluon lines in the same diagram.
- Integrate over every internal momentum not fixed by momentum conservation:
  $$
  \int\frac{d^4\mechanical{k}}{(2\pi)^4}.
  $$

> [!note]
> The source's two symmetry-factor rules are too broad when read as universal prescriptions. A Feynman-diagram symmetry factor is determined by the graph and the contractions, with its external labels held fixed. A factor $1/2!$ is not required for every pair of identical internal gluon lines. Factors for identical particles in final-state phase space are a separate matter. Use the actual graph symmetry to avoid double counting.

Separate three operations when forming a loop amplitude. First route momenta and use the vertex delta functions; only independent loop momenta remain to be integrated. Next contract matrix indices in their order along each closed line. Finally apply the closed-Grassmann-loop sign and the graph's actual contraction factor. The minus sign for a ghost loop does not come with a Dirac trace, because ghosts have no spinor index.

For a connected graph, the number of independent loops is the number of internal lines minus the number of vertices plus one. A tree therefore has no unfixed loop integral even though it has internal propagators. This check detects over-integration and is separate from any identical-particle factor in a cross section.
