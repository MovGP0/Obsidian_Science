---
title: "Running Coupling and the Landau Scale"
---

> [!TLDR]
> At one loop, the strong coupling decreases logarithmically at high energy. Its perturbative growth toward the infrared defines the scale Λ_QCD.

The one-loop renormalization-group equation is
$$
\energy{\mu}\frac{\partial\signal{\alpha}_s}{\partial\energy{\mu}}
=-\frac{\beta_0}{2\pi}\signal{\alpha}_s^2,
\qquad
\signal{\alpha}_s=\frac{\signal{g}_s^2}{4\pi}.
$$
Its solution is
$$
\signal{\alpha}_s(\energy{\mu})
=\frac{\signal{\alpha}_s(\energy{\mu}_0)}
{1+\dfrac{\beta_0}{2\pi}\signal{\alpha}_s(\energy{\mu}_0)
\ln(\energy{\mu}/\energy{\mu}_0)}.
$$

Alternatively, introduce the **QCD scale** $\energy{\Lambda}_{\mathrm{QCD}}$.
The source writes
$$
\signal{\alpha}_s(\energy{\mu}^2)
=\frac{2\pi}
{\beta_0\ln(\energy{\mu}/\energy{\Lambda}_{\mathrm{QCD}})}.
\tag{12.14}
$$
For $N_c=3$ and $N_f=6$,
$\beta_0=(33-12)/3=7$.
The source quotes
$$
\energy{\Lambda}_{\mathrm{QCD}}\approx200\text{–}300\,\unit{MeV},
\qquad
\signal{\alpha}_s(\mechanical{M}_Z)\approx0.118.
$$

> [!note]
> The source places a dashed pole marker at $x=1$, although its formula places the pole at $x=0$. The plot above corrects the marker. The point at $\signal{\alpha}_s=0.118$ lies at $x\approx7.61$ on the displayed fixed-$N_f=6$ one-loop curve. The quoted $200$–$300\,\unit{MeV}$ range is not a simultaneous fit to that curve and point: scale values depend on the scheme, loop order, and active flavors. Quark thresholds must be treated in a quantitative evolution. The source uses $\signal{\alpha}_s(\energy{\mu}^2)$ in (12.14) as a squared-scale notation for the coupling at energy $\energy{\mu}$.

## Example 12.5: Why QED is not asymptotically free

QED has the Abelian gauge group $U(1)$, for which $C_A=0$.
In the electron-only example, there is one type of charged fermion.
There is no charged photon, so there are no gauge or ghost contributions analogous to QCD.
The beta function is
$$
\signal{\beta}^{\mathrm{QED}}(\electric{\alpha})
=\frac{2}{3\pi}\electric{\alpha}^2
+\mathcal{O}(\electric{\alpha}^3)>0.
$$

The electron-loop vacuum polarization gives the positive contribution
$2\electric{\alpha}^2/(3\pi)$ and screens charge.
In the mass-independent one-loop description, QED is infrared free: its coupling grows toward the ultraviolet.
In QCD, gluon and ghost loops contribute with the opposite sign.
For the asymptotically free flavor range, their contribution dominates the quark-loop screening and reverses the beta-function sign.

| $\signal{\alpha}_s,\signal{g}_s$ | $1$ | Strong couplings | The squared-coupling convention and the gauge coupling. |
| $\energy{\mu},\energy{\mu}_0$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Running and reference scales | The scales used in the one-loop solution. |
| $\energy{\Lambda}_{\mathrm{QCD}}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | QCD scale | The scheme- and flavor-dependent scale defining the one-loop logarithm. |
| $\mechanical{M}_Z$ | $\unit{GeV}$ | Z-boson mass scale | The reference energy used for the quoted strong coupling. |
| $\electric{\alpha}$ | $1$ | Fine-structure constant | The electromagnetic coupling in the electron-only comparison. |
