---
title: "Ward–Slavnov–Taylor Identities"
---

> [!TLDR]
> BRST symmetry gives Ward–Slavnov–Taylor identities that relate Green functions and constrain renormalization factors in non-Abelian gauge theories.

BRST symmetry gives relations between Green functions analogous to the QED Ward–Takahashi identities. These **Ward–Slavnov–Taylor (WST) identities** constrain the structure of renormalization and the consistency of the gauge theory.

These identities replace independent guesses for each quantum correction with relations inherited from the gauge redundancy. In the gauge-fixed path integral, perform a BRST change of integration variables. If the measure and regulator preserve the symmetry, the integral of this change vanishes; insertions then give identities among Green functions. An anomaly would obstruct this argument.

Use [[12.08.01 The WST Identity for the Propagator]] for the two-point tensor constraint, then [[12.08.02 Relations between Renormalizations]] for the common coupling extracted from different vertices. A useful distinction is that transversality restricts a tensor, while gauge independence concerns dependence on a gauge choice. A gauge-dependent transverse scalar coefficient satisfies the first condition without satisfying the second.
