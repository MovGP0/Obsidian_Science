---
title: "The WST Identity for the Propagator"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The gluon self-energy is transverse as a consequence of BRST symmetry. This excludes a local Proca mass counterterm in perturbative Yang–Mills theory.

The gluon self-energy obeys the transversality condition
$$
\mechanical{k}^\mu\signal{\Pi}_{\mu\nu}^{ab}(\mechanical{k})=0.
$$
The source states that this keeps the gluon massless and prevents quantum corrections from generating a mass term.
The condition follows directly from BRST invariance.

> [!note]
> In perturbation theory, the identity excludes an independent local gauge-boson mass counterterm. Transversality by itself does not prove that an isolated massless gluon is a physical asymptotic state, nor does it exclude every possible nonperturbative infrared mass scale.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{k}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Gluon momentum | The momentum contracted with the self-energy tensor. |
| $\signal{\Pi}_{\mu\nu}^{ab}$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Gluon self-energy tensor | The transverse two-point correction with adjoint color indices. |
