> [!TLDR]
> Instantons, the large-N_c limit, and loop operators give tools for studying nonperturbative gauge dynamics and the structure of QCD.

The following topics extend perturbative Yang–Mills theory: [[12.09.01 The Topological Sector - Instantons|instantons]], the [[12.09.02 Large-N_c Expansion|large-color limit]], and [[12.09.03 Wilson Operators and ’t Hooft Loops|Wilson and ’t Hooft loop operators]].

Each extension answers a different question. Instantons reveal finite-action sectors missed by an expansion around the trivial vacuum. The large-color limit reorganizes diagrams by color counting while retaining a possibly strong effective coupling. Wilson and magnetic loop probes diagnose the response to external charges through gauge-invariant quantities.

Read the instanton note with its normalization correction, then check which quantity is held fixed in the large-color limit. For the loop classification, specify the gauge group and matter before naming a phase. These are tools for controlled questions in chosen regimes; none is presented here as a general analytic solution of physical QCD. In particular, topological charge, large-color counting, and confinement are distinct statements that require separate arguments.
