> [!TLDR]
> Yang–Mills vertices couple quarks and ghosts to gluons and include three-gluon and four-gluon interactions. Their color factors and momentum conventions must be kept consistent.

![[QFT-12-06-Quark-Gluon-Vertex.svg|**Fig. 12.6** Quark–gluon vertex. The color structure is (Tᵃ)ᵢⱼ.]]

## Quark–gluon vertex

The vertex in Fig. 12.6 is
$$
\signal{V}_{q\bar qg}^{\mu,aij}
=i\signal{g}\gamma^\mu(T^a)_{ij}.
$$

## Three-gluon vertex: Detail

Equation (12.7) is drawn in Fig. 12.1 in [[12.02.05 Self-Interactions of Gauge Bosons]].
All momenta are incoming, with $\mechanical{p}+\mechanical{q}+\mechanical{r}=0$.
The vertex has Bose symmetry under simultaneous exchange of the complete labels
$$
(a,\mu,\mechanical{p})\leftrightarrow(b,\nu,\mechanical{q})
\leftrightarrow(c,\rho,\mechanical{r}).
$$
The antisymmetry of $f^{abc}$ is compensated by the antisymmetry of the Lorentz-momentum structure.

![[QFT-12-07-Ghost-Gluon-Vertex.svg|**Fig. 12.7** Ghost–gluon vertex. The momentum q belongs to the outgoing antighost c̄.]]

## Ghost–gluon vertex

The vertex in Fig. 12.7 is
$$
\signal{V}_{\mathrm{ghost},ac}^{\mu,b}
=-\signal{g}f^{abc}\mechanical{q}^\mu.
$$
Here $\mechanical{q}$ is the momentum of the outgoing antighost $\bar{\signal{c}}^a$.
The vertex carries the outgoing antighost momentum, rather than the incoming ghost momentum.
This asymmetry reflects the antisymmetry of $f^{abc}$ and the derivative acting only on $\bar{\signal{c}}$ in the integrated-by-parts form of (12.10).
