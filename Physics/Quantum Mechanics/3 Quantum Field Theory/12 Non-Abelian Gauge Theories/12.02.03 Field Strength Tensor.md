---
title: "Field Strength Tensor"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The Yang–Mills field strength is the commutator of covariant derivatives. Its commutator term produces gauge-boson interactions, and it transforms covariantly under gauge transformations.

In the Abelian theory, the field strength is
$$
\electric{F}_{\mu\nu}=\partial_\mu\electric{A}_\nu-\partial_\nu\electric{A}_\mu.
$$
With the convention $D_\mu=\partial_\mu-i\electric{e}\electric{A}_\mu$, it follows from
$\electric{F}_{\mu\nu}=[D_\mu,D_\nu]/(-i\electric{e})$.
Generalize this construction:
$$
\begin{aligned}
[D_\mu,D_\nu]
&=[\partial_\mu-i\signal{g}\signal{A}_\mu,
   \partial_\nu-i\signal{g}\signal{A}_\nu]\\
&=-i\signal{g}(\partial_\mu\signal{A}_\nu-\partial_\nu\signal{A}_\mu)
-\signal{g}^2[\signal{A}_\mu,\signal{A}_\nu]\\
&=-i\signal{g}\signal{F}_{\mu\nu}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The **non-Abelian field strength** is
$$
\signal{F}_{\mu\nu}\equiv
\partial_\mu\signal{A}_\nu-\partial_\nu\signal{A}_\mu
-i\signal{g}[\signal{A}_\mu,\signal{A}_\nu].
$$
Write $\signal{F}_{\mu\nu}=\signal{F}_{\mu\nu}^aT^a$.
Its components are
$$
\signal{F}_{\mu\nu}^a
=\partial_\mu\signal{A}_\nu^a-\partial_\nu\signal{A}_\mu^a
+\signal{g}f^{abc}\signal{A}_\mu^b\signal{A}_\nu^c.
\tag{12.4}
$$
The term $\signal{g}f^{abc}\signal{A}_\mu^b\signal{A}_\nu^c$ is absent in QED.
It contains two gauge fields and is nonlinear in the field.
In the Lagrangian density, it generates three- and four-boson interactions.

## Transformation properties

Under the gauge transformation (12.3), the field strength transforms covariantly in the adjoint representation:
$$
\signal{F}_{\mu\nu}\to
U(\mechanical{x})\signal{F}_{\mu\nu}U^\dagger(\mechanical{x}).
$$
Equivalently,
$$
\signal{F}_{\mu\nu}^a\to
\signal{F}_{\mu\nu}^a-f^{abc}\alpha^b\signal{F}_{\mu\nu}^c
+\mathcal{O}(\alpha^2).
$$
Unlike the Abelian field strength, the components $\signal{F}_{\mu\nu}^a$ are not gauge invariant.
They transform in the adjoint representation.
The trace $\operatorname{Tr}(\signal{F}_{\mu\nu}\signal{F}^{\mu\nu})$ is invariant.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\electric{F}_{\mu\nu}$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Abelian field strength | The electromagnetic field tensor used for comparison. |
| $\electric{A}_\mu$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Abelian gauge potential | The QED connection in the stated derivative convention. |
| $\electric{e}$ | $1$ | Abelian coupling | The electric coupling in the comparison. |
| $\signal{A}_\mu,\signal{A}_\mu^a$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Non-Abelian gauge fields | The matrix connection and its components. |
| $\signal{F}_{\mu\nu},\signal{F}_{\mu\nu}^a$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Non-Abelian field strength | The matrix tensor and its adjoint components. |
| $\signal{g}$ | $1$ | Yang–Mills coupling | The coupling in the nonlinear field-strength term. |
| $\mechanical{x}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Spacetime coordinate | The position at which the gauge transformation acts. |
