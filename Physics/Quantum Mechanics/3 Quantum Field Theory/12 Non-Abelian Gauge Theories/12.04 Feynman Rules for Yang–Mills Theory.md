title: "Feynman Rules for Yang–Mills Theory"

> [!TLDR]
> Yang–Mills Feynman rules combine gluon, quark, and ghost propagators with gauge-boson and matter interaction vertices.

The following articles give the full momentum-space Feynman rules for Yang–Mills theory quantized in the Lorenz-gauge family with parameter $\xi$.

Use one convention set for an entire amplitude: the covariant derivative fixes the interaction sign, the Fourier convention fixes derivative momenta, and line arrows specify fermion or ghost ordering. The table is a reference after these choices, rather than a replacement for them.

A quick dimensional check distinguishes the two pure-gluon vertices: three fields require one derivative in a dimension-four action, whereas four fields require none. Color deltas propagate an existing internal label; generators or structure constants change how labels join at a vertex. Sum internal color coherently within the amplitude, then square and perform the specified external-state sums and averages. Loop signs and graph factors are treated in [[12.04.03 Additional Rules]].

## Summary of the rules

| Element | Indices | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gluon propagator | $\mu\nu,ab$ | $\displaystyle\frac{-i\delta^{ab}}{\mechanical{k}^2}\left[g_{\mu\nu}-(1-\xi)\frac{\mechanical{k}_\mu\mechanical{k}_\nu}{\mechanical{k}^2}\right]$ |
| Quark propagator | $ij$ (color) | $\displaystyle\frac{i(\not{\mechanical{p}}+\mechanical{m})\delta_{ij}}{\mechanical{p}^2-\mechanical{m}^2}$ |
| Ghost propagator | $ab$ | $\displaystyle\frac{i\delta^{ab}}{\mechanical{k}^2}$ |
| $q\bar qg$ vertex | $\mu,a,ij$ | $i\signal{g}\gamma^\mu(T^a)_{ij}$ |
| $3g$ vertex | $\mu\nu\rho,abc$ | $\signal{g}f^{abc}[g^{\mu\nu}(\mechanical{p}-\mechanical{q})^\rho+\mathrm{Perm.}]$ |
| $4g$ vertex | $\mu\nu\rho\sigma,abcd$ | $-i\signal{g}^2[f^{abe}f^{cde}(\cdots)+\mathrm{Perm.}]$ |
| Ghost–gluon vertex | $\mu,abc$ | $-\signal{g}f^{abc}\mechanical{q}^\mu$ |

**Table 12.1** Summary of QCD Feynman rules in the Lorenz-gauge family.

> [!note]
> As in the source table, the propagators suppress the $i\epsilon$ prescription, and “Perm.” abbreviates the other terms. Use the complete expressions and momentum conventions in [[12.04.01 Propagators]], [[12.04.02 Vertices]], and equations (12.7)–(12.8).
