---
title: "Relations between Renormalizations"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Slavnov–Taylor identities relate the field and vertex renormalization constants so that the gauge coupling has one consistent renormalization.

The WST identities impose relations between renormalization constants.
Define the bare gauge and ghost fields by
$$
\signal{A}_{\mu,0}^a=Z_3^{1/2}\signal{A}_\mu^a,
\qquad
\signal{c}_0=Z_c^{1/2}\signal{c}.
$$

Define the bare coupling by
$$
\signal{g}_0=Z_g\signal{g}\energy{\mu}^{\varepsilon}.
$$
Let $Z_{qqg},Z_{3g},Z_{4g},Z_{ccg}$ denote the quark–gluon, three-gluon, four-gluon, and ghost–gluon vertex renormalization constants.
Then the WST identities require
$$
Z_g
=\frac{Z_{qqg}}{Z_qZ_A^{1/2}}
=\frac{Z_{3g}}{Z_A^{3/2}}
=\frac{Z_{4g}^{1/2}}{Z_A}
=\frac{Z_{ccg}}{Z_cZ_A^{1/2}}.
$$
These relations reduce the number of independent counterterms and are important in the proof of renormalizability.

> [!note]
> The source switches from $Z_3$ to $Z_A$ for the gluon-field factor; here $Z_A=Z_3$. The factor $Z_q$ is the quark-field renormalization. The power $\energy{\mu}^{\varepsilon}$ corresponds to a dimensionless renormalized coupling in $d=4-2\varepsilon$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{A}_{\mu,0}^a,\signal{A}_\mu^a$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Bare and renormalized gluon fields | The fields related by gluon-field renormalization in four dimensions. |
| $\signal{c}_0,\signal{c}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Bare and renormalized ghost fields | The fields related by ghost-field renormalization in four dimensions. |
| $\signal{g}_0$ | $\unit{MeV^{\varepsilon}}$ | Bare gauge coupling | The dimensionful coupling in d = 4 − 2ε. |
