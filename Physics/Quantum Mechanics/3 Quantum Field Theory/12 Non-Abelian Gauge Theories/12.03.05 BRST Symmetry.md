---
title: "BRST Symmetry"
---

> [!TLDR]
> BRST symmetry is a global fermionic symmetry of the gauge-fixed theory that organizes the cancellation of unphysical states.

The gauge-fixed Lagrangian density (12.11) no longer has the original local gauge invariance. It has a global BRST symmetry instead.

BRST stands for Becchi–Rouet–Stora–Tyutin (1974–1976).
With the nilpotent BRST operator $s$, the source gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
s\signal{A}_\mu^a&=D_\mu^{ab}\signal{c}^b,\\
s\signal{c}^a&=-\frac{\signal{g}}{2}f^{abc}\signal{c}^b\signal{c}^c,\\
s\bar{\signal{c}}^a&=-\frac{1}{\xi}\partial^\mu\signal{A}_\mu^a,\\
s\signal{q}&=i\signal{g}T^a\signal{c}^a\signal{q}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The source states that $s^2=0$ and $s\energy{\mathcal{L}}_{\mathrm{tot}}=0$.
BRST symmetry is used to:

1. Establish unitarity. Physical states are described by BRST cohomology, $\ker s/\operatorname{im}s$.
2. Derive the Ward–Slavnov–Taylor identities, the non-Abelian generalization of Ward–Takahashi identities. These control the relations between renormalizations.
3. Establish renormalizability.

> [!note]
> With the auxiliary field eliminated as in the displayed transformations, nilpotency on the antighost holds only on shell:
> $s^2\bar{\signal{c}}^a=-\partial^\mu D_\mu^{ab}\signal{c}^b/\xi$ vanishes by the ghost equation of motion. An auxiliary Nakanishi–Lautrup field gives an off-shell nilpotent formulation. Invariance of the action allows the Lagrangian density to change by a total derivative. Physical-state cohomology is defined by the BRST charge on the state space. Renormalizability also requires the relevant power-counting and anomaly-cancellation conditions.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{A}_\mu^a$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Gauge field | The gluon field in the BRST transformation. |
| $\signal{c}^a,\bar{\signal{c}}^a$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Ghost and antighost fields | The auxiliary scalar fields in the BRST algebra. |
| $\signal{g}$ | $1$ | Gauge coupling | The coupling in the ghost and quark transformations. |
| $\signal{q}$ | $\unit{MeV^{3/2}}$ | Quark field | The matter spinor transformed by the ghost-valued gauge parameter. |
| $\energy{\mathcal{L}}_{\mathrm{tot}}$ | $\unit{MeV^4}$ | Total gauge-fixed Lagrangian density | The density whose action is BRST invariant. |
