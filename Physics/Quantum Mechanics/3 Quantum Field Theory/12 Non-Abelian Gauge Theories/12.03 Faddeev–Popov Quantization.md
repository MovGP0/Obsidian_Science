---
title: "Faddeev–Popov Quantization"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Faddeev–Popov quantization removes the repeated integration over gauge-equivalent field configurations and introduces the gauge-fixing determinant.

The quantization starts with [[12.03.01 Gauge Orbits and Gauge Fixing|gauge orbits and the need for gauge fixing]].

Gauge fixing is needed before the quadratic gauge-field action can be inverted. Motion along a gauge orbit costs no action, so the naive quadratic kernel has redundant directions. [[12.03.02 The Faddeev–Popov Prescription]] inserts a Jacobian for the gauge condition, and [[12.03.03 Ghost Fields]] expresses that Jacobian with auxiliary anticommuting fields.

The construction is used locally around the perturbative vacuum, with zero modes and residual transformations handled separately. It does not establish a unique gauge representative on the entire configuration space. After gauge fixing, [[12.03.05 BRST Symmetry]] supplies the identities that keep the unphysical fields from becoming physical external particles. In an Abelian linear gauge the determinant is field independent, which explains why interacting FP ghosts are unnecessary there.
