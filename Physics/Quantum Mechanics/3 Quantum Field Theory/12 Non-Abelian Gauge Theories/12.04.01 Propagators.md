> [!TLDR]
> The gluon propagator depends on the gauge parameter and carries adjoint color. Quark and ghost propagators carry fundamental and adjoint color deltas, respectively.

![[QFT-12-03-Gluon-Propagator.svg|**Fig. 12.3** Gluon propagator with momentum k.]]

## Gluon propagator

The propagator in Fig. 12.3 is
$$
\signal{D}_{\mu\nu}^{ab}(\mechanical{k})
=\frac{-i\delta^{ab}}{\mechanical{k}^2+i\epsilon}
\left(g_{\mu\nu}-(1-\xi)\frac{\mechanical{k}_\mu\mechanical{k}_\nu}{\mechanical{k}^2}\right).
\tag{12.12}
$$
Common choices are $\xi=1$ (Feynman gauge) and $\xi=0$ (Landau gauge).
In Feynman gauge, the rule simplifies to
$$
\frac{-i\delta^{ab}g_{\mu\nu}}{\mechanical{k}^2+i\epsilon}.
$$

![[QFT-12-04-Quark-Propagator.svg|**Fig. 12.4** Quark propagator. The indices i and j label color in the fundamental representation.]]

## Quark propagator

The propagator in Fig. 12.4 is
$$
\signal{S}_{ij}(\mechanical{p})
=\frac{i\delta_{ij}}{\not{\mechanical{p}}-\mechanical{m}+i\epsilon}
=\frac{i(\not{\mechanical{p}}+\mechanical{m})\delta_{ij}}
{\mechanical{p}^2-\mechanical{m}^2+i\epsilon}.
$$

![[QFT-12-05-Ghost-Propagator.svg|**Fig. 12.5** Ghost propagator: dashed line with a momentum arrow for an anticommuting scalar field.]]

## Ghost propagator

The propagator in Fig. 12.5 is
$$
\signal{G}^{ab}(\mechanical{k})
=\frac{i\delta^{ab}}{\mechanical{k}^2+i\epsilon}.
$$

> [!note]
> The arrows above the lines label momentum. The quark line also has an inline fermion-flow arrow, as in the source. The first quark fraction is conventional shorthand for the Dirac inverse with the Feynman boundary prescription; the infinitesimal terms in the two fractions are not a literal algebraic equality at finite $\epsilon$.

The ghost propagator is scalar, with no Lorentz index. Its color labels are adjoint indices $a,b=1,\ldots,N^2-1$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{D}_{\mu\nu}^{ab}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-2}}$ | Gluon propagator | The gauge-dependent momentum-space two-point rule. |
| $\signal{S}_{ij}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Quark propagator | The Dirac propagator with a fundamental color delta. |
| $\signal{G}^{ab}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-2}}$ | Ghost propagator | The massless scalar propagator with adjoint color indices. |
| $\mechanical{k},\mechanical{p}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Four-momenta | The momenta in the gluon, ghost, and quark propagators. |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Quark mass | The mass parameter in the Dirac propagator. |
