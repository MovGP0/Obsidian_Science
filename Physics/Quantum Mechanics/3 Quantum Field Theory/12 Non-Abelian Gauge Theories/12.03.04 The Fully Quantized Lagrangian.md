---
title: "The Fully Quantized Lagrangian"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The gauge-fixed QCD Lagrangian combines the Yang–Mills term, a Lorenz-gauge fixing term, ghost fields, and quark fields.

In the Lorenz-gauge family, parametrized by $\xi$, the gauge-fixing density is
$$
\energy{\mathcal{L}}_{\mathrm{gf}}
=-\frac{1}{2\xi}(\partial^\mu\signal{A}_\mu^a)^2.
$$
The fully quantized Lagrangian density is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\energy{\mathcal{L}}_{\mathrm{tot}}
={}&-\frac{1}{4}\signal{F}_{\mu\nu}^a\signal{F}^{a\mu\nu}
-\frac{1}{2\xi}(\partial^\mu\signal{A}_\mu^a)^2\\
&-\bar{\signal{c}}^a\partial^\mu(D_\mu^{\mathrm{adj}})^{ab}\signal{c}^b
+\sum_f\bar{\signal{q}}_f(i\not{D}-\mechanical{m}_f)\signal{q}_f.
\end{aligned}
\tag{12.11}
$$
Here
$$
(D_\mu^{\mathrm{adj}})^{ab}
=\partial_\mu\delta^{ab}+\signal{g}f^{acb}\signal{A}_\mu^c,
$$
so
$$
(D_\mu^{\mathrm{adj}})^{ab}\signal{c}^b
=\partial_\mu\signal{c}^a+\signal{g}f^{abc}\signal{A}_\mu^b\signal{c}^c.
$$

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\energy{\mathcal{L}}_{\mathrm{gf}},\energy{\mathcal{L}}_{\mathrm{tot}}$ | $\unit{MeV^4}$ | Gauge-fixing and total Lagrangian densities | The gauge constraint term and the full gauge-fixed QCD density. |
| $\signal{A}_\mu^a$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Gluon field | The gauge field in the gauge-fixing and adjoint derivative terms. |
| $\signal{F}_{\mu\nu}^a$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Yang–Mills field strength | The non-Abelian tensor in the gauge kinetic term. |
| $\signal{c}^a,\bar{\signal{c}}^a$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Ghost and antighost fields | The auxiliary adjoint scalar fields. |
| $\signal{q}_f,\bar{\signal{q}}_f$ | $\unit{MeV^{3/2}}$ | Quark fields | The Dirac field and adjoint for flavor f. |
| $\mechanical{m}_f$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Quark mass | The mass parameter for flavor f. |
| $\signal{g}$ | $1$ | Gauge coupling | The coupling in the non-Abelian covariant derivative. |
