---
title: "The Yang–Mills Lagrangian"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The Yang–Mills Lagrangian is quadratic in the non-Abelian field strength. It contains kinetic, three-boson, and four-boson terms fixed by one gauge coupling.

Construct the simplest gauge-, Lorentz-, and parity-invariant Lagrangian density that is quadratic in $\signal{F}_{\mu\nu}$.

> [!note]
> **Yang–Mills Lagrangian**
> $$
> \energy{\mathcal{L}}_{\mathrm{YM}}
> =-\frac{1}{4}\signal{F}_{\mu\nu}^a\signal{F}^{a\mu\nu}.
> \tag{12.5}
> $$

The sum over $a$ is implicit.
Using normalization (12.2),
$$
\energy{\mathcal{L}}_{\mathrm{YM}}
=-\frac{1}{2}\operatorname{Tr}
(\signal{F}_{\mu\nu}\signal{F}^{\mu\nu}).
$$
The trace is essential: under conjugation the two field strengths transform together, and cyclicity cancels the outer group matrices. The factor relating the component and trace forms follows from the stated fundamental trace normalization. In four-dimensional natural units each field strength has mass dimension two, so every term in the expanded density has dimension four.

The minimal action used here excludes higher-dimension interactions. Gauge symmetry ties the cubic and quartic coefficients to the kinetic normalization; choosing them independently would generally destroy the symmetry. The Lorentzian minus sign matches the usual mostly-minus metric convention for a positive gauge-field energy. A free-limit check removes both interaction terms at zero coupling while leaving the quadratic kinetic term.

Expand $\signal{F}_{\mu\nu}^a$ and sort by powers of the field:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\energy{\mathcal{L}}_{\mathrm{YM}}
={}&-\frac{1}{4}
(\partial_\mu\signal{A}_\nu^a-\partial_\nu\signal{A}_\mu^a)
(\partial^\mu\signal{A}^{a\nu}-\partial^\nu\signal{A}^{a\mu})\\
&-\signal{g}f^{abc}(\partial_\mu\signal{A}_\nu^a)
\signal{A}^{b\mu}\signal{A}^{c\nu}\\
&-\frac{\signal{g}^2}{4}f^{abc}f^{ade}
\signal{A}_\mu^b\signal{A}_\nu^c\signal{A}^{d\mu}\signal{A}^{e\nu}\\
\equiv{}&\energy{\mathcal{L}}_{\mathrm{kin}}
+\energy{\mathcal{L}}_3+\energy{\mathcal{L}}_4.
\end{aligned}
\tag{12.6}
$$
There are three contributions:

- $\energy{\mathcal{L}}_{\mathrm{kin}}$ is the gauge-boson kinetic term, analogous to QED.
- $\energy{\mathcal{L}}_3\sim\signal{g}f^{abc}(\partial\signal{A})\signal{A}\signal{A}$ is the three-gluon interaction, with the single coupling $\signal{g}$.
- $\energy{\mathcal{L}}_4\sim\signal{g}^2f^{abe}f^{cde}\signal{A}^4$ is the four-gluon interaction.

This self-interaction structure is a defining feature of non-Abelian gauge theories.
In QED, the expansion stops at the kinetic term because
$\electric{F}_{\mu\nu}=\partial_\mu\electric{A}_\nu-\partial_\nu\electric{A}_\mu$
is linear in $\electric{A}_\mu$.

The full QCD Lagrangian density also contains the Dirac terms for quarks:
$$
\energy{\mathcal{L}}_{\mathrm{QCD}}
=-\frac{1}{4}\signal{F}_{\mu\nu}^a\signal{F}^{a\mu\nu}
+\sum_f\bar{\signal{q}}_f(i\not{D}-\mechanical{m}_f)\signal{q}_f.
$$

Here $\not{D}=\gamma^\mu D_\mu$, and the sum runs over the $N_f$ quark flavors $f\in\{u,d,s,c,b,t\}$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\energy{\mathcal{L}}_{\mathrm{YM}},\energy{\mathcal{L}}_{\mathrm{QCD}}$ | $\unit{MeV^4}$ | Yang–Mills and QCD Lagrangian densities | The pure gauge and gauge-plus-quark dynamics. |
| $\energy{\mathcal{L}}_{\mathrm{kin}},\energy{\mathcal{L}}_3,\energy{\mathcal{L}}_4$ | $\unit{MeV^4}$ | Kinetic and interaction densities | The terms of second, third, and fourth order in the gauge field. |
| $\signal{F}_{\mu\nu},\signal{F}_{\mu\nu}^a$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Non-Abelian field strength | The tensor whose square defines the Yang–Mills action density. |
| $\signal{A}_\mu,\signal{A}_\mu^a$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Non-Abelian gauge field | The matrix connection and its adjoint components. |
| $\signal{g}$ | $1$ | Gauge coupling | The common coupling that fixes the cubic and quartic interactions. |
| $\electric{F}_{\mu\nu}$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Electromagnetic field strength | The Abelian tensor used for comparison. |
| $\electric{A}_\mu$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Electromagnetic potential | The Abelian field with no pure-gauge cubic or quartic term. |
| $\signal{q}_f,\bar{\signal{q}}_f$ | $\unit{MeV^{3/2}}$ | Quark field and adjoint | The color spinor of flavor f. |
| $\mechanical{m}_f$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Quark mass | The mass parameter for flavor f. |
