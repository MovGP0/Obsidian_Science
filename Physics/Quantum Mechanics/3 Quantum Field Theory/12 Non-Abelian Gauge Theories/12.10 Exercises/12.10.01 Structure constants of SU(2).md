## Exercise 12.1: SU(2) structure constants

Verify explicitly that the $SU(2)$ structure constants are $f^{abc}=\varepsilon^{abc}$.
Calculate
$$
[T^1,T^2]=i\varepsilon^{12c}T^c
$$
using the Pauli-matrix generators $T^a=\sigma^a/2$.

## Solution 12.1 — Structure constants of SU(2)

Use $T^a=\sigma^a/2$. Then
$$
[T^1,T^2]=\frac14[\sigma^1,\sigma^2].
$$
Direct matrix multiplication gives
$$
\sigma^1\sigma^2
=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}0&-i\\i&0\end{pmatrix}
=\begin{pmatrix}i&0\\0&-i\end{pmatrix}
=i\sigma^3.
$$
Since $\sigma^2\sigma^1=-i\sigma^3$,
$$
[\sigma^1,\sigma^2]=2i\sigma^3.
$$

Thus
$$
[T^1,T^2]=\frac14(2i\sigma^3)=iT^3,
\qquad f^{123}=1.
$$
Antisymmetry and cyclic permutations give $f^{abc}=\varepsilon^{abc}$.
