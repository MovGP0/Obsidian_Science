## Exercise 12.7: Three-gluon vertex and conservation of color momentum

Consider the three-gluon vertex (12.7) with two gluons of the same color, $a=b$.
Use the antisymmetry of $f^{abc}$ to show that the vertex vanishes.
What does this imply for $f^{aac}=0$?

> [!note]
> “Color momentum” is the source's heading. The stated task checks a color-algebra selection rule. It does not introduce another spacetime momentum.

## Solution 12.7 — Three-gluon vertex with two equal color indices

The three-gluon vertex is proportional to $f^{abc}$. Setting $a=b$ gives
$$
f^{aac}=0
$$
by antisymmetry. Thus a vertex with two identical adjoint color labels vanishes.

> [!note] Color selection
> The source describes this as requiring three different “color types.” Distinct adjoint indices are a necessary condition for a nonzero structure constant, but they are not sufficient: some structure constants with three distinct indices also vanish. This is a statement about components in a chosen Lie-algebra basis.
