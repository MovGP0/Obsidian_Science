---
title: "Loop Expansion and Counting Powers of ℏ"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Each additional loop contributes a power of ħ to the effective action. The Gaussian fluctuation term gives the one-loop correction.

Temporarily restore $\mechanical{\hbar}$ in the generating functional:
$$
\signal{Z}[\signal{J}]
=\int\mathcal{D}\signal{\phi}\,
\exp\left[
\frac{i}{\mechanical{\hbar}}
\left(\energy{S}[\signal{\phi}]+\int\signal{J}\signal{\phi}\right)
\right].
$$
The semiclassical expansion is an expansion in $\mechanical{\hbar}$.
Write
$\signal{\phi}
=\signal{\phi_{\mathrm{cl}}}+\mechanical{\hbar}^{1/2}\signal{\eta}$
and expand:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac1{\mechanical{\hbar}}\energy{S}
[\signal{\phi_{\mathrm{cl}}}+\mechanical{\hbar}^{1/2}\signal{\eta}]
={}&\frac1{\mechanical{\hbar}}
\energy{S}[\signal{\phi_{\mathrm{cl}}}]\\
&+\frac12\int\signal{\eta}\,
\left.\frac{\delta^2\energy{S}}{\delta\signal{\phi}^2}
\right|_{\signal{\phi_{\mathrm{cl}}}}\signal{\eta}
+\mathcal{O}(\mechanical{\hbar}^{1/2}).
\end{aligned}
$$
The tree-level exponent is of order $\mechanical{\hbar}^{-1}$.
The Gaussian one-loop term is of order $\mechanical{\hbar}^0$.
Each additional loop adds a factor of $\mechanical{\hbar}$.
Thus the effective action expands as
$$
\begin{aligned}
\energy{\Gamma}[\signal{\phi_{\mathrm{cl}}}]
={}&\energy{S}[\signal{\phi_{\mathrm{cl}}}]
+\mechanical{\hbar}\energy{\Gamma}^{(\text{1 loop})}
[\signal{\phi_{\mathrm{cl}}}]\\
&+\mechanical{\hbar}^2\energy{\Gamma}^{(\text{2 loops})}
[\signal{\phi_{\mathrm{cl}}}]+\cdots.
\end{aligned}
$$
With the book's units $\mechanical{\hbar}=1$, loop number still organizes the semiclassical terms.
Perturbative calculations in small couplings can be organized through this loop expansion.

> [!note]
> The displayed action expansion omits its linear fluctuation term. This requires a stationary source-free background, or cancellation against the linear source term when the full source-coupled action is expanded. For a general off-shell background, the action alone has a nonzero linear term.
>
> Here ħ is restored as a formal loop-counting factor while the surrounding natural-unit dimensions are retained. Before setting ħ to one, action and ħ have the same physical dimension.
