---
title: "Legendre Transform of W[J]"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The Legendre transform exchanges the external source for the mean field. The effective action derivative equals minus that source.

The effective action is the Legendre transform of
$\signal{W}[\signal{J}]$ with respect to the source:
$$
\boxed{
\energy{\Gamma}[\signal{\phi_{\mathrm{cl}}}]
=\signal{W}[\signal{J}]
-\int\mathrm{d}^4\mechanical{x}\,
\signal{J}(\mechanical{x})\signal{\phi_{\mathrm{cl}}}(\mechanical{x}).
}
\tag{11.12}
$$
The mean field is
$\signal{\phi_{\mathrm{cl}}}(\mechanical{x})
=\delta\signal{W}/\delta\signal{J}(\mechanical{x})$.
Invert this relation to express the source as a functional of the mean field.

Functional differentiation gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{\delta\energy{\Gamma}[\signal{\phi_{\mathrm{cl}}}]}
{\delta\signal{\phi_{\mathrm{cl}}}(\mechanical{x})}
={}&\int\mathrm{d}^4\mechanical{y}\,
\frac{\delta\signal{W}}{\delta\signal{J}(\mechanical{y})}
\frac{\delta\signal{J}(\mechanical{y})}
{\delta\signal{\phi_{\mathrm{cl}}}(\mechanical{x})}
-\signal{J}(\mechanical{x})\\
&-\int\mathrm{d}^4\mechanical{y}\,
\signal{\phi_{\mathrm{cl}}}(\mechanical{y})
\frac{\delta\signal{J}(\mechanical{y})}
{\delta\signal{\phi_{\mathrm{cl}}}(\mechanical{x})}\\
={}&-\signal{J}(\mechanical{x}).
\end{aligned}
\tag{11.13}
$$
This is the quantum equation of motion.
At zero source, the vacuum mean field satisfies
$\delta\energy{\Gamma}/\delta\signal{\phi_{\mathrm{cl}}}=0$.

> [!note]
> The source says that the field in the presence of a source extremizes the effective action itself. Equation (11.13) instead gives a nonzero derivative when $\signal{J}\ne0$. In that case the stationary functional is $\energy{\Gamma}+\int\signal{J}\signal{\phi_{\mathrm{cl}}}$ for the imposed source. The effective action alone is stationary at zero source.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\energy{\Gamma}$ | $1$ | Effective action | The Legendre-transformed connected functional in natural units. |
| $\signal{W}$ | $1$ | Connected generating functional | The source functional transformed to the mean-field description. |
| $\signal{J}$ | $\unit{MeV^3}$ | External scalar source | The source expressed as a functional of the mean field. |
| $\signal{\phi_{\mathrm{cl}}}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Mean scalar field | The first source derivative of the connected functional. |
| $\mechanical{x},\mechanical{y}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Spacetime coordinates | The positions used in the functional derivative and source integrals. |
