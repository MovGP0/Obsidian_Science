---
title: "Ghost Fields"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Faddeev–Popov ghost fields represent the gauge-fixing determinant. They couple to non-Abelian gauge fields but decouple in QED with Lorenz gauge.

In a non-Abelian theory such as QCD, the Faddeev–Popov determinant depends on the gauge field $\signal{A}$.
It is no longer a field-independent constant.
Represent a determinant $\det\mathcal{M}[A]$ with anticommuting scalar **Faddeev–Popov ghost fields**:
$$
\det\mathcal{M}[A]
=\int\mathcal{D}\bar{\signal{c}}\mathcal{D}\signal{c}\,
\exp\left[
i\int\mathrm{d}^4\mechanical{x}\,
\bar{\signal{c}}(\mechanical{x})\mathcal{M}[A]
\signal{c}(\mechanical{x})\right].
\tag{11.28}
$$
A field-independent normalization of the Grassmann Gaussian is understood.

The fields $\signal{c}$ and $\bar{\signal{c}}$ are Lorentz scalars but anticommute.
The source calls them ghosts because this combines scalar transformation behavior with fermionic statistics.

In QED with Lorenz gauge, $\mathcal{M}=\square$ is independent of the photon field.
The ghosts decouple completely.
Their free action $\int\bar{\signal{c}}\square\signal{c}$ gives a constant determinant that is absorbed into normalization.
They do not contribute to Green functions with physical external lines.
The source describes the cancellation of unphysical photon modes in QED loops as guaranteed by the Ward identities.

> [!note]
> Ghosts are auxiliary gauge-fixing fields, not physical asymptotic particles. Their scalar Grassmann character does not represent a violation of the spin–statistics theorem in the physical state space.

In QCD, the Faddeev–Popov operator is
$$
\mathcal{M}^{ab}[A]
=-\partial^\mu D_\mu^{ab}
=-\partial^\mu\big(
\partial_\mu\delta^{ab}
+\signal{g}f^{acb}\signal{A}_\mu^c
\big).
$$
It depends explicitly on the gluon field, so ghosts interact with gluons.
After integration by parts, their Lagrangian is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\energy{\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{ghost}}^{\mathrm{QCD}}}
&=-\bar{\signal{c}}^a\partial^\mu D_\mu^{ab}\signal{c}^b\\
&=\partial^\mu\bar{\signal{c}}^a\partial_\mu\signal{c}^a
+\signal{g}f^{abc}
(\partial^\mu\bar{\signal{c}}^a)\signal{A}_\mu^b\signal{c}^c.
\end{aligned}
\tag{11.29}
$$

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{c},\bar{\signal{c}}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Ghost and antighost fields | Independent anticommuting scalar fields representing the determinant in four dimensions. |
| $\signal{A}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Non-Abelian gauge field | The gauge field on which the ghost operator depends. |
| $\mechanical{x}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Spacetime coordinate | The coordinate integrated in the ghost action. |
| $\signal{g}$ | $1$ | Strong gauge coupling | The coupling in the QCD ghost–gluon interaction. |
| $\energy{\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{ghost}}^{\mathrm{QCD}}}$ | $\unit{MeV^4}$ | QCD ghost Lagrangian density | The ghost kinetic and ghost–gluon interaction terms. |
| $\mechanical{k},\mechanical{p},\mechanical{q}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Ghost and antighost momenta | The propagator momentum and vertex momenta shown in Fig. 11.3. |
