title: "The Effective Action Γ[φ_cl]"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The effective action is the Legendre transform of the connected generating functional. It gives quantum field equations and proper vertex functions.

The effective action reorganizes the connected functional into quantum field equations and one-particle-irreducible vertices.

The question is how to describe the response of the mean field without carrying an independent source through every formula. The Legendre transform replaces the source by that mean field as the independent argument. Its first derivative gives the source needed to sustain the field; at zero source it gives a quantum stationary-field equation.

A connected diagram can contain smaller pieces joined by one propagator. Proper vertices remove these single-line connections. [[11.04.02 Proper Vertex Functions]] explains how the exact connected propagator is rebuilt by inverting the two-point kernel. Keep the distinction between a functional Hessian and a Feynman-rule vertex with its extra factor of i.

Use [[11.04.03 Effective Potential]] for constant backgrounds and [[11.04.04 Loop Expansion and Counting Powers of ℏ]] for controlled fluctuation approximations. A constant-field potential contains no full momentum dependence, so its curvature alone does not determine a physical pole mass in a general interacting theory. The free quadratic theory supplies the first check: its effective action has the same field-dependent quadratic part as the classical action.
