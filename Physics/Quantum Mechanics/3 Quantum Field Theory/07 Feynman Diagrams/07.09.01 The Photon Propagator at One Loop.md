---
title: "The Photon Propagator at One Loop"
---

> [!TLDR]
> A closed electron loop corrects the photon propagator and defines the vacuum-polarization tensor.

The fermionic bubble is the one-loop photon-propagator correction.

![[QFT-07-15-Vacuum-Polarization.svg|**Fig. 7.15** Vacuum polarization, or fermionic bubble, in the photon propagator. The closed electron loop supplies a minus sign and a spinor trace.]]

Its contribution defines the vacuum-polarization tensor $i\electric{\Pi}^{\mu\nu}(\mechanical{q})$. The loop momentum is $\mechanical{k}$; the two electron segments carry $\mechanical{k}$ and $\mechanical{k}-\mechanical{q}$.

The loop expression is
$$
\begin{aligned}
i\electric{\Pi}^{\mu\nu}(\mechanical{q})
={}&(-1)(-i\electric{e})^2
\int\frac{d^4\mechanical{k}}{(2\pi)^4}\\
&\times\operatorname{Tr}\left[
\gamma^\mu
\frac{i(\not{\mechanical{k}}+\mechanical{m})}
{\mechanical{k}^{\,2}-\mechanical{m}^2+i\epsilon}
\gamma^\nu
\frac{i(\not{\mechanical{k}}-\not{\mechanical{q}}+\mechanical{m})}
{(\mechanical{k}-\mechanical{q})^2-\mechanical{m}^2+i\epsilon}
\right].
\end{aligned} \tag{7.9}
$$
Gauge invariance requires
$$
\mechanical{q}_\mu\electric{\Pi}^{\mu\nu}(\mechanical{q})=0. \tag{7.10}
$$
Thus its Lorentz structure is
$$
\electric{\Pi}^{\mu\nu}(\mechanical{q})
=(\mechanical{q}^{\,2}g^{\mu\nu}
-\mechanical{q}^\mu\mechanical{q}^\nu)
\electric{\Pi}(\mechanical{q}^{\,2}),
$$
where $\electric{\Pi}(\mechanical{q}^{\,2})$ is a scalar function of the invariant momentum squared.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\electric{\Pi}^{\mu\nu}$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Vacuum-polarization tensor | The photon inverse-propagator correction from a fermion loop. |
| $\mechanical{q}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Photon four-momentum | The external momentum entering the polarization insertion. |
| $\mechanical{k}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Loop four-momentum | The internal electron integration momentum. |
| $\electric{e}$ | $1$ | Electric coupling | The charge magnitude at the two photon vertices. |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Electron mass | The mass in the closed fermion loop. |
| $\electric{\Pi}$ | $1$ | Scalar polarization function | The dimensionless coefficient in the transverse tensor decomposition. |
