---
title: "Signs from Fermion Loops"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Every closed fermion loop contributes a minus sign and a trace over its ordered spinor factors.

Fermionic statistics gives each closed loop of fermion propagators a factor $-1$. This follows from the anticommutation of Dirac field operators and appears as a relative sign in Wick contractions.

The loop rule is therefore:

- Multiply by $-1$ for each closed fermion loop.
- Take the spinor trace of the ordered propagator numerators and gamma matrices around that loop.
