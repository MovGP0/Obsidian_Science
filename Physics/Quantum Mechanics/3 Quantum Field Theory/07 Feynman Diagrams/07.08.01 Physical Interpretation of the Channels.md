---
title: "Physical Interpretation of the Channels"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The s channel carries total incoming energy, while the t and u channels describe direct and crossed exchange.

- **s channel:** $\sqrt{\energy{s}}$ is the CM energy. The incoming pair can produce an intermediate virtual state with virtuality $\energy{s}$, followed by the outgoing pair.
- **t channel:** The exchanged line has virtuality $\mechanical{t}$. For the usual elastic physical region, this transfer is spacelike, $\mechanical{t}<0$ away from the forward limit.
- **u channel:** This is the crossed exchange channel, with the outgoing particle assignments interchanged.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\energy{s}$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Collision invariant | The square of the total CM energy and direct-channel virtuality. |
| $\mechanical{t},\mechanical{u}$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Exchange invariants | The direct and crossed four-momentum transfers squared. |
