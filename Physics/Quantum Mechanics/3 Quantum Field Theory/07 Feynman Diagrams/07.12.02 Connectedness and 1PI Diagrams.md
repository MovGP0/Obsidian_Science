---
title: "Connectedness and 1PI Diagrams"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Connected diagrams generate cumulants, while one-particle-irreducible diagrams are generated by the effective action.

A graph is **connected** when its parts are joined by propagator paths. It is **one-particle irreducible (1PI)** when it is connected and cutting any one internal propagator cannot split it into two parts.

The generating objects organize these structures:

- $Z[J]$ generates full Green functions, including disconnected terms.
- $W[J]=-i\ln Z[J]$ generates connected Green functions.
- The effective action is the Legendre transform
  $$
  \energy{\Gamma}[\signal{\phi}]
  =W[J]-\int d^4\mechanical{x}\,\signal{J}\signal{\phi}.
  $$
  Its field derivatives generate the proper 1PI vertices.

Chapter 11 returns to this structure in the functional-integral approach.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\energy{\Gamma}$ | $1$ | Effective action | The Legendre-transformed action that generates proper vertices. |
| $\signal{\phi}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Scalar field argument | The field used as the effective-action variable. |
| $\signal{J}$ | $\unit{MeV^3}$ | Scalar source | The external source conjugate to the scalar field. |
| $\mechanical{x}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Spacetime point | The coordinate in the source-field integral. |
