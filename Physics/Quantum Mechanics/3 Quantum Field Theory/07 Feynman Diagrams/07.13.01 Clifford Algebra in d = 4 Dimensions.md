---
title: "Clifford Algebra in d = 4 Dimensions"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The four-dimensional Clifford algebra gives contraction rules that shorten products of gamma matrices.

With metric signature $(+,-,-,-)$,
$$
\{\gamma^\mu,\gamma^\nu\}=2g^{\mu\nu}. \tag{7.12}
$$
It gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
\gamma^\mu\gamma_\mu&=4,\\
\gamma^\mu\not a\gamma_\mu&=-2\not a,\\
\gamma^\mu\not a\not b\gamma_\mu&=4(a\cdot b),\\
\gamma^\mu\not a\not b\not c\gamma_\mu&=-2\not c\not b\not a.
\end{aligned}
$$
The vectors $a,b,c$ are arbitrary algebraic arguments, and $\not a=\gamma^\mu a_\mu$. Scalar right-hand sides multiply the identity matrix.
