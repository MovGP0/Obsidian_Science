> [!TLDR]
> Position-space quartic Feynman rules use a scalar propagator for each line and an integrated four-leg interaction vertex.

Use the real scalar density
$$
\energy{\mathcal L}
=\frac12(\partial_\mu\signal{\phi})^2
-\frac12\mechanical{m}^2\signal{\phi}^2
-\frac{g}{4!}\signal{\phi}^4.
$$

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\energy{\mathcal L}$ | $\unit{MeV^4}$ | Scalar Lagrangian density | The free and quartic interaction density. |
| $\signal{\phi}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Real scalar field | The field represented by scalar lines. |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Scalar mass | The mass in the free propagator. |
| $g$ | $1$ | Quartic coupling | The four-field interaction strength. |
