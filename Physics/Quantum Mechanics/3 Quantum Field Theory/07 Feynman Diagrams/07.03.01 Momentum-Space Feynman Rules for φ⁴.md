---
title: "Momentum-Space Feynman Rules for φ⁴"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Scalar momentum-space diagrams use i divided by the inverse propagator for each internal line and −ig for each quartic vertex.

The quartic scalar rules begin with:

1. **Internal line** with momentum $\mechanical{k}$:
   $$
   \frac{i}{\mechanical{k}^{\,2}-\mechanical{m}^2+i\epsilon}.
   $$
2. **Four-leg vertex:**
   $$
   -ig.
   $$

3. **Momentum conservation:** Conserve four-momentum at each vertex.
4. **Loop momenta:** Integrate over each independent internal momentum:
   $$
   \int\frac{d^4\mechanical{k}}{(2\pi)^4}.
   $$
5. **Symmetry factor:** Divide by the diagram factor $S$.
6. **Fermion-loop sign:** Include $(-1)^F$ for $F$ closed fermion loops. This matters for QED and other fermionic theories; it does not add a sign in the pure scalar model.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{k}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Internal momentum | The four-momentum carried by the scalar line. |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Scalar mass | The mass in the propagator. |
| $g$ | $1$ | Quartic coupling | The interaction factor at each vertex. |
