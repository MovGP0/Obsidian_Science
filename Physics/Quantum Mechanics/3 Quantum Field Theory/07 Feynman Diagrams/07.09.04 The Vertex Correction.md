---
title: "The Vertex Correction"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The one-loop electron-photon vertex correction is the third primitive ultraviolet-divergent QED structure.

The vertex correction is the one-loop correction to the electron–photon interaction, shown in Fig. 7.17.

![[QFT-07-17-Vertex-Correction.svg|**Fig. 7.17** One-loop QED vertex correction. A virtual photon joins two electron-line segments. The logarithmic divergence contributes to charge renormalization.]]

The diagram uses external electron momenta $\mechanical{p},\mechanical{p}'$, external photon momentum $\mechanical{q}$, and loop momentum $\mechanical{k}$.

A virtual photon links two segments of the same open electron line. Follow that line through all three vertices to retain matrix order. There is one loop momentum but no closed fermion chain, so no extra closed-loop minus sign applies.

The two internal electron propagators each decrease the large-momentum degree by one and the internal photon propagator decreases it by two. Together with the four-dimensional measure, this gives degree zero: the primitive ultraviolet divergence is logarithmic. The divergent vertex part is constrained by the electron self-energy through the Ward identity. The same diagram also has finite momentum-dependent parts; these must not be discarded with its divergent part, because they contain the magnetic form factor.

Vacuum polarization, electron self-energy, and the vertex correction together form the one-loop QED renormalization program developed in Chapter 9.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{p},\mechanical{p}'$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Electron momenta | The external fermion momenta of the vertex correction. |
| $\mechanical{q}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | External photon momentum | The momentum at the third interaction leg. |
| $\mechanical{k}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Loop momentum | The virtual photon momentum in the vertex loop. |
