title: "The Variables in the Center-of-Mass Frame"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Center-of-mass kinematics expresses momentum-transfer invariants through the collision energy, masses, and scattering angle.

For equal incoming masses $\mechanical{m}_1=\mechanical{m}_2=\mechanical{m}$ and equal outgoing masses $\mechanical{m}_3=\mechanical{m}_4=\mechanical{m}'$,
$$
\mechanical{t}
=\mechanical{m}^2+\mechanical{m}'^{\,2}-\frac{\energy{s}}2
+\frac{\lambda^{1/2}(\energy{s},\mechanical{m}^2,\mechanical{m}^2)
\lambda^{1/2}(\energy{s},\mechanical{m}'^{\,2},\mechanical{m}'^{\,2})}
{2\energy{s}}\cos\mechanical{\theta}.
$$
Here $\mechanical{\theta}$ is the CM scattering angle. The Källén function in this formula is $\lambda(a,b,c)=a^2+b^2+c^2-2ab-2ac-2bc$; it is not the quartic coupling.

In the ultrarelativistic limit $\sqrt{\energy{s}}\gg\mechanical{m},\mechanical{m}'$, the kinematic square roots tend to $\energy{s}$ and the mass terms can be neglected:
$$
\mechanical{t}\approx-\frac{\energy{s}}2(1-\cos\mechanical{\theta}),
\qquad
\mechanical{u}\approx-\frac{\energy{s}}2(1+\cos\mechanical{\theta}),
$$
with $\energy{s}+\mechanical{t}+\mechanical{u}\approx0$ in that limit.

## Example 7.5 — Møller scattering in invariant variables

In the $\mechanical{m}_e\to0$ limit, equation (7.2) gives
$$
\overline{\lvert\signal{\mathcal M}\rvert^2}_{\text{Møller}}
=2\electric{e}^{\,4}
\left[
\frac{\energy{s}^{\,2}+\mechanical{u}^{\,2}}{\mechanical{t}^{\,2}}
+\frac{\energy{s}^{\,2}+\mechanical{t}^{\,2}}{\mechanical{u}^{\,2}}
+\frac{2\energy{s}^{\,2}}{\mechanical{t}\mechanical{u}}
\right].
$$

The three terms come from the direct squared amplitude, the crossed squared amplitude, and their interference $2\operatorname{Re}(\signal{\mathcal M}_t\signal{\mathcal M}_u^*)$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{m}_1,\mechanical{m}_2,\mechanical{m}_3,\mechanical{m}_4,\mechanical{m},\mechanical{m}',\mechanical{m}_e$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | External masses | The incoming and outgoing pair masses and the electron mass. |
| $\energy{s}$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | CM energy squared | The total collision invariant. |
| $\mechanical{t},\mechanical{u}$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Transfer invariants | The direct and crossed momentum transfers squared. |
| $\mechanical{\theta}$ | $1$ | CM scattering angle | The angle between initial and final spatial momenta. |
| $\signal{\mathcal M}$ | $1$ | Møller amplitude | The invariant electron–electron scattering amplitude. |
| $\electric{e}$ | $1$ | Electric coupling | The QED charge magnitude. |
| $\signal{\mathcal M}_t,\signal{\mathcal M}_u$ | $1$ | Channel amplitudes | The direct and crossed Møller scattering terms. |
