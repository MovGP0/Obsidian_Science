---
title: "Møller Scattering — e⁻e⁻ → e⁻e⁻"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Møller scattering has direct and crossed photon-exchange amplitudes with a relative minus sign from identical fermions.

Møller scattering is electron–electron scattering. At leading nonzero order $\electric{e}^{\,2}$, the process
$$
\mathrm e^-(\mechanical{p}_1,s_1)\mathrm e^-(\mechanical{p}_2,s_2)
\to
\mathrm e^-(\mechanical{p}'_1,s'_1)\mathrm e^-(\mechanical{p}'_2,s'_2)
$$
has two tree diagrams.

![[QFT-07-10-Moller-Tree-Diagrams.svg|**Fig. 7.10** Tree-level Møller scattering. The t- and u-channel photon-exchange diagrams differ by exchange of the identical outgoing electrons and have a relative fermionic minus sign.]]

> [!note]
> The source caption says that the incoming particles are exchanged. Its displayed momentum labels exchange the outgoing particles. Both describe the same identical-fermion antisymmetrization, but the caption here follows the drawn labels.

## Direct-channel amplitude

In the matrix order along the two fermion lines,
$$
\begin{aligned}
i\signal{\mathcal M}_t
={}&\bar u^{s'_1}(\mechanical{p}'_1)
(-i\electric{e}\gamma^\mu)u^{s_1}(\mechanical{p}_1)\\
&\times\frac{-ig_{\mu\nu}}
{(\mechanical{p}_1-\mechanical{p}'_1)^2+i\epsilon}\,
\bar u^{s'_2}(\mechanical{p}'_2)
(-i\electric{e}\gamma^\nu)u^{s_2}(\mechanical{p}_2).
\end{aligned}
$$

The direct transfer is $\mechanical{q}=\mechanical{p}_1-\mechanical{p}'_1
=\mechanical{p}'_2-\mechanical{p}_2$, with $\mechanical{q}^{\,2}=\mechanical{t}$.

Exchange the identical outgoing fermions to obtain the crossed contribution:
$$
\begin{aligned}
i\signal{\mathcal M}_u
={}&-\bar u^{s'_2}(\mechanical{p}'_2)
(-i\electric{e}\gamma^\mu)u^{s_1}(\mechanical{p}_1)\\
&\times\frac{-ig_{\mu\nu}}
{(\mechanical{p}_1-\mechanical{p}'_2)^2+i\epsilon}\,
\bar u^{s'_1}(\mechanical{p}'_1)
(-i\electric{e}\gamma^\nu)u^{s_2}(\mechanical{p}_2).
\end{aligned}
$$
The total is $i\signal{\mathcal M}=i\signal{\mathcal M}_t+i\signal{\mathcal M}_u$. Suppressing spin labels,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\signal{\mathcal M}
={}&\frac{\electric{e}^{\,2}}{\mechanical{t}}\,
[\bar u(\mechanical{p}'_1)\gamma^\mu u(\mechanical{p}_1)]
[\bar u(\mechanical{p}'_2)\gamma_\mu u(\mechanical{p}_2)]\\
&-\frac{\electric{e}^{\,2}}{\mechanical{u}}\,
[\bar u(\mechanical{p}'_2)\gamma^\mu u(\mechanical{p}_1)]
[\bar u(\mechanical{p}'_1)\gamma_\mu u(\mechanical{p}_2)].
\end{aligned} \tag{7.2}
$$

> [!note] Remark 7.2 — Identical-fermion sign
> Exchanging the two outgoing electrons changes the amplitude's sign. The relative minus sign in (7.2) follows from fermionic field anticommutation; it is not an extra rule added by hand.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\electric{e}$ | $1$ | Electric coupling | The QED charge magnitude. |
| $\mechanical{p}_1,\mechanical{p}_2,\mechanical{p}'_1,\mechanical{p}'_2$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Electron momenta | The incoming and outgoing electron four-momenta. |
| $u,\bar u$ | $\unit{MeV^{1/2}}$ | Electron spinors | The covariantly normalized external spinor factors. |
| $\signal{\mathcal M}_t$ | $1$ | Direct-channel amplitude | The invariant direct photon-exchange scattering contribution. |
| $\mechanical{q}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Photon transfer | The four-momentum of the direct exchanged photon. |
| $\mechanical{t},\mechanical{u}$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Transfer invariants | The direct and crossed exchanged four-momenta squared. |
| $\signal{\mathcal M},\signal{\mathcal M}_u$ | $1$ | Scattering amplitudes | The total and crossed Møller invariant amplitudes. |
