> [!TLDR]
> Even gamma traces reduce to metric contractions, while γ⁵ with four gamma matrices gives the Levi-Civita tensor.

The trace identities are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Tr}[1]&=4,\\
\operatorname{Tr}[\gamma^\mu]&=0,\\
\operatorname{Tr}[\gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu]&=4g^{\mu\nu},\\
\operatorname{Tr}[\gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu\gamma^\rho\gamma^\sigma]
&=4(g^{\mu\nu}g^{\rho\sigma}-g^{\mu\rho}g^{\nu\sigma}
+g^{\mu\sigma}g^{\nu\rho}),\\
\operatorname{Tr}[\gamma^5]&=0,\\
\operatorname{Tr}[\gamma^5\gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu]&=0,\\
\operatorname{Tr}[\gamma^5\gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu\gamma^\rho\gamma^\sigma]
&=-4i\epsilon^{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}.
\end{aligned} \tag{7.13}
$$
These use the chapter's four-dimensional gamma-matrix convention, consistent with $\epsilon^{0123}=+1$. The four-gamma trace here has the correct final pairing, unlike the repeated pairing printed in (7.4).

## Example 7.8 — Leptonic trace for muon-pair production

For $\mechanical{m}_e=0$,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\signal{L}_e^{\mu\nu}
&=\operatorname{Tr}
[\mechanical{q}_{1\alpha}\gamma^\alpha\gamma^\mu
\mechanical{q}_{2\beta}\gamma^\beta\gamma^\nu]\\
&=\mechanical{q}_{1\alpha}\mechanical{q}_{2\beta}
\operatorname{Tr}[\gamma^\alpha\gamma^\mu\gamma^\beta\gamma^\nu]\\
&=4\mechanical{q}_{1\alpha}\mechanical{q}_{2\beta}
(g^{\alpha\mu}g^{\beta\nu}-g^{\alpha\beta}g^{\mu\nu}
+g^{\alpha\nu}g^{\mu\beta})\\
&=4(\mechanical{q}_1^\mu\mechanical{q}_2^\nu
-g^{\mu\nu}\mechanical{q}_1\cdot\mechanical{q}_2
+\mechanical{q}_1^\nu\mechanical{q}_2^\mu).
\end{aligned}
$$
With the massless muon tensor,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\signal{L}_e^{\mu\nu}\signal{L}_{\mu\nu}^{(m)}
={}&4(\mechanical{q}_1^\mu\mechanical{q}_2^\nu
-g^{\mu\nu}\mechanical{q}_1\cdot\mechanical{q}_2
+\mechanical{q}_1^\nu\mechanical{q}_2^\mu)\\
&\times4(\mechanical{p}_{2\mu}\mechanical{p}_{1\nu}
-g_{\mu\nu}\mechanical{p}_2\cdot\mechanical{p}_1
+\mechanical{p}_{2\nu}\mechanical{p}_{1\mu})\\
={}&16[2(\mechanical{q}_1\cdot\mechanical{p}_2)
(\mechanical{q}_2\cdot\mechanical{p}_1)
+2(\mechanical{q}_1\cdot\mechanical{p}_1)
(\mechanical{q}_2\cdot\mechanical{p}_2)].
\end{aligned}
$$

## Physical quantities
