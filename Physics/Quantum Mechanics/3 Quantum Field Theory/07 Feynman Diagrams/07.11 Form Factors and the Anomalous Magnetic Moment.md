---
title: "Form Factors and the Anomalous Magnetic Moment"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The on-shell Dirac and Pauli form factors describe electric coupling and the anomalous magnetic moment.

For on-shell external fermions in parity-preserving QED, the vertex matrix element can be written with two form factors. With $\mechanical{q}=\mechanical{p}'-\mechanical{p}$,
$$
\Gamma^\mu(\mechanical{p}',\mechanical{p})
=\gamma^\mu\electric{F}_1(\mechanical{q}^{\,2})
+\frac{i\sigma^{\mu\nu}\mechanical{q}_\nu}{2\mechanical{m}}
\magnetic{F}_2(\mechanical{q}^{\,2}),
$$
where $\sigma^{\mu\nu}=\frac i2[\gamma^\mu,\gamma^\nu]$. The functions $\electric{F}_1$ and $\magnetic{F}_2$ are the Dirac and Pauli form factors. At tree level, $\electric{F}_1=1$ and $\magnetic{F}_2=0$.

> [!note]
> The source states this decomposition for the full vertex without specifying on-shell external fermions. A general off-shell vertex has more tensor structures. The two-form-factor statement applies to the on-shell electromagnetic matrix element.

The electron anomaly is
$$
\magnetic{a}_e=\frac{\magnetic{g}-2}{2}=\magnetic{F}_2(0),
$$
where $\magnetic{g}$ is the magnetic g factor, not the scalar coupling used earlier.

Schwinger's 1948 one-loop result is
$$
\magnetic{a}_e^{(1)}
=\frac{\electric{\alpha}}{2\pi}\approx0.00116. \tag{7.11}
$$
The source reports agreement of the measured anomaly with the theoretical prediction through twelve significant figures, including QED terms through order $(\electric{\alpha}/\pi)^5$. This precision statement is quoted from the supplied source.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{p},\mechanical{p}',\mechanical{q}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Vertex momenta | The on-shell fermion momenta and photon momentum transfer. |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Electron mass | The mass in the Pauli vertex normalization. |
| $\electric{F}_1$ | $1$ | Dirac form factor | The electric-current form factor. |
| $\magnetic{F}_2$ | $1$ | Pauli form factor | The magnetic form factor that gives the anomaly at zero transfer. |
| $\magnetic{a}_e$ | $1$ | Electron magnetic anomaly | Half the difference of the magnetic g factor from two. |
| $\magnetic{g}$ | $1$ | Magnetic g factor | The electron's dimensionless magnetic-moment factor. |
| $\electric{\alpha}$ | $1$ | Fine-structure constant | The electromagnetic expansion parameter. |
