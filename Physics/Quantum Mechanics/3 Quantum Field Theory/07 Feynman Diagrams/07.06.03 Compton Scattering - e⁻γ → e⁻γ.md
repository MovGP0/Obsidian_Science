> [!TLDR]
> Tree-level Compton scattering contains two photon orderings on the electron line whose amplitudes must be added.

Compton scattering is the scattering of a real photon by an electron. At tree level it has a direct and a crossed diagram.

The direct contribution is
$$
\begin{aligned}
i\signal{\mathcal M}_{\mathrm{dir}}
={}&\bar u(\mechanical{p}')
(-i\electric{e}\gamma^\nu)\signal{\epsilon}_\nu^*(\mechanical{k}')\\
&\times
\frac{i(\not{\mechanical{p}}+\not{\mechanical{k}}+\mechanical{m})}
{(\mechanical{p}+\mechanical{k})^2-\mechanical{m}^2+i\epsilon}
(-i\electric{e}\gamma^\mu)\signal{\epsilon}_\mu(\mechanical{k})
u(\mechanical{p}).
\end{aligned}
$$

![[QFT-07-12-Compton-Tree-Diagrams.svg|**Fig. 7.12** The two tree-level Compton diagrams. The direct internal electron has momentum p + k; the crossed internal electron has momentum p − k′.]]

The crossed contribution is
$$
\begin{aligned}
i\signal{\mathcal M}_{\mathrm{cross}}
={}&\bar u(\mechanical{p}')
(-i\electric{e}\gamma^\mu)\signal{\epsilon}_\mu(\mechanical{k})\\
&\times\frac{i(\not{\mechanical{p}}-\not{\mechanical{k}}'+\mechanical{m})}
{(\mechanical{p}-\mechanical{k}')^2-\mechanical{m}^2}
(-i\electric{e}\gamma^\nu)\signal{\epsilon}_\nu^*(\mechanical{k}')
u(\mechanical{p}).
\end{aligned}
$$
With $\mechanical{p}^{\,2}=\mechanical{m}^2$ and real-photon $\mechanical{k}^{\,2}=\mechanical{k}'^{\,2}=0$,
$$
(\mechanical{p}+\mechanical{k})^2-\mechanical{m}^2
=2\mechanical{p}\cdot\mechanical{k},
\qquad
(\mechanical{p}-\mechanical{k}')^2-\mechanical{m}^2
=-2\mechanical{p}\cdot\mechanical{k}'.
$$
The full amplitude is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\signal{\mathcal M}
=-\electric{e}^{\,2}\bar u(\mechanical{p}')
\Bigg[
&\frac{\not{\signal{\epsilon}}^{\,*}(\mechanical{k}')
(\not{\mechanical{p}}+\not{\mechanical{k}}+\mechanical{m})
\not{\signal{\epsilon}}(\mechanical{k})}
{2\mechanical{p}\cdot\mechanical{k}}\\
&+\frac{\not{\signal{\epsilon}}(\mechanical{k})
(\not{\mechanical{p}}-\not{\mechanical{k}}'+\mechanical{m})
\not{\signal{\epsilon}}^{\,*}(\mechanical{k}')}
{-2\mechanical{p}\cdot\mechanical{k}'}
\Bigg]u(\mechanical{p}).
\end{aligned}
$$
The usual pole prescription is implicit in the shortened denominators.

## Example 7.4 — Klein–Nishina formula

To calculate the unpolarized squared amplitude, sum photon polarizations. In the full gauge-invariant amplitude, the Ward identity permits the replacement
$$
\sum_\lambda\signal{\epsilon}_\mu^\lambda
\signal{\epsilon}_\nu^{\lambda*}\longrightarrow-g_{\mu\nu}.
$$

The longitudinal terms vanish by the Ward identity. Use the Compton invariants
$$
\energy{s}=(\mechanical{p}+\mechanical{k})^2,
\qquad
\mechanical{u}=(\mechanical{p}-\mechanical{k}')^2.
$$
In the laboratory frame, with the electron initially at rest, the result is the **Klein–Nishina formula**:
$$
\frac{d\mechanical{\sigma}}{d\mechanical{\Omega}}
=\frac{\electric{\alpha}^{\,2}}{2\mechanical{m}_e^2}
\left(\frac{\energy{\omega}'}{\energy{\omega}}\right)^2
\left(
\frac{\energy{\omega}}{\energy{\omega}'}
+\frac{\energy{\omega}'}{\energy{\omega}}
-\sin^2\mechanical{\theta}
\right). \tag{7.8}
$$
Here $\energy{\omega}$ and $\energy{\omega}'$ are the initial and final photon energies, and $\mechanical{\theta}$ is the photon scattering angle. They satisfy
$$
\frac1{\energy{\omega}'}-\frac1{\energy{\omega}}
=\frac{1-\cos\mechanical{\theta}}{\mechanical{m}_e}.
$$
In the low-energy limit $\energy{\omega}\ll\mechanical{m}_e$, this becomes the Thomson formula:
$$
\frac{d\mechanical{\sigma}}{d\mechanical{\Omega}}
=\left(\frac{\electric{\alpha}}{\mechanical{m}_e}\right)^2
\frac12(1+\cos^2\mechanical{\theta}),
$$
$$
\mechanical{\sigma}_{\mathrm{Thomson}}
=\frac{8\pi\electric{\alpha}^{\,2}}{3\mechanical{m}_e^2}
\approx0.665\,\unit{barn}.
$$
The electron mass is denoted $\mechanical{m}$ in the amplitude above and $\mechanical{m}_e$ in these cross-section formulas.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{\mathcal M}_{\mathrm{dir}}$ | $1$ | Direct Compton amplitude | The tree amplitude with the incoming photon encountered first along the electron line. |
| $u,\bar u$ | $\unit{MeV^{1/2}}$ | Electron spinors | The incoming and outgoing on-shell spinors. |
| $\mechanical{p},\mechanical{p}'$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Electron momenta | The incoming and outgoing electron four-momenta. |
| $\mechanical{k},\mechanical{k}'$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Photon momenta | The incoming and outgoing real-photon four-momenta. |
| $\electric{e}$ | $1$ | Electric coupling | The QED interaction strength. |
| $\signal{\epsilon}_\mu,\signal{\epsilon}_\nu^*$ | $1$ | Photon polarizations | The incoming and outgoing polarization vectors. |
| $\mechanical{m},\mechanical{m}_e$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Electron mass | The mass in the intermediate fermion propagator. |
| $\signal{\mathcal M}_{\mathrm{cross}},\signal{\mathcal M}$ | $1$ | Compton amplitudes | The crossed term and the sum of both tree diagrams. |
| $\energy{s}$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Compton collision invariant | The square of the total incoming four-momentum. |
| $\mechanical{u}$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Crossed Compton invariant | The crossed electron-line momentum squared. |
| $\mechanical{\sigma},\mechanical{\sigma}_{\mathrm{Thomson}}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-2}}$ | Compton cross sections | The scattering area and its low-energy total limit. |
| $\mechanical{\Omega},\mechanical{\theta}$ | $1$ | Scattering angles | The photon solid angle and polar scattering angle. |
| $\electric{\alpha}$ | $1$ | Fine-structure constant | The electromagnetic coupling e squared divided by 4π. |
