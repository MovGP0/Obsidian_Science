---
title: "Bhabha Scattering — e⁺e⁻ → e⁺e⁻"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Bhabha scattering combines s-channel annihilation with t-channel photon exchange and a relative fermionic sign.

Bhabha scattering is $\mathrm e^+\mathrm e^-\to\mathrm e^+\mathrm e^-$. Unlike muon-pair production, it has two tree contributions: s-channel annihilation and t-channel photon exchange.

With the electron assigned $\mechanical{p}_1\to\mechanical{p}_3$ and the positron $\mechanical{p}_2\to\mechanical{p}_4$, the source writes
$$
\signal{\mathcal M}_s
=\frac{\electric{e}^{\,2}}{\energy{s}}
[\bar v(\mechanical{p}_2)\gamma^\mu u(\mechanical{p}_1)]
[\bar u(\mechanical{p}_3)\gamma_\mu v(\mechanical{p}_4)],
$$
$$
\signal{\mathcal M}_t
=-\frac{\electric{e}^{\,2}}{\mechanical{t}}
[\bar u(\mechanical{p}_3)\gamma^\mu u(\mechanical{p}_1)]
[\bar v(\mechanical{p}_2)\gamma_\mu v(\mechanical{p}_4)].
$$
The relative sign follows from the ordering of fermion operators in Wick's theorem.

The two diagrams connect the same initial and final states, so their amplitudes must be added coherently. Distinguishing the outgoing electron from the positron removes an identical-particle counting problem; it does not remove interference between annihilation and exchange. The relative sign shown above must remain consistent with the chosen external fermion-state ordering.

The exchange denominator can approach zero in the forward region, whereas the annihilation denominator carries the fixed positive squared CM energy. This explains why the angular distribution can be strongly forward enhanced rather than sharing the simple muon-pair angular form. Both amplitudes are dimensionless: four external spinors supply mass dimension two, canceled by the exchanged invariant.

> [!note]
> The source caption says that there is no minus-sign factor because the outgoing electron and positron are distinct. There is no identical-final-electron antisymmetrization as in Møller scattering, but relative Wick signs between the two Bhabha contributions still matter, as in the amplitudes above.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{\mathcal M}_s,\signal{\mathcal M}_t$ | $1$ | Bhabha channel amplitudes | The annihilation and exchange contributions. |
| $\electric{e}$ | $1$ | Electric coupling | The QED charge magnitude. |
| $\energy{s}$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Collision invariant | The total incoming energy squared. |
| $\mechanical{t}$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Exchange invariant | The transferred momentum squared. |
| $\mechanical{p}_1,\mechanical{p}_2,\mechanical{p}_3,\mechanical{p}_4$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | External four-momenta | The incoming electron/positron and outgoing electron/positron momenta. |
| $u,\bar u,v,\bar v$ | $\unit{MeV^{1/2}}$ | External spinors | The normalized electron and positron spinors. |
