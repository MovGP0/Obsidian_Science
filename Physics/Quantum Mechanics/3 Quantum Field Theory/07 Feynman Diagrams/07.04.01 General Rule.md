title: "General Rule"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Dyson and vertex factorials combine with Wick-contraction multiplicities to give one divided by the diagram symmetry factor.

At order $n$ in the quartic coupling $g$, the Dyson expansion has $1/n!$, and the vertices supply $1/(4!)^n$. The contraction multiplicity cancels part of these factors. The remaining coefficient is the reciprocal symmetry factor.

## Example 7.1 — Quartic-theory symmetry factors

- **Tadpole:** At first order, two legs of a quartic vertex form one loop and two remain external. The factor is $S=2$.
- **Double bubble:** For the source's second-order arrangement of two bubbles in series, $S=4$.
- **Figure-eight vacuum bubble:** Two loops close at one quartic vertex, giving $S=8$.

For the tadpole, attach the first labeled external line to any of four fields and the second to any of the remaining three. The final pair contracts internally. Twelve contractions divided by the vertex factorial twenty-four leave one half. This direct count fixes the result without relying on the shape of the drawing.

For the figure-eight, choose a partner for one of the four fields. There are three choices; the remaining pair is then fixed. Three divided by twenty-four gives one eighth. These examples show why the same interaction vertex can have different residual factors in different graphs.

The source gives the schematic counting rule
$$
S=
\prod_i(\text{symmetry of each repeated propagator})_i
\prod_j(\text{permutations of equivalent vertices})_j.
$$
As a check, the first-order vacuum insertion
$$
\langle0|\mathcal T\left\{
\frac{-ig}{4!}\int d^4\mechanical{z}\,\signal{\phi}^4(\mechanical{z})
\right\}|0\rangle
$$
has three distinct contractions of figure-eight type. They are topologically equivalent, and $4!/S=3$ gives $S=8$.

> [!note]
> The product rule is a counting mnemonic. The graph automorphism group or explicit Wick count remains the general definition; factors must not count the same symmetry more than once.
