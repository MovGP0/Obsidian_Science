---
title: "The QED Vertex"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The QED vertex joins two fermion lines and one photon line and supplies a coupling times a gamma matrix.

The interaction $-\electric{e}\bar{\signal{\psi}}\gamma^\mu\signal{\psi}\electric{A}_\mu$ gives a vertex with two fermion legs and one photon leg. In momentum space its factor is
$$
-i\electric{e}\gamma^\mu.
$$
The index belongs to the photon. It contracts with the photon propagator or external polarization vector.

![[QFT-07-09-QED-Vertex.svg|**Fig. 7.9** Fundamental QED vertex with two fermion legs and one photon leg. The vertex factor is $-i\electric{e}\gamma^\mu$.]]

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\electric{e}$ | $1$ | Electric coupling | The interaction strength at the electron-photon vertex. |
| $\signal{\psi},\bar{\signal{\psi}}$ | $\unit{MeV^{3/2}}$ | Dirac fields | The fermion field and adjoint in the interaction. |
| $\electric{A}_\mu$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Photon field | The electromagnetic four-potential. |
