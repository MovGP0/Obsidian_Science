title: "Internal Propagators"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Internal QED lines use a covariant photon propagator or a Dirac propagator, with fermion arrows recording fermion-number flow.

## Photon propagator

In Feynman gauge, $\xi=1$, a photon with momentum $\mechanical{k}$ supplies
$$
\signal{D}_F^{\mu\nu}(\mechanical{k})
=\frac{-ig^{\mu\nu}}{\mechanical{k}^{\,2}+i\epsilon}.
$$
Represent it with a wavy line.

![[QFT-07-06-Internal-Photon-Propagator.svg|**Fig. 7.6** Internal photon propagator in Feynman gauge. The wavy line carries the photon momentum and its two Lorentz indices.]]

## Electron propagator

For a fermion with momentum $\mechanical{p}$ and mass $\mechanical{m}$,
$$
\signal{S}_F(\mechanical{p})
=\frac{i(\not{\mechanical{p}}+\mechanical{m})}
{\mechanical{p}^{\,2}-\mechanical{m}^2+i\epsilon}
\equiv\frac{i}{\not{\mechanical{p}}-\mechanical{m}+i\epsilon}.
$$
The second expression is a formal inverse with the pole prescription understood as in the first expression, rather than a literal finite-regulator matrix identity.

Use a solid line with an arrow. The arrow indicates fermion-number flow, rather than energy flow.

![[QFT-07-07-Internal-Fermion-Propagator.svg|**Fig. 7.7** Internal fermion propagator for a virtual electron or positron. The solid-line arrow records fermion-number flow, and the line carries the Dirac propagator.]]

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{D}_F^{\mu\nu}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-2}}$ | Photon propagator | The internal momentum-space photon propagator. |
| $\signal{S}_F$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Dirac propagator | The internal momentum-space fermion propagator. |
| $\mechanical{k},\mechanical{p}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Internal four-momenta | The photon and fermion line momenta. |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Fermion mass | The mass in the Dirac propagator. |
