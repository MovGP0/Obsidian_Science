> [!TLDR]
> Graph symmetries can exchange loop lines without changing the labeled external contribution.

![[QFT-07-05-Symmetry-Factor-Examples.svg|**Fig. 7.5** Source examples of nontrivial symmetry factors: (a) a tadpole with S = 2; (b) a bubble on a propagator, labeled S = 2.]]

The external momentum labels are held fixed when counting graph symmetries.

> [!note]
> The source caption places both panels in quartic scalar theory. In panel (b), each of the two vertices has two internal lines and one external line, so it is a cubic, rather than quartic, topology. The drawing and its stated factor are retained as source material.
