---
title: "Ordering along Fermion Lines"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Keep the gamma matrices and propagators in their order along each continuous fermion line.

Read the matrix product from right to left along fermion-number flow. To construct it explicitly, start at the outgoing fermion bra and follow the line against its arrow. Write each matrix in the order encountered, ending at the incoming fermion ket. Matrix order must be preserved.
