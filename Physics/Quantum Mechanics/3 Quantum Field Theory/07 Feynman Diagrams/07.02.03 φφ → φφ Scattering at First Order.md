---
title: "φφ → φφ Scattering at First Order"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Leading scalar two-to-two scattering has one four-leg tree vertex where all external lines meet.

Calculate the first-order contribution in $g$ to two-to-two scalar scattering. The incoming momenta are $\mechanical{p}_1,\mechanical{p}_2$, and the outgoing momenta are $\mechanical{p}'_1,\mechanical{p}'_2$.

At order $g^1$, there is one topological tree type:

![[QFT-07-03-Scalar-Tree-Scattering.svg|**Fig. 7.3** Tree-level scalar two-to-two scattering at first order in the quartic coupling. All four external lines meet at one vertex.]]

The source writes the integrated position-space factor as
$$
\begin{aligned}
i\signal{\mathcal M}
&=(-ig)\int d^4\mechanical{z}\,
e^{i\mechanical{p}_1\cdot\mechanical{z}}
e^{i\mechanical{p}_2\cdot\mechanical{z}}
e^{-i\mechanical{p}'_1\cdot\mechanical{z}}
e^{-i\mechanical{p}'_2\cdot\mechanical{z}}\\
&=(-ig)(2\pi)^4\delta^{(4)}
(\mechanical{p}_1+\mechanical{p}_2-\mechanical{p}'_1-\mechanical{p}'_2).
\end{aligned}
$$
After removing the total momentum-conservation distribution, the invariant amplitude is
$$
\signal{\mathcal M}=-g.
$$
It is momentum independent. At this order the scattering is a direct contact interaction without a virtual exchange, giving an isotropic CM angular distribution.

> [!note]
> The first source display uses $i\signal{\mathcal M}$ for the factor that still includes the momentum delta distribution. The invariant amplitude in the final equation has that distribution removed. These are different normalizations of the displayed quantity.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $g$ | $1$ | Quartic coupling | The interaction expansion parameter. |
| $\mechanical{p}_1,\mechanical{p}_2,\mechanical{p}'_1,\mechanical{p}'_2$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | External four-momenta | The initial and final scalar-particle momenta. |
| $\mechanical{z}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Vertex position | The integrated interaction coordinate. |
| $\signal{\mathcal M}$ | — | Source amplitude notation | The first display includes a momentum delta; the final stripped invariant amplitude is dimensionless. |
