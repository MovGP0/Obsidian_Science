---
title: "The Vertex in Position Space"
---

> [!TLDR]
> A quartic interaction gives a four-leg vertex with factor −ig integrated over its spacetime position.

The interaction $-g\signal{\phi}^4/4!$ gives a four-leg vertex. At position $\mechanical{z}$, its rule is
$$
(-ig)\int d^4\mechanical{z}.
$$
Permutations of the four fields in Wick's theorem cancel the factor $1/4!$.

![[QFT-07-02-Quartic-Vertex.svg|**Fig. 7.2** Four-leg vertex of φ⁴ theory. Each vertex supplies −ig integrated over its spacetime position.]]

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $g$ | $1$ | Quartic coupling | The interaction factor at the vertex. |
| $\signal{\phi}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Scalar field | The real field in the quartic interaction. |
| $\mechanical{z}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Vertex position | The spacetime point integrated for the interaction insertion. |
