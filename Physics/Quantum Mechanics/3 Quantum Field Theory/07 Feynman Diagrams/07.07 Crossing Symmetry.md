---
title: "Crossing Symmetry"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Crossing symmetry relates amplitudes with a particle moved between the incoming and outgoing states as its antiparticle.

Crossing symmetry follows from the analytic dependence of scattering amplitudes on external four-momenta.

Formally, an incoming particle of species $A$ with momentum $\mechanical{p}$ can be crossed to an outgoing antiparticle $\bar A$ with momentum $-\mechanical{p}$. Equivalently, one reverses the energy sign as part of the analytic continuation. Physical reaction regions must be reached with the appropriate continuation and external-state conventions.
