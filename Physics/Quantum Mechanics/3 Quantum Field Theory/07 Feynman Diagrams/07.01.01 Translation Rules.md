---
title: "Translation Rules"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Vertices represent interaction insertions, internal lines represent contractions, and external lines represent asymptotic particles.

The algebra-to-diagram rules begin as follows:

- A four-leg vertex in quartic scalar theory is an integrated spacetime point $\mechanical{x}$, where four lines meet. It supplies $(-ig)\int d^4\mechanical{x}$.
- An internal line joining $\mechanical{x}$ and $\mechanical{y}$ supplies $\signal{\Delta}_F(\mechanical{x}-\mechanical{y})$.

- An incoming scalar line with momentum $\mechanical{p}$ supplies $e^{i\mechanical{p}\cdot\mechanical{x}}$ in the source's convention.
- An outgoing scalar line with momentum $\mechanical{p}'$ supplies $e^{-i\mechanical{p}'\cdot\mechanical{x}}$.
- Divide by the diagram's symmetry factor $S$.

This dictionary maps Wick terms to diagrams and back. It lets one enumerate the diagrams, obtain their amplitude factors, and organize the answer by coupling order. Fermions and photons use different external-state factors.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{x},\mechanical{y}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Vertex positions | The spacetime endpoints of the internal line. |
| $g$ | $1$ | Quartic coupling | The interaction factor at a four-leg vertex. |
| $\signal{\Delta}_F$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Scalar propagator | The contraction assigned to an internal line. |
| $\mechanical{p},\mechanical{p}'$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | External four-momenta | The incoming and outgoing scalar momenta. |
