title: "Crossing Examples"

> [!TLDR]
> Crossing relates annihilation, lepton scattering, Compton scattering, and two-photon pair production through momentum and external-state substitutions.

Electron–positron annihilation into muons and electron–muon scattering are related by crossing.

Starting from $\mathrm e^-(\mechanical{p})\gamma(\mechanical{k})
\to\mathrm e^-(\mechanical{p}')\gamma(\mechanical{k}')$:

- For $\mathrm e^+(\bar{\mechanical{p}})\mathrm e^-(\mechanical{p})
  \to\gamma(\mechanical{k}_1)\gamma(\mechanical{k}_2)$, cross the outgoing electron to an incoming positron and the incoming photon to an outgoing photon. Use $\mechanical{p}'\to-\bar{\mechanical{p}}$, $\bar u(\mechanical{p}')\to\bar v(\bar{\mechanical{p}})$, $\mechanical{k}\to-\mechanical{k}_1$, and $\signal{\epsilon}(\mechanical{k})\to\signal{\epsilon}^*(\mechanical{k}_1)$. The source labels the associated invariant interchange as $\energy{s}\leftrightarrow\mechanical{t}$.
- For $\gamma\gamma\to\mathrm e^+\mathrm e^-$, the source describes simultaneous crossing of both fermionic lines and writes an $\energy{s}\leftrightarrow\mechanical{u}$ interchange.

> [!note]
> Starting directly from Compton scattering, pair production crosses the incoming electron and the outgoing photon. The source's “both fermionic lines” wording does not give that external-state map. Invariant permutations also depend on the final leg labels; specify the momentum map rather than using an isolated invariant interchange.

> [!note]
> Panel (b) is retained as printed, but it is not a valid tree-level QED annihilation diagram. Its upper vertex has three fermion lines, and its lower vertex has two photon lines and one fermion line. A QED vertex must have two fermion legs and one photon leg. The correctly crossed annihilation diagram attaches the incoming electron and positron to different vertices, with one photon at each.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{p},\mechanical{p}',\bar{\mechanical{p}}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Fermion momenta | The electron and positron momenta in the crossed reactions. |
| $\mechanical{k},\mechanical{k}',\mechanical{k}_1,\mechanical{k}_2$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Photon momenta | The momenta before and after crossing. |
| $u,\bar u,v,\bar v$ | $\unit{MeV^{1/2}}$ | Fermion spinors | The external factors exchanged by crossing. |
| $\signal{\epsilon}$ | $1$ | Photon polarization | The vector conjugated when an incoming photon becomes outgoing. |
| $\energy{s},\mechanical{t},\mechanical{u}$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Kinematic invariants | The collision and momentum-transfer invariants whose labels change under crossing. |
