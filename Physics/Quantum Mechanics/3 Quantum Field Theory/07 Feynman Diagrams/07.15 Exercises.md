> [!TLDR]
> These exercises test scalar symmetry factors, propagator asymptotics, gamma traces, and QED scattering rules.

## Exercise 7.1 — Quartic-theory symmetry factors

Calculate the symmetry factors for:

(a) The first-order figure-eight vacuum diagram with two loops.

(b) The source's second-order “trampoline” for two-to-two scattering, described as two vertices connected by three propagators.

(c) The one-loop s-channel bubble for two-to-two scattering.

> [!note]
> Item (b) has a leg-count mismatch. Two quartic vertices connected by three internal propagators leave two external legs, not four. That topology is a two-point setting-sun graph. A two-to-two quartic bubble has two connecting propagators.

## Exercise 7.2 — Position-space scalar propagator

The source asks you to show that, in four Euclidean dimensions at large separation,
$$
\signal{\Delta}_F(\mechanical{x}-\mechanical{y})
\sim\frac{e^{-\mechanical{m}\lvert\mechanical{x}-\mechanical{y}\rvert}}
{4\pi\lvert\mechanical{x}-\mechanical{y}\rvert}.
$$
Interpret the result physically.

> [!note]
> The printed $1/r$ prefactor is the three-dimensional static Yukawa form and has the wrong dimension for a four-dimensional scalar propagator. The massive four-dimensional Euclidean large-distance behavior has an $r^{-3/2}$ prefactor, with the corresponding mass-dependent coefficient. The exponential decay remains the common feature.

## Exercise 7.3 — Gamma-matrix traces

Verify the following identities by direct use of the Clifford algebra.

Use $\{\gamma^\mu,\gamma^\nu\}=2g^{\mu\nu}$ to verify:

(a)
$$
\operatorname{Tr}[\gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu\gamma^\rho\gamma^\sigma]
=4(g^{\mu\nu}g^{\rho\sigma}-g^{\mu\rho}g^{\nu\sigma}
+g^{\mu\sigma}g^{\nu\rho}).
$$

(b) $\gamma^\mu\not a\gamma_\mu=-2\not a$ in four dimensions.

(c) $\gamma^\mu\not a\not b\gamma_\mu=4(a\cdot b)$.

The vectors $a,b$ are arbitrary algebraic arguments.

## Exercise 7.4 — Compton cross section in the Thomson limit

Starting from (7.8), show that for $\energy{\omega}\ll\mechanical{m}_e$,
$$
\left.\frac{d\mechanical{\sigma}}{d\mechanical{\Omega}}\right|_{\mathrm{Thomson}}
=\frac{\electric{\alpha}^{\,2}}{\mechanical{m}_e^2}
\frac{1+\cos^2\mechanical{\theta}}2.
$$
Integrate over solid angle to obtain
$$
\mechanical{\sigma}_T=\frac{8\pi\electric{\alpha}^{\,2}}{3\mechanical{m}_e^2}.
$$

## Exercise 7.7 — Schwinger magnetic moment

At vanishing transfer, $\mechanical{q}\to0$, the one-loop vertex correction gives $\magnetic{F}_2(0)=\electric{\alpha}/(2\pi)$. Interpret this with the Gordon decomposition:
$$
\bar u(\mechanical{p}')\gamma^\mu u(\mechanical{p})
=\bar u(\mechanical{p}')
\left[
\frac{(\mechanical{p}+\mechanical{p}')^\mu}{2\mechanical{m}}
+\frac{i\sigma^{\mu\nu}\mechanical{q}_\nu}{2\mechanical{m}}
\right]u(\mechanical{p}).
$$
The mass in this decomposition is the fermion mass.

Interpret $\magnetic{F}_2(0)$ as a correction to the electron's magnetic $\magnetic{g}$ factor and derive
$$
\magnetic{g}=2\left(1+\frac{\electric{\alpha}}{2\pi}+\cdots\right).
$$

## Exercise 7.8 — Amplitude for electron–muon scattering

Consider $\mathrm e^-\mu^-\to\mathrm e^-\mu^-$.

(a) Draw all tree diagrams. How many diagrams are there, and why?

(b) Express the amplitude $\signal{\mathcal M}$ in terms of the Mandelstam variables $\energy{s},\mechanical{t},\mechanical{u}$.

(c) Calculate $\overline{|\signal{\mathcal M}|^2}$ in the ultrarelativistic limit $\sqrt{\energy{s}}\gg\mechanical{m}_e,\mechanical{m}_\mu$. Compare the result with Møller scattering.

## Exercise 7.9 — Tadpole diagram in φ⁴ theory

The source describes a tadpole as an external line attached to a vertex from which a closed loop also starts. Calculate its one-loop contribution to the renormalized mass of the field $\signal{\phi}$. Show that the ultraviolet divergence is quadratic.

> [!note]
> A quartic tadpole self-energy diagram has two external legs and one closed internal line at the vertex. The source description mentions only one external line.

## Exercise 7.10 — Power counting in φ⁴ theory

In four dimensions, the superficial degree of divergence of a one-particle-irreducible diagram with $E$ external legs and $L$ loops is $D=4-E$.

Verify this formula for $E=2$ (propagator), $E=4$ (scattering amplitude), and $E=6$ (six-point Green function). Conclude that the theory has only a finite number of divergent structures, which makes it renormalizable.
