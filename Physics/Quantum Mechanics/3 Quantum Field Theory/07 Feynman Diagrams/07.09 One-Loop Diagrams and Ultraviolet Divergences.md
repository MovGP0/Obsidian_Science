title: "One-Loop Diagrams and Ultraviolet Divergences"

> [!TLDR]
> Loop corrections introduce momentum integrals whose ultraviolet behavior must be regulated and renormalized.

Tree diagrams have no internal loops and supply the leading powers of $\electric{\alpha}$. They do not contain ultraviolet loop integrals. Higher perturbative orders introduce closed internal loops and the associated momentum integrations.
