---
title: "Momentum-Space Feynman Rules"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Momentum-space rules replace vertex-position integrals with momentum-conservation constraints and leave one integral for each independent loop momentum.

Integrating each internal vertex position gives a four-momentum delta distribution. After using these constraints, one overall delta distribution remains for total momentum conservation. Internal line momenta remain as variables constrained at each vertex.
