---
title: "φφ → φφ Scattering at One Loop"
---

> [!TLDR]
> At second order in the quartic coupling, two-to-two scalar scattering has one-loop bubble diagrams in the s, t, and u channels.

At order $g^2$, the first one-loop scattering diagrams are bubbles in the three channels. Each bubble has two internal propagators. Consider the s-channel diagram:

![[QFT-07-04-Scalar-s-Channel-Bubble.svg|**Fig. 7.4** One-loop s-channel bubble for scalar two-to-two scattering at second order in g. The internal momenta are k and k + q, with q equal to the total incoming momentum.]]

The total incoming four-momentum is $\mechanical{q}=\mechanical{p}_1+\mechanical{p}_2$.

The bubble symmetry factor is $S=2$. Its contribution is
$$
i\signal{\mathcal M}_{\mathrm{bubble}}
=\frac{(-ig)^2}{2}
\int\frac{d^4\mechanical{k}}{(2\pi)^4}
\frac{i}{\mechanical{k}^{\,2}-\mechanical{m}^2+i\epsilon}
\frac{i}{(\mechanical{k}+\mechanical{q})^2-\mechanical{m}^2+i\epsilon}.
$$
The total channel momentum is $\mechanical{q}^\mu=\mechanical{p}_1^\mu+\mechanical{p}_2^\mu$. The integral is logarithmically divergent in the ultraviolet. The renormalization chapter treats this divergence.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $g$ | $1$ | Quartic coupling | The expansion parameter for the one-loop term. |
| $\mechanical{k},\mechanical{q}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Loop and channel momenta | The internal integration momentum and total incoming momentum. |
| $\mechanical{p}_1,\mechanical{p}_2$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Incoming momenta | The two external initial-state four-momenta. |
| $\signal{\mathcal M}_{\mathrm{bubble}}$ | $1$ | Bubble amplitude | The invariant one-loop scalar scattering contribution. |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Scalar mass | The mass in both internal propagators. |
