> [!TLDR]
> The tree-level Z-boson decay width follows from its vector and axial fermion couplings, the spin-summed amplitude, and two-body phase space.

## Example 13.7 — Z decay into a fermion–antifermion pair

Calculate the tree-level width $\energy{\Gamma}(Z\to f\bar f)$.

## Z–fermion vertex

Expand the projector $P_L=(1-\gamma^5)/2$ in (13.6).
The interaction with a general fermion is
$$
\energy{\mathcal L}_{Zff}
=\frac{\signal{g}}{2\cos\signal{\theta}_W}
\signal{Z}_\mu\bar{\signal{f}}\gamma^\mu
\bigl(\signal{g}_V^f-\signal{g}_A^f\gamma^5\bigr)\signal{f},
$$
where the vector and axial couplings are
$$
\signal{g}_V^f=T_f^3-2\electric{Q}_f\sin^2\signal{\theta}_W,
\qquad
\signal{g}_A^f=T_f^3.
$$
Here $T_f^3$ is the fermion's weak-isospin eigenvalue and $\electric{Q}_f$ is its electric charge in units of the positron charge.

For an electron,
$$
\signal{g}_V^e=-\frac12+2\sin^2\signal{\theta}_W\approx-0.04,
\qquad
\signal{g}_A^e=-\frac12.
$$
For a neutrino,
$$
\signal{g}_V^\nu=\frac12,
\qquad
\signal{g}_A^\nu=\frac12.
$$

## Squared amplitude

For $Z(\mechanical{q})\to f(\mechanical{p}_1)\bar f(\mechanical{p}_2)$, the invariant amplitude is
$$
\signal{\mathcal M}
=\frac{\signal{g}}{2\cos\signal{\theta}_W}
\signal{\varepsilon}_\mu^*(\mechanical{q})\,
\bar u(\mechanical{p}_1)\gamma^\mu
\bigl(\signal{g}_V^f-\signal{g}_A^f\gamma^5\bigr)v(\mechanical{p}_2).
$$
Sum over polarizations and final spins, using
$$
\sum_\lambda
\signal{\varepsilon}_\mu^{(\lambda)*}
\signal{\varepsilon}_\nu^{(\lambda)}
=-g_{\mu\nu}+\frac{\mechanical{q}_\mu\mechanical{q}_\nu}{\mechanical{m}_Z^2}.
$$
The source drops the longitudinal term by current conservation in the massless-fermion approximation.

In the limit $\mechanical{m}_f\ll\mechanical{m}_Z$,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\overline{\lvert\signal{\mathcal M}\rvert^2}
&=\frac{\signal{g}^2}{4\cos^2\signal{\theta}_W}
(-g^{\mu\nu})\frac13
\operatorname{Tr}\!\left[
\not{\mechanical{p}}_1\gamma_\mu
(\signal{g}_V^f-\signal{g}_A^f\gamma^5)
\not{\mechanical{p}}_2\gamma_\nu
(\signal{g}_V^f-\signal{g}_A^f\gamma^5)
\right]\\
&=\frac{\signal{g}^2\mechanical{m}_Z^2}{3\cos^2\signal{\theta}_W}
\left[(\signal{g}_V^f)^2+(\signal{g}_A^f)^2\right].
\end{aligned}
$$
The factor $1/3$ averages over the three initial Z-boson polarizations.
For quarks, include the color multiplicity $N_c=3$; for leptons, $N_c=1$.

## Decay width

Use the general rest-frame two-body decay formula from Chapter 7.
For $\mechanical{m}_f\ll\mechanical{m}_Z$, the three-momentum magnitude is
$\lvert\mechanical{\mathbf p}_1\rvert\simeq\mechanical{m}_Z/2$. Thus
$$
\energy{\Gamma}(Z\to f\bar f)
=\frac{\lvert\mechanical{\mathbf p}_1\rvert}{8\pi\mechanical{m}_Z^2}
\,N_c\,\overline{\lvert\signal{\mathcal M}\rvert^2}
=\frac{\signal{g}^2\mechanical{m}_Z N_c}{48\pi\cos^2\signal{\theta}_W}
\left[(\signal{g}_V^f)^2+(\signal{g}_A^f)^2\right].
$$
Rewrite this with
$$
\frac{\signal{G}_F}{\sqrt2}
=\frac{\signal{g}^2}{8\mechanical{m}_W^2},
\qquad
\mechanical{m}_W=\mechanical{m}_Z\cos\signal{\theta}_W.
$$
The result is
$$
\boxed{
\energy{\Gamma}(Z\to f\bar f)
=\frac{\sqrt2\,\signal{G}_F\mechanical{m}_Z^3}{12\pi}
N_c\left[(\signal{g}_V^f)^2+(\signal{g}_A^f)^2\right].
}
\tag{13.8}
$$

> [!note]
> The trace expression above is for one color. The source inserts $N_c$ in the final width; it is shown explicitly in the phase-space expression here. The result neglects fermion masses and radiative corrections.

## Numerical check

For a neutrino pair, $N_c=1$ and
$(\signal{g}_V^\nu)^2+(\signal{g}_A^\nu)^2=1/4+1/4=1/2$.
Therefore
$$
\energy{\Gamma}(Z\to\nu\bar\nu)
=\frac{\sqrt2\,\signal{G}_F\mechanical{m}_Z^3}{24\pi}
\approx166\,\unit{MeV}.
$$
For three neutrino families,
$$
\energy{\Gamma}_{\mathrm{inv}}
=3\times166\,\unit{MeV}
\approx498\,\unit{MeV}.
$$
The source quotes the experimental value
$$
\energy{\Gamma}_{\mathrm{inv}}^{\mathrm{exp}}
=499.0\pm1.5\,\unit{MeV}.
$$

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\energy{\Gamma}$ | $\unit{GeV}$ | Z-boson decay width | The partial width for a selected fermion–antifermion channel. |
| $\energy{\mathcal L}_{Zff}$ | $\unit{MeV^4}$ | Neutral-current Lagrangian density | The interaction of a Z boson with a fermion pair. |
| $\signal{g},\signal{g}_V^f,\signal{g}_A^f$ | $1$ | Weak interaction couplings | The gauge coupling and the vector and axial coefficients. |
| $\signal{\theta}_W$ | $1$ | Weak mixing angle | The angle in the neutral-current coupling. |
| $\signal{Z}_\mu$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Z-boson field | The massive neutral weak gauge field. |
| $\signal{f}$ | $\unit{MeV^{3/2}}$ | Fermion field | The field for the chosen decay product. |
| $\electric{Q}_f$ | $1$ | Fermion electric charge | The charge in units of the positron charge. |
| $\mechanical{q},\mechanical{p}_1,\mechanical{p}_2$ | $\unit{GeV}$ | External four-momenta | The Z-boson, fermion, and antifermion momenta. |
| $\mechanical{m}_Z$ | $\unit{GeV}$ | Z-boson mass | The mass in the polarization sum. |
| $\signal{\varepsilon}_\mu$ | $1$ | Polarization vector | The vector for a physical Z-boson spin state. |
| $\signal{\mathcal M}$ | $\unit{GeV}$ | Decay amplitude | The invariant one-to-two amplitude with relativistic state normalization. |
| $\mechanical{m}_f,\mechanical{m}_W$ | $\unit{GeV}$ | Fermion and W-boson masses | The small final-state mass and the charged weak-boson mass. |
| $\signal{G}_F$ | $\unit{GeV^{-2}}$ | Fermi constant | The low-energy weak coupling used to express the decay width. |
| $\energy{\Gamma}_{\mathrm{inv}}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Invisible Z-boson width | The sum of the three light-neutrino partial widths. |
