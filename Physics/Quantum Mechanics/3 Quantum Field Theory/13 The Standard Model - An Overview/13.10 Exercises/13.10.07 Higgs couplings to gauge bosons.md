## Exercise 13.7

The couplings of the physical Higgs boson $\signal{h}$ to gauge bosons and fermions follow directly from the Lagrangian density.

Read these couplings after spontaneous symmetry breaking.

1. **(a)** Expand $(D_\mu\signal{H})^\dagger D^\mu\signal{H}$ about the vacuum expectation value in unitary gauge. Identify the $hW^+W^-$, $hZZ$, $hhW^+W^-$, and $hhZZ$ interactions.
2. **(b)** Show that the vertex-coupling ratio is $\signal{g}_{hVV}/\signal{g}_{hhVV}=\signal{v}$.
3. **(c)** Calculate the tree-level width $\energy{\Gamma}(h\to W^+W^-)$ for $\mechanical{m}_h>2\mechanical{m}_W$. Sum over the physical W-boson polarizations.

> [!note]
> The source asks for an expansion to third order in the fields, but the listed $hhVV$ interactions are fourth order in the total field count. Retain fourth-order terms to identify them. The ratio in part (b) uses Feynman vertex couplings, including factors for identical Higgs fields. It is not the ratio of the raw Lagrangian coefficients.

## Solution 13.7 — Higgs couplings to gauge bosons

### (a) Expansion of the scalar kinetic term

In unitary gauge,
$$
\signal{H}=\frac1{\sqrt2}
\begin{pmatrix}0\\\signal{v}+\signal{h}\end{pmatrix}.
$$
The kinetic term gives
$$
(D_\mu\signal{H})^\dagger D^\mu\signal{H}
=\frac12(\partial_\mu\signal{h})^2
+\frac{(\signal{v}+\signal{h})^2}{4}
\left[
\signal{g}^2\signal{W}_\mu^+\signal{W}^{-\mu}
+\frac{\signal{g}^2+\signal{g}'^2}{2}\signal{Z}_\mu\signal{Z}^\mu
\right].
$$

Expand $(\signal{v}+\signal{h})^2=\signal{v}^2+2\signal{v}\signal{h}+\signal{h}^2$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\energy{\mathcal L}\supset{}&
\frac14\signal{g}^2\signal{v}^2\signal{W}^+\cdot\signal{W}^-
+\frac12\signal{g}^2\signal{v}\signal{h}\signal{W}^+\cdot\signal{W}^-
+\frac14\signal{g}^2\signal{h}^2\signal{W}^+\cdot\signal{W}^-\\
&+\frac18(\signal{g}^2+\signal{g}'^2)\signal{v}^2\signal{Z}^2
+\frac14(\signal{g}^2+\signal{g}'^2)\signal{v}\signal{h}\signal{Z}^2
+\frac18(\signal{g}^2+\signal{g}'^2)\signal{h}^2\signal{Z}^2.
\end{aligned}
$$
The vertex factors include the identical-field differentiation factors:

| Vertex | Feynman rule |
| --- | --- |
| $hW^+W^-$ | $i(2\mechanical{m}_W^2/\signal{v})g_{\mu\nu}$ |
| $hZZ$ | $i(2\mechanical{m}_Z^2/\signal{v})g_{\mu\nu}$ |
| $hhW^+W^-$ | $i(2\mechanical{m}_W^2/\signal{v}^2)g_{\mu\nu}$ |
| $hhZZ$ | $i(2\mechanical{m}_Z^2/\signal{v}^2)g_{\mu\nu}$ |

For $V=W,Z$, the compact form is
$$
\signal{g}_{hVV}=\frac{2\mechanical{m}_V^2}{\signal{v}},
\qquad
\signal{g}_{hhVV}=\frac{2\mechanical{m}_V^2}{\signal{v}^2}.
$$
The identical-$Z$ factor is two; the double-Higgs rules also include the factor from the two identical Higgs fields.

### (b) Coupling ratio

The ratio is
$$
\frac{\signal{g}_{hhVV}}{\signal{g}_{hVV}}=\frac1{\signal{v}}.
$$
At tree level the doublet kinetic term fixes both couplings through the same vacuum scale. Their measurement tests this structure. The source cites single- and multiple-Higgs vector-boson processes as probes; extracting either coupling requires the complete amplitude for the chosen process.

### (c) Higgs decay into two on-shell W bosons

For $\mechanical{m}_h>2\mechanical{m}_W$, sum physical W polarizations using
$\sum\signal{\varepsilon}_\mu\signal{\varepsilon}_\nu^*
=-g_{\mu\nu}+\mechanical{p}_\mu\mechanical{p}_\nu/\mechanical{m}_W^2$.
Since $2\mechanical{p}_1\cdot\mechanical{p}_2
=\mechanical{m}_h^2-2\mechanical{m}_W^2$,
$$
\sum\lvert\signal{\mathcal M}_{hWW}\rvert^2
=\left(\frac{2\mechanical{m}_W^2}{\signal{v}}\right)^2
\left[
2+\frac{(\mechanical{m}_h^2-2\mechanical{m}_W^2)^2}{4\mechanical{m}_W^4}
\right].
$$

> [!note] Polarization factor
> The supplied intermediate amplitude omits the factor four in the denominator of the second term. The contraction contains $(\mechanical{p}_1\cdot\mechanical{p}_2)^2/\mechanical{m}_W^4$, which supplies that factor. The boxed width in the source agrees with the corrected amplitude.

With $\beta=\sqrt{1-4\mechanical{m}_W^2/\mechanical{m}_h^2}$, the width is
$$
\boxed{
\energy{\Gamma}(h\to W^+W^-)
=\frac{\signal{G}_F\mechanical{m}_h^3}{8\pi\sqrt2}
\,\beta
\left[
1-\frac{4\mechanical{m}_W^2}{\mechanical{m}_h^2}
+\frac{12\mechanical{m}_W^4}{\mechanical{m}_h^4}
\right].
}
$$
This is the two-on-shell-vector result; it does not describe a below-threshold decay with an off-shell W.

For the source values $\mechanical{m}_h=125\,\unit{GeV}$ and
$\mechanical{m}_W=80.4\,\unit{GeV}$, the two-on-shell-W channel is closed. The decay proceeds through an off-shell W, schematically
$h\to WW^*\to Wf\bar f'$, followed by the W decays. The source quotes a branching fraction of about $22\%$ and calls this the second-largest Higgs decay channel; these are supplied phenomenological benchmarks.
