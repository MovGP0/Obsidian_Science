title: "Precision Tests"

> [!TLDR]
> Precision measurements test Standard Model radiative corrections in addition to the direct discovery of its particles.

Beyond particle discoveries, the Standard Model has passed many stringent precision tests.

## Z pole

LEP experiments ALEPH, DELPHI, L3, and OPAL, together with SLD, tested the $Z$ resonance precisely.
The source describes the width measurement as having a precision of “a few parts per million.”
It obtains the total invisible width by summing the channels
$Z\to\nu_i\bar\nu_i$ for $N_\nu$ light-neutrino families:
$$
\energy{\Gamma}_{\mathrm{inv}}
=N_\nu\energy{\Gamma}(Z\to\nu\bar\nu)
=N_\nu\frac{\signal{G}_F\mechanical{m}_Z^3}{12\pi\sqrt2}.
$$
Comparing with the experimental invisible width gives the source value
$$
N_\nu=2.984\pm0.008,
$$
consistent with three light active-neutrino families.

> [!note]
> These numerical and precision statements reproduce the source's summary. Different $Z$-pole observables have different precision; the quoted phrase should not be read as applying uniformly to every resonance parameter. The invisible-width count concerns neutrinos light enough for $Z$ decay that have the assumed Standard Model coupling.

## Muon anomalous magnetic moment

The muon anomaly is
$$
\magnetic{a}_\mu=\frac{\magnetic{g}_\mu-2}{2}.
$$
The Standard Model calculation includes QED, QCD, and electroweak contributions.
The source quotes
$$
\magnetic{a}_\mu^{\mathrm{SM}}
=116\,591\,810(43)\times10^{-11}.
$$
This is the source's theory benchmark; it is retained here rather than presented as an updated precision result.

The source's 2023 experimental summary gives
$$
\magnetic{a}_\mu^{\mathrm{exp}}
=116\,592\,059(22)\times10^{-11}.
$$
Compared with its preceding theory benchmark, it describes a discrepancy of about $5\sigma$ and discusses this as a possible indication of physics beyond the Standard Model.

> [!note]
> This comparison is the dated benchmark supplied by the source. Its significance depends on the experimental combination and the treatment of the hadronic Standard Model contribution. It is not presented here as a current-data assessment.

## Electroweak precision observables

Radiative corrections to gauge-boson masses and couplings have important terms quadratic in the top-quark mass and logarithmic in the Higgs mass.
The source summarizes pre-discovery LEP and SLD fits by
$$
\mechanical{m}_t\approx175\,\unit{GeV},
\qquad
\mechanical{m}_h\lesssim200\,\unit{GeV}.
$$
The later discoveries were compatible with these indirect expectations.
