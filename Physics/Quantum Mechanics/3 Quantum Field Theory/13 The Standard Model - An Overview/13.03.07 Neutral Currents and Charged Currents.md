---
title: "Neutral Currents and Charged Currents"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Charged weak currents change fermion flavor through W exchange. Neutral weak currents couple through Z exchange and include both vector and axial components.

The Standard Model has charged and neutral weak currents.

## Charged currents

The $W^\pm$ bosons mediate charged-current interactions and change fermion flavor:
$$
\energy{\mathcal{L}}_{\mathrm{CC}}
=\frac{\signal{g}}{\sqrt2}
\left(
\signal{W}_\mu^+\bar{\signal{\nu}}_{eL}\gamma^\mu\signal{e}_L
+\signal{W}_\mu^+\bar{\signal{u}}_L\gamma^\mu
\signal{V}_{\mathrm{CKM}}\signal{d}_L
+\mathrm{h.c.}
\right).
$$
These interactions violate parity because they couple only to left-chiral fermion fields.
They also change flavor; see Fig. 13.3.

## Neutral currents

Photon and $Z$ exchange preserve flavor at tree level, but their chiral couplings differ:
$$
\energy{\mathcal{L}}_{\mathrm{NC}}
=\electric{e}\electric{A}^\mu\electric{J}_\mu^{\mathrm{em}}
+\frac{\signal{g}}{\cos\signal{\theta}_W}\signal{Z}^\mu\signal{J}_\mu^Z,
$$
where
$$
\begin{aligned}
\electric{J}_\mu^{\mathrm{em}}
&=\sum_f\electric{Q}_f\,\bar{\signal{f}}\gamma_\mu\signal{f},\\
\signal{J}_\mu^Z
&=\sum_f\bar{\signal{f}}\gamma_\mu
\left(T_f^3P_L-\electric{Q}_f\sin^2\signal{\theta}_W\right)\signal{f},
\end{aligned}
\tag{13.6}
$$
and $P_L=(1-\gamma^5)/2$.
The $Z$ current couples to both left- and right-chiral fermions, with different couplings.
The charged weak current couples only to left-chiral fermion fields.

> [!note]
> **Remark 13.5 from the source:** Weak neutral currents were a characteristic Standard Model prediction. The source cites their 1973 observation by Gargamelle at CERN in
> $\bar\nu_\mu+e^-\to\bar\nu_\mu+e^-$.

![[QFT-13-03-Charged-Current-Exchange.svg|**Fig. 13.3** Charged-current W⁺-exchange diagram. Particle labels and fermion arrows follow the source; see the charge-assignment note below.]]

> [!note]
> The source captions this as $\nu_\mu\mu^-\to u\bar d$. Taken as an ordinary incoming-to-outgoing reaction, that process does not conserve electric charge: the initial charge is $-1$ and the final charge is $+1$. A charge-consistent annihilation example is $\nu_\mu+\mu^+\to u+\bar d$; a charge-consistent scattering example is $\nu_\mu+d\to\mu^-+u$. The displayed source labels and arrow directions require a consistent crossing assignment before use as a physical amplitude.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\energy{\mathcal{L}}_{\mathrm{CC}}$ | $\unit{MeV^4}$ | Charged-current Lagrangian density | The lepton and quark interactions with the charged weak fields. |
| $\signal{g}$ | $1$ | Weak gauge coupling | The coupling in the charged-current interaction. |
| $\signal{W}_\mu^\pm$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Charged weak gauge fields | The fields that mediate the charged currents. |
| $\signal{\nu}_{eL},\signal{e}_L,\signal{u}_L,\signal{d}_L$ | $\unit{MeV^{3/2}}$ | Left-chiral fermion fields | The lepton fields and quark generation vectors in the charged currents. |
| $\signal{V}_{\mathrm{CKM}}$ | $1$ | CKM matrix | The generation-mixing matrix in the quark charged current. |
| $\energy{\mathcal{L}}_{\mathrm{NC}}$ | $\unit{MeV^4}$ | Neutral-current Lagrangian density | The electromagnetic and neutral weak interactions. |
| $\electric{e}$ | $1$ | Electromagnetic coupling | The coupling of the photon to electric current. |
| $\electric{A}_\mu$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Photon potential | The field mediating the electromagnetic neutral current. |
| $\signal{Z}_\mu$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Neutral weak field | The field mediating the weak neutral current. |
| $\electric{J}_\mu^{\mathrm{em}},\signal{J}_\mu^Z$ | $\unit{MeV^3}$ | Electromagnetic and weak neutral currents | The fermion bilinears coupled to the photon and Z boson. |
| $\electric{Q}_f$ | $1$ | Fermion electric-charge quantum number | The charge in units of the electromagnetic coupling. |
| $\signal{f},\bar{\signal{f}}$ | $\unit{MeV^{3/2}}$ | Fermion field and adjoint | A charged or neutral fermion field in the current sum. |
| $\signal{\theta}_W$ | $1$ | Weinberg angle | The electroweak angle in the neutral-current couplings. |
