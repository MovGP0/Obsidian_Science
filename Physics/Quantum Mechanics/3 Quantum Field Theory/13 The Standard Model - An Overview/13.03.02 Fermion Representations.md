---
title: "Fermion Representations"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Left-chiral fermions form weak doublets, while right-chiral charged fermions are weak singlets. Their hypercharges give the observed electric charges through Q_em = T³ + Y.

The Standard Model weak interactions violate parity.
Left- and right-chiral fermions therefore have different representations under
$SU(2)_L\times U(1)_Y$.

## First generation: Leptons

$$
\signal{L}=
\begin{pmatrix}\signal{\nu}_e\\\signal{e}\end{pmatrix}_L
\sim\left(1,2,-\frac{1}{2}\right),
\qquad
\signal{e}_R\sim(1,1,-1).
$$
The notation $(n_c,n_w,Y)$ gives the color representation, the dimension of the weak-isospin multiplet, and the weak hypercharge.
There is no right-handed neutrino in the minimal Standard Model.

## First generation: Quarks

$$
\signal{Q}=
\begin{pmatrix}\signal{u}\\\signal{d}\end{pmatrix}_L
\sim\left(3,2,+\frac{1}{6}\right),
\qquad
\signal{u}_R\sim\left(3,1,+\frac{2}{3}\right),
\qquad
\signal{d}_R\sim\left(3,1,-\frac{1}{3}\right).
$$
Electric charge, weak isospin, and hypercharge obey the Gell-Mann–Nishijima relation:

> [!note]
> **Gell-Mann–Nishijima formula**
> $$
> \electric{Q}_{\mathrm{em}}=T^3+\electric{Y}.
> \tag{13.3}
> $$

> [!note]
> The source states broadly that weak interactions act only on left-chiral fermions. More precisely, the charged weak currents and the $SU(2)_L$ coupling have this chiral structure. The neutral $Z$ boson also couples to right-chiral charged fermions through electroweak mixing. In (13.3), electric charge is measured in units of the positive electromagnetic coupling, and the hypercharge convention has no additional factor of one half.

The charge assignments give
$$
\begin{gathered}
\electric{Q}_{\mathrm{em}}(\signal{u}_L)
=+\frac12+\frac16=+\frac23,
\qquad
\electric{Q}_{\mathrm{em}}(\signal{d}_L)
=-\frac12+\frac16=-\frac13,\\
\electric{Q}_{\mathrm{em}}(\signal{e}_L)
=-\frac12-\frac12=-1,
\qquad
\electric{Q}_{\mathrm{em}}(\signal{\nu}_{eL})
=+\frac12-\frac12=0.
\end{gathered}
$$
The three lepton generations $(e,\nu_e)$, $(\mu,\nu_\mu)$, and $(\tau,\nu_\tau)$, and the three quark generations $(u,d)$, $(c,s)$, and $(t,b)$, have the same corresponding hypercharge assignments.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{L},\signal{Q}$ | $\unit{MeV^{3/2}}$ | Left-chiral lepton and quark multiplets | The first-generation weak doublets. |
| $\signal{\nu}_e,\signal{e},\signal{e}_R$ | $\unit{MeV^{3/2}}$ | Lepton fields | The electron-neutrino and electron chiral fields. |
| $\signal{u},\signal{d},\signal{u}_R,\signal{d}_R$ | $\unit{MeV^{3/2}}$ | Quark fields | The first-generation quark chiral fields. |
| $\electric{Q}_{\mathrm{em}}$ | $1$ | Electric-charge quantum number | The charge eigenvalue in units of the electromagnetic coupling. |
