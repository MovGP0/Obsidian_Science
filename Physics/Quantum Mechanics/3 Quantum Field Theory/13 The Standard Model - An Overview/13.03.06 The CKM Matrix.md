---
title: "The CKM Matrix"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The CKM matrix is the mismatch between left-chiral up- and down-quark rotations to mass eigenstates. For three generations it contains three mixing angles and one possible CP-violating phase.

The up- and down-quark Yukawa matrices are diagonalized separately by unitary transformations:
$$
\signal{Y}_u^{\mathrm{diag}}=V_{uL}^\dagger\signal{Y}_uV_{uR},
\qquad
\signal{Y}_d^{\mathrm{diag}}=V_{dL}^\dagger\signal{Y}_dV_{dR}.
$$
The weak charged current couples the two members of each doublet:
$$
\signal{J}_\mu^+=\bar{\signal{u}}_{Li}\gamma_\mu\signal{d}_{Li}.
$$
In the mass-eigenstate basis, the mismatch between the left-chiral rotations gives the CKM matrix:
$$
\signal{V}_{\mathrm{CKM}}=V_{uL}^\dagger V_{dL}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
\signal{V}_{ud}&\signal{V}_{us}&\signal{V}_{ub}\\
\signal{V}_{cd}&\signal{V}_{cs}&\signal{V}_{cb}\\
\signal{V}_{td}&\signal{V}_{ts}&\signal{V}_{tb}
\end{pmatrix}.
$$
The matrix is unitary,
$$
\signal{V}_{\mathrm{CKM}}^\dagger\signal{V}_{\mathrm{CKM}}=\mathbf{1}.
$$
After unphysical fermion-field phases are removed, it has four physical parameters: three mixing angles and one CP-violating phase, the **Kobayashi–Maskawa phase**.

> [!note]
> Three generations permit a physical CKM phase, but do not by themselves require nonzero CP violation. The phase and mixing parameters must give a nonzero rephasing-invariant CP measure. The source's statement that CP violation follows directly from having at least three generations is therefore too strong.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{Y}_u,\signal{Y}_d$ | $1$ | Quark Yukawa matrices | The generation-space couplings diagonalized to obtain quark masses. |
| $\signal{V}_{\mathrm{CKM}},\signal{V}_{ij}$ | $1$ | Quark mixing matrix and elements | The relative left-chiral rotation in the charged current. |
| $\signal{J}_\mu^+$ | $\unit{MeV^3}$ | Weak charged current | The quark current before rotation to the mass basis. |
| $\signal{u}_{Li},\signal{d}_{Li}$ | $\unit{MeV^{3/2}}$ | Left-chiral quark fields | The up- and down-type fields labeled by generation. |
