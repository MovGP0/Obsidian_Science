---
title: "The Complete Standard Model Lagrangian"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The Standard Model Lagrangian combines gauge dynamics, chiral fermion kinetic terms, the Higgs sector, and Yukawa interactions. Each covariant derivative uses the field's color, weak-isospin, and hypercharge representation.

The Standard Model Lagrangian density is
$$
\energy{\mathcal{L}}_{\mathrm{SM}}
=\energy{\mathcal{L}}_{\mathrm{gauge}}
+\energy{\mathcal{L}}_{\mathrm{fermions}}
+\energy{\mathcal{L}}_{\mathrm{Higgs}}
+\energy{\mathcal{L}}_{\mathrm{Yukawa}}.
$$

## Gauge sector

$$
\energy{\mathcal{L}}_{\mathrm{gauge}}
=-\frac14\signal{G}_{\mu\nu}^a\signal{G}^{a\mu\nu}
-\frac14\signal{W}_{\mu\nu}^i\signal{W}^{i\mu\nu}
-\frac14\signal{B}_{\mu\nu}\signal{B}^{\mu\nu},
$$
with $a=1,\ldots,8$ for $SU(3)_c$ and $i=1,2,3$ for $SU(2)_L$.

## Fermion kinetic sector

Sum over three generations, labeled $p=1,2,3$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\energy{\mathcal{L}}_{\mathrm{fermions}}
=\sum_{p=1}^{3}\big[
&i\bar{\signal{Q}}_p\not{D}\signal{Q}_p
+i\bar{\signal{u}}_{Rp}\not{D}\signal{u}_{Rp}
+i\bar{\signal{d}}_{Rp}\not{D}\signal{d}_{Rp}\\
&+i\bar{\signal{L}}_p\not{D}\signal{L}_p
+i\bar{\signal{e}}_{Rp}\not{D}\signal{e}_{Rp}
\big].
\end{aligned}
$$
The covariant derivative acts according to each field's representation:
$$
D_\mu\signal{Q}
=\left(
\partial_\mu-i\signal{g}_s\frac{\lambda^a}{2}\signal{G}_\mu^a
-i\signal{g}\frac{\sigma^i}{2}\signal{W}_\mu^i
-i\signal{g}'\frac16\signal{B}_\mu
\right)\signal{Q},
$$
$$
D_\mu\signal{u}_R
=\left(
\partial_\mu-i\signal{g}_s\frac{\lambda^a}{2}\signal{G}_\mu^a
-i\signal{g}'\frac23\signal{B}_\mu
\right)\signal{u}_R,
$$
$$
D_\mu\signal{d}_R
=\left(
\partial_\mu-i\signal{g}_s\frac{\lambda^a}{2}\signal{G}_\mu^a
+i\signal{g}'\frac13\signal{B}_\mu
\right)\signal{d}_R,
$$
$$
D_\mu\signal{L}
=\left(
\partial_\mu-i\signal{g}\frac{\sigma^i}{2}\signal{W}_\mu^i
+i\signal{g}'\frac12\signal{B}_\mu
\right)\signal{L},
$$
and
$$
D_\mu\signal{e}_R=(\partial_\mu+i\signal{g}'\signal{B}_\mu)\signal{e}_R.
$$

## Higgs sector

$$
\energy{\mathcal{L}}_{\mathrm{Higgs}}
=(D_\mu\signal{H})^\dagger(D^\mu\signal{H})
+\energy{\mu}^2\signal{H}^\dagger\signal{H}
-\signal{\lambda}(\signal{H}^\dagger\signal{H})^2,
$$
with
$$
D_\mu\signal{H}
=\left(
\partial_\mu-i\signal{g}\frac{\sigma^i}{2}\signal{W}_\mu^i
-i\signal{g}'\frac12\signal{B}_\mu
\right)\signal{H}.
$$

## Yukawa sector

$$
\begin{aligned}
\energy{\mathcal{L}}_{\mathrm{Yukawa}}
=-\big[
&\signal{Y}_{pq}^e\bar{\signal{L}}_p\signal{H}\signal{e}_{Rq}
+\signal{Y}_{pq}^d\bar{\signal{Q}}_p\signal{H}\signal{d}_{Rq}\\
&+\signal{Y}_{pq}^u\bar{\signal{Q}}_p\tilde{\signal{H}}\signal{u}_{Rq}
+\mathrm{h.c.}
\big].
\end{aligned}
$$
The coefficients $\signal{Y}_{pq}^{e,d,u}$ are arbitrary complex $3\times3$ matrices in generation space.

## QCD theta term

Gauge symmetry also permits a topological term.
The source writes
$$
\energy{\mathcal{L}}_\theta
=\frac{\signal{\theta}_{\mathrm{QCD}}}{32\pi^2}
\signal{G}_{\mu\nu}^a\tilde{\signal{G}}^{a\mu\nu},
$$
where
$$
\tilde{\signal{G}}^{a\mu\nu}
=\frac12\varepsilon^{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}\signal{G}_{\rho\sigma}^a.
$$
This term can violate CP through nonperturbative QCD effects.
The source quotes
$$
\lvert\signal{\theta}_{\mathrm{QCD}}\rvert\lesssim10^{-10}.
$$
Explaining such a small strong-CP parameter is the **strong CP problem**, which is not resolved by the minimal Standard Model.

> [!note]
> With the canonical gluon-field normalization used above, the coefficient of the topological density contains $\signal{g}_s^2/(32\pi^2)$. The source's coefficient instead corresponds to a convention with the coupling absorbed into the field strength. The experimentally constrained physical angle also includes the phase of the quark-mass determinant; it is not an independently measurable bare theta coefficient.

## Parameter count

Without neutrino-mass parameters, the quark and charged-lepton Standard Model has 19 free parameters:

- Three gauge couplings, $\signal{g}_s,\signal{g},\signal{g}'$.
- Two Higgs-potential parameters, $\energy{\mu}^2,\signal{\lambda}$.
- Six quark masses.
- Three charged-lepton masses.
- Three CKM mixing angles.
- One CKM CP-violating phase.
- One strong-CP angle.
