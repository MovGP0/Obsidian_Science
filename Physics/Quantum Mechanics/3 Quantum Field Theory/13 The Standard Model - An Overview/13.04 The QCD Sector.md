title: "The QCD Sector"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The QCD sector is the SU(3)_c gauge theory of quarks and gluons. It describes the strong interaction within the Standard Model.

Quantum chromodynamics is the theory of the strong interaction. It uses the color gauge group $SU(3)_c$.

The subscript $c$ denotes color.
Chapter 12 developed the general theory; this section summarizes its role in the Standard Model.

Quarks carry color and transform in the fundamental representation $\mathbf{3}$ of $SU(3)_c$.
Gluons transform in the adjoint representation $\mathbf{8}$ and therefore interact with one another.
The QCD Lagrangian density is
$$
\energy{\mathcal{L}}_{\mathrm{QCD}}
=-\frac14\signal{G}_{\mu\nu}^a\signal{G}^{a\mu\nu}
+\sum_f\bar{\signal{q}}_f(i\not{D}-\mechanical{m}_f)\signal{q}_f,
$$
where
$$
\signal{G}_{\mu\nu}^a
=\partial_\mu\signal{G}_\nu^a-\partial_\nu\signal{G}_\mu^a
+\signal{g}_sf^{abc}\signal{G}_\mu^b\signal{G}_\nu^c.
$$
The $f^{abc}$ are the $SU(3)$ structure constants, and $\signal{g}_s$ is the strong coupling.
The quark covariant derivative is
$$
D_\mu\signal{q}
=(\partial_\mu-i\signal{g}_sT^a\signal{G}_\mu^a)\signal{q},
\qquad T^a=\frac{\lambda^a}{2},
$$
with $\lambda^a$ the Gell-Mann matrices.

QCD has three central properties:

- **Asymptotic freedom.** The coupling
  $\signal{\alpha}_s=\signal{g}_s^2/(4\pi)$ decreases at high energy because the beta function is negative. This permits perturbative calculations. Politzer, Gross, and Wilczek discovered the effect in 1973 and received the 2004 Nobel Prize.
- **Confinement.** Quarks and gluons are not observed as isolated low-energy particles; they form color-singlet hadrons. The effect is nonperturbative. The source notes the lack of a rigorous proof.
- **Chiral symmetry breaking.** At low energy, QCD spontaneously breaks the approximate light-quark symmetry
  $SU(N_f)_L\times SU(N_f)_R$. Pions, and in the three-light-flavor description kaons and the octet eta, are the associated pseudoscalar modes.

> [!note]
> Nonzero quark masses explicitly break the chiral symmetry, so the observed pseudoscalar mesons are pseudo-Goldstone bosons rather than exactly massless Goldstone bosons. The approximate flavor group is chosen for the light quarks relevant to the energy range.
