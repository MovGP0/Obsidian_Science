---
title: "Global and Local Gauge Invariance"
---

> [!TLDR]
> A constant phase rotation leaves the free Dirac Lagrangian invariant and gives a conserved current; local phase rotations require an additional field.

The starting point is the free Dirac Lagrangian studied in Chapter 4:
$$
\energy{\mathcal L}_{\mathrm{Dirac}}
=\bar{\signal{\psi}}(i\not\partial-\mechanical{m})\signal{\psi}.
$$

This density is invariant under the global phase transformation
$$
\signal{\psi}(\mechanical{x})\to e^{-i\electric{e}\signal{\alpha}}\signal{\psi}(\mechanical{x}),
\qquad
\bar{\signal{\psi}}(\mechanical{x})\to e^{+i\electric{e}\signal{\alpha}}\bar{\signal{\psi}}(\mechanical{x}),
$$
where $\signal{\alpha}$ is constant and $\electric{e}$ is the electromagnetic coupling. The source uses $\electric{e}>0$ for the magnitude of the fundamental charge. It identifies the electron's physical charge as $\electric{Q}_e=-|\electric{e}|$ and states that this sign follows from the quantization and identification of $\signal{\psi}$ as the electron field. Positron interactions with an external field have the opposite sign.

Noether's theorem gives conservation of
$$
\signal{j}^\mu=\bar{\signal{\psi}}\gamma^\mu\signal{\psi}.
$$
Multiplication by the charge gives the electromagnetic current.

Can this global symmetry become a local symmetry, with a spacetime-dependent parameter $\signal{\alpha}=\signal{\alpha}(\mechanical{x})$? The required transformation is
$$
\signal{\psi}(\mechanical{x})\to
e^{-i\electric{e}\signal{\alpha}(\mechanical{x})}\signal{\psi}(\mechanical{x}).
$$

The mass term $\mechanical{m}\bar{\signal{\psi}}\signal{\psi}$ is invariant, but the kinetic term transforms as
$$
\bar{\signal{\psi}}\,i\not\partial\,\signal{\psi}
\to
\bar{\signal{\psi}}\,i\not\partial\,\signal{\psi}
+\electric{e}\bar{\signal{\psi}}\gamma^\mu\signal{\psi}\,\partial_\mu\signal{\alpha}(\mechanical{x}).
$$
An extra term proportional to $\partial_\mu\signal{\alpha}$ appears. The ordinary derivative must be modified to restore invariance.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\energy{\mathcal L}_{\mathrm{Dirac}}$ | $\unit{MeV^4}$ | Lagrangian density | The free Dirac density in natural units. |
| $\signal{\psi},\bar{\signal{\psi}}$ | $\unit{MeV^{3/2}}$ | Dirac fields | The fermion field and its adjoint. |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Fermion mass | The mass in the free Dirac equation. |
| $\mechanical{x}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Spacetime coordinate | The argument of the fields and local phase. |
| $\electric{e},\electric{Q}_e$ | $1$ | Electric charge parameters | The positive coupling magnitude and physical electron charge. |
| $\signal{\alpha}$ | $1$ | Gauge parameter | The constant or local phase parameter. |
| $\signal{j}^\mu$ | $\unit{MeV^3}$ | Conserved number current | The Dirac current before multiplication by charge. |
