---
title: "Calculation of the Squared Amplitude"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Spinor completeness converts the unpolarized annihilation probability into a contraction of electron and muon traces.

To obtain the unpolarized cross section, average over initial spins $s,s'$ and sum over final spins $r,r'$:
$$
\overline{|\signal{\mathcal M}|^2}
=\frac12\frac12\sum_{s,s',r,r'}|\signal{\mathcal M}|^2.
$$
The two factors $1/2$ account for the two spin states of the incoming electron and positron.

The amplitude factorizes into electron and muon sectors:
$$
\overline{|\signal{\mathcal M}|^2}
=\frac{\electric{e}^{\,4}}{\mechanical{q}^{\,4}}
\signal{L}^{(e)}_{\mu\nu}\signal{L}^{(m)\mu\nu}.
$$
The electron leptonic tensor is
$$
\signal{L}^{(e)}_{\mu\nu}
=\frac14\sum_{s,s'}
\left[\bar v^{s'}(\bar{\mechanical{p}})\gamma_\mu u^s(\mechanical{p})\right]
\left[\bar v^{s'}(\bar{\mechanical{p}})\gamma_\nu u^s(\mechanical{p})\right]^*.
$$
The muon sector uses the spins $r,r'$ and mass $\mechanical{m}_\mu$.

## Calculation of the electron tensor

For arbitrary spinor arguments, use
$$
[\bar\psi\Gamma\chi]^*
=\bar\chi\,\overline{\Gamma}\psi,
\qquad
\overline{\Gamma}=\gamma^0\Gamma^\dagger\gamma^0.
$$
For $\Gamma=\gamma^\nu$, one has $\overline{\gamma^\nu}=\gamma^\nu$. Thus
$$
\left[\bar v^{s'}(\bar{\mechanical{p}})\gamma_\nu u^s(\mechanical{p})\right]^*
=\bar u^s(\mechanical{p})\gamma_\nu v^{s'}(\bar{\mechanical{p}}).
$$

Then
$$
\begin{aligned}
\signal{L}^{(e)}_{\mu\nu}
&=\frac14\sum_{s,s'}
[\bar v^{s'}(\bar{\mechanical{p}})\gamma_\mu u^s(\mechanical{p})]
[\bar u^s(\mechanical{p})\gamma_\nu v^{s'}(\bar{\mechanical{p}})]\\
&=\frac14\sum_s\sum_{s'}
\bar v^{s'}_\alpha(\bar{\mechanical{p}})
(\gamma_\mu)_{\alpha\beta}u^s_\beta(\mechanical{p})
\bar u^s_\gamma(\mechanical{p})
(\gamma_\nu)_{\gamma\delta}v^{s'}_\delta(\bar{\mechanical{p}})\\
&=\frac14(\gamma_\mu)_{\alpha\beta}
\underbrace{\left[\sum_s
u^s_\beta(\mechanical{p})\bar u^s_\gamma(\mechanical{p})\right]}
_{(\not{\mechanical{p}}+\mechanical{m}_e)_{\beta\gamma}}
(\gamma_\nu)_{\gamma\delta}
\underbrace{\left[\sum_{s'}
v^{s'}_\delta(\bar{\mechanical{p}})
\bar v^{s'}_\alpha(\bar{\mechanical{p}})\right]}
_{(\not{\bar{\mechanical{p}}}-\mechanical{m}_e)_{\delta\alpha}}.
\end{aligned}
$$

Closing the spinor-index chain gives
$$
\signal{L}^{(e)}_{\mu\nu}
=\frac14\operatorname{tr}
[\gamma_\mu(\not{\mechanical{p}}+\mechanical{m}_e)
\gamma_\nu(\not{\bar{\mechanical{p}}}-\mechanical{m}_e)].
$$

Expand the trace using linearity and (8.3)–(8.6):
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\operatorname{tr}
[\gamma_\mu(\not{\mechanical{p}}+\mechanical{m}_e)
\gamma_\nu(\not{\bar{\mechanical{p}}}-\mechanical{m}_e)]\\
&=\operatorname{tr}
[\gamma_\mu\not{\mechanical{p}}\gamma_\nu\not{\bar{\mechanical{p}}}]
-\mechanical{m}_e^2\operatorname{tr}[\gamma_\mu\gamma_\nu]\\
&\quad-\mechanical{m}_e\underbrace{
\operatorname{tr}[\gamma_\mu\not{\mechanical{p}}\gamma_\nu]}_0
+\mechanical{m}_e\underbrace{
\operatorname{tr}[\gamma_\mu\gamma_\nu\not{\bar{\mechanical{p}}}]}_0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The traces with an odd number of gamma matrices vanish by (8.4). Therefore,
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\operatorname{tr}
[\gamma_\mu(\not{\mechanical{p}}+\mechanical{m}_e)
\gamma_\nu(\not{\bar{\mechanical{p}}}-\mechanical{m}_e)]\\
&=\operatorname{tr}
[\gamma_\mu\not{\mechanical{p}}\gamma_\nu\not{\bar{\mechanical{p}}}]
-\mechanical{m}_e^2\operatorname{tr}[\gamma_\mu\gamma_\nu]\\
&=4[\mechanical{p}_\mu\bar{\mechanical{p}}_\nu+
\bar{\mechanical{p}}_\mu\mechanical{p}_\nu
-g_{\mu\nu}(\mechanical{p}\cdot\bar{\mechanical{p}})]
-4\mechanical{m}_e^2g_{\mu\nu}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The four-gamma identity used here is
$$
\operatorname{tr}
[\gamma_\mu\not{\mechanical{p}}\gamma_\nu\not{\bar{\mechanical{p}}}]
=4[\mechanical{p}_\mu\bar{\mechanical{p}}_\nu+
\bar{\mechanical{p}}_\mu\mechanical{p}_\nu
-g_{\mu\nu}(\mechanical{p}\cdot\bar{\mechanical{p}})].
$$
Thus
$$
\signal{L}^{(e)}_{\mu\nu}
=\mechanical{p}_\mu\bar{\mechanical{p}}_\nu+
\bar{\mechanical{p}}_\mu\mechanical{p}_\nu
-g_{\mu\nu}(\mechanical{p}\cdot\bar{\mechanical{p}}+\mechanical{m}_e^2).
$$

## Muon leptonic tensor

Make the replacements $\mechanical{p}\to\mechanical{k}$, $\bar{\mechanical{p}}\to\bar{\mechanical{k}}$, and $\mechanical{m}_e\to\mechanical{m}_\mu$. There is no factor $1/4$ in this final-state spin sum: final spins are summed, not averaged. Hence
$$
\signal{L}^{(m)\mu\nu}
=4[\mechanical{k}^\mu\bar{\mechanical{k}}^\nu+
\mechanical{k}^\nu\bar{\mechanical{k}}^\mu
-g^{\mu\nu}(\mechanical{k}\cdot\bar{\mechanical{k}}+\mechanical{m}_\mu^2)].
\tag{8.7}
$$

## Contraction of the leptonic tensors

Evaluate each contraction separately:
$$
\mechanical{p}_\mu\bar{\mechanical{p}}_\nu\mechanical{k}^\mu\bar{\mechanical{k}}^\nu
=(\mechanical{p}\cdot\mechanical{k})(\bar{\mechanical{p}}\cdot\bar{\mechanical{k}}),
$$
$$
\mechanical{p}_\mu\bar{\mechanical{p}}_\nu\bar{\mechanical{k}}^\mu\mechanical{k}^\nu
=(\mechanical{p}\cdot\bar{\mechanical{k}})(\bar{\mechanical{p}}\cdot\mechanical{k}).
$$

The remaining contractions are
$$
\mechanical{p}_\nu\bar{\mechanical{p}}_\mu\mechanical{k}^\mu\bar{\mechanical{k}}^\nu
=(\mechanical{p}\cdot\bar{\mechanical{k}})(\bar{\mechanical{p}}\cdot\mechanical{k}),
$$
$$
\mechanical{p}_\nu\bar{\mechanical{p}}_\mu\bar{\mechanical{k}}^\mu\mechanical{k}^\nu
=(\mechanical{p}\cdot\mechanical{k})(\bar{\mechanical{p}}\cdot\bar{\mechanical{k}}),
$$
$$
g_{\mu\nu}(\mechanical{p}\cdot\bar{\mechanical{p}}+\mechanical{m}_e^2)
\mechanical{k}^\mu\bar{\mechanical{k}}^\nu
=(\mechanical{k}\cdot\bar{\mechanical{k}})
(\mechanical{p}\cdot\bar{\mechanical{p}}+\mechanical{m}_e^2),
$$
$$
g^{\mu\nu}(\mechanical{k}\cdot\bar{\mechanical{k}}+\mechanical{m}_\mu^2)
\mechanical{p}_\mu\bar{\mechanical{p}}_\nu
=(\mechanical{p}\cdot\bar{\mechanical{p}})
(\mechanical{k}\cdot\bar{\mechanical{k}}+\mechanical{m}_\mu^2),
$$
$$
g_{\mu\nu}g^{\mu\nu}
(\mechanical{p}\cdot\bar{\mechanical{p}}+\mechanical{m}_e^2)
(\mechanical{k}\cdot\bar{\mechanical{k}}+\mechanical{m}_\mu^2)
=4(\mechanical{p}\cdot\bar{\mechanical{p}}+\mechanical{m}_e^2)
(\mechanical{k}\cdot\bar{\mechanical{k}}+\mechanical{m}_\mu^2).
$$

Sum the contributions, including the overall factor $4$ of the muon tensor:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\signal{L}^{(e)}_{\mu\nu}\signal{L}^{(m)\mu\nu}
=4\bigl[&
2(\mechanical{p}\cdot\mechanical{k})(\bar{\mechanical{p}}\cdot\bar{\mechanical{k}})
+2(\mechanical{p}\cdot\bar{\mechanical{k}})(\bar{\mechanical{p}}\cdot\mechanical{k})\\
&-2(\mechanical{p}\cdot\bar{\mechanical{p}}+\mechanical{m}_e^2)
(\mechanical{k}\cdot\bar{\mechanical{k}})\\
&-2(\mechanical{p}\cdot\bar{\mechanical{p}})
(\mechanical{k}\cdot\bar{\mechanical{k}}+\mechanical{m}_\mu^2)\\
&+4(\mechanical{p}\cdot\bar{\mechanical{p}}+\mechanical{m}_e^2)
(\mechanical{k}\cdot\bar{\mechanical{k}}+\mechanical{m}_\mu^2)\bigr]\\
=8\bigl[&
(\mechanical{p}\cdot\mechanical{k})(\bar{\mechanical{p}}\cdot\bar{\mechanical{k}})
+(\mechanical{p}\cdot\bar{\mechanical{k}})(\bar{\mechanical{p}}\cdot\mechanical{k})\\
&+\mechanical{m}_e^2(\mechanical{k}\cdot\bar{\mechanical{k}})
+\mechanical{m}_\mu^2(\mechanical{p}\cdot\bar{\mechanical{p}})
+2\mechanical{m}_e^2\mechanical{m}_\mu^2\bigr].
\end{aligned}
\tag{8.8}
$$

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{\mathcal M}$ | $1$ | Invariant amplitude | The two-to-two annihilation amplitude. |
| $\electric{e}$ | $1$ | Electromagnetic coupling | The charge parameter in the annihilation probability. |
| $\mechanical{q}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Virtual photon momentum | The total incoming four-momentum. |
| $\signal{L}^{(e)}_{\mu\nu},\signal{L}^{(m)\mu\nu}$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Leptonic tensors | The electron and muon spin sums. |
| $\mechanical{p},\bar{\mechanical{p}}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Incoming momenta | The electron and positron four-momenta. |
| $\mechanical{m}_e,\mechanical{m}_\mu$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Lepton masses | The electron and muon masses in the spin sums. |
| $u^s,\bar u^s,v^{s'},\bar v^{s'}$ | $\unit{MeV^{1/2}}$ | External spinors | The electron and positron spinors used in completeness. |
| $\mechanical{k},\bar{\mechanical{k}}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Outgoing momenta | The muon and antimuon four-momenta. |
