> [!TLDR]
> Compton scattering has s-channel and u-channel electron-exchange diagrams, with denominators simplified by the on-shell conditions.

At tree level, Compton scattering has two diagrams, called direct and exchange diagrams. They correspond to the s and u channels.

Let $\mechanical{p}$ be the initial electron momentum, $\mechanical{k}$ the incoming photon momentum, $\mechanical{p}'$ the final electron momentum, and $\mechanical{k}'$ the emitted photon momentum. The amplitude for the two diagrams in Fig. 8.3 is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\signal{\mathcal M}
=-\electric{e}^{\,2}
\signal{\epsilon}^{*\nu}(\mechanical{k}')
\signal{\epsilon}^{\mu}(\mechanical{k})\,
\bar u(\mechanical{p}')\Biggl[
&\gamma_\nu
\frac{\not{\mechanical{p}}+\not{\mechanical{k}}+\mechanical{m}}
{(\mechanical{p}+\mechanical{k})^2-\mechanical{m}^{\,2}}
\gamma_\mu\\
&+\gamma_\mu
\frac{\not{\mechanical{p}}-\not{\mechanical{k}'}+\mechanical{m}}
{(\mechanical{p}-\mechanical{k}')^2-\mechanical{m}^{\,2}}
\gamma_\nu
\Biggr]u(\mechanical{p}).
\end{aligned}
\tag{8.12}
$$

Use the electron mass shell, $\mechanical{p}^{\,2}=\mechanical{m}^{\,2}$, and the real-photon conditions $\mechanical{k}^{\,2}=\mechanical{k}'^{\,2}=0$:
$$
(\mechanical{p}+\mechanical{k})^2-\mechanical{m}^{\,2}
=\mechanical{p}^{\,2}+2\mechanical{p}\cdot\mechanical{k}+\mechanical{k}^{\,2}-\mechanical{m}^{\,2}
=2\mechanical{p}\cdot\mechanical{k},
$$
$$
(\mechanical{p}-\mechanical{k}')^2-\mechanical{m}^{\,2}
=\mechanical{p}^{\,2}-2\mechanical{p}\cdot\mechanical{k}'+\mechanical{k}'^{\,2}-\mechanical{m}^{\,2}
=-2\mechanical{p}\cdot\mechanical{k}'.
$$

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{\mathcal M}$ | $1$ | Invariant amplitude | The complete tree-level Compton amplitude. |
| $\electric{e}$ | $1$ | Electromagnetic coupling | The charge parameter at the two vertices. |
| $\mechanical{p},\mechanical{p}',\mechanical{k},\mechanical{k}'$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | External four-momenta | The initial and final electron and photon momenta. |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Electron mass | The mass in the internal electron propagator. |
| $\signal{\epsilon}^\mu,\signal{\epsilon}^{*\nu}$ | $1$ | Photon polarizations | The incoming polarization and complex-conjugate outgoing polarization. |
| $u,\bar u$ | $\unit{MeV^{1/2}}$ | Electron spinors | The external electron mode functions. |
