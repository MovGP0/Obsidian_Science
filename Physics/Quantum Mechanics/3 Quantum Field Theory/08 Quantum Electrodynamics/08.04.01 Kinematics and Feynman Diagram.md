title: "Kinematics and Feynman Diagram"
---

> [!TLDR]
> One s-channel virtual photon carries the total four-momentum from the electron–positron pair to the muon pair.

At tree level, $\mathrm e^+\mathrm e^-\to\mu^+\mu^-$ has one diagram: annihilation into a virtual photon followed by production of a muon pair. Muons have the same QED couplings as electrons but a different mass, $\mechanical{m}_\mu\approx207\mechanical{m}_e$. The initial and final particles have different flavors, so there are no exchange diagrams.

![[QFT-08-01-Lepton-Pair-Annihilation.svg|**Fig. 8.1** Tree-level electron–positron annihilation into a muon pair. The virtual photon carries four-momentum $\mechanical{q}=\mechanical{p}+\bar{\mechanical{p}}=\mechanical{k}+\bar{\mechanical{k}}$. Arrows on solid lines show fermion number flow; thin arrows show physical momentum.]]

Define the momenta by
$$
\mathrm e^-(\mechanical{p})+\mathrm e^+(\bar{\mechanical{p}})
\to\mu^-(\mechanical{k})+\mu^+(\bar{\mechanical{k}}),
$$
with
$$
\mechanical{q}^\mu=\mechanical{p}^\mu+\bar{\mechanical{p}}^\mu
=\mechanical{k}^\mu+\bar{\mechanical{k}}^\mu.
$$

> [!note]
> “Identical to electrons apart from mass” in the source refers to the form of their QED couplings. Electrons and muons remain distinct particle species. The diagram uses fermion-flow arrows consistent with the spinors in the amplitude; antiparticle momentum points opposite to fermion flow.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{m}_e,\mechanical{m}_\mu$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Lepton masses | The electron and muon masses. |
| $\mechanical{p},\bar{\mechanical{p}},\mechanical{k},\bar{\mechanical{k}}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | External four-momenta | The electron, positron, muon, and antimuon momenta. |
| $\mechanical{q}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Virtual photon momentum | The total incoming and outgoing four-momentum. |
