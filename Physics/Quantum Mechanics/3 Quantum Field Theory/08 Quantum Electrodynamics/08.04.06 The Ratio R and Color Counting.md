---
title: "The Ratio R and Color Counting"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The hadronic-to-muonic production ratio counts the accessible quark charges and colors, with corrections from QCD.

An important application of (8.10) is the ratio
$$
\mechanical{R}
=\frac{\mechanical{\sigma}(\mathrm e^+\mathrm e^-\to\text{hadrons})}
{\mechanical{\sigma}(\mathrm e^+\mathrm e^-\to\mu^+\mu^-)}.
$$
At QED tree level, production of a quark–antiquark pair with fractional charge $\electric{Q}_q$ and color count $N_c=3$ gives
$$
\mechanical{\sigma}_{q\bar q}
=N_c\electric{Q}_q^2\,\mechanical{\sigma}_{\mu^+\mu^-}.
$$
Therefore,
$$
\mechanical{R}=3\sum_q\electric{Q}_q^2,
$$
where the sum includes the quark flavors accessible at the given energy.

Below the charm threshold, the $u,d,s$ flavors give
$$
\mechanical{R}=3\left(\frac49+\frac19+\frac19\right)=2.
$$
Including charm gives $\mechanical{R}=10/3$. Adding bottom, also called beauty, gives $\mechanical{R}=11/3$. The source reports good agreement with experiment, with QCD corrections of order $\signal{\alpha}_s/\pi$.

## Example 8.2 — Massive case at low energy

If $\sqrt{\energy{s}}$ is comparable to $\mechanical{m}_\mu$, retain the masses. Neglect $\mechanical{m}_e$ but keep $\mechanical{m}_\mu$. The full tree-level total cross section is
$$
\mechanical{\sigma}(\mathrm e^+\mathrm e^-\to\mu^+\mu^-)
=\frac{4\pi\electric{\alpha}_{\mathrm{em}}^2}{3\energy{s}}
\sqrt{1-\frac{4\mechanical{m}_\mu^2}{\energy{s}}}
\left(1+\frac{2\mechanical{m}_\mu^2}{\energy{s}}\right).
$$

If both lepton masses are retained, the full phase-space ratio is
$$
\frac{|\mechanical{\mathbf k}|}{|\mechanical{\mathbf p}|}
=\sqrt{\frac{1-4\mechanical{m}_\mu^2/\energy{s}}
{1-4\mechanical{m}_e^2/\energy{s}}}.
$$
The initial leptonic tensor gives an analogous factor $1+2\mechanical{m}_e^2/\energy{s}$ in the total cross section.

Near threshold, $\energy{s}\to4\mechanical{m}_\mu^2$, the cross section grows in proportion to
$$
\mechanical{\beta}_\mu=\sqrt{1-\frac{4\mechanical{m}_\mu^2}{\energy{s}}},
$$
which reflects the two-body phase-space density. Far above threshold, $\energy{s}\gg\mechanical{m}_\mu^2$,
$$
\left(1+\frac{2\mechanical{m}_\mu^2}{\energy{s}}\right)
\sqrt{1-\frac{4\mechanical{m}_\mu^2}{\energy{s}}}\to1,
$$
and the relativistic result (8.10) is recovered.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{R}$ | $1$ | Hadronic production ratio | The hadronic cross section divided by the muon-pair cross section. |
| $\mechanical{\sigma},\mechanical{\sigma}_{q\bar q},\mechanical{\sigma}_{\mu^+\mu^-}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-2}}$ | Production cross sections | The total areas for the stated final states. |
| $\electric{Q}_q$ | $1$ | Fractional quark charge | The quark charge in units of the elementary charge magnitude. |
| $\signal{\alpha}_s$ | $1$ | Strong coupling | The QCD expansion parameter for corrections to the ratio. |
| $\electric{\alpha}_{\mathrm{em}}$ | $1$ | Fine-structure constant | The QED coupling parameter in muon production. |
| $\energy{s}$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Squared centre-of-mass energy | The total collision energy squared. |
| $\mechanical{m}_e,\mechanical{m}_\mu$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Lepton masses | The electron and muon masses in the threshold and flux formulas. |
| $\mechanical{\mathbf p},\mechanical{\mathbf k}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Centre-of-mass momenta | The initial electron and final muon three-momenta. |
| $\mechanical{\beta}_\mu$ | $1$ | Muon speed ratio | The muon speed divided by the speed of light. |
