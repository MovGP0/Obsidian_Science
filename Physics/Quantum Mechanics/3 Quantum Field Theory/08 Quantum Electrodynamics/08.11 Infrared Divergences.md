---
title: "Infrared Divergences"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Massless photons produce infrared divergences in virtual corrections and soft real emission, which must be combined in observable rates.

Can a detector distinguish elastic charged-particle scattering from the same event plus an arbitrarily low-energy photon? Finite energy resolution says it cannot. An exclusive amplitude that forbids all such radiation therefore asks a different question from a measured event count.

Read [[08.11.01 Nature of the Infrared Problem]] for the soft loop-momentum region, then [[08.11.02 The Bloch–Nordsieck Theorem]] for the inclusive probability that matches the resolution. [[08.11.03 The Sudakov Double Logarithm]] explains why finite results can still contain large logarithms. Regulator cancellation removes an auxiliary scale; it does not remove physical dependence on the detector's energy cut.
