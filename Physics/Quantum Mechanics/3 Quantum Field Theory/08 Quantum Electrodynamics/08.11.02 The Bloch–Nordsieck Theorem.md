title: "The Bloch–Nordsieck Theorem"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Inclusive cross sections combine virtual corrections with unresolved soft photons so that infrared regulator divergences cancel order by order.

The **Bloch–Nordsieck theorem** (1937) treats soft infrared divergences in QED by an incoherent sum over photons with energy below the experimental resolution $\energy{\Delta E}$.

The **KLN theorem**, named for Kinoshita (1962) and Lee–Nauenberg (1964), extends the cancellation to general kinematic degeneracies, including collinear ones relevant to massless fermions and non-Abelian theories such as QCD. Sum over all nearly degenerate initial and final states to cancel the divergences between virtual corrections and real emission.

For the vector-like QED setting described here, the initial states do not have the nontrivial degeneracy that would require this wider sum. The source therefore uses the Bloch–Nordsieck theorem.

## Theorem 8.6 — Bloch–Nordsieck

The physical cross section measured with finite energy resolution $\energy{\Delta E}$ is finite order by order in $\electric{\alpha}_{\mathrm{em}}$. Photons with energy $\energy{\omega}<\energy{\Delta E}$ are not resolved separately. The infrared divergences of the loop corrections cancel those of soft bremsstrahlung.

The source writes the physical cross section schematically as
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mechanical{\sigma}(\energy{\Delta E})
&=\mechanical{\sigma}_{\mathrm{virt}}
+\mechanical{\sigma}_{\mathrm{real}}(\energy{\omega}<\energy{\Delta E})\\
&=\mechanical{\sigma}_0
\left[
1+\frac{\electric{\alpha}_{\mathrm{em}}}{\pi}
\left(
-\ln^2\frac{\energy{\Delta E}}{\sqrt{\energy{s}}}
+\text{finite terms}
\right)
\right].
\end{aligned}
$$
The logarithmic regulator divergences from virtual photons in loops and from unresolved real photons cancel term by term.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\energy{\Delta E}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Energy resolution | The maximum photon energy treated as unresolved. |
| $\energy{\omega}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Photon energy | The emitted soft-photon energy. |
| $\mechanical{\sigma},\mechanical{\sigma}_{\mathrm{virt}},\mechanical{\sigma}_{\mathrm{real}},\mechanical{\sigma}_0$ | $\unit{MeV^{-2}}$ | Cross sections | The inclusive rate, virtual and real contributions, and reference scattering area. |
| $\electric{\alpha}_{\mathrm{em}}$ | $1$ | Fine-structure constant | The QED perturbative expansion parameter. |
| $\energy{s}$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Squared collision energy | The hard scale used in the resolution logarithm. |
