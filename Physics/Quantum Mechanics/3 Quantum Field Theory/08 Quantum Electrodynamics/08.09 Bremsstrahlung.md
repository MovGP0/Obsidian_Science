title: "Bremsstrahlung"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Bremsstrahlung is real-photon emission by an accelerated charged particle.

**Bremsstrahlung**, literally braking radiation, describes the emission of real photons by an accelerated electron.

An example is an electron deflected by an external Coulomb field. The tree diagrams are shown in Fig. 8.6.

![[QFT-08-06-Bremsstrahlung-External-Field.svg|**Fig. 8.6** Bremsstrahlung: an electron scatters from an external Coulomb field $\electric{A}_{\mathrm{ext}}^\mu$ and emits a photon. In (a), the photon is emitted before the scattering; in (b), it is emitted afterward.]]

Bremsstrahlung is experimentally relevant to the slowing of charged particles in matter and to detector physics.

It is also closely connected to the **infrared divergences** of QED. The source describes an infrared loop divergence in corrections to processes with external charged fermions, which is canceled by the corresponding contribution from soft bremsstrahlung, that is, emission of photons with very low energy. The cancellation concerns inclusive rates that combine unresolved soft radiation with the virtual correction. Section 8.11 discusses this point.

For soft-photon emission, the source gives
$$
d\mechanical{\sigma}_{\mathrm{brems}}
\approx d\mechanical{\sigma}_0\,
\frac{\electric{\alpha}_{\mathrm{em}}}{\pi}
\int_0^{\signal{\omega}_{\max}}\frac{d\signal{\omega}}{\signal{\omega}}\,
f(\mechanical{\theta},\mechanical{\phi}).
$$
Here $d\mechanical{\sigma}_0$ is the elastic differential cross section and $f$ is an angular factor. The source calls $\signal{\omega}$ the photon frequency; in natural units it is numerically equal to its energy.

The integral $\int d\signal{\omega}/\signal{\omega}$ diverges logarithmically as $\signal{\omega}\to0$. This is the infrared divergence discussed in Section 8.11.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\electric{A}_{\mathrm{ext}}^\mu$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | External four-potential | The Coulomb field from which the electron scatters. |
| $\mechanical{p},\mechanical{p}',\mechanical{k}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | External four-momenta | The initial electron, final electron, and emitted photon momenta in the figure. |
| $\mechanical{\sigma}_{\mathrm{brems}},\mechanical{\sigma}_0$ | $\unit{MeV^{-2}}$ | Scattering cross sections | The soft-emission contribution and elastic reference area. |
| $\electric{\alpha}_{\mathrm{em}}$ | $1$ | Fine-structure constant | The coupling factor for emission of an extra photon. |
| $\signal{\omega},\signal{\omega}_{\max}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Photon frequencies | The soft-photon frequency and upper cutoff in natural units. |
| $\mechanical{\theta},\mechanical{\phi}$ | $1$ | Emission angles | The polar and azimuthal arguments of the angular factor. |
