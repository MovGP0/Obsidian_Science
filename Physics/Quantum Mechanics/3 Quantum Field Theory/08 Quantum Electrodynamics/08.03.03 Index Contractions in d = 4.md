---
title: "Index Contractions in d = 4"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Four-dimensional contraction identities shorten products of gamma matrices.

In $d=4$, the gamma-matrix contraction identities are
$$
\gamma^\mu\gamma_\mu=4\mathbf1,
$$
$$
\gamma^\mu\not a\gamma_\mu=-2\not a,
$$
$$
\gamma^\mu\not a\not b\gamma_\mu
=4(a\cdot b)\mathbf1,
$$
$$
\gamma^\mu\not a\not b\not c\gamma_\mu
=-2\not c\not b\not a.
$$
The four-vectors $a,b,c$ are algebraic arguments. The identity matrix is written explicitly where the source leaves it implicit.
