---
title: "Fermion Self-Energy"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The electron self-energy changes its propagator, with ultraviolet divergences absorbed into mass and field renormalization.

The one-loop self-energy of the electron propagator is shown in Fig. 8.9.

![[QFT-08-09-Electron-Self-Energy.svg|**Fig. 8.9** One-loop fermion self-energy. The virtual photon carries momentum $\mechanical{l}$ and the internal electron carries $\mechanical{p}+\mechanical{l}$.]]

The diagram gives the self-energy function $\energy{\Sigma}(\mechanical{p})$, which modifies the fermion propagator:
$$
\signal{S}(\mechanical{p})
=\frac{i}{\not{\mechanical{p}}-\mechanical{m}-\energy{\Sigma}(\not{\mechanical{p}})}.
$$
Its structure is
$$
\energy{\Sigma}(\not{\mechanical{p}})=A\not{\mechanical{p}}+B\mechanical{m},
$$
where the scalar coefficients $A$ and $B$ have ultraviolet divergences. Mass and field renormalization absorb these divergences. Chapter 9 gives the details.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\energy{\Sigma}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Electron self-energy | The matrix-valued correction to the inverse fermion propagator. |
| $\signal{S}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Fermion propagator | The momentum-space propagator with the self-energy correction. |
| $\mechanical{p},\mechanical{l}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Four-momenta | The external electron and internal photon momenta. |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Electron mass | The mass parameter in the inverse propagator. |
