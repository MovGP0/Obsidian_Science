> [!TLDR]
> Electron–positron annihilation into a muon pair proceeds through one virtual photon at tree level.

The process $\mathrm e^+\mathrm e^-\to\mu^+\mu^-$ is a standard QED example. The following sections give its kinematics, tree amplitude, spin-averaged square, and cross section.
