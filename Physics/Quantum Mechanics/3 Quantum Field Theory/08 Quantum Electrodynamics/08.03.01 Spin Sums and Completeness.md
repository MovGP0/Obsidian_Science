---
title: "Spin Sums and Completeness"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Unmeasured initial spins are averaged and final spins are summed; completeness relations turn fermion bilinears into gamma-matrix traces.

If the initial and final particle spins are not measured, average over the initial spins and sum over the final spins. The Dirac-spinor completeness relations are
$$
\sum_{s=1,2}u^s(\mechanical{p})\bar u^s(\mechanical{p})
=\not{\mechanical{p}}+\mechanical{m},
$$
$$
\sum_{s=1,2}v^s(\mechanical{p})\bar v^s(\mechanical{p})
=\not{\mechanical{p}}-\mechanical{m}.
$$
These formulas convert sums of products of fermion bilinears into traces of products of gamma matrices.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $u^s,\bar u^s,v^s,\bar v^s$ | $\unit{MeV^{1/2}}$ | Dirac spinors | The normalized fermion and antifermion mode functions. |
| $\mechanical{p}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Four-momentum | The on-shell momentum in the spin sum. |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Fermion mass | The mass in the completeness relations. |
