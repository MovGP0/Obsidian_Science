---
title: "Squared Modulus and Interference"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The unpolarized Bhabha probability contains s-channel and t-channel squares plus their interference, with a forward Coulomb divergence.

The spin-averaged squared amplitude contains three terms:
$$
\overline{|\signal{\mathcal M}|^2}
=\overline{|\signal{\mathcal M}_s|^2}
+\overline{|\signal{\mathcal M}_t|^2}
-2\operatorname{Re}\overline{\signal{\mathcal M}_s\signal{\mathcal M}_t^*}.
$$
For $\mechanical{m}_e=0$, trace evaluation gives
$$
\overline{|\signal{\mathcal M}_s|^2}
=\frac{2\electric{e}^{\,4}(\mechanical{t}^{\,2}+\mechanical{u}^{\,2})}{\energy{s}^{\,2}},
$$
$$
\overline{|\signal{\mathcal M}_t|^2}
=\frac{2\electric{e}^{\,4}(\energy{s}^{\,2}+\mechanical{u}^{\,2})}{\mechanical{t}^{\,2}},
$$
$$
\overline{\signal{\mathcal M}_s\signal{\mathcal M}_t^*}
=-\frac{2\electric{e}^{\,4}\mechanical{u}^{\,2}}{\energy{s}\mechanical{t}}.
$$
Thus
$$
\left.\overline{|\signal{\mathcal M}|^2}\right|_{\mathrm{Bhabha}}
=2\electric{e}^{\,4}
\left[
\frac{\mechanical{t}^{\,2}+\mechanical{u}^{\,2}}{\energy{s}^{\,2}}
+\frac{\energy{s}^{\,2}+\mechanical{u}^{\,2}}{\mechanical{t}^{\,2}}
+\frac{2\mechanical{u}^{\,2}}{\energy{s}\mechanical{t}}
\right].
\tag{8.11}
$$
The interference is proportional to $\mechanical{u}^{\,2}$ and affects the angular dependence.

Unlike muon-pair production, the differential Bhabha cross section diverges as $1/\mechanical{t}^{\,2}\sim1/\sin^4(\mechanical{\theta}/2)$ when $\mechanical{\theta}\to0$. This is the classical Coulomb divergence associated with exchange of photons with small momentum.

## Example 8.3 — Forward limit

As $\mechanical{\theta}\to0$, one has $\mechanical{t}\to0$ and the cross section diverges. Physically, this reflects the infinite range of the Coulomb field: its $1/\mechanical{r}$ potential remains relevant at large distances. The source introduces an infinitesimal photon mass $\mechanical{m}_\gamma$ as an infrared regulator and returns to this method later in the chapter.

> [!note]
> A small photon mass is one possible regulator for this calculation. The source's word “requires” does not make it the only possible regularization.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{\mathcal M},\signal{\mathcal M}_s,\signal{\mathcal M}_t$ | $1$ | Invariant amplitudes | The complete and separate-channel Bhabha amplitudes. |
| $\electric{e}$ | $1$ | Electromagnetic coupling | The charge parameter in the tree-level result. |
| $\mechanical{m}_e$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Electron mass | The mass neglected in the relativistic limit. |
| $\energy{s}$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Squared centre-of-mass energy | The energy invariant in the annihilation channel. |
| $\mechanical{t},\mechanical{u}$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Mandelstam transfers | The momentum-transfer invariants. |
| $\mechanical{\theta}$ | $1$ | Scattering angle | The angle that tends to zero in the forward divergence. |
| $\mechanical{r}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Separation | The distance in the Coulomb potential. |
| $\mechanical{m}_\gamma$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Photon mass regulator | An auxiliary small mass used to regulate the infrared behavior. |
