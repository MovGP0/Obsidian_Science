---
title: "Chiral Structure of the Amplitudes"
---

> [!TLDR]
> When fermion masses are negligible, QED amplitudes split into definite helicity sectors constrained by vector-current coupling.

At energies $\sqrt{\energy{s}}\gg\mechanical{m}_e,\mechanical{m}_\mu$, fermions of definite chirality decouple. The source describes an approximately conserved chiral symmetry $\mathrm U(1)_L\times\mathrm U(1)_R$, and QED amplitudes factorize into definite helicity sectors.

> [!note]
> The full $\mathrm U(1)_L\times\mathrm U(1)_R$ symmetry is a statement about the classical massless theory. Its axial combination has a quantum anomaly. The high-energy helicity selection along open massless fermion lines used here remains the relevant approximation for these amplitudes.

For high-energy muon-pair production, the nonzero helicity combinations are those compatible with spin-one photon exchange in the s channel. The source gives
$$
\signal{\mathcal M}
(\mathrm e_L^-\mathrm e_R^+\to\mu_L^-\mu_R^+)
\propto-\electric{e}^{\,2}(1+\cos\mechanical{\theta}).
$$

The other allowed amplitude is
$$
\signal{\mathcal M}
(\mathrm e_L^-\mathrm e_R^+\to\mu_R^-\mu_L^+)
\propto-\electric{e}^{\,2}(1-\cos\mechanical{\theta}).
$$
The other combinations for this initial state vanish. The virtual photon cannot couple the same-helicity particle–antiparticle pair at a vector vertex in the massless limit.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\energy{s}$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Squared collision energy | The high-energy invariant of the electron–positron collision. |
| $\mechanical{m}_e,\mechanical{m}_\mu$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Lepton masses | The masses neglected in the chiral approximation. |
| $\signal{\mathcal M}$ | $1$ | Helicity amplitude | The invariant amplitude for the specified helicity states. |
| $\electric{e}$ | $1$ | Electromagnetic coupling | The charge parameter in the amplitude. |
| $\mechanical{\theta}$ | $1$ | Scattering angle | The polar deflection angle in the helicity amplitude. |
