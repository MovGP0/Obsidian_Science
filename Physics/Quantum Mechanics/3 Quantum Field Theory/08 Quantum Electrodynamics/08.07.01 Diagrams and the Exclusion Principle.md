---
title: "Diagrams and the Exclusion Principle"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The t-channel and u-channel Møller amplitudes have a relative minus sign because the electrons are identical.

Møller scattering is a collision between two electrons. Because the particles are identical, both the t-channel and u-channel exchange diagrams occur, with a relative minus sign from Fermi statistics.

The amplitude for the two diagrams in Fig. 8.4 is
$$
\signal{\mathcal M}_{\mathrm{Møller}}
=\signal{\mathcal M}_t-\signal{\mathcal M}_u.
$$

![[QFT-08-04-Moller-Exchange-Channels.svg|**Fig. 8.4** Tree-level Møller scattering, $\mathrm e^-\mathrm e^-\to\mathrm e^-\mathrm e^-$: (a) t-channel exchange; (b) u-channel exchange. The two outgoing electrons are exchanged between the panels.]]

The channel amplitudes are
$$
\signal{\mathcal M}_t
=\frac{\electric{e}^{\,2}}{\mechanical{t}}
[\bar u(\mechanical{k}_1)\gamma^\mu u(\mechanical{p}_1)]
[\bar u(\mechanical{k}_2)\gamma_\mu u(\mechanical{p}_2)],
$$
$$
\signal{\mathcal M}_u
=\frac{\electric{e}^{\,2}}{\mechanical{u}}
[\bar u(\mechanical{k}_2)\gamma^\mu u(\mechanical{p}_1)]
[\bar u(\mechanical{k}_1)\gamma_\mu u(\mechanical{p}_2)].
$$
The relative minus sign is a consequence of Fermi statistics. Under exchange of the final electrons, $\mechanical{k}_1\leftrightarrow\mechanical{k}_2$, the amplitude must change sign.

The differential cross section has a forward divergence as $\mechanical{\theta}\to0$ and a backward divergence as $\mechanical{\theta}\to\pi$. Since the particles are identical, forward and backward scattering cannot be distinguished by particle identity.

In the relativistic limit $\mechanical{m}_e=0$,
$$
\overline{|\signal{\mathcal M}|^2}_{\mathrm{Møller}}
=2\electric{e}^{\,4}
\left[
\frac{\energy{s}^{\,2}+\mechanical{u}^{\,2}}{\mechanical{t}^{\,2}}
+\frac{\energy{s}^{\,2}+\mechanical{t}^{\,2}}{\mechanical{u}^{\,2}}
+\frac{2\energy{s}^{\,2}}{\mechanical{t}\mechanical{u}}
\right].
\tag{8.14}
$$
