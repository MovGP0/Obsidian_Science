> [!TLDR]
> Work through the chapter's exercises with the question, source solution, and correction notes together in each linked article.

Work first through Exercises 8.1–8.3 to connect local symmetry, conserved current, and spin-sum algebra. Exercises 8.4–8.8 then test the route from amplitude to angular probability, including recoil, identical states, and gauge cancellation. Exercises 8.9 and 8.12 introduce two distinct loop observables: a static potential shift and a magnetic anomaly. Exercise 8.10 tests final-state counting, and Exercise 8.11 tests analytic continuation with particle labels preserved.

Before opening a solution, predict one diagnostic result: a zero gauge variation, a forward enhancement, a vanishing momentum contraction, or a limiting angular factor. Then identify which assumption makes that prediction possible. Source corrections and incomplete derivations are retained in each exercise; a quoted endpoint formula is not evidence that every requested intermediate step was supplied.
