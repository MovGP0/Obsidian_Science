---
title: "Ward–Takahashi Identity"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The Ward–Takahashi identity relates the fermion–photon vertex to the fermion propagator and constrains renormalization.

A gauge-dependent calculation must yield a gauge-independent physical probability. The Ward–Takahashi identity supplies the consistency relation that makes this possible: a change in the fermion propagator is tied to a change in its photon vertex. These corrections cannot be chosen independently.

Read [[08.13.01 Statement and Physical Meaning]] with particular care for the inverse-propagator factor of the imaginary unit. [[08.13.02 Consequences for Renormalization Factors]] then relates the normalizations, and [[08.13.03 Form for External Amplitudes]] gives the practical momentum-replacement test. The latter tests a complete on-shell amplitude; it does not require each separate diagram to pass.
