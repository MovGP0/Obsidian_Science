---
title: "Bhabha Scattering — e⁺e⁻ → e⁺e⁻"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Bhabha scattering receives both annihilation and photon-exchange contributions at tree level.

Bhabha scattering is $\mathrm e^+\mathrm e^-\to\mathrm e^+\mathrm e^-$. The final electron pair can arise from annihilation or from photon exchange between the incident particles. These alternatives have the same external state, so the detector cannot assign an event to one diagram. Their amplitudes must interfere.

Use [[08.05.01 Diagrams and Interference]] to fix the channel and sign conventions before reading [[08.05.02 Squared Modulus and Interference]]. Compare with muon-pair production: the additional exchange channel produces a strong forward peak. A cross section for a finite angular acceptance is meaningful, while integration of the ideal Coulomb result down to zero angle diverges. The displayed high-energy treatment neglects the electron mass and radiative corrections.
