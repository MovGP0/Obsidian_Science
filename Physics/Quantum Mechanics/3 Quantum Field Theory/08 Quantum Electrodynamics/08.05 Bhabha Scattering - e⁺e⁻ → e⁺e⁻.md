---
title: "Bhabha Scattering — e⁺e⁻ → e⁺e⁻"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Bhabha scattering receives both annihilation and photon-exchange contributions at tree level.

Bhabha scattering is $\mathrm e^+\mathrm e^-\to\mathrm e^+\mathrm e^-$. Its tree-level amplitude contains an s-channel contribution and a t-channel contribution.
