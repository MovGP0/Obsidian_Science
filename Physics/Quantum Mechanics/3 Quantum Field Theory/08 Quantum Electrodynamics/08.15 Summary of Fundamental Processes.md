> [!TLDR]
> The leading relativistic QED processes have related invariant probabilities, connected by crossing symmetry.

Table 8.1 collects the main tree-level QED results in the relativistic limit $\mechanical{m}_e=\mechanical{m}_\mu=0$.

Use the table after fixing each process's external labels and physical invariant region. A negative prefactor in the Compton row does not imply a negative probability: its two invariant ratios are negative in the physical massless region. In annihilation into photons the two transfers have the same sign, so the corresponding ratios are positive.

The table compares squared amplitudes, not detector counts. Flux, angular acceptance, populated initial spins, and identical-final-state counting are still needed for a cross section. In particular, the two-photon final-state factor belongs to phase-space integration and is not an extra factor in the amplitude column.

## Remark 8.8

The table displays crossing symmetry. Compton scattering and two-photon annihilation are related by exchange of channels, formally
$$
\energy{s}_{\mathrm{Compton}}\leftrightarrow\mechanical{t}_{\mathrm{annihilation}},
$$
with $\mechanical{u}$ unchanged. Thus
$$
\frac{\mechanical{u}}{\energy{s}}+\frac{\energy{s}}{\mechanical{u}}
\longrightarrow
\frac{\mechanical{u}}{\mechanical{t}}+\frac{\mechanical{t}}{\mechanical{u}},
$$
together with the overall fermionic crossing sign $-1$.

**Table 8.1** Tree-level QED processes in the relativistic limit. The massless Mandelstam relation is $\energy{s}+\mechanical{t}+\mechanical{u}=0$.

| Process | Diagrams | $\overline{\lvert\signal{\mathcal M}\rvert^2}/\electric{e}^{\,4}$ | $\mechanical{\sigma}_{\mathrm{tot}}$ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mathrm e^+\mathrm e^-\to\mu^+\mu^-$ | 1 (s channel) | $\dfrac{2(\mechanical{t}^{\,2}+\mechanical{u}^{\,2})}{\energy{s}^{\,2}}$ | $\dfrac{4\pi\electric{\alpha}_{\mathrm{em}}^2}{3\energy{s}}$ |
| $\mathrm e^+\mathrm e^-\to\mathrm e^+\mathrm e^-$ | 2 (s, t) | $2\left[\begin{gathered}\dfrac{\mechanical{t}^{\,2}+\mechanical{u}^{\,2}}{\energy{s}^{\,2}}+\dfrac{\energy{s}^{\,2}+\mechanical{u}^{\,2}}{\mechanical{t}^{\,2}}\\+\dfrac{2\mechanical{u}^{\,2}}{\energy{s}\mechanical{t}}\end{gathered}\right]$ | — |
| $\mathrm e^-\gamma\to\mathrm e^-\gamma$ | 2 (s, u) | $-2\left[\dfrac{\mechanical{u}}{\energy{s}}+\dfrac{\energy{s}}{\mechanical{u}}\right]$ | $\dfrac{2\pi\electric{\alpha}_{\mathrm{em}}^2}{\energy{s}}\left[\ln\dfrac{\energy{s}}{\mechanical{m}_e^2}+\dfrac12\right]$ |
| $\mathrm e^-\mathrm e^-\to\mathrm e^-\mathrm e^-$ | 2 (t, u) | $2\left[\begin{gathered}\dfrac{\energy{s}^{\,2}+\mechanical{u}^{\,2}}{\mechanical{t}^{\,2}}+\dfrac{\energy{s}^{\,2}+\mechanical{t}^{\,2}}{\mechanical{u}^{\,2}}\\+\dfrac{2\energy{s}^{\,2}}{\mechanical{t}\mechanical{u}}\end{gathered}\right]$ | — |
| $\mathrm e^+\mathrm e^-\to\gamma\gamma$ | 2 (t, u) | $2\left[\dfrac{\mechanical{u}}{\mechanical{t}}+\dfrac{\mechanical{t}}{\mechanical{u}}\right]$ | $\dfrac{2\pi\electric{\alpha}_{\mathrm{em}}^2}{\energy{s}}\left[\ln\dfrac{\energy{s}}{\mechanical{m}_e^2}-1\right]$ |

> [!note]
> The source labels the table as the massless limit. The squared amplitudes use this limit, but the total Compton and two-photon cross sections retain the electron mass inside logarithms as a collinear regulator. The dashes are the source's entries for the uncut Bhabha and Møller total cross sections.

Crossing an external fermion changes the completeness relation from
$$
\sum_r u^r\bar u^r=\not{\mechanical{p}}+\mechanical{m}
$$
to
$$
\sum_r v^r\bar v^r=\not{\mechanical{p}}-\mechanical{m}.
$$
This gives the fermionic sign relation discussed in Remark 8.8. Crossing analytically relates an incoming antiparticle of momentum $\mechanical{p}$ to an outgoing particle with momentum $-\mechanical{p}$.

As a transfer check, interchange the two transfer invariants in the Møller and two-photon rows. Both probabilities remain unchanged because the final particles are identical. The Bhabha row need not have that symmetry: its final electron and positron can be distinguished by charge.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{m}_e,\mechanical{m}_\mu$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Lepton masses | The masses neglected in the relativistic summary. |
| $\energy{s}$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Squared collision energy | The energy invariant in each physical process. |
| $\mechanical{t},\mechanical{u}$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Mandelstam transfers | The crossed momentum-transfer invariants. |
| $\signal{\mathcal M}$ | $1$ | Invariant amplitude | The tree-level amplitude for each listed two-to-two process. |
| $\electric{e},\electric{\alpha}_{\mathrm{em}}$ | $1$ | Electromagnetic couplings | The charge parameter and fine-structure constant. |
| $\mechanical{\sigma}_{\mathrm{tot}}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-2}}$ | Total cross section | The scattering area for each process where the source gives a finite regulated result. |
| $\mechanical{p}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Crossed four-momentum | The momentum argument in the spinor completeness relations. |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Fermion mass | The mass in the completeness relations. |
| $u^r,\bar u^r,v^r,\bar v^r$ | $\unit{MeV^{1/2}}$ | Fermion spinors | The mode functions related by crossing. |
