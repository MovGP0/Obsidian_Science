---
title: "Differential and Total Cross Sections"
---

> [!TLDR]
> At high energy, muon-pair production has an angular factor 1 + cos²θ and a total cross section proportional to 1/s.

An amplitude becomes a cross section only after division by incident flux and integration over allowed final states. The momentum ratio below is the two-body phase-space and flux correction; setting it to one is a high-energy approximation, not part of the general formula. Initial spins are already averaged in the squared amplitude.

In natural units, the differential cross section for a two-to-two process in the centre-of-mass frame is
$$
\frac{d\mechanical{\sigma}}{d\mechanical{\Omega}}
=\frac1{64\pi^2\energy{s}}
\frac{|\mechanical{\mathbf k}|}{|\mechanical{\mathbf p}|}
\overline{|\signal{\mathcal M}|^2}.
$$
Here $|\mechanical{\mathbf p}|$ and $|\mechanical{\mathbf k}|$ are the initial and final three-momentum magnitudes in that frame.

For $\mechanical{m}_e=\mechanical{m}_\mu=0$,
$$
|\mechanical{\mathbf k}|=|\mechanical{\mathbf p}|=\frac{\sqrt{\energy{s}}}2.
$$
Let $\mechanical{\theta}$ be the angle between the electron and the outgoing negative muon. In spherical centre-of-mass coordinates,
$$
\mechanical{t}=-\frac{\energy{s}}2(1-\cos\mechanical{\theta}),\qquad
\mechanical{u}=-\frac{\energy{s}}2(1+\cos\mechanical{\theta}).
$$
Then
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mechanical{t}^{\,2}+\mechanical{u}^{\,2}
&=\frac{\energy{s}^{\,2}}4(1-\cos\mechanical{\theta})^2
+\frac{\energy{s}^{\,2}}4(1+\cos\mechanical{\theta})^2\\
&=\frac{\energy{s}^{\,2}}2(1+\cos^2\mechanical{\theta}),
\end{aligned}
$$
and
$$
\overline{|\signal{\mathcal M}|^2}
=\frac{2\electric{e}^{\,4}}{\energy{s}^{\,2}}
\frac{\energy{s}^{\,2}(1+\cos^2\mechanical{\theta})}2
=\electric{e}^{\,4}(1+\cos^2\mechanical{\theta}).
$$

The differential cross section is
$$
\boxed{
\frac{d\mechanical{\sigma}}{d\mechanical{\Omega}}
(\mathrm e^+\mathrm e^-\to\mu^+\mu^-)
=\frac{\electric{\alpha}_{\mathrm{em}}^2}{4\energy{s}}
(1+\cos^2\mechanical{\theta})}.
\tag{8.9}
$$

Integrate over the solid angle, with
$$
\int d\mechanical{\Omega}
=\int_0^\pi\sin\mechanical{\theta}\,d\mechanical{\theta}
\int_0^{2\pi}d\mechanical{\phi},
$$
to obtain
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mechanical{\sigma}_{\mathrm{tot}}
&=\frac{\electric{\alpha}_{\mathrm{em}}^2}{4\energy{s}}
\,2\pi\int_{-1}^{1}(1+\cos^2\mechanical{\theta})\,d(\cos\mechanical{\theta})\\
&=\frac{\electric{\alpha}_{\mathrm{em}}^2}{4\energy{s}}
\,2\pi\,\frac83
=\frac{4\pi\electric{\alpha}_{\mathrm{em}}^2}{3\energy{s}}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{8.10}
$$

The standard tree-level QED result is
$$
\boxed{
\mechanical{\sigma}_{\mathrm{tot}}(\mathrm e^+\mathrm e^-\to\mu^+\mu^-)
=\frac{4\pi\electric{\alpha}_{\mathrm{em}}^2}{3\energy{s}}}.
$$
For $\sqrt{\energy{s}}=10\,\unit{GeV}$,
$$
\mechanical{\sigma}_{\mathrm{tot}}
\approx\frac{4\pi/137^2}{3(10\,\unit{GeV})^2}
\approx0.87\,\unit{nb}.
$$

The angular factor is symmetric about a right angle and has no forward singularity. Its solid-angle integral is sixteen pi divided by three, which gives (8.10). At fixed coupling, doubling the centre-of-mass energy reduces the total cross section by four. The cross section has inverse-energy-squared units in the natural-unit convention.

The numerical value above is the source's tree-level benchmark with its stated coupling. It does not include detector angular cuts, radiation, the running coupling, or weak exchange. A measured comparison must specify those ingredients. Close to threshold the final momentum tends to zero, so the massless result cannot describe the onset of production.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{\sigma},\mechanical{\sigma}_{\mathrm{tot}}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-2}}$ | Cross sections | The differential scattering area and its angular integral. |
| $\mechanical{\Omega},\mechanical{\theta},\mechanical{\phi}$ | $1$ | Scattering angles | The solid angle, polar angle, and azimuth. |
| $\energy{s}$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Squared centre-of-mass energy | The total energy squared in the collision. |
| $\mechanical{\mathbf p},\mechanical{\mathbf k}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Three-momenta | The incoming electron and outgoing muon spatial momenta. |
| $\mechanical{m}_e,\mechanical{m}_\mu$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Lepton masses | The masses set to zero in the high-energy approximation. |
| $\mechanical{t},\mechanical{u}$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Mandelstam transfers | The momentum-transfer invariants expressed in terms of the scattering angle. |
| $\signal{\mathcal M}$ | $1$ | Invariant amplitude | The tree-level two-to-two amplitude. |
| $\electric{e},\electric{\alpha}_{\mathrm{em}}$ | $1$ | Electromagnetic couplings | The charge parameter and fine-structure constant. |
