---
title: "Nature of the Infrared Problem"
---

> [!TLDR]
> QED loop corrections can diverge at small internal momentum because the photon is massless.

Beyond tree level, QED loop corrections develop ultraviolet divergences at large loop momentum and infrared divergences at small loop momentum. The infrared divergences arise because the photon is massless.

Propagation of photons with arbitrarily long wavelength is not suppressed.

Consider the correction to the vertex $\electric{e}\bar{\signal{\psi}}\gamma^\mu\signal{\psi}\electric{A}_\mu$ at first relative order in $\electric{\alpha}_{\mathrm{em}}$. The one-loop vertex diagram is shown in Fig. 8.7.

![[QFT-08-07-Soft-Vertex-Correction.svg|**Fig. 8.7** One-loop vertex correction. The external photon field $\electric{A}^\mu$ couples at the central vertex. The virtual photon in the loop carries four-momentum $\mechanical{l}^\mu$.]]

The diagram gives an integral of the form
$$
\Gamma^\mu(\mechanical{p},\mechanical{p}')
\sim\int\frac{d^4\mechanical{k}}{(2\pi)^4}
\frac{
\bar u(\mechanical{p}')\gamma^\nu
(\not{\mechanical{p}'}+\not{\mechanical{l}}+\mechanical{m})\gamma^\mu
(\not{\mechanical{p}}+\not{\mechanical{l}}+\mechanical{m})\gamma_\nu u(\mechanical{p})
}{
\mechanical{l}^{\,2}
[(\mechanical{p}'+\mechanical{l})^2-\mechanical{m}^{\,2}]
[(\mechanical{p}+\mechanical{l})^2-\mechanical{m}^{\,2}]
}.
$$

> [!note]
> The source prints $d^4\mechanical{k}$ in the measure but uses $\mechanical{l}$ for the loop momentum everywhere else. A consistent integral uses $d^4\mechanical{l}$. The displayed numerator includes external spinors, so this expression is a spinor-contracted vertex rather than the amputated vertex matrix alone.

For $\mechanical{l}^\mu\to0$, the numerator tends to a constant. The denominator scales as
$$
\mechanical{l}^{\,2}(2\mechanical{p}'\cdot\mechanical{l})(2\mechanical{p}\cdot\mechanical{l})
\sim\mechanical{l}^{\,4}.
$$
The radial measure scales as $d^4\mechanical{l}\sim|\mechanical{l}|^3d|\mechanical{l}|$. A logarithmic divergence $\int d|\mechanical{l}|/|\mechanical{l}|$ remains at small $|\mechanical{l}|$.

The source therefore gives the infrared part schematically as
$$
\delta\Gamma^\mu
\sim\electric{\alpha}_{\mathrm{em}}
\ln\left(\frac{\mechanical{m}_\gamma^2}{\energy{\mu}^{\,2}}\right)
\Gamma^\mu_{\mathrm{tree}},
$$
where $\mechanical{m}_\gamma$ is an auxiliary photon mass that makes the regulated integral finite. The scale $\energy{\mu}$ in the logarithm is a reference mass or energy scale, distinct from the Lorentz index $\mu$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\electric{e},\electric{\alpha}_{\mathrm{em}}$ | $1$ | Electromagnetic couplings | The charge parameter and fine-structure constant. |
