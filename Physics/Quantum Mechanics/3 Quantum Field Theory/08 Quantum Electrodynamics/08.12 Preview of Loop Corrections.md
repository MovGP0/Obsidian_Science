> [!TLDR]
> One-loop QED corrections give measurable changes to tree-level results, including the anomalous magnetic moment.

The tree-level calculations above are only the first approximation. Loop corrections have a typical expansion factor
$$
\frac{\electric{\alpha}_{\mathrm{em}}}{\pi}\approx2.3\times10^{-3}.
$$
They give measurable effects, including the anomalous magnetic moment. This chapter gives a qualitative preview; Chapter 9 develops the detailed calculations.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\electric{\alpha}_{\mathrm{em}}$ | $1$ | Fine-structure constant | The parameter controlling the size of QED loop corrections. |
