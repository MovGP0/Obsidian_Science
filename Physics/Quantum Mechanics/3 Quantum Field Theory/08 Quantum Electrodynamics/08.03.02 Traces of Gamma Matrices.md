---
title: "Traces of Gamma Matrices"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Clifford algebra gives the gamma-matrix traces used to reduce spin-averaged scattering amplitudes.

The following trace identities follow from the Clifford algebra
$$
\{\gamma^\mu,\gamma^\nu\}=2g^{\mu\nu}.
$$
In four dimensions,
$$
\operatorname{tr}[\mathbf1]=4,
$$
$$
\operatorname{tr}[\gamma^\mu]=0,
\tag{8.3}
$$
$$
\operatorname{tr}[\gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu]=4g^{\mu\nu},
$$
$$
\operatorname{tr}[\gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu\gamma^\rho]=0,
\tag{8.4}
$$
$$
\operatorname{tr}[\gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu\gamma^\rho\gamma^\sigma]
=4(g^{\mu\nu}g^{\rho\sigma}
-g^{\mu\rho}g^{\nu\sigma}
+g^{\mu\sigma}g^{\nu\rho}),
\tag{8.5}
$$
$$
\operatorname{tr}[\gamma^5]=0,
\qquad
\operatorname{tr}[\gamma^5\gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu]=0,
$$
$$
\operatorname{tr}[\gamma^5\gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu\gamma^\rho\gamma^\sigma]
=-4i\epsilon^{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}.
$$

Useful consequences for arbitrary four-vector arguments $a,b,c,d$ are
$$
\operatorname{tr}[\not a\not b]=4(a\cdot b),
$$
$$
\operatorname{tr}[\not a\not b\not c\not d]
=4[(a\cdot b)(c\cdot d)
-(a\cdot c)(b\cdot d)
+(a\cdot d)(b\cdot c)].
\tag{8.6}
$$
