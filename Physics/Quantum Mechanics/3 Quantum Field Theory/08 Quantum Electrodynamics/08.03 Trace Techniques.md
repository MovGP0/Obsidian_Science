---
title: "Trace Techniques"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Spin sums and gamma-matrix identities convert unpolarized squared amplitudes into traces.

A detector can count outgoing particles without resolving their spin. The required probability then contains an incoherent sum over those unresolved spin states, rather than one arbitrarily chosen spin amplitude. The **trace technique** evaluates that sum without choosing explicit components for each spinor.

Start with [[08.03.01 Spin Sums and Completeness]] to identify the initial-state average and final-state sum. Use [[08.03.02 Traces of Gamma Matrices]] to reduce the resulting closed matrix chains, then [[08.03.03 Index Contractions in d = 4]] to contract repeated Lorentz indices. Completeness closes the spinor indices; it does not permit the matrices to be reordered.

This route applies directly to unpolarized beams. A prepared polarized state requires its spin projector or density matrix instead of the full unpolarized average. Compare with [[08.04.03 Calculation of the Squared Amplitude]] to see why only the incoming pair supplies the factor of one quarter.
