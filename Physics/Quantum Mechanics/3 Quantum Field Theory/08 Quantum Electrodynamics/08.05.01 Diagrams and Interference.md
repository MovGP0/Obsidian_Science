---
title: "Diagrams and Interference"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Bhabha scattering has s-channel annihilation and t-channel exchange diagrams whose amplitudes interfere.

Unlike muon-pair production, Bhabha scattering has the same particle flavors in the initial and final states. Both diagrams must be included: the **s channel** for annihilation and the **t channel** for exchange.

![[QFT-08-02-Bhabha-Tree-Channels.svg|**Fig. 8.2** Tree diagrams for electron–positron scattering: (a) s channel with $\mechanical{q}_s^2=\energy{s}$; (b) t channel with $\mechanical{q}_t^2=\mechanical{t}$. Solid-line arrows show fermion number flow, and thin arrows show physical momenta.]]

The total amplitude is the sum of these contributions, with their relative sign fixed by Fermi statistics.

Explicitly,
$$
\signal{\mathcal M}=\signal{\mathcal M}_s-\signal{\mathcal M}_t,
$$
where
$$
\signal{\mathcal M}_s
=\frac{\electric{e}^{\,2}}{\energy{s}}
[\bar v(\bar{\mechanical{p}})\gamma^\mu u(\mechanical{p})]
[\bar u(\mechanical{k})\gamma_\mu v(\bar{\mechanical{k}})],
$$
$$
\signal{\mathcal M}_t
=\frac{\electric{e}^{\,2}}{\mechanical{t}}
[\bar u(\mechanical{k})\gamma^\mu u(\mechanical{p})]
[\bar v(\bar{\mechanical{p}})\gamma_\mu v(\bar{\mechanical{k}})].
$$
The relative minus sign comes from the antisymmetry of fermion fields: exchange of two external fermion factors changes the sign of the amplitude.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{q}_s,\mechanical{q}_t$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Photon four-momenta | The virtual photon momenta in the two channels. |
| $\energy{s}$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Annihilation invariant | The squared total incoming four-momentum. |
| $\mechanical{t}$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Exchange invariant | The squared momentum transfer in the t channel. |
| $\signal{\mathcal M},\signal{\mathcal M}_s,\signal{\mathcal M}_t$ | $1$ | Invariant amplitudes | The total Bhabha amplitude and its two channel contributions. |
| $\electric{e}$ | $1$ | Electromagnetic coupling | The charge parameter at both photon vertices. |
| $\mechanical{p},\bar{\mechanical{p}},\mechanical{k},\bar{\mechanical{k}}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | External four-momenta | The incoming and outgoing electron and positron momenta. |
| $u,\bar u,v,\bar v$ | $\unit{MeV^{1/2}}$ | External spinors | The normalized electron and positron factors. |
