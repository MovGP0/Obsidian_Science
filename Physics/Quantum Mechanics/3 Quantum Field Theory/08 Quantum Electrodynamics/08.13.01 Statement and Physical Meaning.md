---
title: "Statement and Physical Meaning"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The Ward–Takahashi identity expresses electromagnetic current conservation as a relation between the quantum vertex and propagator.

The Ward–Takahashi identity is the quantum form of electromagnetic current conservation. It gives a precise relation between the fermion–photon vertex and the fermion propagator.

The compact identity is
$$
\mechanical{q}_\mu\Gamma^\mu(\mechanical{p}',\mechanical{p})
=\signal{S}_F^{-1}(\mechanical{p}')-\signal{S}_F^{-1}(\mechanical{p}).
\tag{8.19}
$$
Here $\mechanical{q}=\mechanical{p}'-\mechanical{p}$ is the momentum transferred to the vertex. The full vertex $\Gamma^\mu$ contains the tree term $\gamma^\mu$ and all one-particle-irreducible corrections at every order in $\electric{\alpha}_{\mathrm{em}}$.

The source defines the amputated inverse denominator by
$$
\signal{S}_F^{-1}(\mechanical{p})
=\not{\mechanical{p}}-\mechanical{m}_R-\energy{\Sigma}(\mechanical{p}),
$$
and writes the renormalized propagator as
$$
\signal{S}_F(\mechanical{p})
=\frac{i}{\not{\mechanical{p}}-\mechanical{m}_R-\energy{\Sigma}(\mechanical{p})}.
$$

> [!note]
> In (8.19), the notation $\signal{S}_F^{-1}$ means the inverse Dirac denominator with the overall factor $i$ removed. It is not the literal matrix inverse of the displayed propagator. With the literal propagator inverse, the right-hand side of (8.19) has an additional factor $i$.

At tree level, $\Gamma^\mu_{\mathrm{tree}}=\gamma^\mu$ and $\left.\signal{S}_F^{-1}(\mechanical{p})\right|_{\mathrm{tree}}=\not{\mechanical{p}}-\mechanical{m}$. Thus
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mechanical{q}_\mu\gamma^\mu
&=\not{\mechanical{q}}\\
&=(\not{\mechanical{p}'}-\mechanical{m})-(\not{\mechanical{p}}-\mechanical{m})\\
&=\signal{S}_F^{-1}(\mechanical{p}')-\signal{S}_F^{-1}(\mechanical{p}),
\end{aligned}
$$
which verifies the identity explicitly.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{q},\mechanical{p},\mechanical{p}'$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Vertex momenta | The transferred, initial, and final four-momenta. |
| $\signal{S}_F$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Renormalized fermion propagator | The propagator with the conventional numerator factor i. |
| $\signal{S}_F^{-1}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Inverse Dirac denominator | The source notation for the propagator denominator with its overall i removed. |
| $\mechanical{m}_R,\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Fermion masses | The renormalized mass and tree-level mass parameter. |
| $\energy{\Sigma}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Fermion self-energy | The correction to the inverse Dirac denominator. |
| $\electric{\alpha}_{\mathrm{em}}$ | $1$ | Fine-structure constant | The QED expansion parameter. |
