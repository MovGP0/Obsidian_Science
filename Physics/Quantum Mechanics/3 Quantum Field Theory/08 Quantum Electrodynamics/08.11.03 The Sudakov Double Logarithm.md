---
title: "The Sudakov Double Logarithm"
---

> [!TLDR]
> At high energy, powers of the coupling can be accompanied by large squared logarithms, called Sudakov logarithms.

At very high energy, $\sqrt{\energy{s}}\gg\mechanical{m}$, the expansion contains **Sudakov logarithms** of the form
$$
\electric{\alpha}_{\mathrm{em}}^n
\ln^{2n}\left(\frac{\energy{s}}{\mechanical{m}^{\,2}}\right).
$$

The large logarithms can make fixed-order perturbation theory inadequate at high energy. Resummation is then required.

The source gives the exclusive electromagnetic form factor in the Sudakov form (1956):
$$
\electric{F}(\energy{s})\sim\electric{F}_0
\exp\left[
-\frac{\electric{\alpha}_{\mathrm{em}}}{4\pi}
\ln^2\frac{|\energy{s}|}{\mechanical{m}^{\,2}}
\right].
$$
For an exclusive cross section with resolution $\energy{\Delta E}\ll\sqrt{\energy{s}}$,
$$
\mechanical{\sigma}_{\mathrm{excl}}(\energy{\Delta E})
\sim\mechanical{\sigma}_0
\exp\left[
-\frac{\electric{\alpha}_{\mathrm{em}}}{\pi}
\ln\frac{\energy{s}}{\mechanical{m}^{\,2}}
\ln\frac{\energy{s}}{(\energy{\Delta E})^2}
\right].
$$
This describes exponential suppression of exclusive states at high energy. As $\sqrt{\energy{s}}\to\infty$, the probability of emitting no soft or collinear photon tends to zero.

> [!note]
> At finite energy resolution, “exclusive” means no resolved real photon. Photons below $\energy{\Delta E}$ remain part of the inclusive unresolved contribution. The source describes this as “without real emission.”

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\energy{s}$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Squared hard energy | The high-energy invariant in the logarithm. |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Fermion mass | The low mass scale compared with the hard energy. |
| $\electric{\alpha}_{\mathrm{em}}$ | $1$ | Fine-structure constant | The QED expansion parameter. |
| $\electric{F},\electric{F}_0$ | $1$ | Electromagnetic form factors | The exclusive form factor and its reference value. |
| $\mechanical{\sigma}_{\mathrm{excl}},\mechanical{\sigma}_0$ | $\unit{MeV^{-2}}$ | Exclusive cross sections | The resolved-photon-veto rate and reference scattering area. |
| $\energy{\Delta E}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Energy resolution | The upper energy of unresolved photon emission. |
