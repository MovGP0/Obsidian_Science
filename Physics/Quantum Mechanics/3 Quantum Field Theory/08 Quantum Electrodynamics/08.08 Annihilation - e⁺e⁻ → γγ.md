---
title: "Annihilation — e⁺e⁻ → γγ"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Electron–positron annihilation into two photons has two crossed diagrams and an identical-photon factor of one half in the integrated cross section.

The process $\mathrm e^+\mathrm e^-\to\gamma\gamma$ is related to Compton scattering by crossing. At tree level, there are two diagrams, in the t and u channels.

> [!note]
> The source calls this the inverse process of Compton scattering. The relation used here is crossing, which changes an external particle between the initial and final states; it is not simply reversal of the same initial and final states.

The final photons are identical bosons. Include a statistical factor $1/2$ in the cross section when the phase-space integral counts both photon permutations. This factor does not belong in the amplitude.

The amplitude is the sum of the contributions in Fig. 8.5:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\signal{\mathcal M}
=-\electric{e}^{\,2}\signal{\epsilon}_1^{*\mu}
\signal{\epsilon}_2^{*\nu}\bar v(\bar{\mechanical{p}})\Biggl[
&\gamma_\nu
\frac{\not{\mechanical{p}}-\not{\mechanical{k}_1}+\mechanical{m}}
{(\mechanical{p}-\mechanical{k}_1)^2-\mechanical{m}^{\,2}}\gamma_\mu\\
&+\gamma_\mu
\frac{\not{\mechanical{p}}-\not{\mechanical{k}_2}+\mechanical{m}}
{(\mechanical{p}-\mechanical{k}_2)^2-\mechanical{m}^{\,2}}\gamma_\nu
\Biggr]u(\mechanical{p}).
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $\mechanical{m}=\mechanical{m}_e$. In the massless limit,
$$
\overline{|\signal{\mathcal M}|^2}_{\mathrm e^+\mathrm e^-\to\gamma\gamma}
=2\electric{e}^{\,4}
\left(\frac{\mechanical{u}}{\mechanical{t}}+\frac{\mechanical{t}}{\mechanical{u}}\right).
\tag{8.15}
$$

![[QFT-08-05-Two-Photon-Annihilation.svg|**Fig. 8.5** Tree-level electron–positron annihilation into two photons: t-channel and u-channel electron exchange. The final photons are identical bosons, so the integrated cross section requires the statistical factor $1/2!$.]]

Here
$$
\mechanical{t}=(\mechanical{p}-\mechanical{k}_1)^2,\qquad
\mechanical{u}=(\mechanical{p}-\mechanical{k}_2)^2.
$$
In the physical annihilation region, $\energy{s}>0$ and $\mechanical{t},\mechanical{u}<0$. Thus $\mechanical{t}/\mechanical{u}+\mechanical{u}/\mechanical{t}>0$, and the squared amplitude is positive. The source relates the overall sign change relative to the massless Compton result to the fermionic crossing sign and the completeness relation
$$
\sum_r v^r\bar v^r=\not{\mechanical{p}}-\mechanical{m}.
$$

For $\sqrt{\energy{s}}\gg\mechanical{m}_e$, integration over solid angle with the identical-photon factor $1/2$ gives
$$
\mechanical{\sigma}(\mathrm e^+\mathrm e^-\to\gamma\gamma)
\simeq\frac{2\pi\electric{\alpha}_{\mathrm{em}}^2}{\energy{s}}
\left[\ln\left(\frac{\energy{s}}{\mechanical{m}_e^2}\right)-1\right].
$$
At high energy, the cross section therefore falls as $\ln(\energy{s})/\energy{s}$, with a fixed reference scale understood in the logarithm.

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{\mathcal M}$ | $1$ | Invariant amplitude | The sum of the two photon-production amplitudes. |

| $\signal{\epsilon}_1^{*\mu},\signal{\epsilon}_2^{*\nu}$ | $1$ | Photon polarizations | The conjugate polarization vectors of the final photons. |

| $u,\bar v$ | $\unit{MeV^{1/2}}$ | Initial spinors | The electron and positron mode factors. |

| $\mechanical{\sigma}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-2}}$ | Annihilation cross section | The integrated two-photon production area. |
