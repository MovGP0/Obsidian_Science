> [!TLDR]
> Work through the chapter's exercises with the question, source solution, and correction notes together in each linked article.

Use Exercises 2.1–2.3 to connect variation, constraints, and conserved currents before reading the supplied solutions. For Proca theory, predict what changes when the mass is zero. For the scalar multiplet, track the generator sign through one explicit charge bracket. For Maxwell theory, compare the canonical tensor with a gauge-invariant energy density.

Exercises 2.4–2.6 test boundaries and identities: scaling needs finite energy, the Chern–Simons gauge variation leaves a surface term, and electromagnetic duality in the stated form needs vacuum equations. Exercises 2.7–2.8 then test algebra and canonical conventions. After each solution, state which assumption would fail first if the problem were changed.
