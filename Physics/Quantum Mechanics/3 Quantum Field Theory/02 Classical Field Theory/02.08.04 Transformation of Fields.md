---
title: "Transformation of Fields"
---

> [!TLDR]
> A field transforms through its Lorentz representation together with a change in its spacetime argument.

For a field in representation $D(\Lambda)$,

$$
\signal{\phi}'^{a}(\mechanical{x}')=D(\Lambda)^{a}{}_{b}\signal{\phi}^{b}(\mechanical{x}),\qquad
\mechanical{x}'^{\mu}=\Lambda^{\mu}{}_{\nu}\mechanical{x}^{\nu}. \tag{2.67}
$$

For $\Lambda=1+\omega$,

$$
\delta\signal{\phi}^{a}=-\frac{i}{2}\omega_{\mu\nu}(S^{\mu\nu})^{a}{}_{b}\signal{\phi}^{b}-\omega^{\mu}{}_{\nu}\mechanical{x}^{\nu}\partial_{\mu}\signal{\phi}^{a}. \tag{2.68}
$$

The matrices $S^{\mu\nu}$ are the representation generators. For a scalar they vanish. For a vector, $(S^{\mu\nu})^{\rho}{}_{\sigma}=i(g^{\mu\rho}\delta^{\nu}{}_{\sigma}-g^{\nu\rho}\delta^{\mu}{}_{\sigma})$. For a Dirac spinor, $S^{\mu\nu}=\tfrac{i}{4}[\gamma^{\mu},\gamma^{\nu}]$, where $\{\gamma^{\mu},\gamma^{\nu}\}=2g^{\mu\nu}$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{\phi}^{a}$ | — | Fields | The components of the selected Lorentz representation. |
| $\mechanical{x}^{\mu},\mechanical{x}'^{\mu}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Spacetime positions | The original and transformed field coordinates. |
