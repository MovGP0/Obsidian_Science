title: "The Real Scalar Klein–Gordon Field"
---

> [!TLDR]
> A real scalar field with a quartic interaction is a basic renormalizable field model in four spacetime dimensions.

A real scalar field $\signal{\phi}(\mechanical{x})\in\mathbb R$ is the simplest relativistic field. Requiring a Lorentz-scalar Lagrangian that is at most quadratic in derivatives, invariant under $\signal{\phi}\to-\signal{\phi}$, and renormalizable in four dimensions gives

$$
\energy{\mathcal L}=\frac12(\partial_{\mu}\signal{\phi})(\partial^{\mu}\signal{\phi})-\frac12\mechanical{m}^{2}\signal{\phi}^{2}-\frac{\lambda}{4!}\signal{\phi}^{4}. \tag{2.44}
$$

Renormalizability is discussed in Chapter 9.

> [!note]
> The source calls the field-sign change parity. This is a field-sign symmetry; its relation to a spacetime parity transformation needs a separate distinction.

With signature $(+,-,-,-)$, the derivative contraction is $\signal{\dot\phi}^{2}-|\nabla\signal{\phi}|^{2}$. The quadratic term has a minus sign in the Lagrangian and a positive sign in the potential; for positive mass squared it gives oscillatory free-field behavior. The quartic term introduces scalar self-interaction.

> [!note]
> The source calls the contracted kinetic term positive definite. The displayed difference of squares is not positive definite; the free Hamiltonian density is nonnegative for nonnegative mass squared.

For $\lambda=0$, the equation of motion is

$$
(\square+\mechanical{m}^{2})\signal{\phi}=0. \tag{2.45}
$$

The general solution is a plane-wave superposition:

$$
\signal{\phi}(\mechanical{x})=\int\frac{d^{3}\mechanical{k}}{(2\pi)^{3}2\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k}}\left[\signal{a}(\mechanical{\mathbf k})e^{-i\mechanical{k}\cdot\mechanical{x}}+\signal{a}^{*}(\mechanical{\mathbf k})e^{+i\mechanical{k}\cdot\mechanical{x}}\right], \tag{2.46}
$$

where $\mechanical{k}^{\mu}=(\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k},\mechanical{\mathbf k})$ and

$$
\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k}=\sqrt{|\mechanical{\mathbf k}|^{2}+\mechanical{m}^{2}}. \tag{2.47}
$$

This is the relativistic relation $\energy{E}^{2}=|\mechanical{\mathbf p}|^{2}+\mechanical{m}^{2}$. The normalization makes the measure Lorentz invariant:

$$
\frac{d^{3}\mechanical{k}}{2\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k}}=d^{4}\mechanical{k}\,\delta(\mechanical{k}^{2}-\mechanical{m}^{2})\theta(\mechanical{k}^{0}). \tag{2.48}
$$

The right side is covariant. The integration region is the positive-energy mass shell, shown in Fig. 2.1.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{\phi}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Real scalar field | The field in the quartic model. |
| $\mechanical{x}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Spacetime position | The field coordinate. |
| $\energy{\mathcal L}$ | $\unit{MeV^{4}}$ | Lagrangian density | The local density of the scalar model. |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Mass | The quadratic-potential mass parameter. |
| $\lambda$ | $1$ | Self-coupling | The quartic interaction strength. |
| $\signal{\dot\phi}$ | $\unit{MeV^{2}}$ | Field time derivative | The temporal part of the kinetic term. |
| $\mechanical{k},\mechanical{\mathbf k},\mechanical{\mathbf p}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Momentum | The four- and three-momentum labels of the modes. |
| $\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k},\energy{E}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Energy | The positive mode energy and dispersion-relation energy. |
| $\signal{a}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Mode amplitude | The classical plane-wave coefficient in the stated normalization. |
