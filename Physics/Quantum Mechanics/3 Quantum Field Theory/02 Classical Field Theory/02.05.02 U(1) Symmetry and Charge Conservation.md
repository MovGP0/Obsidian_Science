---
title: "U(1) Symmetry and Charge Conservation"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Global phase invariance of a complex scalar field gives a conserved charge current.

Consider the complex scalar Lagrangian

$$
\energy{\mathcal L}=(\partial_{\mu}\signal{\phi}^{*})(\partial^{\mu}\signal{\phi})-\mechanical{m}^{2}\signal{\phi}^{*}\signal{\phi}-\lambda(\signal{\phi}^{*}\signal{\phi})^{2}. \tag{2.28}
$$

It is invariant under a global phase change with real constant $\signal{\alpha}$:

$$
\signal{\phi}(\mechanical{x})\to e^{i\signal{\alpha}}\signal{\phi}(\mechanical{x}),\qquad
\signal{\phi}^{*}(\mechanical{x})\to e^{-i\signal{\alpha}}\signal{\phi}^{*}(\mechanical{x}). \tag{2.29}
$$

Choose the infinitesimal parameter as $\signal{\alpha}=-\varepsilon$, so particles carry positive charge in the chosen convention. Then

$$
\Delta\signal{\phi}=-i\signal{\phi},\qquad\Delta\signal{\phi}^{*}=+i\signal{\phi}^{*},\qquad\delta\mechanical{x}^{\mu}=0. \tag{2.30}
$$

Equation (2.26) gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
\electric{J}^{\mu}&=\frac{\partial\energy{\mathcal L}}{\partial(\partial_{\mu}\signal{\phi})}(-i\signal{\phi})+\frac{\partial\energy{\mathcal L}}{\partial(\partial_{\mu}\signal{\phi}^{*})}(i\signal{\phi}^{*})\\
&=(\partial^{\mu}\signal{\phi}^{*})(-i\signal{\phi})+(\partial^{\mu}\signal{\phi})(i\signal{\phi}^{*})\\
&=i[\signal{\phi}^{*}\partial^{\mu}\signal{\phi}-\signal{\phi}\partial^{\mu}\signal{\phi}^{*}].
\end{aligned} \tag{2.31}
$$

This is the Noether current of the $U(1)$ symmetry. Its conserved charge is

$$
\electric{Q}=i\int d^{3}\mechanical{x}\,[\signal{\phi}^{*}\signal{\dot\phi}-\signal{\phi}\signal{\dot\phi}^{*}]. \tag{2.32}
$$

After quantization, this charge is identified with electric charge up to a coupling constant $\electric{e}$. More precisely, it counts particles minus antiparticles. The classical current already contains the structure that becomes explicit after quantization.

## Example 2.7 — Explicit Conservation Check

Calculate

$$
\begin{aligned}
\partial_{\mu}\electric{J}^{\mu}&=i[\partial_{\mu}\signal{\phi}^{*}\partial^{\mu}\signal{\phi}+\signal{\phi}^{*}\square\signal{\phi}-\partial_{\mu}\signal{\phi}\partial^{\mu}\signal{\phi}^{*}-\signal{\phi}\square\signal{\phi}^{*}]\\
&=i[\signal{\phi}^{*}(\square\signal{\phi})-\signal{\phi}(\square\signal{\phi}^{*})].
\end{aligned}
$$

Use $\square\signal{\phi}=-\mechanical{m}^{2}\signal{\phi}-2\lambda(\signal{\phi}^{*}\signal{\phi})\signal{\phi}$ and its conjugate. The terms cancel, giving $\partial_{\mu}\electric{J}^{\mu}=0$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\energy{\mathcal L}$ | $\unit{MeV^{4}}$ | Lagrangian density | The complex scalar dynamical density. |
| $\signal{\phi}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Complex scalar field | The field transformed by a global phase. |
| $\signal{\dot\phi}$ | $\unit{MeV^{2}}$ | Field time derivative | The time derivative in the charge density. |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Mass | The scalar-particle mass parameter. |
| $\lambda$ | $1$ | Self-coupling | The quartic interaction strength. |
| $\signal{\alpha}$ | $1$ | Phase angle | The constant global phase parameter. |
| $\mechanical{x}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Spacetime position | The field and spatial integration coordinate. |
| $\electric{J}^{\mu}$ | $\unit{MeV^{3}}$ | Charge current | The conserved current of global phase invariance. |
| $\electric{Q}$ | $1$ | Charge | The conserved charge in the chosen unit-charge convention. |
| $\electric{e}$ | $1$ | Charge coupling | The factor that converts unit charge to electric charge. |
