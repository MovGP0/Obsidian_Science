---
title: "Rotations and Angular Momentum"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Lorentz invariance gives conserved angular-momentum currents with orbital and spin contributions.

Under $\mechanical{x}^{\mu}\to\mechanical{x}^{\mu}+\omega^{\mu}{}_{\nu}\mechanical{x}^{\nu}$, with $\omega_{\mu\nu}=-\omega_{\nu\mu}$, fields transform according to their Lorentz representation. A scalar has $\Delta\signal{\phi}=0$, and a vector has $\Delta\signal{A}^{\mu}=-\omega^{\mu}{}_{\nu}\signal{A}^{\nu}$. For a field of spin $\mechanical{s}$, the variation contains a spin matrix $\Sigma$.

The Noether current is the angular-momentum tensor:

$$
\mechanical{\mathcal M}^{\mu\nu\rho}=\energy{T}^{\mu\nu}\mechanical{x}^{\rho}-\energy{T}^{\mu\rho}\mechanical{x}^{\nu}+\mechanical{S}^{\mu\nu\rho}. \tag{2.37}
$$

The last term is the spin contribution. For a scalar field it vanishes, leaving only orbital angular momentum. The six conserved quantities $\mechanical{M}^{\nu\rho}=\int d^{3}\mechanical{x}\,\mechanical{\mathcal M}^{0\nu\rho}$ generate Lorentz transformations.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{x}^{\mu}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Spacetime position | The coordinate in the Lorentz transformation. |
| $\signal{\phi}$ | — | Scalar field | The field with no intrinsic spin contribution. |
| $\signal{A}^{\mu}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Vector field | The field in the vector Lorentz representation. |
| $\mechanical{s}$ | $1$ | Spin | The intrinsic spin in natural units. |
| $\energy{T}^{\mu\nu}$ | $\unit{MeV^{4}}$ | Energy–momentum tensor | The tensor that supplies the orbital contribution. |
| $\mechanical{\mathcal M}^{\mu\nu\rho},\mechanical{S}^{\mu\nu\rho}$ | $\unit{MeV^{3}}$ | Angular-momentum currents | The total and spin angular-momentum densities and fluxes. |
| $\mechanical{M}^{\nu\rho}$ | $1$ | Lorentz charges | The integrated rotation and boost generators. |
