---
title: "Algebraic Structure of the Lorentz Group"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The Lorentz group preserves the Minkowski scalar product and has six independent infinitesimal generators.

The Lorentz group $O(1,3)$ consists of linear Minkowski-spacetime transformations preserving the scalar product:

$$
\mechanical{x}'^{\mu}\mechanical{x}'_{\mu}=\mechanical{x}^{\mu}\mechanical{x}_{\mu},\qquad
\Lambda^{\mu}{}_{\rho}\Lambda^{\nu}{}_{\sigma}g_{\mu\nu}=g_{\rho\sigma}. \tag{2.61}
$$

The proper orthochronous group $SO^{+}(1,3)$, with $\det\Lambda=+1$ and $\Lambda^{0}{}_{0}\ge1$, is the component connected to the identity. It describes continuous physical Lorentz transformations.

Write $\Lambda^{\mu}{}_{\nu}=\delta^{\mu}{}_{\nu}+\omega^{\mu}{}_{\nu}$, with $\omega_{\mu\nu}=g_{\mu\rho}\omega^{\rho}{}_{\nu}=-\omega_{\nu\mu}$. There are $4\times3/2=6$ independent parameters. The six antisymmetric generators satisfy

$$
[M^{\mu\nu},M^{\rho\sigma}]=i(g^{\nu\rho}M^{\mu\sigma}-g^{\mu\rho}M^{\nu\sigma}-g^{\nu\sigma}M^{\mu\rho}+g^{\mu\sigma}M^{\nu\rho}). \tag{2.62}
$$
