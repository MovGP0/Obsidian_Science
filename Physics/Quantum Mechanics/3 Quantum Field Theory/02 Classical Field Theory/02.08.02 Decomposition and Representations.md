---
title: "Decomposition and Representations"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Rotation and boost generators organize the six-dimensional Lorentz algebra.

Separate the generators into rotations $J^{i}=\tfrac12\varepsilon^{ijk}M_{jk}$ and boosts $K^{i}=M^{0i}$. Their commutation relations are

$$
[J^{i},J^{j}]=i\varepsilon^{ijk}J^{k}, \tag{2.63}
$$

$$
[J^{i},K^{j}]=i\varepsilon^{ijk}K^{k}, \tag{2.64}
$$

$$
[K^{i},K^{j}]=-i\varepsilon^{ijk}J^{k}. \tag{2.65}
$$

The minus sign in the boost commutator reflects the group's noncompact nature, which constrains its unitary representations. Define $A^{i}=\tfrac12(J^{i}+iK^{i})$ and $B^{i}=\tfrac12(J^{i}-iK^{i})$. Then

$$
[A^{i},A^{j}]=i\varepsilon^{ijk}A^{k},\qquad[B^{i},B^{j}]=i\varepsilon^{ijk}B^{k},\qquad[A^{i},B^{j}]=0. \tag{2.66}
$$

Thus $\mathfrak{so}(1,3)_{\mathbb C}\cong\mathfrak{su}(2)_{\mathbb C}\oplus\mathfrak{su}(2)_{\mathbb C}$. Irreducible complex representations are classified by $(j_A,j_B)$, with each label in $\{0,\tfrac12,1,\ldots\}$.
