---
title: "Noether's Theorem"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Continuous symmetries of the action determine conservation laws such as energy, momentum, and electric charge conservation.

Noether's theorem (1918) connects action symmetries with conservation laws. Energy conservation follows from time-translation invariance, electric-charge conservation from $U(1)$ phase invariance, and momentum conservation from spatial homogeneity.
