---
title: "Translations and the Energy–Momentum Tensor"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Spacetime-translation invariance produces the canonical energy–momentum tensor and four conservation equations.

Consider a spacetime translation

$$
\mechanical{x}^{\mu}\to\mechanical{x}^{\mu}+\mechanical{a}^{\mu}. \tag{2.33}
$$

For a translation in direction $\nu$, write $\mechanical{a}^{\mu}=\varepsilon\delta^{\mu}_{\nu}$, so $\delta\mechanical{x}^{\mu}=\delta^{\mu}_{\nu}$. Scalar fields have $\Delta\signal{\phi}^{a}=0$: their functional form does not change. Equation (2.26) gives the associated tensor

$$
\energy{T}^{\mu}{}_{\nu}=\frac{\partial\energy{\mathcal L}}{\partial(\partial_{\mu}\signal{\phi}^{a})}\partial_{\nu}\signal{\phi}^{a}-g^{\mu}{}_{\nu}\energy{\mathcal L}. \tag{2.34}
$$

Raising the second index with the metric gives

$$
\boxed{\energy{T}^{\mu\nu}=\frac{\partial\energy{\mathcal L}}{\partial(\partial_{\mu}\signal{\phi}^{a})}\partial^{\nu}\signal{\phi}^{a}-g^{\mu\nu}\energy{\mathcal L}.} \tag{2.35}
$$

This is the canonical energy–momentum tensor. Its conservation, $\partial_{\mu}\energy{T}^{\mu\nu}=0$, supplies four conservation laws, one for each value $\nu=0,1,2,3$.

For the time component, $\energy{T}^{00}=\mechanical{\pi}^{a}\signal{\dot\phi}^{a}-\energy{\mathcal L}=\energy{\mathcal H}$ is the energy density. For spatial components, $\energy{T}^{0i}=\mechanical{\pi}^{a}\partial^{i}\signal{\phi}^{a}$ is the momentum density. The conserved quantities are

$$
\mechanical{P}^{\nu}=\int d^{3}\mechanical{x}\,\energy{T}^{0\nu}=(\energy{E},\mechanical{\mathbf P}). \tag{2.36}
$$

Thus $\energy{E}=\int d^{3}\mechanical{x}\,\energy{\mathcal H}$ and $\mechanical{P}^{i}=\int d^{3}\mechanical{x}\,\mechanical{\pi}^{a}\partial^{i}\signal{\phi}^{a}$.

## Physical quantities

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{x}^{\mu},\mechanical{a}^{\mu}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Coordinates and displacement | The spacetime point and constant translation. |

| $\energy{\mathcal L}$ | $\unit{MeV^{4}}$ | Lagrangian density | The translation-invariant dynamical density. |
| $\energy{T}^{\mu\nu}$ | $\unit{MeV^{4}}$ | Energy–momentum tensor | The canonical density and flux tensor for translations. |
| $\mechanical{\pi}^{a}$ | — | Conjugate momentum density | The momentum conjugate to each field component. |
| $\signal{\dot\phi}^{a}$ | — | Field time derivative | The time derivative of each field component. |

| $\energy{E}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Energy | The conserved total energy. |
| $\mechanical{P}^{\nu},\mechanical{\mathbf P}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Four-momentum and momentum | The conserved energy–momentum charges. |
