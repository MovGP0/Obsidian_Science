> [!TLDR]
> The renormalization group makes scale dependence calculable and relates running couplings, fixed points, universality, and effective theories.

The main results of this chapter are:

1. **Scale dependence of renormalized parameters is unphysical and unavoidable.** It is not a defect of the theory; the source presents it as an expression of quantum field theory's structure across multiple scales.

2. **The Callan–Symanzik equation** encodes scale dependence. The beta function $\signal{\beta}(\signal{g})$ controls coupling evolution, while the anomalous dimension $\signal{\gamma}(\signal{g})$ changes field scaling.
3. **QED and QCD have different beta-function signs.** In QED, $\signal{\beta}>0$ and the charge grows toward the ultraviolet through screening. In QCD, $\signal{\beta}<0$ and the coupling decreases through antiscreening and asymptotic freedom.
4. **QCD asymptotic freedom** explains approximate Bjorken scaling and supports perturbation theory for high-energy processes. Confinement at $\energy{\mu}\lesssim\energy{\Lambda_{\mathrm{QCD}}}$ is a nonperturbative phenomenon.
5. **The Wilsonian renormalization group** explains universality and EFT structure. Irrelevant operators decrease toward the infrared, suppressing sensitivity to ultraviolet details at low energy.
6. **Dimensional transmutation** replaces the dimensionless strong coupling $\signal{g_s}$ with the scale $\energy{\Lambda_{\mathrm{QCD}}}$. It explains how a nucleon mass scale can arise from quantum, nonperturbative QCD dynamics.
