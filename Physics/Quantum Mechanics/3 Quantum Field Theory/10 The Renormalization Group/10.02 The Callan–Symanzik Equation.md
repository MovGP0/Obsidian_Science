---
title: "The Callan–Symanzik Equation"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The Callan–Symanzik equation relates explicit scale dependence to the running of couplings and the normalization of fields.

The question is how one fixed theory can have many scale-dependent parameter descriptions. Follow [[10.02.01 Derivation from the Independence of Bare Parameters]] to derive the total scale derivative. Then use [[10.02.02 Physical Meaning of the Beta Function and Anomalous Dimension]] to separate coupling evolution from field normalization, and [[10.02.03 Solution of the Callan–Symanzik Equation]] to solve the resulting equation.

Keep two operations separate during this route: changing the subtraction scale at fixed external momenta, and changing all physical momenta. The first gives a renormalization-group identity. The second also needs dimensional analysis. The free propagator, which decreases as inverse momentum squared, is a useful check when moving between these operations and between propagators and amputated vertices.

The derivation starts from the independence of bare quantities from the renormalization scale; see [[10.02.01 Derivation from the Independence of Bare Parameters]].
