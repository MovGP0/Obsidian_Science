---
title: "Coupling Constants of the Standard Model"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The Standard Model has hypercharge, weak, and strong gauge couplings. A conventional hypercharge normalization permits comparison with larger gauge groups.

The Standard Model has three gauge couplings:
$\signal{g_1}$ for hypercharge $U(1)_Y$,
$\signal{g_2}$ for weak isospin $SU(2)_L$, and
$\signal{g_3}=\signal{g_s}$ for color $SU(3)_c$.
At the Z-boson mass scale, the source gives the approximate values
$$
\begin{aligned}
\signal{\alpha_1}(\mechanical{M_Z})
&=\frac53\frac{\electric{\alpha}(\mechanical{M_Z})}
{\cos^2\mechanical{\theta_W}}\approx\frac1{59},\\
\signal{\alpha_2}(\mechanical{M_Z})
&=\frac{\electric{\alpha}(\mechanical{M_Z})}
{\sin^2\mechanical{\theta_W}}\approx\frac1{29},\\
\signal{\alpha_3}(\mechanical{M_Z})&\approx\frac1{8.5}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The factor $5/3$ is a conventional $U(1)$ normalization chosen for compatibility with embeddings into larger groups such as $SU(5)$.

The one-loop Standard Model evolution equations have the form
$$
\energy{\mu}\frac{\partial\signal{\alpha_i}}{\partial\energy{\mu}}
=\frac{b_i\signal{\alpha_i}^2}{2\pi}.
$$
The numerical values above are reproduced from the source.

The coefficients depend on the Standard Model particle content:
$$
\begin{aligned}
b_1&=+\frac{41}{10}\approx+4.1
&&\text{for }U(1)_Y,\\
b_2&=-\frac{19}{6}\approx-3.17
&&\text{for }SU(2)_L,\\
b_3&=-7
&&\text{for }SU(3)_c.
\end{aligned}
$$
The gauge couplings $\signal{g_2}$ and $\signal{g_3}$ are asymptotically free because $b_2,b_3<0$.
The hypercharge coupling $\signal{g_1}$ is not, because $b_1>0$.
These signs use the convention in the preceding coupling evolution equation.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{g_1},\signal{g_2},\signal{g_3},\signal{g_s}$ | $1$ | Standard Model gauge couplings | The hypercharge, weak-isospin, and strong gauge couplings. |
| $\signal{\alpha_1},\signal{\alpha_2},\signal{\alpha_3},\signal{\alpha_i}$ | $1$ | Normalized gauge fine-structure couplings | The couplings compared in the one-loop scale evolution. |
| $\electric{\alpha}$ | $1$ | Electromagnetic fine-structure constant | The electric coupling used in the electroweak matching relations. |
| $\mechanical{\theta_W}$ | $1$ | Weak mixing angle | The angle relating the electromagnetic and electroweak couplings. |
| $\mechanical{M_Z}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Z-boson mass | The reference scale for the source coupling values. |
| $\energy{\mu}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Renormalization scale | The scale in the Standard Model coupling equations. |
