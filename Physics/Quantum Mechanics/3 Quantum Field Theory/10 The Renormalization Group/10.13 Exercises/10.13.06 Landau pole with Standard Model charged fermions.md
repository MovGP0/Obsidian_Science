## Exercise 10.6: Calculation of the Landau pole

For QED with $N_f$ fermion species of relative charges
$\electric{Q_1},\ldots,\electric{Q_{N_f}}$,
use
$$
\electric{\beta}(\electric{e})
=\frac{\electric{e}^3}{12\pi^2}
\sum_i\electric{Q_i}^2.
$$
The source lists the Standard Model charged fermions as:

- The up-type quarks $(u,c,t)$, with charge magnitude $\electric{Q}=2/3$.
- The down-type quarks $(d,s,b)$, with charge magnitude $\electric{Q}=1/3$.
- The charged leptons $(e,\mu,\tau)$, with charge magnitude $\electric{Q}=1$.

Calculate $\sum_i\electric{Q_i}^2$, including the three colors of each quark.
Determine the QED Landau-pole scale in this Standard Model fermion-content approximation.

> [!note]
> The quoted charges are magnitudes, sufficient for the squared-charge sum. This exercise extrapolates a fermion-loop QED running law; at electroweak and higher scales a complete Standard Model treatment instead evolves the electroweak gauge couplings with their matching conditions.

## Solution 10.6 — Landau pole with Standard Model charged fermions

For all three families, including quark color multiplicities,
$$
\sum_fN_{c,f}\electric{Q}_f^2
=3\left[3\left(\frac23\right)^2
+3\left(\frac13\right)^2\right]+3\cdot1^2.
$$
The outer factor three counts families, and the inner factors three count quark colors. The contributions are four from the up-type quarks, one from the down-type quarks, and three from the charged leptons:
$$
\sum_fN_{c,f}\electric{Q}_f^2=4+1+3=8.
$$
The formal all-species-active QED beta function is
$$
\electric{\beta}(\electric{\alpha})
=\frac{2\electric{\alpha}^2}{3\pi}\,8
=\frac{16\electric{\alpha}^2}{3\pi}.
$$
Treating this charge sum as constant gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac1{\electric{\alpha}(\energy{\mu})}
&=\frac1{\electric{\alpha}(\mechanical{m}_e)}
-\frac{16}{3\pi}\ln\frac{\energy{\mu}}{\mechanical{m}_e}\\
&=\frac1{\electric{\alpha}(\mechanical{m}_e)}
-\frac8{3\pi}\ln\frac{\energy{\mu}^2}{\mechanical{m}_e^2}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This equation is the stated formal approximation. Threshold matching is required for a physical evolution from the electron mass, and above electroweak scales the complete gauge theory must be used.

The denominator of the formal eight-unit charge-squared running vanishes at
$$
\energy{\mu}_{\mathrm{Landau}}
=\mechanical{m}_e\exp\left[
\frac{3\pi}{16\electric{\alpha}(\mechanical{m}_e)}
\right].
$$
For $\electric{\alpha}(\mechanical{m}_e)\approx1/137$,
$$
\frac{3\pi\cdot137}{16}\approx80.7,
$$
$$
\energy{\mu}_{\mathrm{Landau}}
\approx\mechanical{m}_ee^{80.7}
\sim5\times10^{-4}\times10^{35}\unit{GeV}
\sim10^{32}\unit{GeV}.
$$
The source notes the large reduction from the electron-only estimate near $10^{277}\unit{GeV}$. Both extrapolations are far beyond the Planck scale and outside the controlled range of the approximation.
