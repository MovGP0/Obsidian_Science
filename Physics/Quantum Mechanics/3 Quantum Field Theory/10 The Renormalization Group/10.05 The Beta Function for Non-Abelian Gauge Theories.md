title: "The Beta Function for Non-Abelian Gauge Theories"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Non-Abelian gauge self-interactions change the sign structure of the beta function and can make the coupling decrease at high energy.

The physical question is why adding self-interacting gauge fields can reverse QED's screening trend. Use [[10.05.01 Calculation of the Beta Function in Yang–Mills Theory]] to separate gauge, ghost, and matter contributions. Continue with [[10.05.02 Asymptotic Freedom]] for the flavor bound, [[10.05.03 Scale Evolution of αₛ in QCD]] for the integrated flow, and [[10.05.04 Interpretation - Antiscreening and the Color of the Vacuum]] for the vacuum-response interpretation.

Track the convention throughout: a positive coefficient b₀ multiplies a minus sign in the beta function. The flavor bound applies to the specified matter representations and active species. Changing either changes the model; changing only the subtraction scale follows the same model's trajectory.

The non-Abelian calculation starts in [[10.05.01 Calculation of the Beta Function in Yang–Mills Theory]].
