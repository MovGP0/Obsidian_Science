---
title: "Confinement and Asymptotic Freedom"
---

> [!TLDR]
> QCD is weakly coupled at high energy and confines color at low energy. Perturbation theory describes the first regime but cannot by itself establish confinement.

QCD has two apparently different regimes:

1. **Asymptotic freedom**, $\energy{\mu}\gg\energy{\Lambda_{\mathrm{QCD}}}$:
   $\signal{\alpha_s}\ll1$. Quarks behave approximately as free particles and perturbation theory applies.
2. **Confinement**, $\energy{\mu}\sim\energy{\Lambda_{\mathrm{QCD}}}$:
   $\signal{\alpha_s}\sim1$. Perturbation theory fails. Quarks are not observed as isolated free particles; they occur within hadrons.

The crossover to strong coupling occurs near
$\energy{\mu}\sim\energy{\Lambda_{\mathrm{QCD}}}$.
Confinement cannot be derived directly from ordinary perturbation theory. It requires nonperturbative methods, such as lattice QCD, dual descriptions, or flux-tube models.

The source relates confinement to the one-loop evolution (10.6):
$\signal{\alpha_s}\to\infty$ as
$\energy{\mu}\to\energy{\Lambda_{\mathrm{QCD}}}^{+}$.
It then states that the quark force becomes arbitrarily large at large distances and prevents separation.

> [!note]
> The pole in (10.6) signals failure of the perturbative formula. It does not derive a long-distance force or prove confinement. A linearly rising static-source potential corresponds to an approximately constant force, and dynamical light-quark production can break a flux tube. These distinctions are not represented by simply extrapolating the running coupling to infinity.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\energy{\mu}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Energy scale | The scale distinguishing the weak- and strong-coupling regimes. |
| $\energy{\Lambda_{\mathrm{QCD}}}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | QCD scale | The characteristic scale where the perturbative running becomes unreliable. |
| $\signal{\alpha_s}$ | $1$ | Strong coupling | The coupling small in the ultraviolet and large near the hadronic regime. |
