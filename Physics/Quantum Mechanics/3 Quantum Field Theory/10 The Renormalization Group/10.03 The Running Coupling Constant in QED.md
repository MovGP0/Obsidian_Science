title: "The Running Coupling Constant in QED"
---

> [!TLDR]
> In QED, vacuum polarization makes the effective electromagnetic coupling increase with energy.

This section connects a loop correction to a physical comparison between probes at different momentum scales. Start with [[10.03.01 The Beta Function of Quantum Electrodynamics]] for the Ward identity and charged-species counting. Then read [[10.03.02 Scale Evolution of α(μ) and Charge Screening]] for the integrated result, the source benchmark, and the limits of the Landau-pole extrapolation.

Before using a quoted coupling, identify its subtraction convention, reference scale, and active charged particles. The electron-only formula isolates the screening mechanism; the source's electroweak-scale benchmark includes more physics. Agreement between them is therefore not the expected check. The useful check is that both describe increasing electromagnetic coupling over their stated range.

The scale evolution of the QED coupling follows from its beta function. The calculation is given in [[10.03.01 The Beta Function of Quantum Electrodynamics]].
