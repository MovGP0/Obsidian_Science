---
title: "Dimensional Transmutation and Λ_QCD"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Quantum running can replace a dimensionless coupling by a physical energy scale, Λ_QCD.

The central question is how a mass scale can emerge from a classical massless theory with a dimensionless coupling. Read [[10.07.01 A Theory without a Scale Generates a Scale]] for the integration constant of the RG equation. Then read [[10.07.02 The Scale Anomaly and the Energy–Momentum Tensor]] for its expression in the quantum trace relation.

Keep the generated strong-interaction scale separate from a prediction for a particular hadron mass. Dimensional transmutation supplies the scale; dimensionless mass ratios require the dynamics of the theory, usually by nonperturbative calculation. The trace relation also uses a hadron-state matrix element when discussing a hadron mass, rather than the vacuum matrix element.

Dimensional transmutation is introduced in [[10.07.01 A Theory without a Scale Generates a Scale]].
