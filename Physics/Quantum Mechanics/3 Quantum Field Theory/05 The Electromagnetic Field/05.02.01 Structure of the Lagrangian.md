---
title: "Structure of the Lagrangian"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The Maxwell Lagrangian is the difference of electric and magnetic energy densities; its dual contraction is a total derivative.

Use (5.2)–(5.3) to express the field-tensor contraction in terms of electric and magnetic fields:
$$
\electric{F}_{\mu\nu}\electric{F}^{\mu\nu}
=-2(\electric{\mathbf E}^{\,2}-\magnetic{\mathbf B}^{\,2})
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\energy{\mathcal L}_{\mathrm{Maxwell}}
=\frac12(\electric{\mathbf E}^{\,2}-\magnetic{\mathbf B}^{\,2}).
$$
This is the difference between the electric and magnetic energy densities.

The other quadratic invariant is the pseudoscalar
$$
\electric{F}_{\mu\nu}\tilde{\electric{F}}^{\mu\nu}
=-4\electric{\mathbf E}\cdot\magnetic{\mathbf B}.
$$
For the Abelian field with a constant coefficient, it is a total derivative, proportional to $\partial_\mu K^\mu$ for a suitable current $K^\mu$. It does not change the bulk equations of motion.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\electric{F}_{\mu\nu},\tilde{\electric{F}}^{\mu\nu}$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Field tensors | The electromagnetic tensor and its dual. |
| $\electric{\mathbf E}$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Electric field | The electric part of the field strength. |
| $\magnetic{\mathbf B}$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Magnetic field | The magnetic part of the field strength. |
| $\energy{\mathcal L}_{\mathrm{Maxwell}}$ | $\unit{MeV^4}$ | Lagrangian density | The difference of electric and magnetic energy densities. |
