---
title: "Discussion of the Ward Identity"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Ward identities express gauge invariance through relations among current insertions and Green functions.

The Ward identity follows directly from gauge invariance. In perturbation theory, it becomes a relation among Green functions that is tied to conservation of the Noether current.

The source gives the current-insertion identity
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{\partial}{\partial\mechanical{x}^\mu}
\langle0|\mathcal T\{\electric{J}^\mu(\mechanical{x})
\signal{\phi}_1(\mechanical{y}_1)\cdots\signal{\phi}_n(\mechanical{y}_n)\}|0\rangle
={}&\sum_{i=1}^{n}\delta^{(4)}(\mechanical{x}-\mechanical{y}_i)\\
&\times\langle0|\mathcal T\{\signal{\phi}_1(\mechanical{y}_1)\cdots
\delta\signal{\phi}_i(\mechanical{y}_i)\cdots
\signal{\phi}_n(\mechanical{y}_n)\}|0\rangle .
\end{aligned} \tag{5.25}
$$
Here $\delta\signal{\phi}_i$ is the variation of the inserted field under the symmetry. The normalization and factors of $i$ depend on the convention for that variation and for the current.

For local gauge invariance, these identities apply to the full set of Green functions and constrain physical amplitudes.

> [!note] Remark 5.6 — Slavnov–Taylor identities
> In non-Abelian gauge theories, the corresponding relations are the Slavnov–Taylor identities. They also involve the Faddeev–Popov ghost fields. Chapter 12 treats them.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\electric{J}^\mu$ | $\unit{MeV^3}$ | Noether current | The internal-symmetry current inserted in the Green function. |
| $\signal{\phi}_i,\delta\signal{\phi}_i$ | — | Inserted fields and variations | Their units depend on the type of field. |
| $\mechanical{x},\mechanical{y}_i$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Spacetime points | The current and field insertion coordinates. |
