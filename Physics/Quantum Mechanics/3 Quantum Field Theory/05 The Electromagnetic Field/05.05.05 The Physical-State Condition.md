---
title: "The Physical-State Condition"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The Gupta–Bleuler condition imposes only the positive-frequency Lorenz condition on physical states.

Classical Lorenz gauge is $\partial_\mu\electric{A}^\mu=0$. It cannot be imposed as a zero operator on the full covariant Fock space because the divergence is not identically zero there.

Gupta (1950) and Bleuler (1950) instead imposed the weaker condition
$$
\boxed{\partial^\mu\electric{A}_\mu^{(+)}(\mechanical{x})|\mathrm{phys}\rangle=0}. \tag{5.19}
$$
The positive-frequency part $\electric{A}_\mu^{(+)}$ contains the terms $e^{-i\mechanical{k}\cdot\mechanical{x}}$ and the annihilation operators $a_{\mathbf k}^{(\lambda)}$.

In momentum space, condition (5.19) is
$$
\mechanical{k}^\mu a_{\mu,\mathbf k}|\mathrm{phys}\rangle=0,
\qquad
a_{\mu,\mathbf k}=\sum_\lambda\signal{\epsilon}_\mu^{(\lambda)}a_{\mathbf k}^{(\lambda)}.
$$
For $\mechanical{\mathbf k}=\signal{\omega}_{\mathbf k}\hat{\mathbf z}$, one has $\mechanical{k}^\mu=\signal{\omega}_{\mathbf k}(1,0,0,1)$. The condition becomes
$$
(a_{\mathbf k}^{(0)}-a_{\mathbf k}^{(3)})|\mathrm{phys}\rangle=0. \tag{5.20}
$$
Scalar and longitudinal modes occur in a combination whose contributions cancel in physical matrix elements.

## Nonnegative physical norms

Write a physical state as
$$
|\mathrm{phys}\rangle=|\mathrm{transv}\rangle+|\chi\rangle,
$$
where $|\chi\rangle$ contains the unphysical modes in the combination selected by (5.20). Then
$$
\langle\mathrm{phys}|\mathrm{phys}\rangle
=\langle\mathrm{transv}|\mathrm{transv}\rangle\ge0.
$$
The unphysical contribution is null under the Gupta–Bleuler condition. The physical Hilbert space is the quotient by these null states. The physical S-matrix is unitary when the dynamics preserves this state condition. Figure 5.2 shows the resulting structure.

### Example 5.4 — Residual gauge invariance

The states $|\mathrm{phys}\rangle$ and $|\mathrm{phys}\rangle+|\chi\rangle$ are physically indistinguishable when $|\chi\rangle$ is a null state in the physical subspace: $\langle\chi|\chi\rangle=0$. Their physical matrix elements agree. This is the quantum counterpart of residual gauge freedom in Lorenz gauge.

> [!note]
> A zero self-norm in an arbitrary indefinite inner-product space is not enough to identify a physically null state. Here the null states belong to the constrained physical subspace and are orthogonal to that subspace; the quotient removes this null sector.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\electric{A}^\mu,\electric{A}_\mu^{(+)}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Photon fields | The covariant potential and its positive-frequency part. |
| $\mechanical{x}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Spacetime point | The point where the physical-state condition is applied. |
| $\mechanical{k}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Photon four-momentum | The momentum in the positive-frequency phase. |
| $\signal{\epsilon}_\mu^{(\lambda)}$ | $1$ | Polarization basis | The covariant polarization vectors used to form the mode operator. |
| $\signal{\omega}_{\mathbf k}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Mode frequency | The positive frequency for a photon along the z axis. |
