## Exercise 5.5 — Gupta–Bleuler condition and physical states

Consider
$$
|\Psi\rangle=
\left(
c_{11}a_{\mathbf k}^{(1)\dagger}a_{\mathbf k'}^{(1)\dagger}
+c_{33}a_{\mathbf k}^{(3)\dagger}a_{\mathbf k'}^{(3)\dagger}
+c_{03}a_{\mathbf k}^{(0)\dagger}a_{\mathbf k'}^{(3)\dagger}
\right)|0\rangle.
$$
Apply the Gupta–Bleuler condition and determine the constraints on $c_{11},c_{33},c_{03}$. Calculate the resulting norm and verify that it is nonnegative.

The constraint must hold for every test momentum, not only for one contraction chosen to cancel a particular coefficient. Use discrete orthonormal modes or normalized packets to check norms without products of delta distributions. Before calculating, note that the proposed ansatz omits scalar–scalar and one mixed ordering; it need not contain a nontrivial physical null combination.

## Solution 5.5 — Gupta–Bleuler condition and physical states

The proposed state is
$$
|\Psi\rangle=
\left(
c_{11}a_{\mathbf k}^{(1)\dagger}a_{\mathbf k'}^{(1)\dagger}
+c_{33}a_{\mathbf k}^{(3)\dagger}a_{\mathbf k'}^{(3)\dagger}
+c_{03}a_{\mathbf k}^{(0)\dagger}a_{\mathbf k'}^{(3)\dagger}
\right)|0\rangle.
$$
For momentum along the chosen longitudinal direction, the Gupta–Bleuler condition is
$$
(a_{\mathbf q}^{(0)}-a_{\mathbf q}^{(3)})|\Psi\rangle=0
\qquad\text{for every }\mechanical{\mathbf q}.
$$
The annihilation operators commute with the transverse creation operators $a^{(1)\dagger}$ and annihilate the vacuum. Thus the term proportional to $c_{11}$ gives no contribution.

The longitudinal-mode commutator is
$$
[a_{\mathbf q}^{(3)},a_{\mathbf k}^{(3)\dagger}]
=(2\pi)^3\delta^{(3)}(\mechanical{\mathbf q}-\mechanical{\mathbf k}),
$$
while the scalar-mode commutator has the opposite sign:
$$
[a_{\mathbf q}^{(0)},a_{\mathbf k}^{(0)\dagger}]
=-(2\pi)^3\delta^{(3)}(\mechanical{\mathbf q}-\mechanical{\mathbf k}).
$$
Different polarizations commute. Thus
$$
\begin{aligned}
a_{\mathbf q}^{(3)}
a_{\mathbf k}^{(3)\dagger}a_{\mathbf k'}^{(3)\dagger}|0\rangle
=(2\pi)^3\Bigl[
&\delta^{(3)}(\mechanical{\mathbf q}-\mechanical{\mathbf k})a_{\mathbf k'}^{(3)\dagger}\\
+&\delta^{(3)}(\mechanical{\mathbf q}-\mechanical{\mathbf k}')a_{\mathbf k}^{(3)\dagger}
\Bigr]|0\rangle,
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
a_{\mathbf q}^{(0)}
a_{\mathbf k}^{(0)\dagger}a_{\mathbf k'}^{(3)\dagger}|0\rangle
=-(2\pi)^3\delta^{(3)}(\mechanical{\mathbf q}-\mechanical{\mathbf k})
a_{\mathbf k'}^{(3)\dagger}|0\rangle,
$$
$$
a_{\mathbf q}^{(3)}
a_{\mathbf k}^{(0)\dagger}a_{\mathbf k'}^{(3)\dagger}|0\rangle
=(2\pi)^3\delta^{(3)}(\mechanical{\mathbf q}-\mechanical{\mathbf k}')
a_{\mathbf k}^{(0)\dagger}|0\rangle.
$$
For distinct momenta, apply the Gupta–Bleuler condition to each delta-function coefficient:
$$
\begin{aligned}
(a_{\mathbf q}^{(0)}-a_{\mathbf q}^{(3)})|\Psi\rangle
=-(2\pi)^3\Bigl\{
&\delta^{(3)}(\mechanical{\mathbf q}-\mechanical{\mathbf k})
(c_{33}+c_{03})a_{\mathbf k'}^{(3)\dagger}\\
+&\delta^{(3)}(\mechanical{\mathbf q}-\mechanical{\mathbf k}')
\left(c_{33}a_{\mathbf k}^{(3)\dagger}
+c_{03}a_{\mathbf k}^{(0)\dagger}\right)
\Bigr\}|0\rangle.
\end{aligned}
$$
The scalar and longitudinal one-particle states in the second coefficient are linearly independent. Therefore
$$
c_{33}=c_{03}=0,\qquad c_{11}\ \text{is unrestricted}.
$$
For the stated ansatz the physical state is consequently
$$
|\Psi_{\mathrm{phys}}\rangle
=c_{11}a_{\mathbf k}^{(1)\dagger}a_{\mathbf k'}^{(1)\dagger}|0\rangle.
$$
In a discrete orthonormal mode basis its norm is $|c_{11}|^2$ for distinct modes, or $2|c_{11}|^2$ if the two modes coincide. In either case it is nonnegative. Continuum normalization adds the corresponding delta-function factors.

> [!note] Correction to the source solution
> The source states $c_{03}=-2c_{33}$ and proposes a longitudinal–scalar contribution proportional to $a_{\mathbf k}^{(3)\dagger}a_{\mathbf k'}^{(3)\dagger}-2a_{\mathbf k}^{(0)\dagger}a_{\mathbf k'}^{(3)\dagger}$. The separate coefficients above show that this is not annihilated by $a_{\mathbf q}^{(0)}-a_{\mathbf q}^{(3)}$ for all momenta. Its claimed zero norm is also incorrect: in a discrete orthonormal basis with distinct momenta its norm is $1-4=-3$.
>
> If the momenta coincide, direct application gives $(2c_{33}+c_{03})a^{(3)\dagger}+c_{03}a^{(0)\dagger}=0$, which again requires both coefficients to vanish.
>
> Null physical excitations can instead be built from $a^{(0)\dagger}-a^{(3)\dagger}$. A product of two such factors contains scalar–scalar and both mixed terms that are absent from the exercise's ansatz. The Gupta–Bleuler nonnegative-norm result is therefore consistent; the source's proposed coefficient relation is not.

The physical conclusion is specific to the stated ansatz: only its transverse term survives. It does not say that the Gupta–Bleuler subspace contains no null excitations. As a normalization check, two distinct orthonormal transverse modes give unit norm before multiplication by the coefficient, while two creations in one mode give the Bose factor two. A negative norm for a proposed nonzero unphysical remainder is a diagnostic that the state condition was not fully imposed.
