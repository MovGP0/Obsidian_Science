## Exercise 5.8 — Explicit Ward identity

In tree-level QED, consider Compton scattering
$$
\gamma(\mechanical{k})+\mathrm e^-(\mechanical{p})
\to\gamma(\mechanical{k}')+\mathrm e^-(\mechanical{p}').
$$
Calculate the electron-current factors with the external photons and show $\mechanical{k}'_\mu\signal{\mathcal M}^\mu=0$. The index $\mu$ refers to the outgoing photon. Use the equations of motion for $u(\mechanical{p})$ and $\bar u(\mechanical{p}')$.

> [!note]
> The Compton Ward cancellation applies to the complete tree amplitude, including both photon orderings on the electron line. It does not hold for a single Compton diagram on its own.

## Solution 5.8 — Explicit Ward identity for Compton scattering

The two tree diagrams give
$$
\begin{aligned}
i\signal{\mathcal M}_s^\mu
&=(-i\electric{e})^2\bar u(\mechanical{p}')
\not{\signal{\epsilon}}'^{\,*}
\frac{i(\not{\mechanical{p}}+\not{\mechanical{k}}+\mechanical{m}_e)}
{(\mechanical{p}+\mechanical{k})^2-\mechanical{m}_e^2}
\gamma^\mu u(\mechanical{p}),\\
i\signal{\mathcal M}_u^\mu
&=(-i\electric{e})^2\bar u(\mechanical{p}')\gamma^\mu
\frac{i(\not{\mechanical{p}}-\not{\mechanical{k}}'+\mechanical{m}_e)}
{(\mechanical{p}-\mechanical{k}')^2-\mechanical{m}_e^2}
\not{\signal{\epsilon}}'^{\,*}u(\mechanical{p}).
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $\mu$ is the **incoming** photon index, and $\signal{\epsilon}'$ is the outgoing photon polarization. The total amplitude is
$\signal{\mathcal M}^\mu=\signal{\mathcal M}_s^\mu+\signal{\mathcal M}_u^\mu$.

> [!note] Index convention
> The supplied solution leaves the incoming index free and proves $\mechanical{k}_\mu\signal{\mathcal M}^\mu=0$. The exercise statement instead leaves the outgoing index free and asks for the corresponding $\mechanical{k}'$ contraction. Both photon Ward identities apply to the complete two-diagram amplitude. The symbol $\mechanical{m}_e$ here distinguishes the electron mass from the Proca mass used elsewhere in this article.

### s channel

Contracting the incoming vertex gives $\mechanical{k}_\mu\gamma^\mu=\not{\mechanical{k}}$. Write
$$
\not{\mechanical{k}}
=(\not{\mechanical{p}}+\not{\mechanical{k}}-\mechanical{m}_e)
-(\not{\mechanical{p}}-\mechanical{m}_e).
$$
With the common coupling and overall phase suppressed,
$$
\mechanical{k}_\mu\signal{\mathcal M}_s^\mu
\propto
\bar u(\mechanical{p}')\not{\signal{\epsilon}}'^{\,*}
\frac{(\not{\mechanical{p}}+\not{\mechanical{k}}+\mechanical{m}_e)
[(\not{\mechanical{p}}+\not{\mechanical{k}}-\mechanical{m}_e)
-(\not{\mechanical{p}}-\mechanical{m}_e)]}
{(\mechanical{p}+\mechanical{k})^2-\mechanical{m}_e^2}
u(\mechanical{p}).
$$
The first product cancels the denominator:
$$
(\not{\mechanical{p}}+\not{\mechanical{k}}+\mechanical{m}_e)
(\not{\mechanical{p}}+\not{\mechanical{k}}-\mechanical{m}_e)
=(\mechanical{p}+\mechanical{k})^2-\mechanical{m}_e^2.
$$
The second term vanishes by $(\not{\mechanical{p}}-\mechanical{m}_e)u(\mechanical{p})=0$. The reduced result is $+\bar u(\mechanical{p}')\not{\signal{\epsilon}}'^{\,*}u(\mechanical{p})$.

### u channel

The contraction is
$$
\mechanical{k}_\mu\signal{\mathcal M}_u^\mu
\propto
\bar u(\mechanical{p}')\not{\mechanical{k}}
\frac{\not{\mechanical{p}}-\not{\mechanical{k}}'+\mechanical{m}_e}
{(\mechanical{p}-\mechanical{k}')^2-\mechanical{m}_e^2}
\not{\signal{\epsilon}}'^{\,*}u(\mechanical{p}).
$$
Momentum conservation is $\mechanical{p}+\mechanical{k}=\mechanical{p}'+\mechanical{k}'$, so
$$
\mechanical{k}=\mechanical{p}'-(\mechanical{p}-\mechanical{k}').
$$
Using $\bar u(\mechanical{p}')\not{\mechanical{p}}'=\mechanical{m}_e\bar u(\mechanical{p}')$ gives the useful numerator identity
$$
\bar u(\mechanical{p}')\not{\mechanical{k}}
=-\bar u(\mechanical{p}')(\not{\mechanical{p}}-\not{\mechanical{k}}'-\mechanical{m}_e).
$$

> [!note] Correction to the source momentum algebra
> Near the bottom of this page, the source writes $\mechanical{p}'+\mechanical{k}=\mechanical{p}+\mechanical{k}'$ and uses inconsistent rearrangements of $\not{\mechanical{k}}$. The correct conservation law and on-shell reduction are given above.

The two adjacent numerator factors now give
$$
(\not{\mechanical{p}}-\not{\mechanical{k}}'-\mechanical{m}_e)
(\not{\mechanical{p}}-\not{\mechanical{k}}'+\mechanical{m}_e)
=(\mechanical{p}-\mechanical{k}')^2-\mechanical{m}_e^2.
$$
They cancel the denominator in the u channel, leaving the reduced result
$-\bar u(\mechanical{p}')\not{\signal{\epsilon}}'^{\,*}u(\mechanical{p})$.
It cancels the s-channel result:
$$
\mechanical{k}_\mu(\signal{\mathcal M}_s^\mu+\signal{\mathcal M}_u^\mu)=0.
$$
The cancellation requires both diagrams. It follows from the on-shell Dirac equations and conservation of the Dirac vector current.

To match the outgoing-index version in the exercise, contract the outgoing vertex with $\not{\mechanical{k}}'$ instead, while the incoming polarization remains contracted. In the s channel use
$$
\bar u(\mechanical{p}')\not{\mechanical{k}}'
=\bar u(\mechanical{p}')(\not{\mechanical{p}}+\not{\mechanical{k}}-\mechanical{m}_e);
$$
in the u channel use
$$
\not{\mechanical{k}}'u(\mechanical{p})
=-(\not{\mechanical{p}}-\not{\mechanical{k}}'-\mechanical{m}_e)u(\mechanical{p}).
$$
The same two denominator cancellations give equal and opposite reduced terms. Thus the full amplitude with the outgoing index free obeys $\mechanical{k}'_\mu\signal{\mathcal M}^\mu=0$ as requested.
