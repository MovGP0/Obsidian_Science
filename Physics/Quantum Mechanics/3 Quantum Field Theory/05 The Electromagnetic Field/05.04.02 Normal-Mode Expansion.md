---
title: "Normal-Mode Expansion"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The free transverse vector potential obeys a wave equation and can be expanded into photon normal modes.

In Coulomb gauge, the free spatial vector potential satisfies $\Box\magnetic{\mathbf A}=0$. The gauge condition (5.9) removes the derivative term that contains the divergence of the potential.

The plane-wave expansion contains only transverse polarizations:
$$
\magnetic{\mathbf A}(\mechanical{\mathbf x},\signal{t})
=\int\frac{d^3\mechanical{\mathbf k}}{(2\pi)^3}
\frac1{\sqrt{2\signal{\omega}_{\mathbf k}}}
\sum_{\lambda=1}^{2}
\left[
a_{\mathbf k}^{(\lambda)}\signal{\boldsymbol\epsilon}^{(\lambda)}(\mechanical{\mathbf k})e^{-i\mechanical{k}\cdot\mechanical{x}}
+a_{\mathbf k}^{(\lambda)\dagger}\signal{\boldsymbol\epsilon}^{(\lambda)*}(\mechanical{\mathbf k})e^{i\mechanical{k}\cdot\mechanical{x}}
\right].
$$
For a massless photon, $\signal{\omega}_{\mathbf k}=\lvert\mechanical{\mathbf k}\rvert$ and $\mechanical{k}^\mu=(\signal{\omega}_{\mathbf k},\mechanical{\mathbf k})$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\magnetic{\mathbf A}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Vector potential | The free transverse photon field. |
| $\mechanical{\mathbf x},\mechanical{x}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Coordinates | The spatial point and spacetime point in the plane wave. |
| $\signal{t}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Time | The time argument of the field. |
| $\mechanical{k},\mechanical{\mathbf k}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Photon momentum | The null four-momentum and its spatial part. |
| $\signal{\omega}_{\mathbf k}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Mode frequency | The positive photon frequency. |
| $\signal{\boldsymbol\epsilon}^{(\lambda)}$ | $1$ | Polarization vector | A normalized transverse photon polarization. |
