> [!TLDR]
> Photon quantization removes gauge redundancy either before quantization in Coulomb gauge or through the Gupta–Bleuler physical-state condition.

The chapter quantizes the electromagnetic field and treats the constraints introduced by gauge symmetry.

1. The field tensor $\electric{F}^{\mu\nu}=\partial^\mu\electric{A}^\nu-\partial^\nu\electric{A}^\mu$ is invariant under $\electric{A}^\mu\to\electric{A}^\mu+\partial^\mu\signal{\alpha}$. The Maxwell density is $\energy{\mathcal L}=-\frac14\electric{F}_{\mu\nu}\electric{F}^{\mu\nu}$.
2. The momentum $\signal{\pi}^0=\electric{F}^{00}=0$ vanishes identically. The scalar potential is a Lagrange multiplier for Gauss's constraint rather than an independent dynamical variable.
3. **Coulomb gauge** quantizes only two transverse physical polarizations. Its Fock space has positive norm, but Lorentz covariance is not manifest.
4. **Gupta–Bleuler quantization** quantizes all four components. The condition $\partial^\mu\electric{A}^{(+)}_\mu|\mathrm{phys}\rangle=0$ selects the physical subspace. The quotient by null states removes the unphysical sector.
5. The Rξ-gauge photon propagator is
   $$
   \signal{D}_F^{\mu\nu}(\mechanical{k})
   =\frac{-i}{\mechanical{k}^{\,2}+i\epsilon}
   \left[g^{\mu\nu}-(1-\xi)
   \frac{\mechanical{k}^\mu\mechanical{k}^\nu}{\mechanical{k}^{\,2}}\right].
   $$
   The Ward identity makes physical results independent of $\xi$.
6. A massless spin-one photon has two physical helicity states, $+1$ and $-1$. A massive Proca field has three, including a longitudinal state.
