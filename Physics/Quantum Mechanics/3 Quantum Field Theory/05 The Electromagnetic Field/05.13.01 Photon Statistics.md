---
title: "Photon Statistics"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Photons obey Bose statistics, consistent with their integer spin.

The photon is a boson. Creation and annihilation operators for its physical transverse modes obey the bosonic relations (5.12). This agrees with the spin–statistics theorem because the photon has spin one.

Multiphoton states are symmetric under exchange. This property underlies coherence phenomena in quantum optics.
