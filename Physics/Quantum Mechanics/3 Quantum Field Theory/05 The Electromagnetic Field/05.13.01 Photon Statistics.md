---
title: "Photon Statistics"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Photons obey Bose statistics, consistent with their integer spin.

The photon is a boson. Creation and annihilation operators for its physical transverse modes obey the bosonic relations (5.12). This agrees with the spin–statistics theorem because the photon has spin one.

Multiphoton states are symmetric under exchange. To check this directly, create two physical photon wave packets in either order: the creation operators commute, so the resulting state is unchanged. Two photons can also occupy the same normalized mode; there is no Pauli exclusion restriction. In a continuum expansion, form normalized packets or discrete box modes before interpreting a mode occupation as an integer.

Bose statistics supports quantum-optical interference, but it does not make every photon state coherent. A number state, a thermal state, and a coherent state can all obey the same bosonic commutators while giving different field and photon-counting statistics. The spin–statistics statement assumes the relativistic local quantum-field framework with a positive physical state space; the auxiliary negative-norm photon sector is not an exception for observable photons.
