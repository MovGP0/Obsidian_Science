---
title: "The Modified Conjugate Momentum"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Covariant gauge fixing makes the momentum conjugate to the scalar potential nonzero and permits a manifestly covariant canonical relation.

For the gauge-fixed density (5.13), the conjugate momentum is
$$
\signal{\pi}^\mu
=\frac{\partial\energy{\mathcal L}}{\partial(\partial_0\electric{A}_\mu)}
=\electric{F}^{\mu0}
-\frac{g^{\mu0}}{\xi}(\partial_\nu\electric{A}^\nu).
$$
Its time component is $\signal{\pi}^0=-(1/\xi)\partial_\nu\electric{A}^\nu$, which no longer vanishes identically. Gauge fixing has made $\electric{A}^0$ a dynamical variable in the enlarged theory.

One can impose the manifestly covariant relation
$$
[\electric{A}^\mu(\mechanical{\mathbf x},\signal{t}),
\signal{\pi}^\nu(\mechanical{\mathbf y},\signal{t})]
=ig^{\mu\nu}\delta^{(3)}(\mechanical{\mathbf x}-\mechanical{\mathbf y}). \tag{5.16}
$$
