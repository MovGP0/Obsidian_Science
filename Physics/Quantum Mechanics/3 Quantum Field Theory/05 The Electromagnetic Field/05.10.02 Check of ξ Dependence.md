---
title: "Check of ξ Dependence"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The gauge-dependent photon propagator term vanishes between on-shell electron currents by the Dirac equation.

This check uses external electrons on shell with the same mass and free Dirac spinors. The exchanged photon is generally off shell. Gauge independence here follows from current conservation at the vertices, not from assigning a physical polarization to the internal line.

Consider the $t$-channel photon-exchange contribution to electron–electron scattering:
$$
i\signal{\mathcal M}
=(-i\electric{e})^2
\bar u(\mechanical{p}')\gamma^\mu u(\mechanical{p})\,
\signal{D}_{\mu\nu}^{F}(\mechanical{q})\,
\bar u(\mechanical{k}')\gamma^\nu u(\mechanical{k}),
$$
where $\mechanical{q}^\mu=\mechanical{p}^\mu-\mechanical{p}'^\mu$ is the transferred momentum. With (5.21),
$$
\begin{aligned}
i\signal{\mathcal M}
=\frac{i\electric{e}^{\,2}}{\mechanical{q}^{\,2}+i\epsilon}
\Bigg[
&\bar u(\mechanical{p}')\gamma^\mu u(\mechanical{p})\,
\bar u(\mechanical{k}')\gamma_\mu u(\mechanical{k})\\
&-(1-\xi)\frac{\mechanical{q}_\mu\mechanical{q}_\nu}{\mechanical{q}^{\,2}}\,
\bar u(\mechanical{p}')\gamma^\mu u(\mechanical{p})\,
\bar u(\mechanical{k}')\gamma^\nu u(\mechanical{k})
\Bigg].
\end{aligned}
$$
Use $\not{\mechanical{p}}u(\mechanical{p})=\mechanical{m}u(\mechanical{p})$ and $\bar u(\mechanical{p}')\not{\mechanical{p}}'=\mechanical{m}\bar u(\mechanical{p}')$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mechanical{q}_\mu\bar u(\mechanical{p}')\gamma^\mu u(\mechanical{p})
&=\bar u(\mechanical{p}')(\not{\mechanical{p}}-\not{\mechanical{p}}')u(\mechanical{p})\\
&=\bar u(\mechanical{p}')(\mechanical{m}-\mechanical{m})u(\mechanical{p})=0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The $\xi$-dependent term therefore vanishes, and this on-shell exchange contribution is independent of the gauge parameter. At the second vertex, momentum conservation gives the corresponding opposite transfer, which changes no zero contraction.

The cancellation is a useful diagnostic: replacing the propagator by its momentum-proportional gauge term must give zero before spin sums are taken. Off-shell external fermions do not obey the free spinor equations used here; their Green functions instead satisfy a Ward identity with inverse-propagator terms. For identical electrons, the full scattering amplitude also contains the exchanged final-state diagram with its fermion sign. The displayed term alone is not the full electron–electron cross section.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{\mathcal M}$ | $1$ | Scattering amplitude | The invariant two-electron scattering amplitude. |
| $\electric{e}$ | $1$ | Electric coupling | The electron-photon coupling. |
| $\mechanical{p},\mechanical{p}',\mechanical{k},\mechanical{k}'$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Electron four-momenta | The initial and final momenta on the two fermion lines. |
