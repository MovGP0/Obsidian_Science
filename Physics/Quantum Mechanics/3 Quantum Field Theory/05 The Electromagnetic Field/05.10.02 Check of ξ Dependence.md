---
title: "Check of ξ Dependence"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The gauge-dependent photon propagator term vanishes between on-shell electron currents by the Dirac equation.

Consider the $t$-channel photon-exchange contribution to electron–electron scattering:
$$
i\signal{\mathcal M}
=(-i\electric{e})^2
\bar u(\mechanical{p}')\gamma^\mu u(\mechanical{p})\,
\signal{D}_{\mu\nu}^{F}(\mechanical{q})\,
\bar u(\mechanical{k}')\gamma^\nu u(\mechanical{k}),
$$
where $\mechanical{q}^\mu=\mechanical{p}^\mu-\mechanical{p}'^\mu$ is the transferred momentum. With (5.21),
$$
\begin{aligned}
i\signal{\mathcal M}
=\frac{i\electric{e}^{\,2}}{\mechanical{q}^{\,2}+i\epsilon}
\Bigg[
&\bar u(\mechanical{p}')\gamma^\mu u(\mechanical{p})\,
\bar u(\mechanical{k}')\gamma_\mu u(\mechanical{k})\\
&-(1-\xi)\frac{\mechanical{q}_\mu\mechanical{q}_\nu}{\mechanical{q}^{\,2}}\,
\bar u(\mechanical{p}')\gamma^\mu u(\mechanical{p})\,
\bar u(\mechanical{k}')\gamma^\nu u(\mechanical{k})
\Bigg].
\end{aligned}
$$
Use $\not{\mechanical{p}}u(\mechanical{p})=\mechanical{m}u(\mechanical{p})$ and $\bar u(\mechanical{p}')\not{\mechanical{p}}'=\mechanical{m}\bar u(\mechanical{p}')$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mechanical{q}_\mu\bar u(\mechanical{p}')\gamma^\mu u(\mechanical{p})
&=\bar u(\mechanical{p}')(\not{\mechanical{p}}-\not{\mechanical{p}}')u(\mechanical{p})\\
&=\bar u(\mechanical{p}')(\mechanical{m}-\mechanical{m})u(\mechanical{p})=0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The $\xi$-dependent term therefore vanishes, and this on-shell exchange contribution is independent of the gauge parameter.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{\mathcal M}$ | $1$ | Scattering amplitude | The invariant two-electron scattering amplitude. |
