---
title: "Expansion in Four Polarizations"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Feynman gauge expands the photon field into a timelike, a longitudinal, and two transverse polarization modes.

Equation (5.15) gives the same formal wave solution for each component. In Feynman gauge, $\xi=1$,
$$
\electric{A}^\mu(\mechanical{x})
=\int\frac{d^3\mechanical{\mathbf k}}{(2\pi)^3}
\frac1{\sqrt{2\signal{\omega}_{\mathbf k}}}
\sum_{\lambda=0}^{3}
\left[
a_{\mathbf k}^{(\lambda)}\signal{\epsilon}^{\mu(\lambda)}(\mechanical{\mathbf k})e^{-i\mechanical{k}\cdot\mechanical{x}}
+a_{\mathbf k}^{(\lambda)\dagger}\signal{\epsilon}^{\mu(\lambda)*}(\mechanical{\mathbf k})e^{i\mechanical{k}\cdot\mechanical{x}}
\right].
$$
Here $\signal{\omega}_{\mathbf k}=\lvert\mechanical{\mathbf k}\rvert$. The four polarization vectors form a basis of Minkowski space.

For a photon moving along $z$, a convenient choice is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\signal{\epsilon}^{\mu(0)}&=(1,0,0,0),&&\text{scalar or timelike},\\
\signal{\epsilon}^{\mu(1)}&=(0,1,0,0),&&\text{transverse},\\
\signal{\epsilon}^{\mu(2)}&=(0,0,1,0),&&\text{transverse},\\
\signal{\epsilon}^{\mu(3)}&=(0,0,0,1),&&\text{longitudinal}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The transverse modes $\lambda=1,2$ are physical and satisfy $\signal{\epsilon}^{(\lambda)}\cdot\mechanical{k}=0$ with the Minkowski metric.

The completeness relation is
$$
\sum_{\lambda=0}^{3}g_{\lambda\lambda}
\signal{\epsilon}^{\mu(\lambda)}
\signal{\epsilon}^{\nu(\lambda)}
=g^{\mu\nu},
$$
where $g_{00}=+1$ and $g_{\lambda\lambda}=-1$ for $\lambda=1,2,3$. Thus the spatial modes and the timelike mode have opposite metric signs. In the mode commutators, this gives a negative sign for the timelike mode.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\electric{A}^\mu$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Four-potential | The covariantly quantized photon field. |
| $\mechanical{x}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Spacetime point | The coordinate in the plane-wave expansion. |
| $\mechanical{k},\mechanical{\mathbf k}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Photon momentum | The null four-momentum and its spatial part. |
| $\signal{\omega}_{\mathbf k}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Mode frequency | The positive photon frequency. |
| $\signal{\epsilon}^{\mu(\lambda)}$ | $1$ | Polarization vectors | The normalized Minkowski-space basis vectors. |
