---
title: "Feynman Rules for QED"
---

> [!TLDR]
> QED Feynman rules assign a propagator to each internal line and a coupling factor to each vertex.

Table 5.2 collects the QED rules in the Rξ gauge for use in later chapters. Each line and vertex contributes a factor to the transition amplitude. Figure 5.4 shows tree-level Compton scattering as an example.

**Table 5.2** Partial QED Feynman rules. Chapter 7 develops the full derivation.

| Element | Expression | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Photon propagator | $\displaystyle\frac{-i}{\mechanical{k}^{\,2}+i\epsilon}\left[g^{\mu\nu}-(1-\xi)\frac{\mechanical{k}^\mu\mechanical{k}^\nu}{\mechanical{k}^{\,2}}\right]$ | Rξ gauge. |
| Fermion propagator | $\displaystyle\frac{i(\not{\mechanical{p}}+\mechanical{m})}{\mechanical{p}^{\,2}-\mechanical{m}^2+i\epsilon}$ | Internal fermion line. |
| QED vertex | $-i\electric{e}\gamma^\mu$ | Four-momentum conservation at the vertex. |
| External photon | $\signal{\epsilon}^{\mu(\lambda)}(\mechanical{k})$ or $\signal{\epsilon}^{\mu(\lambda)*}(\mechanical{k})$ | Incoming or outgoing. |
| Fermion loop | $(-1)\operatorname{Tr}[\cdots]$ | Fermionic exchange sign. |

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{k}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Photon four-momentum | The internal or external photon momentum. |
| $\mechanical{p}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Fermion four-momentum | The momentum in the internal fermion line. |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Fermion mass | The mass in the fermion propagator. |
| $\electric{e}$ | $1$ | Electric coupling | The QED interaction strength. |
| $\signal{\epsilon}^{\mu(\lambda)}$ | $1$ | Polarization vector | The normalized external photon polarization. |
