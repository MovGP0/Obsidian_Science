---
title: "The Four-Potential and the Field Tensor"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The electromagnetic four-potential and field tensor express Maxwell theory in covariant form.

Use natural units $\hbar=c=1$ and metric $g^{\mu\nu}=\operatorname{diag}(+1,-1,-1,-1)$.

Introduce the four-potential
$$
\electric{A}^\mu=(\electric{\phi},\magnetic{\mathbf A}),
$$
where $\electric{\phi}$ is the scalar potential and $\magnetic{\mathbf A}$ is the vector potential. The electromagnetic field tensor is the antisymmetric two-form
$$
\electric{F}^{\mu\nu}
=\partial^\mu\electric{A}^\nu-\partial^\nu\electric{A}^\mu. \tag{5.1}
$$
Antisymmetry, $\electric{F}^{\mu\nu}=-\electric{F}^{\nu\mu}$, leaves $4\times3/2=6$ independent components. These are the three electric-field components and three magnetic-field components:
$$
\electric{F}^{0i}=-\electric{F}^{i0}=-\electric{E}^{i}, \tag{5.2}
$$
$$
\electric{F}^{ij}=-\epsilon^{ijk}\magnetic{B}^{k}. \tag{5.3}
$$
Explicitly,
$$
(\electric{F}^{\mu\nu})=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&-\electric{E}^{x}&-\electric{E}^{y}&-\electric{E}^{z}\\
\electric{E}^{x}&0&-\magnetic{B}^{z}&\magnetic{B}^{y}\\
\electric{E}^{y}&\magnetic{B}^{z}&0&-\magnetic{B}^{x}\\
\electric{E}^{z}&-\magnetic{B}^{y}&\magnetic{B}^{x}&0
\end{pmatrix}.
$$
With $\epsilon^{0123}=+1$, the dual tensor is
$$
\tilde{\electric{F}}^{\mu\nu}
=\frac12\epsilon^{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}\electric{F}_{\rho\sigma}.
$$
It follows from $\electric{F}^{\mu\nu}$ by replacing $\electric{\mathbf E}\to\magnetic{\mathbf B}$ and $\magnetic{\mathbf B}\to-\electric{\mathbf E}$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\hbar$ | $1$ | Reduced Planck constant | Set to one in natural units. |
| $c$ | $1$ | Speed of light | Set to one in natural units. |
| $\electric{A}^\mu,\electric{\phi}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Electromagnetic potentials | The four-potential and its scalar component. |
| $\magnetic{\mathbf A}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Vector potential | The spatial components of the four-potential. |
| $\electric{F}^{\mu\nu},\tilde{\electric{F}}^{\mu\nu}$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Field tensors | The electromagnetic tensor and its dual. |
| $\electric{\mathbf E}$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Electric field | The electric components of the field tensor. |
| $\magnetic{\mathbf B}$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Magnetic field | The magnetic components of the field tensor. |
