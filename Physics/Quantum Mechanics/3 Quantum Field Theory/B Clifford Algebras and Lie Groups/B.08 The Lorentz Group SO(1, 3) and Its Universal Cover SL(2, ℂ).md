---
title: "The Lorentz Group SO(1, 3) and Its Universal Cover SL(2, ℂ)"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The Lorentz algebra contains rotations and boosts. The connected proper orthochronous Lorentz group has SL(2, ℂ) as its universal double cover.

This section gives the Lorentz algebra, its complexified decomposition, and its spinor representations.

> [!note]
> The universal-cover statement refers to the identity component $SO^+(1,3)$, the proper orthochronous Lorentz group. The full $SO(1,3)$ also has a disconnected component.
