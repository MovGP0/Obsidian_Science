---
title: "Chiral Projectors"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Chiral projectors split a four-dimensional Dirac spinor into complementary left- and right-Weyl components.

The projectors onto the left- and right-chiral subspaces are
$$
P_L=\frac{1-\gamma^5}{2},
\qquad
P_R=\frac{1+\gamma^5}{2}.
\tag{B.31}
$$
They satisfy
$$
P_{L,R}^2=P_{L,R},
\qquad
P_LP_R=0,
\qquad
P_L+P_R=\mathbf1.
$$
The Weyl spinors are
$$
\signal{\psi}_L=P_L\signal{\psi},
\qquad
\signal{\psi}_R=P_R\signal{\psi}.
$$

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{\psi},\signal{\psi}_L,\signal{\psi}_R$ | — | Dirac and Weyl spinors | The full spinor and its chiral components; the representation definition does not fix normalization. |
