title: "The Dirac Propagator"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The Dirac propagator is a time-ordered spinor two-point function related to the scalar propagator by a first-order differential operator.

The propagator answers a source-and-response problem for the Dirac operator and also supplies the internal fermion line in perturbation theory. Its matrix indices carry spinor information; its Feynman boundary prescription specifies time ordering rather than a retarded response.

Read the definition before the momentum formula so that the reverse-order minus sign is fixed. Then use the differential equation to check the overall factor of the imaginary unit. A nonzero Feynman function at spacelike separation does not by itself permit a signal; causal locality is tested with the field anticommutator and local observables.
