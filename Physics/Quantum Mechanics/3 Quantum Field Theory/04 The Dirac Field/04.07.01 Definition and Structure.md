title: "Definition and Structure"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Fermionic time ordering adds a minus sign when the two spinor fields are exchanged.

Define the time-ordered Dirac Green's function by

$$
\begin{aligned}
\signal{S}_{F}(\mechanical{x}-\mechanical{y})_{\alpha\beta}&\equiv\langle0|\mathcal T\{\signal{\psi}_{\alpha}(\mechanical{x})\overline{\signal{\psi}}_{\beta}(\mechanical{y})\}|0\rangle\\
&=\theta(\mechanical{x}^{0}-\mechanical{y}^{0})\langle0|\signal{\psi}_{\alpha}(\mechanical{x})\overline{\signal{\psi}}_{\beta}(\mechanical{y})|0\rangle\\
&\quad-\theta(\mechanical{y}^{0}-\mechanical{x}^{0})\langle0|\overline{\signal{\psi}}_{\beta}(\mechanical{y})\signal{\psi}_{\alpha}(\mechanical{x})|0\rangle.
\end{aligned} \tag{4.42}
$$

The negative reverse-order term follows from fermionic anticommutation and distinguishes this propagator from its bosonic counterpart.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{S}_{F}$ | $\unit{MeV^{3}}$ | Dirac propagator | The coordinate-space spinor two-point function. |
| $\signal{\psi},\overline{\signal{\psi}}$ | $\unit{MeV^{3/2}}$ | Dirac field and adjoint | The fermionic fields in the expectation value. |
| $\mechanical{x},\mechanical{y}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Spacetime positions | The two events ordered in time. |
