---
title: "Calculation of the Propagator"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Positive- and negative-frequency vacuum contractions give spin sums with slashed momentum plus or minus mass.

For the positive time ordering, completeness gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
\langle0|\signal{\psi}_{\alpha}(\mechanical{x})\overline{\signal{\psi}}_{\beta}(\mechanical{y})|0\rangle
&=\int\frac{d^{3}\mechanical{k}}{(2\pi)^{3}2\energy{E}_{\mathbf p}}\sum_s\signal{u}_{\alpha}(\mechanical{p},s)\overline{\signal{u}}_{\beta}(\mechanical{p},s)e^{-i\mechanical{p}\cdot(\mechanical{x}-\mechanical{y})}\\
&=\int\frac{d^{3}\mechanical{k}}{(2\pi)^{3}}\frac{(\not p+\mechanical{m})_{\alpha\beta}}{2\energy{E}_{\mathbf p}}e^{-i\mechanical{p}\cdot(\mechanical{x}-\mechanical{y})}.
\end{aligned} \tag{4.43}
$$

For the reverse ordering,

$$
\begin{aligned}
\langle0|\overline{\signal{\psi}}_{\beta}(\mechanical{y})\signal{\psi}_{\alpha}(\mechanical{x})|0\rangle
&=\int\frac{d^{3}\mechanical{k}}{(2\pi)^{3}2\energy{E}_{\mathbf p}}\sum_s\signal{v}_{\alpha}(\mechanical{p},s)\overline{\signal{v}}_{\beta}(\mechanical{p},s)e^{+i\mechanical{p}\cdot(\mechanical{x}-\mechanical{y})}\\
&=\int\frac{d^{3}\mechanical{k}}{(2\pi)^{3}}\frac{(\not p-\mechanical{m})_{\alpha\beta}}{2\energy{E}_{\mathbf p}}e^{+i\mechanical{p}\cdot(\mechanical{x}-\mechanical{y})}.
\end{aligned} \tag{4.44}
$$

The k/p integration labels follow the source.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{\psi},\overline{\signal{\psi}}$ | $\unit{MeV^{3/2}}$ | Dirac fields | The spinor and adjoint in the vacuum contractions. |
| $\signal{u},\signal{v}$ | $\unit{MeV^{1/2}}$ | Mode spinors | The coefficients eliminated through completeness. |
| $\mechanical{x},\mechanical{y}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Spacetime positions | The two contraction events. |
| $\mechanical{k},\mechanical{p}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Momenta | The printed integration variable and on-shell mode momentum. |
| $\energy{E}_{\mathbf p}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Mode energy | The positive energy in the contraction measure. |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Mass | The fermion rest mass. |
