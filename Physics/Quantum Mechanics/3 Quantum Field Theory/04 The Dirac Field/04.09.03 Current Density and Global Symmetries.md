---
title: "Current Density and Global Symmetries"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The free vector current is conserved, while a nonzero mass breaks axial-current conservation.

The vector current $\signal{V}^\mu=\bar{\signal{\psi}}\gamma^\mu\signal{\psi}$ is conserved for the free field. The axial current $\signal{A}^\mu=\bar{\signal{\psi}}\gamma^\mu\gamma^5\signal{\psi}$ is conserved only in the massless case:
$$
\partial_\mu\signal{A}^\mu
=2i\mechanical{m}\bar{\signal{\psi}}\gamma^5\signal{\psi}. \tag{4.62}
$$
For $\mechanical{m}=0$, the theory has a chiral $U(1)_A$ symmetry. Its field rotation is $\signal{\psi}\to e^{i\signal{\alpha}\gamma^5}\signal{\psi}$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{\psi},\bar{\signal{\psi}}$ | $\unit{MeV^{3/2}}$ | Dirac field | The field and its Dirac adjoint. |
| $\signal{V}^\mu$ | $\unit{MeV^3}$ | Vector current | The conserved free-field bilinear current. |
| $\signal{A}^\mu$ | $\unit{MeV^3}$ | Axial current | Its divergence is proportional to the mass. |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Mass | The Dirac particle mass. |
| $\signal{\alpha}$ | $1$ | Chiral phase | The parameter of the axial rotation. |
