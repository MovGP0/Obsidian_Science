> [!TLDR]
> The Dirac equation and gamma-matrix algebra determine spinor solutions, normalization, and completeness.

The problem is to retain relativistic dispersion while using an equation that is first order in time. The price is a multicomponent amplitude and matrices that act on its spinor components. Begin with the Clifford algebra, then choose a representation only when explicit components are useful.

Read the normalization section before using the spin sums. A change in mode normalization changes completeness factors and the coefficients in the later field expansion together. As a check, reconstruct the rest-frame spin sums before applying their covariant form to moving modes.
