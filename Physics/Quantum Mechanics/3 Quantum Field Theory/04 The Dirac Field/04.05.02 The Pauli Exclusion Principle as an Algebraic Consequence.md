---
title: "The Pauli Exclusion Principle as an Algebraic Consequence"
---

> [!TLDR]
> A fermionic creation operator squares to zero, preventing double occupation of one mode.

The creation–creation anticommutator implies

$$
2b^{s\dagger}_{\mathbf p}b^{s\dagger}_{\mathbf p}=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad(b^{s\dagger}_{\mathbf p})^{2}=0. \tag{4.34}
$$

Applying the squared operator to the vacuum gives zero. Two fermions cannot occupy the same momentum and spin state. The Pauli exclusion principle is thus an algebraic consequence of the anticommutation relations.
