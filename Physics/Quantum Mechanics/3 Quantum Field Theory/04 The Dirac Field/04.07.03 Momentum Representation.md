title: "Momentum Representation"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The momentum-space Dirac propagator has the scalar Feynman denominator and a slashed-momentum-plus-mass numerator.

Use the step-function representation

$$
\theta(\pm\mechanical{x}^{0})=-\frac{1}{2\pi i}\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}\frac{d\signal{\omega}}{\signal{\omega}+i\varepsilon}e^{\mp i\signal{\omega}\mechanical{x}^{0}}. \tag{4.45}
$$

Combining the contractions gives

$$
\boxed{\widetilde{\signal{S}}_{F}(\mechanical{p})=\frac{i(\not p+\mechanical{m})}{\mechanical{p}^{2}-\mechanical{m}^{2}+i\varepsilon}=\frac{i}{\not p-\mechanical{m}+i\varepsilon},} \tag{4.46}
$$

using $(\not p-\mechanical{m})(\not p+\mechanical{m})=\mechanical{p}^{2}-\mechanical{m}^{2}$.

> [!note]
> The last fraction is shorthand for the same Feynman limiting prescription, rather than a literal finite-regulator identity of the two denominators.

The coordinate-space function is

$$
\signal{S}_{F}(\mechanical{x}-\mechanical{y})=\int\frac{d^{4}\mechanical{k}}{(2\pi)^{4}}\widetilde{\signal{S}}_{F}(\mechanical{p})e^{-i\mechanical{p}\cdot(\mechanical{x}-\mechanical{y})}. \tag{4.47}
$$

The source again integrates over k while labeling the integrand by p.

## Example 4.3 — Fermionic Propagator Poles

The mass-shell poles occur at the two signed on-shell energies. The Feynman prescription places the positive-energy pole below the real axis and the negative-energy pole above it. This implements the particle and antiparticle time-ordering structure.

In the nonrelativistic limit, the numerator becomes $\not p+\mechanical{m}\approx\mechanical{m}(\gamma^{0}+1)$, projecting onto the upper spinor components and reproducing the nonrelativistic propagator for mass $\mechanical{m}$.
