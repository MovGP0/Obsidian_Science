---
title: "Traces of Products of Gamma Matrices"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Traces of two or four gamma matrices reduce to metric tensors; the γ⁵ trace uses the Levi-Civita tensor.

The basic traces are
$$
\operatorname{tr}(1)=4,
$$
$$
\operatorname{tr}(\gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu)=4g^{\mu\nu}, \tag{4.74}
$$
$$
\operatorname{tr}(\gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu\gamma^\rho\gamma^\sigma)
=4(g^{\mu\nu}g^{\rho\sigma}-g^{\mu\rho}g^{\nu\sigma}
+g^{\mu\sigma}g^{\nu\rho}), \tag{4.75}
$$
$$
\operatorname{tr}(\gamma^5)=0,\qquad
\operatorname{tr}(\gamma^5\gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu)=0,
$$
$$
\operatorname{tr}(\gamma^5\gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu\gamma^\rho\gamma^\sigma)
=-4i\epsilon^{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}. \tag{4.76}
$$
The Levi-Civita tensor convention is $\epsilon^{0123}=+1$.

### Proof of (4.74)

From the Clifford algebra, $\gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu+\gamma^\nu\gamma^\mu=2g^{\mu\nu}$. Take the trace and use cyclicity:
$$
2\operatorname{tr}(\gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu)
=2g^{\mu\nu}\operatorname{tr}(1)=8g^{\mu\nu}. \tag{4.77}
$$
Thus $\operatorname{tr}(\gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu)=4g^{\mu\nu}$.

### Proof of (4.75)

Move the first gamma matrix to the end with $\gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu=2g^{\mu\nu}-\gamma^\nu\gamma^\mu$. In slash notation,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{tr}(\not a\not b\not c\not d)
&=2(a\cdot b)\operatorname{tr}(\not c\not d)
-\operatorname{tr}(\not b\not a\not c\not d)\\
&=2(a\cdot b)\,4(c\cdot d)
-2(a\cdot c)\operatorname{tr}(\not b\not d)
+\operatorname{tr}(\not b\not c\not a\not d)\\
&=\cdots .
\end{aligned} \tag{4.78}
$$
Continue and use cyclicity to close the cycle:
$$
\operatorname{tr}(\not a\not b\not c\not d)
=4[(a\cdot b)(c\cdot d)-(a\cdot c)(b\cdot d)
+(a\cdot d)(b\cdot c)]. \tag{4.79}
$$

**Table 4.2** Gamma-matrix trace identities in four dimensions.

| Expression | Result |
| --- | --- |
| $\operatorname{tr}(\gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu)$ | $4g^{\mu\nu}$ |
| $\operatorname{tr}(\not a\not b)$ | $4(a\cdot b)$ |
| $\operatorname{tr}(\not a\not b\not c\not d)$ | $4[(a\cdot b)(c\cdot d)-(a\cdot c)(b\cdot d)+(a\cdot d)(b\cdot c)]$ |
| $\operatorname{tr}(\gamma^5\gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu\gamma^\rho\gamma^\sigma)$ | $-4i\epsilon^{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}$ |
| Trace of an odd number of gamma matrices | $0$ |
| $\operatorname{tr}(\gamma^5)$ | $0$ |
| $\operatorname{tr}(\gamma^5\gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu)$ | $0$ |
