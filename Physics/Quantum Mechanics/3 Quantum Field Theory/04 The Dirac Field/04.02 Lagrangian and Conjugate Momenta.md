> [!TLDR]
> The Dirac Lagrangian determines the field equations, conjugate momentum, and Hamiltonian density.

Use these notes to answer a Hamiltonian question: how can a first-order field equation have canonical momenta without independent initial velocities? The Lagrangian fixes a momentum directly in terms of the field adjoint, so the phase-space variables are constrained.

Read the density variation first, then the momentum calculation, and only then the Legendre transformation. The later [[04.15 The Dirac Field as a Dirac–Bergmann System]] explains why these constraints require a reduced bracket rather than an ordinary unconstrained inversion of velocities.
