---
title: "Chiral Projectors"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The right- and left-chiral projectors separate a Dirac spinor into two complementary components.

The chiral projectors are
$$
P_R=\frac{1+\gamma^5}{2},\qquad P_L=\frac{1-\gamma^5}{2}. \tag{4.63}
$$
They satisfy $P_R^2=P_R$, $P_L^2=P_L$, $P_RP_L=0$, and $P_R+P_L=1$. Every spinor decomposes as
$$
\signal{\psi}=P_L\signal{\psi}+P_R\signal{\psi}
\equiv\signal{\psi}_L+\signal{\psi}_R. \tag{4.64}
$$

The components $\signal{\psi}_L$ and $\signal{\psi}_R$ are the left-handed and right-handed Weyl spinors. In the chiral (Weyl) representation,
$$
\signal{\psi}=\begin{pmatrix}\signal{\psi}_L\\\signal{\psi}_R\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\gamma^5\signal{\psi}
=\begin{pmatrix}-\signal{\psi}_L\\+\signal{\psi}_R\end{pmatrix}. \tag{4.65}
$$

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{\psi},\signal{\psi}_L,\signal{\psi}_R$ | $\unit{MeV^{3/2}}$ | Dirac and chiral fields | The full field and its left- and right-chiral components. |
