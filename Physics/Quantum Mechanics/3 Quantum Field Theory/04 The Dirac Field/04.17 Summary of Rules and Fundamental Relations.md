title: "Summary of Rules and Fundamental Relations"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The quantized Dirac field uses canonical anticommutators, positive particle energies, and opposite particle and antiparticle charges.

This note collects the principal relations for the quantized Dirac field.

### Equal-time anticommutation relations

$$
\{\signal{\psi}_\alpha(\mechanical{\mathbf x},\signal{t}),
\signal{\psi}_\beta^\dagger(\mechanical{\mathbf y},\signal{t})\}
=\delta_{\alpha\beta}\delta^{(3)}(\mechanical{\mathbf x}-\mechanical{\mathbf y}). \tag{4.110}
$$

### Mode-operator anticommutation relations

$$
\{b_{\mathbf p}^{s},b_{\mathbf q}^{s'\dagger}\}
=\{d_{\mathbf p}^{s},d_{\mathbf q}^{s'\dagger}\}
=(2\pi)^3\delta^{ss'}\delta^{(3)}(\mechanical{\mathbf p}-\mechanical{\mathbf q}). \tag{4.111}
$$

### Hamiltonian, charge, and four-momentum

$$
\energy{H}
=\int\frac{d^3\mechanical{k}}{(2\pi)^3}\energy{E}_{\mathbf p}
\sum_s(b^{s\dagger}b^s+d^{s\dagger}d^s), \tag{4.112}
$$
$$
\electric{Q}
=\int\frac{d^3\mechanical{k}}{(2\pi)^3}
\sum_s(b^{s\dagger}b^s-d^{s\dagger}d^s), \tag{4.113}
$$
$$
\mechanical{P}^{i}
=\int\frac{d^3\mechanical{k}}{(2\pi)^3}\mechanical{p}^{i}
\sum_s(b^{s\dagger}b^s+d^{s\dagger}d^s). \tag{4.114}
$$

> [!note]
> The source suppresses the mode-momentum arguments in (4.112)–(4.114). It also prints $\mechanical{k}$ in the measure and $\mechanical{p}$ in the energy and momentum factors. These labels must denote the same integration momentum when the formulas are used. The charge is measured in units of the field's charge.

### Feynman propagator

$$
\tilde{\signal{S}}_F(\mechanical{p})
=\frac{i(\not{\mechanical{p}}+\mechanical{m})}
{\mechanical{p}^{\,2}-\mechanical{m}^2+i\epsilon}. \tag{4.115}
$$

### Completeness relations

$$
\sum_su(\mechanical{p},s)\bar u(\mechanical{p},s)
=\not{\mechanical{p}}+\mechanical{m},
\qquad
\sum_sv(\mechanical{p},s)\bar v(\mechanical{p},s)
=\not{\mechanical{p}}-\mechanical{m}. \tag{4.116}
$$

### Fundamental traces

For arbitrary four-vectors $a,b,c,d$,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{tr}(\not a\not b)&=4(a\cdot b),\\
\operatorname{tr}(\not a\not b\not c\not d)
&=4[(a\cdot b)(c\cdot d)-(a\cdot c)(b\cdot d)+(a\cdot d)(b\cdot c)].
\end{aligned} \tag{4.117}
$$

| $\signal{t}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Time | The common time of the canonical relation. |

| $\electric{Q}$ | $1$ | Charge | The particle-minus-antiparticle charge count. |
| $\mechanical{P}^{i}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Total momentum | A spatial component of the total four-momentum. |

| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Mass | The Dirac particle mass. |
| $\tilde{\signal{S}}_F$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Momentum-space propagator | The Fourier transform of the Dirac two-point function. |
