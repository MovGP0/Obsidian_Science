---
title: "General Properties of the Trace"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The trace of an odd number of gamma matrices is zero because γ⁵ anticommutes with each gamma matrix.

The trace of an odd number of gamma matrices vanishes:
$$
\operatorname{tr}(\gamma^{\mu_1}\gamma^{\mu_2}\cdots\gamma^{\mu_{2n+1}})=0. \tag{4.71}
$$
Use $\gamma^5\gamma^\mu=-\gamma^\mu\gamma^5$, $(\gamma^5)^2=1$, and cyclicity of the trace:
$$
\operatorname{tr}(\gamma^{\mu_1}\cdots\gamma^{\mu_{2n+1}})
=\operatorname{tr}(\gamma^5\gamma^{\mu_1}\cdots\gamma^{\mu_{2n+1}}\gamma^5).
$$

Move the final $\gamma^5$ through all $2n+1$ gamma matrices:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{tr}(\gamma^5\gamma^{\mu_1}\cdots\gamma^{\mu_{2n+1}}\gamma^5)
&=(-1)^{2n+1}\operatorname{tr}(\gamma^5\gamma^5\gamma^{\mu_1}\cdots\gamma^{\mu_{2n+1}})\\
&=-\operatorname{tr}(\gamma^{\mu_1}\cdots\gamma^{\mu_{2n+1}}).
\end{aligned} \tag{4.72}
$$
The trace equals its own negative and is therefore zero.
