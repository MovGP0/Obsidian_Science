title: "Connection to Quantum Electrodynamics"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Local U(1) gauge invariance couples a Dirac field to the photon field and gives the QED interaction.

The free Dirac Lagrangian is the starting point for quantum electrodynamics. Depending on the model, $\signal{\psi}$ represents an electron, muon, tau, or quark. The photon is described by a separate vector field $\magnetic{A}^\mu$; see Chapter 5.

Local $U(1)$ gauge invariance introduces the interaction. Replace $\partial_\mu$ with
$$
D_\mu=\partial_\mu+i\electric{e}\magnetic{A}_\mu.
$$
The QED Lagrangian density is
$$
\energy{\mathcal L}_{\mathrm{QED}}
=\bar{\signal{\psi}}(i\not D-\mechanical{m})\signal{\psi}
-\frac14\electric{F}_{\mu\nu}\electric{F}^{\mu\nu}, \tag{4.101}
$$
where $\electric{F}_{\mu\nu}=\partial_\mu\magnetic{A}_\nu-\partial_\nu\magnetic{A}_\mu$ is the electromagnetic field-strength tensor. The interaction term is
$$
\energy{\mathcal L}_{\mathrm{int}}
=-\electric{e}\electric{J}^\mu\magnetic{A}_\mu
=-\electric{e}\bar{\signal{\psi}}\gamma^\mu\signal{\psi}\magnetic{A}_\mu. \tag{4.102}
$$
Here $\electric{J}^\mu=\bar{\signal{\psi}}\gamma^\mu\signal{\psi}$ is the free Dirac Noether current (4.39). The source illustrates the process $\mathrm e^+\mathrm e^-\to\mu^+\mu^-$ in Fig. 4.1.

The QED vertex has Feynman rule $-i\electric{e}\gamma^\mu$. It combines the coupling with the Lorentz structure of the current. The following chapters develop perturbation theory and derive the full Feynman rules.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{\psi},\bar{\signal{\psi}}$ | $\unit{MeV^{3/2}}$ | Dirac field | The charged matter field and its adjoint. |
| $\magnetic{A}^\mu$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Electromagnetic potential | The photon four-potential. |
| $\electric{e}$ | $1$ | Electric coupling | The coupling for the displayed charge convention. |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Fermion mass | The mass of the matter field. |
| $\electric{F}_{\mu\nu}$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Electromagnetic field strength | The tensor formed from derivatives of the potential. |
| $\electric{J}^\mu$ | $\unit{MeV^3}$ | Noether current | The current before multiplication by the electric coupling. |
| $\energy{\mathcal L}_{\mathrm{QED}},\energy{\mathcal L}_{\mathrm{int}}$ | $\unit{MeV^4}$ | Lagrangian densities | The full QED density and its interaction term. |
| $\tilde{\signal{S}}_F^\gamma$ | $\unit{MeV^{-2}}$ | Photon propagator | The momentum-space propagator of the gauge field. |
| $\tilde{\signal{S}}_F$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Dirac propagator | The momentum-space fermion propagator from (4.46). |
