---
title: "Field Expansion"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The Dirac field combines particle annihilation and antiparticle creation modes, while its adjoint contains the conjugate operators.

The general mode expansions are

$$
\signal{\psi}(\mechanical{x})=\int\frac{d^{3}\mechanical{k}}{(2\pi)^{3}\sqrt{2\energy{E}_{\mathbf p}}}\sum_{s=1}^{2}[b^{s}_{\mathbf p}\signal{u}(\mechanical{p},s)e^{-i\mechanical{p}\cdot\mechanical{x}}+d^{s\dagger}_{\mathbf p}\signal{v}(\mechanical{p},s)e^{+i\mechanical{p}\cdot\mechanical{x}}], \tag{4.27}
$$

$$
\overline{\signal{\psi}}(\mechanical{x})=\int\frac{d^{3}\mechanical{k}}{(2\pi)^{3}\sqrt{2\energy{E}_{\mathbf p}}}\sum_{s=1}^{2}[b^{s\dagger}_{\mathbf p}\overline{\signal{u}}(\mechanical{p},s)e^{+i\mechanical{p}\cdot\mechanical{x}}+d^{s}_{\mathbf p}\overline{\signal{v}}(\mechanical{p},s)e^{-i\mechanical{p}\cdot\mechanical{x}}]. \tag{4.28}
$$

The on-shell energy is $\energy{p}^{0}=\energy{E}_{\mathbf p}=\sqrt{\mechanical{\mathbf p}^{2}+\mechanical{m}^{2}}$. The b operators annihilate and create fermions; the d operators annihilate and create antifermions with the indicated momentum and spin labels.

> [!note]
> The integrals print k while their modes use p; the following source sentence calls the measure d³p/(2π)³. The printed integration labels are retained.
