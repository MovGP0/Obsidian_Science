> [!TLDR]
> Fermionic anticommutation relations are required for a stable, local relativistic spin-half field theory.

The choice of brackets determines the physical state space, not only an algebraic sign. The failed construction first exposes the conflict between positive state norms and a lower energy bound. The spin–statistics theorem then states the general relativistic conditions behind this conflict.

Use the final equal-time relations as the canonical input for the free Dirac field. They are operator-valued distribution identities: their delta function is understood under spatial integration. The unequal-time spacelike statement follows from propagation and locality, rather than from replacing the common time by two arbitrary times.
