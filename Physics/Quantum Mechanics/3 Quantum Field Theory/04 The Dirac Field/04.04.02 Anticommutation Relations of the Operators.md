---
title: "Anticommutation Relations of the Operators"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Projection of the field expansion gives independent fermionic anticommutators for particle and antiparticle modes.

Invert (4.27) by spinor projection and use (4.25)–(4.26):

$$
\{b^{s}_{\mathbf p},b^{s'\dagger}_{\mathbf q}\}=(2\pi)^{3}\delta^{(3)}(\mechanical{\mathbf p}-\mechanical{\mathbf q})\delta^{ss'}, \tag{4.29}
$$

$$
\{d^{s}_{\mathbf p},d^{s'\dagger}_{\mathbf q}\}=(2\pi)^{3}\delta^{(3)}(\mechanical{\mathbf p}-\mechanical{\mathbf q})\delta^{ss'}. \tag{4.30}
$$

All other anticommutators vanish, including

$$
\{b^{s}_{\mathbf p},b^{s'}_{\mathbf q}\}=0,\qquad\{d^{s}_{\mathbf p},d^{s'}_{\mathbf q}\}=0,\qquad\{b^{s}_{\mathbf p},d^{s'}_{\mathbf q}\}=0. \tag{4.31}
$$

**Schematic derivation:** project the field relation with spinor coefficients and integrate over both positions. Plane-wave orthogonality produces the momentum delta function, and invariant spinor orthogonality supplies twice the mass. Combining terms with the completeness identities gives the b relation; the d calculation is analogous.

At equal time, Fourier projection separates the spatial phases. The Hermitian spinor products supply twice the positive mode energy, which cancels the square-root energy factors in the field expansion. The mixed branches cancel by their opposite-momentum orthogonality. This is the direct way to track the normalization in (4.29)–(4.30).

> [!note]
> The preceding schematic reference to twice the mass concerns invariant adjoint products. Equal-time inversion with Hermitian spinor projection uses twice the energy instead. These are different products; substituting the invariant mass factor into the Hermitian inversion would give incorrect normalization. Compare [Tong, sections 5.1–5.2](https://www.damtp.cam.ac.uk/user/tong/qft/qfthtml/S5.html).

For continuum momentum labels the right side is a distribution, not the number one. The familiar unit anticommutator applies after finite-volume normalization or projection onto a normalized wave-packet mode.
