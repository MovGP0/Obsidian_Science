---
title: "Fierz Identities"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Fierz identities change the pairing of spinors in products of bilinear forms.

Fierz identities reorder bilinear forms. The source gives
$$
(\bar{\signal{\psi}}_1\signal{\psi}_2)(\bar{\signal{\psi}}_3\signal{\psi}_4)
=-\frac14\sum_\Gamma
(\bar{\signal{\psi}}_1\Gamma\signal{\psi}_4)
(\bar{\signal{\psi}}_3\bar\Gamma\signal{\psi}_2). \tag{4.61}
$$
Here $\Gamma\in\{1,\gamma^\mu,\sigma^{\mu\nu},\gamma^\mu\gamma^5,i\gamma^5\}$ and $\bar\Gamma=\gamma^0\Gamma^\dagger\gamma^0$. These identities help calculate amplitudes for processes with four fermions.

> [!note]
> Equation (4.61) is retained as printed. A complete Fierz sum needs a specified dual basis, contractions, and tensor weights. The displayed set and the definition of $\bar\Gamma$ alone do not specify these details. The overall exchange sign also depends on whether the spinors are Grassmann-valued. Check these conventions before using this compact formula.
