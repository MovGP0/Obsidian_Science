---
title: "Equation Satisfied by the Propagator"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Acting with the Dirac operator on its Feynman propagator gives i times the spacetime delta function and spinor identity.

The propagator satisfies

$$
(i\not{\partial}_x-\mechanical{m})\signal{S}_{F}(\mechanical{x}-\mechanical{y})=i\delta^{(4)}(\mechanical{x}-\mechanical{y})\mathbf1_4. \tag{4.48}
$$

In momentum space,

$$
(\not p-\mechanical{m})\frac{i(\not p+\mechanical{m})}{\mechanical{p}^{2}-\mechanical{m}^{2}+i\varepsilon}=\frac{i(\mechanical{p}^{2}-\mechanical{m}^{2})}{\mechanical{p}^{2}-\mechanical{m}^{2}+i\varepsilon}\longrightarrow i\mathbf1_4. \tag{4.49}
$$

Taking the regulator to zero verifies the source equation.
