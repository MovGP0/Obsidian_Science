title: "Fock Space and the Pauli Exclusion Principle"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Anticommuting creation operators build antisymmetric fermionic Fock states and enforce the Pauli exclusion principle.

The central question is how the operator algebra changes the allowed many-particle states. Read the vacuum and ordered state construction first. Exchange two creation operators to obtain antisymmetry; then repeat the same operator to obtain exclusion. The number-operator calculation packages both facts as occupation zero or one.

“One mode” includes all its quantum labels. Equal momentum with opposite spin is a pair of different modes, so the exclusion rule does not forbid both occupations. This distinction is essential when using the mode count in statistical mechanics.
