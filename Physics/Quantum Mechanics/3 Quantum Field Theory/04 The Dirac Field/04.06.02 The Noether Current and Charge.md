---
title: "The Noether Current and Charge"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The normal-ordered Dirac charge counts fermions minus antifermions.

The free Lagrangian is invariant under $\signal{\psi}\to e^{-i\signal{\alpha}}\signal{\psi}$ and $\overline{\signal{\psi}}\to e^{i\signal{\alpha}}\overline{\signal{\psi}}$. The Noether current is

$$
\electric{J}^{\mu}=\overline{\signal{\psi}}\gamma^{\mu}\signal{\psi}, \tag{4.39}
$$

with on-shell zero divergence. The formal charge is

$$
\electric{Q}=\int d^{3}\mechanical{x}\,\electric{J}^{0}=\int d^{3}\mechanical{x}\,\signal{\psi}^{\dagger}\signal{\psi}.
$$

In mode variables, after normal ordering,

$$
\electric{Q}=\int\frac{d^{3}\mechanical{k}}{(2\pi)^{3}}\sum_s(b^{s\dagger}_{\mathbf p}b^{s}_{\mathbf p}-d^{s\dagger}_{\mathbf p}d^{s}_{\mathbf p})=N_b-N_d. \tag{4.40}
$$

Particles contribute charge +1 and antiparticles −1. In QED, the physical electric charge is measured in units of the charge coupling, with the appropriate sign.

## Remark 4.2 — Comparison with Charged Bosons

The number-difference form is shared by complex scalar fields. Bosonic occupations are unrestricted, while each fermionic mode has occupation zero or one. The total fermionic charge can still be any integer.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{\psi},\overline{\signal{\psi}}$ | $\unit{MeV^{3/2}}$ | Dirac field and adjoint | The fields in the global phase symmetry. |

| $\electric{J}^{\mu}$ | $\unit{MeV^{3}}$ | Charge current | The conserved global-symmetry current. |
