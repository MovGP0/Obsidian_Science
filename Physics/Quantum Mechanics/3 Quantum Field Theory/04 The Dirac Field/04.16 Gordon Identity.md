---
title: "Gordon Identity"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The Gordon identity separates the Dirac vector current into convection and spin-dependent magnetic contributions.

The **Gordon identity** is
$$
\bar u(\mechanical{p}',s')\gamma^\mu u(\mechanical{p},s)
=\bar u(\mechanical{p}',s')
\left[
\frac{(\mechanical{p}'+\mechanical{p})^\mu}{2\mechanical{m}}
+\frac{i\sigma^{\mu\nu}(\mechanical{p}'-\mechanical{p})_\nu}{2\mechanical{m}}
\right]u(\mechanical{p},s). \tag{4.106}
$$
Here $\sigma^{\mu\nu}=\frac i2[\gamma^\mu,\gamma^\nu]$. The term in $\mechanical{p}+\mechanical{p}'$ is the convection contribution; the term with $\sigma^{\mu\nu}$ is the magnetic contribution to the electron current.

In QED, the magnetic form factor $\magnetic{F}_2(\mechanical{q}^{\,2})$ is related to the anomalous magnetic moment by $\magnetic{a}_e=\magnetic{F}_2(0)$. The first-order Schwinger value is $\magnetic{a}_e=\electric{\alpha}/(2\pi)$.

### Proof

The on-shell Dirac equations give $\not{\mechanical{p}}u(\mechanical{p})=\mechanical{m}u(\mechanical{p})$ and $\bar u(\mechanical{p}')\not{\mechanical{p}}'=\mechanical{m}\bar u(\mechanical{p}')$. Therefore,
$$
2\mechanical{m}\bar u(\mechanical{p}')\gamma^\mu u(\mechanical{p})
=\bar u(\mechanical{p}')
[\not{\mechanical{p}}'\gamma^\mu+\gamma^\mu\not{\mechanical{p}}]
u(\mechanical{p}). \tag{4.107}
$$

Separate the operator product into symmetric and antisymmetric parts:
$$
\not{\mechanical{p}}'\gamma^\mu+\gamma^\mu\not{\mechanical{p}}
=\frac12\{\not{\mechanical{p}}+\not{\mechanical{p}}',\gamma^\mu\}
+\frac12[\not{\mechanical{p}}'-\not{\mechanical{p}},\gamma^\mu]. \tag{4.108}
$$
Use $\{\gamma^\mu,\gamma^\nu\}=2g^{\mu\nu}$ and $\sigma^{\mu\nu}=\frac i2[\gamma^\mu,\gamma^\nu]$. Thus $[\gamma^\nu,\gamma^\mu]=-2i\sigma^{\nu\mu}=2i\sigma^{\mu\nu}$, and
$$
\not{\mechanical{p}}'\gamma^\mu+\gamma^\mu\not{\mechanical{p}}
=(\mechanical{p}+\mechanical{p}')^\mu1
+i\sigma^{\mu\nu}(\mechanical{p}'-\mechanical{p})_\nu. \tag{4.109}
$$
Divide (4.107) by $2\mechanical{m}$ to obtain (4.106).

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $u,\bar u$ | $\unit{MeV^{1/2}}$ | Mode spinors | The external on-shell spinors. |
| $\mechanical{p},\mechanical{p}'$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Four-momenta | The incoming and outgoing fermion momenta. |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Mass | The common on-shell fermion mass. |
| $\mechanical{q}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Momentum transfer | The momentum difference used in the form factor. |
| $\magnetic{F}_2$ | $1$ | Magnetic form factor | The Pauli form factor of the interaction vertex. |
| $\magnetic{a}_e$ | $1$ | Magnetic-moment anomaly | The electron anomaly given by the form factor at zero transfer. |
| $\electric{\alpha}$ | $1$ | Fine-structure constant | The electromagnetic coupling strength. |
