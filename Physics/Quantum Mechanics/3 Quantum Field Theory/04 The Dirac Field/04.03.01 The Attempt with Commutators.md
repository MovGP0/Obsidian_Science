---
title: "The Attempt with Commutators"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Using bosonic commutators for the Dirac field produces a negative antiparticle-energy contribution and an unbounded spectrum.

One might try the scalar-field recipe at equal time:

$$
[\signal{\psi}_{\alpha}(\mechanical{\mathbf x},\mechanical{t}),\signal{\psi}^{\dagger}_{\beta}(\mechanical{\mathbf y},\mechanical{t})]=\delta_{\alpha\beta}\delta^{(3)}(\mechanical{\mathbf x}-\mechanical{\mathbf y}). \tag{4.22}
$$

The source uses the mode expansion

$$
\signal{\psi}(\mechanical{\mathbf x},\mechanical{t})=\int\frac{d^{3}\mechanical{k}}{(2\pi)^{3}\sqrt{2\energy{E}_{\mathbf p}}}\sum_s[b^{s}_{\mathbf p}\signal{u}(\mechanical{p},s)e^{-i\mechanical{p}\cdot\mechanical{x}}+d^{s\dagger}_{\mathbf p}\signal{v}(\mechanical{p},s)e^{+i\mechanical{p}\cdot\mechanical{x}}]. \tag{4.23}
$$

It prints the resulting mode condition as $[b^{s}_{\mathbf p},b^{s'\dagger}_{\mathbf q}]=\delta_{ss'}\delta^{(3)}(\mechanical{\mathbf p}-\mechanical{\mathbf q})$, with an analogous d relation.

> [!note]
> The source integrates over k while labeling the modes by p, and suppresses a Fourier-normalization factor in the printed mode commutator. These conventions are retained for this failed construction.

The Hamiltonian then contains

$$
\energy{H}\supset-\int\frac{d^{3}\mechanical{k}}{(2\pi)^{3}}\energy{E}_{\mathbf p}\sum_s d^{s\dagger}_{\mathbf p}d^{s}_{\mathbf p}. \tag{4.24}
$$

Repeated antiparticle creation lowers the energy without bound. Anticommutator quantization avoids this problem.

For a single normalized bosonic mode, unrestricted occupation makes the instability transparent: each additional excitation in the negative-energy branch lowers the energy by the same positive mode energy. Subtracting a constant vacuum energy cannot change this slope.

> [!note]
> The failed construction must also track state norms. With the field commutator (4.22) and the stated frequency assignment, the antiparticle mode commutator has the opposite sign from the particle one. Retaining the usual positive-norm interpretation then requires reversing which antiparticle operator annihilates the vacuum, and the resulting energy is unbounded below. Thus the source's “analogous d relation” must not be read as a second positive-sign commutator consistent with (4.22). See [Tong, section 5.1](https://www.damtp.cam.ac.uk/user/tong/qft/qfthtml/S5.html).

This example diagnoses a failure of bosonic quantization for this local relativistic field. It does not prove the full spin–statistics theorem for all possible theories.
