---
title: "Completeness Relations (Spin Sum)"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Spin sums express mode completeness through slashed momentum and mass.

Summing over the two spin states gives

$$
\sum_{s=1}^{2}\signal{u}(\mechanical{p},s)\overline{\signal{u}}(\mechanical{p},s)=\not p+\mechanical{m}. \tag{4.13}
$$

The negative-frequency sum is

$$
\sum_{s=1}^{2}\signal{v}(\mechanical{p},s)\overline{\signal{v}}(\mechanical{p},s)=\not p-\mechanical{m}. \tag{4.14}
$$

Explicit component substitution from (4.7)–(4.8) proves the identities. In components,

$$
(\not p+\mechanical{m})_{\alpha\beta}=\sum_s\signal{u}_{\alpha}(\mechanical{p},s)\overline{\signal{u}}_{\beta}(\mechanical{p},s). \tag{4.15}
$$

After division by twice the mass, this is the positive-energy projector.

## Example 4.1 — Rest-Frame Completeness Check

At zero spatial momentum, the rest energy equals the mass and $\signal{u}(\mechanical{p},s)=\sqrt{2\mechanical{m}}(\signal{\chi}_s,0)^T$. Summing gives the block-matrix product

$$
\sum_{s=1}^{2}\signal{u}(\mechanical{p},s)\overline{\signal{u}}(\mechanical{p},s)=2\mechanical{m}\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&-1\end{pmatrix}=2\mechanical{m}\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix}. \tag{4.16}
$$

At rest, $\not p+\mechanical{m}=\mechanical{m}(1+\gamma^{0})=2\mechanical{m}\operatorname{diag}(1,1,0,0)$, confirming the result.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{u}$ | $\unit{MeV^{1/2}}$ | Positive-frequency spinor | The spinor summed over its two spin states. |
| $\mechanical{p}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Four-momentum | The on-shell momentum in the completeness relation. |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Mass | The mass term in the spin sum. |
| $\signal{v}$ | $\unit{MeV^{1/2}}$ | Negative-frequency spinor | The second spinor branch in the completeness relation. |
| $\signal{\chi}_s$ | $1$ | Pauli spin basis | The rest-frame two-component spinor. |
