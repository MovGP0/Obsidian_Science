---
title: "Parity (P)"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Parity reverses spatial arguments, multiplies the Dirac field by gamma zero, and assigns opposite relative intrinsic parities to particle and antiparticle.

Parity reverses space while leaving time fixed. In the chosen metric, the coordinate and momentum four-vectors are mapped to their lowered-index forms. For the Dirac field,

$$
\mathcal P\signal{\psi}(\mechanical{\mathbf x},\mechanical{t})\mathcal P^{-1}=\eta_P\gamma^{0}\signal{\psi}(-\mechanical{\mathbf x},\mechanical{t}). \tag{4.50}
$$

The intrinsic phase can be one of the four signs and imaginary signs; its square is plus or minus one. The free theory is independent of this phase choice. Mode operators transform as

$$
\mathcal P b^{s}_{\mathbf p}\mathcal P^{-1}=\eta_P b^{s}_{-\mathbf p},\qquad
\mathcal P d^{s}_{\mathbf p}\mathcal P^{-1}=-\eta_P^{*}d^{s}_{-\mathbf p}. \tag{4.51}
$$

The relative sign gives opposite intrinsic parities for fermions and antifermions. The vector current transforms as

$$
\mathcal P\electric{J}^{0}(\mechanical{\mathbf x},\mechanical{t})\mathcal P^{-1}=+\electric{J}^{0}(-\mechanical{\mathbf x},\mechanical{t}), \tag{4.52}
$$

$$
\mathcal P\electric{J}^{i}(\mechanical{\mathbf x},\mechanical{t})\mathcal P^{-1}=-\electric{J}^{i}(-\mechanical{\mathbf x},\mechanical{t}), \tag{4.53}
$$

where $\electric{J}^{\mu}=\overline{\signal{\psi}}\gamma^{\mu}\signal{\psi}$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{\psi},\overline{\signal{\psi}}$ | $\unit{MeV^{3/2}}$ | Dirac field and adjoint | The spinor fields in the parity transformation. |
| $\mechanical{\mathbf x},\mechanical{t}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Coordinates | The reversed position and unchanged time. |
| $\eta_P$ | $1$ | Intrinsic parity phase | The phase convention of the free fermion field. |
| $\electric{J}^{\mu}$ | $\unit{MeV^{3}}$ | Vector current | The charge current with even time and odd spatial components. |
