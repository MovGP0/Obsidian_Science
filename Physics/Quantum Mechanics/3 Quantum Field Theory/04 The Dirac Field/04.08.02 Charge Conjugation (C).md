---
title: "Charge Conjugation (C)"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Charge conjugation exchanges particles and antiparticles while preserving four-momentum and spin.

The field transforms as

$$
\mathcal C\signal{\psi}(\mechanical{x})\mathcal C^{-1}=\eta_C C\overline{\signal{\psi}}^{T}(\mechanical{x}). \tag{4.54}
$$

The charge-conjugation matrix satisfies

$$
C\gamma^{\mu}C^{-1}=-(\gamma^{\mu})^{T},\qquad C^{\dagger}=C^{-1},\qquad C^{T}=-C. \tag{4.55}
$$

In the Dirac–Pauli representation, $C=i\gamma^{2}\gamma^{0}$. Its mode action is

$$
\mathcal C b^{s}_{\mathbf p}\mathcal C^{-1}=\eta_C d^{s}_{\mathbf p},\qquad
\mathcal C d^{s}_{\mathbf p}\mathcal C^{-1}=\eta_C^{*}b^{s}_{\mathbf p}. \tag{4.56}
$$

The charge current changes sign:

$$
\mathcal C\electric{J}^{\mu}\mathcal C^{-1}=-\electric{J}^{\mu}, \tag{4.57}
$$

as expected for a particle-minus-antiparticle quantity.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{\psi},\overline{\signal{\psi}}$ | $\unit{MeV^{3/2}}$ | Dirac field and adjoint | The fields related by charge conjugation. |
| $\mechanical{x}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Spacetime position | The unchanged field event. |
| $\eta_C$ | $1$ | Charge-conjugation phase | The intrinsic transformation phase. |
| $\electric{J}^{\mu}$ | $\unit{MeV^{3}}$ | Charge current | The current odd under charge conjugation. |
