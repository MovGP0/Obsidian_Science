---
title: "Canonical Anticommutation Relations"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Equal-time fermionic fields satisfy a field–adjoint anticommutator and vanishing same-type anticommutators.

Impose

$$
\{\signal{\psi}_{\alpha}(\mechanical{\mathbf x},\mechanical{t}),\signal{\psi}^{\dagger}_{\beta}(\mechanical{\mathbf y},\mechanical{t})\}=\delta_{\alpha\beta}\delta^{(3)}(\mechanical{\mathbf x}-\mechanical{\mathbf y}), \tag{4.25}
$$

$$
\{\signal{\psi}_{\alpha}(\mechanical{\mathbf x},\mechanical{t}),\signal{\psi}_{\beta}(\mechanical{\mathbf y},\mechanical{t})\}=0,\qquad
\{\signal{\psi}^{\dagger}_{\alpha}(\mechanical{\mathbf x},\mechanical{t}),\signal{\psi}^{\dagger}_{\beta}(\mechanical{\mathbf y},\mechanical{t})\}=0. \tag{4.26}
$$

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{\psi}$ | $\unit{MeV^{3/2}}$ | Dirac field | The canonical fermionic field. |
| $\mechanical{\mathbf x},\mechanical{\mathbf y},\mechanical{t}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Coordinates | The positions and common time in the canonical relation. |
