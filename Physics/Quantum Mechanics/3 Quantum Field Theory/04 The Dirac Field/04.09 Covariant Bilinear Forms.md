title: "Covariant Bilinear Forms"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Dirac bilinears organize the sixteen spinor-matrix components into Lorentz-covariant types.

A spinor is not itself a Lorentz scalar or four-vector. Pairing it with its adjoint and a chosen gamma-matrix structure produces quantities with definite transformation laws. This is the route from a spinor field to mass terms, currents, and possible interactions.

Read the classification before Fierz rearrangement: the sixteen matrices give the complete basis that makes rearrangement possible. Then compare vector and axial currents to see that the same Lorentz rank does not imply the same parity or conservation law. The free equations and the mass term, rather than the component count alone, decide those conservation statements.
