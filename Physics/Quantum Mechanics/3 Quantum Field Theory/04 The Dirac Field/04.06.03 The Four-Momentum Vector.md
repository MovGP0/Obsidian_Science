---
title: "The Four-Momentum Vector"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Fermions and antifermions both contribute positive on-shell energy and the momentum carried by their mode label.

The momentum density gives

$$
\mechanical{P}^{\mu}=\int\frac{d^{3}\mechanical{k}}{(2\pi)^{3}}\mechanical{p}^{\mu}\sum_s(b^{s\dagger}_{\mathbf p}b^{s}_{\mathbf p}+d^{s\dagger}_{\mathbf p}d^{s}_{\mathbf p}). \tag{4.41}
$$

Both particle types contribute with the momentum direction indicated by their mode label.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{P}^{\mu},\mechanical{p}^{\mu}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Four-momenta | The total and single-mode energy–momentum vectors. |
| $\mechanical{k}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Momentum | The source integration variable, retained alongside the p mode label. |
