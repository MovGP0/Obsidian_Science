---
title: "Fundamental Identities for Contractions"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The Clifford algebra reduces contracted gamma-matrix products to shorter expressions.

The Clifford algebra (4.4) gives the four-dimensional contraction identities
$$
\begin{aligned}
\gamma^\mu\gamma_\mu&=4,\\
\gamma^\mu\not a\gamma_\mu&=-2\not a,\\
\gamma^\mu\not a\not b\gamma_\mu&=4(a\cdot b),\\
\gamma^\mu\not a\not b\not c\gamma_\mu&=-2\not c\not b\not a.
\end{aligned} \tag{4.73}
$$
Here $\not a=\gamma^\nu a_\nu$, with the same notation for the arbitrary four-vectors $b$ and $c$. Scalar expressions on the right multiply the identity matrix.
