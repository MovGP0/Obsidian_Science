---
title: "Structure of Fock Space"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Particle and antiparticle creation operators generate fermionic Fock space from a vacuum annihilated by both mode families.

Define the vacuum by

$$
b^{s}_{\mathbf p}|0\rangle=0,\qquad d^{s}_{\mathbf p}|0\rangle=0\qquad\text{for every momentum and spin label}. \tag{4.32}
$$

A state with N fermions and M antifermions is

$$
|\mechanical{\mathbf p}_1,s_1;\ldots;\mechanical{\mathbf p}_N,s_N;\mechanical{\mathbf q}_1,\sigma_1;\ldots;\mechanical{\mathbf q}_M,\sigma_M\rangle
=b^{s_1\dagger}_{\mathbf p_1}\cdots b^{s_N\dagger}_{\mathbf p_N}d^{\sigma_1\dagger}_{\mathbf q_1}\cdots d^{\sigma_M\dagger}_{\mathbf q_M}|0\rangle. \tag{4.33}
$$

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{\mathbf p}_i,\mechanical{\mathbf q}_j$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Momenta | The fermion and antifermion mode labels. |
| $N,M$ | $1$ | Particle counts | The numbers of fermions and antifermions. |
