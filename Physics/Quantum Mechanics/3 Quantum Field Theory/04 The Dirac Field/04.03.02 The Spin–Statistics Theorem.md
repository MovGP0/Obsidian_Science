---
title: "The Spin–Statistics Theorem"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Relativistic locality and positive energy tie integer spin to bosonic commutators and half-integer spin to fermionic anticommutators.

The spin–statistics theorem requires integer-spin bosons to use commutators and half-integer-spin fermions to use anticommutators. The wrong choice conflicts with locality or energy positivity.

For fermions with the wrong statistics, the spectrum can be unbounded below or energy density can fail positivity. The rigorous proof, associated with Pauli (1940) and Lüders–Zumino (1958), uses positive state norms, commutation of spacelike observables, Lorentz covariance, and analytic correlation functions.
