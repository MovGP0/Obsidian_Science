---
title: "Mode Expansion and Creation and Annihilation Operators"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Particle and antiparticle modes expand the Dirac field and inherit fermionic operator anticommutators.

The expansion turns a solution basis into a quantum field. The mode spinors remain ordinary numerical coefficients; their amplitudes become operators on Fock space. Particle and antiparticle labels therefore describe states created by the coefficients, rather than four independently measurable components of the spinor.

Read the expansion and its normalization together with the operator algebra. One can move energy factors between the measure and the mode operators, but only if their anticommutators change consistently. A reliable reconstruction check is to insert the expansion back into the equal-time field anticommutator and recover its position delta function.
