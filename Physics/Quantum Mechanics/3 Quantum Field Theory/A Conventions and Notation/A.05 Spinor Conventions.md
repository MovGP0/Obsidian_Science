title: "Spinor Conventions"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Spinor conventions specify the Dirac adjoint, free solutions, normalization, completeness, and chirality projectors.

The following definitions fix the spinor conventions used in the text.

Read [[A.05.01 Dirac Adjoint]] before forming bilinears. Then use [[A.05.02 The Dirac Equation and Solution Spinors]] and [[A.05.03 Equations of Motion for the Spinors u and v]] to identify the signs of the two free modes. Both modes are labeled by positive on-shell energy.

Keep [[A.05.04 Spinor Normalization]] with [[A.05.05 Completeness Relations]] when comparing amplitudes: changing normalization changes spin sums and mode coefficients together. For unobserved final spins, sum; for an unpolarized initial state, also divide by the number of initial spin states. [[A.05.06 Chiral Projectors]] addresses a different decomposition: chirality is not generally a massive particle's spin projection along its momentum.
