---
title: "Metric and Relativistic Conventions"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Relativistic calculations use the mostly-minus metric and distinguish covariant from contravariant components.

Use the following metric and index conventions for relativistic expressions.

Start with [[A.02.01 Metric Tensor]] to fix the sign of a scalar product. Then use [[A.02.02 Covariant and Contravariant Indices]] to place indices before expanding a contraction. These are coordinate and notation choices in flat spacetime, not additional equations of motion.

A useful check before a long calculation is to contract a vector with itself: a purely temporal nonzero vector has positive squared norm, and a purely spatial nonzero vector has negative squared norm. For applications, compare [[A.03.02 Four-Momentum]] with [[A.03.03 Derivative Operator]]; the same metric fixes both the mass-shell sign and the wave-operator sign. Formulas taken from a mostly-plus reference must be translated consistently, including the gamma-matrix algebra.
