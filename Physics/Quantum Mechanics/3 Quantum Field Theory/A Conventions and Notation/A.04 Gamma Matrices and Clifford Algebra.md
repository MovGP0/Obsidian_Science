---
title: "Gamma Matrices and Clifford Algebra"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Gamma matrices satisfy the Clifford algebra. Dirac–Pauli and Weyl representations arrange these matrices for different calculations.

The following conventions specify the gamma matrices, their representations, and related matrix identities.

Use [[A.04.01 Clifford Algebra]] for identities that do not depend on a basis. Choose [[A.04.02 Dirac–Pauli Representation]] when the rest-frame particle and antiparticle blocks matter, or [[A.04.03 Weyl Representation (Chiral)]] when chirality matters. A change of spinor basis changes individual matrix entries but preserves the Clifford algebra and traces.

For amplitude reduction, go to [[A.04.06 Properties of Gamma Matrices]] for contractions and [[A.04.07 Trace Identities]] for closed spinor chains. Fix [[A.04.04 The Matrix γ⁵]] and [[A.06 Levi-Civita Tensor]] together before using a trace with chirality. The four-dimensional matrix representations do not themselves define a prescription for chirality in dimensional regularization.
