---
title: "Connected Decomposition"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Full Green functions decompose into products of connected correlators; the connected scattering amplitude is selected from the connected part.

An $n$-point Green function is a sum over partitions of the insertion points. Each partition contributes a product of connected Green functions for its subsets.

With a vanishing one-point function, the two- and four-point cases are
$$
\signal{G}^{(2)}(\mechanical{x}_1,\mechanical{x}_2)
=\signal{G}_c^{(2)}(\mechanical{x}_1,\mechanical{x}_2),
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\signal{G}^{(4)}(\mechanical{x}_1,\ldots,\mechanical{x}_4)
={}&\signal{G}_c^{(4)}(\mechanical{x}_1,\ldots,\mechanical{x}_4)\\
&+\signal{G}_c^{(2)}(\mechanical{x}_1,\mechanical{x}_2)
\signal{G}_c^{(2)}(\mechanical{x}_3,\mechanical{x}_4)\\
&+\signal{G}_c^{(2)}(\mechanical{x}_1,\mechanical{x}_3)
\signal{G}_c^{(2)}(\mechanical{x}_2,\mechanical{x}_4)\\
&+\signal{G}_c^{(2)}(\mechanical{x}_1,\mechanical{x}_4)
\signal{G}_c^{(2)}(\mechanical{x}_2,\mechanical{x}_3).
\end{aligned} \tag{6.6}
$$
The factorized terms describe disconnected propagation, with no momentum transfer between the separate groups.

The source argues that each two-point factor carries only one one-particle pole and a momentum delta distribution $(2\pi)^4\delta^{(4)}(\mechanical{p}+\mechanical{p}')$. It then claims that LSZ supplies more factors $(\mechanical{p}_j^{\,2}-\mechanical{m}_{\mathrm{ph}}^2)$ than there are poles, so all disconnected terms vanish.

> [!note]
> This pole-counting argument does not correctly handle the momentum-conservation distributions. LSZ reduction of the full Green function can retain disconnected S-matrix terms, including the identity and independent scattering of separate particle groups. To obtain the connected scattering amplitude, select the connected Green function explicitly. Vacuum-bubble cancellation is a separate normalization step.
>
> Equation (6.6) also assumes that the one-point function vanishes; otherwise partitions containing one-point factors must be included.

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
