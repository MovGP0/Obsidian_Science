---
title: "Differential Cross Section"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The differential cross section is the spin-summed squared amplitude times phase space, divided by the incoming flux.

For $1+2\to f$, the source gives
$$
\boxed{
d\mechanical{\sigma}
=\frac{\overline{\lvert\signal{\mathcal M}\rvert^2}}
{4\energy{E}_1\energy{E}_2
\lvert\mechanical{\mathbf v}_1-\mechanical{\mathbf v}_2\rvert}
\,d\signal{\Phi}_f}. \tag{6.7}
$$
The squared amplitude is summed over unobserved final spins and polarizations and averaged over unpolarized initial ones as appropriate.

The denominator is the incoming flux factor. In a laboratory frame where particle 2 is at rest,
$$
4\energy{E}_1\energy{E}_2
\lvert\mechanical{\mathbf v}_1-\mechanical{\mathbf v}_2\rvert
=4\energy{E}_1\mechanical{m}_2\lvert\mechanical{\mathbf v}_1\rvert
=4\lvert\mechanical{\mathbf p}_1\rvert\mechanical{m}_2.
$$
The invariant form is
$$
\energy{F}
=4\sqrt{(\mechanical{p}_1\cdot\mechanical{p}_2)^2
-\mechanical{m}_1^2\mechanical{m}_2^2}.
$$

> [!note]
> The simple relative-speed expression in (6.7) applies to collinear incoming velocities, including the center-of-mass and target-rest frames. For arbitrary noncollinear velocities, use the invariant flux or the Møller velocity $\sqrt{\lvert\mechanical{\mathbf v}_1-\mechanical{\mathbf v}_2\rvert^2-\lvert\mechanical{\mathbf v}_1\times\mechanical{\mathbf v}_2\rvert^2}$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{\sigma}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-2}}$ | Cross section | The scattering area in natural units. |
| $\signal{\mathcal M}$ | — | Invariant amplitude | The amplitude with process-dependent mass dimension. |
| $d\signal{\Phi}_f$ | — | Final-state phase space | The measure for the specified final particle count. |
| $\energy{E}_1,\energy{E}_2$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Incoming energies | The two beam-particle energies. |
| $\mechanical{\mathbf v}_1,\mechanical{\mathbf v}_2$ | $1$ | Incoming velocities | The particle velocities in the chosen frame. |
| $\mechanical{p}_1,\mechanical{p}_2,\mechanical{\mathbf p}_1$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Incoming momenta | The four-momenta and the first particle's spatial momentum. |
| $\mechanical{m}_1,\mechanical{m}_2$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Incoming masses | The two particle masses. |
| $\energy{F}$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Invariant flux | The covariant incoming-state normalization factor. |
