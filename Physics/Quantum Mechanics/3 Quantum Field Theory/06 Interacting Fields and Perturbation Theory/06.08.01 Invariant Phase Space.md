---
title: "Invariant Phase Space"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Lorentz-invariant phase space combines one on-shell measure per final particle with a total momentum-conservation delta distribution.

For $1+2\to3+4+\cdots+n$, there are two incoming and $n-2$ outgoing particles. The final-state measure is
$$
d\signal{\Phi}_n
=(2\pi)^4\delta^{(4)}
\left(\mechanical{P}_i-\sum_{j=3}^{n}\mechanical{p}_j\right)
\prod_{j=3}^{n}\frac{d^3\mechanical{\mathbf p}_j}
{(2\pi)^3\,2\energy{E}_j},
$$
where $\mechanical{P}_i=\mechanical{p}_1+\mechanical{p}_2$ is the total incoming four-momentum.

Each factor $d^3\mechanical{\mathbf p}/[2\energy{E}(2\pi)^3]$ is invariant. The corresponding on-shell identity is
$$
d^4\mechanical{p}\,
\delta(\mechanical{p}^{\,2}-\mechanical{m}^2)\theta(\mechanical{p}^0)
=\frac{d^3\mechanical{\mathbf p}}{2\energy{E}}.
$$
The on-shell delta distribution has two energy roots. The step function retains the positive one, and integration over $\mechanical{p}^0$ gives the Jacobian $1/(2\energy{E})$. This explains the denominator in each one-particle measure. The invariance statement uses proper orthochronous Lorentz transformations, which preserve the chosen positive-energy branch.

Do not read the subscript here as the final-particle count: it is the source's last particle label. If there are $N$ final particles, the measure has mass dimension $2N-4$, because each $d^3\mechanical{\mathbf p}/(2\energy{E})$ contributes two and the conservation delta contributes minus four. A forbidden reaction has no simultaneous positive-energy on-shell solution and therefore no phase-space support. Labeled identical final particles also require the permutation factor stated in [[06.08.03 Decay Width]].

With this source indexing, the phase-space measure has mass dimension $2n-8$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $d\signal{\Phi}_n$ | — | Invariant phase space | The measure for n minus two final particles; its dimension depends on their count. |
| $\mechanical{P}_i,\mechanical{p}_j,\mechanical{p}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Four-momenta | The total incoming momentum and individual particle momenta. |
| $\mechanical{\mathbf p}_j,\mechanical{\mathbf p}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Spatial momenta | The on-shell integration momenta. |
| $\energy{E}_j,\energy{E}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Particle energies | The positive on-shell energies. |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Particle mass | The mass in the invariant on-shell measure. |
