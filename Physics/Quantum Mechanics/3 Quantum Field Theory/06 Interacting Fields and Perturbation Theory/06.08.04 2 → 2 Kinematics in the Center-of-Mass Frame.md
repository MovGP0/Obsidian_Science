---
title: "2 → 2 Kinematics in the Center-of-Mass Frame"
---

> [!TLDR]
> In the center-of-mass frame, two-body scattering depends on the momentum ratio and the squared amplitude per solid angle.

For $\mechanical{p}_1+\mechanical{p}_2\to\mechanical{p}_3+\mechanical{p}_4$, work in the center-of-mass frame, where $\mechanical{\mathbf p}_1+\mechanical{\mathbf p}_2=0$.

Let $\lvert\mechanical{\mathbf p}_i\rvert$ and $\lvert\mechanical{\mathbf p}_f\rvert$ be the initial and final momentum magnitudes. The two-body phase space is
$$
d\signal{\Phi}_2
=\frac1{(2\pi)^2}
\frac{\lvert\mechanical{\mathbf p}_f\rvert}{4\sqrt{\energy{s}}}\,
d\mechanical{\Omega},
$$
where $\sqrt{\energy{s}}$ is the total center-of-mass energy, and
$d\mechanical{\Omega}=\sin\mechanical{\theta}\,d\mechanical{\theta}\,d\mechanical{\varphi}$ is the final-state solid angle.

The differential cross section is
$$
\left(\frac{d\mechanical{\sigma}}{d\mechanical{\Omega}}\right)_{\mathrm{CM}}
=\frac1{64\pi^2\energy{s}}
\frac{\lvert\mechanical{\mathbf p}_f\rvert}{\lvert\mechanical{\mathbf p}_i\rvert}
\,\overline{\lvert\signal{\mathcal M}\rvert^2}. \tag{6.9}
$$
For elastic scattering, $\mechanical{m}_3=\mechanical{m}_1$ and $\mechanical{m}_4=\mechanical{m}_2$, so the momentum magnitudes are equal.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{p}_1,\mechanical{p}_2,\mechanical{p}_3,\mechanical{p}_4$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | External four-momenta | The two incoming and two outgoing particle momenta. |
| $\mechanical{\mathbf p}_i,\mechanical{\mathbf p}_f$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Center-of-mass momenta | The incoming and outgoing spatial momentum magnitudes. |
| $d\signal{\Phi}_2$ | $1$ | Two-body phase space | The invariant measure for two outgoing particles. |
| $\energy{s}$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Invariant energy squared | The square of the total center-of-mass energy. |
| $\mechanical{\Omega},\mechanical{\theta},\mechanical{\varphi}$ | $1$ | Scattering angles | The solid, polar, and azimuthal angles. |
| $\mechanical{\sigma}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-2}}$ | Cross section | The scattering area in natural units. |
| $\signal{\mathcal M}$ | $1$ | Scattering amplitude | The invariant two-to-two amplitude. |
| $\mechanical{m}_1,\mechanical{m}_2,\mechanical{m}_3,\mechanical{m}_4$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Particle masses | The incoming and outgoing rest masses. |
