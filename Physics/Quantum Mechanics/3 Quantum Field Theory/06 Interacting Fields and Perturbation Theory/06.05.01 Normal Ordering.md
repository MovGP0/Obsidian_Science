---
title: "Normal Ordering"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Normal ordering places creation operators to the left of annihilation operators and removes vacuum expectation values of nonempty free-field products.

The real free scalar field expands as
$$
\signal{\phi}(\mechanical{x})
=\int\frac{d^3\mechanical{\mathbf k}}{(2\pi)^3}
\frac1{\sqrt{2\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k}}}
\left(a_{\mathbf k}e^{-i\mechanical{k}\cdot\mechanical{x}}
+a_{\mathbf k}^\dagger e^{i\mechanical{k}\cdot\mechanical{x}}\right),
$$
where $\mechanical{k}^0=\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k}
=\sqrt{\lvert\mechanical{\mathbf k}\rvert^2+\mechanical{m}^2}$. Define
$$
\signal{\phi}^{+}(\mechanical{x})
=\int\frac{d^3\mechanical{\mathbf k}}{(2\pi)^3}
\frac{a_{\mathbf k}}{\sqrt{2\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k}}}
e^{-i\mechanical{k}\cdot\mechanical{x}},
$$
$$
\signal{\phi}^{-}(\mechanical{x})
=\int\frac{d^3\mechanical{\mathbf k}}{(2\pi)^3}
\frac{a_{\mathbf k}^\dagger}{\sqrt{2\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k}}}
e^{i\mechanical{k}\cdot\mechanical{x}}.
$$
The normal-ordered product puts all creation operators to the left:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal N\{\signal{\phi}(\mechanical{x})\signal{\phi}(\mechanical{y})\}
={}&\signal{\phi}^{-}(\mechanical{x})\signal{\phi}^{-}(\mechanical{y})
+\signal{\phi}^{-}(\mechanical{x})\signal{\phi}^{+}(\mechanical{y})\\
&+\signal{\phi}^{-}(\mechanical{y})\signal{\phi}^{+}(\mechanical{x})
+\signal{\phi}^{+}(\mechanical{x})\signal{\phi}^{+}(\mechanical{y}).
\end{aligned}
$$
For a nonempty normal-ordered product of free fields with no constant term,
$$
\langle0|\mathcal N\{O\}|0\rangle=0.
$$
An annihilation operator kills the vacuum on the right; a creation operator kills the vacuum bra on the left.

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
