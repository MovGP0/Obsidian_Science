> [!TLDR]
> The Dyson formula expresses the S-matrix as a covariant expansion in the interaction density.

The Dyson formula answers how a local interaction density changes a free incoming state. [[06.04.01 Hamiltonian Density and Covariant Form]] first fixes the Hamiltonian sign and the conditions under which the Lagrangian can replace it. [[06.04.02 Perturbative Expansion]] then expands in interaction insertions.

Keep two factorials distinct: the Taylor-series factorial counts interchangeable insertions, while a factorial in the interaction density counts identical fields at one insertion. Wick contractions determine which factors cancel. This distinction is needed before a diagram's symmetry factor can be assigned.
