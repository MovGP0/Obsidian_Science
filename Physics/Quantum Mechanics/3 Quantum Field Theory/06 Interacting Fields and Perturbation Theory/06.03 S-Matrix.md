title: "S-Matrix"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The S-matrix connects asymptotic incoming and outgoing states and encodes scattering and decay amplitudes.

An experiment prepares incoming particles and counts outgoing channels. The S-matrix describes the transition between these asymptotic preparations. Its amplitudes become event probabilities only after the state normalization, incident flux, and final-state phase space have been specified.

Start with [[06.03.01 Definition]] to separate the identity contribution from the transition operator. Then read [[06.03.02 General Properties]] to see how probability conservation and spacetime symmetry restrict the result. The zero-interaction check is that the S-matrix becomes the identity; a nonzero overlap of unchanged states is not an interaction event.
