---
title: "Schrödinger, Heisenberg, and Dirac Pictures"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The Schrödinger, Heisenberg, and interaction pictures assign time dependence differently while describing the same physics.

Equivalent quantum pictures place time dependence in states, operators, or both.

## Schrödinger picture

States obey
$$
i\frac{\partial}{\partial\signal{t}}|\psi(\signal{t})\rangle_S
=\energy{H}|\psi(\signal{t})\rangle_S.
$$
Operators $O_S$ are constant apart from explicit time dependence. For a time-independent Hamiltonian,
$$
|\psi(\signal{t})\rangle_S=e^{-i\energy{H}\signal{t}}|\psi(0)\rangle_S.
$$

## Heisenberg picture

The state is constant: $|\psi\rangle_H=|\psi(0)\rangle_S$. Operators evolve as
$$
O_H(\signal{t})=e^{i\energy{H}\signal{t}}O_S e^{-i\energy{H}\signal{t}}.
$$

For an operator with no explicit time dependence, the Heisenberg equation is $\dot O_H=i[\energy{H},O_H]$. This picture is natural for field theory, where $\signal{\phi}(\mechanical{\mathbf x},\signal{t})$ is a spacetime-dependent operator.

## Interaction picture — Dirac picture

Split the full Hamiltonian into
$$
\energy{H}=\energy{H}_0+\energy{H}_{\mathrm{int}}.
$$
Operators evolve with the free part:
$$
O_I(\signal{t})
=e^{i\energy{H}_0\signal{t}}O_S e^{-i\energy{H}_0\signal{t}}.
$$
States evolve with the interaction expressed in this picture:
$$
i\frac{\partial}{\partial\signal{t}}|\psi(\signal{t})\rangle_I
=\energy{H}_I(\signal{t})|\psi(\signal{t})\rangle_I, \tag{6.1}
$$
where
$$
\energy{H}_I(\signal{t})
=e^{i\energy{H}_0\signal{t}}\energy{H}_{\mathrm{int}}e^{-i\energy{H}_0\signal{t}}.
$$
Interaction-picture fields $\signal{\phi}_I(\mechanical{x})$ obey the free equations of motion. Their free mode expansions can be used directly. The interaction effects are contained in the state-evolution equation (6.1).

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{t}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Time | The evolution parameter in each picture. |
| $\energy{H}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Hamiltonian | The full time-independent energy operator. |
| $\energy{H}_0,\energy{H}_{\mathrm{int}},\energy{H}_I$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Hamiltonian parts | The free Hamiltonian and the interaction in the two pictures. |
| $\signal{\phi},\signal{\phi}_I$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Scalar fields | The scalar field and its interaction-picture form. |
| $\mechanical{\mathbf x},\mechanical{x}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Coordinates | The spatial and spacetime field coordinates. |
