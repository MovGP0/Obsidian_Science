---
title: "Wick's Theorem"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Wick's theorem turns time-ordered products of free fields into normal-ordered products and propagator contractions.

A Dyson coefficient contains many operator products. Wick's theorem is useful because free-field commutators or anticommutators reduce their reordering corrections to numbers, namely contractions. The theorem does not replace an interacting correlation function by a Gaussian one; it evaluates the free-field products produced by its perturbative expansion.

Read [[06.05.01 Normal Ordering]] before [[06.05.02 Contractions and the Feynman Propagator]]. The proof in [[06.05.03 Statement and Proof]] shows why each field can appear in only one contracted pair. The examples in [[06.05.04 Applications]] then connect the algebra to a connected scattering amplitude. Keep the fermion permutation signs when transferring the method to spinor fields.
