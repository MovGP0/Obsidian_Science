---
title: "Total Cross Section and the Optical Theorem"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The optical theorem relates the integrated total cross section to the imaginary part of the forward scattering amplitude.

Integrate over all final-state phase space:
$$
\mechanical{\sigma}_{\mathrm{tot}}
=\int d\mechanical{\sigma}
=\frac1{\energy{F}}\int
\overline{\lvert\signal{\mathcal M}\rvert^2}\,d\signal{\Phi}_f,
$$
where $\energy{F}=4\sqrt{(\mechanical{p}_1\cdot\mechanical{p}_2)^2-\mechanical{m}_1^2\mechanical{m}_2^2}$ is the invariant flux. The integral includes the sum over allowed final channels.

The optical theorem gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mechanical{\sigma}_{\mathrm{tot}}
&=\frac1{2\energy{E}_{\mathrm{CM}}\lvert\mechanical{\mathbf p}_{\mathrm{CM}}\rvert}
\operatorname{Im}\signal{\mathcal M}(\energy{s},\mechanical{t}=0)\\
&=\frac1{2\sqrt{\energy{s}}\lvert\mechanical{\mathbf p}_{\mathrm{CM}}\rvert}
\operatorname{Im}\signal{\mathcal M}(\energy{s},\mechanical{t}=0).
\end{aligned} \tag{6.10}
$$

The CM momentum magnitude is common to the two initial particles. The equivalent invariant form is
$$
\operatorname{Im}\signal{\mathcal M}(\energy{s},\mechanical{t}=0)
=\frac{\energy{F}\mechanical{\sigma}_{\mathrm{tot}}}{2}
=2\sqrt{(\mechanical{p}_1\cdot\mechanical{p}_2)^2
-\mechanical{m}_1^2\mechanical{m}_2^2}\,
\mechanical{\sigma}_{\mathrm{tot}}.
$$
This follows directly from S-matrix unitarity and has both theoretical and experimental uses.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{\sigma}_{\mathrm{tot}}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-2}}$ | Total cross section | The integrated scattering area. |
| $\energy{F}$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Invariant flux | The normalization factor for the two incoming particles. |
| $\signal{\mathcal M}$ | — | Invariant amplitude | Dimensionless for the forward two-to-two amplitude; other channels depend on final multiplicity. |
| $d\signal{\Phi}_f$ | — | Final-state phase space | The measure with dimension set by final multiplicity. |
| $\mechanical{p}_1,\mechanical{p}_2$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Incoming four-momenta | The two initial particle momenta. |
| $\mechanical{m}_1,\mechanical{m}_2$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Incoming masses | The masses in the invariant flux. |
| $\energy{E}_{\mathrm{CM}}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | CM energy | The total center-of-mass energy. |
| $\energy{s}$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Collision energy squared | The invariant square of the total incoming momentum. |
| $\mechanical{\mathbf p}_{\mathrm{CM}}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | CM momentum | The initial spatial momentum magnitude. |
| $\mechanical{t}$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Momentum transfer squared | Zero for forward scattering. |
