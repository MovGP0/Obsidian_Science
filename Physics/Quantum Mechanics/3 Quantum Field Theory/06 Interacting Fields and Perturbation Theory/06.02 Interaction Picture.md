title: "Interaction Picture"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The interaction picture separates free time evolution from the part generated by the interaction Hamiltonian.

The calculation problem is to retain the exactly known free motion while approximating only the interaction. First use [[06.02.01 Schrödinger, Heisenberg, and Dirac Pictures]] to follow how the same expectation value is written in each picture. Then use [[06.02.02 Time-Evolution Operator and Dyson Series]] to derive the operator that changes the interaction-picture state.

The separation is a choice of representation. It does not turn the interacting particles into free particles. A useful check is to set the interaction to zero: the interaction-picture state then stays constant, while its field operators still have free time dependence.
