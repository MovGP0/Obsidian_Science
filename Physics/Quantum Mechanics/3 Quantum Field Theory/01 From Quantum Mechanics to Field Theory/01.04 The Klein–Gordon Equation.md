> [!TLDR]
> The Klein–Gordon equation follows from the relativistic energy–momentum relation and has a Lorentz-covariant form.

Can the relativistic mass-shell relation produce a wave equation without choosing one inertial frame? For a free scalar amplitude it gives the Klein–Gordon equation. Its covariance answers the transformation question, but does not establish a positive local probability density.

Follow the derivation first, then identify the two frequency branches in the plane waves. Finally evaluate the conserved current on each branch. Keep these tests separate: a covariant equation can be useful as a field equation even when its proposed single-particle probability interpretation fails.
