---
title: "Nonconservation of Particle Number"
---

> [!TLDR]
> High-energy processes can create many particles, so a theory with a fixed particle number is insufficient.

At sufficient energy, particle number need not be conserved. A high-energy collision between two protons can produce dozens or hundreds of particles. A Schrödinger equation with a fixed number of particles cannot describe this behavior.

Particle-number conservation is an approximate rule in the nonrelativistic limit. The relativistic equations, Klein–Gordon and Dirac, must be incorporated into a description that permits particle number to change with time. The next section develops that field-based description.
