---
title: "Metric Convention"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The metric signature (+, −, −, −) gives the Lorentz scalar product and the relativistic energy–momentum relation used in this book.

The flat Minkowski metric has signature $(+,-,-,-)$:

$$
g^{\mu\nu}=g_{\mu\nu}=\operatorname{diag}(+1,-1,-1,-1). \tag{1.3}
$$

An event has coordinates $\mechanical{x}^{\mu}=(\mechanical{t},\mechanical{\mathbf x})=(\mechanical{x}^{0},\mechanical{x}^{1},\mechanical{x}^{2},\mechanical{x}^{3})$. Greek indices take the values $\mu,\nu=0,1,2,3$. Lowering and raising indices uses the metric. Thus $\mechanical{x}_{\mu}=g_{\mu\nu}\mechanical{x}^{\nu}=(\mechanical{t},-\mechanical{\mathbf x})$.

For two four-vectors $a^{\mu}$ and $b^{\mu}$, the Lorentz scalar product is

$$
a\cdot b=a^{\mu}b_{\mu}=a^{0}b^{0}-\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b. \tag{1.4}
$$

The four-momentum is $\mechanical{p}^{\mu}=(\energy{E},\mechanical{\mathbf p})$. Its squared norm is

$$
\mechanical{p}^{2}=\mechanical{p}^{\mu}\mechanical{p}_{\mu}
=\energy{E}^{2}-\mechanical{\mathbf p}^{2}=\mechanical{m}^{2}. \tag{1.5}
$$

The last equality is the mass-shell relation for a particle of mass $\mechanical{m}$, in natural units with $\mechanical{c}=1$.

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{x}^{\mu}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Spacetime coordinate | The event coordinates in natural units. |
| $\mechanical{t}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Time | The temporal coordinate in natural units. |
| $\mechanical{\mathbf x}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Position | The spatial coordinates of the event. |
| $\mechanical{p}^{\mu}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Four-momentum | The energy and three-momentum of the particle. |
