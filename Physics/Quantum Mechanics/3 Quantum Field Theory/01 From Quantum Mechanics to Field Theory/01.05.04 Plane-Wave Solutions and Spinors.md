---
title: "Plane-Wave Solutions and Spinors"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Plane-wave Dirac solutions reduce the field equation to algebraic equations for four-component spinors.

Seek plane-wave solutions of the Dirac equation:

$$
\signal{\psi}(\mechanical{x})=\signal{u}(\mechanical{\mathbf p})e^{-i\mechanical{p}\cdot\mechanical{x}}, \tag{1.41}
$$

where $\signal{u}(\mechanical{\mathbf p})$ is a four-component spinor. Substitution gives

$$
(\not{p}-\mechanical{m})\signal{u}(\mechanical{\mathbf p})=0, \tag{1.42}
$$

with $\mechanical{p}^{\mu}=(\energy{E},\mechanical{\mathbf p})$. Write the spinor in two two-component blocks:

$$
\signal{u}(\mechanical{\mathbf p})=\begin{pmatrix}\signal{\xi}\\\signal{\chi}\end{pmatrix}. \tag{1.43}
$$

The spinors $\signal{\xi}$ and $\signal{\chi}$ become the large and small components, respectively, in the nonrelativistic limit. Equation (1.42) becomes

$$
\begin{pmatrix}
\energy{E}-\mechanical{m}&-\boldsymbol{\sigma}\cdot\mechanical{\mathbf p}\\
\boldsymbol{\sigma}\cdot\mechanical{\mathbf p}&-\energy{E}-\mechanical{m}
\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}\signal{\xi}\\\signal{\chi}\end{pmatrix}=0. \tag{1.44}
$$

Thus,

$$
(\energy{E}-\mechanical{m})\signal{\xi}
=(\boldsymbol{\sigma}\cdot\mechanical{\mathbf p})\signal{\chi},
$$

$$
(\energy{E}+\mechanical{m})\signal{\chi}
=(\boldsymbol{\sigma}\cdot\mechanical{\mathbf p})\signal{\xi}.
$$

For $\energy{E}+\mechanical{m}\ne0$, the second relation gives

$$
\signal{\chi}=\frac{\boldsymbol{\sigma}\cdot\mechanical{\mathbf p}}{\energy{E}+\mechanical{m}}\signal{\xi}. \tag{1.45}
$$

Substitute this into the first relation and use $(\boldsymbol{\sigma}\cdot\mechanical{\mathbf p})^{2}=|\mechanical{\mathbf p}|^{2}$. This gives $(\energy{E}^{2}-\mechanical{m}^{2}-|\mechanical{\mathbf p}|^{2})\signal{\xi}=0$, which holds for $\energy{E}=\pm\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf p}$.

For $\energy{E}=+\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf p}$, the positive-energy spinors are

$$
\signal{u}^{(s)}(\mechanical{\mathbf p})
=\sqrt{\energy{E}+\mechanical{m}}
\begin{pmatrix}
\signal{\xi}^{(s)}\\
\dfrac{\boldsymbol{\sigma}\cdot\mechanical{\mathbf p}}{\energy{E}+\mechanical{m}}\signal{\xi}^{(s)}
\end{pmatrix},\qquad s=1,2. \tag{1.46}
$$

The basis spinors for the two spin polarizations are

$$
\signal{\xi}^{(1)}=\begin{pmatrix}1\\0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\signal{\xi}^{(2)}=\begin{pmatrix}0\\1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

For negative-energy solutions, write $\signal{\psi}(\mechanical{x})=\signal{v}(\mechanical{\mathbf p})e^{+i\mechanical{p}\cdot\mechanical{x}}$. Then

$$
\signal{v}^{(s)}(\mechanical{\mathbf p})
=\sqrt{\energy{E}+\mechanical{m}}
\begin{pmatrix}
\dfrac{\boldsymbol{\sigma}\cdot\mechanical{\mathbf p}}{\energy{E}+\mechanical{m}}\signal{\eta}^{(s)}\\
\signal{\eta}^{(s)}
\end{pmatrix},\qquad s=1,2, \tag{1.47}
$$

where

$$
\signal{\eta}^{(1)}=\begin{pmatrix}1\\0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\signal{\eta}^{(2)}=\begin{pmatrix}0\\1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

The covariant normalization relations are

$$
\overline{\signal{u}}^{(r)}(\mechanical{\mathbf p})\signal{u}^{(s)}(\mechanical{\mathbf p})
=2\mechanical{m}\delta^{rs},
$$

$$
\overline{\signal{v}}^{(r)}(\mechanical{\mathbf p})\signal{v}^{(s)}(\mechanical{\mathbf p})
=-2\mechanical{m}\delta^{rs},
$$

$$
\overline{\signal{u}}^{(r)}(\mechanical{\mathbf p})\signal{v}^{(s)}(\mechanical{\mathbf p})=0.
$$

The Dirac adjoint is $\overline{\signal{\psi}}=\signal{\psi}^{\dagger}\gamma^{0}$. The completeness relations are

$$
\sum_{s=1}^{2}\signal{u}^{(s)}(\mechanical{\mathbf p})\overline{\signal{u}}^{(s)}(\mechanical{\mathbf p})
=\not{p}+\mechanical{m},
$$

$$
\sum_{s=1}^{2}\signal{v}^{(s)}(\mechanical{\mathbf p})\overline{\signal{v}}^{(s)}(\mechanical{\mathbf p})
=\not{p}-\mechanical{m}.
$$

Chapter 8 uses these completeness relations to calculate cross sections.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{\psi}$ | — | Dirac wavefunction | The plane-wave solution in spacetime. |
| $\signal{u}$ | — | Positive-energy spinor | The momentum-dependent four-component amplitude. |
| $\signal{\xi}$, $\signal{\chi}$ | — | Two-component spinors | The upper and lower blocks of the Dirac spinor. |
| $\mechanical{x}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Spacetime position | The coordinates in the plane-wave phase. |
| $\mechanical{p}$, $\mechanical{\mathbf p}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Momentum | The four-momentum and three-momentum of the state. |
| $\energy{E}$, $\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf p}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Energy | The signed energy and the positive on-shell energy. |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Mass | The rest mass in the Dirac equation. |
| $\signal{v}$ | — | Negative-energy spinor | The four-component amplitude of the negative-energy branch. |
| $\signal{\eta}$ | $1$ | Spin basis amplitude | The basis spinors for the negative-energy branch. |
