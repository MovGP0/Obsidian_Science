---
title: "Natural Units"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Natural units set the reduced Planck constant and the speed of light to one, so physical quantities can be expressed as powers of an energy unit.

Particle physics and field theory normally use natural units, defined by

$$
\energy{\hbar}=\mechanical{c}=1. \tag{1.2}
$$

The constants $\energy{\hbar}$ and $\mechanical{c}$ convert between conventional units, such as seconds, meters, and kilograms. These separate unit systems have no fundamental role in the theory.

With $\energy{\hbar}=\mechanical{c}=1$, all physical quantities are measured in powers of one energy unit. The usual choice is the electronvolt, $\unit{eV}$, or a multiple such as $\unit{MeV}$, $\unit{GeV}$, or $\unit{TeV}$.

Useful conversion relations are

$$
1\,\unit{fm}=1\,\unit{Fermi}=10^{-15}\,\unit{m}
\approx\frac{1}{197.3\,\unit{MeV}},
$$

$$
1\,\unit{s}\approx1.52\times10^{24}\,\unit{GeV^{-1}}.
$$

$$
1\,\unit{kg}\approx5.61\times10^{26}\,\unit{GeV}.
$$

The dimensions of the basic quantities become:

| Quantity | SI unit | Natural unit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Length | $\unit{m}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ |
| Time | $\unit{s}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ |
| Mass | $\unit{kg}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ |
| Momentum | $\unit{kg\,m\,s^{-1}}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ |
| Energy | $\unit{J}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ |

The fine-structure constant is $\electric{\alpha}=\electric{e}^2/(\energy{\hbar}\mechanical{c})\approx1/137$ in Gaussian units. In the Heaviside–Lorentz system with $\energy{\hbar}=\mechanical{c}=1$, it is $\electric{\alpha}=\electric{e}^2/(4\pi)\approx1/137$. This book uses the Heaviside–Lorentz system for quantum electrodynamics.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\energy{\hbar}$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Reduced Planck constant | The action constant set to one in natural units. |
| $\mechanical{c}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Speed of light | The vacuum speed of light, set to one in natural units. |
| — | $\unit{m}$ | Length | A femtometer expressed as an inverse energy in natural units. |
| — | $\unit{s}$ | Time | A second expressed as an inverse energy in natural units. |
| — | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | A kilogram expressed as an energy in natural units. |
| $\electric{\alpha}$ | $1$ | Fine-structure constant | The dimensionless electromagnetic coupling strength. |
| $\electric{e}$ | $1$ | Elementary charge | The electromagnetic coupling in the stated natural-unit convention. |
