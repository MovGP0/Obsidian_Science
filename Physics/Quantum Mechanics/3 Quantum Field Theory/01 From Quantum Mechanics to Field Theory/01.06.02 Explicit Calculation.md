---
title: "Explicit Calculation"
---

> [!TLDR]
> One-dimensional stationary Dirac solutions reduce potential-step scattering to a two-component matching problem.

Consider motion along the x-axis and use reduced two-component spinors. The Dirac equation in 1+1 dimensions with potential $\energy{V}(\mechanical{x})$ is

$$
\left[i\gamma^{\mu}\partial_{\mu}-\energy{V}(\mechanical{x})\gamma^{0}-\mechanical{m}\right]\signal{\psi}=0. \tag{1.55}
$$

Seek stationary solutions $\signal{\psi}(\mechanical{t},\mechanical{x})=e^{-i\energy{E}\mechanical{t}}\signal{\psi}(\mechanical{x})$.

## Region I

For $\mechanical{x}<0$ and $\energy{V}=0$, the incident and reflected waves are

$$
\signal{\psi}_{\mathrm I}(\mechanical{x})
=\signal{A}\begin{pmatrix}1\\q_1\end{pmatrix}e^{i\mechanical{k}_1\mechanical{x}}
+\signal{B}\begin{pmatrix}1\\-q_1\end{pmatrix}e^{-i\mechanical{k}_1\mechanical{x}}, \tag{1.56}
$$

where $\mechanical{k}_1=\sqrt{\energy{E}^{2}-\mechanical{m}^{2}}$ is the momentum and $q_1=\mechanical{k}_1/(\energy{E}+\mechanical{m})$.

## Region II

For $\mechanical{x}>0$ and $\energy{V}=\energy{V}_0$, the momentum is

$$
\mechanical{k}_2=\sqrt{(\energy{E}-\energy{V}_0)^{2}-\mechanical{m}^{2}}. \tag{1.57}
$$

The transmitted wave is

$$
\signal{\psi}_{\mathrm{II}}(\mechanical{x})
=\signal{C}\begin{pmatrix}1\\q_2\end{pmatrix}e^{i\mechanical{k}_2\mechanical{x}}, \tag{1.58}
$$

with $q_2=\mechanical{k}_2/(\energy{E}-\energy{V}_0+\mechanical{m})$.

## Matching at the Step

Continuity at $\mechanical{x}=0$ requires $\signal{\psi}_{\mathrm I}(0)=\signal{\psi}_{\mathrm{II}}(0)$:

$$
\signal{A}+\signal{B}=\signal{C}, \tag{1.59}
$$

$$
q_1(\signal{A}-\signal{B})=q_2\signal{C}. \tag{1.60}
$$

Solving gives

$$
\frac{\signal{B}}{\signal{A}}=\frac{q_1-q_2}{q_1+q_2},\qquad
\frac{\signal{C}}{\signal{A}}=\frac{2q_1}{q_1+q_2}. \tag{1.61}
$$

The reflection and transmission coefficients refer to probability current, rather than wavefunction amplitude:

$$
\signal{R}=\left|\frac{\signal{B}}{\signal{A}}\right|^{2}
=\left(\frac{q_1-q_2}{q_1+q_2}\right)^{2},
$$

$$
\signal{T}=\frac{q_2}{q_1}\left|\frac{\signal{C}}{\signal{A}}\right|^{2}
=\frac{4q_1q_2}{(q_1+q_2)^{2}}.
$$

Direct substitution verifies $\signal{R}+\signal{T}=1$, as required by current conservation.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{\psi}$ | — | Dirac amplitude | The stationary two-component wavefunction. |
| $\energy{V}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Potential energy | The electrostatic step potential. |
| $\mechanical{x}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Position | The coordinate along the direction of motion. |
| $\mechanical{t}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Time | The time coordinate of the stationary solution. |
| $\energy{E}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Energy | The stationary-state energy. |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Mass | The particle rest mass. |
| $\energy{V}_0$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Step energy | The constant potential in Region II. |
| $\mechanical{k}_1$, $\mechanical{k}_2$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Momenta | The particle momenta in the two regions. |
| $\signal{A}$, $\signal{B}$, $\signal{C}$ | — | Wave amplitudes | The incident, reflected, and transmitted amplitudes. |
| $\signal{R}$, $\signal{T}$ | $1$ | Current coefficients | The reflected and transmitted currents relative to the incident current. |
