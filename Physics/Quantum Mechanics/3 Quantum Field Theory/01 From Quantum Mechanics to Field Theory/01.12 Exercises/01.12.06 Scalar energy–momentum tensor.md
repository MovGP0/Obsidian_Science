## Exercise 1.6 — Energy–Momentum Tensor of the Scalar Field

**(a)** Derive $\energy{T}^{\mu\nu}$ for the real scalar field with Lagrangian (1.76), using (1.80).

**(b)** Calculate $\energy{T}^{00}$, $\energy{T}^{0i}$, and $\energy{T}^{ij}$ explicitly.

**(c)** Verify that $\energy{T}^{00}=\energy{\mathcal H}$.

**(d)** Show that

$$
\mechanical{P}^{\mu}=\int\frac{d^{3}\mechanical{k}}{(2\pi)^{3}}\energy{T}^{0\mu}
$$

is a conserved four-vector by calculating it for the classical field evaluated on the plane-wave solution (1.12).

> [!note]
> Part (d) preserves the source's momentum-space integration measure, as in Exercise 1.4 and [[01.10.02 Noether's Theorem]].

## Solution 1.6 — Scalar energy–momentum tensor

**(a)** For
$$
\energy{\mathcal L}
=\frac12(\partial_\mu\signal{\phi})(\partial^\mu\signal{\phi})
-\frac12\mechanical{m}^2\signal{\phi}^2,
$$
the canonical tensor is
$$
\energy{T}^{\mu\nu}
=\frac{\partial\energy{\mathcal L}}{\partial(\partial_\mu\signal{\phi})}
\partial^\nu\signal{\phi}-g^{\mu\nu}\energy{\mathcal L}
=\partial^\mu\signal{\phi}\,\partial^\nu\signal{\phi}
-g^{\mu\nu}\energy{\mathcal L}.
$$

**(b)** The explicit tensor components are
$$
\energy{T}^{00}
=\dot{\signal{\phi}}^{\,2}-\energy{\mathcal L}
=\frac12\dot{\signal{\phi}}^{\,2}
+\frac12\lvert\boldsymbol\nabla\signal{\phi}\rvert^2
+\frac12\mechanical{m}^2\signal{\phi}^2,
$$
$$
\energy{T}^{0i}
=\dot{\signal{\phi}}\,\partial^i\signal{\phi}
=-\dot{\signal{\phi}}\,\partial_i\signal{\phi},
$$
$$
\energy{T}^{ij}
=\partial^i\signal{\phi}\,\partial^j\signal{\phi}
-g^{ij}\energy{\mathcal L}
=\partial^i\signal{\phi}\,\partial^j\signal{\phi}
+\delta^{ij}\energy{\mathcal L}.
$$

**(c)** Since the canonical momentum density is
$\mechanical{\pi}=\dot{\signal{\phi}}$,
$$
\energy{T}^{00}
=\frac12\mechanical{\pi}^2
+\frac12\lvert\boldsymbol\nabla\signal{\phi}\rvert^2
+\frac12\mechanical{m}^2\signal{\phi}^2
=\energy{\mathcal H}.
$$

**(d)** For the real plane-wave solution
$$
\signal{\phi}(\mechanical{x})
=\signal{a}e^{-i\mechanical{p}\cdot\mechanical{x}}
+\signal{a}^*e^{+i\mechanical{p}\cdot\mechanical{x}},
\qquad
\mechanical{p}^2=\mechanical{m}^2,
$$
the derivative is
$$
\partial_\mu\signal{\phi}
=-i\mechanical{p}_\mu\signal{a}e^{-i\mechanical{p}\cdot\mechanical{x}}
+i\mechanical{p}_\mu\signal{a}^*e^{+i\mechanical{p}\cdot\mechanical{x}}.
$$
Integrate over a large volume $\mechanical{V}$ and average over time so that oscillating terms vanish. The result is
$$
\mechanical{P}^\mu
=\int d^3\mechanical{\mathbf x}\,\energy{T}^{0\mu}
=\mechanical{p}^\mu\lvert\signal{a}\rvert^2\,2\signal{\omega}_{\mathbf p}\mechanical{V}.
$$
The normalization depends on the chosen plane-wave amplitude.
The total four-momentum is proportional to the mode four-momentum, with $\mechanical{P}^0>0$ for positive mode energy $\signal{\omega}_{\mathbf p}$.

Local conservation follows from the Klein–Gordon equation:
$$
\partial_\mu\energy{T}^{\mu\nu}
=(\Box\signal{\phi}+\mechanical{m}^2\signal{\phi})\partial^\nu\signal{\phi}
=0.
$$

> [!note]
> Unlike the momentum-space measure printed in the exercise statement, the supplied solution correctly integrates the local tensor over spatial coordinates, $d^3\mechanical{\mathbf x}$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{\phi}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Scalar field | The field with negative mass squared. |
| $\mechanical{\mathbf p}$, $\mechanical{k}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Momentum | The state label and the source charge-integration variable. |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Particle mass | The rest-mass parameter in the scattering and field equations. |
| $\energy{T}^{\mu\nu}$ | $\unit{MeV^{4}}$ | Energy–momentum tensor | The scalar-field tensor calculated in Exercise 1.6. |
| $\energy{\mathcal H}$ | $\unit{MeV^{4}}$ | Hamiltonian density | The energy density compared with the tensor's time component. |
| $\mechanical{P}^{\mu}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Four-momentum | The conserved field energy–momentum. |
| $\energy{\mathcal L}$ | $\unit{MeV^{4}}$ | Lagrangian density | The local dynamical density of the Proca field. |
| $\mechanical{p}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Mode four-momentum | The mode four-momentum, or its positive longitudinal magnitude where specified. |
| $\mechanical{x},\mechanical{\mathbf x}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Spacetime and spatial coordinates | The coordinates in the plane-wave and growing-mode phases. |
| $\mechanical{\pi}$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Canonical scalar momentum density | The field momentum equal to the time derivative of the scalar. |
| $\signal{a}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Classical plane-wave amplitude | The complex amplitude in the real scalar solution. |
| $\mechanical{V}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-3}}$ | Spatial normalization volume | The large volume over which the tensor is integrated. |
| $\signal{\omega}_{\mathbf p}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Positive mode energy | The plane-wave frequency in natural units. |
