> [!TLDR]
> Dirac sought a relativistic wave equation that is first order in both time and space.

The goal is a local free equation for a massive spin-one-half particle with first-order time evolution and the relativistic dispersion relation. The coefficient matrices introduce spinor components; they are not extra spatial coordinates.

Read the strategy and Clifford algebra together to see how squaring the equation recovers the mass shell. Use the explicit representation only after that algebra is clear. The plane-wave and current notes then test two different properties: the spectrum and a nonnegative density. The final note explains why the second property does not remove negative-energy branches.
