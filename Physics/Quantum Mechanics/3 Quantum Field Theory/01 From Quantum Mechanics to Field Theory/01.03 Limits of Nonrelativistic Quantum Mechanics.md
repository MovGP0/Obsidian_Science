title: "Limits of Nonrelativistic Quantum Mechanics"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The Schrödinger equation treats time and space differently, which prevents Lorentz covariance.

[[01.03.01 Covariance of the Schrödinger Equation]] examines this limitation and introduces the relativistic dispersion relation as a starting point for a covariant wave equation.
