---
title: "Bare and Renormalized Quantities"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Field and parameter renormalization rewrites the bare QED Lagrangian as a renormalized Lagrangian plus counterterms.

The QED Lagrangian in bare, unrenormalized fields and parameters is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\energy{\mathcal{L}_0}
={}&\bar{\signal{\psi}}_0(i\not\partial-\mechanical{m_0})\signal{\psi_0}
-\frac14\electric{F}_{0\mu\nu}\electric{F}_0^{\mu\nu}\\
&-\electric{e_0}\bar{\signal{\psi}}_0\gamma^\mu\signal{\psi_0}\electric{A}_{0\mu}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{9.79}
$$
Define the renormalized fields by
$$
\signal{\psi_0}=\signal{Z_2}^{1/2}\signal{\psi},
\qquad
\electric{A_0}^\mu=\signal{Z_3}^{1/2}\electric{A}^\mu.
$$
The physical, renormalized parameters satisfy
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mechanical{m_0}
&=\mechanical{m}+\mechanical{\delta m}=\signal{Z_m}\mechanical{m},\\
\electric{e_0}
&=\signal{Z_e}\electric{e}
=\frac{\signal{Z_1}}{\signal{Z_2}\signal{Z_3}^{1/2}}\electric{e}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Substitute these definitions into the bare Lagrangian:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\energy{\mathcal{L}_0}
={}&\signal{Z_2}\bar{\signal{\psi}}(i\not\partial)\signal{\psi}
-\signal{Z_2}\signal{Z_m}\mechanical{m}\bar{\signal{\psi}}\signal{\psi}\\
&-\frac{\signal{Z_3}}4\electric{F}_{\mu\nu}\electric{F}^{\mu\nu}
-\signal{Z_1}\electric{e}\bar{\signal{\psi}}\gamma^\mu\signal{\psi}\electric{A}_\mu\\
={}&\energy{\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{rin}}}
+\energy{\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{ct}}}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{9.80}
$$
Here $\energy{\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{rin}}}$ is the renormalized Lagrangian with physical parameters. The counterterms are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\energy{\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{ct}}}
={}&(\signal{Z_2}-1)\bar{\signal{\psi}}\,i\not\partial\signal{\psi}
-(\signal{Z_2}\signal{Z_m}-1)\mechanical{m}\bar{\signal{\psi}}\signal{\psi}\\
&-\frac{\signal{Z_3}-1}{4}\electric{F}_{\mu\nu}\electric{F}^{\mu\nu}
-(\signal{Z_1}-1)\electric{e}\bar{\signal{\psi}}\gamma^\mu\signal{\psi}\electric{A}_\mu.
\end{aligned}
\tag{9.81}
$$
Write $\signal{\delta_i}=\signal{Z_i}-1$.
At one loop in the $\overline{\mathrm{MS}}$ scheme, the source gives
$$
\signal{\delta_2}
=-\frac{\electric{\alpha}}{4\pi}\frac1{\bar\epsilon}
+\text{finite terms}.
\tag{9.82}
$$

> [!note]
> The source uses the label $\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{rin}}$ for the renormalized Lagrangian. The charge equations are written in the four-dimensional convention; dimensional regularization also requires $\energy{\mu}^\epsilon$ when a dimensionful bare charge is related to a dimensionless renormalized charge. Renormalized parameters coincide with directly measured on-shell parameters only after suitable renormalization conditions are imposed.

The source continues the one-loop constants:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\signal{\delta_m}=\signal{Z_m}-1
&=-\frac{3\electric{\alpha}}{4\pi}\frac1{\bar\epsilon}
+\text{finite terms},\\
\signal{\delta_3}
&=-\frac{\electric{\alpha}}{3\pi}\frac1{\bar\epsilon}
+\text{finite terms},\\
\signal{\delta_1}&=\signal{\delta_2}
\qquad(\signal{Z_1}=\signal{Z_2}).
\end{aligned}
\tag{9.83}
$$
The source then states
$\signal{\delta_m}\ne\mechanical{\delta m}/\mechanical{m}$.
It calls $\signal{\delta_m}=\signal{Z_m}-1$ the multiplicative mass renormalization.
It instead describes $\mechanical{\delta m}=\mechanical{m_{\mathrm{phys}}}-\mechanical{m}$ as the difference between the physical and renormalized masses, and says this difference is positive in the on-shell scheme.

> [!note]
> This statement conflicts with the definitions above. The relation $\mechanical{m_0}=\mechanical{m}+\mechanical{\delta m}=\signal{Z_m}\mechanical{m}$ directly gives $\signal{\delta_m}=\mechanical{\delta m}/\mechanical{m}$. If $\mechanical{m}$ denotes the on-shell renormalized mass, it already equals $\mechanical{m_{\mathrm{phys}}}$, so their difference is zero. A bare-mass counterterm is a different quantity, and its sign is not universally positive.
>
> The “finite terms” in (9.82) and (9.83) describe a general subtraction choice. Strict $\overline{\mathrm{MS}}$ counterterms subtract the $1/\bar\epsilon$ poles without additional finite parts.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\energy{\mathcal{L}_0},\energy{\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{rin}}},\energy{\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{ct}}}$ | $\unit{MeV^4}$ | Lagrangian densities | The bare, renormalized, and counterterm parts of the QED Lagrangian. |
| $\signal{\psi_0},\signal{\psi},\bar{\signal{\psi}}_0,\bar{\signal{\psi}}$ | $\unit{MeV^{3/2}}$ | Dirac fields | The bare and renormalized electron fields and their adjoints. |
| $\electric{A_0}^\mu,\electric{A}^\mu$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Electromagnetic potentials | The bare and renormalized photon fields. |
| $\electric{F}_{0\mu\nu},\electric{F}_{\mu\nu}$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Electromagnetic field tensors | The tensors formed from the respective photon fields. |
| $\mechanical{m_0},\mechanical{m},\mechanical{\delta m}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Bare mass, renormalized mass, and mass counterterm | The electron mass parameters related by additive and multiplicative renormalization. |
| $\electric{e_0},\electric{e}$ | — | Bare and renormalized charges | Dimensionless in the displayed four-dimensional relations; the bare charge has mass dimension ε in d dimensions. |
| $\signal{Z_1},\signal{Z_2},\signal{Z_3},\signal{Z_m},\signal{Z_e},\signal{Z_i}$ | $1$ | Renormalization constants | The factors for the vertex, fields, mass, charge, and their generic notation. |
| $\signal{\delta_i},\signal{\delta_2}$ | $1$ | Renormalization counterterm coefficients | The deviations of the corresponding renormalization constants from one. |
| $\electric{\alpha}$ | $1$ | Fine-structure constant | The electromagnetic coupling in the one-loop pole. |
| $\energy{\mu}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Renormalization scale | The scale in the dimensional charge relation. |
| $\signal{\delta_m},\signal{\delta_1},\signal{\delta_3}$ | $1$ | Mass, vertex, and photon counterterm coefficients | The dimensionless coefficients defined as the corresponding Z factor minus one. |
| $\mechanical{m_{\mathrm{phys}}}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Physical electron mass | The pole mass, equal to the mass parameter in the on-shell scheme. |
