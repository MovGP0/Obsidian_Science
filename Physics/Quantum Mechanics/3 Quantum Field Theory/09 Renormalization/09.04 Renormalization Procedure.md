---
title: "Renormalization Procedure"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Renormalization expresses the bare QED fields and parameters through renormalized quantities and counterterms.

A divergent loop becomes useful only after the parameters in it have an operational definition. First choose a regulator and write the bare action in renormalized fields. Then compute loop contributions and counterterm insertions at the same perturbative order. Fix their finite parts with subtraction conditions, combine them, and remove the regulator. The conditions specify which mass and charge the calculation uses.

Follow [[09.04.01 Bare and Renormalized Quantities]] for the algebraic split, [[09.04.02 On-Shell Renormalization Conditions]] for pole and Thomson-limit definitions, and [[09.04.03 The MS and MS-Bar Schemes]] for pole-only subtraction. Compare schemes only after converting their parameter values. Equality of the physical prediction is the consistency check; equality of their intermediate masses or counterterms is not required.

The procedure separates the QED Lagrangian into a renormalized part and counterterms. The definitions are given in [[09.04.01 Bare and Renormalized Quantities]].
