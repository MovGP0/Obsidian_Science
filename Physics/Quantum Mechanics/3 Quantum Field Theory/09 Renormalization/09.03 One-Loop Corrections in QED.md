---
title: "One-Loop Corrections in QED"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The QED Lagrangian generates the one-loop electron self-energy, photon polarization, and vertex corrections.

The QED Lagrangian density is
$$
\energy{\mathcal L}_{\mathrm{QED}}
=\bar{\signal{\psi}}(i\not\partial-\mechanical{m})\signal{\psi}
-\frac14\electric{F}_{\mu\nu}\electric{F}^{\mu\nu}
-\electric{e}\bar{\signal{\psi}}\gamma^\mu\signal{\psi}\electric{A}_\mu.
\tag{9.20}
$$

The source uses $\electric{e}>0$ for the positron charge and $-\electric{e}$ for the electron charge. Its Feynman rules are:

| Element | Factor |
| --- | --- |
| Electron propagator | $\dfrac{i(\not{\mechanical{p}}+\mechanical{m})}{\mechanical{p}^{\,2}-\mechanical{m}^{\,2}+i\epsilon}$ |
| Photon propagator in Feynman gauge | $\dfrac{-ig_{\mu\nu}}{\mechanical{q}^{\,2}+i\epsilon}$ |
| Vertex | $-i\electric{e}\gamma^\mu$ |

> [!note]
> Keep the signed coupling and particle-charge assignment consistent. The convention issue in the source's Lagrangian is explained in [[08.01.02 Covariant Derivative and Minimal Coupling]].

The purpose of these rules is to compute corrections to propagation and scattering from the same interaction, so their signs and momentum routing must be consistent across all three diagrams. Work in four-dimensional natural units before continuation; each term in (9.20) has mass dimension four. A gauge-fixing term is implicit in the stated Feynman-gauge photon propagator; the displayed gauge-invariant density alone does not specify that propagator.

Use [[09.03.01 Electron Self-Energy Σ(p)]] to locate the electron pole and residue, [[09.03.02 Vacuum Polarization Πμν(q)]] to test photon transversality, and [[09.03.03 Vertex Correction Γμ(p′, p) and Anomalous Moment]] to extract the magnetic form factor. Individual off-shell functions depend on gauge and parameter conventions. Physical predictions require the consistent combination of these corrections and the counterterms, rather than interpreting an isolated diagram as an observable.

At one loop, QED has exactly three divergent one-particle-irreducible diagram types.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\energy{\mathcal L}_{\mathrm{QED}}$ | $\unit{MeV^4}$ | QED Lagrangian density | The four-dimensional density used to define the loop corrections. |
| $\signal{\psi},\bar{\signal{\psi}}$ | $\unit{MeV^{3/2}}$ | Dirac fields | The electron field and its adjoint. |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Electron mass | The mass parameter in the free Dirac term. |
| $\electric{F}_{\mu\nu},\electric{F}^{\mu\nu}$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Field-strength tensor | The electromagnetic field tensor in the photon kinetic term. |
| $\electric{e}$ | $1$ | Electromagnetic coupling | The charge parameter in four dimensions. |
| $\electric{A}_\mu$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Four-potential | The photon field in the interaction. |
| $\mechanical{p},\mechanical{q}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Propagator momenta | The electron and photon four-momentum arguments. |
