> [!TLDR]
> Lamb and Retherford measured a hydrogen-level splitting in 1947. The result provided direct evidence of QED radiative effects.

In 1947, Willis Lamb and Robert Retherford measured transition frequencies in hydrogen with high precision.
They found that the $2S_{1/2}$ and $2P_{1/2}$ levels, which the Dirac equation predicts to be degenerate, are split by about $1058\,\unit{MHz}$.

This splitting is called the **Lamb shift**.
The Dirac theory alone could not explain it.
It provided the first direct experimental evidence of QED radiative effects.

Bethe (1947) calculated the Lamb shift with a semi-relativistic method.

Dyson, French, Weisskopf, and Kroll later used fully relativistic methods. These calculations confirmed the QED prediction.
