## Exercise 9.6: Complete electron self-energy calculation on the mass shell

Start from the finite part of the electron self-energy in (9.34).
Calculate the mass and wavefunction corrections explicitly in the on-shell scheme.

(a) Calculate
$\left.\energy{\Sigma}(\not{\mechanical{p}})\right|_{\not{\mechanical{p}}=\mechanical{m}}$
using (9.34) and $\mechanical{p}^2=\mechanical{m}^2$.

(b) Verify that the on-shell denominator parameter is
$\energy{\Delta}_\Sigma(x,\mechanical{m}^2)
=\mechanical{m}^2(1-x)^2$.
Evaluate
$$
\energy{\Sigma_{\mathrm{OS}}}
=\left.
\frac{\electric{\alpha}}{4\pi}
\int_0^1\mathrm{d}x\,
(2x\not{\mechanical{p}}-4\mechanical{m})
\ln\frac{\mechanical{m}^2(1-x)^2}{\energy{\mu}^2}
\right|_{\not{\mechanical{p}}=\mechanical{m}}.
$$

(c) Extract $\mechanical{\delta m}/\mechanical{m}$ and $\signal{\delta Z_2}$ in the on-shell scheme.

> [!note]
> The source's (9.34) has the finite-numerator omission identified in [[09.03.01 Electron Self-Energy Σ(p)]]. A complete on-shell calculation also needs the ultraviolet counterterms and an infrared prescription for the wavefunction correction. The displayed finite integral alone does not specify all of these ingredients.

## Solution 9.6 — Electron self-energy on shell

**(a)** The source starts from the logarithmic finite piece of (9.34):
$$
\energy{\Sigma}_{\mathrm{log,source}}(\not{\mechanical{p}})
=\frac{\electric{\alpha}}{4\pi}
\int_0^1dx\,(-2x\not{\mechanical{p}}+4\mechanical{m})
\ln\frac{\energy{\mu}^2}{\energy{\Delta}_\Sigma(x,\mechanical{p}^2)}.
$$
At $\mechanical{p}^2=\mechanical{m}^2$,
$$
\energy{\Delta}_\Sigma(x,\mechanical{m}^2)
=(1-x)(\mechanical{m}^2-x\mechanical{m}^2)
=\mechanical{m}^2(1-x)^2.
$$

**(b)** Setting $\not{\mechanical{p}}=\mechanical{m}$ on an external on-shell spinor,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\energy{\Sigma}_{\mathrm{log,source}}(\mechanical{m})
&=\frac{\electric{\alpha}}{4\pi}
\int_0^1dx\,(-2x\mechanical{m}+4\mechanical{m})
\ln\frac{\energy{\mu}^2}{\mechanical{m}^2(1-x)^2}\\
&=\frac{\electric{\alpha}\mechanical{m}}{4\pi}
\int_0^1dx\,(-2x+4)\,
2\left[\ln\frac{\energy{\mu}}{\mechanical{m}}-\ln(1-x)\right].
\end{aligned}
$$
Use
$$
\int_0^1x\,dx=\frac12,\qquad
\int_0^1dx=1,\qquad
\int_0^1\ln(1-x)\,dx=-1,\qquad
\int_0^1x\ln(1-x)\,dx=-\frac34.
$$
The result is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\energy{\Sigma}_{\mathrm{log,source}}(\mechanical{m})
&=\frac{\electric{\alpha}\mechanical{m}}{2\pi}
\left[
3\ln\frac{\energy{\mu}}{\mechanical{m}}
-\left(-2\left(-\frac34\right)+4(-1)\right)
\right]\\
&=\frac{\electric{\alpha}\mechanical{m}}{2\pi}
\left[3\ln\frac{\energy{\mu}}{\mechanical{m}}+\frac52\right].
\end{aligned}
$$

> [!note] Missing finite numerator and scheme distinction
> As noted in [[09.03.01 Electron Self-Energy Σ(p)]], (9.34) omits the finite term $(\electric{\alpha}/4\pi)(\not{\mechanical{p}}-2\mechanical{m})$ from the dimension-dependent numerator. On shell this subtracts $\electric{\alpha}\mechanical{m}/(4\pi)$ from the result above. The corrected ultraviolet-subtracted finite self-energy is therefore $(\electric{\alpha}\mechanical{m}/4\pi)[3\ln(\energy{\mu}^2/\mechanical{m}^2)+4]$ in the stated modified-minimal-subtraction convention.
>
> The source calls its finite integral an “on-shell scheme” result after removing the ultraviolet pole. Evaluating an integral on shell is not the same as imposing on-shell renormalization conditions. The fully renormalized on-shell self-energy also includes its mass and residue counterterms.

**(c)** The source defines its loop mass shift by evaluating the self-energy on shell. Restoring the finite numerator term identified above gives
$$
\frac{\Delta\mechanical{m}_{\mathrm{loop}}}{\mechanical{m}}
=\frac{\energy{\Sigma}_{\mathrm{loop}}(\mechanical{m})}{\mechanical{m}}
=\frac{3\electric{\alpha}}{4\pi}
\left[
\frac1{\bar\epsilon}
+\ln\frac{\energy{\mu}^2}{\mechanical{m}^2}
+\frac43
\right]
$$
at this order. The source instead has $5/3$ in the bracket, inherited from its incomplete logarithmic integral.

For the kinetic counterterm, the source uses the residue condition on the derivative of the total self-energy and quotes
$$
\signal{\delta}_{2,\mathrm{source}}
=-\frac{\electric{\alpha}}{4\pi}
\left[
\frac1{\bar\epsilon}
+2\ln\frac{\energy{\mu}^2}{\mechanical{m}^2}
+4+\cdots
\right].
$$

> [!note] Incomplete on-shell residue formula
> The displayed source expression does not separate ultraviolet and infrared singularities and does not specify the omitted terms. The full on-shell wavefunction correction is infrared divergent and depends on the infrared prescription. This expression therefore does not give a complete finite on-shell residue counterterm. Its ultraviolet pole is $-\electric{\alpha}/(4\pi\bar\epsilon)$.

In modified minimal subtraction, only the ultraviolet pole is subtracted:
$$
\signal{\delta}_2=-\frac{\electric{\alpha}}{4\pi\bar\epsilon},
\qquad
\signal{\delta}_m=\signal{Z}_m-1
=-\frac{3\electric{\alpha}}{4\pi\bar\epsilon}.
$$
The loop mass shift $\Delta\mechanical{m}_{\mathrm{loop}}$ is distinct from the mass counterterm. With the convention below, the on-shell mass renormalization coefficient cancels that loop shift.
