---
title: "Neutral Current (Z–Fermion–Fermion Vertex)"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The Z vertex combines vector and axial couplings fixed by weak isospin, electric charge, and the weak mixing angle.

The neutral-current rule is
$$
Z_\mu\bar f f:\qquad
+\frac{i\signal{g}}{2\cos\signal{\theta}_W}
\gamma^\mu\left[\signal{g}_V^f-\signal{g}_A^f\gamma^5\right],
\tag{C.16}
$$
with
$$
\signal{g}_V^f=T_3^f-2\electric{Q}_f\sin^2\signal{\theta}_W,
\qquad
\signal{g}_A^f=T_3^f.
\tag{C.17}
$$
The left-doublet weak-isospin values are $T_3=+1/2$ for neutrinos and up-type quarks, and $T_3=-1/2$ for charged leptons and down-type quarks.
The source explicitly lists $\nu_e,u,c,t$ and $\ell^-,d,s,b$.

The notation $T_3^f$ in (C.17) denotes the left-doublet isospin of that fermion species. It does not assign the same weak isospin to a right-handed singlet. To see the distinction, write the bracket as
$$
\signal{g}_V^f-\signal{g}_A^f\gamma^5
=(\signal{g}_V^f+\signal{g}_A^f)P_L
+(\signal{g}_V^f-\signal{g}_A^f)P_R.
$$
The left coefficient is twice the isospin minus twice the charge term; the right coefficient contains only the charge term. For a neutral neutrino, the right coefficient vanishes. A charged right-chiral fermion can still couple to the Z.

The current preserves electric charge and is diagonal in fermion flavor at tree level in the minimal model. Attach the same fermion species to both fermion legs and contract the Z index with its line or polarization. The coefficient is dimensionless and tends to zero with the weak coupling. A useful reconstruction check is to insert the electron's isospin and charge and obtain $\signal{g}_A^e=-1/2$ and $\signal{g}_V^e=-1/2+2\sin^2\signal{\theta}_W$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{g},\signal{g}_V^f,\signal{g}_A^f$ | $1$ | Weak interaction couplings | The gauge, vector, and axial coefficients in the Z vertex. |
| $\signal{\theta}_W$ | $1$ | Weak mixing angle | The angle controlling the neutral-current couplings. |
| $T_3^f$ | $1$ | Weak isospin component | The left-doublet value for the fermion species. |
| $\electric{Q}_f$ | $1$ | Fermion electric charge | The charge number in units of the positive elementary charge. |
