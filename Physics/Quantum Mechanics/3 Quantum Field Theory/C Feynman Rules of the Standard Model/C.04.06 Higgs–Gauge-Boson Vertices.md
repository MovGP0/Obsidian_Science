---
title: "Higgs–Gauge-Boson Vertices"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Single-Higgs gauge vertices are proportional to a weak-boson mass, while double-Higgs gauge vertices are proportional to the squared weak coupling.

The Higgs–gauge-boson vertices are
$$
hW_\mu^+W_\nu^-:\qquad
+i\signal{g}\mechanical{m}_Wg^{\mu\nu},
\tag{C.27}
$$
$$
hZ_\mu Z_\nu:\qquad
+i\frac{\signal{g}\mechanical{m}_Z}{\cos\signal{\theta}_W}g^{\mu\nu}
=+i\frac{\signal{g}\mechanical{m}_Z^2}{\mechanical{m}_W}g^{\mu\nu},
\tag{C.28}
$$
$$
hhW_\mu^+W_\nu^-:\qquad
+i\frac{\signal{g}^2}{2}g^{\mu\nu},
\tag{C.29}
$$
$$
hhZ_\mu Z_\nu:\qquad
+i\frac{\signal{g}^2}{2\cos^2\signal{\theta}_W}g^{\mu\nu}.
\tag{C.30}
$$

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{g}$ | $1$ | Weak gauge coupling | The gauge coefficient in the Higgs–vector vertices. |
| $\mechanical{m}_W,\mechanical{m}_Z$ | $\unit{GeV}$ | Weak-boson masses | The masses in the single-Higgs vertex coefficients. |
| $\signal{\theta}_W$ | $1$ | Weak mixing angle | The angle in the Z-boson vertex coefficients. |
