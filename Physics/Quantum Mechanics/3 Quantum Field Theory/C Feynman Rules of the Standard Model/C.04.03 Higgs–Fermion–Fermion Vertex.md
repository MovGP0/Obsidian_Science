---
title: "Higgs–Fermion–Fermion Vertex"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The physical Higgs coupling to a fermion is proportional to the fermion mass divided by the Higgs vacuum scale.

For a fermion with mass $\mechanical{m}_f$, the physical Higgs Yukawa vertex is
$$
h\bar f f:\qquad
-\frac{i\mechanical{m}_f}{\signal{v}}
=-\frac{i\signal{g}\mechanical{m}_f}{2\mechanical{m}_W}.
\tag{C.18}
$$

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{m}_f$ | $\unit{GeV}$ | Fermion mass | The mass that sets the Higgs–fermion coupling. |
| $\signal{v}$ | $\unit{GeV}$ | Higgs vacuum scale | The vacuum magnitude in the Yukawa coefficient. |
| $\signal{g}$ | $1$ | Weak gauge coupling | The coupling used to rewrite the vacuum scale. |
| $\mechanical{m}_W$ | $\unit{GeV}$ | W-boson mass | The charged weak-boson mass in the equivalent vertex expression. |
