---
title: "Quark–Gluon Vertex"
---

> [!TLDR]
> A quark–gluon vertex is diagonal in quark flavor and carries the strong coupling, a fundamental color generator, and a gamma matrix.

For a quark of any flavor, with color indices $i,j$ at the vertex,
$$
g_\mu^a\bar q_iq_j:\qquad
+i\signal{g}_s(T^a)_{ij}\gamma^\mu.
\tag{C.33}
$$

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{g}_s$ | $1$ | Strong gauge coupling | The interaction coefficient at the quark–gluon vertex. |
