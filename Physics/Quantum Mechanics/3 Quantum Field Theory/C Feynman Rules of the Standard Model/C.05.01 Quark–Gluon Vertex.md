---
title: "Quark–Gluon Vertex"
---

> [!TLDR]
> A quark–gluon vertex is diagonal in quark flavor and carries the strong coupling, a fundamental color generator, and a gamma matrix.

For a quark of any flavor, with color indices $i,j$ at the vertex,
$$
g_\mu^a\bar q_iq_j:\qquad
+i\signal{g}_s(T^a)_{ij}\gamma^\mu.
\tag{C.33}
$$

The gluon couples to the local color current. The matrix acts on the quark color indices in the displayed order; it can change color while leaving the quark flavor unchanged. Contract its Lorentz index with the gluon line, and keep the spinor chain in the fermion-arrow order. For an external antiquark, use the crossed spinor and consistent color-index ordering; do not insert a second minus sign by changing the displayed generator by hand.

The coefficient is dimensionless and tends to zero with $\signal{g}_s$. Replacing the color generator by an Abelian charge produces the same vector-current structure as the photon vertex. Unlike a charged weak current, no chiral projector appears: both quark chiralities couple to color. A useful check is to follow one flavor through two successive quark–gluon vertices; the color matrices remain ordered along the line and cannot in general be interchanged. The rule is a perturbative parton rule, not a model of a free observed quark.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{g}_s$ | $1$ | Strong gauge coupling | The interaction coefficient at the quark–gluon vertex. |
