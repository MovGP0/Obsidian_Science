---
title: "Four-Gluon Vertex"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The four-gluon vertex has three products of structure constants, each paired with its own difference of metric contractions.

The four-gluon contact interaction comes from squaring the non-Abelian part of the field strength. It has no derivative, so its tensor contains metrics rather than momenta. The three displayed color pairings are parts of one local vertex; they are not three exchange propagators.

For external gluons ordered as $g_\mu^a,g_\nu^b,g_\rho^c,g_\sigma^d$,
$$
\begin{aligned}
gggg:\qquad-i\signal{g}_s^2\Bigl[
&f^{abe}f^{cde}
(g^{\mu\rho}g^{\nu\sigma}-g^{\mu\sigma}g^{\nu\rho})\\
&+f^{ace}f^{bde}
(g^{\mu\nu}g^{\rho\sigma}-g^{\mu\sigma}g^{\nu\rho})\\
&+f^{ade}f^{bce}
(g^{\mu\nu}g^{\rho\sigma}-g^{\mu\rho}g^{\nu\sigma})
\Bigr].
\end{aligned}
\tag{C.35}
$$

The repeated color index is summed over all eight adjoint values. Keep each external color label attached to its Lorentz index when applying or permuting the rule. The complete sum has the Bose symmetry of four identical gluons; an individual color pairing need not have every permutation symmetry by itself. The vertex already contains the identical-field permutations from the gauge Lagrangian, so do not multiply it by $4!$.

Each term is dimensionless and proportional to the square of the strong coupling. The rule vanishes in the zero-coupling limit and in a commuting-generator algebra. In gluon–gluon scattering, this contact term must be combined with the exchange diagrams formed from two three-gluon vertices. Checking only one metric contraction, or retaining only one color pairing, cannot establish the gauge-invariant amplitude.
