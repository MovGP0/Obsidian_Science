---
title: "Four-Gluon Vertex"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The four-gluon vertex has three products of structure constants, each paired with its own difference of metric contractions.

For external gluons ordered as $g_\mu^a,g_\nu^b,g_\rho^c,g_\sigma^d$,
$$
\begin{aligned}
gggg:\qquad-i\signal{g}_s^2\Bigl[
&f^{abe}f^{cde}
(g^{\mu\rho}g^{\nu\sigma}-g^{\mu\sigma}g^{\nu\rho})\\
&+f^{ace}f^{bde}
(g^{\mu\nu}g^{\rho\sigma}-g^{\mu\sigma}g^{\nu\rho})\\
&+f^{ade}f^{bce}
(g^{\mu\nu}g^{\rho\sigma}-g^{\mu\rho}g^{\nu\sigma})
\Bigr].
\end{aligned}
\tag{C.35}
$$

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{g}_s$ | $1$ | Strong gauge coupling | The squared coupling in the four-gluon rule. |
