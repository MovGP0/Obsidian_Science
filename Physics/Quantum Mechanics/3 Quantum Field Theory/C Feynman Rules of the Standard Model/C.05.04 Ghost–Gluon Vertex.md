---
title: "Ghost–Gluon Vertex"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The ghost–gluon vertex depends on the strong coupling, the color structure constant, and the momentum assigned to the outgoing ghost line.

Let $\mechanical{p}^\mu$ be the momentum of the outgoing ghost line, that is, the ghost line that leaves the vertex.
The color indices $a,b,c$ refer to the incoming ghost, outgoing ghost, and gluon, respectively.
With the source's ordering,
$$
\bar c^{\,b}c^a g_\mu^c:\qquad
-\signal{g}_sf^{abc}\mechanical{p}^\mu.
\tag{C.36}
$$
Keep this momentum and ghost-line orientation convention when applying the rule.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{g}_s$ | $1$ | Strong gauge coupling | The coefficient of the ghost–gluon interaction. |
| $\mechanical{p}^\mu$ | $\unit{GeV}$ | Outgoing ghost-line momentum | The momentum selected by the source's vertex convention. |
