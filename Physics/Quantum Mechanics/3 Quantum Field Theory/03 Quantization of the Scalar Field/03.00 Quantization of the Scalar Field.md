title: "Quantization of the Scalar Field"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The real scalar quantum field introduces Fock space, Bose–Einstein statistics, propagators, and causality in the simplest spinless setting.

> “The career of a young theoretical physicist consists of treating the harmonic oscillator in ever-increasing levels of abstraction.”
>
> — Sidney Coleman

The real scalar field is the simplest starting point for quantum field theory. It has no spin or charge and describes bosonic particles of mass $\mechanical{m}$. Despite its simplicity, it contains central QFT concepts: Fock space, Bose–Einstein statistics, propagators, and causality. It is the natural starting point before fields with richer spin structure are considered.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Mass | The scalar-particle rest mass in natural units. |
