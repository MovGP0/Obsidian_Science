title: "The Charge Operator"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The conserved complex-field charge counts particles minus antiparticles.

Substitute (3.52) into (3.50) and normal order:

$$
\electric{Q}=\int\frac{d^{3}\mechanical{k}}{(2\pi)^{3}}[a^{\dagger}(\mechanical{\mathbf k})a(\mechanical{\mathbf k})-b^{\dagger}(\mechanical{\mathbf k})b(\mechanical{\mathbf k})]=N_a-N_b, \tag{3.55}
$$

where $N_a=\int d^{3}\mechanical{k}\,a^{\dagger}a/(2\pi)^{3}$ is particle number and the corresponding b expression is antiparticle number. Charge is conserved: $[\energy{H},\electric{Q}]=0$. Particles created by the first operator set have charge +1, and antiparticles have charge −1. Charged pions provide an example.

## Remark 3.7 — Real-Field Limit

For a real field, $\signal{\phi}=\signal{\phi}^{\dagger}$ and $a(\mechanical{\mathbf k})=b(\mechanical{\mathbf k})$. Particle and antiparticle coincide, as in a neutral-pion field description.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\electric{Q}$ | $1$ | Charge | The particle-minus-antiparticle count in unit-charge normalization. |
| $N_a,N_b$ | $1$ | Particle counts | The particle and antiparticle numbers. |
| $\mechanical{k},\mechanical{\mathbf k}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Momentum | The mode momentum and integration variable. |
| $\energy{H}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Hamiltonian | The energy observable commuting with charge. |
| $\signal{\phi}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Scalar field | The field in the real-field limit. |
