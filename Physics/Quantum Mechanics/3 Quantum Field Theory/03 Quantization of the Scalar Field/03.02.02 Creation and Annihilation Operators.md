---
title: "Creation and Annihilation Operators"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Plane-wave orthogonality extracts the creation and annihilation operators from the field and conjugate momentum.

Define the mode function

$$
f_{\mathbf k}(\mechanical{\mathbf x},\mechanical{t})=\frac{e^{-i\mechanical{k}\cdot\mechanical{x}}}{\sqrt{2\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k}}}. \tag{3.15}
$$

At zero time, spatial orthogonality gives

$$
\int d^{3}\mechanical{x}\,e^{-i\mechanical{\mathbf k}\cdot\mechanical{\mathbf x}}\signal{\phi}(\mechanical{\mathbf x},0)=\frac{a(\mechanical{\mathbf k})+a^{\dagger}(-\mechanical{\mathbf k})}{\sqrt{2\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k}}}, \tag{3.16}
$$

$$
\int d^{3}\mechanical{x}\,e^{-i\mechanical{\mathbf k}\cdot\mechanical{\mathbf x}}\mechanical{\pi}(\mechanical{\mathbf x},0)=-i\sqrt{\frac{\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k}}{2}}a(\mechanical{\mathbf k})+i\sqrt{\frac{\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k}}{2}}a^{\dagger}(-\mechanical{\mathbf k}). \tag{3.17}
$$

Combine the two equations to invert the mode expansion:

$$
a(\mechanical{\mathbf k})=\int d^{3}\mechanical{x}\,e^{-i\mechanical{\mathbf k}\cdot\mechanical{\mathbf x}}\left[\sqrt{\frac{\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k}}{2}}\signal{\phi}(\mechanical{\mathbf x},0)+\frac{i}{\sqrt{2\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k}}}\mechanical{\pi}(\mechanical{\mathbf x},0)\right], \tag{3.18}
$$

$$
a^{\dagger}(\mechanical{\mathbf k})=\int d^{3}\mechanical{x}\,e^{+i\mechanical{\mathbf k}\cdot\mechanical{\mathbf x}}\left[\sqrt{\frac{\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k}}{2}}\signal{\phi}(\mechanical{\mathbf x},0)-\frac{i}{\sqrt{2\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k}}}\mechanical{\pi}(\mechanical{\mathbf x},0)\right]. \tag{3.19}
$$

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{\phi}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Scalar field | The field used to extract mode operators. |
| $\mechanical{\pi}$ | $\unit{MeV^{2}}$ | Conjugate momentum density | The canonical field momentum. |
| $\mechanical{x},\mechanical{\mathbf x},\mechanical{t}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Coordinates | The spacetime, spatial, and time arguments. |
| $\mechanical{k},\mechanical{\mathbf k}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Momentum | The on-shell four-momentum and mode momentum. |
| $\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Mode energy | The positive energy in the inversion factors. |
