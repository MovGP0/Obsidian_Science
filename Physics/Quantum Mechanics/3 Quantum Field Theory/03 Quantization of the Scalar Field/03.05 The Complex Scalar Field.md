---
title: "The Complex Scalar Field"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Independent particle and antiparticle operators allow a complex scalar field to carry a conserved charge.

A neutral real field has one oscillator species. A charged complex field needs two independent species with equal mass and opposite charge. Begin with the global phase symmetry, then derive the two mode algebras and the charge count. The symmetry conserves net charge; it does not require separate conservation of particle and antiparticle counts once charge-preserving interactions are included.

Track the current sign convention through all three notes. A useful check is that pair creation can increase both counts while leaving their difference unchanged. A complex scalar with a global phase symmetry is still a free matter model here; an electromagnetic gauge field has not yet been introduced.
