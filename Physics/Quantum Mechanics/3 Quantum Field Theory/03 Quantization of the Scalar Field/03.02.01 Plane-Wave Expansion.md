---
title: "Plane-Wave Expansion"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Periodic-box modes become a continuum plane-wave expansion when the box size tends to infinity.

The Klein–Gordon equation is linear with constant coefficients, so plane waves provide its natural solutions. Begin in a cubic box of side $\mechanical{L}$ with periodic boundary conditions, which discretize momentum. In the limit $\mechanical{L}\to\infty$, replace

$$
\frac{1}{\mechanical{L}^{3}}\sum_{\mathbf k}\longrightarrow\int\frac{d^{3}\mechanical{k}}{(2\pi)^{3}}. \tag{3.10}
$$

The general real classical field is

$$
\signal{\phi}(\mechanical{\mathbf x},\mechanical{t})=\int\frac{d^{3}\mechanical{k}}{(2\pi)^{3}}\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k}}}\left[\signal{a}(\mechanical{\mathbf k})e^{-i\mechanical{k}\cdot\mechanical{x}}+\signal{a}^{*}(\mechanical{\mathbf k})e^{+i\mechanical{k}\cdot\mechanical{x}}\right]. \tag{3.11}
$$

The on-shell momentum is $\mechanical{k}^{\mu}=(\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k},\mechanical{\mathbf k})$, with

$$
\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k}=\sqrt{\mechanical{\mathbf k}^{2}+\mechanical{m}^{2}}, \tag{3.12}
$$

and $\mechanical{k}\cdot\mechanical{x}=\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k}\mechanical{t}-\mechanical{\mathbf k}\cdot\mechanical{\mathbf x}$. The square-root normalization gives convenient operator commutators.

Promote the field, momentum density, and mode coefficients to Hilbert-space operators:

$$
\boxed{\signal{\phi}(\mechanical{\mathbf x},\mechanical{t})=\int\frac{d^{3}\mechanical{k}}{(2\pi)^{3}}\frac{a(\mechanical{\mathbf k})e^{-i\mechanical{k}\cdot\mechanical{x}}+a^{\dagger}(\mechanical{\mathbf k})e^{+i\mechanical{k}\cdot\mechanical{x}}}{\sqrt{2\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k}}}.} \tag{3.13}
$$

The conjugate momentum is

$$
\mechanical{\pi}(\mechanical{\mathbf x},\mechanical{t})=\int\frac{d^{3}\mechanical{k}}{(2\pi)^{3}}(-i)\sqrt{\frac{\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k}}{2}}[a(\mechanical{\mathbf k})e^{-i\mechanical{k}\cdot\mechanical{x}}-a^{\dagger}(\mechanical{\mathbf k})e^{+i\mechanical{k}\cdot\mechanical{x}}]. \tag{3.14}
$$

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{L}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Box side length | The size of the periodic quantization box. |
| $\mechanical{k},\mechanical{\mathbf k}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Momentum | The mode four-momentum and three-momentum. |
| $\signal{\phi}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Real scalar field | The canonically normalized classical field. |
| $\mechanical{x},\mechanical{\mathbf x},\mechanical{t}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Coordinates | The spacetime, position, and time arguments. |
| $\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Mode energy | The positive energy in the mode normalization. |
| $\signal{a}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-3/2}}$ | Mode amplitude | The coefficient for the square-root-energy normalization. |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Mass | The scalar-particle rest mass. |
| $\mechanical{\pi}$ | $\unit{MeV^{2}}$ | Conjugate momentum density | The quantized field time derivative. |
