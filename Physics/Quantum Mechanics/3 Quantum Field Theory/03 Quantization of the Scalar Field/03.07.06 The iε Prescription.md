---
title: "The iε Prescription"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The positive infinitesimal shifts positive-energy poles below the real axis and negative-energy poles above it.

The denominator without a regulator vanishes at the on-shell energies. The prescription adds a positive imaginary infinitesimal and moves the poles away from the integration axis:

$$
\mechanical{k}^{2}-\mechanical{m}^{2}+i\varepsilon=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\energy{k}^{0}=\pm\sqrt{\mechanical{\mathbf k}^{2}+\mechanical{m}^{2}-i\varepsilon}\approx\pm(\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k}-i\varepsilon'). \tag{3.82}
$$

The positive-frequency pole moves into the lower half-plane, and the negative-frequency pole into the upper half-plane. This is the pole structure used in the residue calculation.

The prescription also fixes the vacuum boundary condition. For $\mechanical{x}^{0}>\mechanical{y}^{0}$, it selects forward-time particle propagation. For the reverse ordering, negative-energy propagation has the particle–antiparticle reinterpretation as forward-time propagation. Time ordering selects this combination.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{k},\mechanical{\mathbf k}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Momentum | The Fourier four-momentum and spatial momentum. |
| $\energy{k}^{0},\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Energies | The complex integration energy and positive on-shell energy. |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Mass | The scalar mass in the propagator denominator. |
| $\mechanical{x},\mechanical{y}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Spacetime positions | The two events whose time ordering selects the pole contribution. |
