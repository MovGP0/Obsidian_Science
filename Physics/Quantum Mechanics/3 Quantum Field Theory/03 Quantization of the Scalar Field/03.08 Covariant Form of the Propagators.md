> [!TLDR]
> Covariant momentum integrals express the commutator and relate the scalar propagation functions.

The equal-time construction selected an inertial time coordinate. The covariant mass-shell form now shows how its result respects proper orthochronous Lorentz transformations. Read the commutator note first to identify its initial derivative and causal support, then compare the propagation functions with those signs fixed.

Keep two distinctions visible: the on-shell commutator integral is a homogeneous solution with canonical initial data, whereas Green functions have point sources; and the Feynman numerator i differs from the retarded and advanced conventions here. Reconstructing their relations separately for positive and negative time difference is a quick test against lost signs or factors.
