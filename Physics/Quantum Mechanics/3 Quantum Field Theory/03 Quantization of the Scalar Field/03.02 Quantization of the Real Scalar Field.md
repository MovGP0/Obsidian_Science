title: "Quantization of the Real Scalar Field"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The real scalar field is quantized by turning its plane-wave coefficients into creation and annihilation operators.

Keep two operations separate. First solve the linear classical equation in a complete plane-wave basis. Then replace the expansion coefficients by operators and choose their commutators so that the field satisfies the canonical algebra. The plane waves remain ordinary functions; their coefficients act on Fock space.

Follow the four notes in order: expansion, inversion, operator algebra, and reverse substitution. The reverse substitution is a useful normalization test: both frequency branches must contribute to recover the full equal-time delta distribution. This construction assumes the free translation-invariant theory; an interacting field cannot in general be expanded at all times using constant free creation and annihilation operators.
