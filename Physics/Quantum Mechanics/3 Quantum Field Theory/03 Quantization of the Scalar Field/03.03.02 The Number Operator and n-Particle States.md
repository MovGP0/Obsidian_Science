---
title: "The Number Operator and n-Particle States"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The number observable counts particles, and creation operators increase the count by one.

The momentum-space particle-number density is

$$
N(\mechanical{\mathbf k})=\frac{a^{\dagger}(\mechanical{\mathbf k})a(\mechanical{\mathbf k})}{(2\pi)^{3}}. \tag{3.27}
$$

The total number observable is

$$
\hat N=\int\frac{d^{3}\mechanical{k}}{(2\pi)^{3}}a^{\dagger}(\mechanical{\mathbf k})a(\mechanical{\mathbf k}). \tag{3.28}
$$

Using (3.20),

$$
[\hat N,a^{\dagger}(\mechanical{\mathbf k})]=+a^{\dagger}(\mechanical{\mathbf k}). \tag{3.29}
$$

The annihilation commutator is

$$
[\hat N,a(\mechanical{\mathbf k})]=-a(\mechanical{\mathbf k}). \tag{3.30}
$$

Creation raises particle number by one; annihilation lowers it by one. A state containing n particles is

$$
|\mechanical{\mathbf k}_1,\ldots,\mechanical{\mathbf k}_n\rangle
=\sqrt{2\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k_1}}\cdots\sqrt{2\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k_n}}\,a^{\dagger}(\mechanical{\mathbf k}_1)\cdots a^{\dagger}(\mechanical{\mathbf k}_n)|0\rangle. \tag{3.31}
$$

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $N(\mechanical{\mathbf k})$ | $\unit{MeV^{-3}}$ | Momentum-space number density | The particle count per momentum-space volume. |
| $\hat N$ | $1$ | Particle number | The total particle count. |
| $\mechanical{k},\mechanical{\mathbf k}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Momentum | The mode momentum and integration variable. |
| $n$ | $1$ | Particle count | The number of particles in the multiparticle state. |
| $\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k_i}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Mode energies | The positive energies of the occupied modes. |
