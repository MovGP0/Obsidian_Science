---
title: "The Real Scalar Field and the Klein–Gordon Equation"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The free real scalar Lagrangian contains a relativistic kinetic term and a quadratic mass term.

For a free real scalar field of mass $\mechanical{m}$,

$$
\energy{\mathcal L}=\frac12(\partial_{\mu}\signal{\phi})(\partial^{\mu}\signal{\phi})-\frac12\mechanical{m}^{2}\signal{\phi}^{2}
=\frac12\signal{\dot\phi}^{2}-\frac12(\nabla\signal{\phi})^{2}-\frac12\mechanical{m}^{2}\signal{\phi}^{2}. \tag{3.6}
$$

The Euler–Lagrange equations give

$$
(\partial_{\mu}\partial^{\mu}+\mechanical{m}^{2})\signal{\phi}=(\square+\mechanical{m}^{2})\signal{\phi}=0, \tag{3.7}
$$

with $\square=\partial_{\mechanical{t}}^{2}-\nabla^{2}$ in signature $(+,-,-,-)$. The conjugate momentum is

$$
\mechanical{\pi}(\mechanical{\mathbf x},\mechanical{t})=\frac{\partial\energy{\mathcal L}}{\partial\signal{\dot\phi}}=\signal{\dot\phi}(\mechanical{\mathbf x},\mechanical{t}). \tag{3.8}
$$

The Legendre transformation gives

$$
\energy{\mathcal H}=\mechanical{\pi}\signal{\dot\phi}-\energy{\mathcal L}=\tfrac12\mechanical{\pi}^{2}+\tfrac12(\nabla\signal{\phi})^{2}+\tfrac12\mechanical{m}^{2}\signal{\phi}^{2}. \tag{3.9}
$$

The total Hamiltonian is $\energy{H}=\int d^{3}\mechanical{x}\,\energy{\mathcal H}(\mechanical{\mathbf x},\mechanical{t})$.
