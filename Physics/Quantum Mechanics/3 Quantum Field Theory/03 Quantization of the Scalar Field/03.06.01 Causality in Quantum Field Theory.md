title: "Causality in Quantum Field Theory"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Commuting observables at spacelike separation prevent a physical signal from propagating faster than light.

Relativistic causality forbids signals faster than light. QFT expresses this through microcausality.

## Definition 3.8 — Microcausality

For observables at spacelike-separated events, $(\mechanical{x}-\mechanical{y})^{2}<0$,

$$
[\mathcal O_1(\mechanical{x}),\mathcal O_2(\mechanical{y})]=0. \tag{3.56}
$$

For the scalar field, require $[\signal{\phi}(\mechanical{x}),\signal{\phi}(\mechanical{y})]=0$ at such separations.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{x},\mechanical{y}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Spacetime positions | The two events separated outside the light cone. |
| $\signal{\phi}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Scalar field | The field subject to the spacelike commutation condition. |
