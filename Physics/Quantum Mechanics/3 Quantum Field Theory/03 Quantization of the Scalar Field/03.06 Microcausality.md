---
title: "Microcausality"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Microcausality requires spacelike-separated observables to commute.

The physical question is whether operations in spacelike-separated regions can influence one another. A vacuum two-point correlation is insufficient to answer it: correlations can be present without a controllable response. Microcausality instead constrains the commutator of local observables.

Read the causality condition, derive the free scalar commutator as a Pauli–Jordan distribution, and then use Lorentz symmetry to prove its spacelike vanishing. The equal-time canonical algebra is the initial condition for this calculation; the free field equation supplies its evolution. The proof establishes consistency of this free scalar construction with locality, rather than deriving all QFT locality from classical relativity alone.
