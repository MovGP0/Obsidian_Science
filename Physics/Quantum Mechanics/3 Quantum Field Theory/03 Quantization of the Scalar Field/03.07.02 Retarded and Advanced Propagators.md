---
title: "Retarded and Advanced Propagators"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Retarded and advanced functions vanish before and after the source event, respectively.

The retarded function satisfies

$$
(\square_x+\mechanical{m}^{2})\signal{\Delta}_{R}(\mechanical{x}-\mechanical{y})=-\delta^{(4)}(\mechanical{x}-\mechanical{y}), \tag{3.64}
$$

with zero support when the first event is earlier than the source. The advanced function vanishes when the first event is later than the source.

The convention $\signal{\Delta}_{R}=\theta(\mechanical{x}^{0}-\mechanical{y}^{0})\signal{\Delta}$ gives a source without a factor of i. The Feynman function instead has source $-i\delta^{(4)}$. The relation $\signal{\Delta}_{F}=i\signal{\Delta}_{R}+\signal{\Delta}^{-}$ makes the extra factor explicit.

Choose contours in the complex energy plane to obtain

$$
\widetilde{\signal{\Delta}}_{R}(\mechanical{k})=\frac{1}{(\energy{k}^{0}+i\varepsilon)^{2}-\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k}^{2}}=\frac{1}{(\energy{k}^{0}+i\varepsilon)^{2}-\mechanical{\mathbf k}^{2}-\mechanical{m}^{2}}, \tag{3.65}
$$

$$
\widetilde{\signal{\Delta}}_{A}(\mechanical{k})=\frac{1}{(\energy{k}^{0}-i\varepsilon)^{2}-\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k}^{2}}=\frac{1}{(\energy{k}^{0}-i\varepsilon)^{2}-\mechanical{\mathbf k}^{2}-\mechanical{m}^{2}}. \tag{3.66}
$$
