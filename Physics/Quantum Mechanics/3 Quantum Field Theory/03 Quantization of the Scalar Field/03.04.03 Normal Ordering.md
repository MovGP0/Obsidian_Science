> [!TLDR]
> Normal ordering places creation operators to the left and sets the free-field vacuum energy to zero by convention.

## Definition 3.6 — Normal Ordering

Normal ordering, denoted $\mathcal N\{\cdots\}$, places every creation operator to the left of all annihilation operators. During reordering, treat the bosonic operators as if they commute. For two fields,

$$
\mathcal N\{\signal{\phi}(\mechanical{x})\signal{\phi}(\mechanical{y})\}
=\signal{\phi}^{-}(\mechanical{x})\signal{\phi}^{-}(\mechanical{y})+\signal{\phi}^{-}(\mechanical{x})\signal{\phi}^{+}(\mechanical{y})+\signal{\phi}^{-}(\mechanical{y})\signal{\phi}^{+}(\mechanical{x})+\signal{\phi}^{+}(\mechanical{x})\signal{\phi}^{+}(\mechanical{y}). \tag{3.39}
$$

The positive-frequency part contains annihilation operators; the negative-frequency part contains creation operators. The normal-ordered Hamiltonian is

$$
\energy{H}=\mathcal N\left\{\int d^{3}\mechanical{x}\,\energy{\mathcal H}\right\}
=\int\frac{d^{3}\mechanical{k}}{(2\pi)^{3}}\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k}a^{\dagger}(\mechanical{\mathbf k})a(\mechanical{\mathbf k}). \tag{3.40}
$$

It satisfies $\energy{H}|0\rangle=0$. For a multiparticle state,

$$
\energy{H}|\mechanical{\mathbf k}_1,\ldots,\mechanical{\mathbf k}_n\rangle
=(\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k_1}+\cdots+\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k_n})|\mechanical{\mathbf k}_1,\ldots,\mechanical{\mathbf k}_n\rangle. \tag{3.41}
$$
