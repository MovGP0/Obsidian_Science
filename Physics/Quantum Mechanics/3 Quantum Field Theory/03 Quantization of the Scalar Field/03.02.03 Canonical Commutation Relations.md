> [!TLDR]
> Canonical equal-time field commutators give independent harmonic-oscillator commutators for each momentum mode.

Impose the canonical relation (3.5). It determines the mode commutators.

## Theorem 3.1 — Oscillator Commutation Relations

If $[\signal{\phi}(\mechanical{\mathbf x},\mechanical{t}),\mechanical{\pi}(\mechanical{\mathbf y},\mechanical{t})]=i\delta^{(3)}(\mechanical{\mathbf x}-\mechanical{\mathbf y})$ and the equal-time field–field and momentum–momentum commutators vanish, then

$$
[a(\mechanical{\mathbf k}),a^{\dagger}(\mechanical{\mathbf k}')]=(2\pi)^{3}\delta^{(3)}(\mechanical{\mathbf k}-\mechanical{\mathbf k}'),\qquad
[a(\mechanical{\mathbf k}),a(\mechanical{\mathbf k}')]=[a^{\dagger}(\mechanical{\mathbf k}),a^{\dagger}(\mechanical{\mathbf k}')]=0. \tag{3.20}
$$

**Proof.** Use (3.18)–(3.19) at zero time. Suppressing the zero-time arguments,

$$
\begin{aligned}
[a(\mechanical{\mathbf k}),a^{\dagger}(\mechanical{\mathbf k}')]
&=\int d^{3}\mechanical{x}\,d^{3}\mechanical{y}\,e^{-i\mechanical{\mathbf k}\cdot\mechanical{\mathbf x}}e^{i\mechanical{\mathbf k}'\cdot\mechanical{\mathbf y}}\left[\sqrt{\frac{\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k}}2}\frac{-i}{\sqrt{2\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k'}}}[\signal{\phi}(\mechanical{\mathbf x}),\mechanical{\pi}(\mechanical{\mathbf y})]+\frac{i}{\sqrt{2\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k}}}\sqrt{\frac{\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k'}}2}[\mechanical{\pi}(\mechanical{\mathbf x}),\signal{\phi}(\mechanical{\mathbf y})]\right]\\
&=\int d^{3}\mechanical{x}\,d^{3}\mechanical{y}\,e^{-i\mechanical{\mathbf k}\cdot\mechanical{\mathbf x}}e^{i\mechanical{\mathbf k}'\cdot\mechanical{\mathbf y}}\delta^{(3)}(\mechanical{\mathbf x}-\mechanical{\mathbf y})\frac12\left(\sqrt{\frac{\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k}}{\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k'}}}+\sqrt{\frac{\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k'}}{\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k}}}\right)\\
&=\int d^{3}\mechanical{x}\,e^{-i(\mechanical{\mathbf k}-\mechanical{\mathbf k}')\cdot\mechanical{\mathbf x}}\\
&=(2\pi)^{3}\delta^{(3)}(\mechanical{\mathbf k}-\mechanical{\mathbf k}').
\end{aligned} \tag{3.21}
$$

On the delta-function support, the two mode energies agree, so the square-root sum is two. The same calculation gives zero for the two annihilation or two creation operators.

## Remark 3.2 — Oscillator Interpretation

Each momentum mode of the quantized scalar field is an independent quantum harmonic oscillator. The full field is an infinite collection of these oscillators.

The familiar properties of the quantum harmonic oscillator therefore carry over to the field.
