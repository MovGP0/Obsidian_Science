---
title: "Mode Expansion and Operators"
---

> [!TLDR]
> A complex scalar expansion uses independent particle and antiparticle mode operators.

The conjugate momentum densities are

$$
\mechanical{\pi}=\frac{\partial\energy{\mathcal L}}{\partial\signal{\dot\phi}}=\signal{\dot\phi}^{*},\qquad
\mechanical{\pi}^{*}=\frac{\partial\energy{\mathcal L}}{\partial\signal{\dot\phi}^{*}}=\signal{\dot\phi}. \tag{3.51}
$$

The field expansion is

$$
\signal{\phi}(\mechanical{\mathbf x},\mechanical{t})=\int\frac{d^{3}\mechanical{k}}{(2\pi)^{3}}\frac{a(\mechanical{\mathbf k})e^{-i\mechanical{k}\cdot\mechanical{x}}+b^{\dagger}(\mechanical{\mathbf k})e^{+i\mechanical{k}\cdot\mechanical{x}}}{\sqrt{2\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k}}}. \tag{3.52}
$$

The adjoint field is

$$
\signal{\phi}^{\dagger}(\mechanical{\mathbf x},\mechanical{t})=\int\frac{d^{3}\mechanical{k}}{(2\pi)^{3}}\frac{b(\mechanical{\mathbf k})e^{-i\mechanical{k}\cdot\mechanical{x}}+a^{\dagger}(\mechanical{\mathbf k})e^{+i\mechanical{k}\cdot\mechanical{x}}}{\sqrt{2\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k}}}. \tag{3.53}
$$

The complex field needs two operator sets: a and its adjoint for particles, and b and its adjoint for antiparticles. The equal-time relations $[\signal{\phi}(\mechanical{\mathbf x}),\mechanical{\pi}(\mechanical{\mathbf y})]=i\delta^{(3)}(\mechanical{\mathbf x}-\mechanical{\mathbf y})$ and $[\signal{\phi}(\mechanical{\mathbf x}),\mechanical{\pi}^{*}(\mechanical{\mathbf y})]=0$ imply

$$
[a(\mechanical{\mathbf k}),a^{\dagger}(\mechanical{\mathbf k}')]=(2\pi)^{3}\delta^{(3)}(\mechanical{\mathbf k}-\mechanical{\mathbf k}'),\qquad
[b(\mechanical{\mathbf k}),b^{\dagger}(\mechanical{\mathbf k}')]=(2\pi)^{3}\delta^{(3)}(\mechanical{\mathbf k}-\mechanical{\mathbf k}'). \tag{3.54}
$$

All other commutators between the two sets vanish.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{\phi}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Complex scalar field | The charged scalar amplitude. |
| $\signal{\dot\phi},\mechanical{\pi},\mechanical{\pi}^{*}$ | $\unit{MeV^{2}}$ | Field rates and momenta | The derivatives and their conjugate canonical momenta. |
| $\energy{\mathcal L}$ | $\unit{MeV^{4}}$ | Lagrangian density | The density defining the conjugate momenta. |
| $\mechanical{x},\mechanical{\mathbf x},\mechanical{t}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Coordinates | The field's spacetime, position, and time arguments. |
| $\mechanical{k},\mechanical{\mathbf k}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Momentum | The mode four-momentum and three-momentum. |
| $\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Mode energy | The positive on-shell scalar energy. |
| $\mechanical{\mathbf y}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Second position | The second argument of the equal-time commutator. |
