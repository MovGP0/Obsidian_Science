> [!TLDR]
> Scalar-field propagation functions differ in boundary conditions and time-ordering prescriptions.

Choose a propagation function by the physical question. Retarded propagation describes a response after a source; the time-ordered vacuum function organizes perturbative amplitudes. Wightman functions are correlations, and the Pauli–Jordan function controls commutators. They solve related differential equations but have different support and source factors.

Begin with the definitions and keep the chapter's factors of i fixed. Read the retarded/advanced note before the Feynman construction, then use the Fourier, pole, and contour notes to reconstruct the same time-ordering rule. Finish with the Green-function source check and physical interpretation. A common denominator without a pole prescription is not enough to select a unique distribution.
