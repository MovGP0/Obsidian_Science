---

> [!TLDR]
> Canonical quantization promotes classical field variables and their brackets to quantum operators and commutators.

The task is to specify quantum initial data for a relativistic field. Choose one inertial time slice, define the canonical momentum from the Lagrangian, and impose the equal-time algebra. The commutator is a quantization postulate; it is not obtained from the classical field equation alone.

Read the bracket prescription first and then apply it to the real scalar Lagrangian. The second note checks that the resulting Hamiltonian has nonnegative kinetic, gradient, and mass contributions for a nonnegative mass squared. This simple case has no gauge constraint, so its canonical variables can be used directly; constrained gauge fields need an additional constraint analysis.
