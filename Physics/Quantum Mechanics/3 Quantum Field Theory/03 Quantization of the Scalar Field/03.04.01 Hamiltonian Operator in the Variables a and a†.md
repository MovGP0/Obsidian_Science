---
title: "Hamiltonian Operator in the Variables a and a†"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The mode expansion expresses the Hamiltonian as the energy-weighted sum of creation–annihilation products.

Substitute the field and momentum expansions into the classical Hamiltonian density (3.9), and use plane-wave orthogonality:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\energy{H}&=\int d^{3}\mechanical{x}\,\energy{\mathcal H}\\
&=\int\frac{d^{3}\mechanical{k}}{(2\pi)^{3}}\frac{\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k}}{2}[a(\mechanical{\mathbf k})a^{\dagger}(\mechanical{\mathbf k})+a^{\dagger}(\mechanical{\mathbf k})a(\mechanical{\mathbf k})].
\end{aligned} \tag{3.35}
$$

## Kinetic Contribution

Insert (3.14) into the kinetic term. With $a_{\mathbf k}=a(\mechanical{\mathbf k})$,

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac12\int d^{3}\mechanical{x}\,\mechanical{\pi}^{2}
&=\frac12\int\frac{d^{3}\mechanical{k}}{(2\pi)^{3}}\frac{d^{3}\mechanical{k}'}{(2\pi)^{3}}\left(-\frac{\sqrt{\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k}\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k'}}}{2}\right)\int d^{3}\mechanical{x}\,[a_{\mathbf k}e^{-i\mechanical{k}\cdot\mechanical{x}}-a_{\mathbf k}^{\dagger}e^{i\mechanical{k}\cdot\mechanical{x}}][a_{\mathbf k'}e^{-i\mechanical{k}'\cdot\mechanical{x}}-a_{\mathbf k'}^{\dagger}e^{i\mechanical{k}'\cdot\mechanical{x}}]\\
&=\frac12\int\frac{d^{3}\mechanical{k}}{(2\pi)^{3}}\frac{d^{3}\mechanical{k}'}{(2\pi)^{3}}\left(-\frac{\sqrt{\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k}\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k'}}}{2}\right)(2\pi)^{3}\bigl[
 a_{\mathbf k}a_{\mathbf k'}e^{-i(\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k}+\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k'})\mechanical{t}}\delta^{(3)}(\mechanical{\mathbf k}+\mechanical{\mathbf k}')\\
&\qquad-a_{\mathbf k}a_{\mathbf k'}^{\dagger}\delta^{(3)}(\mechanical{\mathbf k}-\mechanical{\mathbf k}')-a_{\mathbf k}^{\dagger}a_{\mathbf k'}\delta^{(3)}(\mechanical{\mathbf k}-\mechanical{\mathbf k}')\\
&\qquad+a_{\mathbf k}^{\dagger}a_{\mathbf k'}^{\dagger}e^{i(\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k}+\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k'})\mechanical{t}}\delta^{(3)}(\mechanical{\mathbf k}+\mechanical{\mathbf k}')\bigr].
\end{aligned} \tag{3.36}
$$

The internal signs follow from multiplying the two differences. The sum-momentum delta functions set the second momentum to the negative of the first, so their energies agree. The oscillating terms are proportional to two annihilation or two creation operators. The gradient and mass contributions cancel them, leaving (3.35).

Formally, $[a(\mechanical{\mathbf k}),a^{\dagger}(\mechanical{\mathbf k})]=(2\pi)^{3}\delta^{(3)}(0)$ in the infinite-volume limit. Therefore

$$
\energy{H}=\int\frac{d^{3}\mechanical{k}}{(2\pi)^{3}}\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k}\left[a^{\dagger}(\mechanical{\mathbf k})a(\mechanical{\mathbf k})+\frac12(2\pi)^{3}\delta^{(3)}(0)\right]. \tag{3.37}
$$

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\energy{H}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Hamiltonian | The total quantum-field energy. |
| $\energy{\mathcal H}$ | $\unit{MeV^{4}}$ | Hamiltonian density | The local scalar-field energy density. |
| $\mechanical{x}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Position | The spatial integration coordinate. |
| $\mechanical{k},\mechanical{\mathbf k}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Momentum | The oscillator momentum and integration variable. |
| $\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Mode energy | The positive energy weighting each mode. |
| $\mechanical{\pi}$ | $\unit{MeV^{2}}$ | Momentum density | The canonical field momentum in the kinetic term. |
| $\mechanical{t}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Time | The time in the oscillating mode products. |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Mass | The mass in the scalar Hamiltonian density. |
