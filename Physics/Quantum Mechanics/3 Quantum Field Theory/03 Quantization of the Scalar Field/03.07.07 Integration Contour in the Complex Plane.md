**Fig. 3.1** Complex-energy integration for the Feynman propagator. For x⁰ > y⁰, the solid blue contour closes clockwise below and encloses +ωₖ − iε′. For x⁰ < y⁰, the dashed red contour closes counterclockwise above and encloses −ωₖ + iε′. The prescription shifts positive-frequency and negative-frequency poles into opposite half-planes and implements time ordering.
