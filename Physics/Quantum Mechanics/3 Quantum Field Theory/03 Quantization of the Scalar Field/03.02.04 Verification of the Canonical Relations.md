> [!TLDR]
> Substituting the mode commutators back into the field expansion reproduces the equal-time canonical relation.

Check consistency in the reverse direction, starting from (3.20).

## Example 3.3 — Verification of the Canonical Field Commutator

Using (3.13)–(3.14) and keeping nonzero terms,

$$
\begin{aligned}
[\signal{\phi}(\mechanical{\mathbf x},\mechanical{t}),\mechanical{\pi}(\mechanical{\mathbf y},\mechanical{t})]
=\int\frac{d^{3}\mechanical{k}}{(2\pi)^{3}}\frac{d^{3}\mechanical{k}'}{(2\pi)^{3}}\frac{-i}{\sqrt{2\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k}}}\sqrt{\frac{\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k'}}2}
\bigl(&-[a(\mechanical{\mathbf k}),a^{\dagger}(\mechanical{\mathbf k}')]e^{-i\mechanical{k}\cdot\mechanical{x}+i\mechanical{k}'\cdot\mechanical{y}}\\
&+[a^{\dagger}(\mechanical{\mathbf k}),a(\mechanical{\mathbf k}')]e^{i\mechanical{k}\cdot\mechanical{x}-i\mechanical{k}'\cdot\mechanical{y}}\bigr).
\end{aligned} \tag{3.22}
$$

Use the positive annihilation–creation commutator and the negative creation–annihilation commutator. Then

$$
\begin{aligned}
[\signal{\phi}(\mechanical{\mathbf x},\mechanical{t}),\mechanical{\pi}(\mechanical{\mathbf y},\mechanical{t})]
&=\int\frac{d^{3}\mechanical{k}}{(2\pi)^{3}}\frac{-i}{2}[-e^{-i\mechanical{k}\cdot\mechanical{x}+i\mechanical{k}\cdot\mechanical{y}}-e^{i\mechanical{k}\cdot\mechanical{x}-i\mechanical{k}\cdot\mechanical{y}}]\\
&=\left.\int\frac{d^{3}\mechanical{k}}{(2\pi)^{3}}\frac{-i}{2}(-2)\cos[\mechanical{k}\cdot(\mechanical{x}-\mechanical{y})]\right|_{t_x=t_y}\\
&=i\int\frac{d^{3}\mechanical{k}}{(2\pi)^{3}}e^{i\mechanical{\mathbf k}\cdot(\mechanical{\mathbf x}-\mechanical{\mathbf y})}=i\delta^{(3)}(\mechanical{\mathbf x}-\mechanical{\mathbf y}).
\end{aligned} \tag{3.23}
$$
