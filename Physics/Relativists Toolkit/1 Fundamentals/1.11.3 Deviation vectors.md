> [!TLDR]
> Changes in the geodesic direction or starting time give four deviation vectors that obey the geodesic deviation equation.

<!-- PDF page 37; printed page 21 -->

Suppose now that in the relations $x^{\alpha'}(t,\Omega^a,s)$, the parameters $\Omega^a$ are varied while keeping $t$ and $s$ fixed. This defines new curves that connect different geodesics $\beta$ at the same proper distance $s$ from their common intersection point $P$ on $\gamma$. This is very similar to the construction described in Section 1.10, and the vectors

$$
\xi^{\alpha'}_a=\left(\frac{\partial x^{\alpha'}}{\partial\Omega^a}\right)_{t,s} \tag{1.56}
$$

are deviation vectors relating geodesics $\beta(t,\Omega^a)$ with different coefficients $\Omega^a$. Similarly,

$$
\xi^{\alpha'}_t=\left(\frac{\partial x^{\alpha'}}{\partial t}\right)_{s,\Omega^a} \tag{1.57}
$$

is a deviation vector relating geodesics $\beta(t,\Omega^a)$ that start at different points on $\gamma$, but share the same coefficients $\Omega^a$. The four vectors defined by Eqs. (1.56) and (1.57) satisfy the geodesic deviation equation, Eq. (1.46); it must be kept in mind that in this equation, the tangent vector is $v^{\alpha'}$, not $u^{\alpha'}$, and the affine parameter is $s$, not $t$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\xi^{\alpha'}_a,\xi^{\alpha'}_t$ | — | Deviation vectors | The vectors compare members of the spacelike geodesic family. |
| $t$ | $\unit{m}$ | Proper time | The starting time along the reference geodesic. |
| $s$ | $\unit{m}$ | Proper distance | The affine parameter along the spacelike geodesics. |
| $\Omega^a$ | $1$ | Direction components | The parameters varied to construct three deviation vectors. |
| $v^{\alpha'},u^{\alpha'}$ | — | Geodesic tangents | The spacelike and timelike tangent components. |
