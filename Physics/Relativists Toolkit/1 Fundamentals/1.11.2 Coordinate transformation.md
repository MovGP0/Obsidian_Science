> [!TLDR]
> The spacelike geodesic family gives the coordinate transformation; its Jacobian on γ equals the transported orthonormal frame.

<!-- PDF page 36; printed page 20 -->

We note first that we can describe the family of geodesics $\beta(t,\Omega^a)$ by relations of the form $x^{\alpha'}(t,\Omega^a,s)$. In these, the parameters $t$ and $\Omega^a$ serve to specify which geodesic, and $s$ is proper distance along this geodesic. If we substitute $s=0$ in these relations, we recover the description of the timelike geodesic $\gamma$ in terms of its proper time $t$; the parameters $\Omega^a$ are then irrelevant. The tangent to the geodesics $\beta(t,\Omega^a)$ is

$$
v^{\alpha'}=\left(\frac{\partial x^{\alpha'}}{\partial s}\right)_{t,\Omega^a}; \tag{1.52}
$$

the notation indicates explicitly that the derivative with respect to $s$ is taken while keeping $t$ and $\Omega^a$ fixed. This vector is a solution to the geodesic equation subjected to the initial condition $v^{\alpha'}|_{s=0}=\Omega^a\hat e^{\alpha'}_a$. But the geodesic equation is invariant under a rescaling of the affine parameter, $s\to s/c$, in which $c$ is a constant. Under this rescaling, $v^{\alpha'}\to cv^{\alpha'}$ and as a consequence we have that $\Omega^a\to c\Omega^a$. We

<!-- PDF page 37; printed page 21; text before section 1.11.3 -->

have therefore established the identity $x^{\alpha'}(t,\Omega^a,s)=x^{\alpha'}(t,c\Omega^a,s/c)$, and as a special case we find

$$
x^{\alpha'}(t,\Omega^a,s)=x^{\alpha'}(t,\Omega^a s,1)\equiv x^{\alpha'}(x^\alpha). \tag{1.53}
$$

By virtue of Eqs. (1.51), this relation is the desired transformation between $x^{\alpha'}$ and the Fermi normal coordinates.

Now, as a consequence of Eqs. (1.50), (1.52), and (1.53) we have

$$
\Omega^a\hat e^{\alpha'}_a=v^{\alpha'}|_\gamma=
\left.\frac{\partial x^{\alpha'}}{\partial s}\right|_{s=0}=
\left.\frac{\partial x^{\alpha'}}{\partial x^a}\right|_{s=0}\Omega^a,
$$

which shows that

$$
\left.\frac{\partial x^{\alpha'}}{\partial x^a}\right|_\gamma=\hat e^{\alpha'}_a. \tag{1.54}
$$

From our previous observation that the relations $x^{\alpha'}(t,\Omega^a,0)$ describe the geodesic $\gamma$, we also have

$$
\left.\frac{\partial x^{\alpha'}}{\partial t}\right|_\gamma=u^{\alpha'}\equiv\hat e^{\alpha'}_t. \tag{1.55}
$$

Equations (1.54) and (1.55) tell us that on $\gamma$, $\partial x^{\alpha'}/\partial x^\mu=\hat e^{\alpha'}_\mu$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $t$ | $\unit{m}$ | Proper time | Proper time along the reference geodesic. |
| $s$ | $\unit{m}$ | Proper distance | The affine distance along a spacelike geodesic. |
| $\Omega^a$ | $1$ | Direction components | The components that select the spacelike geodesic. |
| $v^{\alpha'},u^{\alpha'}$ | — | Geodesic tangents | The component units depend on the original coordinates. |
