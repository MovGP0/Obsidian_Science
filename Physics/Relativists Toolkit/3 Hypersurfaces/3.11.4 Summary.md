> [!TLDR]
> The null-shell surface tensor follows from transverse-curvature jumps, while a common observer factor gives the measured quantities.

<!-- Source: PDF page 120, printed page 104. -->

A singular null hypersurface $\Sigma$ possesses a surface stress-energy tensor characterized by tangent vectors $\mechanical{k}^\alpha_\pm$ and $e^\alpha_{\pm A}$, as well as a surface density $\energy{\mu}$, a surface current $\mechanical{j^A}$, and an isotropic surface pressure $\mechanical{p}$. The surface quantities can all be related to a discontinuity in the surface’s transverse curvature,

$$
C_{ab}=-N_\alpha e^\alpha_{a;\beta}e^\beta_b,
$$

which is defined on either side of $\Sigma$ in the appropriate coordinate system $x^\alpha_\pm$. The relations are

$$
\energy{\mu}=-\frac{1}{8\pi}\sigma^{AB}[C_{AB}],\qquad
\mechanical{j^A}=\frac{1}{8\pi}\sigma^{AB}[C_{\mechanical{\lambda} B}],\qquad
\mechanical{p}=-\frac{1}{8\pi}[C_{\mechanical{\lambda}\mechanical{\lambda}}].
$$

The surface stress-energy tensor is given by

$$
\energy{S^{\alpha\beta}}=\energy{\mu}\mechanical{k}^\alpha \mechanical{k}^\beta+\mechanical{j^A}(\mechanical{k}^\alpha e^\beta_A+e^\alpha_Ak^\beta)+\mechanical{p}\sigma^{AB}e^\alpha_Ae^\beta_B,
$$

and the complete stress-energy tensor of the surface layer is

$$
\energy{T_\Sigma^{\alpha\beta}}=(-\mechanical{k}_\mu \mechanical{u}^\mu)^{-1}\energy{S^{\alpha\beta}}\delta(\mechanical{\tau}).
$$

In this expression, the factor $(-\mechanical{k}_\mu \mechanical{u}^\mu)^{-1}$ is continuous at $\Sigma$, and the vector field $\mechanical{u}^\alpha_\pm$ is tangent to an arbitrary congruence of timelike geodesics parameterized by proper time $\mechanical{\tau}$ (the congruence represents a family of observers making measurements on the shell). The presence of this factor implies that $\energy{\mu}$, $\mechanical{j^A}$, and $\mechanical{p}$ are not truly the surface quantities that would be measured by the observers. The physically-measured surface quantities are given instead by

$$
\begin{aligned}
\energy{\mu_{\mathrm{physical}}}&=(-\mechanical{k}_\mu \mechanical{u}^\mu)^{-1}\energy{\mu},\qquad
\mechanical{j^A_{\mathrm{physical}}}=(-\mechanical{k}_\mu \mechanical{u}^\mu)^{-1}\mechanical{j^A},\\
\mechanical{p_{\mathrm{physical}}}&=(-\mechanical{k}_\mu \mechanical{u}^\mu)^{-1}\mechanical{p}.
\end{aligned}
$$

The arbitrariness associated with the choice of congruence is thus limited to a single multiplicative factor. The ‘bare’ quantities $\energy{\mu}$, $\mechanical{j^A}$, and $\mechanical{p}$ are independent of this choice, and it is often more convenient to work in terms of those.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{k}^\alpha$ | — | Null tangent | The generator tangent and shell normal. |
| $C_{ab}$ | — | Transverse curvature | Its jump gives the bare shell quantities. |
| $\sigma^{AB}$ | — | Inverse induced metric | The inverse two-metric on the shell. |
| $\energy{\mu},\energy{\mu_{\mathrm{physical}}}$ | $\unit{\mathrm{m}^{-1}}$ | Surface densities | The bare density and the density measured by the observers. |
| $\mechanical{j^A},\mechanical{j^A_{\mathrm{physical}}}$ | — | Surface currents | The bare and measured matter-current components. |
| $\mechanical{p},\mechanical{p_{\mathrm{physical}}}$ | $\unit{\mathrm{m}^{-1}}$ | Surface pressures | The bare and measured isotropic pressures. |
| $\energy{S^{\alpha\beta}}$ | — | Surface stress-energy | The surface tensor before observer normalization. |
| $\energy{T_\Sigma^{\alpha\beta}}$ | — | Shell stress-energy | The full distributional shell tensor. |
| $\mechanical{u}^\alpha$ | — | Observer four-velocity | The velocity of the selected measuring observers. |
| $\mechanical{\tau}$ | $\unit{\mathrm{m}}$ | Proper time | The observer time from the shell crossing. |
