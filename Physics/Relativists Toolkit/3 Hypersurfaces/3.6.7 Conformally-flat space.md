> [!TLDR]
> A conformally flat initial metric converts the scalar constraint to Poisson's equation for the conformal factor.

<!-- Source: PDF page 100, printed page 84. -->

A powerful technique for generating solutions to the constraint equations consists of writing the three-metric as

$$
h_{ab}=\psi^4\delta_{ab},
$$

where $\psi(y^a)$ is a scalar field on the hypersurface. Such a metric is said to be conformally related to the flat metric, and the space is said to be conformally flat. For this metric the Ricci scalar is $\,{}^3R=-8\psi^{-5}\nabla^2\psi$, and Eq. (3.44) takes the form of Poisson’s equation,

$$
\nabla^2\psi=-2\pi\energy{\rho_{\mathrm{eff}}},
$$

where

$$
\energy{\rho_{\mathrm{eff}}}=\psi^5\left[\energy{\rho}+\frac{1}{16\pi}\left(K^{ab}K_{ab}-K^2\right)\right]
$$

is an effective mass density on the hypersurface. At a moment of time symmetry this simplifies to $\energy{\rho_{\mathrm{eff}}}=\psi^5\energy{\rho}$, and one possible strategy for solving the constraint is to specify $\energy{\rho_{\mathrm{eff}}}$, solve for $\psi$, and then see what this produces for the actual mass density $\energy{\rho}$. If $\energy{\rho}=0$ at the moment of time symmetry, then the constraint becomes Laplace’s equation $\nabla^2\psi=0$, and this admits many interesting solutions. A well-known example is Misner’s (1960) solution, which describes two black holes about to undergo a head-on collision. This initial data set has been vigorously studied by numerical relativists.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $h_{ab}$ | — | Induced metric | The conformally flat spatial metric. |
| $\,{}^3R$ | $\unit{\mathrm{m}^{-2}}$ | Intrinsic Ricci scalar | The curvature determined by the conformal factor. |
| $K_{ab}$ | — | Extrinsic curvature | The tensor in the effective-density source. |
| $K$ | $\unit{\mathrm{m}^{-1}}$ | Extrinsic-curvature trace | The trace in the scalar constraint. |
| $\energy{\rho},\energy{\rho_{\mathrm{eff}}}$ | $\unit{\mathrm{m}^{-2}}$ | Mass densities | The actual and effective mass densities. |
