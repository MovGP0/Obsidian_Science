> [!TLDR]
> Contracted Gauss–Codazzi equations give normal projections of the Einstein tensor.

<!-- Source: PDF page 94, printed page 78. -->

The Gauss–Codazzi equations can also be written in contracted form, in terms of the Einstein tensor $G_{\alpha\beta}=R_{\alpha\beta}-\frac12 Rg_{\alpha\beta}$. The spacetime Ricci tensor is given by

$$
\begin{aligned}
R_{\alpha\beta}&=g^{\mu\nu}R_{\mu\alpha\nu\beta}\\
&=\left(\varepsilon n^\mu n^\nu+h^{mn}e^\mu_me^\nu_n\right)R_{\mu\alpha\nu\beta}\\
&=\varepsilon R_{\mu\alpha\nu\beta}n^\mu n^\nu+h^{mn}R_{\mu\alpha\nu\beta}e^\mu_me^\nu_n,
\end{aligned}
$$

and the Ricci scalar is

$$
\begin{aligned}
R&=g^{\alpha\beta}R_{\alpha\beta}\\
&=\left(\varepsilon n^\alpha n^\beta+h^{ab}e^\alpha_ae^\beta_b\right)\left(\varepsilon R_{\mu\alpha\nu\beta}n^\mu n^\nu+h^{mn}R_{\mu\alpha\nu\beta}e^\mu_me^\nu_n\right)\\
&=2\varepsilon h^{ab}R_{\mu\alpha\nu\beta}n^\mu e^\alpha_an^\nu e^\beta_b+h^{ab}h^{mn}R_{\mu\alpha\nu\beta}e^\mu_me^\alpha_ae^\nu_ne^\beta_b.
\end{aligned}
$$

A little algebra then reveals the relations

$$
-2\varepsilon G_{\alpha\beta}n^\alpha n^\beta={}^{3}R+\varepsilon\left(K^{ab}K_{ab}-K^2\right) \tag{3.41}
$$

and

$$
G_{\alpha\beta}e^\alpha_an^\beta=K^b{}_{a|b}-K_{,a}. \tag{3.42}
$$

Here, $\,{}^{3}R=h^{ab}R^m{}_{amb}$ is the three-dimensional Ricci scalar. The importance of Eqs. (3.41) and (3.42) lies in the fact that they form part of the Einstein field equations on a hypersurface $\Sigma$; this observation will be elaborated in the next section. We note that $G_{\alpha\beta}e^\alpha_ae^\beta_b$, the remaining components of the Einstein tensor, cannot be expressed solely in terms of $h_{ab}$, $K_{ab}$, and related quantities.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $G_{\alpha\beta}$ | — | Einstein tensor | The spacetime tensor in the field equations. |
| $R_{\alpha\beta},R_{\mu\alpha\nu\beta}$ | — | Spacetime curvature | The Ricci and Riemann tensor components. |
| $R$ | $\unit{\mathrm{m}^{-2}}$ | Spacetime Ricci scalar | The scalar curvature of spacetime. |
| $\,{}^{3}R$ | $\unit{\mathrm{m}^{-2}}$ | Intrinsic Ricci scalar | The scalar curvature of the hypersurface. |
| $g_{\alpha\beta}$ | — | Spacetime metric | The four-dimensional metric. |
| $h_{ab}$ | — | Induced metric | The metric on the hypersurface. |
| $K_{ab}$ | — | Extrinsic curvature | The curvature components of the hypersurface in spacetime. |
| $K$ | $\unit{\mathrm{m}^{-1}}$ | Extrinsic-curvature trace | The trace of the extrinsic curvature. |
| $n^\alpha$ | — | Unit normal | The normal direction to the hypersurface. |
