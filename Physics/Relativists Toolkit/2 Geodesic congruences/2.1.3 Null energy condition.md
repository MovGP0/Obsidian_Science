> [!TLDR]
> The null energy condition requires a nonnegative stress-energy contraction along every null direction.

<!-- PDF page 47; printed page 31 -->

The null energy condition makes the same statement as the weak form, except that $\mechanical{v}^\alpha$ is replaced by an arbitrary, future-directed null vector $\mechanical{k}^\alpha$. Thus,

$$
\energy{T}_{\alpha\beta}\mechanical{k}^\alpha\mechanical{k}^\beta\ge0
\tag{2.8}
$$

is the statement of the null energy condition. Substituting Eqs. (2.1) and (2.5) gives

$$
\energy{\rho}+a'^2\mechanical{p}_1+b'^2\mechanical{p}_2+c'^2\mechanical{p}_3\ge0.
$$

Choosing $b'=c'=0$ enforces $a'=1$, and we obtain $\energy{\rho}+\mechanical{p}_1\ge0$, with similar expressions holding for $\mechanical{p}_2$ and $\mechanical{p}_3$. The null energy condition therefore implies

$$
\energy{\rho}+\mechanical{p}_i\ge0.
\tag{2.9}
$$

Notice that the weak energy condition implies the null form.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\energy{T}_{\alpha\beta},\energy{T}^\alpha{}_\beta$ | — | Stress-energy tensor | The matter tensor with coordinate-dependent component units. |
| $\energy{\rho}$ | $\unit{\mathrm{m^{-2}}}$ | Energy density | The density in the matter's orthonormal frame, in geometrized units. |
| $\mechanical{p}_i$ | $\unit{\mathrm{m^{-2}}}$ | Principal pressures | The pressure eigenvalues in geometrized units. |
| $\mechanical{v}^\alpha$ | — | Observer four-velocity | The observer's future-directed timelike velocity. |
| $\mechanical{k}^\alpha$ | — | Null vector | The future-directed null vector with arbitrary normalization. |
