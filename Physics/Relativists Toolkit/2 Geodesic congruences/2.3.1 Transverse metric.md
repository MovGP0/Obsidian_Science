> [!TLDR]
> The transverse metric projects spacetime geometry into the three-dimensional space orthogonal to the timelike flow.

<!-- PDF page 53; printed page 37 -->

Given the congruence and the associated timelike vector field $\mechanical{u}^\alpha$, the spacetime metric $g_{\alpha\beta}$ can be decomposed into a longitudinal part $-\mechanical{u}_\alpha\mechanical{u}_\beta$ and a transverse part $h_{\alpha\beta}$ given by

$$
h_{\alpha\beta}=g_{\alpha\beta}+\mechanical{u}_\alpha\mechanical{u}_\beta.
\tag{2.14}
$$

The transverse metric is purely ‘spatial’, in the sense that it is orthogonal to $\mechanical{u}^\alpha$: $\mechanical{u}^\alpha h_{\alpha\beta}=0=h_{\alpha\beta}\mechanical{u}^\beta$. It is effectively three-dimensional: in a comoving Lorentz frame at some point $P$ within the congruence, $\mechanical{u}_\alpha\stackrel{*}{=}(-1,0,0,0)$, $g_{\alpha\beta}\stackrel{*}{=}\operatorname{diag}(-1,1,1,1)$, and $h_{\alpha\beta}\stackrel{*}{=}\operatorname{diag}(0,1,1,1)$. We may also note the relations $h^\alpha{}_\alpha=3$ and $h^\alpha{}_\mu h^\mu{}_\beta=h^\alpha{}_\beta$.

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{u}^\alpha,\mechanical{u}_\alpha$ | — | Timelike tangent | The congruence's four-velocity or its metric dual. |
| $g_{\alpha\beta},h_{\alpha\beta}$ | — | Metric tensors | The spacetime metric and its transverse projection. |
