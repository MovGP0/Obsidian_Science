> [!TLDR]
> The projected null-tangent gradient gives transverse expansion, shear, and rotation; its expansion is independent of the auxiliary null vector.

<!-- PDF page 63; printed page 47 -->

As before, we introduce the tensor field

$$
\mechanical{B}_{\alpha\beta}=\mechanical{k}_{\alpha;\beta}
\tag{2.30}
$$

as a measure of the failure of $\mechanical{\xi}^\alpha$ to be parallel transported along the congruence:

$$
\mechanical{\xi}^\alpha{}_{;\beta}\mechanical{k}^\beta
=\mechanical{B}^\alpha{}_\beta\mechanical{\xi}^\beta.
\tag{2.31}
$$

As before, $\mechanical{B}_{\alpha\beta}$ is orthogonal to the tangent vector field: $\mechanical{k}^\alpha\mechanical{B}_{\alpha\beta}=0=\mechanical{B}_{\alpha\beta}\mechanical{k}^\beta$. However, $\mechanical{B}_{\alpha\beta}$ is *not* orthogonal to $N^\alpha$, and Eq. (2.31) has a non-transverse component that should be removed.

We begin by isolating the purely transverse part of the deviation vector, which we denote $\mechanical{\tilde\xi}^\alpha$. Because $h_{\alpha\beta}$ is itself purely transverse, it is easy to see that

$$
\mechanical{\tilde\xi}^\alpha\equiv h^\alpha{}_\mu\mechanical{\xi}^\mu
=\mechanical{\xi}^\alpha+(N_\mu\mechanical{\xi}^\mu)\mechanical{k}^\alpha
\tag{2.32}
$$

is the desired object. Its covariant derivative in the direction of $\mechanical{k}^\alpha$ represents the relative velocity of two neighbouring geodesics. This is given by

$$
\mechanical{\tilde\xi}^\mu{}_{;\beta}\mechanical{k}^\beta
=h^\mu{}_\nu\mechanical{B}^\nu{}_\beta\mechanical{\xi}^\beta
+h^\mu{}_{\nu;\beta}\mechanical{\xi}^\nu\mechanical{k}^\beta,
$$

where we have inserted Eq. (2.31) in the first term of the right-hand side. Calculating the second term gives

$$
\mechanical{\tilde\xi}^\mu{}_{;\beta}\mechanical{k}^\beta
=h^\mu{}_\nu\mechanical{B}^\nu{}_\beta\mechanical{\xi}^\beta
+(N_{\nu;\beta}\mechanical{\xi}^\nu\mechanical{k}^\beta)\mechanical{k}^\mu,
$$

and we see that the vector $\mechanical{\tilde\xi}^\mu{}_{;\beta}\mechanical{k}^\beta$ has a component along $\mechanical{k}^\mu$. Once again we remove this by projecting with $h^\alpha{}_\mu$. Using the last of Eqs. (2.29) we obtain

$$
\begin{aligned}
(\mechanical{\tilde\xi}^\alpha{}_{;\beta}\mechanical{k}^\beta)^\sim
&\equiv h^\alpha{}_\mu(\mechanical{\tilde\xi}^\mu{}_{;\beta}\mechanical{k}^\beta)
=h^\alpha{}_\mu\mechanical{B}^\mu{}_\nu\mechanical{\xi}^\nu\\
&=h^\alpha{}_\mu\mechanical{B}^\mu{}_\nu\mechanical{\tilde\xi}^\nu\\
&=h^\alpha{}_\mu h^\nu{}_\beta\mechanical{B}^\mu{}_\nu\mechanical{\tilde\xi}^\beta
\end{aligned}
$$

for the *transverse components* of the relative velocity. In the first line we have replaced $\mechanical{\xi}^\nu$ with $\mechanical{\tilde\xi}^\nu$ because $\mechanical{B}^\mu{}_\nu\mechanical{k}^\nu=0$. In the third line we have inserted the relation $\mechanical{\tilde\xi}^\nu=h^\nu{}_\beta\mechanical{\tilde\xi}^\beta$; this holds because $\mechanical{\tilde\xi}^\nu$ is already purely transverse.

We have obtained

$$
(\mechanical{\tilde\xi}^\alpha{}_{;\beta}\mechanical{k}^\beta)^\sim
=\mechanical{\tilde B}^\alpha{}_\beta\mechanical{\tilde\xi}^\beta,
\tag{2.33}
$$

<!-- PDF page 64; printed page 48 -->

where

$$
\mechanical{\tilde B}_{\alpha\beta}
=h^\mu{}_\alpha h^\nu{}_\beta\mechanical{B}_{\mu\nu}
\tag{2.34}
$$

is the purely transverse part of $\mechanical{B}_{\mu\nu}=\mechanical{k}_{\mu;\nu}$. This can be expressed in a more explicit form by using Eq. (2.28):

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mechanical{\tilde B}_{\alpha\beta}
&=(g^\mu{}_\alpha+\mechanical{k}_\alpha N^\mu+N_\alpha\mechanical{k}^\mu)
(g^\nu{}_\beta+\mechanical{k}_\beta N^\nu+N_\beta\mechanical{k}^\nu)\mechanical{B}_{\mu\nu}\\
&=(g^\mu{}_\alpha+\mechanical{k}_\alpha N^\mu+N_\alpha\mechanical{k}^\mu)
(\mechanical{B}_{\mu\beta}+\mechanical{k}_\beta\mechanical{B}_{\mu\nu}N^\nu)\\
&=\mechanical{B}_{\alpha\beta}
+\mechanical{k}_\alpha N^\mu\mechanical{B}_{\mu\beta}
+\mechanical{k}_\beta\mechanical{B}_{\alpha\mu}N^\mu
+\mechanical{k}_\alpha\mechanical{k}_\beta\mechanical{B}_{\mu\nu}N^\mu N^\nu.
\end{aligned}
\tag{2.35}
$$

Equation (2.33) governs the purely transverse behaviour of the null congruence, and the vector $\mechanical{\tilde B}^\alpha{}_\beta\mechanical{\tilde\xi}^\beta$ can be interpreted as the transverse relative velocity between two neighbouring geodesics.

As we did before, we decompose the evolution tensor $\mechanical{\tilde B}_{\alpha\beta}$ into its irreducible parts:

$$
\mechanical{\tilde B}_{\alpha\beta}
=\frac12\mechanical{\theta}h_{\alpha\beta}
+\mechanical{\sigma}_{\alpha\beta}+\mechanical{\omega}_{\alpha\beta},
\tag{2.36}
$$

where $\mechanical{\theta}=\mechanical{\tilde B}^\alpha{}_\alpha$ is the expansion scalar, $\mechanical{\sigma}_{\alpha\beta}=\mechanical{\tilde B}_{(\alpha\beta)}-\tfrac12\mechanical{\theta}h_{\alpha\beta}$ the shear tensor, and $\mechanical{\omega}_{\alpha\beta}=\mechanical{\tilde B}_{[\alpha\beta]}$ the rotation tensor. The expansion is given more explicitly by

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mechanical{\theta}&=g^{\alpha\beta}\mechanical{\tilde B}_{\alpha\beta}\\
&=g^{\alpha\beta}\mechanical{B}_{\alpha\beta},
\end{aligned}
$$

which follows from Eq. (2.35) and the fact that $\mechanical{B}_{\alpha\beta}$ is orthogonal to $\mechanical{k}^\alpha$. From this we obtain

$$
\mechanical{\theta}=\mechanical{k}^\alpha{}_{;\alpha}.
\tag{2.37}
$$

We see explicitly that $\mechanical{\theta}$ does not depend on the choice of auxiliary null vector $N^\alpha$: the expansion is unique. The geometric meaning of the expansion will be considered in detail below; we will show that $\mechanical{\theta}$ is the fractional rate of change – per unit affine-parameter distance – of the congruence's cross-sectional area. (Recall that here, the transverse space is two-dimensional.)

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{k}^\alpha,\mechanical{k}_\alpha$ | — | Null tangent | The tangent to the null geodesics with arbitrary normalization. |
| $\mechanical{\xi}^\alpha,\mechanical{\xi}_\alpha,\mechanical{\tilde\xi}^\alpha$ | — | Deviation vector | The geodesic separation or its purely transverse part. |
| $g_{\alpha\beta},g^{\alpha\beta},h_{\alpha\beta},h'_{\alpha\beta}$ | — | Metric tensors | The spacetime and transverse metrics with coordinate-dependent component units. |
| $\mechanical{B}_{\alpha\beta},\mechanical{B}^\alpha{}_\beta,\mechanical{\tilde B}_{\alpha\beta},\mechanical{\tilde B}^\alpha{}_\beta$ | — | Deformation tensors | The null-tangent gradient and its transverse projection. |
| $\mechanical{\theta}$ | $\unit{\mathrm{m^{-1}}}$ | Expansion scalar | The fractional area-change rate per unit affine-parameter distance. |
| $\mechanical{\sigma}_{\alpha\beta}$ | — | Shear tensor | The transverse symmetric tracefree deformation. |
| $\mechanical{\omega}_{\alpha\beta}$ | — | Rotation tensor | The transverse antisymmetric deformation. |
