> [!TLDR]
> A null congruence needs an extra projection to isolate transverse separation from the null tangent itself.

<!-- PDF page 61; printed page 45 -->

We now turn to the case of null geodesics. The geometric setup is the same as in the preceding section, except that the tangent vector field, denoted $\mechanical{k}^\alpha$, is null. We assume that the geodesics are affinely parameterized by $\mechanical{\lambda}$, so that a displacement along a member of the congruence is described by $dx^\alpha=\mechanical{k}^\alpha\,d\mechanical{\lambda}$. The deviation vector will again be denoted $\mechanical{\xi}^\alpha$, and we again take it to be orthogonal to, and Lie transported along, the geodesics. The following equations therefore hold:

$$
\mechanical{k}^\alpha\mechanical{k}_\alpha=0,\quad
\mechanical{k}^\alpha{}_{;\beta}\mechanical{k}^\beta=0,\quad
\mechanical{k}^\alpha{}_{;\beta}\mechanical{\xi}^\beta
=\mechanical{\xi}^\alpha{}_{;\beta}\mechanical{k}^\beta,\quad
\mechanical{k}^\alpha\mechanical{\xi}_\alpha=0.
$$

<!-- PDF page 62; printed page 46 -->

As we were in the preceding section, we will be interested in the transverse properties of the congruence, which are determined by the deviation vector $\mechanical{\xi}^\alpha$. We can, however, anticipate some difficulties, because here the condition $\mechanical{k}^\alpha\mechanical{\xi}_\alpha=0$ fails to remove an eventual component of $\mechanical{\xi}^\alpha$ in the direction of $\mechanical{k}^\alpha$. One of our first tasks, therefore, will be to isolate the purely transverse part of the deviation vector. This we will do with the help of $h_{\alpha\beta}$, the transverse metric.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{k}^\alpha,\mechanical{k}_\alpha$ | — | Null tangent | The tangent to the null geodesics with arbitrary normalization. |
| $\mechanical{\xi}^\alpha,\mechanical{\xi}_\alpha,\mechanical{\tilde\xi}^\alpha$ | — | Deviation vector | The geodesic separation or its purely transverse part. |
| $\mechanical{\lambda}$ | $\unit{\mathrm{m}}$ | Affine parameter | The length parameter along a null geodesic. |
| $g_{\alpha\beta},g^{\alpha\beta},h_{\alpha\beta},h'_{\alpha\beta}$ | — | Metric tensors | The spacetime and transverse metrics with coordinate-dependent component units. |
