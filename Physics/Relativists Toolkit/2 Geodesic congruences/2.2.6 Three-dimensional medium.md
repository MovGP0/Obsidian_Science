> [!TLDR]
> In three dimensions, the trace of the deformation tensor gives the fractional volume change.

<!-- PDF page 51; printed page 35 -->

In three dimensions the tensor $\mechanical{B}_{ab}$ would be decomposed as

$$
\mechanical{B}_{ab}=\frac13\mechanical{\theta}\delta_{ab}+\mechanical{\sigma}_{ab}+\mechanical{\omega}_{ab},
$$

where $\mechanical{\theta}=\mechanical{B}^a{}_a$ is the expansion scalar, $\mechanical{\sigma}_{ab}=\mechanical{B}_{(ab)}-\tfrac13\mechanical{\theta}\delta_{ab}$ the shear tensor, and $\mechanical{\omega}_{ab}=\mechanical{B}_{[ab]}$ the rotation tensor. In the three-dimensional case, the expansion is the fractional change of *volume* per unit time:

$$
\mechanical{\theta}=\frac{1}{\mechanical{V}}\frac{\Delta\mechanical{V}}{\Delta\mechanical{t}}.
$$

To see this, treat the three-dimensional relation

$$
\mechanical{\xi}^a(\mechanical{t}_1)
=(\delta^a_b+\mechanical{B}^a{}_b\Delta\mechanical{t})\mechanical{\xi}^b(\mechanical{t}_0)
$$

<!-- PDF page 52; printed page 36 -->

as a coordinate transformation from $\mechanical{\xi}^a(\mechanical{t}_0)$ to $\mechanical{\xi}^a(\mechanical{t}_1)$. The Jacobian of this transformation is

$$
\begin{aligned}
J&=\det[\delta^a_b+\mechanical{B}^a{}_b\Delta\mechanical{t}]\\
&=1+\operatorname{Tr}[\mechanical{B}^a{}_b\Delta\mechanical{t}]\\
&=1+\mechanical{\theta}\Delta\mechanical{t}.
\end{aligned}
$$

This implies that volumes at $\mechanical{t}_0$ and $\mechanical{t}_1$ are related by $\mechanical{V}_1=(1+\mechanical{\theta}\Delta\mechanical{t})\mechanical{V}_0$, so that $\mechanical{V}_0\mechanical{\theta}=(\mechanical{V}_1-\mechanical{V}_0)/\Delta\mechanical{t}$. This argument shows also that the volume is not affected by the shear and rotation tensors.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{B}^a{}_b,\mechanical{B}_{ab}$ | $\unit{\mathrm{m^{-1}}}$ | Deformation rate | The tensor that gives the rate of change of displacement. |
| $\mechanical{\theta}$ | $\unit{\mathrm{m^{-1}}}$ | Expansion rate | The fractional area or volume change per unit geometrized time. |
| $\mechanical{\sigma}_+,\mechanical{\sigma}_\times,\mechanical{\sigma}_{ab}$ | $\unit{\mathrm{m^{-1}}}$ | Shear rate | The two shear parameters or the shear tensor. |
| $\mechanical{\omega},\mechanical{\omega}_{ab}$ | $\unit{\mathrm{m^{-1}}}$ | Rotation rate | The angular rate or its antisymmetric tensor. |
| $\mechanical{V},\mechanical{V}_0,\mechanical{V}_1,\Delta\mechanical{V}$ | $\unit{\mathrm{m^3}}$ | Volume | The volume before or after deformation and its change. |
| $\mechanical{t},\mechanical{t}_0,\mechanical{t}_1,\Delta\mechanical{t}$ | $\unit{\mathrm{m}}$ | Time | Time and its interval in the book's geometrized units. |
| $\mechanical{\xi}^a,\Delta\mechanical{\xi}^a$ | $\unit{\mathrm{m}}$ | Displacement | The displacement from the reference point and its change. |
