> [!TLDR]
> A timelike congruence is a nonintersecting family of geodesics whose separation is described by a transverse deviation vector.

<!-- PDF page 52; printed page 36 -->

Let $\mathcal O$ be an open region in spacetime. A *congruence* in $\mathcal O$ is a family of curves such that through each point in $\mathcal O$ there passes one and only one curve from this family. (The curves do not intersect; picture this as a tight bundle of copper wires.) In this section we will be interested in congruences of timelike geodesics, which means that each curve in the family is a timelike geodesic; congruences of null geodesics will be considered in the following section. We wish to determine how such a congruence evolves with time. More precisely stated, we want to determine the behaviour of the *deviation vector* $\mechanical{\xi}^\alpha$ between two neighbouring geodesics in the congruence (Fig. 2.3), as a function of proper time $\mechanical{\tau}$ along the reference geodesic. The geometric setup is the same as in Section 1.10, and the relations

$$
\mechanical{u}^\alpha\mechanical{u}_\alpha=-1,\quad
\mechanical{u}^\alpha{}_{;\beta}\mechanical{u}^\beta=0,\quad
\mechanical{u}^\alpha{}_{;\beta}\mechanical{\xi}^\beta
=\mechanical{\xi}^\alpha{}_{;\beta}\mechanical{u}^\beta,\quad
\mechanical{u}^\alpha\mechanical{\xi}_\alpha=0,
$$

where $\mechanical{u}^\alpha$ is tangent to the geodesics, will be assumed to hold. Notice in particular that $\mechanical{\xi}^\alpha$ is orthogonal to $\mechanical{u}^\alpha$: the deviation vector points in the directions *transverse* to the flow of the congruence.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{u}^\alpha,\mechanical{u}_\alpha$ | — | Timelike tangent | The congruence's four-velocity or its metric dual. |
| $\mechanical{\xi}^\alpha,\mechanical{\xi}_\alpha$ | — | Deviation vector | The separation of neighbouring geodesics, with coordinate-dependent component units. |
| $\mechanical{\tau}$ | $\unit{\mathrm{m}}$ | Proper time | Proper time along the reference geodesic in geometrized units. |
