> [!TLDR]
> The null Raychaudhuri equation gives the expansion rate through transverse shear, rotation, and Ricci curvature.

<!-- PDF page 66; printed page 50 -->

The derivation of the null version of Raychaudhuri's equation proceeds much as in Section 2.3.4. In particular, the equation

$$
\frac{d\mechanical{\theta}}{d\mechanical{\lambda}}
=-\mechanical{B}^{\alpha\beta}\mechanical{B}_{\beta\alpha}
-R_{\alpha\beta}\mechanical{k}^\alpha\mechanical{k}^\beta
$$

follows from the same series of steps. It is then easy to check that $\mechanical{B}^{\alpha\beta}\mechanical{B}_{\beta\alpha}=\mechanical{\tilde B}^{\alpha\beta}\mechanical{\tilde B}_{\beta\alpha}=\tfrac12\mechanical{\theta}^2+\mechanical{\sigma}^{\alpha\beta}\mechanical{\sigma}_{\alpha\beta}-\mechanical{\omega}^{\alpha\beta}\mechanical{\omega}_{\alpha\beta}$, which gives

$$
\frac{d\mechanical{\theta}}{d\mechanical{\lambda}}
=-\frac12\mechanical{\theta}^2
-\mechanical{\sigma}^{\alpha\beta}\mechanical{\sigma}_{\alpha\beta}
+\mechanical{\omega}^{\alpha\beta}\mechanical{\omega}_{\alpha\beta}
-R_{\alpha\beta}\mechanical{k}^\alpha\mechanical{k}^\beta.
\tag{2.39}
$$

This is Raychaudhuri's equation for a congruence of null geodesics. It should be noted that this equation is invariant under a change of auxiliary null vector $N^\alpha$; this is established in Section 2.6, Problem 6. We also note that because the shear and rotation tensors are purely transverse, $\mechanical{\sigma}^{\alpha\beta}\mechanical{\sigma}_{\alpha\beta}\ge0$ and $\mechanical{\omega}^{\alpha\beta}\mechanical{\omega}_{\alpha\beta}\ge0$, with the equality sign holding if and only if the tensor vanishes.
