> [!TLDR]
> The strong energy condition constrains the Ricci tensor through the energy density and principal pressures.

<!-- PDF page 47; printed page 31 -->

The statement of the strong energy condition is

$$
\left(\energy{T}_{\alpha\beta}-\frac12\energy{T}g_{\alpha\beta}\right)
\mechanical{v}^\alpha\mechanical{v}^\beta\ge0,
\tag{2.10}
$$

or $\energy{T}_{\alpha\beta}\mechanical{v}^\alpha\mechanical{v}^\beta\ge-\tfrac12\energy{T}$, where $\mechanical{v}^\alpha$ is any future-directed, normalized, timelike vector. Because $\energy{T}_{\alpha\beta}-\tfrac12\energy{T}g_{\alpha\beta}=R_{\alpha\beta}/8\pi$ by virtue of the Einstein field equations, the strong energy condition is really a statement about the Ricci tensor. Substituting Eqs. (2.1) and (2.4) gives

$$
\mechanical{\gamma}^2(\energy{\rho}+a^2\mechanical{p}_1+b^2\mechanical{p}_2+c^2\mechanical{p}_3)
\ge\frac12(\energy{\rho}-\mechanical{p}_1-\mechanical{p}_2-\mechanical{p}_3).
$$

Choosing $a=b=c=0$ enforces $\mechanical{\gamma}=1$, and we obtain $\energy{\rho}+\mechanical{p}_1+\mechanical{p}_2+\mechanical{p}_3\ge0$. Alternatively, choosing $b=c=0$ implies $\mechanical{\gamma}^2=1/(1-a^2)$, and after some simple algebra we obtain $\energy{\rho}+\mechanical{p}_1+\mechanical{p}_2+\mechanical{p}_3\ge a^2(\mechanical{p}_2+\mechanical{p}_3-\energy{\rho}-\mechanical{p}_1)$. Because this must hold for any $a^2<1$, we have $\energy{\rho}+\mechanical{p}_1\ge0$, with similar relations holding for $\mechanical{p}_2$ and $\mechanical{p}_3$. The strong energy condition therefore implies

$$
\energy{\rho}+\mechanical{p}_1+\mechanical{p}_2+\mechanical{p}_3\ge0,\qquad
\energy{\rho}+\mechanical{p}_i\ge0.
\tag{2.11}
$$

It should be noted that the strong energy condition does *not* imply the weak form.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\energy{T}_{\alpha\beta},\energy{T}^\alpha{}_\beta$ | — | Stress-energy tensor | The matter tensor with coordinate-dependent component units. |
| $\energy{T}$ | $\unit{\mathrm{m^{-2}}}$ | Stress-energy trace | The invariant trace in the strong energy condition. |
| $\energy{\rho}$ | $\unit{\mathrm{m^{-2}}}$ | Energy density | The density in the matter's orthonormal frame, in geometrized units. |
| $\mechanical{p}_i$ | $\unit{\mathrm{m^{-2}}}$ | Principal pressures | The pressure eigenvalues in geometrized units. |
| $\mechanical{v}^\alpha$ | — | Observer four-velocity | The observer's future-directed timelike velocity. |
| $g_{\alpha\beta}$ | — | Metric tensor | The spacetime metric with coordinate-dependent component units. |
| $R_{\alpha\beta}$ | — | Ricci tensor | The curvature tensor with coordinate-dependent component units. |
| $\mechanical{\gamma}$ | $1$ | Velocity normalization | The factor that normalizes the observer's four-velocity. |
