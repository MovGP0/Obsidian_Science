> [!TLDR]
> The velocity gradient separates into expansion, shear, and rotation and determines how nearby geodesics separate.

<!-- PDF page 53; printed page 37 -->

We now introduce the tensor field

$$
\mechanical{B}_{\alpha\beta}=\mechanical{u}_{\alpha;\beta}.
\tag{2.15}
$$

Like $h_{\alpha\beta}$, this tensor is purely transverse, as $\mechanical{u}^\alpha\mechanical{B}_{\alpha\beta}=\mechanical{u}^\alpha\mechanical{u}_{\alpha;\beta}=\tfrac12(\mechanical{u}_\alpha\mechanical{u}^\alpha)_{;\beta}=0$ and $\mechanical{B}_{\alpha\beta}\mechanical{u}^\beta=\mechanical{u}_{\alpha;\beta}\mechanical{u}^\beta=0$. It determines the evolution of the deviation vector: from $\mechanical{\xi}^\alpha{}_{;\beta}\mechanical{u}^\beta=\mechanical{u}^\alpha{}_{;\beta}\mechanical{\xi}^\beta$ we immediately obtain

$$
\mechanical{\xi}^\alpha{}_{;\beta}\mechanical{u}^\beta
=\mechanical{B}^\alpha{}_\beta\mechanical{\xi}^\beta,
\tag{2.16}
$$

and we see that $\mechanical{B}^\alpha{}_\beta$ measures the failure of $\mechanical{\xi}^\alpha$ to be parallel transported along the congruence.

Equation (2.16) is directly analogous to the first equation of Section 2.2. We may decompose $\mechanical{B}_{\alpha\beta}$ into trace, symmetric-tracefree, and antisymmetric parts. This gives

$$
\mechanical{B}_{\alpha\beta}
=\frac13\mechanical{\theta}h_{\alpha\beta}
+\mechanical{\sigma}_{\alpha\beta}+\mechanical{\omega}_{\alpha\beta},
\tag{2.17}
$$

where $\mechanical{\theta}=\mechanical{B}^\alpha{}_\alpha=\mechanical{u}^\alpha{}_{;\alpha}$ is the *expansion scalar*, $\mechanical{\sigma}_{\alpha\beta}=\mechanical{B}_{(\alpha\beta)}-\tfrac13\mechanical{\theta}h_{\alpha\beta}$ the *shear tensor*, and $\mechanical{\omega}_{\alpha\beta}=\mechanical{B}_{[\alpha\beta]}$ the *rotation tensor*. These quantities come with the same interpretation as in Section 2.2. In particular, the congruence will be diverging (geodesics flying apart) if $\mechanical{\theta}>0$, and it will be converging (geodesics coming together) if $\mechanical{\theta}<0$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{u}^\alpha,\mechanical{u}_\alpha$ | — | Timelike tangent | The congruence's four-velocity or its metric dual. |
| $g_{\alpha\beta},h_{\alpha\beta}$ | — | Metric tensors | The spacetime metric and its transverse projection. |
| $\mechanical{B}_{\alpha\beta},\mechanical{B}^\alpha{}_\beta$ | — | Deformation tensor | The velocity gradient that changes the deviation vector. |
| $\mechanical{\xi}^\alpha,\mechanical{\xi}_\alpha$ | — | Deviation vector | The separation of neighbouring geodesics, with coordinate-dependent component units. |
| $\mechanical{\theta}$ | $\unit{\mathrm{m^{-1}}}$ | Expansion scalar | The fractional volume-change rate per unit proper time. |
| $\mechanical{\sigma}_{\alpha\beta}$ | — | Shear tensor | The symmetric tracefree deformation with coordinate-dependent component units. |
| $\mechanical{\omega}_{\alpha\beta}$ | — | Rotation tensor | The antisymmetric deformation with coordinate-dependent component units. |
