> [!TLDR]
> Expansion changes a small region's area through the trace of the deformation tensor.

<!-- PDF page 50; printed page 34 -->

Suppose first that $\mechanical{B}^a{}_b$ is proportional to the identity matrix, so that

$$
\mechanical{B}^a{}_b=\begin{pmatrix}\tfrac12\mechanical{\theta}&0\\0&\tfrac12\mechanical{\theta}\end{pmatrix},
$$

where $\mechanical{\theta}=\mechanical{B}^a{}_a$. Then $\Delta\mechanical{\xi}^a=\tfrac12\mechanical{\theta}\mechanical{r}_0\Delta\mechanical{t}(\cos\mechanical{\phi},\sin\mechanical{\phi})$, which corresponds to a change in the circle's radius: $\mechanical{r}_1=\mechanical{r}_0+\tfrac12\mechanical{\theta}\mechanical{r}_0\Delta\mechanical{t}$. The corresponding change in area is then $\Delta\mechanical{A}=\mechanical{A}_1-\mechanical{A}_0=\pi\mechanical{r}_0^2\mechanical{\theta}\Delta\mechanical{t}$, so that

$$
\mechanical{\theta}=\frac{1}{\mechanical{A}_0}\frac{\Delta\mechanical{A}}{\Delta\mechanical{t}}.
$$

The quantity $\mechanical{\theta}$ is therefore the fractional change of area per unit time; we shall call it the *expansion parameter*. This is actually a function, because $\mechanical{\theta}$ may depend on time and on the choice of reference point $O$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{B}^a{}_b,\mechanical{B}_{ab}$ | $\unit{\mathrm{m^{-1}}}$ | Deformation rate | The tensor that gives the rate of change of displacement. |
| $\mechanical{\theta}$ | $\unit{\mathrm{m^{-1}}}$ | Expansion rate | The fractional area or volume change per unit geometrized time. |
| $\mechanical{\xi}^a,\Delta\mechanical{\xi}^a$ | $\unit{\mathrm{m}}$ | Displacement | The displacement from the reference point and its change. |
| $\mechanical{r}_0,\mechanical{r}_1$ | $\unit{\mathrm{m}}$ | Radius | The original radius or the radius after deformation. |
| $\mechanical{t},\mechanical{t}_0,\mechanical{t}_1,\Delta\mechanical{t}$ | $\unit{\mathrm{m}}$ | Time | Time and its interval in the book's geometrized units. |
| $\mechanical{\phi},\mechanical{\phi}'$ | $\unit{\mathrm{rad}}$ | Orientation angle | The angle that specifies a direction in the medium. |
| $\mechanical{A}_0,\mechanical{A}_1,\Delta\mechanical{A}$ | $\unit{\mathrm{m^2}}$ | Area | The initial area, final area, and change in area. |
