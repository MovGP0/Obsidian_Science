> [!TLDR]
> The dominant energy condition requires matter's measured momentum density to point along a future-directed timelike or null direction.

<!-- PDF page 48; printed page 32 -->

The dominant energy condition embodies the notion that matter should flow along timelike or null world lines. Its precise statement is that if $\mechanical{v}^\alpha$ is an arbitrary, future-directed, timelike vector field, then

$$
-\energy{T}^\alpha{}_\beta\mechanical{v}^\beta
\text{ is a future-directed, timelike or null, vector field.}
\tag{2.12}
$$

The quantity $-\energy{T}^\alpha{}_\beta\mechanical{v}^\beta$ is the matter's momentum density as measured by an observer with four-velocity $\mechanical{v}^\alpha$, and this is required to be timelike or null. Substituting Eqs. (2.1) and (2.4) and demanding that $-\energy{T}^\alpha{}_\beta\mechanical{v}^\beta$ not be spacelike gives

$$
\energy{\rho}^2-a^2\mechanical{p}_1^2-b^2\mechanical{p}_2^2-c^2\mechanical{p}_3^2\ge0.
$$

Choosing $a=b=c=0$ gives $\energy{\rho}^2\ge0$, and demanding that $-\energy{T}^\alpha{}_\beta\mechanical{v}^\beta$ be future directed selects the positive branch: $\energy{\rho}\ge0$. Alternatively, choosing $b=c=0$ gives $\energy{\rho}^2\ge a^2\mechanical{p}_1^2$. Because this must hold for any $a^2<1$, we have $\energy{\rho}\ge|\mechanical{p}_1|$, having taken the future direction for $-\energy{T}^\alpha{}_\beta\mechanical{v}^\beta$. Similar relations hold for $\mechanical{p}_2$ and $\mechanical{p}_3$. The dominant energy condition therefore implies

$$
\energy{\rho}\ge0,\qquad\energy{\rho}\ge|\mechanical{p}_i|.
\tag{2.13}
$$

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\energy{T}_{\alpha\beta},\energy{T}^\alpha{}_\beta$ | — | Stress-energy tensor | The matter tensor with coordinate-dependent component units. |
| $\energy{\rho}$ | $\unit{\mathrm{m^{-2}}}$ | Energy density | The density in the matter's orthonormal frame, in geometrized units. |
| $\mechanical{p}_i$ | $\unit{\mathrm{m^{-2}}}$ | Principal pressures | The pressure eigenvalues in geometrized units. |
| $\mechanical{v}^\alpha$ | — | Observer four-velocity | The observer's future-directed timelike velocity. |
