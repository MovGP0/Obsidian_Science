> [!TLDR]
> The antisymmetric part of the deformation tensor rotates a small region.

<!-- PDF page 51; printed page 35 -->

Finally, we suppose that $\mechanical{B}^a{}_b$ is antisymmetric, so that

$$
\mechanical{B}^a{}_b=\begin{pmatrix}0&\mechanical{\omega}\\-\mechanical{\omega}&0\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Then $\Delta\mechanical{\xi}^a=\mechanical{r}_0\mechanical{\omega}\Delta\mechanical{t}(\sin\mechanical{\phi},-\cos\mechanical{\phi})$, and the new displacement vector is $\mechanical{\xi}^a(\mechanical{t}_1)=\mechanical{r}_0(\cos\mechanical{\phi}',\sin\mechanical{\phi}')$, where $\mechanical{\phi}'=\mechanical{\phi}-\mechanical{\omega}\Delta\mechanical{t}$. This clearly represents an overall rotation of the original figure, and this operation also leaves the area unchanged; $\mechanical{\omega}$ is called the *rotation parameter*.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{B}^a{}_b,\mechanical{B}_{ab}$ | $\unit{\mathrm{m^{-1}}}$ | Deformation rate | The tensor that gives the rate of change of displacement. |
| $\mechanical{\omega},\mechanical{\omega}_{ab}$ | $\unit{\mathrm{m^{-1}}}$ | Rotation rate | The angular rate or its antisymmetric tensor. |
| $\mechanical{\xi}^a,\Delta\mechanical{\xi}^a$ | $\unit{\mathrm{m}}$ | Displacement | The displacement from the reference point and its change. |
| $\mechanical{r}_0,\mechanical{r}_1$ | $\unit{\mathrm{m}}$ | Radius | The original radius or the radius after deformation. |
| $\mechanical{t},\mechanical{t}_0,\mechanical{t}_1,\Delta\mechanical{t}$ | $\unit{\mathrm{m}}$ | Time | Time and its interval in the book's geometrized units. |
| $\mechanical{\phi},\mechanical{\phi}'$ | $\unit{\mathrm{rad}}$ | Orientation angle | The angle that specifies a direction in the medium. |
