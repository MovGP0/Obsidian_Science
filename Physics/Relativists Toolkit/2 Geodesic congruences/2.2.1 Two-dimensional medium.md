> [!TLDR]
> A local deformation tensor relates a small displacement to its rate of change.

<!-- PDF page 49; printed page 33 -->

As a warm-up for what is to follow, consider, in a purely Newtonian context, the internal motion of a two-dimensional deformable medium. (Picture this as a thin sheet of rubber; see Fig. 2.1.) How the medium actually moves depends on its internal dynamics, which will remain unspecified for the purpose of this discussion. From a purely kinematical point of view, however, we may always write that for a sufficiently small displacement $\mechanical{\xi}^a$ about a reference point $O$,

$$
\frac{d\mechanical{\xi}^a}{d\mechanical{t}}
=\mechanical{B}^a{}_b(\mechanical{t})\mechanical{\xi}^b+O(\mechanical{\xi}^2),
$$

for some tensor $\mechanical{B}^a{}_b$. The time dependence of this tensor is determined by the medium's dynamics. For short time intervals,

$$
\mechanical{\xi}^a(\mechanical{t}_1)
=\mechanical{\xi}^a(\mechanical{t}_0)+\Delta\mechanical{\xi}^a(\mechanical{t}_0),
$$

where

$$
\Delta\mechanical{\xi}^a
=\mechanical{B}^a{}_b(\mechanical{t}_0)\mechanical{\xi}^b(\mechanical{t}_0)\Delta\mechanical{t}
+O(\Delta\mechanical{t}^2),
$$

and $\Delta\mechanical{t}=\mechanical{t}_1-\mechanical{t}_0$. To describe the action of $\mechanical{B}^a{}_b$ we will consider the simple figure described by $\mechanical{\xi}^a(\mechanical{t}_0)=\mechanical{r}_0(\cos\mechanical{\phi},\sin\mechanical{\phi})$; this is a circle of radius $\mechanical{r}_0$ drawn in the two-dimensional medium.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{\xi}^a,\Delta\mechanical{\xi}^a$ | $\unit{\mathrm{m}}$ | Displacement | The displacement from the reference point and its change. |
| $\mechanical{t},\mechanical{t}_0,\mechanical{t}_1,\Delta\mechanical{t}$ | $\unit{\mathrm{m}}$ | Time | Time and its interval in the book's geometrized units. |
| $\mechanical{B}^a{}_b,\mechanical{B}_{ab}$ | $\unit{\mathrm{m^{-1}}}$ | Deformation rate | The tensor that gives the rate of change of displacement. |
| $\mechanical{r}_0,\mechanical{r}_1$ | $\unit{\mathrm{m}}$ | Radius | The original radius or the radius after deformation. |
| $\mechanical{\phi},\mechanical{\phi}'$ | $\unit{\mathrm{rad}}$ | Orientation angle | The angle that specifies a direction in the medium. |
