> [!TLDR]
> Energy conditions constrain matter's stress-energy tensor and its measured density, pressures, and flow direction.

<!-- PDF page 45; printed page 29 -->

**Table 2.1** *Energy conditions.*

| Name | Statement | Conditions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Weak | $\energy{T}_{\alpha\beta}\mechanical{v}^\alpha\mechanical{v}^\beta\ge0$ | $\energy{\rho}\ge0,\quad\energy{\rho}+\mechanical{p}_i>0$ |
| Null | $\energy{T}_{\alpha\beta}\mechanical{k}^\alpha\mechanical{k}^\beta\ge0$ | $\energy{\rho}+\mechanical{p}_i\ge0$ |
| Strong | $(\energy{T}_{\alpha\beta}-\tfrac12\energy{T}g_{\alpha\beta})\mechanical{v}^\alpha\mechanical{v}^\beta\ge0$ | $\energy{\rho}+\sum_i\mechanical{p}_i\ge0,\quad\energy{\rho}+\mechanical{p}_i\ge0$ |
| Dominant | $-\energy{T}^\alpha{}_\beta\mechanical{v}^\beta$ future directed | $\energy{\rho}\ge0,\quad\energy{\rho}\ge\lvert\mechanical{p}_i\rvert$ |

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\energy{T}_{\alpha\beta},\energy{T}^\alpha{}_\beta$ | — | Stress-energy tensor | The matter tensor with component units set by the coordinates. |
| $\energy{T}$ | $\unit{\mathrm{m^{-2}}}$ | Stress-energy trace | The invariant trace in geometrized units. |
| $\energy{\rho}$ | $\unit{\mathrm{m^{-2}}}$ | Energy density | The energy density in the matter's orthonormal frame. |
| $\mechanical{p}_i$ | $\unit{\mathrm{m^{-2}}}$ | Principal pressures | The pressure eigenvalues in geometrized units. |
| $\mechanical{v}^\alpha$ | — | Observer four-velocity | The future-directed timelike vector used to measure energy density. |
| $\mechanical{k}^\alpha$ | — | Null vector | The future-directed null vector in the null condition. |
| $g_{\alpha\beta}$ | — | Metric tensor | The spacetime metric with coordinate-dependent component units. |
