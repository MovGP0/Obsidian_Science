> [!TLDR]
> A general two-dimensional deformation separates into expansion, shear, and rotation.

<!-- PDF page 51; printed page 35 -->

The most general matrix $\mechanical{B}^a{}_b$ has $2\times2=4$ components, and it may be expressed as

$$
\mechanical{B}^a{}_b
=\begin{pmatrix}\tfrac12\mechanical{\theta}&0\\0&\tfrac12\mechanical{\theta}\end{pmatrix}
+\begin{pmatrix}\mechanical{\sigma}_+&\mechanical{\sigma}_\times\\\mechanical{\sigma}_\times&-\mechanical{\sigma}_+\end{pmatrix}
+\begin{pmatrix}0&\mechanical{\omega}\\-\mechanical{\omega}&0\end{pmatrix}.
$$

The action of this most general tensor is a linear combination of expansion, shear, and rotation. The tensor can also be expressed as

$$
\mechanical{B}_{ab}=\frac12\mechanical{\theta}\delta_{ab}+\mechanical{\sigma}_{ab}+\mechanical{\omega}_{ab},
$$

where $\mechanical{\theta}=\mechanical{B}^a{}_a$ (the expansion scalar) is the trace part of $\mechanical{B}_{ab}$, $\mechanical{\sigma}_{ab}=\mechanical{B}_{(ab)}-\tfrac12\mechanical{\theta}\delta_{ab}$ (the shear tensor) is the symmetric-tracefree part of $\mechanical{B}_{ab}$, and $\mechanical{\omega}_{ab}=\mechanical{B}_{[ab]}$ (the rotation tensor) is the antisymmetric part of $\mechanical{B}_{ab}$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{B}^a{}_b,\mechanical{B}_{ab}$ | $\unit{\mathrm{m^{-1}}}$ | Deformation rate | The tensor that gives the rate of change of displacement. |
| $\mechanical{\theta}$ | $\unit{\mathrm{m^{-1}}}$ | Expansion rate | The fractional area or volume change per unit geometrized time. |
| $\mechanical{\sigma}_+,\mechanical{\sigma}_\times,\mechanical{\sigma}_{ab}$ | $\unit{\mathrm{m^{-1}}}$ | Shear rate | The two shear parameters or the shear tensor. |
| $\mechanical{\omega},\mechanical{\omega}_{ab}$ | $\unit{\mathrm{m^{-1}}}$ | Rotation rate | The angular rate or its antisymmetric tensor. |
