> [!TLDR]
> Independent lapse, shift, metric, and momentum variations give the full gravitational Hamiltonian variation after boundary terms cancel.

<!-- Source: PDF page 157, printed page 141. -->

The equations of motion for the gravitational field are obtained by varying the action of Eq. (4.55) with respect to $N$, $N^a$, $h_{ab}$, and $\mechanical{p^{ab}}$, which are all treated as independent variables. The variation is restricted by the conditions

$$
\delta N=\delta N^a=\delta h_{ab}=0\qquad\text{on }S_t, \tag{4.63}
$$

but there is no requirement that $\delta\mechanical{p^{ab}}$ vanish on the boundary. As a preliminary step toward calculating $\delta\energy{S_G}$, we will now carry out the variation of $\energy{H_G}$. The computations presented here are rather formidable; the punch line is delivered in Eq. (4.73) below.

We begin with a variation with respect to both $N$ and $N^a$. Taking Eq. (4.63) into account, Eq. (4.61) gives

$$
(16\pi)\delta_N\energy{H_G}=\int_{\Sigma_t}(-\hat{\mathcal C}\delta N-2\hat{\mathcal C}_a\delta N^a)\sqrt{h}\,\mathrm d^3y, \tag{4.64}
$$

where

$$
\hat{\mathcal C}\equiv{}^3R+K^2-K^{ab}K_{ab},\qquad
\hat{\mathcal C}_a\equiv(K_a{}^b-K\delta_a{}^b)_{|b}. \tag{4.65}
$$

This was easy; the remaining variations will require a much larger effort.

To carry out a variation with respect to $h_{ab}$ or $\mechanical{p^{ab}}$, we must express $\energy{H_G}$ in terms of these variables, instead of $h_{ab}$ and $K^{ab}$ as was done in Eq. (4.61). Using Eq. (4.62), a few steps of algebra give

$$
(16\pi)\energy{H_G}=\energy{\hat H_\Sigma}+\energy{\hat H_S}, \tag{4.66}
$$

where

$$
\energy{\hat H_\Sigma}=\int_{\Sigma_t}\left[
Nh^{-1/2}\left(\mechanical{\hat p^{ab}}\mechanical{\hat p_{ab}}-\tfrac12\mechanical{\hat p}^2\right)
-Nh^{1/2}{}^3R-2N_ah^{1/2}\left(h^{-1/2}\mechanical{\hat p^{ab}}\right)_{|b}\right]\mathrm d^3y \tag{4.67}
$$

is the ‘volume’ term, while

$$
\energy{\hat H_S}=-2\oint_{S_t}\left[N(k-k_0)-N_ah^{-1/2}\mechanical{\hat p^{ab}}r_b\right]\sqrt{\sigma}\,\mathrm d^2\theta \tag{4.68}
$$

is the ‘boundary’ term. We have introduced the notation $\energy{\hat H_\Sigma}\equiv(16\pi)\energy{H_\Sigma}$, $\mechanical{\hat p^{ab}}\equiv(16\pi)\mechanical{p^{ab}}$, and so on; this usage was anticipated in Eqs. (4.65).

We first vary $\energy{\hat H_G}$ with respect to $\mechanical{\hat p^{ab}}$. From Eq. (4.67) we have

$$
\delta_p\energy{\hat H_\Sigma}
=\int_{\Sigma_t}Nh^{-1/2}\delta_p\left(\mechanical{\hat p^{ab}}\mechanical{\hat p_{ab}}-\tfrac12\mechanical{\hat p}^2\right)\mathrm d^3y
-2\delta_p\int_{\Sigma_t}N_a\left(h^{-1/2}\mechanical{\hat p^{ab}}\right)_{|b}h^{1/2}\,\mathrm d^3y.
$$

We substitute

$$
\delta_p\left(\mechanical{\hat p^{ab}}\mechanical{\hat p_{ab}}-\tfrac12\mechanical{\hat p}^2\right)
=2\left(\mechanical{\hat p_{ab}}-\tfrac12\mechanical{\hat p}h_{ab}\right)\delta\mechanical{\hat p^{ab}}
$$

<!-- Source: PDF page 158, printed page 142. -->

inside the first integral, and we integrate the second by parts. This gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
\delta_p\energy{\hat H_\Sigma}
&=\int_{\Sigma_t}2\left[Nh^{-1/2}\left(\mechanical{\hat p_{ab}}-\tfrac12\mechanical{\hat p}h_{ab}\right)+N_{(a|b)}\right]\delta\mechanical{\hat p^{ab}}\,\mathrm d^3y\\
&\quad-2\oint_{S_t}N_ah^{-1/2}\delta\mechanical{\hat p^{ab}}r_b\sqrt{\sigma}\,\mathrm d^2\theta.
\end{aligned}
$$

The boundary term is precisely equal to (minus) the variation of $\energy{\hat H_S}$. We therefore have obtained

$$
\delta_p\energy{\hat H_G}=\int_{\Sigma_t}\mathcal H_{ab}\delta\mechanical{\hat p^{ab}}\,\mathrm d^3y, \tag{4.69}
$$

where

$$
\mathcal H_{ab}=2Nh^{-1/2}\left(\mechanical{\hat p_{ab}}-\frac12\mechanical{\hat p}h_{ab}\right)+2N_{(a|b)}. \tag{4.70}
$$

To vary $\energy{\hat H_G}$ with respect to $h_{ab}$ is more laborious, and we will rely on computations already presented in Section 4.1. We begin with the volume term:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\delta_h\energy{\hat H_\Sigma}
&=\int_{\Sigma_t}\left[
-Nh^{-1}\left(\mechanical{\hat p^{ab}}\mechanical{\hat p_{ab}}-\tfrac12\mechanical{\hat p}^2\right)\delta_h h^{1/2}
+Nh^{-1/2}\delta_h\left(\mechanical{\hat p^{ab}}\mechanical{\hat p_{ab}}-\tfrac12\mechanical{\hat p}^2\right)
-N\delta_h(h^{1/2}{}^3R)\right]\mathrm d^3y\\
&\quad-2\delta_h\oint_{S_t}N_ah^{-1/2}\mechanical{\hat p^{ab}}r_b\sqrt{\sigma}\,\mathrm d^2\theta
+2\delta_h\int_{\Sigma_t}N_{a|b}\mechanical{\hat p^{ab}}\,\mathrm d^3y,
\end{aligned}
$$

in which the last term on the right-hand side of Eq. (4.67) was integrated by parts. The variation of the integral over $S_t$ vanishes because $h_{ab}$ is fixed on the boundary. In the first term within the integral over $\Sigma_t$ we substitute

$$
\delta_h h^{1/2}=\frac12h^{1/2}h^{ab}\delta h_{ab},
$$

while in the second term,

$$
\delta_h\left(\mechanical{\hat p^{ab}}\mechanical{\hat p_{ab}}-\tfrac12\mechanical{\hat p}^2\right)
=2\left(\mechanical{\hat p^a{}_c}\mechanical{\hat p^{cb}}-\tfrac12\mechanical{\hat p}\mechanical{\hat p^{ab}}\right)\delta h_{ab}.
$$

In the third term, we use the three-dimensional version of Eq. (4.16),

$$
\delta_h(h^{1/2}{}^3R)=-h^{1/2}G^{ab}\delta h_{ab}+h^{1/2}\not\!\delta v^c{}_{|c},
$$

where $G^{ab}=R^{ab}-\frac12{}^3Rh^{ab}$ is the three-dimensional Einstein tensor and $\not\!\delta v^c=h^{ab}\delta\Gamma^c_{ab}-h^{ac}\delta\Gamma^b_{ab}$. Finally, in the last term we substitute

$$
\delta_h N_{a|b}=N^c{}_{|b}\delta h_{ac}+h_{ac}N^d\delta\Gamma^c_{bd}.
$$

After a few steps of algebra we obtain

$$
\begin{aligned}
\delta_h\energy{\hat H_\Sigma}
&=\int_{\Sigma_t}\left[
-\tfrac12Nh^{-1/2}\left(\mechanical{\hat p^{cd}}\mechanical{\hat p_{cd}}-\tfrac12\mechanical{\hat p}^2\right)h^{ab}
+2Nh^{-1/2}\left(\mechanical{\hat p^a{}_c}\mechanical{\hat p^{bc}}-\tfrac12\mechanical{\hat p}\mechanical{\hat p^{ab}}\right)\right.\\
&\qquad\left.+Nh^{1/2}G^{ab}+2\mechanical{\hat p^{c(a}}N^{b)}{}_{|c}\right]\delta h_{ab}\,\mathrm d^3y\\
&\quad+\int_{\Sigma_t}\left[-Nh^{1/2}\not\!\delta v^c{}_{|c}+2\mechanical{\hat p^b{}_c}N^d\delta\Gamma^c_{bd}\right]\mathrm d^3y.
\end{aligned}
$$

We now leave the first integral alone and set to work on the second integral, beginning with the first term. After integrating by parts,

$$
\begin{aligned}
-\int_{\Sigma_t}Nh^{1/2}\not\!\delta v^c{}_{|c}\,\mathrm d^3y
&=\int_{\Sigma_t}N_{,c}\not\!\delta v^ch^{1/2}\,\mathrm d^3y-\oint_{S_t}N\not\!\delta v^cr_c\sqrt{\sigma}\,\mathrm d^2\theta\\
&=\int_{\Sigma_t}N_{,c}\not\!\delta v^ch^{1/2}\,\mathrm d^3y+\oint_{S_t}Nh^{ab}\delta h_{ab,c}r^c\sqrt{\sigma}\,\mathrm d^2\theta,
\end{aligned}
$$

where the three-dimensional version of Eq. (4.17) was used. To express the first integral in terms of $\delta h_{ab}$ we use the relation

$$
\delta\Gamma^c_{ab}=\frac12h^{cd}\left[(\delta h_{da})_{|b}+(\delta h_{db})_{|a}-(\delta h_{ab})_{|d}\right],
$$

which is easy to establish. (Note that the covariant derivative is defined with respect to the reference metric $h_{ab}$, about which the variation is taken.) We have

$$
\not\!\delta v^c=\frac12(h^{ab}h^{cd}-h^{ac}h^{bd})\left[(\delta h_{da})_{|b}+(\delta h_{db})_{|a}-(\delta h_{ab})_{|d}\right]
$$

and then

$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{,c}\not\!\delta v^c
&=\frac12(h^{ab}N^{,d}-N^{,a}h^{bd})\left[(\delta h_{da})_{|b}+(\delta h_{db})_{|a}-(\delta h_{ab})_{|d}\right]\\
&=-(h^{ab}N^{,d}-N^{,a}h^{bd})(\delta h_{ab})_{|d};
\end{aligned}
$$

the second line follows by virtue of the antisymmetry in $a$ and $d$ of the first factor. After another integration by parts we obtain

$$
\begin{aligned}
-\int_{\Sigma_t}Nh^{1/2}\not\!\delta v^c{}_{|c}\,\mathrm d^3y
&=\int_{\Sigma_t}(h^{ab}N^{|d}{}_d-N^{|ab})\delta h_{ab}h^{1/2}\,\mathrm d^3y\\
&\quad+\oint_{S_t}Nh^{ab}\delta h_{ab,c}r^c\sqrt{\sigma}\,\mathrm d^2\theta,
\end{aligned}
$$

where we have used the fact that $\delta h_{ab}$ vanishes on $S_t$. All this takes care of the first term inside the second integral for $\delta_h\energy{\hat H_\Sigma}$. We now turn to the second term inside the same integral. We have

<!-- Source: PDF page 160, printed page 144. -->

$$
\begin{aligned}
\int_{\Sigma_t}2\mechanical{\hat p^b{}_c}N^d\delta\Gamma^c_{bd}\,\mathrm d^3y
&=\int_{\Sigma_t}\mechanical{\hat p^{ab}}N^d\left[(\delta h_{ab})_{|d}+(\delta h_{ad})_{|b}-(\delta h_{bd})_{|a}\right]\mathrm d^3y\\
&=\int_{\Sigma_t}h^{-1/2}\mechanical{\hat p^{ab}}N^d(\delta h_{ab})_{|d}h^{1/2}\,\mathrm d^3y\\
&=-\int_{\Sigma_t}\left(h^{-1/2}\mechanical{\hat p^{ab}}N^d\right)_{|d}\delta h_{ab}h^{1/2}\,\mathrm d^3y,
\end{aligned}
$$

where we have integrated by parts and put $\delta h_{ab}=0$ on $S_t$.

Gathering the results, we find that the variation of the volume term is

$$
\delta_h\energy{\hat H_\Sigma}=\int_{\Sigma_t}\hat{\mathcal P}^{ab}\delta h_{ab}\,\mathrm d^3y
+\oint_{S_t}Nh^{ab}\delta h_{ab,c}r^c\sqrt{\sigma}\,\mathrm d^2\theta,
$$

where $\hat{\mathcal P}^{ab}$ will be written in full below. On the other hand, variation of the boundary term gives

$$
\delta_h\energy{\hat H_S}=-2\oint_{S_t}N\delta k\sqrt{\sigma}\,\mathrm d^2\theta,
$$

and $\delta k=\frac12h^{ab}\delta h_{ab,c}r^c$ is the three-dimensional analogue of a result previously derived in Section 4.1.5. Thus,

$$
\delta_h\energy{\hat H_S}=-\oint_{S_t}Nh^{ab}\delta h_{ab,c}r^c\sqrt{\sigma}\,\mathrm d^2\theta,
$$

and this cancels out the boundary integral in $\delta_h\energy{\hat H_\Sigma}$. The variation of the full Hamiltonian is therefore

$$
\delta_h\energy{\hat H_G}=\int_{\Sigma_t}\hat{\mathcal P}^{ab}\delta h_{ab}\,\mathrm d^3y, \tag{4.71}
$$

where

$$
\begin{aligned}
\hat{\mathcal P}^{ab}
&=Nh^{1/2}G^{ab}-\frac12Nh^{-1/2}\left(\mechanical{\hat p^{cd}}\mechanical{\hat p_{cd}}-\tfrac12\mechanical{\hat p}^2\right)h^{ab}
+2Nh^{-1/2}\left(\mechanical{\hat p^a{}_c}\mechanical{\hat p^{bc}}-\tfrac12\mechanical{\hat p}\mechanical{\hat p^{ab}}\right)\\
&\quad-h^{1/2}(N^{|ab}-h^{ab}N^{|c}{}_c)
-h^{1/2}\left(h^{-1/2}\mechanical{\hat p^{ab}}N^c\right)_{|c}
+2\mechanical{\hat p^{c(a}}N^{b)}{}_{|c}.
\end{aligned} \tag{4.72}
$$

Here, as before, $G^{ab}=R^{ab}-\frac12{}^3Rh^{ab}$ is the three-dimensional Einstein tensor.

Combining Eqs. (4.64), (4.69), and (4.71) we find that the complete variation of the gravitational Hamiltonian, under the conditions of Eq. (4.63), is given by

$$
\delta\energy{H_G}=\int_{\Sigma_t}\left(\mathcal P^{ab}\delta h_{ab}+\mathcal H_{ab}\delta\mechanical{p^{ab}}-\mathcal C\delta N-2\mathcal C_a\delta N^a\right)\mathrm d^3y, \tag{4.73}
$$

where $\mathcal P^{ab}\equiv\hat{\mathcal P}^{ab}/(16\pi)$ is given by Eq. (4.72), $\mathcal H_{ab}$ by Eq. (4.70), while $\mathcal C\equiv\hat{\mathcal C}/(16\pi)$ and $\mathcal C^a\equiv\hat{\mathcal C}^a/(16\pi)$ are given by Eq. (4.65).
