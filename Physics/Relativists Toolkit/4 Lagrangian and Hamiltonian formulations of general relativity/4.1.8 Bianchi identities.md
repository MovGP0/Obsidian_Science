> [!TLDR]
> Coordinate invariance of the gravitational action gives the contracted Bianchi identities.

<!-- Source: PDF page 143, printed page 127. -->

The Lagrangian formulation of general relativity provides us with an elegant derivation of the contracted Bianchi identities,

$$
G^{\alpha\beta}{}_{;\beta}=0. \tag{4.25}
$$

In this approach, Eq. (4.25) comes as a consequence of the invariance of $\energy{S_G}[g]$ under a change of coordinates in $\mathcal V$.

To prove this it is sufficient to consider infinitesimal transformations,

$$
x^\alpha\to x'^\alpha=x^\alpha+\epsilon^\alpha, \tag{4.26}
$$

where $\epsilon^\alpha$ is an infinitesimal vector field, arbitrary within $\mathcal V$ but constrained to vanish on $\partial\mathcal V$. The variation of the metric under such a transformation is

$$
\begin{aligned}
\delta g_{\alpha\beta}&\equiv g'_{\alpha\beta}(x)-g_{\alpha\beta}(x)\\
&=g'_{\alpha\beta}(x')-g_{\alpha\beta}(x)+g'_{\alpha\beta}(x)-g'_{\alpha\beta}(x')\\
&=\frac{\partial x^\mu}{\partial x'^\alpha}\frac{\partial x^\nu}{\partial x'^\beta}g_{\mu\nu}(x)-g_{\alpha\beta}(x)+g'_{\alpha\beta}(x)-g'_{\alpha\beta}(x+\epsilon)\\
&=(\delta^\mu_\alpha-\epsilon^\mu{}_{,\alpha})(\delta^\nu_\beta-\epsilon^\nu{}_{,\beta})g_{\mu\nu}(x)-g_{\alpha\beta}(x)-g_{\alpha\beta,\mu}(x)\epsilon^\mu\\
&=-\epsilon^\mu{}_{,\alpha}g_{\beta\mu}-\epsilon^\mu{}_{,\beta}g_{\alpha\mu}-g_{\alpha\beta,\mu}\epsilon^\mu\\
&=-\mathcal L_\epsilon g_{\alpha\beta},
\end{aligned}
$$

discarding all terms of the second order in $\epsilon^\alpha$. Using Eq. (4.14) we find that the metric variation is

$$
\delta g^{\alpha\beta}=\epsilon^{\alpha;\beta}+\epsilon^{\beta;\alpha}. \tag{4.27}
$$

<!-- Source: PDF page 144, printed page 128. -->

Substituting this into Eq. (4.20), we find

$$
\begin{aligned}
(8\pi)\delta\energy{S_G}&=\int_{\mathcal V}G^{\alpha\beta}\epsilon_{\alpha;\beta}\sqrt{-g}\,\mathrm d^4x\\
&=-\int_{\mathcal V}G^{\alpha\beta}{}_{;\beta}\epsilon_\alpha\sqrt{-g}\,\mathrm d^4x+\oint_{\partial\mathcal V}G^{\alpha\beta}\epsilon_\alpha\,\mathrm d\Sigma_\beta.
\end{aligned}
$$

With $\epsilon^\alpha$ arbitrary within $\mathcal V$ but vanishing on $\partial\mathcal V$, the contracted Bianchi identities follow from the requirement that $\delta\energy{S_G}=0$ under the variation of Eq. (4.27).

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $G^{\alpha\beta}$ | — | Einstein tensor | The tensor whose covariant divergence vanishes. |
| $\energy{S_G}$ | $\unit{\mathrm{m}^2}$ | Gravitational action | The coordinate-invariant gravitational action. |
| $g_{\alpha\beta},g^{\alpha\beta}$ | — | Metrics | The metric and its inverse under the coordinate change. |
| $x^\alpha,x'^\alpha$ | — | Coordinates | The original and transformed spacetime coordinates. |
