> [!TLDR]
> The momentum conjugate to the spatial metric determines extrinsic curvature, and the gravitational Hamiltonian contains volume constraints and boundary terms.

<!-- Source: PDF page 155, printed page 139. -->

To construct the Hamiltonian we must express $\energy{S_G}$ in terms of

$$
\dot h_{ab}\equiv\mathcal L_t h_{ab}, \tag{4.56}
$$

where $t^\alpha$ is the timelike vector field defined by Eq. (4.36). We calculate this as follows. First we recall the definition of the induced metric and write

$$
\dot h_{ab}=\mathcal L_t(g_{\alpha\beta}e^\alpha_ae^\beta_b)=(\mathcal L_t g_{\alpha\beta})e^\alpha_ae^\beta_b,
$$

where we have used Eq. (4.34). Equation (4.36) implies that the Lie derivative of the metric is given by

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal L_t g_{\alpha\beta}&=t_{\alpha;\beta}+t_{\beta;\alpha}\\
&=(Nn_\alpha+N_\alpha)_{;\beta}+(Nn_\beta+N_\beta)_{;\alpha}\\
&=n_\alpha N_{,\beta}+N_{,\alpha}n_\beta+N(n_{\alpha;\beta}+n_{\beta;\alpha})+N_{\alpha;\beta}+N_{\beta;\alpha},
\end{aligned}
$$

where $N^\alpha=N^ae^\alpha_a$. Finally, projecting this along $e^\alpha_ae^\beta_b$ gives

$$
\dot h_{ab}=2NK_{ab}+N_{a|b}+N_{b|a},
$$

where we have used the definitions of extrinsic curvature and intrinsic covariant differentiation found in Section 3.4.

We have obtained

$$
K_{ab}=\frac{1}{2N}\left(\dot h_{ab}-N_{a|b}-N_{b|a}\right). \tag{4.57}
$$

The gravitational action therefore depends on $\dot h_{ab}$ through the extrinsic curvature. Notice that the action does not involve $\dot N$ nor $\dot N^a$, so that momenta conjugate to $N$ and $N^a$ are *not defined*. This means that unlike $h_{ab}$, the lapse and the shift are not *dynamical* variables. This was to be expected: $N$ and $N^a$ only serve to specify the foliation of $\mathcal V$ into the spacelike hypersurfaces $\Sigma_t$; because this foliation is arbitrary, we are completely free in our choice of lapse and shift.

The momentum conjugate to $h_{ab}$ is defined by

$$
\mechanical{p^{ab}}=\frac{\partial}{\partial\dot h_{ab}}\left(\sqrt{-g}\,\energy{\mathcal L_G}\right), \tag{4.58}
$$

where $\energy{\mathcal L_G}$ is the ‘volume part’ of the gravitational Lagrangian. (The ‘boundary part’ is independent of $\dot h_{ab}$.) Because $\energy{\mathcal L_G}$ is expressed in terms of $K_{ab}$, it is convenient to write Eq. (4.58) in the form

$$
(16\pi)\mechanical{p^{ab}}=\frac{\partial K_{mn}}{\partial\dot h_{ab}}\frac{\partial}{\partial K_{mn}}\left(16\pi\sqrt{-g}\,\energy{\mathcal L_G}\right),
$$

<!-- Source: PDF page 156, printed page 140. -->

where

$$
16\pi\sqrt{-g}\,\energy{\mathcal L_G}
=\left[{}^3R+(h^{ac}h^{bd}-h^{ab}h^{cd})K_{ab}K_{cd}\right]N\sqrt{h}
$$

follows from Eq. (4.55). Evaluating the partial derivatives gives

$$
(16\pi)\mechanical{p^{ab}}=\sqrt{h}\left(K^{ab}-Kh^{ab}\right), \tag{4.59}
$$

and we see that the canonical momentum is closely related to the extrinsic curvature.

The ‘volume part’ of the Hamiltonian density is

$$
\energy{\mathcal H_G}=\mechanical{p^{ab}}\dot h_{ab}-\sqrt{-g}\,\energy{\mathcal L_G}. \tag{4.60}
$$

Using our previous results, we have

$$
\begin{aligned}
(16\pi)\energy{\mathcal H_G}
&=\sqrt{h}(K^{ab}-Kh^{ab})(2NK_{ab}+N_{a|b}+N_{b|a})\\
&\quad-\left({}^3R+K^{ab}K_{ab}-K^2\right)N\sqrt{h}\\
&=\left(K^{ab}K_{ab}-K^2-{}^3R\right)N\sqrt{h}+2(K^{ab}-Kh^{ab})N_{a|b}\sqrt{h}\\
&=\left(K^{ab}K_{ab}-K^2-{}^3R\right)N\sqrt{h}+2\left[(K^{ab}-Kh^{ab})N_a\right]_{|b}\sqrt{h}\\
&\quad-2(K^{ab}-Kh^{ab})_{|b}N_a\sqrt{h}.
\end{aligned}
$$

The full Hamiltonian is obtained by integrating $\energy{\mathcal H_G}$ over $\Sigma_t$ and adding the boundary terms:

$$
\begin{aligned}
(16\pi)\energy{H_G}
&=\int_{\Sigma_t}16\pi\energy{\mathcal H_G}\,\mathrm d^3y-2\oint_{S_t}(k-k_0)N\sqrt{\sigma}\,\mathrm d^2\theta\\
&=\int_{\Sigma_t}\left[N(K^{ab}K_{ab}-K^2-{}^3R)-2N_a(K^{ab}-Kh^{ab})_{|b}\right]\sqrt{h}\,\mathrm d^3y\\
&\quad+2\oint_{S_t}(K^{ab}-Kh^{ab})N_a\,\mathrm dS_b-2\oint_{S_t}(k-k_0)N\sqrt{\sigma}\,\mathrm d^2\theta.
\end{aligned}
$$

Writing $\mathrm dS_b=r_b\sqrt{\sigma}\,\mathrm d^2\theta$, the gravitational Hamiltonian becomes

$$
\begin{aligned}
(16\pi)\energy{H_G}
&=\int_{\Sigma_t}\left[N(K^{ab}K_{ab}-K^2-{}^3R)-2N_a(K^{ab}-Kh^{ab})_{|b}\right]\sqrt{h}\,\mathrm d^3y\\
&\quad-2\oint_{S_t}\left[N(k-k_0)-N_a(K^{ab}-Kh^{ab})r_b\right]\sqrt{\sigma}\,\mathrm d^2\theta.
\end{aligned} \tag{4.61}
$$

It is understood that here, $K_{ab}$ stands for the function of $h_{ab}$ and $\mechanical{p^{ab}}$ defined by Eq. (4.59); this is given explicitly by

$$
\sqrt{h}\,K^{ab}=16\pi\left(\mechanical{p^{ab}}-\frac12\mechanical{p}h^{ab}\right), \tag{4.62}
$$

where $\mechanical{p}\equiv h_{ab}\mechanical{p^{ab}}$.
