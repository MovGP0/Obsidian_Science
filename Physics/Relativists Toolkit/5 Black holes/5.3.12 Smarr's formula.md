> [!TLDR]
> Smarr’s formula relates Kerr mass to angular momentum, horizon rotation, surface gravity, and horizon area.

<!-- Source: PDF page 216, printed page 200. -->

There exists a simple algebraic relation between the black-hole mass $\mechanical{M}$, its angular momentum $\mechanical{J}\equiv\mechanical{M}\mechanical{a}$, and its surface area $\mechanical{A}$. This is defined by

$$
\mechanical{A}=\oint_{\mathcal H}\sqrt\sigma\,\mathrm d^2\theta, \tag{5.82}
$$

where $\mathcal H$ is a two-dimensional cross section of the event horizon, described by $\mechanical{v}=\text{constant}$, $\mechanical{r}=\mechanical{r_+}$, $0\le\mechanical{\theta}\le\pi$, and $0\le\mechanical{\psi}<2\pi$. From Eq. (5.65) we find that the induced metric is given by

$$
\sigma_{AB}\,\mathrm d\theta^A\,\mathrm d\theta^B
=\rho^2\mathrm d\mechanical{\theta}^2+\frac{\Sigma}{\rho^2}\sin^2\mechanical{\theta}\,\mathrm d\mechanical{\psi}^2,
$$

so that $\sqrt\sigma\,\mathrm d^2\theta=\sqrt\Sigma\sin\mechanical{\theta}\,\mathrm d\mechanical{\theta}\,\mathrm d\mechanical{\psi}=(\mechanical{r_+}^2+\mechanical{a}^2)\sin\mechanical{\theta}\,\mathrm d\mechanical{\theta}\,\mathrm d\mechanical{\psi}$. Integration yields

$$
\mechanical{A}=4\pi(\mechanical{r_+}^2+\mechanical{a}^2). \tag{5.83}
$$

The algebraic relation, which was discovered by Larry Smarr in 1973, reads

$$
\mechanical{M}=2\mechanical{\Omega_H}\mechanical{J}+\frac{\mechanical{\kappa}\mechanical{A}}{4\pi}, \tag{5.84}
$$

where $\mechanical{\Omega_H}$ is the hole’s angular velocity and $\mechanical{\kappa}$ its surface gravity. Smarr’s formula is established by straightforward algebra: Substituting Eqs. (5.55), (5.80), and (5.83) into the right-hand side of Eq. (5.84) reveals that it is indeed equal to

<!-- Source: PDF page 217, printed page 201. -->

$\mechanical{M}$. We will generalize Smarr’s formula, and present an alternative derivation, in Section 5.5.2.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{M},\mechanical{a}$ | $\unit{\mathrm m}$ | Mass and specific angular momentum | The Kerr parameters in geometrized units. |
| $\mechanical{J}$ | $\unit{\mathrm m^2}$ | Angular momentum | The black-hole angular momentum. |
| $\mechanical{A}$ | $\unit{\mathrm m^2}$ | Horizon area | The area of a horizon cross section. |
| $\mechanical{\Omega_H}$ | $\unit{\mathrm m^{-1}}$ | Horizon angular velocity | The rotation rate in Smarr’s formula. |
| $\mechanical{\kappa}$ | $\unit{\mathrm m^{-1}}$ | Surface gravity | The acceleration parameter in Smarr’s formula. |
| $\mechanical{v},\mechanical{r},\mechanical{r_+}$ | $\unit{\mathrm m}$ | Time and radial coordinates | The fixed coordinates used to define a horizon cross section. |
| $\mechanical{\theta},\mechanical{\psi}$ | $\unit{\mathrm{rad}}$ | Angular coordinates | The angles integrated over the horizon. |
