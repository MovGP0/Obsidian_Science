> [!TLDR]
> Horizon coordinates, surface elements, and the nonaffine Raychaudhuri equation provide the framework for the four laws.

<!-- Source: PDF page 223, printed page 207. -->

We begin our discussion of the four laws by collecting a few important results from preceding chapters; these will form the bulk of the mathematical framework required for the derivations.

Let $y^a=(\mechanical{v},\theta^A)$ be coordinates on the event horizon. The advanced-time coordinate $\mechanical{v}$ is a non-affine parameter on the horizon’s null generators, and $\theta^A$ labels the generators. The vectors

$$
\xi^\alpha=\left(\frac{\partial x^\alpha}{\partial\mechanical{v}}\right)_{\theta^A},\qquad
e^\alpha_A=\left(\frac{\partial x^\alpha}{\partial\theta^A}\right)_v \tag{5.94}
$$

are tangent to the horizon; they satisfy $\xi_\alpha e^\alpha_A=0=\xi^\alpha e^A_\alpha$ and $\xi^\alpha=t^\alpha+\mechanical{\Omega_H}\phi^\alpha$ is a Killing vector. We complete the basis by introducing an auxiliary null vector $N^\alpha$, normalized by $N_\alpha\xi^\alpha=-1$. This basis gives us the completeness relations (Section 3.1)

$$
g^{\alpha\beta}=-\xi^\alpha N^\beta-N^\alpha\xi^\beta+\sigma^{AB}e^\alpha_Ae^\beta_B,
$$

where $\sigma^{AB}$ is the inverse of $\sigma_{AB}=g_{\alpha\beta}e^\alpha_Ae^\beta_B$, the metric on the two-dimensional space transverse to the generators. The determinant of the two-metric will be denoted $\sigma$.

The vectorial surface element on the event horizon can be expressed as (Section 3.2)

$$
\mathrm d\Sigma_\alpha=-\xi_\alpha\,\mathrm d\mechanical{S}\,\mathrm d\mechanical{v}, \tag{5.95}
$$

where $\mathrm d\mechanical{S}=\sqrt\sigma\,\mathrm d^2\theta$. The two-dimensional surface element on a cross section $\mechanical{v}=\text{constant}$ is

$$
\mathrm dS_{\alpha\beta}=2\xi_{[\alpha}N_{\beta]}\,\mathrm d\mechanical{S}. \tag{5.96}
$$

We shall denote such a cross section by $\mathcal H(\mechanical{v})$.

Finally, we will need Raychaudhuri’s equation for the congruence of null generators, expressed in a form that does not require the parameter to be affine. This was worked out in Section 2.6, Problem 8 and the answer is

$$
\frac{\mathrm d\mechanical{\theta}}{\mathrm d\mechanical{v}}
=\mechanical{\kappa}\mechanical{\theta}-\frac12\mechanical{\theta}^2
-\mechanical{\sigma^{\alpha\beta}}\mechanical{\sigma_{\alpha\beta}}
-8\pi\energy{T_{\alpha\beta}}\xi^\alpha\xi^\beta; \tag{5.97}
$$

the last term would normally involve the Ricci tensor, but we have used the Einstein field equations to write it in terms of the stress-energy tensor. We recall that $\mechanical{\theta}$ is the fractional rate of change of the congruence’s cross-sectional area: $\mechanical{\theta}=(\mathrm d\mechanical{S})^{-1}\mathrm d(\mathrm d\mechanical{S})/\mathrm d\mechanical{v}$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{v}$ | $\unit{\mathrm m}$ | Advanced time | The nonaffine parameter of the horizon generators. |
| $\mechanical{\Omega_H}$ | $\unit{\mathrm m^{-1}}$ | Horizon angular velocity | The rotation rate in the horizon Killing vector. |
| $\mathrm d\mechanical{S}$ | $\unit{\mathrm m^2}$ | Area element | The cross-sectional area carried by the generators. |
| $\mechanical{\theta}$ | $\unit{\mathrm m^{-1}}$ | Expansion | The fractional area change per unit advanced time. |
| $\mechanical{\kappa}$ | $\unit{\mathrm m^{-1}}$ | Surface gravity | The nonaffinity parameter in Raychaudhuri’s equation. |
| $\mechanical{\sigma_{\alpha\beta}},\mechanical{\sigma^{\alpha\beta}}$ | — | Shear tensor | Shear components in the coordinate basis. |
| $\energy{T_{\alpha\beta}}$ | — | Stress-energy tensor | The source components contracted with horizon tangents. |
