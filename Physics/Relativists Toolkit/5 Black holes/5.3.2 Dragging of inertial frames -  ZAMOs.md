> [!TLDR]
> A zero-angular-momentum observer rotates with the black hole because the metric drags local inertial frames.

<!-- Source: PDF page 204, printed page 188. -->

ZAMOs are observers with zero angular momentum: if $\mechanical{u^\alpha}$ is the four-velocity, then $\mechanical{\tilde L}\equiv\mechanical{u_\alpha}\phi^\alpha=0$. This implies that $g_{\phi t}\mechanical{\dot t}+g_{\phi\phi}\mechanical{\dot\phi}=0$, where an overdot indicates differentiation with respect to proper time $\mechanical{\tau}$. Using Eqs. (5.44) this translates to

$$
\mechanical{\Omega}\equiv\frac{\mathrm d\mechanical{\phi}}{\mathrm d\mechanical{t}}=\mechanical{\omega}, \tag{5.48}
$$

and we see that ZAMOs possess an angular velocity equal to $\mechanical{\omega}=-g_{t\phi}/g_{\phi\phi}$. This angular velocity increases as the observer approaches the black hole, and it goes in the same direction as the hole’s own rotation – the ZAMOs rotate with the black hole. This striking property of the Kerr black hole, which in fact is shared by all rotating bodies, is called the *dragging of inertial frames* (see Section 3.10). At large distances from the black hole, $\mechanical{\omega}\simeq2\mechanical{J}/\mechanical{r}^3$, and the dragging disappears completely at infinity.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{u^\alpha},\mechanical{u_\alpha}$ | — | Four-velocity | The velocity components of a ZAMO in the coordinate basis. |
| $\mechanical{\tilde L}$ | $\unit{\mathrm m}$ | Specific angular momentum | The observer’s angular momentum per unit mass. |
| $\mechanical{\Omega},\mechanical{\omega}$ | $\unit{\mathrm m^{-1}}$ | Angular velocities | The observer’s angular velocity and the frame-dragging rate. |
| $\mechanical{t},\mechanical{\tau},\mechanical{r}$ | $\unit{\mathrm m}$ | Time and radial coordinates | The coordinate time, proper time, and areal coordinate. |
| $\mechanical{\phi}$ | $\unit{\mathrm{rad}}$ | Azimuthal angle | The rotation coordinate of the observer. |
| $\mechanical{\dot t}$ | $1$ | Coordinate time rate | The derivative of coordinate time with respect to proper time. |
| $\mechanical{\dot\phi}$ | $\unit{\mathrm m^{-1}}$ | Proper-time angular rate | The azimuthal rate with respect to proper time. |
| $\mechanical{J}$ | $\unit{\mathrm m^2}$ | Black-hole angular momentum | The angular momentum that produces frame dragging. |
