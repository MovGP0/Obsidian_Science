## Reading the evidence

Use this list to trace the chapter's treatment of expansion, CMB observations, cosmic matter, curvature, and dark energy. Bracketed numbers in the lecture account refer to the original numbered entries below. A reference records provenance; it does not by itself establish the conclusion for which the lecture cites it.

## Study route

Return to [[08.00 Our Universe]] after selecting one source. Identify its measured quantity, theoretical assumptions, and uncertainty or stated limitation. For each quoted cosmological number, identify the data set and assumed cosmological model rather than treating all density components as directly counted matter. Keep historical descriptions separate from later experimental results, and distinguish the author's own proposals from independent tests.
