title: "An Interpretation of the Critical Density"

> [!TLDR]
> The author interprets critical density through balanced kinetic and gravitational energies and relates the cosmic potential to negative squared light speed.

## Physical question and model

When does the Newtonian energy analogy recover the critical-density formula? Consider a homogeneous pressureless sphere, use the mass interior to a test shell, and relate shell speed to the expansion rate. It is a restricted cosmological derivation, not a universal potential assignment.

## Lecture account

Critical density $\mechanical{\rho}_c=3\mechanical{H}_0^2/(8\pi\mechanical{G})$, corresponding to a spatially flat Universe, has a Newtonian interpretation: a particle’s kinetic energy equals its negative gravitational potential energy. The source writes $\mechanical{m}\mechanical{v}^2/2=\mechanical{m}\dot{\mechanical{r}}^2/2=4\pi\mechanical{G}\mechanical{\rho}\mechanical{r}^2/3$. Then $\mechanical{\rho}=3\dot{\mechanical{r}}^2/(8\pi\mechanical{G}\mechanical{r}^2)=3\mechanical{H}^2/(8\pi\mechanical{G})$, the critical density.

> [!note]
> The last member of the printed kinetic-energy equality omits test mass $\mechanical{m}$. It has energy-per-mass units, while the first two members have energy units. The resulting density relation is dimensionally consistent.

This can express energy conservation when cosmic matter is ‘created’ from vacuum or ‘nothing’. Total energy before and after creation must be zero, achieved by critical density at every epoch. It also fixes the gravitational potential to $\mechanical{\Phi}_u=-\mechanical{c}^2$. Then rest energy $\energy{E}=\mechanical{m}\mechanical{c}^2$ is balanced by equal negative gravitational potential energy, whose magnitude the source writes as $\energy{E}_g=\mechanical{m}\mechanical{c}^2$. This factual interpretation also guides exact numerical calculations in Cosmic Relativity, developed later.

This completes the survey of cosmic matter–energy components and distribution relevant to gravity. For subsequent topics, the slowly evolving Universe’s present state matters, since we study cosmic gravity’s effects on processes known since civilisation began. A few thousand years of physical knowledge is a tiny cosmic interval over which the expanding Universe’s gravitational properties can be treated as unchanged.

## Interpretation and checks

Setting the shell's specific kinetic plus gravitational energy to zero gives the familiar critical-density scale with the factor of three. Check that the shell radius cancels. The printed energy equality omits a mass factor on one side, so read it as a specific-energy relation or restore the factor consistently. Adding pressure or a cosmological constant changes the dynamics and the interpretation of the energy criterion.
