## Physical question and model

How is total cosmic matter inferred when much of it does not shine? Galaxy light traces only selected components. Combine dynamics, lensing, and cosmological observations under an explicit gravitational model and selection function.

## Lecture account

Matter with mass and gravity is relevant to relativity, beyond global markers for motion and time. This section reviews all gravitating components [17]. Familiar atoms and elementary particles are measured by direct ‘seeing’, mainly received light. Figure 8.7 gives a representative view. Matter distribution is nearly isotropic and homogeneous on scales below ten per cent of the Universe’s size, suggesting highly uniform gravitational potentials. Mean normal-matter density $\mechanical{\rho}_m$ is estimated at about $5\%$ of critical: $\mechanical{\rho}_m/\mechanical{\rho}_c\simeq0.05$. Familiar matter cannot make the Universe closed or even critically evolving.

Large telescopes traditionally reveal light-emitting matter. Their scope has expanded greatly through collection and detection across a broad energy range, from gamma rays to radio waves. Space instruments have also extended the depth and coverage of our cosmic view.

## Interpretation and checks

The reported percentage of baryonic matter is a fitted model parameter, not a direct count of everything in an image. A sky projection also mixes distance, brightness, and survey selection. Read the figure credit and colour convention before interpreting it as density. Ask whether a proposed local cosmic potential includes radiation, dark matter, and dark-energy stress consistently, rather than adding only familiar stars.
