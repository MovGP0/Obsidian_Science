## Reading the evidence

## Study route

Return to [[12.00 Cosmic Relativity and Quantum Dynamics]] after selecting one source. Identify its measured quantity, theoretical assumptions, and uncertainty or stated limitation. Distinguish a proposed interpretation from a dynamical model with independently checked probability, coupling, and measurement rules. Keep historical descriptions separate from later experimental results, and distinguish the author's own proposals from independent tests.
