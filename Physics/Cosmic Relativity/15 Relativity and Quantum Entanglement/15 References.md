## Reading the evidence

Use this list to trace the chapter's treatment of EPR completeness, entangled states, Bell inequalities, and action-wave locality claims. Bracketed numbers in the lecture account refer to the original numbered entries below. A reference records provenance; it does not by itself establish the conclusion for which the lecture cites it.
