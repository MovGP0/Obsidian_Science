> [!TLDR]

## Physical question and model

Are centrifugal and Coriolis terms new interactions or the same trajectory described in rotating coordinates? The standard calculation differentiates the coordinate transformation twice. The source instead attributes the resulting terms to cosmic matter currents.

## Lecture account

Inertial forces, proportional to inertia, are traditionally called pseudo-forces because no physical source was known. Cosmic Relativity offers a complete explanation of this four-century problem as real gravitational forces. Cosmic matter is the source; relative matter currents generate time-dependent relativistic potentials. Thus, centrifugal force, Coriolis force, and Newtonian inertial reaction all have gravitomagnetic origins.

## Interpretation and checks

The two accounts must first reproduce the same signs and velocity dependence for a specified lab rotation. To establish a new mechanism, give a further prediction for changed surrounding matter or boundary conditions. An accelerometer and a trajectory fit need not measure the same named force. Keep the inertial-frame real forces separate from terms introduced by the rotating description.
