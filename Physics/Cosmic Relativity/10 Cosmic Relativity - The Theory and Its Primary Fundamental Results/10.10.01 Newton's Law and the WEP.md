## Physical question and model

Are Newton's response law and the weak equivalence principle logically identical? A force-response relation defines inertial mass, while a gravity experiment compares that response with passive gravitational coupling. Their equality is extra empirical content unless a model supplies a common origin.

## Lecture account

Cosmic Relativity has many unifications because its single premise is the factual Universe’s gravity. Most fundamentally it connects relativity and dynamics. *Newton’s law and the WEP have the same physical content*, united through gravitational inertia.

Consequently, the validity of Newton’s law implies the WEP. Any particle obeying the law in *any force field* also obeys the WEP in gravity to the same precision. This matters for antiparticles and systems difficult to test by direct free fall, which needs many slowly moving particles. If such a system obeys Newton’s law in an electric field to a known precision, the author argues that WEP and UFF follow to that precision [16, 17]. In any force field, motion balances that force against relativistic cosmic gravity.

## Interpretation and checks

Consider electric acceleration as a contrast: the same response law applies while charge-to-mass ratios differ. Thus the motion law alone does not imply universal gravitational free fall. The author's common-coupling proposal can connect them conditionally. Show that connection with the separate parameters still visible before setting them equal.
