## Physical question and model

What is the proposed physical meaning of inertial mass? The author identifies it with a cosmic gravitational interaction-energy scale divided by light speed squared. This is a hypothesis relating a local response to a global background.

## Lecture account

Explaining the physical origin of the motion law also addresses inertia’s old puzzle. Inertial mass $\mechanical{m}\mechanical{\Phi}_u/\mechanical{c}^2$ is the cosmic gravitational potential energy’s mass equivalent. Approximately, $\mechanical{m}_i=\mechanical{G}\mechanical{M}\mechanical{m}/(\mechanical{R}\mechanical{c}^2)$, with total cosmic mass $\mechanical{M}$ and causal size $\mechanical{R}$.

This is presented as fulfilment of Mach’s suggestion [11, 12]. After centuries of mystery, the following sections claim solutions to every debated aspect of physical inertia. It also proves the WEP: the inertial-to-gravitational mass ratio is identical for all bodies, regardless of their properties. This immediately implies universal free fall, discussed after inertial forces.

Universal gravitational coupling to mass and every energy form gives inertia to anything physical. A slightly charged Universe would produce analogous charge-proportional inertia, but only for charged-particle dynamics. Its absence reflects exact proton–electron charge equality and neutron neutrality [6].

## Interpretation and checks

Check the dimensions: potential divided by light speed squared is dimensionless, so multiplying by mass gives mass. The numerical equality then depends on the potential estimate and normalisation. Vary density or the cutoff and state the predicted change in inertia. If every local reference changes together, identify an independently measurable ratio; an unobservable common rescaling is not a direct test.
