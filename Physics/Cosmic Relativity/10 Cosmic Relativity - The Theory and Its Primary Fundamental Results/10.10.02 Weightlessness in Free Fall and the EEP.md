## Physical question and model

What does weightlessness mean operationally? A freely falling accelerometer has no support force and therefore reads zero. In standard geometry this is free motion, while the source describes a cancellation of local and cosmic terms.

## Lecture account

Assuming UFF, different objects in a freely falling frame have no relative motion, giving kinematic weightlessness. Einstein used this to postulate local equivalence between gravity and acceleration, the EEP and GTR foundation. Here the author proposes to show that *EEP content follows from local balance between two real gravitational forces*.

All effects in Cosmic Relativity arise from gravitational interaction, with dynamics preceding kinematics. Free-fall weightlessness is not merely relative kinematics. The local field of a massive body is balanced by an induced relativistic cosmic field. We analyse this physically (Fig. 10.7).

The local field *does not disappear*. Potential $\mechanical{\phi}$ and gradient $\mechanical{\mathbf g}=-\nabla\mechanical{\phi}$ do not change because an observer falls. The falling frame has a relative cosmic matter current. Its potential $\mechanical{g}_{0i}=-\mechanical{v}/\mechanical{c}$ is time-dependent through acceleration $\mechanical{a}=\mechanical{g}$. The induced force is

$$
\mechanical{\mathbf F}_i=-\frac{\mechanical{m}\mechanical{\Phi}}{\mechanical{c}^2}\frac{\partial\mechanical{\mathbf v}}{\partial t}=-\mechanical{m}_i\mechanical{\mathbf g}. \tag{10.23}
$$

Its spatial derivatives vanish because the corresponding cosmic potential has no spatial variation or tidal derivatives. It exactly balances the local field sustaining free fall. Weightlessness and UFF thus follow from balancing local gravity against acceleration-induced cosmic gravity: *two real forces act, rather than the GTR’s ‘no force’ description*.

The two force types balance at a point:

$$
-\mechanical{m}\mechanical{\mathbf g}=-\mechanical{m}\nabla\mechanical{\phi}_l(\mechanical{x})=\mechanical{m}_i\frac{d\mechanical{\mathbf v}(\mechanical{x})}{dt}\equiv\mechanical{m}_i\mechanical{\mathbf a}. \tag{10.24}
$$

With $\mechanical{\Phi}_u/\mechanical{c}^2=1$, the source gives $\mechanical{\mathbf g}=-\mechanical{\mathbf a}$. There is no net force or acceleration inside the infinitesimal region. The local field has spatial gradients, but *the induced field remains homogeneous at every instant*, following the cosmic potential’s homogeneity. Cancellation and EEP are therefore strictly local.

> [!note]
> The source’s signs are retained. With its earlier definition $\mechanical{\mathbf g}=-\nabla\mechanical{\phi}$, the first equality in (10.24) would have the opposite potential-gradient sign. The frame’s falling acceleration and the compensating field must also be distinguished when comparing $\mechanical{a}=\mechanical{g}$ with the later $\mechanical{\mathbf g}=-\mechanical{\mathbf a}$.

If another force accelerates a frame without local gravity, nothing balances the induced cosmic field. It is experienced as a real force in that frame. This derives EEP quantitatively from cosmic gravity rather than postulating it. Einstein’s GTR treats accelerated-frame force as a coordinate-generated pseudo-force because equivalence is postulated. The author instead identifies a real cosmic gravitational force, homogeneous in space. Unlike ordinary mass-source gravity, it is not a potential gradient but the time derivative of a gravitomagnetic potential, $d\mechanical{g}_{0i}/dt$. This reveals an underlying physical inequivalence with important consequences for the GTR’s completeness.

The inductive cosmic gravitational force is similar to the electromagnetic induction force $\mechanical{F}=-\electric{e}\,d\magnetic{A}_i/(\mechanical{c}\,dt)$ when the vector potential $\magnetic{A}_i=\mechanical{v}_i\electric{\phi}/\mechanical{c}$ depends on time. This is why the source calls it a gravitomagnetic force.

GTR and other metric theories assume WEP as a fundamental postulate, mainly from experimental evidence. Here it follows from the proposed cosmic gravitomagnetic reactions that determine dynamics in any force field. The author therefore predicts no experimental WEP violation up to the homogeneity limit of the cosmic gravitational potential, which he places far beyond conceivable experimental sensitivity. UFF could still fail if an unknown composition-dependent interaction exists. Such experiments remain relevant to the search for additional short-range or intermediate-range forces.

## Interpretation and checks

The cancellation should not be counted twice by also inserting the same apparent force into an inertial-frame equation. Follow one body in one chart and identify the measured support force. Finite laboratories can still detect tidal relative acceleration. Predict a pair of falling bodies separated radially: they can drift apart even when each ideal accelerometer reads zero.
