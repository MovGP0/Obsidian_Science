> [!TLDR]
> The author proposes deriving laws of motion and equivalence principles from cosmic gravity rather than accepting them as independent postulates.

## Physical question and model

Can an observed Universe determine a theory without additional modelling assumptions? Observations constrain candidate laws, but many field equations and boundary conditions can describe matter-filled worlds. The question is which extra choices make the author's proposal predictive.

## Lecture account

Anchoring dynamics and relativity in real cosmic matter and gravity leaves no freedom to tune motion theory. Motion, evolution, and changing space-time intervals should follow directly from gravitational interaction with universal matter. Matter and gravity replace space, time, and matter as physical starting points; everything else should follow. Remarkably, it does.

Some foundational postulates are unavoidable in physics. They agree with known facts but are not directly experimentally established. When evidence supports theories with conflicting postulates, those postulates require scrutiny and possible tests. Dynamics begins with Newton’s prescribed laws; the Schrödinger equation is quantum systems’ postulated evolution law. Relativity has fundamental postulates and secondary principles. The Principle of Relativity is foundational. Invariant relative light speed distinguishes the STR. EEP, empirically grounded in UFF and the postulated weak equivalence of inertial and gravitational mass, underlies the GTR. General coordinate invariance additionally determines its mathematical form.

Strikingly, deeper physical reasons for postulates and dynamical laws are rarely demanded. Over time, they acquire the appearance of established truth, while their physical causes remain unknown. Inertial fictitious forces are a troubling example: centrifugal and Coriolis origins have been unexplained since Newton. More than 300 years and the GTR have not changed this.

Using the unique Universe’s gravity promises a substantial change. Motion laws, postulates, and equivalence principles must emerge from interaction with cosmic matter–energy. If so, this advances fundamental physics substantially. Cosmic Relativity promises precisely this reconstruction in a matter-filled Universe near critical density, without an additional postulate.

## Interpretation and checks

List the assumed source averaging, potential definition, reference congruence, coupling law, and rule for clock readings. Check where each enters the derivation. If removing one prevents the prediction, it is part of the model even if it is not called a postulate. A useful learning exercise is to construct two different matter-filled cosmological solutions and identify what their mere matter content does not determine.
