## Study route

Return to [[04.00 The Special Theory of Relativity and Its Empirical Foundations]] after selecting one source. Identify its measured quantity, theoretical assumptions, and uncertainty or stated limitation. Check the frame used to define each angle and each clock interval before comparing two apparently different formulas. Keep historical descriptions separate from later experimental results, and distinguish the author's own proposals from independent tests.
