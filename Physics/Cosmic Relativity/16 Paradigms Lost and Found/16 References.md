## Reading the evidence

Use this list to trace the chapter's treatment of the author’s synthesis of cosmic gravity, inertia, and absolute time. Bracketed numbers in the lecture account refer to the original numbered entries below. A reference records provenance; it does not by itself establish the conclusion for which the lecture cites it.

1. C. S. Unnikrishnan, Physics in the ‘once-given’ Universe, in *Recent Developments in Theoretical Physics*, ed. by S. Ghosh, G. Kar (World Scientific, Singapore, 2009), pp. 99–120.
2. C. S. Unnikrishnan, Matter, space, time, and motion: a unified gravitational perspective, in *Space, Time and the Limits of Human Understanding*, ed. by S. Wuppuluri, G. Ghirardi (Springer Frontiers Collection, 2016), pp. 167–183.
3. C. S. Unnikrishnan, A new gravitational paradigm for relativity and dynamics. J. Phys. (Conf. Ser.) **1466**, 012007 (2020).
4. C. S. Unnikrishnan, *Gravity's Time* (Jenny Stanford Publishing, Singapore, 2022).
