> [!TLDR]
> The author argues that general relativity assumes the equivalence principles, while Cosmic Relativity seeks to explain them through the gravity of matter and energy in the Universe.

## Physical question and model

Does explaining an equivalence principle require a deeper interaction model? General relativity uses universal local metric coupling and conservation to describe free motion. A proposed cosmic explanation must specify which assumption it replaces and how its coupling yields the observed universality.

## Lecture account

Despite the progress in experimentally establishing the UFF and the WEP with high precision, the fundamental reason, if there is one, why the UFF, WEP, and EEP operate in the physical world remains an open question. Within the current paradigm, one must accept their a priori status. The perfect validity of the WEP permits construction of the relativistic theory of gravitation, the General Theory of Relativity, as a geometric theory. However, *the GTR does not explain the WEP or the UFF*. A theory cannot derive and explain its postulates. It uses the idealised situation to construct a gravity theory, with dynamics interpreted as geodesic motion in four-dimensional ‘curved’ space-time, whose curvature is determined by massive matter sources. The GTR postulates that all objects move along geodesics independently of mass or composition.

This implies something profound: *a more fundamental understanding of the UFF and the WEP goes beyond the GTR, and beyond our present understanding of the relation between matter and gravity*. Cosmic Relativity accomplishes this special understanding naturally, relying on the factual gravity of matter–energy in the Universe as the physical reason for all features of dynamics and relativity. The UFF and the WEP then emerge transparently as natural physical consequences.

## Interpretation and checks

Show where the passive gravitational mass and inertial response enter separately, and only then demonstrate their cancellation. If the same universal factor is assumed in both, the equality has been encoded rather than independently derived. A decisive test would find a composition, self-energy, or environment dependence predicted differently by the two models. The existence of a deeper explanatory question does not negate successful tests of the existing model.
