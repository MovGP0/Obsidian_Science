## Study route

Return to [[11.00 The Crucial Experimental Tests of Cosmic Relativity]] after selecting one source. Identify its measured quantity, theoretical assumptions, and uncertainty or stated limitation. For each claimed decisive result, seek the complete apparatus model, original data, and treatment of synchronisation and systematic errors. Keep historical descriptions separate from later experimental results, and distinguish the author's own proposals from independent tests.
