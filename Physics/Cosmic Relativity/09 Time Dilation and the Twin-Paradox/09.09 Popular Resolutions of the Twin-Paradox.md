> [!TLDR]
> The author examines standard STR resolutions after interpreting the clock experiments as evidence for an absolute reference frame.

## Physical question and model

What must a resolution of the twin problem actually compute? It must assign proper time to both specified worldlines and reproduce the same reunion readings in another chart. A verbal claim that one twin accelerated is not a substitute for that calculation.

## Lecture account

Having interpreted motional-clock experiments as contradicting the STR and supporting an absolute-frame theory, we now examine standard resolutions of the twin-clock problem entirely within the STR.

## Interpretation and checks

A useful test is to construct three descriptions of the same setup: a home inertial chart, a boosted inertial chart, and coordinates tied to the traveller. The first two use flat inertial metrics; the third needs the full noninertial metric. Equal physical answers are expected. The author's criticisms should be evaluated against complete calculations rather than only a short textbook slogan.
