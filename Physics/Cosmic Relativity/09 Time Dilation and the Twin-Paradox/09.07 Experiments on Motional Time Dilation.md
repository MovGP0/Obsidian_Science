> [!TLDR]
> Time-dilation experiments compare independent clocks and must distinguish changes in either clock’s rate from the measured difference between them.

## Physical question and model

What does a time-dilation experiment measure besides a theoretical clock rate? Identify preparation, reference clock, readout, and the relevant proper-time interval. Separate particle survival, spectroscopy, and reunion-clock comparisons.

## Lecture account

Textbooks often discuss the twin paradox without experimental knowledge. Even widely used texts make statements inconsistent with time-dilation observations. Attention to experiments is necessary before claiming an STR resolution.

Relative time measurements always compare two different, independently moving clocks. A difference can arise from either clock changing rate or their rates being unequal. Since clocks are independent, each rate should depend on its own motion rather than other clocks’ motion. The two readings can then be compared directly together without signal exchange. Meaningful comparison requires initial synchronisation in proximity.

## Interpretation and checks

Each method has different calibration and environmental uncertainties. A result agreeing with a relativistic rate does not automatically select its preferred causal interpretation. Conversely, a claimed discrepancy must compare the complete prediction with the same event selection. Require a residual not explained by velocity distribution, gravity, field shifts, or signal transfer before attributing it to cosmic motion.
