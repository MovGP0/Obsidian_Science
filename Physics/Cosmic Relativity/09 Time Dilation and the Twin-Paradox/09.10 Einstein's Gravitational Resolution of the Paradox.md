## Physical question and model

Why can an accelerating-frame account resemble a gravitational redshift calculation? Noninertial coordinates can have position-dependent clock rates in flat spacetime. This does not require a new physical source of curvature.

## Lecture account

STR textbooks confidently claim a complete resolution through the observers’ unequal space-time world-line intervals. This claim is crucial because the paradox seriously criticised the STR. Einstein did not respond for several years. At *Naturwissenschaften* editor Arnold Berliner’s insistence, he published a 1918 resolution [4], rejecting STR-only resolutions. It takes the form of dialogue between a critic and a relativist. The critic accuses the relativist of long evasion. The relativist agrees that observer-symmetric STR cannot resolve the paradox and expresses relief at a GTR resolution. The critic then agrees that the relativist has “extricated skilfully”, although questions remain. The whole dialogue reflects Einstein’s reasoning.

His answer combines STR motional dilation with GTR gravitational dilation from equivalent pseudo-gravitational fields during brief acceleration. Both theories were needed for the paradox of the earlier one. Acceleration is crucial, but the resolution goes beyond the STR to gravity. *Einstein’s solution confirms symmetric STR time dilation and the impossibility of an STR-only resolution.*

> [!note]
> Einstein’s twin-paradox resolution admitted that the STR alone was insufficient. It used STR motional dilation and GTR gravitational dilation, adapted through the equivalence principle to an accelerating frame.

Before the calculation, note that this necessarily requires one or both frames to reverse through acceleration and experience an equivalent field. The author regards it as incorrect because an experiment and paradox can be posed without acceleration. A clock switched off during acceleration would also avoid any field effect on its rate; Einstein’s ‘critic’ did not make this objection. *Careful study is still needed to see why even this GTR resolution cannot solve the problem* (Fig. 9.5).

> [!note]
> The middle-panel label compares a dimensionless potential difference with a duration. A gravitational time difference requires multiplication by the acceleration interval, as in the right panel’s $\mechanical{a}t\mechanical{L}/\mechanical{c}^2$. The printed middle-panel relation is retained above.

Einstein began with symmetric STR dilation: A calls B younger by factor $\mechanical{\gamma}=1/\sqrt{1-\mechanical{v}^2/\mechanical{c}^2}$ and B says the same about A. At speeds much below light, B’s dilation relative to A is $\delta\mechanical{T}\simeq-\mechanical{T}\mechanical{v}^2/(2\mechanical{c}^2)=-(2\mechanical{L}/\mechanical{v})\mechanical{v}^2/(2\mechanical{c}^2)$, with separation $\mechanical{L}$ after travelling for $\mechanical{T}/2$ at $\mechanical{v}$. A’s motional dilation relative to B is identical: $\delta\mechanical{T}\simeq-(2\mechanical{L}/\mechanical{v})\mechanical{v}^2/(2\mechanical{c}^2)$.

To compare clocks, A and B must reunite. When B fires its rocket, it decelerates at $\mechanical{a}$. With $\mechanical{a}t=2\mechanical{v}$, relative velocity reverses from $\mechanical{v}$ to $-\mechanical{v}$ in time $t$. B’s inertial force is indistinguishable from a uniform field $\mechanical{g}=-\mechanical{a}$ for that duration. The equivalence principle and GTR then predict relative gravitational dilation, A ageing more, between the separated clocks:

$$
\frac{\Delta t}{t}=-\frac{\mechanical{g}\mechanical{L}}{\mechanical{c}^2}=\frac{\mechanical{a}\mechanical{L}}{\mechanical{c}^2}=\frac{2\mechanical{v}\mechanical{L}}{t\mechanical{c}^2}.
$$

Thus, $\Delta t=2\mechanical{v}\mechanical{L}/\mechanical{c}^2$ is independent of acceleration duration. It is twice the motional dilation and has the opposite sign: accelerated B runs slower than A. The sum is $\delta\mechanical{T}+\Delta t=\mechanical{v}\mechanical{L}/\mechanical{c}^2=+\mechanical{T}\mechanical{v}^2/(2\mechanical{c}^2)$. At reunion A is older, and both twins agree. This is Einstein’s resolution [4, 17].

Yet acceleration asymmetry cannot be the real answer. Comparison can be arranged without acceleration, or a clock can be frozen during the short acceleration interval so even a gravitational field cannot alter its reading. A distant clock cannot change because a trajectory reverses far away. Simultaneity and Doppler-asymmetry resolutions also fail. *There is no STR resolution, because its time dilation is symmetric between relatively moving observers.*

Some accounts use the simultaneity change when velocity reverses; others use acceleration as the key physical difference. Both occur together, yet their predicted dilation is not doubled. This shows that claimed resolutions lack substance. A complete quantitative calculation in each observer’s rest frame should remove residual doubts.

*Experimentally observed motional time dilation comes from real motion in an absolute frame, rather than mere relative motion.* Hypothetical clock comparisons suggest this, Hafele–Keating data verify it in the author’s analysis, and the simultaneity–light-hypothesis inconsistency also supports it.

What absolute frame universally determines light propagation and clock dilation? Our survey points to the Universe’s mean matter frame. The next chapter establishes it. *The final resolution is that clock dilation physically depends on velocity in the matter-filled Universe’s unique frame, with factor $\sqrt{1-\mechanical{v}^2(t)/\mechanical{c}^2}$.* Subtracting clock times then gives relative dilation.

Millions of sentences about the paradox and its resolutions are irrelevant except for sustaining discussion, given this single-sentence answer. My matter-filled-Universe resolution applies to every motional-dilation situation [17].

This is neither an ether nor Newton’s empty absolute space, nor merely fixed stars and CMBR as superficial motion markers. The crucial cause is universal matter and energy’s enormous gravity, which determines light propagation and clock rates. Motional dilation is gravitational dilation from different potentials experienced by a cosmic-rest clock and a moving clock. The absolute dynamical frame is the matter-filled Universe, whose gravity causes motional relativistic effects and known dynamics. This is Cosmic Relativity’s essence, developed from the next chapter.

## Interpretation and checks

The local equivalence argument is a useful approximation during a finite turnaround, with separation and acceleration specified. Its integrated effect must agree with an inertial proper-time calculation. A formally vanishing turnaround duration while holding a velocity change fixed is a limit requiring care; dropping the associated change of distant simultaneity gives a wrong answer. Historical language about a gravitational resolution should not be read as showing that flat-spacetime worldline methods are inconsistent.
