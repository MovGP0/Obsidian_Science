> [!TLDR]
> The author seeks a common microscopic mechanism because integer and fractional Hall states alternate smoothly in the same sample.

## Physical question and model

When does a desire for unification justify changing a model? Different approximations can apply to different phases of one system. A unified account should identify the common Hamiltonian and explain the parameter ranges in which each approximation works.

## Lecture account

Two reasons motivate the proposed theory. First, IQHE and FQHE alternate in one sample as field or density changes. The author argues that this favours universal underlying physics over repeated changes between independent-particle and strongly correlated mechanisms.

Second, the observed cosmic matter has a gravitational potential that, in his account, must affect electrons [1, 2]. He claims that including it changes Landau-level degeneracy without extra assumptions about carriers or energy levels [13]. This change is then used to obtain both integer and fractional Hall plateaux.

See [[14.06.01 Summary of the Reasons for a New Theory]] and [[14.07 New Physics Input to the QHE - Cosmic Gravity]]. References: [[14 References]].

## Interpretation and checks

Alternation between integer and fractional plateaux does not by itself refute an interaction-based theory. The relevant question is whether one set of physical inputs predicts the transitions and measured responses. Set a fair comparison: specify parameters before fitting the plateau sequence and compare gaps, density dependence, and transport. A simpler interpretation is useful only if it retains or improves this predictive content.
