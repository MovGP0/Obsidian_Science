## Study route

Return to [[07.00 Einstein's General Theory of Relativity]] after selecting one source. Identify its measured quantity, theoretical assumptions, and uncertainty or stated limitation. For an alleged coordinate contradiction, compare invariant readings along the same worldlines before comparing component formulas. Keep historical descriptions separate from later experimental results, and distinguish the author's own proposals from independent tests.
