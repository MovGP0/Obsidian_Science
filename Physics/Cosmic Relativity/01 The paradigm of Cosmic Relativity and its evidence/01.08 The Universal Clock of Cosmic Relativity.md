> [!TLDR]
> The source treats cosmic expansion and the falling microwave background temperature as a universal clock.

## Physical question and model

Can cosmic background temperature label time? In a homogeneous expanding model, temperature decreases monotonically under suitable thermal-history assumptions. This allows it to label cosmological epochs. A local thermometer, however, also responds to motion, anisotropy, foreground radiation, and gravitational conditions.

## Lecture account

Cosmic Relativity has the preferred frame of the matter-filled Universe to measure real motion, as distinct from mere kinematic relative motion in the STR. Therefore, it is obvious that no observer can claim a state of rest while moving through the Universe; any such claim would be inconsistent with what is observed in the frame, like the dipole anisotropy of the CMBR. What is even more significant is the fact that the slowly expanding evolution of the Universe provides us with an absolute time. Therefore, absolute space and time are firmly and decisively back in physics with full consistency. The CMBR acts as the physically manifest proxy of this profound fact. The equivalent temperature of the CMBR in the Universe is the same everywhere to a great accuracy (better than a part in a million), and it is slowly decreasing, with its present value at $2.72548\pm0.00057\,\unit{K}$ (above absolute zero, about $-270.425\,\unit{^{\circ}C}$). The monotonic decrease of the temperature makes the CMBR equivalent to a clock that keeps a globally synchronised time everywhere in the Universe. In fact, the ‘time’ $t$ in the FLRW line element

$$
ds^2=-\mechanical{c}^2dt^2+a^2(t)\left(\frac{1}{1-k\mechanical{r}^2}d\mechanical{r}^2+\mechanical{r}^2d\mechanical{\theta}^2+\mechanical{r}^2\sin^2\mechanical{\theta}\,d\mechanical{\phi}^2\right) \tag{1.22}
$$

is this universal absolute time with its metric $g_{00}=-\mechanical{c}^2$ that is independent of the location or the epoch. The existence of such a universal clock that is synchronised everywhere establishes the notion of universal simultaneity, again discrediting the STR, by exposing its inconsistency with the factual gravitational Universe.

## Interpretation and checks

Distinguish cosmic time, the proper time of ideal comoving observers, from an operational synchronisation of arbitrary separated clocks. A local temperature measurement does not by itself transmit a simultaneity relation across space. Check a contracting or reheated phase: temperature may cease to be a unique global label. A practical comparison would require a thermal-history model and an error budget, not only a quoted mean CMB temperature.
