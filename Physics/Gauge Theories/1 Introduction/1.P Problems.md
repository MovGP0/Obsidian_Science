### 1.1

Consider bound states composed of a $b$-quark and a $b$-antiquark.

**(a)** Show that a bound state with orbital angular momentum $L$ must have quantum numbers

$$
C=(-1)^{L+s};\qquad P=(-1)^{L+1},
$$

<!-- PDF page 33; printed page 19 -->

where $s$ is the spin of the composite system.

**(b)** Allowing for both orbital and radial excitations, construct a schematic mass spectrum of ($b\bar b$) bound states. Label each state with its quantum numbers $J^{PC}$.

### 1.2

Consider bound states composed of color-triplet scalars (denoted $\sigma$) and their antiparticles.

**(a)** Show that a ($\sigma\bar\sigma$) bound state with angular momentum $L$ (i.e., an orbital excitation) must have quantum numbers

$$
C=(-1)^L;\qquad P=(-1)^L.
$$

**(b)** Allowing for both orbital and radial excitations, construct a schematic mass spectrum of ($\sigma\bar\sigma$) bound states. Label each state with its quantum numbers $J^{PC}$. How does the spectrum differ from the quarkonium spectrum constructed in problem 1.1?

### 1.4

Using the Feynman rules given in appendix B.5, compute the differential cross section $d\sigma/d\Omega$ and the total (integrated) cross section $\sigma=\int d\Omega(d\sigma/d\Omega)$ for the reaction $e^+e^-\to\sigma^+\sigma^-$, where $\sigma^\pm$ is a charged scalar particle. Work in the center-of-momentum frame and in the high-energy limit (where all the masses may be neglected). Assume that the colliding beams are unpolarized.

### 1.7

Assume that the charged weak current has the left-handed form of equation (1.2.2) and that the interaction Hamiltonian is of the “current–current” form, $\energy{\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{W}}}\sim JJ^\dagger+J^\dagger J$.

**(a)** Enumerate the kinds of interactions (i.e., terms) in the Hamiltonian that may occur in a world composed of the electron and muon doublets.

**(b)** List the leptonic processes such as $\nu_\mu e\to\nu_\mu e$ that are consistent with the known selection rules but do not appear in the charged-current Hamiltonian.

### 1.8

Derive the connection between $|\Psi(0)|^2$ and the leptonic decay rate of a ($q\bar q$) vector meson. It is convenient to proceed by the following steps:

**(a)** Compute the spin-averaged cross section for the reaction $q\bar q\to e^+e^-$. Show that it is

$$
\sigma=\frac{\pi\alpha^2\electric{e_q}^2}{12\energy{E}^2}\cdot\frac{\beta_e}{\beta_q}(3-\beta_e^2)(3-\beta_q^2),
$$

where $\energy{E}$ is the c.m. energy of a quark and $\beta_i$ is the speed of particle $i$.

**(b)** The annihilation rate in a $^3\mathrm{S}_1$ vector meson is the density × relative velocity × 4/3 (to undo the spin average) × the cross section, or

$$
\Gamma=|\Psi(0)|^2\times2\beta_q\times\frac43\times\sigma.
$$

**(c)** How is the result modified if the vector-meson wave function is

$$
|V^0\rangle=\sum_i c_i|q_i\bar q_i\rangle?
$$

**(d)** Now neglect the lepton mass and the quark binding energy and assume that the quarks move nonrelativistically. Show that

$$
\Gamma(V^0\to e^+e^-)=\frac{16\pi\alpha^2}{3\mechanical{M_V}^2}|\Psi(0)|^2\left(\sum_i c_i\electric{e_i}\right)^2.
$$

<!-- PDF page 35; printed page 21; text before For Further Reading -->

**(e)** How is this result modified if quarks come in $N_c$ colors and hadrons are color singlets? [This result is due to R. Van Royen and V. F. Weisskopf, *Nuovo Cim.* **50**, 617 (1967); *Nuovo Cim.* **51**, 583 (1967); and to H. Pietschmann and W. Thirring, *Phys. Lett.* **21**, 713 (1966).]

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $J,L,s$ | $1$ | Angular-momentum quantum numbers | Total, orbital, and spin angular momentum in units of ℏ. |
| $P,C$ | $1$ | Parity and charge conjugation | The state eigenvalues used in Problems 1.1 and 1.2. |
| $\sigma$ | $\unit{m^2}$ | Cross section | The reaction cross section; the scalar-particle symbol σ has a separate meaning. |
| $\Omega$ | $1$ | Solid angle | The angular integration variable. |
| $g_\tau$ | $1$ | Tau gyromagnetic factor | The factor to be measured in Problem 1.6. |
| $\energy{\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{W}}}$ | — | Weak interaction Hamiltonian | The current–current Hamiltonian; units depend on its field convention. |
| $\Psi(0)$ | $\unit{m^{-3/2}}$ | Bound-state wavefunction | The normalized spatial wavefunction at zero quark separation. |
| $\energy{E}$ | $\unit{GeV}$ | Quark center-of-mass energy | The quark energy in the annihilation cross section. |
| $\beta_e,\beta_q$ | $1$ | Particle speed fractions | Electron and quark speeds in the source’s relativistic convention. |
| $\electric{e_q},\electric{e_i}$ | $1$ | Charge quantum numbers | The quark charges in units of the elementary charge. |
| $\alpha$ | $1$ | Fine structure constant | The electromagnetic coupling constant. |
| $\Gamma$ | $\unit{GeV}$ | Decay width | The vector-meson annihilation rate in energy units. |
| $\mechanical{M_V}$ | $\unit{GeV}$ | Vector-meson mass-energy | The meson mass in the decay-width formula. |
| $N_c$ | $1$ | Number of colors | The number of quark colors. |
