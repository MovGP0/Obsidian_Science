## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{c}$ | $\unit{\mathrm{cm}\,\mathrm{s}^{-1}}$ | Speed of light | The speed of light in vacuum. |
| $\mechanical{\hbar}$ | $\unit{\mathrm{MeV}\,\mathrm{s}}$ | Reduced Planck constant | The action constant used in quantum mechanics. |
| $\mechanical{\hbar}\mechanical{c}$ | $\unit{\mathrm{MeV}\,\mathrm{cm}}$ | Energy–length conversion | The product that relates inverse length to energy. |
| $(\mechanical{\hbar}\mechanical{c})^2$ | $\unit{\mathrm{GeV}^{2}\,\mathrm{mb}}$ | Energy–area conversion | The squared product used in cross-section conversions. |

| $G_F$ | $\unit{\mathrm{GeV}^{-2}}$ | Fermi constant | The weak interaction coupling constant. |
| $G_F^2$ | $\unit{\mathrm{cm}^{2}\,\mathrm{GeV}^{-2}}$ | Squared Fermi constant | The squared coupling in the source’s area conversion. |
| $\mechanical{G}_N$ | $\unit{\mathrm{cm}^{3}\,\mathrm{g}^{-1}\,\mathrm{s}^{-2}}$ | Gravitational constant | The coupling in Newtonian gravitation. |
| $\mechanical{M}_{\mathrm{Pl}}$ | $\unit{\mathrm{kg}}$ | Planck mass | The mass defined by the reduced Planck constant, light speed, and gravitational constant. |
