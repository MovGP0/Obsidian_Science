## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\electric{Q_i},\electric{e_i}$ | $1$ | Electric charge number | Two notations for fermion charges in units of the proton charge. |
| $\mechanical{Q}^2$ | $\unit{GeV}^2$ | Squared momentum-transfer scale | The four-momentum-transfer variable and the scattering scale quoted in reference 22. |
| $\mechanical{\sigma}_{\mathrm{S}},\mechanical{\sigma}_{\mathrm{T}}$ | $\unit{GeV}^{-2}$ | Absorption cross sections | Scalar and transverse cross sections in natural units; their ratio is dimensionless. |
| $\energy{\nu}$ | $\unit{GeV}$ | Energy transfer | The energy variable quoted for neutrino–nucleon scattering in reference 22. |
| $\mathcal{F}_2^{en}$ | $1$ | Neutron structure function | The electron–neutron structure function discussed in reference 23. |
| $\energy{s}$ | $\unit{GeV}^2$ | Squared c.m. energy | The squared collision energy in collider measurements and searches. |
| $V_{ud},V_{us},V_{tb}$ | $1$ | Quark-mixing elements | Flavor-transition parameters and the bound quoted in reference 65. |
| $\rho$ | $1$ | Electroweak rho parameter | The parameter used to constrain heavy-fermion masses in reference 74. |
| $z$ | $1$ | Cosmological redshift | The redshift designation in the High-$z$ Supernova Team name. |
