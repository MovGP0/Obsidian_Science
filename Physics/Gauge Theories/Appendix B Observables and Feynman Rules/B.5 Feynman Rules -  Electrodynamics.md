> [!TLDR]
> Spinor electrodynamics has a one-photon vertex; scalar electrodynamics has both one-photon and two-photon vertices.

<!-- PDF page 467; printed page 453 -->

The Feynman rules for spinor and scalar electrodynamics are needed for several illustrative examples and problems. Complete rules and their derivations are given in many places; only the elementary vertices deduced in section 3.6 are required here.

For spinor electrodynamics of a fermion with charge $\electric{q}$, the only interaction is the emission or absorption of a photon,

![[gauge-theories-B-5-fermion-photon-vertex.svg|The fermion–photon vertex, with initial and final momenta p and p′.]]

$$
-i\electric{q}\gamma_\mu
$$

In the case of a spinless boson with charge $\electric{q}$, the one- and two-photon vertices are

![[gauge-theories-B-5-scalar-one-photon-vertex.svg|The one-photon scalar vertex.]]

$$
-i\electric{q}(\mechanical{p}+\mechanical{p}')_\mu
$$

![[gauge-theories-B-5-scalar-two-photon-vertex.svg|The two-photon scalar vertex.]]

$$
2i\electric{q}^{\,2}g_{\mu\nu}.
$$

The needed Feynman rules for other theories are given in the text where the theories are introduced.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\electric{q}$ | $1$ | Electric charge | The charged particle coupling, in natural units. |
| $\mechanical{p},\mechanical{p}'$ | $\unit{\mathrm{GeV}}$ | Particle momentum | The incoming and outgoing particle four-momenta. |
