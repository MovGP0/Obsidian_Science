## How to use this collection

This collection has one document for each of the video’s 25 timestamp chapters: 24 teaching chapters and one credits/further-study note. Use the chapter links below to read in sequence or to work beside the video. The previous and next links in each note preserve the sequence.

These are original explanatory notes for self-study. The retrieved English automatic captions supplied the topic map and helped identify the arguments to develop. The collection adds independently written derivations, numerical examples, exercises, and solutions. It does not reproduce the full spoken text or reconstruct every slide and aside. Where a video chapter is short or primarily reflective, the corresponding note develops the underlying concept rather than inventing missing source content.

For each technical note, reproduce the equations on paper before reading the next step. Do the self-check questions before opening their answers. Use the cumulative problems in chapter 24 to check whether the earlier calculations fit together.

The earlier [[Electromagnetism as a Fifth Dimension - Kaluza-Klein]] remains available as a short overview. It is not the main study text.

## Notation and units

The collection uses a mostly-plus metric, with one negative time direction and four positive spatial directions. The compact direction is spacelike. The circular coordinate is length-valued unless a note explicitly introduces an angle. Its coordinate period is one circumference. The reference radius is separate from the radion, which multiplies the physical circle size.

Natural units set the speed of light and reduced Planck constant to one. Energy, momentum, and mass can then all be measured in GeV; length and time use inverse GeV. Explicit SI conversions restore the constants. Charge uses Heaviside–Lorentz normalization, so the fine-structure constant equals elementary charge squared divided by four pi.

The potential is normalized so that the Maxwell Lagrangian has its conventional coefficient of minus one quarter. A plus sign in the shifted circular differential and a plus sign in the circular-coordinate redefinition lead to a minus gradient in the potential transformation. The matter phase and covariant derivative use the matching signs. Do not mix them with a source that chooses the opposite convention.

Greek indices refer to ordinary spacetime. Capital Latin indices refer to five-dimensional coordinates. Lowercase Latin indices in the curvature chapter refer to orthonormal frames. Repeated indices are summed. Indices, operators, temporary matrix blocks, gauge parameters, and other mathematical helpers remain uncolored. Physical quantities use the semantic macros from the vault’s preamble.

The fixed-radius action calculation is a restricted calculation. The scalar equation of the full vacuum theory is restored in chapter 23. A cylinder-independent background and a Fourier expansion of a test matter field are also distinct assumptions. The latter does not include unrestricted backreaction.

## Scope of the study text

The notes develop the elementary Abelian circle construction. They do not derive general non-Abelian compactifications, a complete chiral fermion model, a radion-stabilization potential, or Witten’s decay solution. These subjects require additional assumptions and calculations. A mathematical relationship between gauge theory and higher-dimensional geometry is not by itself evidence that the simplest five-dimensional model describes nature.
