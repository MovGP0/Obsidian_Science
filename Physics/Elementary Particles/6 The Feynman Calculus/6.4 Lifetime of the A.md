> [!TLDR]
> The lowest-order ABC decay amplitude equals the toy coupling and gives the lifetime of particle A.

<!-- PDF page 212; printed page 204 -->

![[elementary-particles-fig-6-6-lowest-order-abc-decay.svg|**Fig. 6.6** Lowest-order contribution to A → B + C.]]

The simplest possible diagram, representing the lowest-order contribution to $A\to B+C$, has no internal lines at all (Fig. 6.6). There is one vertex, at which we pick up a factor of $-ig$ (rule 2) and a delta function

$$
(2\pi)^{4}\delta^{4}(\mechanical{p_1}-\mechanical{p_2}-\mechanical{p_3})
$$

(rule 4), which we promptly discard (rule 6), obtaining $-i\signal{\mathcal M}=-ig$, or

$$
\signal{\mathcal M}=g.
\tag{6.45}
$$

This is the *amplitude* (to lowest order); the decay rate is found by plugging $\signal{\mathcal M}$ into equation (6.32):

$$
\mechanical{\Gamma}=\frac{g^{2}\lvert\mechanical{\mathbf p}\rvert}
{8\pi\energy{\hbar}\mechanical{m_A}^{2}\mechanical{c}}
\tag{6.46}
$$

where $\lvert\mechanical{\mathbf p}\rvert$ (the magnitude of either outgoing momentum) is

$$
\lvert\mechanical{\mathbf p}\rvert=\frac{\mechanical{c}}{2\mechanical{m_A}}
\sqrt{\mechanical{m_A}^{4}+\mechanical{m_B}^{4}+\mechanical{m_C}^{4}
-2\mechanical{m_A}^{2}\mechanical{m_B}^{2}
-2\mechanical{m_A}^{2}\mechanical{m_C}^{2}
-2\mechanical{m_B}^{2}\mechanical{m_C}^{2}}.
\tag{6.47}
$$

The lifetime of the $A$, then, is

$$
\mechanical{\tau}=\frac{1}{\mechanical{\Gamma}}
=\frac{8\pi\energy{\hbar}\mechanical{m_A}^{2}\mechanical{c}}
{g^{2}\lvert\mechanical{\mathbf p}\rvert}.
\tag{6.48}
$$

You should check for yourself that $\mechanical{\tau}$ comes out with the correct units.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{\mathcal M}$ | $\unit{\mathrm{g\,cm\,s^{-1}}}$ | Decay amplitude | The lowest-order amplitude for A to decay into B and C. |
| $g$ | $\unit{\mathrm{g\,cm\,s^{-1}}}$ | Toy coupling constant | The interaction strength of the ABC vertex. |
| $\mechanical{p_1},\mechanical{p_2},\mechanical{p_3}$ | $\unit{\mathrm{g\,cm\,s^{-1}}}$ | Four-momenta | The incoming A and outgoing B and C momenta. |
| $\mechanical{\mathbf p}$ | $\unit{\mathrm{g\,cm\,s^{-1}}}$ | Outgoing three-momentum | The momentum of either final particle in the A rest frame. |
| $\mechanical{m_A},\mechanical{m_B},\mechanical{m_C}$ | $\unit{\mathrm{g}}$ | Particle masses | The masses in the decay momentum formula. |
| $\energy{\hbar}$ | $\unit{\mathrm{erg\,s}}$ | Reduced Planck constant | The constant in the decay rate. |
| $\mechanical{c}$ | $\unit{\mathrm{cm\,s^{-1}}}$ | Speed of light | The speed in the momentum and rate formulas. |
| $\mechanical{\Gamma}$ | $\unit{\mathrm{s^{-1}}}$ | Decay rate | The decay probability per unit time. |
| $\mechanical{\tau}$ | $\unit{\mathrm{s}}$ | Lifetime | The inverse decay rate. |
