## Problem 8.4

Prove equation (8.16). [*Hint:* First show that $\mechanical{q_\mu}L^{\mu\nu}=0$. Then argue that we *may as well* take $K^{\mu\nu}$ such that $\mechanical{q_\mu}K^{\mu\nu}=0$, in the sense that any term in $K^{\mu\nu}$ that does *not* obey $\mechanical{q_\mu}K^{\mu\nu}=0$ will contribute nothing to $L^{\mu\nu}K_{\mu\nu}$.] *Comment:* Equation (8.16) actually follows more simply and generally from charge conservation at the proton vertex, but I have not developed the formalism here to make this argument (see Halzen and Martin, ref. 2, Sects. 8.2 and 8.3).

[One way to proceed is as follows. Take $\mechanical{q^\mu}=(0,0,0,\mechanical{q})$; then

$$
\mechanical{q_\mu}L^{\mu\nu}=0\ \Rightarrow\
L^{\mu\nu}=
\begin{pmatrix}
\boxed{\phantom{\begin{matrix}0&0&0\\0&0&0\\0&0&0\end{matrix}}}
&\begin{matrix}0\\0\\0\end{matrix}\\
\begin{matrix}0&0&0\end{matrix}&0
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

So

$$
L^{\mu\nu}K_{\mu\nu}=
\begin{pmatrix}
\boxed{\phantom{\begin{matrix}0&0&0\\0&0&0\\0&0&0\end{matrix}}}
&\begin{matrix}0\\0\\0\end{matrix}\\
\begin{matrix}0&0&0\end{matrix}&0
\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}
\boxed{\phantom{\begin{matrix}0&0&0\\0&0&0\\0&0&0\end{matrix}}}
&\begin{matrix}\times\\\times\\\times\end{matrix}\\
\begin{matrix}\times&\times&\times\end{matrix}&\times
\end{pmatrix},
$$

and

$$
\begin{matrix}
&&&\times\\
&&&\times\\
&&&\times\\
\times&\times&\times&\times
\end{matrix}
\quad\text{may as well be zero.}
$$

]

## Problem 8.14

Suppose the proton were a “Dirac” charge; would the structure functions scale? If so, what are $F_1(x)$ and $F_2(x)$ in this case? Is the Callan–Gross relation satisfied?

[*Answer:* $F_1(x)=\frac12\delta(x-1)$; $F_2(x)=\delta(x-1)$; yes.]
