| Physical constant | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Speed of light | $\mechanical{c}=2.9979\times10^8\,\unit{\mathrm{m/s}}$ |
| Planck's constant | $\energy{\hbar}=\energy{h}/2\pi=1.0546\times10^{-34}\,\unit{\mathrm{J\,s}}=6.5822\times10^{-22}\,\unit{\mathrm{MeV\,s}}$ |
| Electron charge (magnitude) | $\electric{e}=1.6022\times10^{-19}\,\unit{\mathrm{C}}=4.8032\times10^{-10}\,\unit{\mathrm{esu}}$ |
| Electron mass | $\mechanical{m}_e=0.511003\,\unit{\mathrm{MeV}}/\mechanical{c}^2=9.10953\times10^{-31}\,\unit{\mathrm{kg}}$ |
| Proton mass | $\mechanical{m}_p=938.280\,\unit{\mathrm{MeV}}/\mechanical{c}^2=1.67265\times10^{-27}\,\unit{\mathrm{kg}}$ |
| Fine structure constant | $\electric{\alpha}=\electric{e}^2/\energy{\hbar}\mechanical{c}=1/137.036$ |
| Classical electron radius | $\mechanical{r}_e=\electric{e}^2/\mechanical{m}_e\mechanical{c}^2=2.8179\times10^{-15}\,\unit{\mathrm{m}}$ |
| Electron compton wavelength | $\signal{\lambda}_e=\energy{h}/\mechanical{m}_e\mechanical{c}=2.4263\times10^{-12}\,\unit{\mathrm{m}}$ |
| Bohr radius | $\mechanical{a}=\energy{\hbar}^2/\electric{e}^2\mechanical{m}_e=5.29177\times10^{-11}\,\unit{\mathrm{m}}$ |
| Bohr energies | $\energy{E}_n=-\mechanical{m}_e\electric{e}^4/(2\energy{\hbar}^2n^2)=-\electric{\alpha}^2\mechanical{m}_e\mechanical{c}^2/2n^2=-13.6\,\unit{\mathrm{eV}}/n^2$ |

$$
\begin{aligned}

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{c}$ | $\unit{\mathrm{m/s}}$ | Speed of light | The speed used in the energy and mass conversion. |
| $\energy{\hbar},\energy{h}$ | $\unit{\mathrm{J\,s}}$ | Planck constants | The reduced and unreduced quantum constants. |
| $\electric{e}$ | $\unit{\mathrm{C}}$ | Elementary charge | The magnitude of the electron charge. |
| $\mechanical{m}_e$ | $\unit{\mathrm{kg}}$ | Electron mass | The mass of the electron. |
| $\mechanical{m}_p$ | $\unit{\mathrm{kg}}$ | Proton mass | The mass of the proton. |
| $\electric{\alpha}$ | $1$ | Fine-structure constant | The electromagnetic coupling strength in Gaussian units. |
| $\mechanical{r}_e$ | $\unit{\mathrm{m}}$ | Classical electron radius | The length defined from electron charge and rest energy. |
| $\signal{\lambda}_e$ | $\unit{\mathrm{m}}$ | Electron Compton wavelength | The wavelength defined from Planck's constant and electron mass. |
| $\mechanical{a}$ | $\unit{\mathrm{m}}$ | Bohr radius | The length scale of the hydrogen atom. |
| $\energy{E}_n$ | $\unit{\mathrm{eV}}$ | Bohr energy | The bound-state energy at principal quantum number n. |
