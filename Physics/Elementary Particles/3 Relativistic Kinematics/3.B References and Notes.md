> [!TLDR]
> The source lists textbooks for special relativity and relativistic kinematics.

<!-- PDF page 107; printed page 99 -->

1. There are many excellent textbooks on Special Relativity. I recommend J. H. Smith, *Introduction to Special Relativity* (New York: Benjamin, 1967). For a fascinating (but unorthodox) approach, see E. F. Taylor and J. A. Wheeler, *Spacetime Physics* (San Francisco: Freeman, 1966).
2. If you want to go into this more deeply, I recommend R. Hagedorn, *Relativistic Kinematics* (New York: Benjamin, 1964).
