---
title: "D.1 External Lines"
---

> [!TLDR]
> External lines contribute spinors or polarization vectors according to the particle spin.

<!-- PDF page 388; printed page 380 -->

Spin 0: (nothing)

Spin $\tfrac12$:

$$
\begin{cases}
\text{Incoming particle: }&\signal{u}\\
\text{Incoming antiparticle: }&\signal{\bar v}\\
\text{Outgoing particle: }&\signal{\bar u}\\
\text{Outgoing antiparticle: }&\signal{v}
\end{cases}
$$

Spin 1:

$$
\begin{cases}
\text{incoming: }&\signal{\epsilon}^{\mu}\\
\text{outgoing: }&\signal{\epsilon}^{\mu*}
\end{cases}
$$

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{u},\signal{v},\signal{\bar u},\signal{\bar v}$ | — | External spinors | The particle and antiparticle spinors with the normalization used for the amplitude. |
| $\signal{\epsilon}^{\mu}$ | $1$ | Polarization vector | The polarization of an external spin-one particle. |
