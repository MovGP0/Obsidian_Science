## Choose a next study direction

If tensor calculations remain difficult, work through [[1.1 Vectors, dual vectors, and tensors]], [[1.2 Covariant differentiation]], and [[1.9 Curvature]]. Reproduce the oblique-coordinate and gauge-transformation calculations after that review.

If the local phase argument remains difficult, use [[3.3 Phase Invariance in Quantum Mechanics]], [[3.5 Phase Invariance in Field Theory]], and [[11.3 Local Gauge Invariance]]. Compare their covariant derivative with the horizontal derivative of the compact geometry.

If the particle interpretation is your main interest, continue through [[Elementary Particles - Contents]] and [[Standard Model]]. Pay particular attention to spin, chirality, gauge representations, and the [[11.9 The Higgs Mechanism|Higgs mechanism]]. Those are among the ingredients missing from the simple scalar circle model.

To extend the model, choose a single new ingredient and repeat the consistency checks: define its fields, derive their transformation laws, reduce the action, vary every retained field, and compare the resulting spectrum with the target physical system. A proposed extension is incomplete if it only changes a diagram or an interpretation.

## Final self-check

Close the notes and explain the construction in your own words. Include the assumptions, the gauge mechanism, the action, the matter modes, and the failure of the simple mass–charge identification. If one of those steps is missing, return to its chapter rather than trying to memorize only the final formulas.
