title: "The Unsolved Riddle of Inertia: The Incompleteness of the GTR"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The author argues that explaining inertia requires a unique cosmic gravitational background, which conflicts with unrestricted general coordinate invariance.

## Physical question and model

What is an explanation of inertia expected to add? A theory can predict free motion from a spacetime geometry without giving the deeper microscopic mechanism the author seeks. Separate that explanatory goal from whether the existing equations include cosmic matter.

## Lecture account

Every discussion of gravity and dynamics raises why equivalence of inertia and gravitational mass must be assumed as a postulate. Should such a fundamental result, seen throughout dynamics, not be derived from principles tied to observed nature? Einstein’s repeated references to Mach’s suggestion show this wish. Yet Mach’s principle entered the GTR only as a trace of small effects near external masses. *Without the Machian scheme, inertia’s origin remains unexplained.*

To implement inertia as interaction with the Universe’s other matter, necessary conditions are: (1) the field at a point must be uniquely determined by universal matter and energy in causal contact; (2) large-scale matter distribution must be highly homogeneous and isotropic to conform to the Principle of Relativity. Thus, Mach’s principle requires a unique gravitational background and source-mass distribution *within the theory itself*. This gives an absolute frame for relativistic dynamics. To paraphrase Mach, the theory cannot be “twice given, one with the gravitational background of the Universe, and one with the freedom of arbitrary spaces and coordinate transformations therein, including the situation of empty space”. *This is the GTR’s incompleteness.*

The GTR became physically incomplete and unable to explain inertia because it sought mathematical completeness through general coordinate invariance, an idealistic prejudice. This was paradoxical given the single Universe in which all gravitational and physical processes occur, amid its entire matter–energy and gravity.

## Interpretation and checks

For a cosmic mechanism, calculate the response of a test body to a specified change in surrounding matter while preserving conservation and local equivalence. The proposed explanation must not assume its desired inertial force when defining the coupling. A useful check is to compare two cosmological solutions with different densities and ask whether the model predicts an independently measurable local difference.
