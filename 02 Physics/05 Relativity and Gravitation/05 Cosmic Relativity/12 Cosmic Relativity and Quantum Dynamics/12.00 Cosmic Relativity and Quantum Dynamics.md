## Physical question and model

What must a universal microscopic dynamics explain? It must give normalised probabilities, interference, conserved quantities, and the observed classical limit. The author proposes action waves as the underlying dynamical entities and assigns ordinary wavefunctions to ensembles.

## Lecture account

## Abstract

Cosmic Relativity is presented as a gravitational framework for all dynamics and relativity. Since its effects arise from cosmic matter's gravity, the author rejects a fundamental division between classical and quantum phenomena.

This chapter explains that claim through aspects of quantum dynamics relevant to Cosmic Relativity. The central proposal is that the Schrödinger equation is a classical continuity equation for a statistical ensemble and that the wavefunction is an ensemble-averaged quantity. The author further proposes a modified Hamilton equation for action dynamics as a universal dynamical equation consistent with Cosmic Relativity.

## Interpretation and checks

An interpretation is not yet a replacement equation. State how particles, action waves, measurement couplings, and ensemble averaging jointly predict each outcome distribution. Reproducing a familiar interference cosine is a necessary check, not proof that all quantum experiments follow. The later entanglement chapter is an independent consistency test of the same assumptions.
