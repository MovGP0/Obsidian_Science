---
title: "The Minuteness of the Quantum Zero-Point Energy"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The author rejects field-vacuum zero-point energy and assigns small ground-state energy only to real confined particles.

## Physical question and model

What part of zero-point physics is being denied or reassigned? A ground-state energy in a specified quantum system, a difference in vacuum stress between boundaries, and a cosmological vacuum-energy source are distinct quantities.

## Lecture account

The proposed wave-like entity in particle and field dynamics is the action wave. It carries action rather than energy or momentum and therefore, in the author's account, contributes no gravitational source. All energy resides in real particles. Where there are no particles, he predicts no zero-point energy (ZPE).

He contrasts this with assigning $\energy{h}\signal{\nu}/2$ to each field mode even without particle excitations, which would produce an enormous energy density. See [[08.06.04 The Dark Energy and the Quantum Vacuum]].

For a particle confined to length $\mechanical{l}$, the source writes total action as

$$
\energy{S}=\int\mechanical{p}\,d\mechanical{x}=N\mechanical{p}\mechanical{l}\pm\energy{\hbar}.
$$

Its proposed lowest-state momentum is $\mechanical{p}_0=\energy{h}/(2\mechanical{l})$, giving ground-state energy $\mechanical{p}_0^2/(2\mechanical{m})$. The author describes this as very small relative to rest energy $\mechanical{m}\mechanical{c}^2$ and concludes that particle ZPE has negligible cosmic energy density.

He therefore predicts neither divergent nor significant vacuum ZPE. If observations establish an energy component with pressure $\energy{p}=-\mechanical{\rho}\mechanical{c}^2$, he argues that it cannot originate in the empty-field vacuum.

The source presents this as resolving the longstanding large or divergent cosmological-constant problem [20]. Conventional field theories predict much larger ZPE than cosmic expansion permits. The author attributes zero-point dynamics to energy-free action waves and states that this removes the discrepancy. He regards the result as especially important for Cosmic Relativity, which depends on the gravity of all cosmic matter and energy.

## Interpretation and checks

A model with no action-wave energy must still reproduce measured energy differences and forces attributed to quantum ground states. Removing an absolute divergent sum by interpretation is not a calculation of those differences. Check the harmonic-oscillator ground-state limit and specify how the detector exchanges energy. The cosmological-constant problem concerns gravitational coupling and renormalised stress–energy, not simply whether a particle detector sees a material wave.
