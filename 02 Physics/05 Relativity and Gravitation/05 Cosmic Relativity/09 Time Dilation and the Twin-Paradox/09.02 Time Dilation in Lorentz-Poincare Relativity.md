> [!TLDR]
> Lorentz–Poincaré time dilation depends on speed relative to the ether’s unique rest frame; acceleration is not a parameter in its clock-rate formula.

## Physical question and model

When can an ether interpretation and a Lorentz-covariant clock model predict the same observations? A hidden preferred frame is not measurable if rods, clocks, fields, and synchronisation transform so that all instrument readings coincide.

## Lecture account

Fitzgerald and Lorentz independently proposed length contraction to explain the Michelson–Morley null result. This was the first observationally important second-order effect, $\mechanical{v}^2/\mechanical{c}^2$, in electrodynamics. The final Lorentz transformations contained $\mechanical{\gamma}=1/\sqrt{1-\mechanical{v}^2/\mechanical{c}^2}$, modifying both length and duration. Lorentz’s time transformation, $t'=\mechanical{\gamma}(t-\mechanical{v}\mechanical{x}/\mechanical{c}^2)$, predicts *dilation of a moving clock relative to one at rest in the ether*: $\Delta\mechanical{T}'=\sqrt{1-\mechanical{v}^2/\mechanical{c}^2}\,\Delta\mechanical{T}$ (Section 3.6, equation (3.28)). Unlike the STR, $\mechanical{v}$ is absolute velocity relative to the hypothetical ether’s unique rest frame.

No experiment detects the ether, as Michelson–Morley and many other situations show. Thus, one cannot predict which of two relatively moving clocks runs slower, but can claim afterwards that the clock with less duration moved relative to the ether. Although this is unhelpful, velocity is the only parameter needed. In particular, *acceleration is irrelevant to Lorentz time dilation*. This matters because both Lorentz–Poincaré relativity and Einstein’s STR use identical transformations for formal deductions. Lorentz–Poincaré theory is not restricted to inertial motion and is more general in that respect. If acceleration cannot decide which clock dilates in an absolute-frame theory, it cannot decide this in a theory whose transformations contain only relative velocity.

## Interpretation and checks

Treat the proposed absolute speed as a model variable, not a quantity obtained merely by calling one clock stationary. Compare its predicted reunion reading with the proper-time integral in an arbitrary inertial chart. If the theory only reinterprets that integral, the experiment tests the shared formula. A new cosmic interaction requires an additional residual signature with a specified apparatus dependence.
