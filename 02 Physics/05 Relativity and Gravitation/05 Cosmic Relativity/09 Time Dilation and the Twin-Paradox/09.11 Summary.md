## Physical question and model

Review moving-clock experiments by their common departure and reunion events, complete paths, and instrument corrections. The source argues for a cosmic preferred frame; the standard comparison uses invariant proper time in a chosen chart.

## Lecture account

We surveyed relative two-clock dilation to distinguish relative-motion STR from a theory of real absolute motion in a unique privileged frame. The twin-clock problem and physical cautions against inadvertent rest-frame bias were explained. We compared the two theories’ formulae. A typical experiment synchronises clocks in proximity, carries B while reference A stays in the laboratory, and compares them together again. In the STR, the reference frame’s own velocity never appears. In an absolute-frame theory, each clock’s speed relative to the preferred frame determines its dilation; subtracting them gives the relative difference. Observable distinctions occur when both transported and reference speeds are much below light, as for terrestrial atomic-clock tests.

After fast unstable-particle tests, we examined Hafele–Keating’s eastward and westward caesium-clock flights. Subtracting gravitational effects leaves motional dilation. Westward clocks returned older than laboratory references, contrary to the STR as presented here, and agreed with the absolute-frame predictions. This is taken as direct evidence against relative velocity alone determining dilation.

We then examined standard STR resolutions. Acceleration at turnaround fails on several grounds and is unnecessary to pose the comparison. Einstein’s own gravitational resolution used the GTR and the equivalence principle, but likewise requires acceleration. The simultaneity account assigns a sudden distant-clock change when another clock reverses; this was also judged invalid.

The author concludes that experiments and analysis establish motional dilation as real absolute-frame motion. *The final twin-clock resolution is a physical clock-rate effect depending on velocity in the matter-filled Universe’s unique frame, accurately given by $\sqrt{1-\mechanical{v}^2(t)/\mechanical{c}^2}$.* Relative dilation follows by subtracting clock times.

The author therefore rejects the STR as a motional-relativity theory and identifies the absolute frame with universal matter–energy, whose gravity causes motional effects. Every absolute-frame consequence should appear in appropriate experiments. The next chapters develop Cosmic Relativity, predictions and decisive tests, solutions to open dynamical problems, a necessary modification of Einstein’s field equation, and classical and quantum applications.
