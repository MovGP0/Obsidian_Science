## Lecture account

> [!note]
> Cosmic Relativity is both strongly motivated and robustly verified by a number of experiments. Apart from being in total agreement with all the experimental results usually quoted as ‘evidence’ for the conventional theory of relativity, Cosmic Relativity exclusively confirms the necessity of the concept of absolute rest, and of referring the motional velocity to the preferred frame of the matter-filled Universe, for the correct and consistent calculations of relativistic effects.

Cosmic Relativity predicts new phenomena in electrodynamics of moving bodies that Special Relativity is not even able to formulate without confusion in its restricted structure of inertial motion. Laboratory experiments have confirmed these predictions. Most importantly, it is experimentally confirmed that the relative one-way velocity of light is Galilean to first order ($\mechanical{v}/\mechanical{c}$). This result is strengthened by the fact that the working algorithms in the Global Navigational Satellite Systems like the GPS conform to the Galilean propagation of light, with the velocity of the observer explicitly required in its relativistic corrections. This is a fact everybody can examine and verify. Then there is no justification to maintain that a theory of relativity based on the invariance of the relative velocity of light as its fundamental postulate could continue as the correct theory of relativity and motion. Faced with this statement, one might outright dismiss the experimental result (of Galilean propagation) as impossible, because there is a general belief that experiments of the Michelson-Morley type had proved the invariance of the relative velocity of light in any inertial frame. However, the rigorous fact is that *such experiments confirmed only the invariance of the two-way average velocity of light and not the invariance of the genuine one-way relative velocity*; the null result of Michelson-Morley type experiments is perfectly consistent with an absolute frame theory including the Lorentz-Poincaré ether theory, in which the relative velocity of light is Galilean. Apart from the vital results from the experiments on the one-way relative velocity of light, a wealth of results from both conventional and novel experiments that decisively test the theories of relativity are now available [19–22]. The total support from experimental results is indeed the strength of Cosmic Relativity.

## Interpretation and checks

Terms involving receiver motion occur in a relativistic signal-interception calculation because the receiver changes position while the pulse travels. Their occurrence in a GPS algorithm does not alone establish Galilean velocity addition. Compare complete predictions for the same events and clock convention. Use [Ashby and Weiss on GPS receivers](https://www.nist.gov/publications/global-positioning-system-receivers-and-relativity) as a check on the standard treatment. A null result constrains a specified model and sensitivity, rather than proving every premise of an alternative.
