## Lecture account

The exact empirical and logical reasons for the need to reconsider the very physical paradigm for the theories of relativity and dynamics were presented and justified in this chapter. The fact that modern observational cosmology and extra-galactic astronomy started more than a decade after our fundamental theories were completed, is behind the thoroughly unphysical situation that our current theories assume explicitly an empty space, instead of the factual matter-filled Universe, as the background space of dynamics. This crucial difference is fatal for the theories because the cosmic matter and energy are the sources of large gravitational potentials that affect and influence every physical phenomenon that happens in this Universe; but such effects are completely missed in the description of dynamics in the current theories.

As soon as the gravitational Universe is acknowledged and incorporated in a new theoretical description of relativity and dynamics, every important result and hypothesis that constitute the current description of dynamics and relativity emerge, as straightforward consequences of the gravitational interaction with the factual cosmic matter distributed in this vast Universe. All major outstanding questions and unsolved problems are answered and solved; there are no exceptions. What was assumed and accepted as hypotheses are now the calculable physical consequences of the cosmic gravity. The physical origin of the relativistic effects, the inertial forces, and the specific forms of the laws of dynamics are all just cosmic gravitational effects. Even the Principle of Relativity is a simple physical consequence of the uniformity of the gravitational potentials of the near homogeneous distribution of the cosmic matter.

The matter-filled Universe and its gravity determine the absolute reference frame for motion and also a universal absolute cosmic time, synchronised everywhere. Cosmic gravity determines the Galilean propagation of light and gravitational waves, and all relativistic effects. The General Theory of Relativity requires an important modification, or completion, that replaces the superfluous general coordinate invariance with a privileged frame of the factual Universe, with a Machian absolute character. The new gravitational paradigm of Cosmic Relativity integrates centuries of insights of dynamics and relativity into one unified theory that is consistent with the fact that all dynamical phenomena happen in the gravitational presence of this Universe.

## Interpretation and checks

A useful review exercise is to choose one claimed success and name a competing standard prediction for the same setup. State what is held fixed and what measurement separates them. If both give the same result, that result supports consistency but not a unique mechanism. Continue to the historical chapters with this distinction in mind.
