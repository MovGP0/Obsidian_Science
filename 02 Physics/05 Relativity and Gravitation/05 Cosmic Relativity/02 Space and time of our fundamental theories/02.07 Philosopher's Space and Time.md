## Physical question and model

Which questions about space and time are operational, and which concern interpretation? A philosophical criterion can clarify how a model is used, but it cannot replace an instrument prediction. The lecture's assessment of logical positivism is a historical and philosophical argument.

## Lecture account

A new agenda for dealing with space and time was outlined by the logical positivists in the 20th century. These philosophers were excited by the new developments in physics and the apparent overthrow of metaphysical elements from the physical theories. Of course, their philosophic enthusiasm about the new physics turned out to be a bit premature, as proved by the ‘larger than philosophy’ avatar of quantum mechanics. But, that is a topic for another discussion.

H. Reichenbach asserted that the philosophy of space and time was essentially the philosophy concerning the theories of relativity. The announced motivation was to purge metaphysical notions from the description of the physical nature. O. Neurath, a prominent philosopher of the Vienna circle, wrote [21],

> Although what is called philosophical speculation is undoubtedly on the decline, many of the practically minded have not yet freed themselves from a method of reasoning, which, in the last analysis, has its roots in theology and metaphysics. No science which pretends to be exact can accept an untested theory or doctrine; yet even in an exact science there is often an admixture of magic, theology and philosophy. It is one of the tasks of our time to aid scientific reasoning to attain its goal without hindrance...
>
> Physics has been successfully purged of metaphysical formulas. For example, the conception of ‘absolute motion’ has been discarded, a conception which acquired meaning only if one thought of ‘absolute space’ as a gigantic glass case in which ‘coordinates’ were woven like spider webs so that it became possible to determine whether a body is at absolute rest or whether it is moving about within the case. The Mach-Einstein conception dispenses with this ‘absolute space’...

One would think that it was not proper to juxtapose theology and metaphysics in the discussion of the methods of reasoning. However, physics cannot be free, and never has been, from certain metaphysical priors, especially while dealing with the difficult concepts of space and time. In any case, it turns out that the current facts about theoretical physics contradict what the logical positivists favoured. Not even physicists would claim that ‘physics has been successfully purged of metaphysical formulas’. One often sees, in physics texts and lectures, a space and time represented by woven coordinate lines of space and time, and called the ‘fabric of space-time’, even though none of that exists verifiably. In fact, one can see that the enthusiasm expressed by Neurath ironically shatters against the even more speculative shores of modern physics.

The empirical facts discussed in this book, and the necessity to make physics consistent with our cosmology, will clearly demand the return of the ‘absolute’ into the core of physics. The gravitational paradigm of Cosmic Relativity provides the complete derivation and understanding of dynamics and relativity. Then the philosophy of physics has to respond in a logically consistent manner. This needs much revision of the current thinking, not only about the physics of space and time, but also about the relation of the individual with the encompassing cosmic gravitational presence, which affects every physical process. Given the cosmic markers, accurately represented by the CMBR, nobody can assert the equivalence of the states of rest and motion, without being explicitly in conflict with the factual empirical situation. Also, the temperature of the CMBR is monotonically decreasing, as it has been for billions of years, and provides a universal time that is everywhere the same in the Universe to high accuracy. A local clock can be synchronised to the locally measured averaged temperature of the background and it will read the same time as any other clock anywhere else. In the new gravitational paradigm for physics, there is clear ontological priority for the matter-energy in the Universe and its gravity, with space and time as emergent secondary notions. The details will unfold as we progress in the discussions and demonstrations in this book.

## Interpretation and checks

Try expressing one disputed claim as an experiment with specified preparation and readout. If that cannot be done, record its role as interpretation rather than calling it a measurement. Conversely, a coordinate convention can be useful without being a directly sensed substance. The learning goal is to keep empirical adequacy, mathematical consistency, and a preferred account of reality distinct when comparing philosophies.
