## Study route

Return to [[02.00 Space and Time of Our Fundamental Theories]] after selecting one source. Identify its measured quantity, theoretical assumptions, and uncertainty or stated limitation. When using a philosophical or historical source, separate its interpretation from the mechanical or optical result being interpreted. Keep historical descriptions separate from later experimental results, and distinguish the author's own proposals from independent tests.
