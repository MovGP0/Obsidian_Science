## Physical question and model

How should a proposed replacement for an established theory be assessed? It must recover successful predictions in their tested domain, give well-defined dynamics, and identify observations that distinguish it. A broad interpretive synthesis does not remove these requirements.

## Abstract

The author presents cosmic matter and energy as the gravitational basis of dynamics and relativity, with an absolute reference for motion and universal cosmic time. He argues that general relativity must be completed with this physical cosmic frame. Having claimed solutions to longstanding foundational problems, he asks whether the cosmic gravitational paradigm is an ultimate foundation for dynamics.

## Introduction

Broad paradigms in physics and astronomy change rarely because they integrate decades of experiments and observations around a few principles. A major revision needs compelling empirical evidence.

The source argues that re-examining the evidence reveals conflicts between existing theories and the gravitational properties of the matter-filled Universe. Cosmic Relativity is offered as a unified alternative answering longstanding questions [1–3]. The chapter tests the plausibility of an ultimate foundation against physical facts and the history of ideas.
