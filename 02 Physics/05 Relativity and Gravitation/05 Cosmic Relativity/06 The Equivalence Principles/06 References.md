## Reading the evidence

Use this list to trace the chapter's treatment of free-fall universality, equivalence principles, and precision comparisons. Bracketed numbers in the lecture account refer to the original numbered entries below. A reference records provenance; it does not by itself establish the conclusion for which the lecture cites it.

## Study route

Return to [[06.00 The Equivalence Principles]] after selecting one source. Identify its measured quantity, theoretical assumptions, and uncertainty or stated limitation. Read experimental bounds with their parameter convention and uncertainty budget; compare quantum phase tests with trajectory tests only after defining the observable. Keep historical descriptions separate from later experimental results, and distinguish the author's own proposals from independent tests.
