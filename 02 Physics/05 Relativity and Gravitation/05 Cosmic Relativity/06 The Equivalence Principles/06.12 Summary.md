> [!TLDR]
> The chapter reviews equivalence principles, their experimental tests, the author’s gravitational account of inertia, and compatibility with quantum dynamics.

## Physical question and model

Use this summary to connect a hierarchy of principles with a hierarchy of tests. Free-fall universality, local nongravitational physics, and gravitational self-energy are different physical targets.

## Lecture account

This chapter reviewed the universality of free fall (UFF) in a gravitational field and the principles of equivalence that form the foundation of the GTR. The progressive refinement of their statements and underlying reasons was discussed. The logical and physical path from the UFF to the WEP and then to the EEP was explained. Galileo’s well-known but rarely analysed logical proof of universal free fall was examined, and its fallacy identified. Thus, the UFF is a fundamental observation that needs experimental study. Experimental designs with freely falling masses, torsion balances, and astrophysical bodies were explained. The SEP, asserting UFF even for gravitational self-energy, was also discussed.

Having participated in precision equivalence-principle tests, I explained the central quantitative aspects and constraints from significant experiments, with recent updates.

I emphasised that the GTR assumes as postulates, but does not explain, the equivalence of inertial and gravitational mass (UFF and WEP), and that of a uniform gravitational field and an accelerated frame (EEP). Thus, the UFF and the origin of inertia are important open problems. I then mentioned the answer to the origin of inertia and inertial forces through gravitational interaction with all matter in the Universe. This was a glimpse of the gravitational paradigm of Cosmic Relativity in the context of equivalence principles.

Finally, I discussed the consistency of the equivalence principle with quantum phenomena. A proof was given, dismissing confusion in applying the principle to quantum dynamics.

In the next chapter, we follow Einstein towards completion of his GTR, starting from universal free fall and the equivalence of inertial and gravitational mass.

## Interpretation and checks

A review task is to choose a torsion balance, a lunar ranging experiment, and a quantum interferometer. For each, state the measured quantity, the leading nuisance force or phase, and the equivalence assumption under test. Explain why a null result in one does not automatically prove the strongest principle. This supplies the physical starting point for the geometric theory in Chapter 7.
