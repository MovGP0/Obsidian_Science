> [!TLDR]
> Cosmic Relativity uses the slowly changing gravitational properties of the present Universe as the background for human observations of dynamics.

## Physical question and model

Which cosmic properties can be treated as fixed during a laboratory experiment? Compare the experiment duration with the cosmic expansion timescale. A nearly constant mean density or temperature can be a useful local-time approximation without making cosmic evolution absent.

## Lecture account

The Universe’s evolutionary history spans about $13.8$ billion years. In the hot-big-bang model, early processes were rapid. Some proposed aspects lack evidence, such as an inflationary phase of exponential expansion about $10^{-32}\,\unit{s}$ after the big bang. The earliest observed signature is CMBR from about $380{,}000\,\unit{yr}$ afterwards. Stars and structures that became galaxies appeared before $500$ million years. Matter-dominated steady expansion later became slightly accelerated, according to the currently favoured interpretation. Chapter 8 discussed the features relevant to Cosmic Relativity.

Expansion has been slow for about ten billion years, so mean gravitational properties have changed little. Compare this with the much shorter age of civilisations observing nature and seeking laws of dynamics and relative motion.

Civilisation’s roughly $10{,}000\,\unit{yr}$ is insignificant compared with ten billion matter-dominated years (Fig. 10.1). Today’s observed Universe determines familiar dynamics. Observations, theories, and tests occur in its unavoidable constant gravitational presence. No dynamical experience is free from relevant gravity because all experience belongs to this single Universe. Theories omitting its gravity are therefore wrong or incomplete from the start. A foundational postulate explicitly denying universal matter and gravity is similarly suspect. Empty, gravity-free space remaining isotropic in every inertial frame is one example; we know we do not inhabit such space.

A material motion reference alone affects only kinematics. A fixed coordinate system for measuring motion does not determine dynamics or light propagation. That requires a universal interaction insensitive to particle type, interactions, and intrinsic properties. Gravity alone qualifies. Hence, universal matter–energy’s enormous gravity is central. ## Interpretation and checks

Historical cosmological epochs are inferred through a model and observational evidence of different kinds. The CMB is not the earliest physical process described by the model, only an early electromagnetic record available today. For an inertia calculation, identify exactly which current density, causal boundary, or expansion parameter enters. Then estimate whether its neglected change matters at the precision of the proposed test.
