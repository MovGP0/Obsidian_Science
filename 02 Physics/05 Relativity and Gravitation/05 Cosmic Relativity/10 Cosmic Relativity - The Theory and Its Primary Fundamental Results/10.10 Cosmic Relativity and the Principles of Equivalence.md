> [!TLDR]
> The author derives a universal inertial-to-gravitational mass ratio from cosmic gravity and uses it to explain the equivalence principles.

## Physical question and model

How would one cosmic coupling explain several equivalence principles? Track the same potential normalisation through inertial response, passive gravitational force, and clock behaviour. These are distinct operational roles.

## Lecture account

The Newton-law derivation showed inertia emerging from interaction with cosmic matter. The relation is $\mechanical{m}_i=\mechanical{m}\mechanical{\Phi}_u/\mechanical{c}^2$. Thus, *the ratio $\mechanical{m}_i/\mechanical{m}$ is the cosmic gravitational potential*, in the stated normalisation, and is universal for all bodies regardless of their properties. This both explains inertia and states the WEP. Its universality implies UFF, making free fall a consequence of interaction of every body and energy with universal matter–energy. Several related fundamental results follow, each presented as providing a new physical explanation.

## Interpretation and checks

A universal factor in both response and forcing can cancel in an acceleration ratio. That establishes a conditional result within the model, not its microscopic origin. Also separate universality for test bodies from strong equivalence for self-gravitating systems. A useful study check is to locate every point where composition independence is assumed rather than derived.
