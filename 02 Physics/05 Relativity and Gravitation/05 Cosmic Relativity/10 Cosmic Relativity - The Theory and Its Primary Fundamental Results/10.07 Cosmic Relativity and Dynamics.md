## Physical question and model

What must a gravitational origin of Newton's law calculate? It needs an action or force coupling that predicts response to an applied external force, while identifying the cosmic source and any reaction or energy flow.

## Lecture account

Cosmic Relativity’s dynamical implications are central: it incorporates dynamics into relativistic gravity. This unification may seem surprising because earlier relativity lacked such a strong connection with physical interactions. The STR is mainly kinematic, whereas dynamics involves interactions. Universal gravity supplies Cosmic Relativity’s connection, so motion laws must emerge as relativistic gravitational effects. We begin with Newton’s law.

## Interpretation and checks

A matching acceleration term is not yet a full interacting theory. Verify momentum and energy balance for the body, local apparatus, and fields. A proposed universal coupling must also account for different kinds of applied force without changing the inertial response arbitrarily. The low-speed limit should recover ordinary mass times acceleration, with a positive inertial coefficient.
