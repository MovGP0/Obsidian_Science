## Physical question and model

Which physical measurements are being derived in the next subsections? Clock duration, rod length, local forces, and propagation times are distinct readouts. Each requires its own event selection or dynamical model, even if a common metric is proposed.

## Lecture account

Cosmic Relativity’s reach is broad, so its coverage of dynamics requires systematic steps. Each should connect a complete solution of a long-open problem with its cosmic gravitational explanation. We first consider temporal and spatial intervals, which follow from elementary considerations, then clock dilation, before deeper dynamics.

## Interpretation and checks

Prepare a prediction ledger with one row per readout: assumed background, apparatus worldline, coordinate convention, and measured quantity. A factor occurring in one metric component is not automatically the response of every instrument. Carry the same normalisation and sign conventions through all later applications, and use the zero-speed limit as a first check.
