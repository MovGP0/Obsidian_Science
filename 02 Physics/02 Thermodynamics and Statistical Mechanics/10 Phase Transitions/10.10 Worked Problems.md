> [!TLDR]
> Each worked problem has its question, calculations, explanation, and solution in one file.

The source gives four worked problems in this section. Open a problem below to study the complete calculation.

Use the problems to practise choosing constraints before doing algebra. In the water problem, predict the melting-slope sign from the two densities and keep mass-specific latent heat consistent with specific volume. In the Ising problem, identify which path defines each exponent and expand only where the order parameter is small.

For the superconducting problem, fix Gaussian magnetic units before using the critical-field derivative, and check the sign of absorbed heat. In the final problem, insert the exact Ising exponents into each scaling identity and distinguish a logarithmic heat-capacity singularity from a nonzero power. These checks test thermodynamic reasoning, approximation order, and algebra separately. The supplied numbers and model formulas are inputs to worked solutions; solving them is not an independent experimental validation of the models.
