> [!TLDR]
> Phase space represents a microscopic state by its coordinates and conjugate momenta.

Use phase space to answer a counting question: how many possible complete states satisfy the macroscopic constraints? A position-space picture alone cannot answer it, because particles at the same positions can carry different momenta and energies.

Read the definitions to identify the state, the trajectories to identify its motion, and the volume element to identify the measure used for probabilities. One complete gas occupies one point in Γ-space. A cloud in Γ-space represents an ensemble of possible complete gases. It does not represent the particles of a single gas; those particles can instead be shown as points in μ-space. Check this distinction before interpreting any ensemble diagram.
