> [!TLDR]
> Boltzmann entropy relates a macrostate to the number of compatible microscopic states.

The counting problem starts with a macrostate, not with a visual judgment of order. Specify which energy interval, volume, particle count, and other macroscopic restrictions define that macrostate. Its multiplicity then counts the compatible microscopic states using one consistent convention.

Read the state-counting rule before the logarithm formula. The division by action cells makes the logarithm dimensionless, and particle indistinguishability determines which permutations count as new states. The thermodynamic derivatives then give a test of the construction: the same entropy must predict the temperature and pressure for that model. These equilibrium derivatives do not determine how quickly a prepared nonequilibrium gas reaches equilibrium.
