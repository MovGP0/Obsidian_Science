> [!TLDR]
> Gibbs entropy applies to a probability distribution and reduces to Boltzmann entropy for equal probabilities.

Use this formulation when the preparation gives unequal probabilities to the allowed microstates. A probability distribution and a macrostate count are different inputs: the distribution says how states are weighted, whereas the count says how many states are compatible with selected constraints.

Read the discrete formula first, then the continuous form to see why a reference measure matters. Constrained maximization selects an equilibrium distribution only after the physical constraints have been specified. The final note checks the uniform case explicitly. During exact Hamiltonian transport, the fine-grained distribution can change shape while its Gibbs entropy stays constant; maximizing entropy is not that equation of motion.
