> [!TLDR]
> For an isolated system in equilibrium, all accessible microstates are assigned equal probability.

## Definition 2.9 — Fundamental postulate: equal a priori probabilities

For an isolated system in thermodynamic equilibrium with energy ${\energy{E}}$, volume ${\mechanical{V}}$, and particle count ${\mechanical{N}}$, all accessible microstates are equally likely:

$$
p_i=\frac1{\Omega({\energy{E}},{\mechanical{V}},{\mechanical{N}})}
\qquad\text{for every accessible microstate }i.
$$

Accessible means compatible with all imposed constraints, including additional conserved quantities when they matter. States outside this set receive zero probability. For $\Omega$ discrete allowed states, summing the displayed probability gives one. In a classical continuum, equal weighting means uniform density with respect to the specified invariant measure on the shell; it does not assign a finite equal probability to every individual point.

The equilibrium condition is essential. An isolated gas prepared in one half of a container has information beyond its total energy, volume, and particle count. Immediately after the partition is removed, applying uniform weighting to the whole container would discard that preparation information. The postulate describes the equilibrium ensemble and supplies no relaxation time for this expansion.

This is the fundamental postulate on which equilibrium statistical mechanics is built.

See [[02.07.01 Justification and Status of the Postulate]].

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\energy{E}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Energy | The system or level energy; ΔE is an energy interval. |
| ${\mechanical{V}}$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Volume | The physical volume occupied by the system. |
| ${\mechanical{N}}$ | $1$ | Particle or oscillator count | The number of particles or distinguishable oscillators. |
| $p_i$ | $1$ | Microstate probability | The probability assigned to accessible microstate i. |
| $\Omega$ | $1$ | Multiplicity | The number of microstates compatible with the stated constraints. |
