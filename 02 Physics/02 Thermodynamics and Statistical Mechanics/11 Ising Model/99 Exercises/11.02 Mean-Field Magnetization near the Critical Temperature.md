> [!TLDR]
> Expand the mean-field equation to find the leading coefficient of the spontaneous magnetization.

**Exercise 11.2.** Show that, for $\thermal{T}\to\thermal{T}_{\mathrm c}^-$ in mean-field theory, the magnetization has the form $\magnetic{m}\sim(\thermal{T}_{\mathrm c}-\thermal{T})^{1/2}$. Determine the coefficient:

$$
\magnetic{m}
=\sqrt3\left(1-\frac{\thermal{T}}{\thermal{T}_{\mathrm c}}\right)^{1/2}
+O\!\left(\left(1-\frac{\thermal{T}}{\thermal{T}_{\mathrm c}}\right)^{3/2}\right).
$$

See [[11.03.02 Analysis of the Self-Consistency Equation]].

## Derivation and range

Select the positive ferromagnetic branch at zero field. Define the small positive reduced distance below the transition by $\thermal{\epsilon}=1-\thermal{T}/\thermal{T}_{\mathrm c}$. The mean-field equation is

$$
\magnetic{m}=\tanh\!\left(\frac{\magnetic{m}}{1-\thermal{\epsilon}}\right)
=\frac{\magnetic{m}}{1-\thermal{\epsilon}}
-\frac{\magnetic{m}^3}{3(1-\thermal{\epsilon})^3}
+O(\magnetic{m}^5).
$$

The cubic term is needed because a purely linear equation locates the transition but cannot set the nonzero amplitude. Divide by the nonzero magnetization and balance the leading terms: $\thermal{\epsilon}\sim\magnetic{m}^2/3$. Hence $\magnetic{m}^2=3\thermal{\epsilon}+O(\thermal{\epsilon}^2)$, which gives the stated coefficient $\sqrt3$. The negative branch has the opposite sign. The unmagnetized root remains a formal solution and must not be obtained by dividing by zero.

The expansion requires $\thermal{\epsilon}\ll1$ and a small magnetization. The coefficient is dimensionless because the temperature difference was divided by the critical temperature. If the leading term were used at zero temperature it would give $\sqrt3>1$, in conflict with a normalized binary spin. This is a direct check of its limited range, not a defect in the full mean-field equation. The result describes the approximation's exponent; it does not replace the exact two-dimensional exponent.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\magnetic{m}$ | $1$ | Magnetization per spin | The mean normalized spin; subscripts identify particular or spontaneous values. |
| $\thermal{T}$ | $\unit{K}$ | Temperature | The absolute temperature; a c subscript marks a critical value. |
| $\thermal{\epsilon}$ | $1$ | Reduced distance below the transition | One minus temperature divided by the critical temperature. |
