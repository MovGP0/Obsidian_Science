> [!TLDR]
> Decimating the zero-field Ising chain gives an exact example of a renormalization-group transformation.

This chain is a useful control case because the partial spin sum can be done exactly. First match the weights of parallel and antiparallel retained spins. Then follow the resulting coupling map to identify the finite-temperature fate of the model. Finally, check the transformation independently through the exact correlation length.

The exact closure shown here depends on one dimension, zero field and nearest-neighbour interactions. It should not be assumed for a higher-dimensional block model. Retaining the spin-independent normalization is necessary if the free energy, rather than only normalized correlations, is wanted.
