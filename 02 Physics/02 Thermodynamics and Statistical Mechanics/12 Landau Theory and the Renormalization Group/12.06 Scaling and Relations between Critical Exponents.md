> [!TLDR]
> The scaling form of the singular free energy gives relations among critical exponents.

This section asks whether independently defined critical powers are actually independent. Begin with the homogeneous singular free energy, derive how its derivatives scale, and then compare the relations with the exact and rounded exponent tables. The correlation integral provides a second route linking spatial decay to a bulk response.

Keep hyperscaling separate from the other relations. It adds the assumption that a correlated volume sets the singular free-energy scale and can fail above the upper critical dimension. Agreement among rounded exponents is an internal consistency check, not an independent measurement of their values.
