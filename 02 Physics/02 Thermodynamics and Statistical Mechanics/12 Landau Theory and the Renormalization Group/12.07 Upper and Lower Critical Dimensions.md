> [!TLDR]
> Upper and lower critical dimensions delimit the role of fluctuations and the possibility of long-range order.

These two thresholds answer different questions. The upper critical dimension asks when interactions between fluctuations change the mean-field critical powers. The lower critical dimension asks whether thermal fluctuations allow the specified form of long-range order at positive temperature.

Read power counting first to locate the upper threshold for the short-range quartic model. The epsilon expansion then treats the nearby interacting fixed point perturbatively. Finish with the lower threshold, where symmetry and interaction range decide whether order is possible at all. Four is not a universal upper threshold for every transition; the quartic model assumptions are part of the result.
