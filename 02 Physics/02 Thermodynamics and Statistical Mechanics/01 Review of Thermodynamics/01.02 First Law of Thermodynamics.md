> [!TLDR]
> The first law expresses energy conservation through internal energy, heat transfer, and work. This section gives its integral and differential forms and defines heat capacities.

The question in this section is how an equilibrium energy change can be reconstructed from transfers across a boundary. Use a closed system and first state the signs of heat and work. Then distinguish a balance valid between endpoints from formulas that require a slow, mechanically reversible path.

Heating a gas at fixed volume and heating it under a movable piston are different experiments. The second permits expansion work, so the same heat input need not give the same temperature rise. This is why the heat-capacity constraint belongs in its definition.
