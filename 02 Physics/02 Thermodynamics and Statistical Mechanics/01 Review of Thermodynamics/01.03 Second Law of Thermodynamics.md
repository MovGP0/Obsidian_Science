> [!TLDR]
> The second law restricts possible processes. This section develops heat-engine limits, entropy, the Clausius inequality, and the maximum-entropy principle.

An energy balance alone would allow all the heat from one reservoir to become cyclic work. The second law excludes that proposal and supplies a direction for spontaneous changes. Follow the engine argument first, then the construction of entropy, and finally the inequality that applies to irreversible changes.

Keep the system and its surroundings together when testing the law. A refrigerator can lower the entropy of its cold contents because it receives work and transfers heat to the room. The entropy of one subsystem is therefore not required to increase in every process.
