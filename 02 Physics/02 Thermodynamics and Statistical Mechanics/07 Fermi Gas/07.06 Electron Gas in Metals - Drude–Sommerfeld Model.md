> [!TLDR]
> The Sommerfeld model combines Fermi statistics with the relaxation-time transport model.

Equilibrium state counting does not by itself predict a steady current. An electric field drives the electron distribution away from equilibrium, and momentum relaxation limits the current. The relaxation time is an additional transport input; it cannot be obtained from exclusion statistics alone.

Read the Drude result to identify that transport assumption. Then replace the classical thermal distribution by the degenerate Fermi distribution in the Sommerfeld model. Finally compare electrical and thermal conduction under a common scattering assumption. Agreement of a conductivity formula with experiment does not separately verify a free-electron band, an energy-independent relaxation time, and the carrier density; these inputs can compensate for each other.
