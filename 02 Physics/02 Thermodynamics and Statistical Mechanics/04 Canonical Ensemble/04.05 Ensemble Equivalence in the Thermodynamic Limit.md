> [!TLDR]
> A saddle-point approximation connects the canonical partition function to microcanonical entropy.

The ensembles impose different boundaries even for a large system: one fixes energy, while the other permits heat exchange at fixed temperature. Equivalence concerns bulk thermodynamic predictions when their energy and temperature are matched. It does not make their microscopic probability measures identical.

The proof follows the competition between the number of states and their Boltzmann suppression. A single dominant energy gives a narrow range of energy per particle. Several competing peaks or nonadditive interactions require a separate argument, so small relative width is not a universal theorem for every many-particle model.

See [[04.05.01 Formal Proof]] for the derivation and its conditions.
