> [!TLDR]
> Energy fluctuates in a canonical ensemble, and its variance is set by heat capacity.

An equilibrium mean does not imply that every energy measurement gives the mean. The system can give heat to the bath or receive heat from it. The variance quantifies this spread across identically prepared equilibrium samples; it does not describe measurement error or an unmodeled drift of temperature.

Read the variance relation first to connect microscopic spread to heat capacity. Then compare the spread with an extensive energy scale as system size increases. The absolute spread can increase while its fraction of the total energy decreases, so these are different questions.
