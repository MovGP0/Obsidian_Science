> [!TLDR]
> Independent magnetic spins give saturation magnetization and Curie behavior at weak field.

This model describes localized moments that can exchange heat with a bath in a prescribed magnetic field. It neglects interactions between moments and changes of the applied field caused by the sample. Thermal disorder competes with the lower energy of a moment aligned with the field.

The Hamiltonian note fixes which sign denotes a moment direction. The response note then compares the population imbalance with the field derivative of free energy. The entropy note explains the loss of accessible spin configurations during alignment. This independent-spin model predicts no spontaneous moment at zero field and finite temperature; magnetic ordering needs additional physics.
