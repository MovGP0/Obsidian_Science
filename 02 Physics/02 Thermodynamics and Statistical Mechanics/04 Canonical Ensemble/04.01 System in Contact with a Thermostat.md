> [!TLDR]
> Weak thermal contact with a large reservoir gives the canonical distribution.

An isolated composite system consists of the system of interest and a much larger heat bath.

The question is why a system at a fixed temperature does not have a fixed energy. Energy transfers continue at equilibrium, but there is no net heat flow. The reservoir is large enough that those transfers do not appreciably change its temperature. No particles pass through the boundary, and the system volume is held fixed.

Weak contact has two roles: it permits equilibration, and it makes the interaction energy negligible in the state count. Zero contact would conserve the system energy and would not by itself establish the canonical probabilities.
