> [!TLDR]
> A hard-core diameter produces an excluded-volume parameter equal to four times the volume of one sphere.

Model ${\mechanical{N}}$ molecules as hard spheres of diameter ${\mechanical{\sigma}}$. The centers of two spheres cannot be closer than ${\mechanical{\sigma}}$.

The excluded region for the center of a second molecule is a sphere of radius ${\mechanical{\sigma}}$:

$$
{\mechanical{v}}_{\mathrm{excl}}=\frac{4\pi{\mechanical{\sigma}}^3}{3}=8{\mechanical{v}}_0,
\qquad {\mechanical{v}}_0=\frac{\pi{\mechanical{\sigma}}^3}{6}.
$$

Each pair is shared by two molecules. The van der Waals covolume per molecule is therefore

$$
{\mechanical{b}}=\frac12{\mechanical{v}}_{\mathrm{excl}}=4{\mechanical{v}}_0=\frac{2\pi{\mechanical{\sigma}}^3}{3}.
$$

The factor one half belongs to pair counting, not to the physical radius of a molecule. One molecule occupies the volume of a sphere of radius half the collision diameter; the excluded region for the other center has the full collision diameter as its radius. Confusing these radii loses the factor eight before the pair-counting correction.

At low density, expand the final pressure denominator as a geometric series. Its first correction is the number density times the covolume, in agreement with the exact hard-sphere second virial integral. At higher density, excluded regions overlap. Subtracting the same covolume for every molecule then fails to count the accessible configurations exactly, even while the algebraic denominator is positive.

The mean-field available-volume approximation replaces ${\mechanical{V}}$ by ${\mechanical{V}}-{\mechanical{N}}{\mechanical{b}}$:

$$
{\mechanical{P}}({\mechanical{V}}-{\mechanical{N}}{\mechanical{b}})={\mechanical{N}}{\thermal{k}}_B{\thermal{T}}.
$$

> [!note]
> This is an approximate equation of state for hard spheres. It reproduces their second virial coefficient. It is not an exact count of available volume at arbitrary density.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\mechanical{N}}$ | $\unit{1}$ | Particle number | Number of molecules. |
| ${\mechanical{\sigma}}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Molecular length | Hard-sphere diameter or Lennard–Jones zero-crossing distance, as stated. |
| ${\mechanical{v}}_0$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Molecular sphere volume | Geometric volume of a sphere with the stated diameter. |
| ${\mechanical{v}}_{\mathrm{excl}}$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Excluded center volume | Region forbidden to the center of a second hard sphere. |
| ${\mechanical{b}}$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Excluded-volume parameter | Molecular van der Waals covolume per particle. |
| ${\mechanical{V}}$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Volume | Total volume occupied by the gas. |
| ${\mechanical{P}}$ | $\unit{Pa}$ | Pressure | Mechanical pressure of the gas. |
| ${\thermal{k}}_B$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Boltzmann constant | Converts temperature to energy per particle. |
| ${\thermal{T}}$ | $\unit{K}$ | Temperature | Absolute temperature of the gas. |
