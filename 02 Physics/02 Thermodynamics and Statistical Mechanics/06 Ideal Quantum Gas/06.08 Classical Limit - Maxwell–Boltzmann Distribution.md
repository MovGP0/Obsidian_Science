> [!TLDR]
> Both quantum distributions reduce to Maxwell–Boltzmann statistics when occupations are small.

The question is when exchange changes occupations by so little that classical counting is sufficient. Low occupation of each relevant complete state is the criterion. High temperature helps at fixed density and mass, but temperature alone does not define a classical gas.

Start with the denominator expansion, then express the criterion in terms of density and the thermal wavelength. This connects an abstract state occupation to experimentally adjustable density and temperature. In using the limit, retain the identical-particle counting already built into quantum number states.

The dilute limit is described in [[06.08.01 Derivation as a Limit of Quantum Statistics]]. The thermal length and its comparison with particle spacing are developed in [[06.08.02 Thermal de Broglie Wavelength]] and [[06.08.03 Quantum Degeneracy Parameter]].
