> [!TLDR]
> Fermi–Dirac, Bose–Einstein, and Maxwell–Boltzmann laws agree at low occupation but differ near the chemical potential.

Before comparing the curves, decide what is held fixed. At the same temperature, chemical potential, and state energy, the Bose mean is larger than the Maxwell–Boltzmann value and the Fermi mean is smaller, within the common positive-gap domain. At the same total density, the chemical potentials generally differ and must be solved separately. The first comparison does not alone establish an ordering at every physical energy in the second problem.

Use the table to select the admissible model, the graph to inspect its state occupations, and the analysis to recover the dilute limit. None of these mean-occupation curves is a normalized energy-probability density.
