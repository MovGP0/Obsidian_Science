> [!TLDR]
> The grand partition function normalizes all particle-number sectors and generates thermodynamic responses.

The partition function solves two tasks: it normalizes probabilities across different particle numbers and generates thermodynamic responses. Read [[05.03.01 Definition and Properties]] to identify which states are counted. Then [[05.03.02 Grand Potential]] converts the statistical sum into a potential with the reservoir controls as natural variables.

[[05.03.03 Relation between Grand Potential and Pressure]] uses an additional bulk-extensivity assumption. [[05.03.04 Calculation of Thermodynamic Quantities]] gives the derivative rules. Check the held variables before every derivative: fixed fugacity and fixed chemical potential are different temperature paths. A convergent sum and a stated path are needed before a formal derivative becomes a physical prediction.
