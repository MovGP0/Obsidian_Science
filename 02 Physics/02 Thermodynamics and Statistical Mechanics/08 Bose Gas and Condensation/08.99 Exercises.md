## Practice route

Begin with Exercise 8.1 to separate an ideal-gas temperature scale from an interacting-liquid transition. Use Exercise 8.2 next to connect microscopic excitation populations with a thermodynamic identity and the third law. Exercises 8.3 and 8.5 then test two distinct operations on radiation: changing spectral variables and integrating a spectrum to obtain energy, number, and pressure.

Exercise 8.4 asks for an approximation error within a chosen model; do not confuse that error with disagreement between the model and a real solid. End with Exercise 8.6, where changing geometry requires a new state count rather than a reused critical-temperature formula. For each solution, state the conserved number, the low-energy spectrum, and one limiting case before checking the numerical result.
