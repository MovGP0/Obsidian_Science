> [!TLDR]
> Cumulative phase-space volume and its energy derivative provide two related measures of accessible states.

An energy surface has zero volume in the full phase space. To count states, one must specify either a finite shell, or a surface measure obtained by an energy derivative. The cumulative count includes all states below the boundary and is a third, distinct object.

Read the definitions first. The ball-volume proof then supplies the geometric factor needed for a free gas. The asymptotic note explains why different counts can give the same leading entropy without becoming identical finite-system functions.
