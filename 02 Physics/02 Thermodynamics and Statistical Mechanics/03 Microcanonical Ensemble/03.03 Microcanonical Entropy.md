> [!TLDR]
> Microcanonical entropy turns an accessible-state count into a thermodynamic state function.

The logarithm converts products of independent state counts into sums of entropies. Derivatives of the resulting function then describe how two weakly coupled systems divide energy, volume, or particles at equilibrium.

Keep the counting convention fixed when taking these derivatives. A shell entropy and a cumulative-volume entropy agree at leading thermodynamic order for the ideal gas, but need not give the same finite-system derivatives. Read the Boltzmann definition before the intensive-variable relations.
