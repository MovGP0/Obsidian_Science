> [!TLDR]
> An isolated system occupies an accessible energy surface or a narrow shell in phase space.

A rigid, impermeable, thermally insulating boundary keeps the volume and particle count fixed and prevents energy exchange. For an autonomous Hamiltonian, an individual trajectory has one exact energy. The finite shell describes the energy tolerance of an ensemble of possible preparations; it is not an energy range swept out by one isolated trajectory.
