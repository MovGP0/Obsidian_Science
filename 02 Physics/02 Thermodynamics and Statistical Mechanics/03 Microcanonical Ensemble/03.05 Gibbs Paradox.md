> [!TLDR]
> Counting permutations of identical particles as new states creates a false mixing entropy.

The diagnostic experiment is to remove a partition between equal samples of the same gas. A counting rule must not assign a bulk entropy increase merely because fictitious particle labels can now cross the former boundary. Mixing different species is a different physical preparation and can have a real entropy increase.

Read the apparent contradiction, identify the duplicate label permutations, then apply the correction and repeat the scaling test. The last step is essential: a proposed counting rule must reproduce the thermodynamic limit.
