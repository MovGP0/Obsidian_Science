> [!TLDR]
> Boltzmann entropy is the logarithm of the number of states compatible with a macrostate.

## Definition 3.4 — Boltzmann entropy

For a system with $\Omega({\energy{E}},{\mechanical{V}},{\mechanical{N}})$ accessible states:

$$
{\thermal{S}}({\energy{E}},{\mechanical{V}},{\mechanical{N}})={\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}\ln\Omega({\energy{E}},{\mechanical{V}},{\mechanical{N}}).
$$

This formula is engraved on Boltzmann's grave in Vienna. It turns the qualitative ideas of disorder or missing microscopic information into a quantitative relation between a macroscopic quantity and the number of compatible microstates.

The count in the logarithm must be dimensionless and must use a fixed energy-window convention. For two weakly interacting systems with separately fixed energies, independent accessible states combine in pairs. Their counts multiply, so the logarithm makes their entropies add. If energy can be exchanged, the total count must instead include the allowed energy divisions.

For a discrete model, one accessible state gives zero entropy in this counting convention; doubling the number of equally probable states adds a fixed entropy increment. This concerns the number of compatible states, not whether a drawing looks visually disordered.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\thermal{S}}$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Entropy | The total thermodynamic entropy or its stated statistical approximation. |
| ${\energy{E}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Energy | The system energy; subscripts identify levels or allocated energy contributions. |
| ${\mechanical{V}}$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Volume | The physical volume of the gas or system. |
| ${\mechanical{N}}$ | $1$ | Particle count | The number of particles, spins, or distinguishable oscillators. |
| ${\thermal{k}_{\mathrm B}}$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Boltzmann constant | The conversion between microscopic counting and thermodynamic entropy. |
| $\Omega$ | $1$ | Accessible-state count | The number or reduced shell count compatible with the constraints. |
