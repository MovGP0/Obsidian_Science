> [!TLDR]
> Each independent classical quadratic energy term contributes half a thermal energy on average.

Count independent quadratic energy terms, rather than spatial coordinates alone. A one-dimensional harmonic oscillator has both kinetic and potential terms; a free particle coordinate has no potential term to count. The average is an equilibrium statement and does not mean that each term has the same energy at every instant.

Read the Gaussian proof for its integration-domain assumptions, apply the term count to gases and solids, then check whether quantum level spacings permit a classical treatment.
