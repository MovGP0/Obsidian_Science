> [!TLDR]
> The Sackur–Tetrode equation gives the entropy of a dilute monatomic ideal gas, including the quantum action scale.

The formula answers how the absolute counting normalization enters the thermodynamic entropy. Two ingredients matter: one action cell per conjugate coordinate pair, and removal of particle-label overcounting. The large-particle-number approximation then gives the bulk expression.

Use the derivation to track these constants rather than only the temperature derivative. Use the properties note to test extensivity and the low-temperature boundary. The formula assumes a monatomic ideal gas with no active internal degeneracy factor.
