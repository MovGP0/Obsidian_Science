title: "Confinement"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Confinement means that quarks and gluons do not occur as isolated physical asymptotic states. Observable QCD states are color singlets.

**Confinement** means that color charges—quarks and gluons—are not observed as free asymptotic states. The observable states in the QCD sector are hadrons, which are color singlets. The source presents confinement as a well-supported QCD prediction and notes that perturbation theory does not provide a rigorous analytic proof.

The key distinction is between short-distance constituents and long-distance observable states. Perturbative amplitudes use quark and gluon fields, but the asymptotic particles in a detector are color-neutral combinations. A colored propagator in a gauge-fixed calculation therefore does not contradict confinement.

Read [[12.07.01 Evidence for Confinement]] to separate the perturbative loss-of-control argument from numerical and observational evidence. Then read [[12.07.02 Theoretical Criteria for Confinement]] for the more precise static-source diagnostic. State the theory and probes before applying that diagnostic: pure gauge theory and QCD with light dynamical quarks need different large-distance qualifications. Color singletness is a constraint on physical states; it is not, by itself, a derivation of the flux-tube energy or hadron spectrum.
