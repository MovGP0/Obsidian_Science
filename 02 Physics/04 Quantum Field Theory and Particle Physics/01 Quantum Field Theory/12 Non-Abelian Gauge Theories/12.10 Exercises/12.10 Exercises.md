> [!TLDR]
> Work through the chapter's exercises with the question, source solution, and correction notes together in each linked article.

Use the first four problems to fix generator normalization and the difference between a representation matrix and a structure constant. Problems 12.5–12.6 then separate the action-dependent field equation from the geometric Bianchi identity. Problems 12.7–12.8 test color components and the order of summing, squaring, and averaging.

Finish with running, propagators, and vertex symmetry. Before calculating 12.10, predict whether a lower scale raises or lowers the coupling. In 12.11, predict which contraction vanishes in Landau gauge without assuming that a transverse scalar is gauge independent. Read the marked source corrections as part of each solution, especially the color-factor mismatch and the fixed-flavor numerical comparison. These checks prevent algebraically correct operations from answering a different physical question.
