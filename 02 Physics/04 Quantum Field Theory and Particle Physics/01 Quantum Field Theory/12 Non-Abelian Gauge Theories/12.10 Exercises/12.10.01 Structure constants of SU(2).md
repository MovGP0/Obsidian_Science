## Exercise 12.1: SU(2) structure constants

Verify explicitly that the $SU(2)$ structure constants are $f^{abc}=\varepsilon^{abc}$.
Calculate
$$
[T^1,T^2]=i\varepsilon^{12c}T^c
$$
using the Pauli-matrix generators $T^a=\sigma^a/2$.

## Solution 12.1 — Structure constants of SU(2)

Predict the sign before multiplying. Reversing the two generator labels must reverse the commutator, so the answer for the reversed pair must be minus the answer for the stated pair. The factor of one half in each generator is essential; using unscaled Pauli matrices would give structure constants twice as large.

After the calculation, check that equal labels give zero and cyclic permutations preserve the sign. These tests fix the complete three-generator algebra in this basis. The result describes internal SU(2) rotations; it does not require the doublet to represent physical spin or ordinary spatial rotations in every application.

Use $T^a=\sigma^a/2$. Then
$$
[T^1,T^2]=\frac14[\sigma^1,\sigma^2].
$$
Direct matrix multiplication gives
$$
\sigma^1\sigma^2
=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}0&-i\\i&0\end{pmatrix}
=\begin{pmatrix}i&0\\0&-i\end{pmatrix}
=i\sigma^3.
$$
Since $\sigma^2\sigma^1=-i\sigma^3$,
$$
[\sigma^1,\sigma^2]=2i\sigma^3.
$$

Thus
$$
[T^1,T^2]=\frac14(2i\sigma^3)=iT^3,
\qquad f^{123}=1.
$$
Antisymmetry and cyclic permutations give $f^{abc}=\varepsilon^{abc}$.
