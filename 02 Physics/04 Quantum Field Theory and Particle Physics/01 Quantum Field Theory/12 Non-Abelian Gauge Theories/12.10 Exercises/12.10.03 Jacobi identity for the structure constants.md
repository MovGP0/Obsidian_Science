## Exercise 12.3: Jacobi identity and structure constants

Show that the structure constants obey
$$
f^{abe}f^{ecd}+f^{bce}f^{ead}+f^{cae}f^{ebd}=0.
$$
Derive this from the Jacobi identity
$$
[[T^a,T^b],T^c]+[[T^b,T^c],T^a]+[[T^c,T^a],T^b]=0.
$$

## Solution 12.3 — Jacobi identity for the structure constants

Start with the operator identity
$$
[[T^a,T^b],T^c]+[[T^b,T^c],T^a]+[[T^c,T^a],T^b]=0.
$$
Using $[T^a,T^b]=if^{abe}T^e$ gives
$$
[[T^a,T^b],T^c]
=if^{abe}[T^e,T^c]
=-f^{abe}f^{ecd}T^d.
$$
The cyclic sum is therefore
$$
-\left(f^{abe}f^{ecd}+f^{bce}f^{ead}+f^{cae}f^{ebd}\right)T^d=0.
$$

In a faithful representation the generators are linearly independent, so
$$
\boxed{f^{abe}f^{ecd}+f^{bce}f^{ead}+f^{cae}f^{ebd}=0.}
$$
This also establishes that $(T_{\mathrm{adj}}^a)_{bc}=-if^{abc}$ obey the Lie-algebra commutation relations.

There are two separate steps in the proof. Associativity first makes the cyclic sum of nested commutators vanish: each ordered triple matrix product occurs once with each sign. Linear independence then permits each generator coefficient to be set to zero. A nonfaithful matrix representation can hide algebra elements, so coefficient comparison should be done in the algebra itself or in a faithful representation as stated.

The two commutator substitutions produce two factors of $i$ and hence the minus sign before the coefficient sum. That sign cancels when the equation is set to zero. As a check, substitute the SU(2) Levi-Civita constants or set all constants to zero; both satisfy the identity. This is algebraic consistency, not a field equation.
