> [!TLDR]
> The Yang–Mills Lagrangian fixes three-gluon and four-gluon vertices. Their Lorentz and color structure follows from the non-Abelian field-strength term.

The self-interaction vertices follow from (12.6).

![[QFT-12-01-Three-Gluon-Vertex.svg|**Fig. 12.1** Three-gluon vertex. All momenta are incoming: p + q + r = 0.]]

## Three-gluon vertex

The Lagrangian density (12.6) gives the following Feynman rule, with all momenta $\mechanical{p}^\mu,\mechanical{q}^\nu,\mechanical{r}^\rho$ incoming:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\signal{V}_3^{\mu\nu\rho}(\mechanical{p},\mechanical{q},\mechanical{r})
=\signal{g}f^{abc}\big[
&g^{\mu\nu}(\mechanical{p}-\mechanical{q})^\rho
+g^{\nu\rho}(\mechanical{q}-\mechanical{r})^\mu\\
&+g^{\rho\mu}(\mechanical{r}-\mechanical{p})^\nu
\big],
\end{aligned}
\tag{12.7}
$$
subject to $\mechanical{p}+\mechanical{q}+\mechanical{r}=0$.

![[QFT-12-02-Four-Gluon-Vertex.svg|**Fig. 12.2** Four-gluon vertex in non-Abelian SU(N) gauge theory.]]

## Four-gluon vertex

The four-gluon vertex shown in Fig. 12.2 is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\signal{V}_4^{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}
=-i\signal{g}^2\big[
&f^{abe}f^{cde}
(g^{\mu\rho}g^{\nu\sigma}-g^{\mu\sigma}g^{\nu\rho})\\
&+f^{ace}f^{bde}
(g^{\mu\nu}g^{\rho\sigma}-g^{\mu\sigma}g^{\nu\rho})\\
&+f^{ade}f^{bce}
(g^{\mu\nu}g^{\rho\sigma}-g^{\mu\rho}g^{\nu\sigma})
\big].
\end{aligned}
\tag{12.8}
$$
Each color structure multiplies an antisymmetric Lorentz tensor.
The three channels $(s,t,u)$ correspond to the three ways to pair the four color indices.

The two diagrams encode different terms of the same action, not two independent interaction strengths. The cubic term has one derivative and therefore one momentum in its rule; the quartic term has none. This gives the energy dimensions listed below. All-incoming momenta are bookkeeping variables: an outgoing physical momentum enters these rules with its sign reversed.

A Bose-symmetry check must exchange color, Lorentz index, and momentum together. In the cubic rule, exchanging two legs reverses both the structure constant and the momentum tensor, so the product is unchanged. Setting all structure constants to zero removes both vertices and reduces the field equation below to independent vacuum Maxwell equations.

## Example 12.2: Classical Yang–Mills equations of motion

Apply the Euler–Lagrange equations to
$\energy{\mathcal{L}}_{\mathrm{YM}}
=-\tfrac14\signal{F}_{\mu\nu}^a\signal{F}^{a\mu\nu}$
with respect to $\signal{A}_\mu^a$:
$$
D_\nu^{ab}\signal{F}^{b\mu\nu}=0.
$$
The adjoint derivative is
$$
(D_\nu^{\mathrm{adj}})^{ab}
=\partial_\nu\delta^{ab}
+\signal{g}f^{acb}\signal{A}_\nu^c.
$$
In matrix notation,
$$
[D_\nu,\signal{F}^{\mu\nu}]=0.
$$
This is the non-Abelian counterpart of the vacuum Maxwell equation
$\partial_\nu\electric{F}^{\mu\nu}=0$.
Even without external sources, the Yang–Mills equations are nonlinear.
The term $\signal{g}f^{abc}\signal{A}_\nu^b\signal{F}^{c\mu\nu}$ describes the color source carried by the gluons themselves.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{g}$ | $1$ | Gauge coupling | The coupling in the three-gluon rule. |
| $\mechanical{p},\mechanical{q},\mechanical{r}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Gluon four-momenta | All momenta enter the three-gluon vertex. |
| $\signal{V}_3^{\mu\nu\rho}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Three-gluon vertex factor | The momentum-dependent interaction factor with color labels a, b, c. |
| $\signal{V}_4^{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}$ | $1$ | Four-gluon vertex factor | The momentum-independent Lorentz and color interaction factor. |
| $\energy{\mathcal{L}}_{\mathrm{YM}}$ | $\unit{MeV^4}$ | Yang–Mills Lagrangian density | The action density varied for the classical field equations. |
| $\signal{F}_{\mu\nu},\signal{F}_{\mu\nu}^a$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Yang–Mills field strength | The matrix tensor and its adjoint components. |
| $\signal{A}_\mu^a$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Gauge field | The gluon field in the adjoint covariant derivative. |
| $\electric{F}^{\mu\nu}$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Electromagnetic field strength | The Abelian tensor in the vacuum Maxwell equation. |
