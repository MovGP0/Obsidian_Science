---
title: "The WST Identity for the Propagator"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The gluon self-energy is transverse as a consequence of BRST symmetry. This excludes a local Proca mass counterterm in perturbative Yang–Mills theory.

The gluon self-energy obeys the transversality condition
$$
\mechanical{k}^\mu\signal{\Pi}_{\mu\nu}^{ab}(\mechanical{k})=0.
$$
The source states that this keeps the gluon massless and prevents quantum corrections from generating a mass term.
The condition follows directly from BRST invariance.

> [!note]
> In perturbation theory, the identity excludes an independent local gauge-boson mass counterterm. Transversality by itself does not prove that an isolated massless gluon is a physical asymptotic state, nor does it exclude every possible nonperturbative infrared mass scale.

For a Lorentz-invariant, color-symmetric perturbative vacuum, write a two-point tensor as a metric term plus a term containing two momenta. Contraction with the external momentum relates their coefficients and leaves the transverse combination used in [[12.06.01 Structure of the One-Loop Calculation]]. This argument restricts the tensor structure, but does not fix its scalar function.

A local Proca mass term would contribute a momentum-independent metric tensor to the inverse propagator. Its contraction is nonzero for generic momentum, which shows why that independent counterterm is incompatible with the identity. A dynamically generated infrared scale is a different question and must not be ruled out by this local-counterterm test. The statement assumes consistent gauge and ghost contributions and an anomaly-free symmetry.
