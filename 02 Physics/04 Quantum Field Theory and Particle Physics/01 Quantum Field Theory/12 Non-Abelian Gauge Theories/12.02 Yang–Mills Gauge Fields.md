---
title: "Yang–Mills Gauge Fields"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Yang–Mills theory extends local gauge invariance to non-Abelian groups and introduces gauge fields that interact with one another.

The construction begins with [[12.02.01 Local Non-Abelian Gauge Invariance|local non-Abelian transformations of matter fields]].

Read the construction in three steps. [[12.02.01 Local Non-Abelian Gauge Invariance]] identifies the unwanted derivative of a local rotation. [[12.02.02 Transformation of Gauge Fields]] selects the connection law that cancels it. [[12.02.03 Field Strength Tensor]] then measures the failure of two covariant derivatives to commute. The curvature transforms without the connection's derivative term, so its traced square supplies the action in [[12.02.04 The Yang–Mills Lagrangian]].

This order separates a covariance requirement from a choice of dynamics. Gauge covariance fixes how fields transform; the stated minimal Yang–Mills action specifies how they evolve. As a check, set the structure constants to zero: the curvature becomes Abelian and the pure gauge-boson self-interactions disappear.
