---
title: "Ghost Fields"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Anticommuting scalar ghost fields express the Faddeev–Popov determinant as a fermionic Gaussian functional integral.

Write the FP determinant as a fermionic Gaussian functional integral by introducing anticommuting scalar fields, called **ghost fields**.

The Faddeev–Popov fields give
$$
\det(M)=\int\mathcal{D}\bar{\signal{c}}\,\mathcal{D}\signal{c}\,
\exp\left(
i\int d^4\mechanical{x}\,\bar{\signal{c}}^aM^{ab}\signal{c}^b
\right),
$$
up to a field-independent normalization.
The fields $\signal{c}^a(\mechanical{x})$ and $\bar{\signal{c}}^a(\mechanical{x})$ are anticommuting scalars: they have spin zero and Fermi statistics.
They do not occur as physical asymptotic states.

The ghost Lagrangian density is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\energy{\mathcal{L}}_{\mathrm{ghost}}
&=\bar{\signal{c}}^a
(-\partial^\mu D_\mu^{\mathrm{adj},ab})\signal{c}^b\\
&=\partial^\mu\bar{\signal{c}}^a\,\partial_\mu\signal{c}^a
+\signal{g}f^{abc}(\partial^\mu\bar{\signal{c}}^a)
\signal{A}_\mu^b\signal{c}^c.
\end{aligned}
\tag{12.10}
$$
The second equality holds up to a total derivative, after integration by parts in the action.
The second term gives the **ghost–gluon vertex**:
a gluon with color index $b$ couples to the pair
$(\bar{\signal{c}}^a,\signal{c}^c)$ with color-coupling factor $\signal{g}f^{abc}$.

> [!note]
> The source calls the ghosts a violation of the spin–statistics theorem. The theorem applies to physical states under its positivity assumptions. Auxiliary ghost fields lie outside that physical state space, so their scalar anticommutation does not violate the physical theorem.

The determinant identity follows from Grassmann integration: each independent pair of anticommuting integration variables selects a matrix element, and the permutation signs produce a determinant. A commuting Gaussian would instead give an inverse determinant. Ghosts therefore keep the gauge-fixing Jacobian in the perturbation series; they are not new detectable scalar matter.

Integration by parts assumes boundary terms vanish. It places the derivative on the antighost and fixes which momentum enters the ghost vertex. Each ghost has mass dimension one in the symmetric four-dimensional normalization, so the kinetic and cubic ghost densities both have dimension four. In the Abelian limit the structure constants vanish, the ghost interaction disappears, and the remaining free determinant cancels from normalized physical quantities.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{c}^a,\bar{\signal{c}}^a$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Ghost and antighost fields | Auxiliary anticommuting scalar fields in the adjoint representation. |
| $\mechanical{x}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Spacetime coordinate | The position in the ghost functional integral. |
