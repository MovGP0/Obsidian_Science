---
title: "Local Non-Abelian Gauge Invariance"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Matter fields in the fundamental representation transform as vectors under SU(N). Local transformations require a gauge-covariant derivative.

Consider a matter field $\signal{\psi}(\mechanical{x})$ in the fundamental representation of $G=SU(N)$.
The field is a column vector with $N$ components, each of which is a spinor field.
A global gauge transformation is
$$
\signal{\psi}(\mechanical{x})\to U\signal{\psi}(\mechanical{x}),
\qquad U\in SU(N),
\qquad U\text{ constant}.
$$

The free Dirac Lagrangian density
$\bar{\signal{\psi}}(i\not{\partial}-\mechanical{m})\signal{\psi}$
is invariant under a global transformation.
As in QED, promote the symmetry to a local one, $U=U(\mechanical{x})$.
The ordinary derivative transforms as
$$
\partial_\mu\signal{\psi}
\to U(\mechanical{x})\partial_\mu\signal{\psi}
+\left(\partial_\mu U(\mechanical{x})\right)\signal{\psi}.
$$
The global invariance assumes that the free mass term is proportional to the identity in this internal multiplet. Spinor and color indices describe separate spaces; the group matrices act on color, while the Dirac matrices act on spin. Promoting the transformation to a local redundancy is a model-building requirement here, not a consequence of the free equation alone.

The connection compares internal vectors at neighboring points. Its task is precise: cancel the extra derivative-of-transformation term without changing the transformation of the matter field itself. In four-dimensional natural units, a derivative and the connection times its dimensionless coupling both have one power of energy, so they can be added in the following expression.

The extra term breaks local invariance.
Introduce a connection through the covariant derivative
$$
D_\mu=\partial_\mu-i\signal{g}\signal{A}_\mu^a(\mechanical{x})T^a
\equiv\partial_\mu-i\signal{g}\signal{A}_\mu(\mechanical{x}),
$$
where the gauge-field matrix is
$$
\signal{A}_\mu(\mechanical{x})\equiv\signal{A}_\mu^a(\mechanical{x})T^a.
$$
The fields $\signal{A}_\mu^a$ are the gauge-boson fields.
For $G=SU(3)$, they describe the eight gluons.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{\psi}$ | $\unit{MeV^{3/2}}$ | Matter spinor | The N-component multiplet in the fundamental representation in four dimensions. |
| $\mechanical{x}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Spacetime coordinate | The position of the matter field. |
| $\bar{\signal{\psi}}$ | $\unit{MeV^{3/2}}$ | Dirac adjoint | The adjoint matter field in the invariant Lagrangian density. |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Matter-field mass | The mass in the Dirac Lagrangian. |
