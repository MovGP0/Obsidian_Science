---
title: "Ghost–Gluon Vertex"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The ghost–gluon vertex depends on the strong coupling, the color structure constant, and the momentum assigned to the outgoing ghost line.

The Faddeev–Popov action contains a derivative coupling between a ghost, an antighost, and a gluon. This selects one oriented ghost-line momentum rather than a symmetric difference of both ghost momenta. The fields are Grassmann odd even though the Lorentz structure is scalar.

Let $\mechanical{p}^\mu$ be the momentum of the outgoing ghost line, that is, the ghost line that leaves the vertex.
The color indices $a,b,c$ refer to the incoming ghost, outgoing ghost, and gluon, respectively.
With the source's ordering,
$$
\bar c^{\,b}c^a g_\mu^c:\qquad
-\signal{g}_sf^{abc}\mechanical{p}^\mu.
\tag{C.36}
$$
Keep this momentum and ghost-line orientation convention when applying the rule.

This outgoing-momentum convention is an exception to the appendix's default all-incoming convention. If the same oriented leg is instead labelled by an incoming momentum, substitute its negative in (C.36). Keep the color order unchanged during that substitution. Reversing the color order is a separate operation and can introduce another sign from the antisymmetric structure constant. Thus a sign comparison with another table requires its momentum and ghost/antighost conventions, not just its printed coefficient.

The coefficient has one energy dimension and vanishes with the selected momentum, with $\signal{g}_s$, or with the structure constant. Pair the rule with the ghost propagator and the minus sign for a closed ghost loop. Ghost loops contribute to cancellation of unphysical covariant-gauge degrees of freedom; the oriented ghost lines are never physical external particles. The source's outgoing-line convention is retained here rather than converted silently.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{g}_s$ | $1$ | Strong gauge coupling | The coefficient of the ghost–gluon interaction. |
| $\mechanical{p}^\mu$ | $\unit{GeV}$ | Outgoing ghost-line momentum | The momentum selected by the source's vertex convention. |
