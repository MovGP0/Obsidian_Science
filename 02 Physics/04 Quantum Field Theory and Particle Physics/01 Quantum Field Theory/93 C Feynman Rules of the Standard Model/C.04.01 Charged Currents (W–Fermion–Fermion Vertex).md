---
title: "Charged Currents (W–Fermion–Fermion Vertex)"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The W boson couples to left-chiral fermion currents. Quark and massive-neutrino mass bases introduce CKM and PMNS mixing factors.

A W interaction changes one member of a weak doublet into the other. The displayed factor equals a weak coupling divided by $\sqrt2$ times $\gamma^\mu P_L$; the factor one half is already contained in the projector. The Lorentz index contracts with the W line. Keep its charge and the fermion-arrow ordering together when selecting the rule or its Hermitian conjugate.

## Leptons

Without lepton mixing, as in the massless-neutrino limit, the rule is
$$
W_\mu^+\bar\nu_e\ell:\qquad
+\frac{i\signal{g}}{2\sqrt2}\gamma^\mu(1-\gamma^5).
\tag{C.14}
$$
For massive neutrinos in the mass basis, include the PMNS mixing matrix $\signal{U}_{\ell i}$ with the conjugation required by the chosen W charge and fermion ordering.

> [!note]
> Equation (C.14) displays the source's unmixed vertex. A charged lepton and neutrino flavor must belong to the same weak doublet before changing to the neutrino mass basis.

## Quarks

With $\signal{V}_{ud}\equiv\signal{V}_{u_i d_j}$ from the CKM matrix,
$$
W_\mu^+\bar u_i d_j:\qquad
+\frac{i\signal{g}}{2\sqrt2}\gamma^\mu(1-\gamma^5)
\signal{V}_{u_i d_j}.
\tag{C.15}
$$
The conjugate $W_\mu^-\bar d_j u_i$ vertex uses $\signal{V}_{u_i d_j}^*$.

For the displayed quark ordering, the charges sum to zero: a positive W, an anti-up field, and a down field carry $+1$, $-2/3$, and $-1/3$. This is a useful orientation check before crossing a leg. The Hermitian-conjugate vertex conjugates the mixing element as well as reversing the charged-field assignment.

All coefficients here are dimensionless. In a basis with no intergeneration mixing, the CKM matrix becomes the identity and the current joins only paired doublet members. In the massless limit the projector removes a right-chiral fermion input; do not replace this statement by a universal helicity rule for a massive particle. To test the convention, reconstruct the allowed charge assignments for a positive W decay into an up-type quark and a down-type antiquark, then cross the outgoing legs into the displayed all-incoming field ordering.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{g}$ | $1$ | Weak gauge coupling | The coefficient of the charged-current vertex. |
| $\signal{V}_{u_i d_j}$ | $1$ | CKM matrix element | The quark flavor-mixing factor. |
| $\signal{U}_{\ell i}$ | $1$ | PMNS matrix element | The lepton flavor-mixing factor for neutrino mass eigenstates. |
