---
title: "Higgs–Gauge-Boson Vertices"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Single-Higgs gauge vertices are proportional to a weak-boson mass, while double-Higgs gauge vertices are proportional to the squared weak coupling.

The Higgs kinetic term gives both the weak-boson masses and their coupling to the physical Higgs fluctuation. Expanding its vacuum-dependent mass terms to first and second order in that fluctuation gives the single- and double-Higgs rules below. Thus these coefficients test the mass-generation structure of the single-doublet model.

The Higgs–gauge-boson vertices are
$$
hW_\mu^+W_\nu^-:\qquad
+i\signal{g}\mechanical{m}_Wg^{\mu\nu},
\tag{C.27}
$$
$$
hZ_\mu Z_\nu:\qquad
+i\frac{\signal{g}\mechanical{m}_Z}{\cos\signal{\theta}_W}g^{\mu\nu}
=+i\frac{\signal{g}\mechanical{m}_Z^2}{\mechanical{m}_W}g^{\mu\nu},
\tag{C.28}
$$
$$
hhW_\mu^+W_\nu^-:\qquad
+i\frac{\signal{g}^2}{2}g^{\mu\nu},
\tag{C.29}
$$
$$
hhZ_\mu Z_\nu:\qquad
+i\frac{\signal{g}^2}{2\cos^2\signal{\theta}_W}g^{\mu\nu}.
\tag{C.30}
$$

For the neutral Z mass term, the Lagrangian contains the usual one-half factor for identical real vector fields. Differentiation with respect to the two Z fields removes that factor. Differentiation with respect to two identical Higgs fields also supplies a factor of two for the double-Higgs rules. These factors are already included in (C.27)–(C.30).

The single-Higgs coefficient has one energy dimension, whereas the double-Higgs coefficient is dimensionless. This matches three versus four boson fields in a four-dimensional interaction density. The Lorentz tensors apply to all physical W and Z polarizations, including longitudinal ones; the external polarizations still have to be contracted with the listed indices. The W pair has opposite charges, and the Higgs and Z are neutral.

An on-shell Higgs decay into two real weak bosons requires its mass to exceed twice the corresponding vector mass. With the source's Higgs benchmark, this condition is not met for either W or Z. The vertices still describe decays with a virtual vector and vector-boson fusion. There is no direct Higgs–photon–photon vertex in this tree-level list; loop amplitudes are a different perturbative contribution.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{g}$ | $1$ | Weak gauge coupling | The gauge coefficient in the Higgs–vector vertices. |
| $\mechanical{m}_W,\mechanical{m}_Z$ | $\unit{GeV}$ | Weak-boson masses | The masses in the single-Higgs vertex coefficients. |
| $\signal{\theta}_W$ | $1$ | Weak mixing angle | The angle in the Z-boson vertex coefficients. |
