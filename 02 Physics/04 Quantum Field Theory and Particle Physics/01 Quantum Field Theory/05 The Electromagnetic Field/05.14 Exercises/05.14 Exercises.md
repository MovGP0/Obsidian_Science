> [!TLDR]
> Work through the chapter's exercises with the question, source solution, and correction notes together in each linked article.

Use Exercises 5.1–5.2 to connect tensor signs to measurable field energy and flux. Exercises 5.3–5.4 then test how transverse modes are selected. Complete 5.5 before using the covariant state space: satisfying the constraint is stronger than guessing a cancellation of norms.

Exercises 5.6–5.8 connect the mode description to propagators and scattering. The useful checks are the pole boundary prescription, the massive rest-frame count, and the cancellation between both Compton diagrams. Exercises 5.9–5.10 return to constrained Hamiltonians and vacuum ordering.

Attempt each derivation before reading its solution. Where a source correction is marked, use the stated sign, norm or conservation test to decide which expression is consistent. In particular, a desired two-mode answer does not justify dropping a minus sign from an indefinite-metric commutator.
