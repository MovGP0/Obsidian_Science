> [!TLDR]
> Transverse and longitudinal projectors give a direct derivation of the covariant Rξ-gauge photon propagator.

This section asks how to invert a tensor operator without guessing its components. Work off shell at finite nonzero gauge parameter, separate the four-momentum transverse and longitudinal sectors, and invert each scalar coefficient. The Feynman pole prescription then supplies the time-ordering boundary condition.

The second article checks what the inversion means in a process: the gauge-dependent sector vanishes between on-shell conserved electron currents. Changing the gauge parameter changes the auxiliary potential correlator, not an experimental control setting. Reconstruct the Feynman-gauge numerator by setting the parameter to one before using the result in a scattering amplitude.
