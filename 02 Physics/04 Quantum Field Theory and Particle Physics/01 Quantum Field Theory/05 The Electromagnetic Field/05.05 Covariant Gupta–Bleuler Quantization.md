title: "Covariant Gupta–Bleuler Quantization"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Gupta–Bleuler quantization keeps Lorentz covariance by quantizing four photon polarizations and selecting physical states afterward.

This construction asks how to keep Lorentz indices explicit without interpreting every potential component as a photon. It first enlarges the free-field description and then restricts its state space. The gauge-fixing term permits an invertible kinetic operator; it is not a new electromagnetic interaction.

Read the momentum and four-polarization expansion together. Their relative metric signs produce a timelike negative-norm sector. The physical-state condition then selects a nonnegative subspace, and quotienting its null states gives the physical Hilbert space. Restriction and quotient are separate steps.

A first check is that the vacuum and arbitrary transverse-photon states satisfy the condition. A second is that a timelike one-photon state alone does not. These algebraic checks concern the free construction; an interacting calculation must also preserve the physical subspace.
