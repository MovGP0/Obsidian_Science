---
title: "Quantization of the Proca Field"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The Proca field expands into two transverse polarizations and one longitudinal polarization.

The normal-mode expansion uses two transverse vectors $\signal{\epsilon}^{\mu(\lambda)}$, $\lambda=1,2$, and a longitudinal vector $\signal{\epsilon}^{\mu(L)}$.

For $\mechanical{k}^\mu=(\energy{E}_{\mathbf k},\mechanical{\mathbf k})$, with $\energy{E}_{\mathbf k}=\sqrt{\lvert\mechanical{\mathbf k}\rvert^2+\mechanical{m}^2}$, the longitudinal polarization is
$$
\signal{\epsilon}^{\mu(L)}(\mechanical{k})
=\frac1{\mechanical{m}}
\left(\lvert\mechanical{\mathbf k}\rvert,
\energy{E}_{\mathbf k}\hat{\mechanical{\mathbf k}}\right).
$$

The longitudinal vector satisfies $\mechanical{k}_\mu\signal{\epsilon}^{\mu(L)}=0$ and $\signal{\epsilon}^{(L)}\cdot\signal{\epsilon}^{(L)}=-1$. To check both signs, the contraction with momentum subtracts two equal products, while the norm is the spatial momentum squared minus the energy squared, divided by the mass squared. The dispersion relation makes that norm minus one.

At rest, the momentum direction is undefined, so choose any spin axis before taking the rest limit of this formula. The longitudinal vector then becomes an ordinary unit spatial spin direction. It is a physical state at every positive mass.

The on-shell completeness relation for the three physical polarizations is
$$
\sum_\lambda
\signal{\epsilon}^{\mu(\lambda)}
\signal{\epsilon}^{\nu(\lambda)*}
=-g^{\mu\nu}
+\frac{\mechanical{k}^\mu\mechanical{k}^\nu}{\mechanical{m}^2}. \tag{5.29}
$$
The longitudinal term has an explicit $1/\mechanical{m}^2$ singularity. The massless limit is not uniform at the level of polarization vectors. For conserved-current couplings, the leading momentum-proportional contribution decouples. In taking this limit, hold nonzero spatial momentum fixed and require the current components to remain bounded. The singular vector is then almost proportional to the four-momentum, whose current contraction vanishes. This is a statement about a specified coupling limit, not permission to substitute zero mass into the vector or completeness relation.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{\epsilon}^{\mu(\lambda)},\signal{\epsilon}^{\mu(L)}$ | $1$ | Polarization vectors | The transverse and longitudinal massive-vector polarizations. |
| $\mechanical{k},\mechanical{\mathbf k}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Vector-boson momentum | The on-shell four-momentum and its spatial part. |
| $\energy{E}_{\mathbf k}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Mode energy | The positive energy of the massive vector mode. |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Vector mass | The positive mass of the Proca field. |
