title: "The Photon Propagator"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The photon propagator is a time-ordered two-point function whose momentum-space form follows from the gauge-fixed kinetic operator.

The propagator answers which Green function represents a free photon field inside a time-ordered calculation. It is not a probability for a photon to move between two measured points. Because it correlates potentials, its individual components depend on gauge.

Read the Feynman-gauge derivation to see how gauge fixing makes the kinetic operator invertible and how the pole prescription fixes boundary conditions. Then compare Coulomb gauge, where the scalar constraint and transverse radiation appear in separate terms. The useful comparison is their contraction with conserved sources, not equality of every tensor component.

A free internal line includes off-shell momentum. Counting the indices on that line as observable photon polarizations would confuse the auxiliary calculation with the two on-shell radiation states.
