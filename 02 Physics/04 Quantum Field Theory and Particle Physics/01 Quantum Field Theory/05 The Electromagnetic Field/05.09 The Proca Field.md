---
title: "The Proca Field"
---

> [!TLDR]
> A Proca mass term gives a vector field three physical polarizations and a mass-dependent longitudinal propagator term.

The Proca model describes a free massive spin-one field in flat spacetime. Its mass term removes Maxwell gauge redundancy, but the field equations still constrain one component. The aim is to see why the remaining longitudinal mode is physical.

Start with the divergence of the equation of motion: the nonzero mass makes four-transversality a consequence, rather than a gauge choice. Next construct the three normalized polarizations and check them in the rest frame. Finally invert the kinetic operator to understand why the propagator contains an inverse mass squared. The propagator warns about some interacting high-energy limits; the free model alone does not determine an interacting theory's ultraviolet consistency.
