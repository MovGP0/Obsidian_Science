---
title: "The Gauge-Fixing Term"
---

> [!TLDR]
> A covariant gauge-fixing term changes the Maxwell kinetic operator so that all potential components can be quantized.

To retain manifest Lorentz covariance, add a gauge-fixing term:
$$
\energy{\mathcal L}
=-\frac14\electric{F}_{\mu\nu}\electric{F}^{\mu\nu}
-\frac1{2\xi}(\partial_\mu\electric{A}^\mu)^2. \tag{5.13}
$$

The gauge parameter $\xi$ is positive and real in this treatment. It labels the auxiliary description, not a measurable property of the photon. The added term changes the equations on the enlarged space of potentials, so a physical-state condition is still required to recover Maxwell radiation states.

- $\xi=1$: **Feynman gauge**.
- $\xi\to0$: **Landau gauge**. The Lorenz condition $\partial_\mu\electric{A}^\mu=0$ is imposed as a limiting condition.
- $\xi\to\infty$: The gauge-fixing term vanishes and the unfixed Maxwell Lagrangian is recovered.

The Euler–Lagrange equations are
$$
\Box\electric{A}^\nu
-\left(1-\frac1\xi\right)\partial^\nu(\partial_\mu\electric{A}^\mu)=0. \tag{5.14}
$$
In Feynman gauge, they reduce to
$$
\Box\electric{A}^\nu=0. \tag{5.15}
$$
Each of the four potential components then satisfies a wave equation without an additional constraint at this stage. This is a check on the coefficient in (5.14): at $\xi=1$ the mixed derivative term cancels exactly. It does not establish four physical polarizations.

The two endpoint gauges must be treated as limits. At $\xi=0$ the displayed density has a divergent coefficient; at infinite $\xi$ the original Maxwell gauge degeneracy returns and prevents direct inversion. Neither endpoint can be treated as an ordinary finite-parameter canonical calculation without checking the constraint or limiting prescription.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\energy{\mathcal L}$ | $\unit{MeV^4}$ | Lagrangian density | The Maxwell density with covariant gauge fixing. |
| $\electric{F}_{\mu\nu}$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Field tensor | The electromagnetic field strength. |
| $\electric{A}^\mu$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Four-potential | The four covariantly quantized field components. |
