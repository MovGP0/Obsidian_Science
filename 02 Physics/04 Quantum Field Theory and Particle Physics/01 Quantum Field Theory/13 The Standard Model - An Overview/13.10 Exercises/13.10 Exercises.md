> [!TLDR]
> Work through the chapter's exercises with the question, source solution, and correction notes together in each linked article.

Use Exercises 13.1 and 13.2 as a pair: first count physical Goldstone modes of a global symmetry, then follow the same scalar coordinates into a gauge theory. Exercises 13.3, 13.4, and 13.9 connect vertices and input conventions to measurable widths and cross sections. Exercises 13.5 and 13.10 separate consistency of a gauge symmetry from possible violation of a global charge.

Exercises 13.6 and 13.7 test the doublet Higgs structure through mass and coupling relations. Exercise 13.8 changes the field content and asks which assumptions permit a heavy scale to be inferred from a light mass. Before reading each solution, predict a zero-coupling, threshold, or symmetry limit. After the calculation, state which measured input was used and which relation remains a prediction. Keep the supplied numerical benchmarks and the explicit source corrections with the solution.
