---
title: "Charge Conjugation (C)"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Charge conjugation exchanges particles and antiparticles while preserving four-momentum and spin.

The field transforms as

$$
\mathcal C\signal{\psi}(\mechanical{x})\mathcal C^{-1}=\eta_C C\overline{\signal{\psi}}^{T}(\mechanical{x}). \tag{4.54}
$$

The charge-conjugation matrix satisfies

$$
C\gamma^{\mu}C^{-1}=-(\gamma^{\mu})^{T},\qquad C^{\dagger}=C^{-1},\qquad C^{T}=-C. \tag{4.55}
$$

In the Dirac–Pauli representation, $C=i\gamma^{2}\gamma^{0}$. Its mode action is

$$
\mathcal C b^{s}_{\mathbf p}\mathcal C^{-1}=\eta_C d^{s}_{\mathbf p},\qquad
\mathcal C d^{s}_{\mathbf p}\mathcal C^{-1}=\eta_C^{*}b^{s}_{\mathbf p}. \tag{4.56}
$$

The charge current changes sign:

$$
\mathcal C\electric{J}^{\mu}\mathcal C^{-1}=-\electric{J}^{\mu}, \tag{4.57}
$$

as expected for a particle-minus-antiparticle quantity.

Distinguish the unitary operator on Fock space, written with a calligraphic C, from the numerical matrix C acting on spinor indices. Complex conjugation enters the latter field construction through the transposed adjoint; it does not make the state-space charge-conjugation operator antiunitary.

The simple mode interchange in (4.56) presumes compatible particle and antiparticle spin bases and phases. Arbitrary independent choices of the two-component bases in (4.7)–(4.8) can insert a spin-basis matrix into this relation. To check a convention, charge-conjugate an explicit rest spinor and identify its image in the selected antiparticle basis before assigning the same spin label.

The current's minus sign checks the physical charge exchange, while unchanged spacetime arguments check that C is not a coordinate reversal. In an electromagnetic interaction the photon field must transform as well; a transformation of the matter field alone is not a test of the full action's symmetry.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{\psi},\overline{\signal{\psi}}$ | $\unit{MeV^{3/2}}$ | Dirac field and adjoint | The fields related by charge conjugation. |
| $\mechanical{x}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Spacetime position | The unchanged field event. |
| $\eta_C$ | $1$ | Charge-conjugation phase | The intrinsic transformation phase. |
| $\electric{J}^{\mu}$ | $\unit{MeV^{3}}$ | Charge current | The current odd under charge conjugation. |
