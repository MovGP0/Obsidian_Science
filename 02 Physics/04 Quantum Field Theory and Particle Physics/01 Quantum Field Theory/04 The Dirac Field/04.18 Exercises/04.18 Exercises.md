> [!TLDR]
> Work through the chapter's exercises with the question, source solution, and correction notes together in each linked article.

Use Exercises 4.1–4.3 to check algebra and state counting before calculating a Green's function. Exercises 4.4–4.6 then test Fourier normalization, charge, and conjugation. Exercises 4.7–4.9 connect the spinor algebra to currents and parity-sensitive amplitudes. Finish with Exercises 4.10–4.12, where field labels, physical states, and causal distributions must be distinguished.

For each exercise, identify the convention that could change a sign or factor before reading its solution. Pay particular attention to the source corrections in 4.1, 4.9, 4.10, and 4.11: agreement with a printed result is not a sufficient check when the question or proof contains a stated error.
