## Exercise 4.5 — Noether current and charge

Verify directly that $\electric{J}^\mu=\bar{\signal{\psi}}\gamma^\mu\signal{\psi}$ is conserved on shell. Insert mode expansions (4.27)–(4.28) into
$$
\electric{Q}=\int d^3\mechanical{\mathbf x}\,\electric{J}^0
$$
and use spinor orthogonality to derive (4.40).

## Solution 4.5 — Noether current and charge

### On-shell conservation

For $\electric{J}^\mu=\bar{\signal{\psi}}\gamma^\mu\signal{\psi}$,
$$
\partial_\mu\electric{J}^\mu
=(\partial_\mu\bar{\signal{\psi}})\gamma^\mu\signal{\psi}
+\bar{\signal{\psi}}\gamma^\mu\partial_\mu\signal{\psi}.
$$
The Dirac equation and its adjoint give
$$
\gamma^\mu\partial_\mu\signal{\psi}=-i\mechanical{m}\signal{\psi},
\qquad
(\partial_\mu\bar{\signal{\psi}})\gamma^\mu
=i\mechanical{m}\bar{\signal{\psi}}.
$$
Thus
$$
\partial_\mu\electric{J}^\mu
=i\mechanical{m}\bar{\signal{\psi}}\signal{\psi}
-i\mechanical{m}\bar{\signal{\psi}}\signal{\psi}=0.
$$

### Charge in the mode basis

Insert (4.27)–(4.28) into
$$
\electric{Q}=\int d^3\mechanical{\mathbf x}\,
\signal{\psi}^\dagger\signal{\psi}.
$$
Spatial integration produces $(2\pi)^3\delta^{(3)}(\mechanical{\mathbf p}-\mechanical{\mathbf q})$ or $(2\pi)^3\delta^{(3)}(\mechanical{\mathbf p}+\mechanical{\mathbf q})$, according to the exponentials. The mixed terms vanish by spinor orthogonality. As in Solution 4.2, the remaining terms are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\electric{Q}
=\int\frac{d^3\mechanical{\mathbf p}}{(2\pi)^3}
\frac1{2\energy{E}_{\mathbf p}}
\sum_{s,s'}\Bigl[
&b_{\mathbf p}^{\,s\dagger}b_{\mathbf p}^{\,s'}
u^\dagger(\mechanical{\mathbf p},s)u(\mechanical{\mathbf p},s')\\
+&d_{\mathbf p}^{\,s}d_{\mathbf p}^{\,s'\dagger}
v^\dagger(\mechanical{\mathbf p},s)v(\mechanical{\mathbf p},s')
\Bigr].
\end{aligned}
$$

Before the orthogonality relations are applied, the mixed contribution to the same integrand is
$$
\begin{aligned}
&b_{\mathbf p}^{\,s\dagger}d_{-\mathbf p}^{\,s'\dagger}
u^\dagger(\mechanical{\mathbf p},s)v(-\mechanical{\mathbf p},s')
e^{2i\energy{E}_{\mathbf p}\mechanical{t}}\\
&\quad+
d_{\mathbf p}^{\,s}b_{-\mathbf p}^{\,s'}
v^\dagger(\mechanical{\mathbf p},s)u(-\mechanical{\mathbf p},s')
e^{-2i\energy{E}_{\mathbf p}\mechanical{t}}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Here the reflected on-shell four-momentum is $(\energy{E}_{\mathbf p},-\mechanical{\mathbf p})$. Use
$$
u^\dagger(\mechanical{\mathbf p},s)u(\mechanical{\mathbf p},s')
=v^\dagger(\mechanical{\mathbf p},s)v(\mechanical{\mathbf p},s')
=2\energy{E}_{\mathbf p}\delta_{ss'},
$$
$$
u^\dagger(\mechanical{\mathbf p},s)v(-\mechanical{\mathbf p},s')
=v^\dagger(\mechanical{\mathbf p},s)u(-\mechanical{\mathbf p},s')=0.
$$
The oscillating mixed terms vanish, leaving
$$
\electric{Q}
=\int\frac{d^3\mechanical{\mathbf p}}{(2\pi)^3}
\sum_s\left(
b_{\mathbf p}^{\,s\dagger}b_{\mathbf p}^{\,s}
+d_{\mathbf p}^{\,s}d_{\mathbf p}^{\,s\dagger}
\right).
$$
The antiparticle anticommutator gives
$$
d_{\mathbf p}^{\,s}d_{\mathbf p}^{\,s\dagger}
=(2\pi)^3\delta^{(3)}(0)
-d_{\mathbf p}^{\,s\dagger}d_{\mathbf p}^{\,s}.
$$
The delta term is the infinite vacuum constant. Normal ordering removes it:
$$
:\electric{Q}:
=\int\frac{d^3\mechanical{\mathbf p}}{(2\pi)^3}
\sum_s\left(
b_{\mathbf p}^{\,s\dagger}b_{\mathbf p}^{\,s}
-d_{\mathbf p}^{\,s\dagger}d_{\mathbf p}^{\,s}
\right)
=\signal{N}_b-\signal{N}_d.
$$
This is (4.40): particles and antiparticles carry opposite charge.

The two parts test different claims. The field equations give local continuity before normal ordering; integrating that equation gives conservation of total charge only if there is no net current through the spatial boundary. Normal ordering then fixes the vacuum reference for the quantum charge.

The antiparticle minus sign arises from reordering the mode operators, not from a negative norm of the antiparticle spinor. Test the final result on a one-antiparticle state and on a particle–antiparticle pair. Their charges are minus one and zero in the stated field-charge units, respectively, even though all excitation energies remain positive.
