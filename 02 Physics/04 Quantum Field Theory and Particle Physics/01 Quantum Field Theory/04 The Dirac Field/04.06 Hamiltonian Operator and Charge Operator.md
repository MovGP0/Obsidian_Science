---
title: "Hamiltonian Operator and Charge Operator"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The normal-ordered Dirac Hamiltonian counts positive particle energies, while its charge distinguishes particles from antiparticles.

Compare the same Fock state under two observables. Energy adds the positive contributions of particles and antiparticles, whereas the global charge subtracts their occupations. The momentum operator completes the energy to a four-vector and carries the same plus sign for both families.

A particle–antiparticle pair with opposite spatial momenta is a useful check: its total charge and spatial momentum vanish, but its energy does not. This test separates charge neutrality from absence of excitations and exposes an incorrect antiparticle sign immediately.
