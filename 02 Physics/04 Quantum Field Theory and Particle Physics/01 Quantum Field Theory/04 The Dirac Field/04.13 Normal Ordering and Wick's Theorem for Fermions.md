title: "Normal Ordering and Wick's Theorem for Fermions"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Fermionic normal ordering and Wick's theorem include signs from exchanges of fermion operators.

The following notes define normal ordering, contractions, and the fermionic Wick expansion.

The task is to keep the vacuum contributions while changing operator order correctly. First define normal ordering relative to the chosen free vacuum. Then define the contraction as the difference between time ordering and normal ordering. Wick's theorem combines those two operations for longer products.

Use a fixed field order and count the parity of each required permutation before inserting a propagator. The matrix indices and the reverse-order contraction sign must travel with their fields. This procedure supplies the exchange signs in fermion diagrams; it is not an additional force or interaction.
