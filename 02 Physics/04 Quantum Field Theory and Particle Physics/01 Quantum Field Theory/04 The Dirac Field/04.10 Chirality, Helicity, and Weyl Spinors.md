> [!TLDR]
> Chiral projectors separate Dirac spinors into Weyl components and connect chirality with helicity.

The following notes cover the chiral decomposition and the Majorana condition.

These are three distinct classifications. Chirality labels the two Lorentz spinor representations; helicity projects spin along a nonzero momentum in a chosen frame; the Majorana condition relates a field to its charge conjugate. None should be defined only by the words “left” or “right.”

Start with the projectors, then use the chiral equations to see exactly where the mass couples the two parts. Only after that compare particle helicity in the massless limit. Finally impose charge self-conjugacy and count the remaining independent modes. A massive Majorana particle still has two spin states; self-conjugacy does not select a single helicity.
