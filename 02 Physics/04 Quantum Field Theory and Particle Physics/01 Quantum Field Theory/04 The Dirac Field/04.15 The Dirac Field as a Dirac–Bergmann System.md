> [!TLDR]
> The first-order Dirac Lagrangian has momentum constraints and requires the Dirac–Bergmann treatment.

The following notes describe the constraints and the brackets used for quantization.

The first-order action leaves fewer independent phase-space coordinates than an unconstrained Legendre transformation would suggest. The aim here is to remove the redundant momenta while preserving the physical first-order evolution, not to add a new dynamics.

Read the full primary-constraint warning before using the bracket formula. The bracket correction requires the inverse constraint matrix, so a partial constraint list is insufficient. Then compare the reduced field bracket with the equal-time anticommutator used earlier: agreement checks that the direct canonical prescription respects the constrained classical structure.
