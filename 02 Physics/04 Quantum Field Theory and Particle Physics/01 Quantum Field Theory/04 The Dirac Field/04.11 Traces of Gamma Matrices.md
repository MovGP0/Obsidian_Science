> [!TLDR]
> Gamma-matrix trace identities reduce spinor expressions to metric tensors and antisymmetric tensors.

The following notes collect trace rules and contraction identities.

Spin sums turn products of external spinors into gamma matrices; traces then turn the closed spinor-index chains into Lorentz tensors. This is why unpolarized scattering calculations can avoid explicit spinor components.

Use the odd-trace proof first as a zero test, then simplify contracted products before expanding a long trace. Finally fix the metric, gamma-five definition, and Levi-Civita orientation before using a parity-sensitive trace. These notes use ordinary four-dimensional matrices; the identities must not be continued to a regulator dimension by changing only the number four.
