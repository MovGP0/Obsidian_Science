title: "The Dirac Field"

> [!TLDR]
> Quantizing spin-half fields requires anticommutators so that the theory obeys Fermi–Dirac statistics.

> “God used beautiful mathematics in creating the world.”
>
> — P. A. M. Dirac

The previous chapter quantized a spin-zero real scalar field using creation and annihilation operators with commutation relations. The Dirac field requires a more delicate step: spin-half particles obey Fermi–Dirac statistics, and quantization must express this through anticommutation relations. The failure of commutator quantization and its explanation by the spin–statistics theorem are fundamental to relativistic QFT.

Read the chapter in three stages. First, use [[04.01 The Dirac Equation and Its Solutions]] to construct the classical spinor modes. Next, use [[04.03 Canonical Quantization and Anticommutation Relations]] through [[04.06 Hamiltonian Operator and Charge Operator]] to turn their coefficients into operators and identify the physical states. Finally, use [[04.07 The Dirac Propagator]] and the symmetry sections to prepare for scattering calculations.

Keep two questions separate: which functions solve the relativistic wave equation, and which quantum states have positive energy? The negative-frequency solutions answer the first question. Antiparticle creation operators give their role in the second. This separation is the main conceptual step from one-particle wave mechanics to the field theory.
