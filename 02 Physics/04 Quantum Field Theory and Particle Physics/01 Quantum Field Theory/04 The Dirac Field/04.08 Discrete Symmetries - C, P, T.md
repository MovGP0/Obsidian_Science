title: "Discrete Symmetries — C, P, T"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Charge conjugation, parity, and time reversal classify interactions, while their combined CPT transformation is an exact symmetry under the stated field-theory assumptions.

Charge conjugation C, spatial parity P, and time reversal T are important for classifying interactions. The CPT theorem guarantees an exact combined symmetry for local Lorentz-covariant Hermitian field theories.

A transformation law first tells how an object changes. A symmetry of the theory preserves its action, but it can map a particular state to a different state. Whether the state under study is invariant is a separate question. The free massive Dirac action supplies the starting example here; an interaction can preserve the combined CPT operation while violating one of C, P, or T separately.

Read each field transformation together with its current transformation. Currents give a physical sign check: parity reverses charge flow, charge conjugation reverses charge, and time reversal reverses motion. Keep the antiunitary nature of T when combining the operations, since it also conjugates numerical phases.
