---
title: "A Theory without a Scale Generates a Scale"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Massless classical QCD has no intrinsic mass scale. Quantum running introduces Λ_QCD and sets a scale for hadron masses.

Classical QCD with massless quarks contains no intrinsic dimensionful scale.
The coupling $\signal{g_s}$ is dimensionless, and the Lagrangian contains no fundamental length or mass parameters.
Yet protons and neutrons have masses of order $1\,\unit{GeV}$.

The origin of this scale is **dimensional transmutation**.
Quantum renormalization breaks classical scale invariance and introduces
$\energy{\Lambda_{\mathrm{QCD}}}$ through the scale dependence of the running coupling.

For QCD with massless quarks, the one-loop running coupling is given by (10.6), with $\energy{\Lambda_{\mathrm{QCD}}}$ as an integration constant.
Different values of $\signal{\alpha_s}(\energy{\mu_0})$ at a reference scale $\energy{\mu_0}$ correspond to different values of $\energy{\Lambda_{\mathrm{QCD}}}$.
The dimensionless coupling has been replaced by the dimensionful QCD scale.

This replacement does not add a free parameter. Once a coupling is fixed at one reference energy, the integration constant is fixed too. Changing the arbitrary reference energy along the same RG trajectory must leave that constant unchanged. At one loop, this can be checked by differentiating the logarithmic form of (10.6).

The generated scale has mass dimension one, while the running coupling is dimensionless. A hadron mass can therefore be a dimensionless number times that scale in the massless theory. The number is not supplied by dimensional analysis. Nonzero quark masses add further dimensionless mass-to-scale ratios, and a quoted numerical Λ_QCD also requires a scheme, flavor number, and loop convention.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{g_s}$ | $1$ | Strong gauge coupling | The classically dimensionless coupling of four-dimensional QCD. |
| $\energy{\Lambda_{\mathrm{QCD}}}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | QCD scale | The dimensionful scale introduced by quantum running. |
| — | $\unit{GeV}$ | Nucleon mass scale | The order-of-magnitude proton and neutron masses quoted by the source. |
| $\signal{\alpha_s}$ | $1$ | Strong fine-structure coupling | The reference coupling traded for the QCD scale. |
| $\energy{\mu_0}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Reference scale | The scale at which the strong coupling is specified. |
