---
title: "Scale Evolution of αₛ in QCD"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The one-loop QCD coupling decreases logarithmically with energy. Its scale dependence can be expressed through a reference coupling or Λ_QCD.

Integrate the evolution equation with
$\signal{\beta}(\signal{g})=-b_0\signal{g}^3/(16\pi^2)$.
For $\signal{\alpha_s}=\signal{g_s}^2/(4\pi)$,
$$
\signal{\alpha_s}(\energy{\mu})
=\frac{\signal{\alpha_s}(\energy{\mu_0})}
{1+\dfrac{b_0\signal{\alpha_s}(\energy{\mu_0})}{2\pi}
\ln\left(\dfrac{\energy{\mu}}{\energy{\mu_0}}\right)}.
$$

An equivalent form introduces the characteristic QCD scale
$\energy{\Lambda_{\mathrm{QCD}}}$:
$$
\signal{\alpha_s}(\energy{\mu})
=\frac{2\pi}{b_0\ln(\energy{\mu}/\energy{\Lambda_{\mathrm{QCD}}})}.
\tag{10.6}
$$
The source gives
$\signal{\alpha_s}(\mechanical{M_Z})\approx0.118$.
It states that the frequently quoted
$\energy{\Lambda_{\mathrm{QCD}}}\approx200$–$300\,\unit{MeV}$
refers to the two-loop $\overline{\mathrm{MS}}$ scheme with five active fermion species, $N_f=5$.
The one-loop formula with $b_0=7$ gives a substantially smaller value.

Check the solution at the reference scale: it returns the input coupling. At higher scales the positive logarithm increases the denominator for positive b₀, so the coupling decreases. Both formulas contain only logarithms of energy ratios, as required for a dimensionless coupling.

The scale Λ_QCD is an integration constant of the chosen running equation, not a new independently adjustable parameter once a reference coupling is fixed. It depends on scheme, perturbative order, and active flavor number. Therefore, the source's five-flavor two-loop scale cannot be inserted into a six-flavor one-loop formula as if these conventions were identical. Across a heavy-quark threshold, evolve in the appropriate flavor theory and match its coupling to the next one. The need for flavor-dependent running and matching is described in [PDG, QCD review, §9.1.1](https://pdg.lbl.gov/2024/reviews/rpp2024-rev-qcd.pdf).

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{g},\signal{g_s},\signal{\alpha_s}$ | $1$ | Gauge and strong coupling parameters | The gauge coupling and the equivalent QCD coupling conventions. |
| $\signal{\beta}$ | $1$ | QCD beta function | The logarithmic scale derivative of the gauge coupling. |
| $\energy{\mu},\energy{\mu_0}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Evolution and reference scales | The energy scales in the running-coupling solution. |
| $\energy{\Lambda_{\mathrm{QCD}}}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | QCD scale parameter | The integration constant of the running coupling, dependent on scheme, order, and active flavors. |
| $\mechanical{M_Z}$ | $\unit{GeV}$ | Z-boson mass | The reference energy for the quoted coupling values. |
| $\electric{\alpha_{\mathrm{QED}}}$ | $1$ | Electromagnetic coupling | The slowly increasing coupling in the comparison plot. |
