---
title: "Fixed Points of the Renormalization Group"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Renormalization-group fixed points have zero beta function. Nearby flows determine whether a fixed point attracts the theory in the infrared or ultraviolet.

A fixed point answers whether a theory can look the same after a change of resolution. Read [[10.04.01 Definition and Classification]] first to fix the direction of flow and test local stability. Then use [[10.04.02 The Gaussian Fixed Point and the Wilson–Fisher Point]] to compare a free fixed point with an interacting critical theory.

Always specify which couplings may vary. Stability in the quartic coupling does not imply stability against a mass perturbation. A critical theory can attract flows within its critical surface while requiring a separate mass tuning to reach that surface. This distinction prevents a one-dimensional flow diagram from being read as a statement about every deformation of the theory.

Fixed points organize the flow of couplings. Their definition and stability are developed in [[10.04.01 Definition and Classification]].
