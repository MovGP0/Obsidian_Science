> [!TLDR]
> Quantum anomalies can break classical symmetries. The scale anomaly links this breaking to renormalization-group flow.

This section connects two uses of renormalization. [[10.11.01 Scale Anomalies and Conformal Invariance]] asks which classical scale symmetries survive quantization. [[10.11.02 The c-Theorem (Zamolodchikov) and the a-Theorem]] asks which directions a flow between fixed points can take under the theorems' assumptions.

A vanishing beta function removes a particular running contribution to the trace; it is not a statement that every anomaly disappears. Also keep energy-flow conventions separate from the coarse-graining direction used in monotonicity statements. The latter runs toward the infrared, opposite to increasing subtraction energy.

The relation between scale anomalies and conformal symmetry starts in [[10.11.01 Scale Anomalies and Conformal Invariance]].
