Use Exercises 10.1 and 10.2 to connect a loop coefficient to running, then compare 10.3 and 10.4 to test fixed-point stability and the direction of energy flow. Exercise 10.5 checks the separate roles of canonical dimension, the running coupling, and field normalization. Exercises 10.6 and 10.7 test how much a high-energy extrapolation depends on particle content and thresholds. Exercise 10.8 organizes low-energy corrections by operator dimension.

Before reading each solution, predict the sign or limiting behavior. After the calculation, check the reference-scale boundary condition or mass dimensions. In 10.6, keep the formal all-species-active estimate separate from a physical Standard Model evolution. In 10.7, compare all three pairwise scales before calling the result a common meeting. The source questions and explicit correction notes remain part of each exercise.
