## Exercise 7.8 — Amplitude for electron–muon scattering

Consider $\mathrm e^-\mu^-\to\mathrm e^-\mu^-$.

(a) Draw all tree diagrams. How many diagrams are there, and why?

(b) Express the amplitude $\signal{\mathcal M}$ in terms of the Mandelstam variables $\energy{s},\mechanical{t},\mechanical{u}$.

(c) Calculate $\overline{|\signal{\mathcal M}|^2}$ in the ultrarelativistic limit $\sqrt{\energy{s}}\gg\mechanical{m}_e,\mechanical{m}_\mu$. Compare the result with Møller scattering.

## Solution 7.8 — Electron–muon scattering amplitude

**(a)** Electrons and muons are distinct particles. At tree level, $\mathrm e^-\mu^-\to\mathrm e^-\mu^-$ has one diagram: t-channel photon exchange. There is no second diagram from exchanging identical final fermions, unlike Møller scattering.

The missing exchange graph is a species constraint, not an approximation that neglects a small muon effect. Each QED fermion line preserves its species. Swapping the outgoing electron and muon would connect unlike fermions along a line and would not be generated by the stated interaction.

In the high-energy comparison, setting both masses to zero changes the kinematics and trace numerators; it does not turn the species into identical particles. The Møller exchange term therefore cannot be restored by simply taking the muon mass to the electron mass.

**(b)** Let $\mechanical{p},\mechanical{q}$ be the incoming electron and muon momenta and $\mechanical{p}',\mechanical{q}'$ their outgoing momenta. Then
$$
i\signal{\mathcal M}
=\bar u_e(\mechanical{p}')(-i\electric{e}\gamma^\mu)u_e(\mechanical{p})
\frac{-ig_{\mu\nu}}{\mechanical{t}}
\bar u_\mu(\mechanical{q}')(-i\electric{e}\gamma^\nu)u_\mu(\mechanical{q}),
$$
so
$$
\signal{\mathcal M}
=\frac{\electric{e}^2}{\mechanical{t}}
[\bar u_e(\mechanical{p}')\gamma^\mu u_e(\mechanical{p})]
[\bar u_\mu(\mechanical{q}')\gamma_\mu u_\mu(\mechanical{q})].
$$
Here $\energy{s}=(\mechanical{p}+\mechanical{q})^2$,
$\mechanical{t}=(\mechanical{p}-\mechanical{p}')^2$, and
$\mechanical{u}=(\mechanical{p}-\mechanical{q}')^2$.

**(c)** In the ultrarelativistic limit, neglect both lepton masses in the spin traces. With the initial spins averaged, define
$$
\signal{L}_e^{\mu\nu}
=\frac12\operatorname{Tr}
[\not{\mechanical{p}}'\gamma^\mu\not{\mechanical{p}}\gamma^\nu],
\qquad
\signal{L}_{\mu,\mu\nu}
=\frac12\operatorname{Tr}
[\not{\mechanical{q}}'\gamma_\mu\not{\mechanical{q}}\gamma_\nu].
$$
Then
$$
\begin{aligned}
\overline{|\signal{\mathcal M}|^2}
&=\frac{\electric{e}^4}{\mechanical{t}^2}\signal{L}_e^{\mu\nu}\signal{L}_{\mu,\mu\nu}\\
&=\frac{8\electric{e}^4}{\mechanical{t}^2}
\left[
(\mechanical{p}\cdot\mechanical{q})(\mechanical{p}'\cdot\mechanical{q}')
+(\mechanical{p}\cdot\mechanical{q}')(\mechanical{p}'\cdot\mechanical{q})
\right]\\
&=\frac{2\electric{e}^4(\energy{s}^2+\mechanical{u}^2)}{\mechanical{t}^2}.
\end{aligned}
$$

The massless scalar products used in the last line are
$$
\mechanical{p}\cdot\mechanical{q}=\mechanical{p}'\cdot\mechanical{q}'=\frac{\energy{s}}2,
\qquad
\mechanical{p}\cdot\mechanical{q}'=\mechanical{p}'\cdot\mechanical{q}=-\frac{\mechanical{u}}2,
\qquad
\energy{s}+\mechanical{t}+\mechanical{u}=0.
$$
For Møller scattering of identical electrons, the result is instead
$$
\overline{|\signal{\mathcal M}_{\mathrm{Moller}}|^2}
=2\electric{e}^4\left[
\frac{\energy{s}^2+\mechanical{u}^2}{\mechanical{t}^2}
+\frac{\energy{s}^2+\mechanical{t}^2}{\mechanical{u}^2}
+\frac{2\energy{s}^2}{\mechanical{t}\mechanical{u}}
\right].
$$
The first term is the electron–muon result. The extra u-channel diagram and its interference term arise from the identical final electrons.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{m}_e,\mechanical{m}_\mu$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Lepton masses | The electron and muon masses neglected in the stated high-energy limit. |
| $\mechanical{p},\mechanical{p}'$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Electron momenta | The incoming and outgoing electron four-momenta. |
| $\mechanical{q},\mechanical{q}'$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Muon momenta | The incoming and outgoing muon four-momenta. |
| $\energy{s}$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Collision invariant | The square of the total incoming momentum. |
| $\mechanical{t},\mechanical{u}$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Transfer invariants | The direct and crossed momentum differences squared. |
| $\signal{\mathcal M},\signal{\mathcal M}_{\mathrm{Moller}}$ | $1$ | Scattering amplitudes | The distinct-lepton and identical-electron invariant amplitudes. |
| $\electric{e}$ | $1$ | Electric coupling | The QED charge magnitude at both lepton vertices. |
| $u_e,\bar u_e,u_\mu,\bar u_\mu$ | $\unit{MeV^{1/2}}$ | Lepton spinors | The covariantly normalized external electron and muon factors. |
| $\signal{L}_e^{\mu\nu},\signal{L}_{\mu,\mu\nu}$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Lepton tensors | The spin-averaged electron and muon trace factors. |
