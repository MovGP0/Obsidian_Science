> [!TLDR]
> Work through the chapter's exercises with the question, source solution, and correction notes together in each linked article.

Use Exercises 7.1 and 7.3 to establish combinatorial and matrix-algebra control before calculating a rate. Exercise 7.2 tests whether a position-space formula has the correct dimension and distinguishes a four-dimensional propagator from a static potential. Exercises 7.4, 7.5, and 7.8 then connect those tools to low-energy limits, crossed kinematics, and particle-species constraints.

The loop exercises test different layers of the calculation. Exercise 7.6 checks a gauge identity, Exercise 7.7 extracts a finite response coefficient, and Exercises 7.9–7.10 separate a regulated integral from its superficial degree. Attempt each question before reading its paired solution. When a source error is flagged, reconstruct the stated leg count, dimension, or momentum map rather than memorizing the printed result.
