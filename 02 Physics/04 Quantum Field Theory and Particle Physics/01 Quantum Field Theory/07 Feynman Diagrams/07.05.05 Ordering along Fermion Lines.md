---
title: "Ordering along Fermion Lines"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Keep the gamma matrices and propagators in their order along each continuous fermion line.

An open fermion line is a sequence of spinor-index contractions, so its matrix factors do not commute. For an incoming and outgoing electron, the rightmost factor acts on the incoming column spinor and the leftmost factor meets the outgoing adjoint spinor. This index test fixes the order even when a drawing is rotated.

For a two-photon interaction on an electron line, exchanging the photon attachments changes both the gamma-matrix order and the intermediate momentum. These are two separate Compton diagrams. One cannot obtain the second term by commuting the first term's matrices. For an antifermion endpoint, use the external-line table to select the correct row or column factor.

Read the matrix product from right to left along fermion-number flow. To construct it explicitly, start at the outgoing fermion bra and follow the line against its arrow. Write each matrix in the order encountered, ending at the incoming fermion ket. Matrix order must be preserved.
