> [!TLDR]
> Gamma-matrix trace identities reduce spin-summed squared amplitudes to Lorentz-invariant scalar products.

The following notes collect the Clifford algebra and the trace rules used in unpolarized scattering calculations.

The trace method answers a practical question: how can a spin-insensitive rate be obtained without choosing explicit spinor components for every state? First multiply the amplitude by its complex conjugate, then use spinor completeness to close each summed spin index into a trace. Only after that step can the identities below reduce the result to scalar products.

Keep initial-state averaging outside the spin sum unless it has already been included in the definition of a leptonic tensor. For two unpolarized spin-one-half incoming particles the factor is one quarter. Final spin states are summed, not averaged. A fixed polarized initial state instead requires its spin projector, so an unpolarized completeness replacement would erase physical information.
