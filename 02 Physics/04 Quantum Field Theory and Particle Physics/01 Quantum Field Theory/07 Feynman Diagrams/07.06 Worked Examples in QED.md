> [!TLDR]
> The QED rules give tree amplitudes for electron scattering, lepton-pair annihilation, and Compton scattering.

These examples isolate three different reasons for more than one diagram. Møller scattering requires two attachments of identical outgoing electrons and a relative fermion sign. Muon-pair production has only the annihilation graph in the stated QED model. Compton scattering has two photon orderings on one electron line; both are required for the gauge check.

For each example, first predict the number of diagrams from the interaction and particle labels. Then compare the ordered amplitude with the rule table. The annihilation example develops the separate step from amplitudes to an unpolarized rate; its negligible-mass limit must not be applied at the pair-production threshold.
