---
title: "Graphical Examples of Symmetry Factors"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Graph symmetries can exchange loop lines without changing the labeled external contribution.

![[QFT-07-05-Symmetry-Factor-Examples.svg|**Fig. 7.5** Source examples of nontrivial symmetry factors: (a) a tadpole with S = 2; (b) a bubble on a propagator, labeled S = 2.]]

In panel (a), reversing the two ends of the self-contracted scalar line leaves both external attachments unchanged. This gives two equivalent assignments and the factor one half. In panel (b), the two parallel internal lines can be exchanged with the external endpoints fixed, again giving a factor one half for the displayed topology.

First count the legs at each vertex, then count its symmetries. These are independent tests: a correct symmetry factor does not establish that the diagram belongs to the stated interaction theory. The source-topology warning below is therefore necessary even when the displayed factor is correct.

The external momentum labels are held fixed when counting graph symmetries.

> [!note]
> The source caption places both panels in quartic scalar theory. In panel (b), each of the two vertices has two internal lines and one external line, so it is a cubic, rather than quartic, topology. The drawing and its stated factor are retained as source material.
