---
title: "Feynman Rules for QED"
---

> [!TLDR]
> QED diagrams combine Dirac propagators, photon propagators, external spinors or polarizations, and the electron-photon vertex.

QED describes interactions between charged Dirac fermions, such as electrons, positrons, and muons, and photons. Its Lagrangian density is
$$
\energy{\mathcal L}_{\mathrm{QED}}
=\bar{\signal{\psi}}(i\not\partial-\mechanical{m})\signal{\psi}
-\frac14\electric{F}_{\mu\nu}\electric{F}^{\mu\nu}
-\electric{e}\bar{\signal{\psi}}\gamma^\mu\signal{\psi}\electric{A}_\mu. \tag{7.1}
$$

The interaction term couples the conserved fermion current to the electromagnetic potential. It fixes the allowed elementary vertex: one photon attaches to a continuous charged-fermion line. The rules below use perturbation theory, asymptotic free external states, and natural units. The propagator article also specifies a gauge; an individual internal photon line is not an observable.

Start with internal and external line factors, then establish matrix order before combining diagrams. The four-dimensional unit check is immediate: a fermion bilinear has mass dimension three and the potential has dimension one, so the electric coupling has dimension zero. This makes the charge convention, rather than a unit conversion, the relevant issue for its sign.

The source defines $\electric{e}>0$ as the charge magnitude and gives the electron's physical charge as $-\electric{e}$. It uses the Heaviside–Lorentz convention, with $\electric{\alpha}=\electric{e}^{\,2}/(4\pi)\approx1/137$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\energy{\mathcal L}_{\mathrm{QED}}$ | $\unit{MeV^4}$ | QED Lagrangian density | The Dirac, Maxwell, and interaction density. |
| $\signal{\psi},\bar{\signal{\psi}}$ | $\unit{MeV^{3/2}}$ | Dirac fields | The charged fermion field and adjoint. |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Fermion mass | The charged-particle mass. |
| $\electric{F}_{\mu\nu}$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Electromagnetic field tensor | The photon field strength. |
| $\electric{A}_\mu$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Electromagnetic potential | The photon four-potential. |
| $\electric{e}$ | $1$ | Electric coupling | The interaction strength at the QED vertex. |
| $\electric{\alpha}$ | $1$ | Fine-structure constant | The dimensionless electromagnetic interaction strength. |
