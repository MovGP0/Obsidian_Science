---
title: "External Lines"
---

> [!TLDR]
> External QED lines supply the spinor or polarization factor of each real incoming or outgoing particle.

External lines represent real particles in the initial and final states. Their factors are the corresponding asymptotic spinors or photon polarization vectors.

| External particle | Factor |
| --- | --- |
| Incoming electron, momentum $\mechanical{p}$ and spin label $s$ | $u^s(\mechanical{p})$ |
| Outgoing electron, momentum $\mechanical{p}'$ and spin label $s'$ | $\bar u^{s'}(\mechanical{p}')$ |
| Incoming positron, momentum $\mechanical{p}$ and spin label $s$ | $\bar v^s(\mechanical{p})$ |
| Outgoing positron, momentum $\mechanical{p}'$ and spin label $s'$ | $v^{s'}(\mechanical{p}')$ |
| Incoming photon, momentum $\mechanical{k}$ and polarization $\lambda$ | $\signal{\epsilon}_\mu^\lambda(\mechanical{k})$ |
| Outgoing photon, momentum $\mechanical{k}'$ and polarization $\lambda'$ | $\signal{\epsilon}_\mu^{\lambda'*}(\mechanical{k}')$ |

Choose the row from the physical in or out state and from whether the particle is a fermion or antifermion. The drawn arrow alone does not specify whether the particle enters or leaves the experiment. A row spinor must meet one end of a matrix chain and a column spinor the other; this is a practical check on positron assignments.

The listed spinors use on-shell momenta and the covariant normalization used by the later completeness relations. These relations sum over a complete set of spin states. An unpolarized cross section averages the initial spin choices and sums the unobserved final choices; the external-line factors themselves contain neither average.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{p},\mechanical{p}'$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Fermion momenta | The incoming and outgoing fermion or antifermion four-momenta. |
| $\mechanical{k},\mechanical{k}'$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Photon momenta | The incoming and outgoing photon four-momenta. |
| $u,\bar u,v,\bar v$ | $\unit{MeV^{1/2}}$ | External spinors | The covariantly normalized fermion and antifermion wavefunctions. |
| $\signal{\epsilon}_\mu^\lambda$ | $1$ | Photon polarization | The normalized external photon polarization vector. |
