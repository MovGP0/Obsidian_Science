---
title: "Mandelstam Variables and Scattering Channels"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Mandelstam variables give Lorentz-invariant labels for the energy and transfer channels of two-body scattering.

For $\mechanical{p}_1,\mechanical{p}_2\to\mechanical{p}_3,\mechanical{p}_4$ with $\mechanical{p}_1+\mechanical{p}_2=\mechanical{p}_3+\mechanical{p}_4$, define
$$
\begin{aligned}
\energy{s}&=(\mechanical{p}_1+\mechanical{p}_2)^2
=(\mechanical{p}_3+\mechanical{p}_4)^2,\\
\mechanical{t}&=(\mechanical{p}_1-\mechanical{p}_3)^2
=(\mechanical{p}_2-\mechanical{p}_4)^2,\\
\mechanical{u}&=(\mechanical{p}_1-\mechanical{p}_4)^2
=(\mechanical{p}_2-\mechanical{p}_3)^2.
\end{aligned}
$$

- [[07.08.01 Physical Interpretation of the Channels]]
- [[07.08.02 The Variables in the Center-of-Mass Frame]]

These definitions use the metric with positive time component and the on-shell external masses. They avoid choosing a laboratory frame, but they still depend on which outgoing particle is labeled third or fourth. Exchanging those labels interchanges the transfer invariants.

To derive the sum rule, expand all three squares. Their cross terms contain the first momentum dotted with the second minus the third minus the fourth. Momentum conservation replaces that combination by minus the first momentum, leaving just the sum of the four external mass squares. This gives an algebraic check on any CM-angle formula.

The invariants satisfy
$$
\energy{s}+\mechanical{t}+\mechanical{u}
=\sum_{i=1}^{4}\mechanical{m}_i^2,
$$
where $\mechanical{m}_i^2=\mechanical{p}_i^{\,2}$. For massless external particles, the sum is zero.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{p}_1,\mechanical{p}_2,\mechanical{p}_3,\mechanical{p}_4$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | External four-momenta | The initial and final scattering momenta. |
| $\energy{s}$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Collision invariant | The total center-of-mass energy squared. |
| $\mechanical{t},\mechanical{u}$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Transfer invariants | The direct and crossed four-momentum transfers squared. |
| $\mechanical{m}_i$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | External masses | The on-shell masses in the invariant sum. |
