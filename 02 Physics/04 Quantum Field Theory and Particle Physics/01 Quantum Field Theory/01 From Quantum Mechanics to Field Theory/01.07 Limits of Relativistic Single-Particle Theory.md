> [!TLDR]
> Localization, causality, and changes in particle number expose systematic limits of relativistic single-particle quantum mechanics.

Free relativistic single-particle theories are useful and mathematically consistent within their scope. The problem is to make that scope large enough to describe local interactions, localization measurements, and particle creation together. The Klein step is one warning that mode amplitudes and signed currents need a careful physical interpretation.

Read the position note to distinguish a frame-dependent localization operator from a local field, then the propagator note to distinguish a propagation amplitude from a controllable signal. Finish with particle-number change, which directly requires more than a single fixed-number sector.
