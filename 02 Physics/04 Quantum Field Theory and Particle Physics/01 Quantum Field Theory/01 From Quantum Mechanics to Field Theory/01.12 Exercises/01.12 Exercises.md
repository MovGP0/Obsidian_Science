> [!TLDR]
> Work through the chapter's exercises with the question, source solution, and correction notes together in each linked article.

Use these problems to test the chapter's modeling choices, not only its algebra. Before opening a solution, predict the units, allowed signs, and a limiting case. Compare that prediction with the result and its source-correction note.

A useful first pass is 1.1 for scale conversion, 1.2 for the matrix algebra, and 1.4 with 1.6 for charge and energy conservation. Then contrast 1.3, 1.5, and 1.8: an unstable background, a strong external step, and a change of gauge constraints are different reasons a familiar approximation can fail. Exercise 1.7 tests which helicity statements require exactly zero mass.
