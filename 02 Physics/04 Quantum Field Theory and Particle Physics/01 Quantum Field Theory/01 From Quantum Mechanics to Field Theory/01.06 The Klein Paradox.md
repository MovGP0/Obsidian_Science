> [!TLDR]
> The Klein paradox tests the single-particle interpretation of the Dirac equation at a strong electrostatic potential step.

A weak step should connect to ordinary scattering. A strong step can instead place the right-hand solution on a negative-frequency continuum. The question is then which mode carries flux toward or away from the step, not simply whether its momentum is real.

Read the formulation for the external-field assumptions, the explicit calculation for current matching, and the Klein regime for the interpretation of signed flux. Keep the incident state and the boundary condition in Region II fixed when comparing coefficients; changing that boundary condition changes the scattering problem.
