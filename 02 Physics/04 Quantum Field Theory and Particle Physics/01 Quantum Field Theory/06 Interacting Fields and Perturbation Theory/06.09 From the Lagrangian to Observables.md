> [!TLDR]
> The perturbative program turns an interaction Lagrangian into invariant amplitudes and measurable scattering or decay rates.

Use this section to connect the formal tools to one chosen prediction. [[06.09.01 Synthesis of the Perturbative Program]] tracks the calculation from a local interaction to a rate. [[06.09.02 Physical Meaning of Propagators]] explains which parts of that calculation describe measured particles and which are internal correlation factors.

Before calculating, specify the prepared initial particles, the counted final channel, the perturbative order, and the energy range. After calculating, check conservation and normalization before comparing with an event distribution. A correct contraction count alone does not establish either the range of validity or agreement with experiment.
