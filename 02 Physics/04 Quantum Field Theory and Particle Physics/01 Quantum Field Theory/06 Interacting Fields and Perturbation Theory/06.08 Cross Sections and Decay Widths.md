title: "Cross Sections and Decay Widths"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Invariant amplitudes become cross sections and decay widths after integration over final-state phase space and the appropriate initial normalization.

The same amplitude can give different observed event counts when beam intensity, available final momenta, or detector selection changes. Cross sections divide out the incident flux; decay widths use the normalization of one parent particle. Both retain the phase-space restriction from energy and momentum conservation.

Read [[06.08.01 Invariant Phase Space]] first. Choose [[06.08.02 Differential Cross Section]] for a collision and [[06.08.03 Decay Width]] for a decay. The two-body and Mandelstam notes turn the invariant expressions into angular predictions. Finish with [[06.08.06 Total Cross Section and the Optical Theorem]] as a probability-conservation check on the integrated result.
