title: "LSZ Reduction Formula"
---

> [!TLDR]
> LSZ reduction connects physical asymptotic particle amplitudes to time-ordered correlation functions of interacting fields.

Correlation functions describe local field insertions, whereas detectors identify particles with definite masses and spin. LSZ connects these descriptions by isolating one-particle poles. Its particle assumptions must be checked before it is used as an amputation rule.

Follow [[06.06.01 Asymptotic States]] and [[06.06.02 Renormalization Constant Z]] to identify the states and their normalization. Then read [[06.06.03 Formula for Scalar Fields]] with its source-convention corrections. [[06.06.04 Dirac and Vector Fields]] adds the physical spin and polarization data. Finally, [[06.06.05 Physical Interpretation]] explains what field normalization can change and what a scattering measurement can determine.
