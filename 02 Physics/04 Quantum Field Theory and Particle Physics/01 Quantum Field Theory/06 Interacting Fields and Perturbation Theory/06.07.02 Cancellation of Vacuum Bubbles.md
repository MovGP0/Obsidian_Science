---
title: "Cancellation of Vacuum Bubbles"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Dividing by the vacuum-to-vacuum amplitude cancels vacuum-bubble factors from normalized interacting Green functions.

Vacuum-bubble factors cancel in physical S-matrix elements. With the usual vacuum projection and boundary prescription, the interacting Green function is
$$
\signal{G}^{(n)}(\mechanical{x}_1,\ldots,\mechanical{x}_n)
=
\frac{\langle0|\mathcal T\{
\signal{\phi}_I(\mechanical{x}_1)\cdots
\signal{\phi}_I(\mechanical{x}_n)\mathcal S\}|0\rangle}
{\langle0|\mathcal S|0\rangle}.
$$
The denominator is the sum of vacuum diagrams, including their disconnected products. It cancels the identical multiplicative vacuum-bubble factor in the numerator. The normalized Green function is therefore free of separate vacuum bubbles.

The ratio can be checked order by order. At first order, expand its reciprocal denominator. The term in which the external fields propagate freely while an independent vacuum bubble occurs is canceled by the free external correlator times the first-order denominator term. Higher disconnected vacuum products factor in the same way. This argument requires the same regulator and vacuum prescription in numerator and denominator.

The cancellation concerns components with no external points. Products connecting separate groups of external points remain in the normalized full correlator. Also, normalization of scattering correlators does not assert that every vacuum-energy question is physically irrelevant; the calculation here concerns particle scattering in a fixed background.

Connected scattering amplitudes are calculated from connected amputated Green functions. Connected correlators are generated by the logarithm
$$
W[J]=-i\ln Z[J].
$$
Here $Z[J]$ is the generating functional, also called the partition functional in the source. Chapter 11 develops this construction.

The argument $J$ is an external scalar source coupled linearly to the field. Its generating role differs from the pole residue denoted by $\signal{Z}$ in LSZ reduction.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{G}^{(n)}$ | — | Interacting Green function | The normalized position-space correlator; its mass dimension depends on n. |
| $\signal{\phi}_I$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Interaction-picture scalar field | The free field used inside the perturbative expectation value. |
| $\mechanical{x}_i$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Spacetime points | The external field insertion coordinates. |
| $J$ | $\unit{MeV^3}$ | Scalar source | The external source used to generate scalar correlators. |
| $\signal{Z}$ | $1$ | Pole residue | The LSZ field residue, distinct from a generating or vacuum functional. |
