---
title: "Dirac and Vector Fields"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Dirac and vector LSZ reduction amputates external propagators and contracts the remaining indices with physical spinors or polarization vectors.

For a Dirac field $\signal{\psi}(\mechanical{x})$, replace the Klein–Gordon reduction operator with $(i\not\partial-\mechanical{m}_{\mathrm{ph}})$. This removes the external fermion propagator. Contract the remaining indices with free on-shell spinors, schematically
$$
i\signal{\mathcal M}
\sim\bar u(\mechanical{p}')\,
[\tilde{\signal{G}}_{\psi}^{\mathrm{amp}}]\,
u(\mechanical{p}).
$$
The amputated Green function is evaluated on shell. The free spinors satisfy
$$
(\not{\mechanical{p}}-\mechanical{m}_{\mathrm{ph}})u(\mechanical{p})=0,
\qquad
(\not{\mechanical{p}}+\mechanical{m}_{\mathrm{ph}})v(\mechanical{p})=0.
$$
The reduction operator gives a zero on shell that cancels the external propagator pole, as in the scalar case.

For external photons, contract with $\signal{\epsilon}^\mu$ for an incoming photon and $\signal{\epsilon}^{\mu*}$ for an outgoing photon. External fermions use their asymptotic spinors. Each external leg includes its field-residue normalization, expressed in the reduction formula by $1/\sqrt{\signal{Z}_\psi}$ or $1/\sqrt{\signal{Z}_A}$.

> [!note]
> The source assigns $v$ to incoming antifermions and $\bar v$ to outgoing antifermions. With the standard fermion-line convention used for QED external states, these assignments are reversed: an incoming antifermion supplies $\bar v$, and an outgoing antifermion supplies $v$. Incoming fermions supply $u$, and outgoing fermions supply $\bar u$.

The first displayed amplitude is schematic: it suppresses other legs, residue factors, and spin labels. It is a scalar after the remaining spinor indices have been contracted. For a consistent fermion sign, keep a fixed order of the external fermionic states as well as a fixed orientation of each fermion line. Exchanging two identical external fermions changes the amplitude sign and is a useful check on a sum of diagrams.

For a photon, use physical transverse polarizations. A practical gauge check is that replacing one external polarization by that photon's four-momentum gives zero in the complete physical amplitude, with the other external states on shell. This cancellation can require the sum of several diagrams; it need not hold for each diagram alone. [Burdman, Lecture 18, section 18.4, equation (18.51)](https://fma.if.usp.br/~burdman/QFT1/lecture_18.pdf) derives this Ward identity from current conservation. Neither a covariant propagator's four components nor its gauge parameter counts four observable photon polarization states.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{\psi}$ | $\unit{MeV^{3/2}}$ | Dirac field | The interacting fermion field used in LSZ reduction. |
| $\mechanical{x}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Spacetime point | The field insertion coordinate. |
| $\mechanical{m}_{\mathrm{ph}}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Physical fermion mass | The on-shell mass in the external Dirac equation. |
| $\mechanical{p},\mechanical{p}'$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | External four-momenta | The incoming and outgoing fermion momenta. |
| $u,\bar u,v,\bar v$ | $\unit{MeV^{1/2}}$ | External spinors | The covariantly normalized fermion and antifermion spinors. |
| $\signal{\epsilon}^\mu$ | $1$ | Photon polarization | The normalized external photon polarization vector. |
| $\signal{Z}_\psi,\signal{Z}_A$ | $1$ | Field residues | The fermion and photon external-field normalization factors. |
| $\signal{\mathcal M}$ | — | Invariant amplitude | The physical amplitude; its dimension depends on the process. |
| $\tilde{\signal{G}}_{\psi}^{\mathrm{amp}}$ | — | Amputated fermionic Green function | The remaining spinor tensor after external propagators are removed. |
