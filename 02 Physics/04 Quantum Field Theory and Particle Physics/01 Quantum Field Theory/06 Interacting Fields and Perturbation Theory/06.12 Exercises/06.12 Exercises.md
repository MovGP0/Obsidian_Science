> [!TLDR]
> Work through the chapter's exercises with the question, source solution, and correction notes together in each linked article.

Use Exercises 6.1–6.3 to establish propagator boundary conditions, contraction counts, and fermion signs before calculating rates. Exercises 6.4–6.6 then connect invariant kinematics and flux to an identical-particle cross section. Exercise 6.8 provides a decay comparison, where one parent normalization replaces the incident collision flux.

Read the correction in Exercise 6.7 carefully: free propagation has a nonzero identity overlap and zero nontrivial connected scattering. Exercises 6.9 and 6.10 then check different structures: unitarity matches an imaginary loop term to a lower-order cross section, while vacuum exponentiation fixes the multiplicity of disconnected components. Try to predict the sign, threshold, or combinatorial count before reading each solution.
