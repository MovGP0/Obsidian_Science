---
title: "Connected Diagrams and Vacuum Bubbles"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Connected scattering contributions can be separated from disconnected propagation and vacuum bubbles.

Three operations have different purposes. Remove vacuum-only components by vacuum normalization. Select connected correlators to isolate a single connected scattering process. Amputate external propagators and apply LSZ to obtain its particle amplitude. Performing the first operation does not perform the other two.

Use [[06.07.01 Structure of the Diagrams]] to classify components, [[06.07.02 Cancellation of Vacuum Bubbles]] to understand the normalization ratio, and [[06.07.03 Connected Decomposition]] to reconstruct a full correlator from connected parts. The free four-point function is a useful test: it is nonzero through pair products even though its connected four-point part vanishes.
