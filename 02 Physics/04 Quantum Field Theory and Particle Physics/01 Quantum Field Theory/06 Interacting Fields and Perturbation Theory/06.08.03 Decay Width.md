---
title: "Decay Width"
---

> [!TLDR]
> A rest-frame decay width is the squared amplitude integrated over final-state phase space and divided by twice the parent mass.

For $1\to2+3+\cdots+n$, with parent mass $\mechanical{M}$, the rest-frame differential width is
$$
\boxed{
d\energy{\Gamma}
=\frac{\overline{\lvert\signal{\mathcal M}\rvert^2}}{2\mechanical{M}}
\,d\signal{\Phi}_n}. \tag{6.8}
$$
The factor $2\mechanical{M}$ comes from relativistic normalization of the one-particle initial state. The mean lifetime is
$$
\signal{\tau}=\frac{\hbar}{\energy{\Gamma}_{\mathrm{tot}}}
=\frac1{\energy{\Gamma}_{\mathrm{tot}}}
\quad(\hbar=1).
$$
In natural units the width also gives the inverse lifetime. For both (6.7) and (6.8), include a factor $1/n!$ for each set of $n$ identical final particles to avoid counting their permutations more than once.

The integrated width for one specified final channel is a partial width. Summing mutually exclusive allowed channels gives the total width in the lifetime formula. A branching fraction is the partial width divided by the total width. All are nonnegative when calculated consistently to the stated approximation.

Equation (6.8) is a rest-frame rate. A moving parent's decay probability per unit laboratory time is reduced by its Lorentz time-dilation factor; its proper lifetime remains the value above. The channel closes when the parent mass is below the sum of the final rest masses. For a two-body channel with a nonsingular amplitude, its phase space vanishes at threshold. These rate and lifetime conventions are summarized in [PDG Kinematics, section 49.4](https://pdg.lbl.gov/2025/reviews/rpp2025-rev-kinematics.pdf).

The identical-particle factorial applies when the integration covers all labeled momenta. Do not divide again if an equivalent restricted integration region has already counted each physical final state once.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\energy{\Gamma},\energy{\Gamma}_{\mathrm{tot}}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Decay widths | The differential-channel width and total width; equal to inverse time in natural units. |
| $\mechanical{M}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Parent mass | The rest mass of the decaying particle. |
| $\signal{\mathcal M}$ | — | Decay amplitude | The invariant amplitude; its dimension depends on final multiplicity. |
| $d\signal{\Phi}_n$ | — | Decay phase space | The final-state phase-space measure, with multiplicity-dependent dimension. |
| $\signal{\tau}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Mean lifetime | The rest-frame mean time before decay. |
| $\hbar$ | $1$ | Reduced Planck constant | Set to one in natural units. |
