title: "Euler–Lagrange Equations for Fields"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Stationary action determines the field equations, including the Maxwell equations.

The variational principle turns a chosen local action into differential equations. It does not select the action or supply initial data. The main step is to separate a bulk term, which must vanish for arbitrary interior variations, from a surface term, which depends on the physical boundary problem.

Use [[02.03.01 Systematic Derivation]] to follow that separation. Then compare [[02.03.02 Equations of Motion for the Maxwell Field]]: variation gives the dynamical Maxwell equations, while the homogeneous equations follow from the potential definition. This comparison prevents counting an identity as an extra independent equation of motion.
