title: "The Lorentz Group and Its Representations"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Lorentz representations classify how scalar, vector, and spinor fields transform in spacetime.

The physical question is how two inertial observers compare the components of the same field. The Lorentz group preserves the spacetime metric, but different fields realize that group through different matrices. A scalar, a four-vector, and a spinor cannot share one component transformation rule.

Follow [[02.08.01 Algebraic Structure of the Lorentz Group]] for the generators, then [[02.08.02 Decomposition and Representations]] for the finite-dimensional labels. [[02.08.03 Fundamental Representations]] connects those labels to familiar fields. Finally, [[02.08.04 Transformation of Fields]] combines the component change with the change of spacetime argument. Keep finite-dimensional field representations distinct from the unitary representations on quantum states discussed later.
