---
title: "Functional Integrals for Gauge Fields"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Gauge-field functional integrals must account for redundant configurations related by gauge transformations.

The construction starts with [[11.06.01 Gauge Redundancy]].

The new difficulty is not a strong interaction: even the free Maxwell action has directions in field space that leave all electromagnetic field strengths unchanged. A Gaussian integral along those gauge directions has no restoring quadratic term. A gauge choice removes this redundant description and makes a propagator available. It does not remove physical photon states.

Read [[11.06.02 The Faddeev–Popov Procedure]] for the gauge-slice Jacobian, then [[11.06.03 Ghost Fields]] for its determinant representation. In linear Abelian gauges the determinant is field independent; in non-Abelian theories it usually produces interactions. [[11.06.04 QED - The Complete Functional Integral]] combines this construction with the Dirac integral.

The test of a gauge choice is a physical amplitude. The potential propagator can change with the gauge parameter, while its momentum-dependent gauge terms vanish between conserved external currents. Exercise 11.9 tests this cancellation explicitly. A consistent regulator, boundary conditions, and treatment of residual gauge transformations are part of this construction.
