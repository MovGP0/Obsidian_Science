---
title: "QED — The Complete Functional Integral"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The gauge-fixed QED functional integrates photon and Dirac fields with their sources. Perturbative expansion reproduces the canonical QED Feynman rules.

This functional combines three tasks: the photon gauge term makes its quadratic operator invertible, the Grassmann measure supplies fermion exchange signs, and the sources identify the external field insertions. It uses vacuum boundary conditions and the Feynman prescriptions of the free photon and Dirac inverses. A field-independent Abelian ghost determinant and the common vacuum normalization are absorbed into the measure.

The full QED generating functional in Feynman gauge,
$\signal{\xi}=1$, is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\signal{Z}[\bar{\signal{\eta}},\signal{\eta},\electric{J}^\mu]
={}&\int\mathcal{D}\bar{\signal{\psi}}\mathcal{D}\signal{\psi}
\mathcal{D}\electric{A}_\mu\\
&\times\exp\Bigg[
i\int\mathrm{d}^4\mechanical{x}\,
\Big(
\energy{\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{QED}}^{\mathrm{gf}}}
+\bar{\signal{\eta}}\signal{\psi}
+\bar{\signal{\psi}}\signal{\eta}
+\electric{J}^\mu\electric{A}_\mu
\Big)\Bigg],
\end{aligned}
\tag{11.30}
$$
with
$$
\begin{aligned}
\energy{\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{QED}}^{\mathrm{gf}}}
={}&\bar{\signal{\psi}}(i\not\partial-\mechanical{m})\signal{\psi}
-\electric{e}\electric{A}_\mu
\bar{\signal{\psi}}\gamma^\mu\signal{\psi}\\
&-\frac14\electric{F}_{\mu\nu}\electric{F}^{\mu\nu}
-\frac12(\partial^\mu\electric{A}_\mu)^2.
\end{aligned}
$$
Evaluate the photon and fermion integrals perturbatively, treating
$-\electric{e}\electric{A}_\mu
\bar{\signal{\psi}}\gamma^\mu\signal{\psi}$
as the interaction.
This gives the same QED Feynman rules as canonical quantization, summarized in Fig. 11.4.
The source presents this agreement as the equivalence of the two formulations.

At zero electric coupling, the integral factors into independent free photon and free Dirac source functionals. Expanding the interaction exponential once instead gives the factor $-i\electric{e}\gamma^\mu$ after removing the external propagators. These two checks account for all three rules in Fig. 11.4 and fix the sign of the vertex.

Source derivatives first give time-ordered correlators, not cross sections. To obtain scattering predictions, amputate the external legs, put the physical external states on shell, and include their normalization before squaring the amplitude and integrating phase space. A regulator and counterterms are needed for ultraviolet-divergent loop terms. Agreement with canonical Feynman rules checks the reformulation; comparison with measured scattering tests QED itself.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{Z}$ | $1$ | QED generating functional | The gauge-fixed vacuum functional with photon and fermion sources. |
| $\signal{\xi}$ | $1$ | Gauge parameter | Set to one for Feynman gauge. |
| $\signal{\psi},\bar{\signal{\psi}}$ | $\unit{MeV^{3/2}}$ | Dirac fields | The anticommuting electron and adjoint field variables. |
| $\signal{\eta},\bar{\signal{\eta}}$ | $\unit{MeV^{5/2}}$ | Fermion sources | The independent Grassmann sources for the Dirac fields. |
| $\electric{A}_\mu$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Photon potential | The electromagnetic field integrated in the QED functional. |
| $\electric{J}^\mu$ | $\unit{MeV^3}$ | External photon source | The current-like source coupled to the photon potential. |
| $\electric{F}_{\mu\nu}$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Electromagnetic field tensor | The tensor in the photon kinetic term. |
| $\electric{e}$ | $1$ | Electromagnetic coupling | The electric charge parameter in the interaction. |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Electron mass | The mass in the Dirac kinetic term. |
| $\energy{\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{QED}}^{\mathrm{gf}}}$ | $\unit{MeV^4}$ | Gauge-fixed QED Lagrangian density | The fermion, photon, interaction, and gauge-fixing terms. |
| $\mechanical{x}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Spacetime coordinate | The coordinate integrated in the full action. |
| $\mechanical{k},\mechanical{p},\mechanical{p}'$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Photon and electron momenta | The momenta labeling the propagator and vertex rules. |
