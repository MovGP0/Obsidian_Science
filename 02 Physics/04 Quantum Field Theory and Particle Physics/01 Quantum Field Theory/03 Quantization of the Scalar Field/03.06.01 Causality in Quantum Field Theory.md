title: "Causality in Quantum Field Theory"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Commuting observables at spacelike separation prevent a physical signal from propagating faster than light.

Relativistic causality forbids signals faster than light. QFT expresses this through microcausality.

## Definition 3.8 — Microcausality

For observables at spacelike-separated events, $(\mechanical{x}-\mechanical{y})^{2}<0$,

$$
[\mathcal O_1(\mechanical{x}),\mathcal O_2(\mechanical{y})]=0. \tag{3.56}
$$

For spacelike events, different inertial frames can reverse the temporal order. If the corresponding local observables commute, their product is independent of that reversal. A local unitary operation built from one observable then leaves a commuting remote observable unchanged. This is the operational content of the condition; it does not require the vacuum correlation to factor into two separate expectations.

For the scalar field, require $[\signal{\phi}(\mechanical{x}),\signal{\phi}(\mechanical{y})]=0$ at such separations.

Point fields are distributions, so a precise local operation uses fields smeared over separated spacetime regions. For the real scalar field Hermiticity permits the field itself to serve as a local observable. For a charged complex field one must also check the field–adjoint commutator or neutral observable combinations; the field–field commutator alone is not a sufficient locality test. The spacelike condition excludes the light cone, where singular distributions can remain.
