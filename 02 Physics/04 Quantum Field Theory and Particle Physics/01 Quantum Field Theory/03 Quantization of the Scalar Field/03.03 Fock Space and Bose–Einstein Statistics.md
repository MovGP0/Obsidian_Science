title: "Fock Space and Bose–Einstein Statistics"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Oscillator creation operators build bosonic multiparticle states and organize them into Fock space.

The operator algebra does not yet specify the physical states. Choose the free vacuum and apply creation operators to build the one-particle and multiparticle sectors. The energy of each added quantum is positive, and commuting creation operators make the resulting states symmetric under exchange.

Read vacuum normalization before particle counting, then distinguish exchange statistics from thermal occupation statistics. The final Fock-space note combines all number sectors. A direct sum permits variable particle number; it does not by itself imply that the free Hamiltonian changes particle number.
