---
title: "Covariant Commutator"
---

> [!TLDR]
> A mass-shell integral with the sign of energy makes Lorentz invariance of the commutator explicit.

The Pauli–Jordan representation is

$$
i\signal{\Delta}(\mechanical{x})=\int\frac{d^{4}\mechanical{k}}{(2\pi)^{4}}\,2\pi\delta(\mechanical{k}^{2}-\mechanical{m}^{2})\operatorname{sgn}(\energy{k}^{0})e^{-i\mechanical{k}\cdot\mechanical{x}}. \tag{3.86}
$$

Here $\operatorname{sgn}(\energy{k}^{0})=\theta(\energy{k}^{0})-\theta(-\energy{k}^{0})$. The mass shell, energy sign, and measure are invariant under proper orthochronous transformations.

Integrating over energy splits the mass-shell delta into its positive- and negative-energy roots. Each root supplies the inverse twice-energy weight; the sign function subtracts the negative root. Spatial momentum reversal then reproduces (3.58). This shows why a sign factor is needed: a sum of both roots instead would describe a different, even correlation combination.

The basic properties begin with:

1. Oddness: $\signal{\Delta}(-\mechanical{x})=-\signal{\Delta}(\mechanical{x})$.
2. Equal-time vanishing: $\signal{\Delta}(\mechanical{x})|_{x^{0}=0}=0$.

> [!note]
> The source labels the second property “vanishing on the vacuum.” Its displayed condition is instead equal-time vanishing; that label is corrected here.

3. Initial derivative: $\partial_0\signal{\Delta}(\mechanical{x})|_{x^{0}=0}=-\delta^{(3)}(\mechanical{\mathbf x})$.
4. Klein–Gordon equation: $(\square+\mechanical{m}^{2})\signal{\Delta}(\mechanical{x})=0$.
5. Causal support: $\signal{\Delta}(\mechanical{x})=0$ for spacelike separation.

The negative sign in property 3 differentiates the first event time. The canonical momentum commutator differentiates the second event time and therefore has the opposite sign. This checks consistency with (3.5), not a failure of it. Property 4 follows because the Klein–Gordon Fourier multiplier vanishes on the mass shell. Together, the zero initial field commutator and its delta initial derivative specify the free commutator's evolution.
