## Exercise 3.4 — Noether Current for Spatial Translations

Use $\mechanical{x}^{\mu}\to\mechanical{x}^{\mu}+\varepsilon^{\mu}$ and the energy–momentum tensor to derive the conserved momentum $\mechanical{P}^{i}=\int d^{3}\mechanical{x}\,\energy{T}^{0i}$. Verify agreement with Exercise 3.3.

A scalar has no intrinsic component rotation under translation, but its value at a fixed coordinate point changes because its argument changes. Keep that fixed-point variation distinct from the intrinsic zero used below. The symmetry is global translation of the free system, and surface terms vanish for localized wave packets or compatible periodic boundary conditions.

## Solution 3.4 — Spatial translations

Under $\mechanical{x}^\mu\to\mechanical{x}^\mu+\mechanical{\varepsilon}^\mu$, the intrinsic scalar variation is $\Delta\signal{\phi}=0$.
The Noether translation current is
$$
\energy{T}^\mu{}_\nu
=\frac{\partial\energy{\mathcal L}}{\partial(\partial_\mu\signal{\phi})}
\partial_\nu\signal{\phi}
-\delta^\mu{}_\nu\energy{\mathcal L}
=\partial^\mu\signal{\phi}\,\partial_\nu\signal{\phi}
-\delta^\mu{}_\nu\energy{\mathcal L}.
$$
After raising the spatial charge index,
$$
\mechanical{P}^i
=\int d^3\mechanical{\mathbf x}\,\energy{T}^{0i}
=\int d^3\mechanical{\mathbf x}\,
\partial^0\signal{\phi}\,\partial^i\signal{\phi}
=\int d^3\mechanical{\mathbf x}\,\mechanical{\pi}\,\partial^i\signal{\phi}.
$$

Current conservation uses the field equation; conservation of its spatial integral also requires zero net momentum flux through the boundary. A raised spatial index introduces the metric minus sign. This explains why the canonical momentum times an ordinary spatial gradient has the opposite sign from the physical contravariant momentum.

Using $\partial^i=-\partial_i$,
$$
\mechanical{P}^i=-\int d^3\mechanical{\mathbf x}\,\mechanical{\pi}\,\partial_i\signal{\phi}.
$$
Inserting the mode expansions gives
$$
\mechanical{P}^i
=\int\frac{d^3\mechanical{\mathbf k}}{(2\pi)^3}
\mechanical{k}^i\,a^\dagger(\mechanical{\mathbf k})a(\mechanical{\mathbf k}),
$$
in agreement with Solution 3.3.

Agreement with Exercise 3.3 is an independent check: Noether symmetry and oscillator algebra must give the same charge. Its dimension is one power of energy, because the stress tensor has dimension four and spatial volume dimension minus three. A fixed external wall can exchange momentum with the field, so the isolated-field charge need not remain conserved in that changed setup.
