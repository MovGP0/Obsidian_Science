> [!TLDR]
> Work through the chapter's exercises with the question, source solution, and correction notes together in each linked article.

Use Exercises 3.1–3.2 to test canonical normalization and energy positivity. Then compare 3.3 with 3.4: the same momentum must emerge from oscillator modes and from translation symmetry. In 3.5, track the source's current sign convention through the charge count before testing its conservation.

Exercises 3.6–3.8 distinguish three objects: a spacelike-zero commutator, a spacelike-nonzero Feynman correlation, and a retarded response with support in the future cone. Preserve the stated source signs when comparing them. Finish with 3.9 to test Lorentz-generator signs and 3.10 to recover a classical field expectation from a quantum state. Predict each check before opening its solution; use the retained correction notes when the printed source conventions disagree.
