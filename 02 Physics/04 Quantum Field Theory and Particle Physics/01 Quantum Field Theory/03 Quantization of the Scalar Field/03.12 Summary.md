> [!TLDR]
> Quantized scalar modes build bosonic Fock space and provide the basic field and propagation structures of QFT.

The chain of reasoning is: choose a free quadratic model, impose its canonical equal-time algebra, realize that algebra with oscillator modes, and build the positive-energy Fock representation. The free field equation is a model input; canonical commutation is a quantum postulate; the multiparticle spectrum and spacelike commutator are consequences. The construction uses flat spacetime and natural units.

## Canonical Quantization

The real scalar field is quantized by $[\signal{\phi}(\mechanical{\mathbf x},\mechanical{t}),\mechanical{\pi}(\mechanical{\mathbf y},\mechanical{t})]=i\delta^{(3)}(\mechanical{\mathbf x}-\mechanical{\mathbf y})$. Its Fourier modes are independent harmonic oscillators.

## Creation and Annihilation

The mode coefficients become operators with bosonic commutators. They create and annihilate particles with momentum $\mechanical{\mathbf k}$ and energy $\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k}=\sqrt{\mechanical{\mathbf k}^{2}+\mechanical{m}^{2}}$.

## Fock Space

The state space is built by repeatedly applying creation operators to the vacuum.

Commuting creation operators automatically give exchange-symmetric multiparticle states and Bose–Einstein statistics.

## Hamiltonian and Normal Ordering

The normal-ordered Hamiltonian is $\energy{H}=\int d^{3}\mechanical{k}\,\energy{\omega}_{\mathbf k}a^{\dagger}a/(2\pi)^{3}$. The zero-point sum diverges; boundary-dependent vacuum effects are measurable through the Casimir force.

## Complex Scalar Field

Independent operator sets describe particles and antiparticles. Global U(1) symmetry gives the conserved charge $\electric{Q}=N_a-N_b$.

## Causality

The commutator function $\signal{\Delta}(\mechanical{x}-\mechanical{y})$ vanishes at spacelike separation, implementing microcausality.

## Propagators

The momentum-space Feynman propagator is $\widetilde{\signal{\Delta}}_{F}(\mechanical{k})=i/(\mechanical{k}^{2}-\mechanical{m}^{2}+i\varepsilon)$. It is the Klein–Gordon Green's function in the stated convention. Time ordering combines the forward-time particle and antiparticle interpretations associated with the Feynman–Stueckelberg description. The spacelike commutator condition remains the causality requirement.

To reconstruct the chapter, check three distinctions. Negative frequency supplies creation terms without negative excitation energy. Charge is a difference of two species counts, while their energies add. Finally, a spacelike Feynman correlation can be nonzero while its commutator vanishes, so it is the commutator that tests causal independence.

A useful boundary test is to introduce an interaction: free number operators need not remain conserved, the same constant free-mode expansion no longer solves the full equation, and a two-point function can acquire a spectral continuum. Global phase symmetry can still preserve net charge. Normal ordering of the free Hamiltonian alone does not settle these interacting questions or an absolute gravitational vacuum energy.
