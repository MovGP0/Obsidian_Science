## Exercise 9.3

Verify the Ward–Takahashi identity (9.72) at one loop.

(a) Write the Feynman-rule integral for
$\mechanical{q}_\mu\Lambda^\mu(\mechanical{p}',\mechanical{p})$.

(b) Use the algebraic identity
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mechanical{q}_\mu\gamma^\mu
&=\not{\mechanical{p}}'-\not{\mechanical{p}}\\
&\equiv(\not{\mechanical{p}}'-\not{\mechanical{k}}+\mechanical{m})
-(\not{\mechanical{p}}-\not{\mechanical{k}}+\mechanical{m})
\end{aligned}
$$
to split the integral into two contributions, one for each fermion propagator.

(c) Show that these contributions match
$\energy{\Sigma}(\mechanical{p}')$ and
$\energy{\Sigma}(\mechanical{p})$, and verify (9.72).

## Solution 9.3 — Ward–Takahashi identity at one loop

**(a)** Define $\Gamma^\mu=\gamma^\mu+\Lambda^\mu$ with the overall QED vertex factor removed. Using the same convention as Solution 8.12,
$$
\Lambda^\mu(\mechanical{p}',\mechanical{p})
=-i\electric{e}^2\energy{\mu}^{2\epsilon}
\int\frac{d^d\mechanical{k}}{(2\pi)^d}
\frac{N^\mu}
{(\mechanical{k}^2+i0)
[(\mechanical{p}'-\mechanical{k})^2-\mechanical{m}^2+i0]
[(\mechanical{p}-\mechanical{k})^2-\mechanical{m}^2+i0]},
$$
where
$$
N^\mu
=\gamma^\alpha(\not{\mechanical{p}}'-\not{\mechanical{k}}+\mechanical{m})
\gamma^\mu(\not{\mechanical{p}}-\not{\mechanical{k}}+\mechanical{m})\gamma_\alpha.
$$
For $\mechanical{q}=\mechanical{p}'-\mechanical{p}$,
$$
\mechanical{q}_\mu N^\mu
=\gamma^\alpha(\not{\mechanical{p}}'-\not{\mechanical{k}}+\mechanical{m})
\not{\mechanical{q}}(\not{\mechanical{p}}-\not{\mechanical{k}}+\mechanical{m})\gamma_\alpha.
$$

> [!note] Vertex sign convention
> The source again prints $+i\electric{e}^2$ for $\Lambda^\mu$. With $\Gamma^\mu=\gamma^\mu+\Lambda^\mu$, the $-i\electric{e}\gamma^\mu$ Feynman vertex, and the propagators used here, the overall factor is $-i\electric{e}^2$. The corresponding self-energy convention must match it in the Ward identity.

Contracting the photon vertex is useful because it replaces the middle gamma matrix by a difference of inverse free Dirac denominators. Those factors cancel adjacent propagators, leaving two self-energy integrals with the same loop routing. Matrix order must be retained in this step; the cancellation is multiplication of adjacent inverse factors, not a scalar partial-fraction trick.

Use the same regulator, causal prescription, and extracted vertex factor on both sides. Otherwise a momentum shift or an untracked overall sign can mimic a violation of the identity. The check is an off-shell identity between functions, so setting external spinors on shell before comparing the integrands would discard information that the exercise is meant to test.

**(b)** Insert
$$
\not{\mechanical{q}}
=(\not{\mechanical{p}}'-\not{\mechanical{k}}-\mechanical{m})
-(\not{\mechanical{p}}-\not{\mechanical{k}}-\mechanical{m}).
$$
The numerator between the outer gamma matrices becomes
$$
\begin{aligned}
&(\not{\mechanical{p}}'-\not{\mechanical{k}}+\mechanical{m})
\not{\mechanical{q}}
(\not{\mechanical{p}}-\not{\mechanical{k}}+\mechanical{m})\\
={}&(\not{\mechanical{p}}'-\not{\mechanical{k}}+\mechanical{m})
(\not{\mechanical{p}}'-\not{\mechanical{k}}-\mechanical{m})
(\not{\mechanical{p}}-\not{\mechanical{k}}+\mechanical{m})\\
&-(\not{\mechanical{p}}'-\not{\mechanical{k}}+\mechanical{m})
(\not{\mechanical{p}}-\not{\mechanical{k}}-\mechanical{m})
(\not{\mechanical{p}}-\not{\mechanical{k}}+\mechanical{m}).
\end{aligned}
$$
Using $(\not a+\mechanical{m})(\not a-\mechanical{m})=(a^2-\mechanical{m}^2)\mathbf1$, each term cancels one denominator. Thus
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mechanical{q}_\mu\Lambda^\mu(\mechanical{p}',\mechanical{p})
=-i\electric{e}^2\energy{\mu}^{2\epsilon}
\int\frac{d^d\mechanical{k}}{(2\pi)^d}
\frac{\gamma^\alpha}{\mechanical{k}^2+i0}
\left[
\frac{\not{\mechanical{p}}-\not{\mechanical{k}}+\mechanical{m}}
{(\mechanical{p}-\mechanical{k})^2-\mechanical{m}^2+i0}
-\frac{\not{\mechanical{p}}'-\not{\mechanical{k}}+\mechanical{m}}
{(\mechanical{p}'-\mechanical{k})^2-\mechanical{m}^2+i0}
\right]\gamma_\alpha.
\end{aligned}
$$

**(c)** The self-energy is defined by
$$
-i\energy{\Sigma}(\mechanical{p})
=(-i\electric{e})^2\energy{\mu}^{2\epsilon}
\int\frac{d^d\mechanical{k}}{(2\pi)^d}
\frac{-ig_{\alpha\beta}}{\mechanical{k}^2+i0}
\gamma^\alpha
\frac{i(\not{\mechanical{p}}-\not{\mechanical{k}}+\mechanical{m})}
{(\mechanical{p}-\mechanical{k})^2-\mechanical{m}^2+i0}
\gamma^\beta.
$$
Comparing the two integrals,
$$
\mechanical{q}_\mu\Lambda^\mu(\mechanical{p}',\mechanical{p})
=\energy{\Sigma}(\mechanical{p})-\energy{\Sigma}(\mechanical{p}').
$$
For the reduced inverse propagator
$$
\signal{S}_F^{-1}(\mechanical{p})=\not{\mechanical{p}}-\mechanical{m}-\energy{\Sigma}(\mechanical{p}),
$$
where the propagator's overall factor $i$ is removed, its one-loop correction is $-\energy{\Sigma}$. Hence
$$
\mechanical{q}_\mu\Lambda^\mu(\mechanical{p}',\mechanical{p})
=\signal{S}_F^{-1}(\mechanical{p}')\big|_{\text{one loop}}
-\signal{S}_F^{-1}(\mechanical{p})\big|_{\text{one loop}}.
$$
Adding the tree vertex gives (9.72):
$$
\mechanical{q}_\mu\Gamma^\mu(\mechanical{p}',\mechanical{p})
=\signal{S}_F^{-1}(\mechanical{p}')-\signal{S}_F^{-1}(\mechanical{p}).
$$
The source's final self-energy difference agrees with this convention once the overall vertex-integral sign identified above is corrected.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{p},\mechanical{p}'$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Electron momenta | The two external arguments of the vertex and self-energy difference. |
| $\mechanical{k}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Loop photon momentum | The common routing used on both sides of the identity. |
| $\mechanical{q}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | External photon momentum | The difference of the outgoing and incoming electron momenta. |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Electron mass | The mass in the inverse Dirac factors. |
| $\energy{\mu}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Renormalization scale | The mass scale in dimensional regularization. |
| $\electric{e}$ | $1$ | Electromagnetic coupling | The dimensionless coupling after the scale factor is extracted. |
| $\energy{\Sigma}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Electron self-energy | The correction obtained after canceling one internal electron denominator. |
| $\signal{S}_F$ | $\unit{MeV^{-1}}$ | Electron propagator | Its reduced inverse Dirac denominator is used in the Ward identity. |
