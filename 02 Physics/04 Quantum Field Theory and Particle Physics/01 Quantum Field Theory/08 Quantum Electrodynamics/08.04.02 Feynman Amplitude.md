---
title: "Feynman Amplitude"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The tree amplitude is the product of electron and muon currents joined by the photon propagator.

Keep the external spins fixed at this stage. Each bracket is a transition current for one lepton species; the propagator transfers four-momentum between them and contracts their Lorentz indices. The result is a complex probability amplitude, not yet a rate or cross section.

Apply the Feynman rules to Fig. 8.1:
$$
i\signal{\mathcal M}
=\left[\bar v^{s'}(\bar{\mechanical{p}})
(-i\electric{e}\gamma^\mu)u^s(\mechanical{p})\right]
\frac{-ig_{\mu\nu}}{\mechanical{q}^{\,2}}
\left[\bar u^r(\mechanical{k})
(-i\electric{e}\gamma^\nu)v^{r'}(\bar{\mechanical{k}})\right].
$$
Thus
$$
\signal{\mathcal M}
=\frac{\electric{e}^{\,2}}{\mechanical{q}^{\,2}}
\left[\bar v^{s'}(\bar{\mechanical{p}})\gamma^\mu u^s(\mechanical{p})\right]
\left[\bar u^r(\mechanical{k})\gamma_\mu v^{r'}(\bar{\mechanical{k}})\right].
$$

The two vertex factors and photon propagator give the displayed overall phase for the convention in which the diagram equals the amplitude multiplied by the imaginary unit. An overall common phase drops out of a single squared amplitude, but relative phases matter when another diagram contributes.

There is a useful normalization check: four external spinors supply two powers of energy and the propagator removes two, so this two-to-two invariant amplitude is dimensionless. The on-shell Dirac equations also make each current transverse to the internal photon momentum, which removes propagator terms proportional to that momentum.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{\mathcal M}$ | $1$ | Invariant amplitude | The tree-level two-to-two scattering amplitude. |
| $\electric{e}$ | $1$ | Electromagnetic coupling | The lepton charge parameter at both vertices. |
| $\mechanical{p},\bar{\mechanical{p}},\mechanical{k},\bar{\mechanical{k}}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | External four-momenta | The incoming and outgoing lepton momenta. |
| $\mechanical{q}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Virtual photon momentum | The momentum through the internal photon. |
| $u^s,\bar v^{s'},\bar u^r,v^{r'}$ | $\unit{MeV^{1/2}}$ | External spinors | The normalized electron, positron, muon, and antimuon factors. |
