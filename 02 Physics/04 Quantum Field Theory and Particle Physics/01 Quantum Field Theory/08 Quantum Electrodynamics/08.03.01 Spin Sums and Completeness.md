---
title: "Spin Sums and Completeness"
---

> [!TLDR]
> For unpolarized beams, initial spins are averaged and unobserved final spins are summed; completeness relations turn fermion bilinears into traces.

For equally populated, incoherent initial spin states, average over the initial spins and sum over unobserved final spins. Lack of a spin measurement alone does not prove that the beam is unpolarized. The average describes the preparation of the incident ensemble; the final sum describes distinct outcomes accepted by the measurement. The Dirac-spinor completeness relations are
$$
\sum_{s=1,2}u^s(\mechanical{p})\bar u^s(\mechanical{p})
=\not{\mechanical{p}}+\mechanical{m},
$$
$$
\sum_{s=1,2}v^s(\mechanical{p})\bar v^s(\mechanical{p})
=\not{\mechanical{p}}-\mechanical{m}.
$$
These formulas convert sums of products of fermion bilinears into traces of products of gamma matrices. Each outer product keeps the row and column order shown. Insert it only after multiplying the amplitude by its complex conjugate; summing the amplitude over spin first would introduce interference between orthogonal spin states.

The relations use on-shell momenta and the spinor normalization of this chapter. Each outer product has one power of energy, consistent with the momentum and mass on the right. The opposite mass signs for particle and antiparticle spin sums follow their different Dirac equations.

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
