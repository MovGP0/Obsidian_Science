---
title: "Traces of Gamma Matrices"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Clifford algebra gives the gamma-matrix traces used to reduce spin-averaged scattering amplitudes.

Trace reduction replaces spinor-basis details by Lorentz contractions. Its signs therefore matter physically: an incorrect middle sign in the four-gamma trace changes the angular distribution of a scattering cross section. Keep the matrix order fixed and use cyclicity only to rotate the complete product.

The following trace identities follow from the Clifford algebra
$$
\{\gamma^\mu,\gamma^\nu\}=2g^{\mu\nu}.
$$
In four dimensions,
$$
\operatorname{tr}[\mathbf1]=4,
$$
$$
\operatorname{tr}[\gamma^\mu]=0,
\tag{8.3}
$$
$$
\operatorname{tr}[\gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu]=4g^{\mu\nu},
$$
$$
\operatorname{tr}[\gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu\gamma^\rho]=0,
\tag{8.4}
$$
$$
\operatorname{tr}[\gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu\gamma^\rho\gamma^\sigma]
=4(g^{\mu\nu}g^{\rho\sigma}
-g^{\mu\rho}g^{\nu\sigma}
+g^{\mu\sigma}g^{\nu\rho}),
\tag{8.5}
$$
$$
\operatorname{tr}[\gamma^5]=0,
\qquad
\operatorname{tr}[\gamma^5\gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu]=0,
$$
$$
\operatorname{tr}[\gamma^5\gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu\gamma^\rho\gamma^\sigma]
=-4i\epsilon^{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}.
$$

The final identity also depends on the definitions of gamma five, the metric, and the orientation tensor. Its sign is a convention package, not a separately adjustable choice. These are four-dimensional identities; a loop calculation continued away from four dimensions requires separate treatment, especially for gamma five.

As an algebraic check, take the four arguments in (8.6) equal. The three terms combine with signs plus, minus, plus, leaving four times the square of the vector norm, as required by the Clifford algebra.

Useful consequences for arbitrary four-vector arguments $a,b,c,d$ are
$$
\operatorname{tr}[\not a\not b]=4(a\cdot b),
$$
$$
\operatorname{tr}[\not a\not b\not c\not d]
=4[(a\cdot b)(c\cdot d)
-(a\cdot c)(b\cdot d)
+(a\cdot d)(b\cdot c)].
\tag{8.6}
$$
