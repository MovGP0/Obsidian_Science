---
title: "Vacuum Polarization"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Gauge invariance and current conservation force the one-loop photon polarization tensor to be transverse.

Vacuum polarization is the one-loop correction to the photon propagator, shown in Fig. 8.8. It asks whether the charge inferred from scattering is independent of the momentum scale used to probe it. The screening picture is an interpretation of the loop calculation; the internal charged pair is not an observed pair of particles in the event.

Gauge invariance and current conservation require the transverse tensor structure
$$
\electric{\Pi}^{\mu\nu}(\mechanical{q})
=(\mechanical{q}^{\,2}g^{\mu\nu}-\mechanical{q}^\mu\mechanical{q}^\nu)
\electric{\Pi}(\mechanical{q}^{\,2}).
$$

![[QFT-08-08-Vacuum-Polarization.svg|**Fig. 8.8** Vacuum polarization: a fermion-loop insertion in the photon propagator.]]

The resummed photon propagator has the form
$$
\signal{D}^{\mu\nu}(\mechanical{q})
=\frac{-ig^{\mu\nu}}
{\mechanical{q}^{\,2}[1-\electric{\Pi}(\mechanical{q}^{\,2})]}
+\text{longitudinal part}.
$$
Current conservation removes the longitudinal contribution from physical amplitudes. For $|\mechanical{q}^{\,2}|\gg\mechanical{m}_e^2$,
$$
\electric{\Pi}(\mechanical{q}^{\,2})
=\frac{\electric{\alpha}_{\mathrm{em}}}{3\pi}
\left[
\ln\left(\frac{-\mechanical{q}^{\,2}}{\mechanical{m}_e^2}\right)
-\frac53
+\mathcal O\left(\frac{\mechanical{m}_e^2}{\mechanical{q}^{\,2}}\right)
\right].
\tag{8.16}
$$

The quantum interpretation is **dielectric screening**. Virtual electron–positron pairs polarize the vacuum and screen electric charge at large distances. The effective fine-structure constant therefore depends on the energy scale:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\electric{\alpha}_{\mathrm{em}}(\mechanical{q}^{\,2})
&=\frac{\electric{\alpha}_{\mathrm{em}}}
{1-\electric{\Pi}(\mechanical{q}^{\,2})}\\
&\approx\electric{\alpha}_{\mathrm{em}}
\left[
1+\frac{\electric{\alpha}_{\mathrm{em}}}{3\pi}
\ln\left(\frac{|\mechanical{q}^{\,2}|}{\mechanical{m}_e^2}\right)
\right].
\end{aligned}
$$

At the Z-boson mass scale,
$$
\mechanical{q}^{\,2}=\mechanical{M}_Z^2\approx(91.2\,\unit{GeV})^2,
$$
the source quotes $\electric{\alpha}_{\mathrm{em}}(\mechanical{M}_Z^2)\approx1/128$. This includes contributions from all charged fermions: leptons and the hadronic contribution extracted from $\mechanical{R}(\energy{s})$ through dispersion relations. It differs substantially from the Thomson value $\electric{\alpha}_{\mathrm{em}}(0)=1/137.036$. The single electron loop in (8.16) accounts for only part of this running.

> [!note]
> The logarithm in (8.16) is real for spacelike $\mechanical{q}^{\,2}<0$. At timelike momentum it requires the causal analytic continuation and can have an imaginary part. The quoted real running-coupling estimate uses the corresponding real-part convention.

Contracting the tensor with its momentum gives zero term by term between the metric and momentum-product pieces. Its scalar coefficient is dimensionless, while the tensor has two powers of energy, as required for an inverse-propagator correction. The renormalized coupling must also be tied to a reference measurement; the large-momentum expression (8.16) cannot be extrapolated to zero transfer.

Expanding the resummed denominator once recovers the single insertion. Keeping the denominator unexpanded includes a selected chain of repeated insertions, not every higher-order QED diagram. The source's Z-scale benchmark includes more charged matter than the displayed electron loop and must retain that distinction.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\electric{\Pi}^{\mu\nu}$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Vacuum-polarization tensor | The transverse photon inverse-propagator correction. |
| $\electric{\Pi}$ | $1$ | Scalar polarization function | The scalar coefficient of the transverse tensor. |
| $\mechanical{q}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Photon four-momentum | The external momentum through the polarization insertion. |
| $\signal{D}^{\mu\nu}$ | $\unit{MeV^{-2}}$ | Photon propagator | The resummed propagator including polarization insertions. |
| $\electric{\alpha}_{\mathrm{em}}$ | $1$ | Fine-structure constant | The low-energy coupling and its effective momentum-dependent value. |
| $\mechanical{m}_e$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Electron mass | The mass scale in the electron-loop result. |
| $\mechanical{M}_Z$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Z-boson mass | The reference electroweak mass scale. |
| $\mechanical{R}$ | $1$ | Hadronic production ratio | The measured hadronic-to-muonic ratio used in the dispersion relation. |
| $\energy{s}$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Squared collision energy | The energy argument of the hadronic production ratio. |
