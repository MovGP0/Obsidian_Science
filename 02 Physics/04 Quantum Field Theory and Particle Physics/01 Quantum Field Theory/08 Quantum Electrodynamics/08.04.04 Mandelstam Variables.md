---
title: "Mandelstam Variables"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Mandelstam invariants express the annihilation probability in Lorentz-invariant form and simplify it at high energy.

The same collision can be described in many inertial frames. Mandelstam variables keep the dynamical result independent of that choice: the first measures total energy squared, while the other two distinguish which outgoing particle is paired with the incoming electron. Their definitions depend on the external-particle labels and must travel with any crossing argument.

For this two-to-two process, define
$$
\begin{aligned}
\energy{s}&=(\mechanical{p}+\bar{\mechanical{p}})^2
=(\mechanical{k}+\bar{\mechanical{k}})^2,\\
\mechanical{t}&=(\mechanical{p}-\mechanical{k})^2
=(\bar{\mechanical{p}}-\bar{\mechanical{k}})^2,\\
\mechanical{u}&=(\mechanical{p}-\bar{\mechanical{k}})^2
=(\bar{\mechanical{p}}-\mechanical{k})^2.
\end{aligned}
$$
They satisfy
$$
\energy{s}+\mechanical{t}+\mechanical{u}
=2\mechanical{m}_e^2+2\mechanical{m}_\mu^2.
$$

At high energy, $\sqrt{\energy{s}}\gg\mechanical{m}_e,\mechanical{m}_\mu$, neglect the masses. Then $\mechanical{q}^{\,2}=\energy{s}$ and
$$
\mechanical{p}\cdot\mechanical{k}=-\frac{\mechanical{t}}2,\qquad
\mechanical{p}\cdot\bar{\mechanical{k}}=-\frac{\mechanical{u}}2,\qquad
\bar{\mechanical{p}}\cdot\mechanical{k}=-\frac{\mechanical{u}}2,\qquad
\bar{\mechanical{p}}\cdot\bar{\mechanical{k}}=-\frac{\mechanical{t}}2.
$$

In the relativistic massless limit, (8.8) becomes
$$
\left.
\signal{L}^{(e)}_{\mu\nu}\signal{L}^{(m)\mu\nu}
\right|_{\mechanical{m}_e=\mechanical{m}_\mu=0}
=8[(\mechanical{p}\cdot\mechanical{k})(\bar{\mechanical{p}}\cdot\bar{\mechanical{k}})
+(\mechanical{p}\cdot\bar{\mechanical{k}})(\bar{\mechanical{p}}\cdot\mechanical{k})].
$$

Therefore,
$$
\left.
\signal{L}^{(e)}_{\mu\nu}\signal{L}^{(m)\mu\nu}
\right|_{\mechanical{m}_e=\mechanical{m}_\mu=0}
=8\left(\frac{\mechanical{t}^{\,2}}4+\frac{\mechanical{u}^{\,2}}4\right)
=2(\mechanical{t}^{\,2}+\mechanical{u}^{\,2}).
$$
It follows that
$$
\left.\overline{|\signal{\mathcal M}|^2}\right|_{\mechanical{m}_e=\mechanical{m}_\mu=0}
=\frac{\electric{e}^{\,4}}{\energy{s}^{\,2}}
\,2(\mechanical{t}^{\,2}+\mechanical{u}^{\,2})
=\frac{2\electric{e}^{\,4}(\mechanical{t}^{\,2}+\mechanical{u}^{\,2})}{\energy{s}^{\,2}}.
$$

In the physical massless region, the total-energy invariant is positive and both transfer invariants are nonpositive. Their sum is zero. At a right-angle centre-of-mass event, the two transfers are equal, each minus half the total-energy invariant. Substitution makes the squared amplitude positive and dimensionless.

Neglecting the masses is appropriate far above threshold at fixed angle. It is not a prescription for threshold kinematics, and in processes with collinear singularities a small mass can remain important even at high energy.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\energy{s}$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Squared centre-of-mass energy | The invariant total energy squared. |
| $\mechanical{t},\mechanical{u}$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Mandelstam transfers | The two momentum-transfer invariants. |
| $\mechanical{p},\bar{\mechanical{p}},\mechanical{k},\bar{\mechanical{k}}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | External four-momenta | The incoming electron pair and outgoing muon pair momenta. |
| $\mechanical{m}_e,\mechanical{m}_\mu$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Lepton masses | The masses neglected in the high-energy limit. |
| $\mechanical{q}$ | $\unit{MeV}$ | Virtual photon momentum | The total incoming four-momentum. |
| $\signal{L}^{(e)}_{\mu\nu},\signal{L}^{(m)\mu\nu}$ | $\unit{MeV^2}$ | Leptonic tensors | The electron spin average and muon spin sum. |
| $\signal{\mathcal M}$ | $1$ | Invariant amplitude | The tree-level annihilation amplitude. |
| $\electric{e}$ | $1$ | Electromagnetic coupling | The charge parameter in the squared amplitude. |
