title: "Preview of Loop Corrections"
---

> [!TLDR]
> One-loop QED corrections give measurable changes to tree-level results, including the anomalous magnetic moment.

The tree-level calculations above are only the first approximation. Loop corrections have a typical expansion factor
$$
\frac{\electric{\alpha}_{\mathrm{em}}}{\pi}\approx2.3\times10^{-3}.
$$
They give measurable effects, including the anomalous magnetic moment. This chapter gives a qualitative preview; Chapter 9 develops the detailed calculations.

Three corrections modify different parts of a tree diagram. [[08.12.01 Vacuum Polarization]] changes photon propagation and the effective charge; [[08.12.02 Vertex Correction and Anomalous Magnetic Moment]] changes the electron's response to an external field; [[08.12.03 Fermion Self-Energy]] changes the electron inverse propagator. Gauge symmetry links these corrections, so they cannot be assigned unrelated normalization conventions.

The quoted expansion factor is a typical size estimate. Kinematic logarithms can enhance it, thresholds can change the useful expansion, and ultraviolet divergent terms need renormalization before comparison with a measurement. In an observable scattering rate, the loop contribution can also require unresolved real radiation to remove infrared regulator dependence.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
