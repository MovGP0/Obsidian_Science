---
title: "Møller Scattering — e⁻e⁻ → e⁻e⁻"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Møller scattering describes electron–electron collisions and requires antisymmetry under exchange of identical fermions.

Møller scattering is $\mathrm e^-\mathrm e^-\to\mathrm e^-\mathrm e^-$. Can a final electron be identified as the continuation of one particular incident electron? Quantum mechanics assigns no such particle label to an observable final state. Both assignments contribute, with their relative sign fixed by fermion antisymmetry.

[[08.07.01 Diagrams and the Exclusion Principle]] develops the two channels and their high-energy squared amplitude. Distinguish antisymmetry of the amplitude from symmetry of the measured probability under exchange of the outgoing electrons. When integrating over labeled final momenta, identical-state counting also requires care; that counting factor is separate from the exchange minus sign.
