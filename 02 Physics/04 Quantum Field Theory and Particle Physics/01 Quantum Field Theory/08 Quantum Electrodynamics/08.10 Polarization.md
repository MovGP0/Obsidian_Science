title: "Polarization"
---

> [!TLDR]
> QED polarization rules distinguish the two physical transverse photon states from unphysical gauge components.

Polarization records information that an unpolarized cross section discards. The first question is which photon states can be prepared or detected; the second is how to sum over them without introducing gauge artifacts. [[08.10.01 Transverse and Longitudinal Polarization]] defines the physical basis, and [[08.10.02 Sum over Physical Polarizations]] explains when a covariant replacement is valid.

[[08.10.03 Helicity Asymmetries]] then keeps fermion spin information to expose angular-momentum selection. Keep a fixed-polarization amplitude until its probability is formed; average only over the actual incident ensemble. As a reconstruction check, summing the allowed final helicities and averaging the four initial electron–positron assignments must recover the unpolarized muon-pair distribution.
