---
title: "Composite Systems and Partial Trace"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The partial trace gives the state seen by local measurements. A pure entangled state can have mixed states on both subsystems.

A composite system $AB$ has Hilbert space $\mathcal H_A\otimes\mathcal H_B$ and density operator $\hat\rho_{AB}$. To describe $A$ alone, take the **partial trace** over $B$.

$$
\hat\rho_A=\operatorname{Tr}_B\hat\rho_{AB}
=\sum_n(\hat I_A\otimes\langle n\rvert_B)\hat\rho_{AB}
(\hat I_A\otimes\lvert n\rangle_B),
$$

where $\{\lvert n\rangle_B\}$ is any orthonormal basis of $\mathcal H_B$. The result is independent of the basis. It is the unique operator that gives the expectation of every local observable $\hat O_A\otimes\hat I_B$ through $\langle\hat O_A\rangle=\operatorname{Tr}_A(\hat\rho_A\hat O_A)$.

## Example. A Bell state

The state

$$
\lvert\Phi^+\rangle
=\frac{\lvert0\rangle_A\lvert0\rangle_B+\lvert1\rangle_A\lvert1\rangle_B}{\sqrt2}
$$

is pure, with $\hat\rho_{AB}=\lvert\Phi^+\rangle\langle\Phi^+\rvert$. The partial trace over $B$ gives

$$
\hat\rho_A=\tfrac12(\lvert0\rangle_A\langle0\rvert_A+\lvert1\rangle_A\langle1\rvert_A)
=\tfrac12\hat I_A.
$$

The local state is maximally mixed with $\mathcal P(\hat\rho_A)=1/2$. The joint state contains correlations unavailable from either reduced state alone. See [[15.00 Entanglement, EPR, and Quantum Information]] for their measurement consequences.

> [!note] Remark 13.2. Local purity and entanglement
> For a pure joint state, local purity quantifies entanglement. In particular, $\mathcal P(\hat\rho_A)=1$ if and only if the joint state is a product $\lvert\signal{\psi}_{AB}\rangle=\lvert\signal{\psi}_A\rangle\otimes\lvert\signal{\psi}_B\rangle$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\hat\rho_{AB},\hat\rho_A$ | $1$ | Joint and reduced density operators | The state of both subsystems and the state of $A$ alone. |
| $\hat O_A$ | Depends on observable | Local observable | An operator measured only on subsystem $A$. |
| $\lvert\Phi^+\rangle$ | $1$ | Bell state | The pure two-qubit state in the example. |
| $\mathcal P(\hat\rho_A)$ | $1$ | Local purity | The trace of the squared reduced density operator. |
