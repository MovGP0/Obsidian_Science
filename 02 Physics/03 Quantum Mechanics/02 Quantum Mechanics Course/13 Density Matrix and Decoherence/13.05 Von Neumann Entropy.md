---
title: "Von Neumann Entropy"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Von Neumann entropy is the Shannon entropy of the density-operator eigenvalues. For a pure bipartite state, the equal entropies of its two reduced states quantify entanglement.

The **von Neumann entropy** is

$$
\thermal S(\hat\rho)=-\operatorname{Tr}(\hat\rho\log\hat\rho)
=-\sum_n\lambda_n\log\lambda_n,
$$

where $\lambda_n$ are the eigenvalues of $\hat\rho$ and $0\log0=0$. It is the quantum counterpart of Shannon entropy. It is invariant under unitary transformations because it depends only on the eigenvalues.

## Properties

- $\thermal S(\hat\rho)\ge0$, with equality exactly for a pure state.
- In finite dimension $d$, $\thermal S(\hat\rho)\le\log d$, with equality exactly for the maximally mixed state $\hat I/d$.
- **Subadditivity.** $\thermal S(\hat\rho_{AB})\le\thermal S(\hat\rho_A)+\thermal S(\hat\rho_B)$, with equality exactly for $\hat\rho_{AB}=\hat\rho_A\otimes\hat\rho_B$ when the entropies are finite.
- **Strong subadditivity.** $\thermal S(\hat\rho_{ABC})+\thermal S(\hat\rho_B)\le\thermal S(\hat\rho_{AB})+\thermal S(\hat\rho_{BC})$.
- For a pure joint state $\lvert\signal{\psi}_{AB}\rangle$, the two reduced states have equal entropy, $\thermal S(\hat\rho_A)=\thermal S(\hat\rho_B)$.

The marginal entropy of a pure bipartite state quantifies the entanglement shared by the two subsystems. For a Bell pair, each reduced state has $\thermal S=\log2$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\hat\rho,\hat\rho_{AB},\hat\rho_A,\hat\rho_B$ | $1$ | Density operators | The general, joint, and reduced states used in the entropy formulas. |
| $\lambda_n$ | $1$ | Density eigenvalue | The statistical weight in the spectral decomposition. |
| $\thermal S$ | $1$ | Von Neumann entropy | The dimensionless entropy, in nats with natural logarithms or bits with base-two logarithms. |
| $d$ | $1$ | Dimension | The dimension of the finite state space in the upper bound. |
