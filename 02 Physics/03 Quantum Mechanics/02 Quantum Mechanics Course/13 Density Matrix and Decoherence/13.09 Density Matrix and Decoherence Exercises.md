---
title: "Density Matrix and Decoherence Exercises"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Construct density operators and calculate their purity, entropy, reduced states, and time evolution.

1. Write the density matrix for $\lvert\signal{\psi}\rangle=\cos(\theta/2)\lvert0\rangle+\sin(\theta/2)\lvert1\rangle$ and verify $\hat\rho^2=\hat\rho$.  
   **Result.**
   $$
   \hat\rho=\begin{pmatrix}
   \cos^2(\theta/2)&\sin(\theta/2)\cos(\theta/2)\\
   \sin(\theta/2)\cos(\theta/2)&\sin^2(\theta/2)
   \end{pmatrix},
   \qquad \operatorname{Tr}(\hat\rho^2)=1.
   $$
2. Show that $\hat\rho=\hat I/2$ describes both an equal mixture of $\lvert0\rangle,\lvert1\rangle$ and an equal mixture of $\lvert\pm\rangle=(\lvert0\rangle\pm\lvert1\rangle)/\sqrt2$.  
   **Result.** Both ensembles give $\hat I/2$ and the same measurement statistics.
3. Calculate the purity of $\hat\rho=(3\lvert\uparrow\rangle\langle\uparrow\rvert+\lvert\downarrow\rangle\langle\downarrow\rvert)/4$.  
   **Result.** $\mathcal P=9/16+1/16=5/8$.
4. For a spin-$1/2$ state with $\mathbf a=(1/2,0,0)$, calculate the three spin component means.  
   **Result.** $\langle\mechanical{\hat S}_x\rangle=\mechanical{\hbar}/4$ and $\langle\mechanical{\hat S}_y\rangle=\langle\mechanical{\hat S}_z\rangle=0$.
5. Verify that for orthogonal states $\lvert\signal{\psi}_1\rangle,\lvert\signal{\psi}_2\rangle$ and weights $p_1+p_2=1$, the operator $\hat\rho=p_1\lvert\signal{\psi}_1\rangle\langle\signal{\psi}_1\rvert+p_2\lvert\signal{\psi}_2\rangle\langle\signal{\psi}_2\rvert$ has eigenvalues $p_1,p_2$ on their span.  
   **Result.** The decomposition is already spectral.
6. Trace out the second qubit of $\lvert\Phi^+\rangle\langle\Phi^+\rvert$, where $\lvert\Phi^+\rangle=(\lvert00\rangle+\lvert11\rangle)/\sqrt2$.  
   **Result.** $\hat\rho_A=\hat I_A/2$, the maximally mixed state.
7. Calculate $\hat\rho_A$ for the product state $\lvert\signal{\psi}\rangle=[(\lvert0\rangle+\lvert1\rangle)/\sqrt2]\otimes\lvert0\rangle$.  
   **Result.** $\hat\rho_A=(\lvert0\rangle+\lvert1\rangle)(\langle0\rvert+\langle1\rvert)/2$ is pure with $\mathcal P=1$.
8. Calculate the von Neumann entropy of $\hat\rho=\operatorname{diag}(1/2,1/3,1/6)$.  
   **Result.** $\thermal S=(\log2)/2+(\log3)/3+(\log6)/6\simeq1.011$ nats.
9. For $\hat\rho=(\hat I+a\sigma_z)/2$ with $a\in[0,1]$, write the density matrix and calculate its entropy.  
   **Result.** $\thermal S(a)=-[(1+a)/2]\log[(1+a)/2]-[(1-a)/2]\log[(1-a)/2]$, with $\thermal S(0)=\log2$ and $\thermal S(1)=0$.
10. For spin $1/2$ in $\magnetic{\mathbf B}=\magnetic B\hat{\mathbf z}$ with $\energy{\hat H}=-\magnetic{\gamma}\magnetic B\mechanical{\hat S}_z$, solve the Liouville–von Neumann equation for $\hat\rho(0)=(\hat I+a_x(0)\sigma_x)/2$.  
    **Result.** The Bloch vector rotates in the $xy$ plane. With signed $\mechanical{\omega}=\magnetic{\gamma}\magnetic B$, $a_x(t)=a_x(0)\cos(\mechanical{\omega}t)$ and $a_y(t)=-a_x(0)\sin(\mechanical{\omega}t)$.
11. In the Lindblad equation, let $\hat L=\sqrt{\gamma}\sigma_z$ and $\energy{\hat H}=0$. Write equations for the entries $\rho_{ij}$ and show that $\rho_{00},\rho_{11}$ stay constant while $\rho_{01}(t)=\rho_{01}(0)e^{-2\gamma t}$. Find $T_\phi$.  
    **Result.** $\dot\rho_{01}=-2\gamma\rho_{01}$, so $T_\phi=1/(2\gamma)$. This is pure dephasing in the computational basis.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\hat\rho,\hat\rho_A,\rho_{ij}$ | $1$ | Density operators and entries | The full and reduced states and their matrix elements. |
| $\mathcal P,\thermal S$ | $1$ | Purity and entropy | The squared trace and spectral entropy, with natural logarithms for entropy in nats. |
| $a,\mathbf a,\sigma_i$ | $1$ | Bloch coordinates and Pauli matrices | The coordinates of the qubit state and the operator basis. |
| $\mechanical{\hat S}_i$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Spin component | The operator whose expectation is proportional to the corresponding Bloch coordinate. |
| $\magnetic{\mathbf B},\magnetic{\gamma}$ | $\unit T,\unit{rad\,s^{-1}\,T^{-1}}$ | Magnetic field and gyromagnetic ratio | The field and signed coupling in the precession problem. |
| $\energy{\hat H},\mechanical{\omega}$ | $\unit{J},\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Hamiltonian and angular frequency | The spin interaction energy and its signed precession parameter. |
| $\hat L,\gamma$ | $\unit{s^{-1/2}},\unit{s^{-1}}$ | Jump operator and rate parameter | The normalized dephasing operator and its squared prefactor. |
| $t,T_\phi,\mechanical{\hbar}$ | $\unit s,\unit s,\unit{J\,s}$ | Time, dephasing time, and reduced Planck constant | The time scales and the constant used in the spin operators. |
