---
title: "Many-Particle Systems and Identical Particles Exercises"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Use exchange symmetry, spin coupling, and orbital filling to analyze many-particle states.

1. Verify that the singlet $\lvert\Psi^-\rangle=(\lvert+-\rangle-\lvert-+\rangle)/\sqrt2$ is invariant under a common rotation of both spins.
2. Write the four states of two spin-$1/2$ particles in the coupled basis $\lvert s,m_s\rangle$.
3. For two identical bosons in an infinite well, write the ground-state wave function and calculate its energy.  
   **Result.** $\signal{\phi}_1(x_1)\signal{\phi}_1(x_2)$ with $\energy E=2\energy E_1$.
4. For two identical fermions without spin in an infinite well, write the ground state and its energy.  
   **Result.** $\signal{\Psi}_-=\frac{1}{\sqrt2}\begin{vmatrix}\signal{\phi}_1(x_1)&\signal{\phi}_2(x_1)\\\signal{\phi}_1(x_2)&\signal{\phi}_2(x_2)\end{vmatrix}$ and $\energy{E}=\energy{E}_1+\energy{E}_2$.
5. Show that a Slater determinant is zero if two rows are identical.
6. For two bosons in an infinite well, use the symmetric state $\signal{\Psi}_+=[\signal{\phi}_1(x_1)\signal{\phi}_2(x_2)+\signal{\phi}_2(x_1)\signal{\phi}_1(x_2)]/\sqrt2$. Calculate $\langle(x_1-x_2)^2\rangle$ and compare with the result for distinguishable particles.  
   **Hint.** The mean squared separation is smaller for bosons. This is bosonic bunching.
7. Repeat Exercise 6 for two fermions in the antisymmetric state $\signal{\Psi}_-=[\signal{\phi}_1(x_1)\signal{\phi}_2(x_2)-\signal{\phi}_2(x_1)\signal{\phi}_1(x_2)]/\sqrt2$.  
   **Hint.** The mean squared separation is larger for fermions. This is Pauli antibunching.
8. Show that $\langle\signal{\Psi}_-\mid\signal{\Psi}_+\rangle=0$. Calculate all pairwise overlaps between the four Bell states.
9. For helium, use the trial spatial wave function $\signal{\psi}_T=\signal{\phi}_{1s}(Z_{\mathrm{eff}};1)\signal{\phi}_{1s}(Z_{\mathrm{eff}};2)$ with the spin singlet. Derive the variational energy as a function of $Z_{\mathrm{eff}}$ and minimize it.  
   **Result.** $Z_{\mathrm{eff}}=Z-5/16\approx1.69$ and $\energy{E}_0^{\mathrm{var}}\approx\quantity{-77.5}{eV}$.
10. Use Hund's rules to find the ground term $^{2S+1}L_J$ for two electrons in a $p$ subshell.  
    **Result.** $^3P_0$.
11. Consider $N$ noninteracting fermions in a three-dimensional box. Calculate the Fermi energy $\energy{E}_F$ as a function of the number density $n=N/\mechanical{V}$ and show that $\energy{E}_F\propto n^{2/3}$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $s,m_s$ | $1$ | Spin quantum numbers | Total spin and its projection in units of $\mechanical{\hbar}$. |
| $\signal{\phi}_n,\signal{\Psi}_\pm$ | $\unit{m^{-1/2}},\unit{m^{-1}}$ | Spatial wave functions | A normalized well orbital and the normalized two-particle states in the well. |
| $x_1,x_2$ | $\unit{m}$ | Positions | The two particle coordinates along the well. |
| $\energy{E},\energy{E}_n,\energy{E}_0^{\mathrm{var}},\energy{E}_F$ | $\unit{J}$ | Energies | Total energy, orbital energy, variational ground-state energy, and Fermi energy. |
| $Z,Z_{\mathrm{eff}}$ | $1$ | Charge numbers | The nuclear charge number and the effective charge number in the trial state. |
| $S,L,J$ | $1$ | Term quantum numbers | Total spin, orbital angular momentum, and combined angular momentum in the term symbol. |
| $N,\mechanical{V},n$ | $1,\unit{m^3},\unit{m^{-3}}$ | Particle count, volume, and number density | The gas variables related by $n=N/\mechanical{V}$. |
