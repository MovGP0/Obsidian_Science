> [!TLDR]
> Screening changes orbital energies in multielectron atoms. The resulting subshell order helps explain the periodic table.

In a many-electron atom, each electron can be approximated as moving in an effective mean potential $\energy V_{\mathrm{eff}}(r)$. It includes nuclear attraction and the average repulsion from the other electrons, which screens the nucleus. This potential differs from a pure inverse-distance potential. The Coulomb $\mathrm{SO}(4)$ symmetry is broken, and the energy can depend on both $n$ and $\ell$.

A useful approximate filling order is

$$
\begin{aligned}
1s&<2s<2p<3s<3p<4s<3d<4p<5s\\
&<4d<5p<6s<4f<5d<6p<\cdots.
\end{aligned}
$$

At fixed $n$, smaller $\ell$ generally allows stronger penetration toward the nucleus because the centrifugal term is smaller. Such levels can be more strongly bound. Filling the available states with the exclusion rule accounts for the sequence of period lengths $2,8,8,18,18,32$. Actual configurations can depart from this approximate order.

## Hund's rules

For a partially filled subshell in the regime of Russell–Saunders coupling, the lowest-energy term typically has the following labels.

1. Maximize the total spin $S$, which favors parallel spins when the orbitals permit them.
2. At the resulting $S$, maximize the total orbital label $L$.
3. At fixed $L,S$, choose $J=\lvert L-S\rvert$ for a less-than-half-filled subshell and $J=L+S$ for a more-than-half-filled subshell.

These rules account for observed ground term symbols such as $^{2}S_{1/2}$ and $^{3}P_2$.

## Read the ground term of the carbon p subshell

For two electrons in the carbon $2p$ subshell, the largest total spin label is $S=1$. With parallel spins the orbital labels must differ. Their largest allowed sum is $M_L=1+0=1$, which gives $L=1$ for the term of maximum spin and maximum orbital angular momentum. The possible coupled values are $J=0,1,2$. The subshell is less than half filled, so Hund’s third rule selects $J=0$, giving the ground term $\vphantom{P}^{3}P_0$ in the LS-coupling model. The spin multiplicity is $2S+1=3$ and $P$ denotes $L=1$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\energy V_{\mathrm{eff}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Effective potential | The central approximation to nuclear attraction and screening by other electrons. |
| $r$ | $\unit m$ | Radius | The distance of an electron from the nucleus. |
| $n,\ell$ | $1$ | Single-particle quantum numbers | The principal and orbital angular momentum labels of a subshell. |
| $S,L,J$ | $1$ | Term quantum numbers | The total spin, total orbital angular momentum, and combined angular momentum labels. |
