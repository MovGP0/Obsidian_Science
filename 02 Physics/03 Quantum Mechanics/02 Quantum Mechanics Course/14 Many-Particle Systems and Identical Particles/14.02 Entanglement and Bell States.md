title: "Entanglement and Bell States"

> [!TLDR]
> An entangled pure state cannot be factored into individual states. The spin singlet gives perfectly opposite outcomes when both spins are measured along the same axis.

> [!definition] Definition 14.1. Separable and entangled pure states
> A pure state $\lvert\signal{\Psi}\rangle\in\mathcal H_A\otimes\mathcal H_B$ is separable if it can be written as $\lvert\alpha\rangle_A\otimes\lvert\beta\rangle_B$. Otherwise it is entangled.

The four **Bell states** form an orthonormal basis of maximally entangled states for two spin-$1/2$ systems. In the $\mechanical{\hat S}_z$ basis, $\lvert+\rangle=\lvert\uparrow\rangle$ and $\lvert-\rangle=\lvert\downarrow\rangle$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\lvert\Phi^+\rangle&=\frac{\lvert++\rangle+\lvert--\rangle}{\sqrt2},
&\lvert\Phi^-\rangle&=\frac{\lvert++\rangle-\lvert--\rangle}{\sqrt2},\\
\lvert\Psi^+\rangle&=\frac{\lvert+-\rangle+\lvert-+\rangle}{\sqrt2},
&\lvert\Psi^-\rangle&=\frac{\lvert+-\rangle-\lvert-+\rangle}{\sqrt2}.
\end{aligned}
$$

The state $\lvert\Psi^-\rangle$ is the singlet introduced in [[10.00 Spin|Chapter 10]].

## Correlations of the singlet

In the singlet, the probability that both measurements along $z$ give the positive projection is $P(A+,B+)=\lvert\langle++\mid\Psi^-\rangle\rvert^2=0$. The probability of $A+,B-$ is $1/2$. The two outcomes are always opposite. This perfect anticorrelation holds for any shared measurement axis, because a common rotation leaves the singlet unchanged. If Alice and Bob measure along the same axis, Bob always obtains the opposite result to Alice.

## Bell inequalities

With suitably chosen measurement axes, the singlet correlations violate Bell inequalities. Such correlations cannot be reproduced by a local hidden-variable model satisfying the Bell assumptions. The source cites Aspect and colleagues (1982), the loophole-free test of Hensen and colleagues (2015), and the 2022 Nobel Prize. Experiments have confirmed the violations with increasing precision. See [[15.00 Entanglement, EPR, and Quantum Information]] for the detailed treatment.

## Physical meaning

The two parts of an entangled pure state generally have mixed reduced states. The reduced states describe local measurement statistics, while the joint state gives the correlations. Entanglement is a resource in quantum teleportation, entanglement-based quantum key distribution, and quantum computing.
