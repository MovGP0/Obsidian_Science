> [!TLDR]
> Use the Schrödinger equation to calculate probability currents, plane-wave dispersion, and the time evolution of expectation values.

Problems are in [[04.08 Schrödinger Equation Exercises]].

## Exercise 1 · Current for a sum of two plane waves

For $\signal\psi=Ae^{ikx}+Be^{-ikx}$,

$$
J=\frac{\mechanical{\hbar}}{\mechanical m}\operatorname{Im}(\signal\psi^*\partial_x\signal\psi)
=\frac{\mechanical{\hbar}k}{\mechanical m}(\lvert A\rvert^2-\lvert B\rvert^2).
$$

The cross terms in $\signal\psi^*\partial_x\signal\psi$ are $ik(AB^*e^{2ikx}-A^*Be^{-2ikx})=-2k\operatorname{Im}(AB^*e^{2ikx})$, which is real. The diagonal terms give the difference between the fluxes to the right and to the left.

## Exercise 2 · Plane wave as a free solution

For $\signal\psi=e^{i(kx-\mechanical{\hbar}k^2t/(2\mechanical m))}$, the two sides of the free equation both give $(\mechanical{\hbar}^2 k^2/(2\mechanical m))\signal\psi$. The plane wave is a generalized eigenstate of the free Hamiltonian with $\energy E=\mechanical{\hbar}^2 k^2/(2\mechanical m)$.

## Exercise 3 · Phase and group velocities

Use $\omega=\mechanical{\hbar}k^2/(2\mechanical m)$. Then $v_{\mathrm{ph}}=\omega/k=\mechanical{\hbar}k/(2\mechanical m)$ and $v_{\mathrm g}=\mathrm d\omega/\mathrm dk=\mechanical{\hbar}k/\mechanical m=2v_{\mathrm{ph}}$. The group velocity is the velocity of the center of a narrow wave packet, $\mechanical p/\mechanical m$.

## Exercise 4 · Current of a real wave function

If $\signal\psi$ is real, the product $\signal\psi^*\partial_x\signal\psi$ is real and its imaginary part vanishes. Thus $J=0$. A nonzero current requires a spatially varying phase.

## Exercise 5 · Momentum in a real stationary state

For $\signal\psi(x,t)=\signal\psi(x)e^{-i\energy E t/\mechanical{\hbar}}$ with real $\signal\psi(x)$, the time phases cancel in

$$
\langle\mechanical p\rangle=-i\mechanical{\hbar}\int\signal\psi(x)\,\partial_x\signal\psi(x)\,\mathrm dx.
$$

The integrand is $\frac12\partial_x(\signal\psi^2)$, so only a boundary term remains. It vanishes for a bound state with $\signal\psi(\pm\infty)=0$. Thus $\langle\mechanical p\rangle=0$ and $\mathrm d\langle\mechanical p\rangle/\mathrm dt=0$. The real spatial state is a standing wave with a symmetric momentum distribution.

## Exercise 6 · Commutator of position with the Hamiltonian

Since $[\hat x,\energy V(\hat x)]=0$ and $[\hat x,\mechanical{\hat p}^2]=2i\mechanical{\hbar}\mechanical{\hat p}$,

$$
[\hat x,\energy{\hat H}]
=\frac1{2\mechanical m}[\hat x,\mechanical{\hat p}^2]
=\frac{i\mechanical{\hbar}}{\mechanical m}\mechanical{\hat p}.
$$

## Exercise 7 · Explicit evolution of a Gaussian packet

Start with $\signal\phi(k)=(2\sigma^2/\pi)^{1/4}e^{-\sigma^2(k-k_0)^2}$, and define $\beta(t)=\sigma^2+i\mechanical{\hbar}t/(2\mechanical m)$ and $v_{\mathrm g}=\mechanical{\hbar}k_0/\mechanical m$. Complete the square in the Fourier integral.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\signal\psi(x,t)
&=\int\signal\phi(k)e^{i(kx-\mechanical{\hbar}k^2t/(2\mechanical m))}
\frac{\mathrm dk}{\sqrt{2\pi}}\\
&=\frac{e^{-(x-v_{\mathrm g}t)^2/(4\beta(t))+ik_0[x-\mechanical{\hbar}k_0t/(2\mechanical m)]}}
{(2\pi)^{1/4}\sqrt{\beta(t)/\sigma}}.
\end{aligned}
$$

The probability density is

$$
\lvert\signal\psi(x,t)\rvert^2
=\frac{e^{-(x-v_{\mathrm g}t)^2/(2\sigma(t)^2)}}{\sqrt{2\pi}\sigma(t)},
\qquad
\sigma(t)^2=\sigma^2\left[1+\left(\frac{\mechanical{\hbar}t}{2\mechanical m\sigma^2}\right)^2\right].
$$

The width equals $\sigma$ at $t=0$ and grows as $\mechanical{\hbar}t/(2\mechanical m\sigma)$ at large positive $t$.

## Exercise 8 · Ehrenfest equations for the harmonic oscillator

For $\energy V(x)=\mechanical m\omega^2x^2/2$, linearity gives $\langle\energy V'(\hat x)\rangle=\mechanical m\omega^2\langle\hat x\rangle$. Thus $\mathrm d\langle\hat x\rangle/\mathrm dt=\langle\mechanical{\hat p}\rangle/\mechanical m$ and $\mathrm d\langle\mechanical{\hat p}\rangle/\mathrm dt=-\mechanical m\omega^2\langle\hat x\rangle$. Combine them to obtain the harmonic equation. With initial means $x_0,\mechanical p_0$,

$$
\begin{aligned}
\langle\hat x(t)\rangle&=x_0\cos\omega t+\frac{\mechanical p_0}{\mechanical m\omega}\sin\omega t,\\
\langle\mechanical{\hat p}(t)\rangle&=\mechanical p_0\cos\omega t-\mechanical m\omega x_0\sin\omega t.
\end{aligned}
$$

## Exercise 9 · Energy in a time-dependent potential

The evolution law for $\langle\hat A\rangle$ contains $(1/(i\mechanical{\hbar}))\langle[\hat A,\energy{\hat H}]\rangle+\langle\partial_t\hat A\rangle$. Set $\hat A=\energy{\hat H}$. The commutator vanishes. For $\energy V(x,t)=\energy V_0(x)+f(t)x$,

$$
\frac{\mathrm d}{\mathrm dt}\langle\energy{\hat H}\rangle
=\langle\partial_t\energy{\hat H}\rangle
=\dot f(t)\langle\hat x\rangle.
$$

The force term is $-f(t)$. The change of total energy shown here is the parametric power from varying the potential.

## Exercise 10 · Free propagator

Insert the Fourier transform of $\signal\psi(x',0)$ in the free solution. This gives

$$
\signal\psi(x,t)=\int \signal\psi(x',0)\,\mathrm dx'
\int\frac{\mathrm dk}{2\pi}e^{ik(x-x')-i\mechanical{\hbar}k^2 t/(2\mechanical m)}.
$$

Evaluating the Gaussian integral in $k$ with the usual convergence prescription gives

$$
K(x,t;x',0)=\sqrt{\frac{\mechanical m}{2\pi i\mechanical{\hbar}t}}\,
e^{i\mechanical m(x-x')^2/(2\mechanical{\hbar}t)}.
$$

## Exercise 11 · Decay from a non-Hermitian Hamiltonian

Set $\energy{\hat H}_{\mathrm{eff}}=\energy{\hat H}-i\mechanical{\hbar}\gamma\hat I/2$ with Hermitian $\energy{\hat H}$ and $\gamma>0$. Then

$$
\hat U(t)=e^{-i\energy{\hat H}t/\mechanical{\hbar}}e^{-\gamma t/2},
\qquad
\lVert\signal\psi(t)\rVert^2=\lVert\signal\psi(0)\rVert^2e^{-\gamma t}.
$$

The survival probability decays with mean lifetime $\tau=1/\gamma$. The complex level energy $\energy E-i\mechanical{\hbar}\gamma/2$ gives a Lorentzian with full width $\energy\Gamma=\mechanical{\hbar}\gamma$ in the propagator. This is the effective resonance model used here.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $J$ | $\unit{s^{-1}}$ in one dimension | Probability current | The difference of directed probability fluxes. |
| $\sigma,\sigma(t)$ | $\unit{m}$ | Initial and evolved widths | The standard deviations of the position density. |
| $v_{\mathrm{ph}},v_{\mathrm g}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Phase and group velocities | The velocity of a phase crest and the velocity of the packet center. |
| $K(x,t;x',0)$ | $\unit{m^{-1}}$ | Free propagator | The kernel that evolves the initial one-dimensional wave function. |
| $\gamma,\tau,\energy\Gamma$ | $\unit{s^{-1}},\unit{s},\unit{J}$ | Decay rate, lifetime, and width | The scales of exponential survival and its energy resonance. |
