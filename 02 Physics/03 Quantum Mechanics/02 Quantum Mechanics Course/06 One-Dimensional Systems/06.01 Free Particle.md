---
title: "Free Particle"
---

> [!TLDR]
> A free particle on the full line has a continuous nonnegative energy spectrum and plane-wave generalized eigenstates.

For $\energy V(x)=0$, Eq. (6.1) reduces to $\signal{\psi}''(x)=-\signal k^{\,2}\signal{\psi}(x)$, with $\signal k^{\,2}=2\mechanical m\energy E/\mechanical{\hbar}^{\,2}$. For $\energy E>0$, the solutions are plane waves $e^{\pm i\signal kx}$. For $\energy E<0$, the exponential solutions cannot satisfy the conditions on the full line. The spectrum is continuous, $\sigma(\energy{\hat H})=[0,\infty)$.

The generalized eigenstates $\signal{\psi}_k(x)=e^{i\signal kx}/\sqrt{2\pi}$ for $k\in\mathbb R$ have the distributional normalization $\int \signal{\psi}_{k'}^*(x)\signal{\psi}_k(x)\,dx=\delta(k-k')$. A physical state is a [[04.04 Wave Packets|wave packet]] formed from these plane waves.

$$
\signal{\psi}(x,t)=\int_{\mathbb R}\frac{dk}{\sqrt{2\pi}}\signal{\phi}(k)
e^{i\signal kx-i\mechanical{\hbar}\signal k^{\,2}t/(2\mechanical m)}.
$$

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{\psi}_k$ | $1$ | Generalized eigenfunction | A plane wave with delta-function normalization in wavenumber. |
| $\signal{\psi}(x,t)$ | $\unit{m^{-1/2}}$ | Position wave function | The normalized probability amplitude in one dimension. |
| $\signal{\phi}(k)$ | $\unit{m^{1/2}}$ | Wavenumber amplitude | The coefficient in the plane-wave superposition. |
| $\signal k$ | $\unit{m^{-1}}$ | Wavenumber | The spatial frequency of the plane wave. |
| $\energy V,\energy E,\energy{\hat H}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Energy quantities | The potential, energy eigenvalue, and Hamiltonian. |
| $\mechanical m$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | The particle mass. |
| $\mechanical{\hbar}$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Reduced Planck constant | The constant in the dispersion relation. |
| $x,t$ | $\unit m,\unit s$ | Position and time | The independent variables of the wave packet. |
