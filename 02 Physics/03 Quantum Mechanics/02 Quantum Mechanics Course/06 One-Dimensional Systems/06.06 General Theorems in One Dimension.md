---
title: "General Theorems in One Dimension"
---

> [!TLDR]
> In a connected one-dimensional well, bound levels are nondegenerate and ordered by node count. For an even potential, the bound eigenstates alternate between even and odd parity.

The following results apply to one-dimensional bound states on a connected interval with the usual separated boundary conditions.

> [!theorem] Theorem 6.1. Nondegeneracy of the bound spectrum in one dimension
> If $\signal{\psi}_1$ and $\signal{\psi}_2$ are bound eigenfunctions of Eq. (6.1) with the same energy $\energy E$, they are proportional.

**Proof.** The Wronskian is $W(x)=\signal{\psi}_1(x)\signal{\psi}_2'(x)-\signal{\psi}_1'(x)\signal{\psi}_2(x)$. Equation (6.1) gives $W'(x)=0$. The bound-state boundary conditions force $W$ to vanish. Where $\signal{\psi}_1\ne0$, the derivative of $\signal{\psi}_2/\signal{\psi}_1$ is zero. Uniqueness for the differential equation extends the proportionality across nodes.

> [!theorem] Theorem 6.2. Node theorem
> Order the bound eigenstates $\{\signal{\psi}_n\}$ by increasing energy $\energy E_1<\energy E_2<\cdots$. Then $\signal{\psi}_n$ has exactly $n-1$ nodes in the interior of the confinement interval.

The ground state has no interior node. Choose its overall phase so it is real and positive in the interior.

> [!theorem] Theorem 6.3. Parity for an even potential
> If $\energy V(-x)=\energy V(x)$ and the boundary conditions are symmetric, every nondegenerate eigenstate has definite parity, $\signal{\psi}(-x)=\pm\signal{\psi}(x)$.

The ground state is even. The first excited bound state, when it exists, is odd. The higher bound states alternate in parity.

## Physical meaning

These results give the bound spectrum a common structure across different potentials. The levels are discrete and nondegenerate. Node count orders them, and parity alternates when the potential is symmetric. This structure follows from the second-order differential operator and uniqueness of the ordinary differential equation. In three dimensions, angular degrees of freedom allow degeneracy.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{\psi}_n$ | $\unit{m^{-1/2}}$ | Bound eigenfunction | The normalized amplitude of the state with $n-1$ interior nodes. |
| $\energy V,\energy E_n$ | $\unit{J}$ | Potential and bound energies | The potential function and the increasing energy eigenvalues. |
| $W$ | $\unit{m^{-2}}$ | Wronskian | The expression used to show linear dependence for the same energy. |
