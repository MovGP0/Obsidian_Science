title: "Generalizations and Outlook"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Geometric phases extend to cyclic evolution at finite speed and to matrix-valued transport in degenerate eigenspaces.

The Aharonov–Anandan phase applies to cyclic evolution without the assumption of slow driving. The relevant path is the path of the state in projective Hilbert space. In a degenerate eigenspace, the adiabatic connection is matrix valued. The curvature has noncommuting matrix components, and the holonomy is an element of a non-Abelian unitary group. In proposals for topological quantum computing, braiding non-Abelian anyons implements logical gates that are intrinsically protected against local noise under suitable conditions.

[[19.00 Bands in Periodic Potentials|Bands in Periodic Potentials]] applies Berry curvature to the Brillouin zone of a crystal. The curvature contributes an anomalous term to the electron group velocity. Its integral over the zone gives an integer Chern number that determines the quantized Hall conductivity for an isolated filled band.
