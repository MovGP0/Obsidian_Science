---
title: "Aharonov–Bohm Effect"
---

> [!TLDR]
> In the magnetic Aharonov–Bohm effect, a confined magnetic flux shifts the interference of charged particles that travel through a field-free region.

Consider a particle of charge $\electric q$ in a multiply connected region outside a confined magnetic field. An ideal infinitely long solenoid confines a uniform magnetic field with total flux $\magnetic\Phi_B$. Outside, $\magnetic{\mathbf B}=0$. The vector potential $\magnetic{\mathbf A}$ cannot vanish everywhere in a single-valued gauge on that region. For a positively oriented loop $C$ that winds once around the solenoid,

$$
\oint_C\magnetic{\mathbf A}\cdot\mathrm d\boldsymbol\ell=\magnetic\Phi_B.
\tag{18.6}
$$

The minimally coupled Hamiltonian in the exterior region is

$$
\energy{\hat H}=\frac{1}{2\mechanical m}
\left(\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf p}}-\electric q\,\magnetic{\mathbf A}(\mathbf r)\right)^2.
$$

Along a path $\Gamma$ in the field-free region, the vector potential contributes a phase relative to a solution $\signal{\psi}_0$ with $\magnetic{\mathbf A}=0$.

$$
\signal{\psi}(\mathbf r)
=\exp\left(\frac{i\electric q}{\mechanical{\hbar}}\int_\Gamma\magnetic{\mathbf A}\cdot\mathrm d\boldsymbol\ell\right)
\signal{\psi}_0(\mathbf r).
$$

In a double-slit interferometer with the solenoid between the two paths, one path followed by the reverse of the other forms a loop. The relative phase for the orientation in Eq. (18.6) is

$$
\phi_{\mathrm{AB}}
=\frac{\electric q}{\mechanical{\hbar}}\oint_C\magnetic{\mathbf A}\cdot\mathrm d\boldsymbol\ell
=\frac{\electric q\,\magnetic\Phi_B}{\mechanical{\hbar}}.
$$

The interference pattern shifts as the flux changes, even though the magnetic field is zero along both paths. This geometric phase depends on the topology of the accessible spatial region. The path coordinates here are the particle's positions.

> [!note] Gauge-invariant content
> The vector potential depends on the gauge. Its circulation around a loop that cannot contract within the accessible region determines the observable interference shift modulo $2\pi$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\electric q$ | $\unit{C}$ | Particle charge | Its sign sets the sign of the phase for a fixed orientation. |
| $\magnetic{\mathbf B},\magnetic{\mathbf A}$ | $\unit{T},\unit{T\,m}$ | Magnetic field and vector potential | The field is confined to the solenoid. The vector potential can have nonzero circulation outside. |
| $\magnetic\Phi_B$ | $\unit{Wb}$ | Magnetic flux | The flux enclosed by the loop. |
| $\phi_{\mathrm{AB}}$ | $\unit{rad}$ | Interference phase | The relative phase between the two electron paths. |
