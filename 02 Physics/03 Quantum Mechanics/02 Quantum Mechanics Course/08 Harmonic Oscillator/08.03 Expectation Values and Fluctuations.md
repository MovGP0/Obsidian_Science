---
title: "Expectation Values and Fluctuations"
---

> [!TLDR]
> In a number state, the mean position and momentum vanish. Their variances grow with the level number, and the kinetic and potential energies contribute equally.

In the oscillator basis $\{\lvert n\rangle\}$, the position and momentum matrix elements are

$$
\begin{aligned}
\langle m\lvert\hat x\rvert n\rangle
&=\sqrt{\frac{\mechanical{\hbar}}{2\mechanical m\mechanical{\omega}}}
\left(\sqrt n\,\delta_{m,n-1}+\sqrt{n+1}\,\delta_{m,n+1}\right),\\
\langle m\lvert\mechanical{\hat p}\rvert n\rangle
&=-i\sqrt{\frac{\mechanical m\mechanical{\omega}\mechanical{\hbar}}2}
\left(\sqrt n\,\delta_{m,n-1}-\sqrt{n+1}\,\delta_{m,n+1}\right).
\end{aligned}
$$

The diagonal elements vanish, so $\langle n\lvert\hat x\rvert n\rangle=\langle n\lvert\mechanical{\hat p}\rvert n\rangle=0$. This also follows from parity. The squared operators have expectation values

$$
\langle n\lvert\hat x^2\rvert n\rangle
=\frac{\mechanical{\hbar}}{\mechanical m\mechanical{\omega}}\left(n+\frac12\right),\qquad
\langle n\lvert\mechanical{\hat p}^{\,2}\rvert n\rangle
=\mechanical m\mechanical{\hbar}\mechanical{\omega}\left(n+\frac12\right).
$$

The mean energy is $\mechanical{\hbar}\mechanical{\omega}(n+1/2)$. The kinetic and potential terms each contribute one half.

The uncertainties are

$$
\Delta x=\sqrt{\frac{\mechanical{\hbar}}{\mechanical m\mechanical{\omega}}\left(n+\frac12\right)},\qquad
\Delta\mechanical p=\sqrt{\mechanical m\mechanical{\hbar}\mechanical{\omega}\left(n+\frac12\right)}.
$$

Their product is

$$
\Delta x\,\Delta\mechanical p=\mechanical{\hbar}\left(n+\frac12\right).
$$

At $n=0$, the product equals the Heisenberg lower bound. For $n>0$ it is larger, in agreement with the greater spread of the excited states.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\hat x,\Delta x$ | $\unit m$ | Position operator and uncertainty | The observable and its standard deviation in a number state. |
| $\mechanical{\hat p},\Delta\mechanical p$ | $\unit{kg\,m\,s^{-1}}$ | Momentum operator and uncertainty | The observable and its standard deviation in a number state. |
| $\energy{\hat H}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Hamiltonian | The sum of oscillator kinetic and potential energy. |
| $\mechanical m$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | The oscillator mass. |
| $\mechanical{\omega}$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Angular frequency | The natural frequency of the oscillator. |
| $\mechanical{\hbar}$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Reduced Planck constant | The scale of the uncertainty product. |
| $n$ | $1$ | Level index | The number-state quantum number. |
