> [!TLDR]
> Coherent states follow the classical oscillator in their mean position and momentum while preserving their minimum-uncertainty shape.

Each number state $\lvert n\rangle$ is stationary. Its position density remains symmetric about $x=0$. **Coherent states**, introduced by Schrödinger in 1926 and rediscovered by Glauber in 1963, are the oscillator states closest to classical motion.

> [!definition] Definition 8.1. Coherent state
> A coherent state $\lvert\alpha\rangle$, with $\alpha\in\mathbb C$, is an eigenstate of the annihilation operator.
>
> $$
> \hat a\lvert\alpha\rangle=\alpha\lvert\alpha\rangle.
> $$

Write $\lvert\alpha\rangle=\sum_n c_n\lvert n\rangle$. The eigenvalue equation gives

$$
\sum_n c_n\sqrt n\,\lvert n-1\rangle=\alpha\sum_n c_n\lvert n\rangle
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
c_n\sqrt n=\alpha c_{n-1}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
c_n=\frac{\alpha^n}{\sqrt{n!}}c_0.
$$

Normalization requires $\sum_n\lvert c_n\rvert^2=\lvert c_0\rvert^2 e^{\lvert\alpha\rvert^2}=1$. Choose $c_0$ positive to obtain

$$
\lvert\alpha\rangle=e^{-\lvert\alpha\rvert^2/2}\sum_{n=0}^\infty\frac{\alpha^n}{\sqrt{n!}}\lvert n\rangle.
$$

## Properties

- **Number distribution.** The probability $P_n=\lvert\langle n\mid\alpha\rangle\rvert^2=e^{-\lvert\alpha\rvert^2}\lvert\alpha\rvert^{2n}/n!$ is Poisson with mean $\langle\hat N\rangle=\lvert\alpha\rvert^2$.
- **Mean position and momentum.** $\langle\hat x\rangle=x_0\sqrt2\operatorname{Re}\alpha$ and $\langle\mechanical{\hat p}\rangle=(\mechanical{\hbar}/x_0)\sqrt2\operatorname{Im}\alpha$, with $x_0=\sqrt{\mechanical{\hbar}/(\mechanical m\mechanical{\omega})}$.
- **Time evolution.** $\lvert\signal{\psi}(t)\rangle=e^{-i\mechanical{\omega}t/2}\lvert\alpha e^{-i\mechanical{\omega}t}\rangle$. The coherent-state parameter rotates in the complex plane. The mean position oscillates with amplitude $\sqrt{2\mechanical{\hbar}/(\mechanical m\mechanical{\omega})}\lvert\alpha\rvert$.
- **Uncertainties.** $\Delta x=x_0/\sqrt2=\sqrt{\mechanical{\hbar}/(2\mechanical m\mechanical{\omega})}$ and $\Delta\mechanical p=\sqrt{\mechanical m\mechanical{\omega}\mechanical{\hbar}/2}$ are independent of $\alpha$. Their product is $\mechanical{\hbar}/2$.

## Physical meaning

An idealized laser field is approximately in a coherent state. Its photon number statistics are Poisson, as predicted by $P_n$. Coherent states of field modes, mechanical oscillations in optomechanics, and superconducting currents have applications in quantum computing and metrology.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\alpha,c_n$ | $1$ | Coherent-state parameter and coefficient | The annihilation eigenvalue and the amplitude of level $n$. |
| $\hat a,\hat N,n$ | $1$ | Operators and number index | The annihilation operator, number operator, and level index. |
| $P_n$ | $1$ | Number probability | The probability of measuring $n$ quanta. |
| $\langle\hat x\rangle,x_0,\Delta x$ | $\unit m$ | Mean position, oscillator length, and uncertainty | The packet center, natural length, and constant width. |
| $\langle\mechanical{\hat p}\rangle,\Delta\mechanical p$ | $\unit{kg\,m\,s^{-1}}$ | Mean momentum and uncertainty | The packet's momentum center and standard deviation. |
| $t,T$ | $\unit s$ | Time and period | The elapsed time and one full oscillation. |
| $\mechanical m$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | The oscillator mass. |
| $\mechanical{\omega}$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Angular frequency | The rotation rate of the coherent-state parameter. |
| $\mechanical{\hbar}$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Reduced Planck constant | The action scale of the minimum uncertainty product. |
