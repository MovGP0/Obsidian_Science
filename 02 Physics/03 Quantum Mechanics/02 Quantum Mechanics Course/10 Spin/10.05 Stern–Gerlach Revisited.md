---
title: "Stern–Gerlach Revisited"
---

> [!TLDR]
> A Stern–Gerlach apparatus separates the discrete spin projections. A measurement along a perpendicular axis changes the statistics of a later measurement along the original axis.

A magnetic dipole in an inhomogeneous field $\magnetic{\mathbf B}(\mathbf r)$ experiences the classical force $\mechanical{\mathbf F}=\nabla(\magnetic{\boldsymbol{\mu}}\cdot\magnetic{\mathbf B})$. For an atom with specified $\magnetic{\mu}_z$ in a mainly axial gradient, $F_z=\magnetic{\mu}_z\,\partial_z\magnetic B$. The sign of $\magnetic{\mu}_z$ determines the vertical deflection.

For atoms with one unpaired electron, the spin component $\mechanical{\hat S}_z$ has the two eigenvalues $\pm\mechanical{\hbar}/2$. The beam therefore separates into two discrete components. Selecting a component in the idealized [[01.07 Stern–Gerlach Experiment|Stern–Gerlach experiment]] implements a projective spin measurement.

## Sequential analyzers

1. Send a beam through a $z$ analyzer and keep the upper spin component, $\lvert+z\rangle$.
2. Send this state through an $x$ analyzer. Since $\lvert+z\rangle=(\lvert+x\rangle+\lvert-x\rangle)/\sqrt2$, the two outcomes are equally probable. Keep $\lvert+x\rangle$.
3. Send this state through a third analyzer along $z$. The outcomes $\lvert+z\rangle$ and $\lvert-z\rangle$ each have probability $1/2$.

The intermediate $x$ measurement prepared an eigenstate of $\mechanical{\hat S}_x$. The final $z$ measurement therefore has equal probabilities despite the first selection. Sequential measurements of these incompatible observables do not accumulate definite values of both spin components.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\magnetic{\mathbf B},\partial_z\magnetic B$ | $\unit T,\unit{T\,m^{-1}}$ | Magnetic flux density and gradient | The field and axial gradient in the analyzer. |
| $\magnetic{\boldsymbol{\mu}},\magnetic{\mu}_z$ | $\unit{J\,T^{-1}}$ | Magnetic moment | The dipole moment and its axial component. |
| $\mechanical{\mathbf F},F_z$ | $\unit N$ | Force and axial force | The force that separates the beam components. |
| $\mechanical{\hat S}_x,\mechanical{\hat S}_z$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Spin components | The incompatible observables measured by the two analyzer orientations. |
| $\mechanical{\hbar}$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Reduced Planck constant | Twice the magnitude of either spin outcome. |
