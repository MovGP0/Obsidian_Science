> [!TLDR]
> A wave packet combines plane waves to localize a particle. Its evolution depends on the dispersion relation.

A plane wave $\signal{\psi}(\mechanical{x},t)=e^{i(\signal{k}\mechanical{x}-\signal{\omega}t)}$ is not square-integrable on the whole line. Describe a localized particle with a **wave packet**, a superposition of plane waves.

$$
\signal{\psi}(\mechanical{x},t)=\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}
\signal{\phi}(\signal{k})e^{i(\signal{k}\mechanical{x}-\signal{\omega}(\signal{k})t)}
\frac{d\signal{k}}{\sqrt{2\pi}}.
\tag{4.4}
$$

For a free particle, $\signal{\omega}(\signal{k})=\hbar\signal{k}^2/(2\mechanical{m})$. Equation (4.4) is an inverse Fourier transform. With $\mechanical{p}=\hbar\signal{k}$, the momentum representation is $\signal{\tilde\psi}(\mechanical{p},t)=\signal{\phi}(\mechanical{p}/\hbar)e^{-i\signal{\omega}(\mechanical{p}/\hbar)t}/\sqrt{\hbar}$. Therefore $\int\lvert\signal{\tilde\psi}\rvert^2\,d\mechanical{p}=\int\lvert\signal{\phi}\rvert^2\,d\signal{k}$.

## Phase and group velocities

For a spectrum concentrated near $\signal{k}_0$, expand the dispersion relation.

$$
\signal{\omega}(\signal{k})\simeq\signal{\omega}(\signal{k}_0)+
\signal{\omega}'(\signal{k}_0)(\signal{k}-\signal{k}_0)+
\tfrac12\signal{\omega}''(\signal{k}_0)(\signal{k}-\signal{k}_0)^2.
$$

The **group velocity** is $\mechanical{v}_g=\signal{\omega}'(\signal{k}_0)=\hbar\signal{k}_0/\mechanical{m}$. This is the classical velocity $\mechanical{p}_0/\mechanical{m}$. The **phase velocity** is $\mechanical{v}_f=\signal{\omega}(\signal{k}_0)/\signal{k}_0=\hbar\signal{k}_0/(2\mechanical{m})=\mechanical{v}_g/2$ for nonzero $\signal{k}_0$. It describes the motion of the carrier phase.

## Spreading of a Gaussian packet

Take $\signal{\phi}(\signal{k})=(2\sigma^2/\pi)^{1/4}e^{-\sigma^2(\signal{k}-\signal{k}_0)^2}$. At $t=0$, the inverse transform gives a Gaussian centered at $\mechanical{x}=0$, with $\Delta\mechanical{x}(0)=\sigma$ and mean momentum $\hbar\signal{k}_0$. At later times,

$$
\lvert\signal{\psi}(\mechanical{x},t)\rvert^2=
\frac{\exp[-(\mechanical{x}-\mechanical{v}_g t)^2/(2\sigma(t)^2)]}{\sqrt{2\pi}\sigma(t)},
\qquad
\sigma(t)=\sigma\sqrt{1+\left(\frac{\hbar t}{2\mechanical{m}\sigma^2}\right)^2}.
$$

The packet center moves with $\mechanical{v}_g$ while the packet spreads. At large $t$, the variance grows as $\sigma(t)^2\sim(\hbar t/(2\mechanical{m}\sigma))^2$. Different Fourier components propagate with different group velocities. Figure 4.1 shows the effect.

## Example. Spreading time of an electron

For $\sigma=\quantity{10^{-10}}{m}$, the characteristic time is $t_*=2\mechanical{m}\sigma^2/\hbar\sim\quantity{2\times10^{-16}}{s}$ for an electron. At $t=t_*$, the width increases by a factor $\sqrt2$. After about half a femtosecond, of order $3t_*$, it has increased by about a factor of three. For a macroscopic object with $\mechanical{m}=\quantity{1}{kg}$ and $\sigma=\quantity{10^{-3}}{m}$, $t_*$ is of order $\quantity{10^{28}}{s}$, many billions of times the age of the universe.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal{\psi}(\mechanical{x},t)$ | $\unit{m^{-1/2}}$ | Position wave function | The normalized amplitude over position. |
| $\signal{\tilde\psi}(\mechanical{p},t)$ | $\unit{kg^{-1/2}\,m^{-1/2}\,s^{1/2}}$ | Momentum wave function | The normalized amplitude over momentum. |
| $\signal{\phi}(\signal{k})$ | $\unit{m^{1/2}}$ | Wavenumber amplitude | The normalized spectral amplitude in Eq. (4.4). |
| $\mechanical{x},\Delta\mechanical{x},\sigma,\sigma(t)$ | $\unit{m}$ | Position and widths | The coordinate, position standard deviation, initial width, and evolved width. |
| $\mechanical{p},\mechanical{p}_0$ | $\unit{kg\,m\,s^{-1}}$ | Momentum | The momentum coordinate and mean momentum. |
| $\signal{k},\signal{k}_0$ | $\unit{m^{-1}}$ | Wavenumber | The spatial frequency and center of the packet spectrum. |
| $\signal{\omega}$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Angular frequency | The rate of phase change for a Fourier component. |
| $\mechanical{v}_f,\mechanical{v}_g$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Phase and group velocities | The carrier phase velocity and packet center velocity. |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | The mass of the freely evolving object. |
| $t,t_1,t_2,t_*$ | $\unit{s}$ | Times | The elapsed time, example snapshot times, and characteristic spreading time. |
| $\hbar$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Reduced Planck constant | The constant in the free dispersion relation. |
