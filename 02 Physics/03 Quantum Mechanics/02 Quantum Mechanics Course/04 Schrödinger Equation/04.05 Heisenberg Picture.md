---
title: "Heisenberg Picture"
---

> [!TLDR]
> In the Heisenberg picture, states remain fixed while operators carry the time dependence.

In the **Schrödinger picture**, states evolve and operators have only any explicit time dependence. The **Heisenberg picture** gives an equivalent description in which states are fixed and operators evolve.

Set $\lvert\signal{\psi}_H\rangle=\lvert\signal{\psi}(0)\rangle$. The Heisenberg operator and state are

$$
\hat A_H(t)=\hat U^\dagger(t,0)\hat A\hat U(t,0),\qquad
\lvert\signal{\psi}_H\rangle=\hat U^\dagger(t,0)\lvert\signal{\psi}_S(t)\rangle.
\tag{4.5}
$$

The expectation value is the same in both pictures.

$$
\langle\hat A\rangle_\psi(t)=
\langle\signal{\psi}_S(t)\lvert\hat A\rvert\signal{\psi}_S(t)\rangle
=\langle\signal{\psi}_H\lvert\hat A_H(t)\rvert\signal{\psi}_H\rangle.
$$

Differentiate Eq. (4.5) and apply the [[04.01 From Unitary Time Evolution to the Equation|Schrödinger equation]] to get the **Heisenberg equation**.

$$
i\mechanical{\hbar}\frac{d\hat A_H}{dt}
=[\hat A_H,\energy{\hat H}_H]+i\mechanical{\hbar}\left(\frac{\partial\hat A}{\partial t}\right)_H.
$$

The second term occurs only if $\hat A$ explicitly depends on time. In one dimension, take $\energy{\hat H}=\mechanical{\hat p}^{\,2}/(2\mechanical m)+\energy V(\hat x)$. The equations for position and momentum are

$$
\frac{d\hat x_H}{dt}=\frac{\mechanical{\hat p}_H}{\mechanical m},
\tag{4.6}
$$

$$
\frac{d\mechanical{\hat p}_H}{dt}=-\energy{V'}(\hat x_H).
\tag{4.7}
$$

These equations have the same form as the classical Hamilton equations and express the correspondence principle directly. Their expectation values give the [[04.06 Ehrenfest Theorem|Ehrenfest theorem]].

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\lvert\signal{\psi}_S(t)\rangle,\lvert\signal{\psi}_H\rangle$ | $1$ | State vectors | The evolving Schrödinger state and the fixed Heisenberg state. |
| $\hat U(t,0)$ | $1$ | Time evolution operator | The unitary operator from time zero to $t$. |
| $\hat A,\hat A_H(t)$ | Depends on observable | Observable | The same observable in the two pictures. |
| $\energy{\hat H},\energy{\hat H}_H$ | $\unit{J}$ | Hamiltonian | The energy operator in the two pictures. |
| $\hat x_H$ | $\unit{m}$ | Position operator | The position in the Heisenberg picture. |
| $\mechanical{\hat p}_H$ | $\unit{kg\,m\,s^{-1}}$ | Momentum operator | The momentum in the Heisenberg picture. |
| $\energy V$ | $\unit{J}$ | Potential energy | The potential in the Hamiltonian. |
| $\mechanical m$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | The mass of the particle. |
| $\mechanical{\hbar}$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Reduced Planck constant | The constant that relates energy and time evolution. |
| $t$ | $\unit{s}$ | Time | The elapsed time. |
