---
title: "Transition Amplitude and Propagator"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The position-space propagator is the matrix element of the time evolution operator between an initial and a final position.

Let $\energy{\hat H}$ be the time-independent Hamiltonian of a one-dimensional particle. Between times $t_i$ and $t_f$, evolution is given by $\hat U(t_f,t_i)=e^{-i\energy{\hat H}(t_f-t_i)/\mechanical{\hbar}}$. For an initial state $\lvert\signal{\psi}(t_i)\rangle$, the final state is $\lvert\signal{\psi}(t_f)\rangle=\hat U(t_f,t_i)\lvert\signal{\psi}(t_i)\rangle$.

Project onto the position basis and insert the identity $\int dx_i\,\lvert x_i\rangle\langle x_i\rvert=\hat 1$ to obtain

$$
\signal{\psi}(x_f,t_f)=\int dx_i\,\signal K(x_f,t_f;x_i,t_i)\signal{\psi}(x_i,t_i),
\tag{5.1}
$$

where the **propagator** is the integral kernel

$$
\signal K(x_f,t_f;x_i,t_i)=\langle x_f\lvert e^{-i\energy{\hat H}(t_f-t_i)/\mechanical{\hbar}}\rvert x_i\rangle.
$$

It is a Green function for the time-dependent Schrödinger equation with the initial condition below.

> [!theorem] Proposition 5.1. Composition and initial condition
> For any $t\in(t_i,t_f)$,
>
> $$
> \signal K(x_f,t_f;x_i,t_i)
> =\int dx\,\signal K(x_f,t_f;x,t)\signal K(x,t;x_i,t_i).
> $$
>
> In the limit $t_f\to t_i^+$, the kernel tends to $\delta(x_f-x_i)$.

Insert the position-space identity between the operators in $\hat U(t_f,t_i)=\hat U(t_f,t)\hat U(t,t_i)$ to get the composition law. The initial condition follows from the orthonormality relation $\langle x_f\mid x_i\rangle=\delta(x_f-x_i)$.

## Physical meaning

The propagator determines the full quantum dynamics of the system. Equation (5.1) evolves any initial state linearly and unitarily. The composition of kernels over successive intervals leads to a sum over all histories of the system.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\signal K$ | $\unit{m^{-1}}$ | Propagator | The position-space kernel for one-dimensional time evolution. |
| $\signal{\psi}$ | $\unit{m^{-1/2}}$ | Position wave function | The probability amplitude over one-dimensional position. |
| $\energy{\hat H}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Hamiltonian | The generator of time evolution. |
| $\hat U$ | $1$ | Evolution operator | The unitary operator that evolves the state between two times. |
| $x_i,x_f,x$ | $\unit{m}$ | Positions | The initial, final, and intermediate coordinates. |
| $t_i,t_f,t$ | $\unit{s}$ | Times | The initial, final, and intermediate times. |
| $\mechanical{\hbar}$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Reduced Planck constant | The constant that relates energy to phase evolution. |
