title: "Two-Level Dynamics and Rabi Oscillations"
---

> [!TLDR]
> A coherent drive transfers population periodically between two levels. Resonant driving can transfer the full population, while detuning reduces the maximum transfer.

Near $\omega=\omega_{fi}$, the two resonant states $\lvert i\rangle$ and $\lvert f\rangle$ dominate. The rotating-wave approximation gives a solvable $2\times2$ system for $c_i(t)$ and $c_f(t)$. Define the resonant Rabi frequency and detuning by

$$
\Omega_R=\frac{2\lvert\energy{V}_{0,fi}\rvert}{\mechanical{\hbar}},
\qquad
\delta=\omega-\omega_{fi}.
$$

For an initial state $\lvert i\rangle$,

$$
P_{i\to f}(t)=\frac{\Omega_R^2}{\Omega_R^2+\delta^2}
\sin^2\left(\sqrt{\Omega_R^2+\delta^2}\,\frac{t}{2}\right).
$$

At resonance, $\delta=0$ and $P_{i\to f}(t)=\sin^2(\Omega_Rt/2)$. The probability oscillates from zero to one with period $2\pi/\Omega_R$. These oscillations are the basis of magnetic resonance and coherent qubit control. At times comparable to $1/\Omega_R$ on resonance, the transferred population is comparable to one, so the small-probability condition of the golden rule fails.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\Omega_R,\delta$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Rabi frequency and detuning | The resonant drive strength and the signed difference between drive and transition frequencies. |
| $\energy{V}_{0,fi}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Coupling matrix element | The resonant Fourier component between the two levels. |
| $P_{i\to f}(t)$ | $1$ | Transition probability | The population of the target level at time $t$. |
| $t$ | $\unit{s}$ | Time | The elapsed time under the coherent drive. |
