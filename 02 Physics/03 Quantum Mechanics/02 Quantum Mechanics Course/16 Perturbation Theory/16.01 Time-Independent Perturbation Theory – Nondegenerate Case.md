---
title: "Time-Independent Perturbation Theory – Nondegenerate Case"
---

> [!TLDR]
> For an isolated nondegenerate level, expand its energy and eigenstate in powers of the perturbation strength. Match equal powers in the eigenvalue equation.

Consider

$$
\energy{\hat H}=\energy{\hat H}_0+\lambda\energy{\hat V},
$$

where the unperturbed Hamiltonian has known eigenstates $\lvert n^{(0)}\rangle$ and nondegenerate energies $\energy{E}_n^{(0)}$. For small $\lambda$, write

$$
\begin{aligned}
\energy{E}_n&=\energy{E}_n^{(0)}+\lambda\energy{E}_n^{(1)}+\lambda^2\energy{E}_n^{(2)}+\cdots,\\
\lvert n\rangle&=\lvert n^{(0)}\rangle+\lambda\lvert n^{(1)}\rangle+\lambda^2\lvert n^{(2)}\rangle+\cdots.
\end{aligned}
$$

Substitute the expansions into $\energy{\hat H}\lvert n\rangle=\energy{E}_n\lvert n\rangle$ and match the powers of $\lambda$.

## Zeroth order

The coefficient of $\lambda^0$ gives the unperturbed equation $\energy{\hat H}_0\lvert n^{(0)}\rangle=\energy{E}_n^{(0)}\lvert n^{(0)}\rangle$.

## First order

The coefficient of $\lambda$ gives

$$
(\energy{\hat H}_0-\energy{E}_n^{(0)})\lvert n^{(1)}\rangle+(\energy{\hat V}-\energy{E}_n^{(1)})\lvert n^{(0)}\rangle=0.
$$

Project onto $\langle n^{(0)}\rvert$. The first-order energy correction is the expectation value of the perturbation in the unperturbed state.

$$
\energy{E}_n^{(1)}=\langle n^{(0)}\rvert\energy{\hat V}\lvert n^{(0)}\rangle.
$$

Project onto $\langle m^{(0)}\rvert$ for $m\ne n$ and impose $\langle n^{(0)}\mid n^{(1)}\rangle=0$.

$$
\lvert n^{(1)}\rangle=\sum_{m\ne n}\frac{\langle m^{(0)}\rvert\energy{\hat V}\lvert n^{(0)}\rangle}{\energy{E}_n^{(0)}-\energy{E}_m^{(0)}}\lvert m^{(0)}\rangle.
$$

The first-order state correction mixes in other unperturbed states. Each coefficient is proportional to a matrix element of $\energy{\hat V}$ and inversely proportional to the signed energy gap.

## Second order

Project the coefficient of $\lambda^2$ onto $\langle n^{(0)}\rvert$.

$$
\energy{E}_n^{(2)}=\sum_{m\ne n}\frac{\lvert\langle m^{(0)}\rvert\energy{\hat V}\lvert n^{(0)}\rangle\rvert^2}{\energy{E}_n^{(0)}-\energy{E}_m^{(0)}}.
$$

For the ground state, every denominator is negative. Therefore $\energy{E}_0^{(2)}\le0$, with a strict decrease if $\energy{\hat V}$ couples the ground state to any excited state.

> [!note] Sign of the ground-state correction
> The source states a strict decrease. If all couplings to excited states vanish, the second-order correction is zero.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\energy{\hat H},\energy{\hat H}_0,\energy{\hat V}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Hamiltonians and perturbation | The full Hamiltonian, unperturbed Hamiltonian, and correction operator. |
| $\lambda$ | $1$ | Perturbation parameter | A dimensionless expansion parameter for this split of the Hamiltonian. |
| $\energy{E}_n^{(j)}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Energy coefficient | The coefficient of order $j$ in the expansion for level $n$. |
