> [!TLDR]
> For a degenerate unperturbed level, diagonalize the perturbation within that eigenspace. Its eigenvalues give the first-order energy corrections.

If $\energy{E}_n^{(0)}$ has degeneracy $g$, the nondegenerate formula has zero denominators for other states in the same eigenspace. Use an orthonormal basis $\{\lvert n,i\rangle\}_{i=1}^g$ of that eigenspace and form

$$
\energy{V}_{ij}^{(n)}=\langle n,i\rvert\energy{\hat V}\lvert n,j\rangle.
$$

The eigenvalues of this $g\times g$ matrix are the first-order energy coefficients $\energy{E}_{n,k}^{(1)}$. Its eigenvectors give the zeroth-order combinations $\lvert n,k\rangle^{(0)\prime}=\sum_j U_{jk}\lvert n,j\rangle$ suited to the perturbation. The first-order shifts are $\lambda\energy{E}_{n,k}^{(1)}$. Different eigenvalues split the level. Equal eigenvalues leave a residual degeneracy at this order.

## Linear Stark effect for hydrogen at n = 2

A uniform electric field along $z$ gives the perturbation $\energy{\hat V}=\electric{e}\,\electric{E}\hat z$. Ignore spin and use the unperturbed $n=2$ orbital basis $\lvert2s\rangle=\lvert200\rangle$ and $\lvert2p_m\rangle=\lvert21m\rangle$ for $m=0,\pm1$.

- Parity gives $\langle2s\rvert\hat z\lvert2s\rangle=0$ and $\langle2p_m\rvert\hat z\lvert2p_{m'}\rangle=0$.
- The wave functions give $\langle2s\rvert\hat z\lvert2p_0\rangle=\langle2p_0\rvert\hat z\lvert2s\rangle=-3a_0$.
- The azimuthal integral gives $\langle2s\rvert\hat z\lvert2p_{\pm1}\rangle=0$.

Only the submatrix on $\{\lvert2s\rangle,\lvert2p_0\rangle\}$ has nonzero entries.

$$
\energy{V}=\electric{e}\electric{E}\begin{pmatrix}0&-3a_0\\-3a_0&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\energy{E}_{\pm}^{(1)}=\mp3\electric{e}\electric{E}a_0.
$$

The combinations $(\lvert2s\rangle\pm\lvert2p_0\rangle)/\sqrt2$ shift by $\mp3\electric{e}\electric{E}a_0$. The doublet $\lvert2p_{\pm1}\rangle$ stays at the unperturbed energy at first order. The shift is linear in the field because the Coulomb $1/r$ potential makes the unperturbed levels degenerate across different values of $\ell$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $g$ | $1$ | Degeneracy | The dimension of the unperturbed eigenspace. |
| $\energy{V}_{ij}^{(n)},\energy{E}_{n,k}^{(1)}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Matrix elements and energy coefficients | The entries of the projected perturbation and its eigenvalues. |
| $\electric{e},\electric{E}$ | $\unit{C},\unit{V\,m^{-1}}$ | Elementary charge and electric field | The positive elementary charge and the field component along $z$. |
| $a_0$ | $\unit{m}$ | Bohr radius | The hydrogen length scale that determines the nonzero position matrix element. |
