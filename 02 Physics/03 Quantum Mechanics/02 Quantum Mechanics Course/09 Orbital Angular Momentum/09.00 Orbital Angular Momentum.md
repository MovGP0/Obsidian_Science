title: "Orbital Angular Momentum"

> [!TLDR]
> Orbital angular momentum generates rotations. Its commutation relations determine the allowed values of its magnitude and a component, independently of the potential.

Angular momentum is conserved in rotationally symmetric systems. In classical mechanics, $\mechanical{\mathbf L}=\mathbf r\times\mechanical{\mathbf p}$ is conserved if the potential depends only on $r=\lvert\mathbf r\rvert$. Quantum mechanics defines it with the canonical operators. The possible values of $\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf L}}^{\,2}$ and $\mechanical{\hat L}_z$ follow from their algebra and the orbital boundary conditions. This makes angular momentum a general tool for analyzing three-dimensional systems with spherical or partial rotational symmetry.

## Chapter summary

- The commutators are $[\mechanical{\hat L}_i,\mechanical{\hat L}_j]=i\mechanical{\hbar}\epsilon_{ijk}\mechanical{\hat L}_k$ and $[\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf L}}^{\,2},\mechanical{\hat L}_i]=0$.
- On $\lvert\ell,m\rangle$, the eigenvalues are $\mechanical{\hbar}^{\,2}\ell(\ell+1)$ and $m\mechanical{\hbar}$, with $\ell=0,1,\ldots$ and $m=-\ell,\ldots,\ell$.
- The ladder operators give $\mechanical{\hat L}_\pm\lvert\ell,m\rangle=\mechanical{\hbar}\sqrt{\ell(\ell+1)-m(m\pm1)}\,\lvert\ell,m\pm1\rangle$.
- The spherical harmonics $Y_{\ell m}(\theta,\phi)$ form an orthonormal basis of $L^2(S^2)$. Their parity is $Y_{\ell m}(-\hat{\mathbf r})=(-1)^\ell Y_{\ell m}(\hat{\mathbf r})$.
- The centrifugal term $\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf L}}^{\,2}/(2\mechanical m r^2)$ is the angular part of kinetic energy. This term separates from the radial derivative in a central potential.
- A rotation is $\hat R_{\hat{\mathbf n}}(\alpha)=e^{-i\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf L}}\cdot\hat{\mathbf n}\alpha/\mechanical{\hbar}}$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{\hat L}_i,\mechanical{\hat L}_\pm$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Angular momentum and ladder operators | The Cartesian components and the operators that change $m$. |
| $\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf L}}^{\,2}$ | $\unit{J^2\,s^2}$ | Squared angular momentum | The invariant squared magnitude operator. |
| $\ell,m,Y_{\ell m}$ | $1$ | Angular quantum numbers and harmonic | The eigenvalue labels and the angular eigenfunction. |
| $r$ | $\unit m$ | Radial distance | The distance from the origin. |
| $\theta,\phi,\alpha$ | $\unit{rad}$ | Angles | The polar angle, azimuth, and rotation angle. |
| $\mechanical m$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | The particle mass in the centrifugal term. |
| $\mechanical{\hbar}$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Reduced Planck constant | The unit of angular momentum. |
