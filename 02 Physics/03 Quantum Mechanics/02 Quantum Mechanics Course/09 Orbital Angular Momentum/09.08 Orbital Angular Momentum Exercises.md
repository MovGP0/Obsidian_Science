---
title: "Orbital Angular Momentum Exercises"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Use angular momentum commutators, spherical harmonics, and the rigid-rotor spectrum.

1. Calculate $[\mechanical{\hat L}_x,\mechanical{\hat L}_y]$ from the definitions and the canonical commutation relations.
2. Verify $[\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf L}}^{\,2},\mechanical{\hat L}_x]=0$ by expanding the squared operator in Cartesian components.
3. For $\ell=1$, write the $3\times3$ matrices of $\mechanical{\hat L}_z$ and $\mechanical{\hat L}_\pm$ in the ordered basis $\lvert1,1\rangle,\lvert1,0\rangle,\lvert1,-1\rangle$.  
   **Result.** $\mechanical{\hat L}_z=\mechanical{\hbar}\operatorname{diag}(1,0,-1)$. The two nonzero elements of $\mechanical{\hat L}_+$ are both $\mechanical{\hbar}\sqrt2$.
4. Verify the normalization $\int_{S^2}\lvert Y_{10}\rvert^2\,d\Omega=1$.
5. For $\lvert\signal{\psi}\rangle=(\lvert1,1\rangle+\lvert1,-1\rangle)/\sqrt2$, calculate $\langle\mechanical{\hat L}_z\rangle$, $\langle\mechanical{\hat L}_x\rangle$, and $\langle\mechanical{\hat L}_y\rangle$.  
   **Result.** All three means are zero. Check whether this state is an eigenstate of $\mechanical{\hat L}_y$ and how that relates to the result for $\langle\mechanical{\hat L}_x\rangle$.
6. Write $\mechanical{\hat L}_x^{\,2}+\mechanical{\hat L}_y^{\,2}$ using $\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf L}}^{\,2}$ and $\mechanical{\hat L}_z$.  
   **Result.** $\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf L}}^{\,2}-\mechanical{\hat L}_z^{\,2}$.
7. For $Y_{11}$, calculate $\langle\mechanical{\hat L}_x^{\,2}\rangle$ and $\Delta\mechanical L_x$. Verify the Robertson inequality for $\mechanical L_x$ and $\mechanical L_y$.  
   **Result.** $\langle\mechanical{\hat L}_x^{\,2}\rangle=\mechanical{\hbar}^{\,2}/2$, $\Delta\mechanical L_x=\mechanical{\hbar}/\sqrt2$, and $\Delta\mechanical L_x\,\Delta\mechanical L_y=\mechanical{\hbar}^{\,2}/2=\mechanical{\hbar}\lvert\langle\mechanical{\hat L}_z\rangle\rvert/2$.
8. For a particle on a sphere of radius $R$, find the energy difference between $\ell$ and $\ell+1$ and the photon frequency. The upward transition absorbs the photon, and the reverse transition emits it.  
   **Result.** $\Delta\energy E=\mechanical{\hbar}^{\,2}(\ell+1)/(\mechanical mR^2)$.
9. For a classical rotating sphere, compare the axial component with the quantum eigenvalue in the limit $\ell\to\infty$ with $m=\ell$. Verify the correspondence using dimensional estimates.
10. An electron in state $Y_{10}$ is perturbed by $\energy V=\lambda z$. Calculate $\langle Y_{10}\lvert\energy V\rvert Y_{10}\rangle$.  
    **Result.** Zero by parity.
11. **Wigner–Eckart theorem for a scalar.** Let $\hat S$ commute with all components of angular momentum. Show that, on the angular basis with one copy of each $\ell$, $\langle\ell',m'\lvert\hat S\rvert\ell,m\rangle=S_\ell\delta_{\ell\ell'}\delta_{mm'}$, where $S_\ell$ is independent of $m$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{\hat L}_i,\mechanical{\hat L}_\pm,\Delta\mechanical L_i$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Angular momentum operators and uncertainties | The components, ladder operators, and component standard deviations. |
| $\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf L}}^{\,2}$ | $\unit{J^2\,s^2}$ | Squared angular momentum | The sum of the squared components. |
| $\ell,m,Y_{\ell m}$ | $1$ | Angular labels and harmonic | The angular quantum numbers and the corresponding normalized angular function. |
| $R,z$ | $\unit m$ | Radius and axial coordinate | The rotor radius and the coordinate in the perturbing potential. |
| $\energy V,\Delta\energy E$ | $\unit{J}$ | Perturbation energy and energy difference | The potential and the gap of a rotor transition. |
| $\lambda$ | $\unit N$ | Potential slope | The coefficient of $z$ in the perturbation. |
| $\hat S,S_\ell$ | Depends on observable | Scalar operator and matrix element | The operator that commutes with rotations and its diagonal value at fixed $\ell$. |
| $\mechanical m$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | The mass of the particle on the sphere. |
| $\mechanical{\hbar}$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Reduced Planck constant | The angular momentum scale. |
