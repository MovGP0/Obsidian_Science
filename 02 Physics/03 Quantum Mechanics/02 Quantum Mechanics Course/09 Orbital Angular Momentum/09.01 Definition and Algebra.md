title: "Definition and Algebra"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Orbital angular momentum is the cross product of the position and momentum operators. Its components generate rotations and obey the angular momentum algebra.

> [!definition] Definition 9.1. Orbital angular momentum
> In three dimensions the components are
>
> $$
> \mechanical{\hat L}_x=\hat y\mechanical{\hat p}_z-\hat z\mechanical{\hat p}_y,\qquad
> \mechanical{\hat L}_y=\hat z\mechanical{\hat p}_x-\hat x\mechanical{\hat p}_z,\qquad
> \mechanical{\hat L}_z=\hat x\mechanical{\hat p}_y-\hat y\mechanical{\hat p}_x.
> $$

In the position representation,

$$
\mechanical{\hat L}_z=-i\mechanical{\hbar}(x\partial_y-y\partial_x)=-i\mechanical{\hbar}\partial_\phi,
$$

where $\phi$ is the azimuthal angle. The derivative with respect to $\phi$ expresses the role of $\mechanical{\hat L}_z$ as the generator of rotations about the $z$ axis.

## Commutators

The canonical relation $[\hat x_i,\mechanical{\hat p}_j]=i\mechanical{\hbar}\delta_{ij}\hat I$ gives

$$
[\mechanical{\hat L}_i,\mechanical{\hat L}_j]
=i\mechanical{\hbar}\epsilon_{ijk}\mechanical{\hat L}_k,
$$

with a sum over $k$. The three components cannot all have sharp values simultaneously in a general state. The squared magnitude commutes with each component.

$$
\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf L}}^{\,2}=\mechanical{\hat L}_x^{\,2}+\mechanical{\hat L}_y^{\,2}+\mechanical{\hat L}_z^{\,2},
\qquad
[\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf L}}^{\,2},\mechanical{\hat L}_i]=0.
$$

Thus $\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf L}}^{\,2}$ and $\mechanical{\hat L}_z$ are compatible and have common basis states $\lvert\ell,m\rangle$ for the angular part of the state.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{\hat L}_i$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Angular momentum component | The generator of rotations about axis $i$. |
| $\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf L}}^{\,2}$ | $\unit{J^2\,s^2}$ | Squared angular momentum | The sum of the squared components. |
| $\hat x_i$ | $\unit m$ | Position component | The coordinate operator along axis $i$. |
| $\mechanical{\hat p}_i$ | $\unit{kg\,m\,s^{-1}}$ | Momentum component | The momentum operator along axis $i$. |
| $\phi$ | $\unit{rad}$ | Azimuth | The angle about the $z$ axis. |
| $\mechanical{\hbar}$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Reduced Planck constant | The action scale of the angular momentum algebra. |
| $\ell,m$ | $1$ | Angular quantum numbers | The labels of the squared magnitude and $z$ component. |
