---
title: "Atomic Units"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Atomic units use the Bohr radius and Hartree energy as the natural scales for atomic calculations.

In atomic units, set $\mechanical{\hbar}=\mechanical{m_e}=\electric e^2/(4\pi\electric{\varepsilon_0})=1$. Then express

- Length in Bohr radii $\mechanical{a_0}$.
- Energy in Hartrees $\energy{\mathrm{Ha}}$.
- Time in units of $\mechanical{\hbar}/\energy{\mathrm{Ha}}\approx\quantity{2.419\times10^{-17}}{s}$.
- Speed in units of $\alpha\mechanical c\approx\quantity{2.19\times10^6}{m\,s^{-1}}$.

In these units, the speed of light is $\mechanical c=1/\alpha\approx137.036$. The hydrogen Hamiltonian is

$$
\energy{\hat H}=-\frac{\nabla^2}{2}-\frac{1}{\mechanical r},
$$

and the ground-state energy is $-1/2$ Hartree.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{a_0}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Bohr radius | Atomic unit of length. |
| $\energy{\mathrm{Ha}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Hartree energy | Atomic unit of energy. |
| $\mechanical{\hbar}$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Reduced Planck constant | Sets the atomic time scale with the Hartree energy. |
| $\mechanical c$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Speed of light | Speed of light in vacuum. |
| $\alpha$ | $1$ | Fine-structure constant | Ratio of the atomic unit of speed to the speed of light. |
| $\mechanical r$ | $\unit{m}$ | Radius | Distance from the nucleus, expressed here in Bohr radii. |
