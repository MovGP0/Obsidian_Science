---
title: "Derived Atomic Constants"
---

> [!TLDR]
> These constants set the length, energy, and magnetic moment scales used in atomic physics. The numerical values are rounded.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\alpha$ | $1$ | Fine-structure constant | $\electric e^2/(4\pi\electric{\varepsilon_0}\mechanical{\hbar}\mechanical c)\approx1/137.036$. |
| $\mechanical{a_0}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Bohr radius | $4\pi\electric{\varepsilon_0}\mechanical{\hbar}^2/(\mechanical{m_e}\electric e^2)\approx\quantity{5.292\times10^{-11}}{m}$. |
| $\energy{\mathrm{Ha}}$ | $\unit{eV}$ | Hartree energy | $\mechanical{m_e}\electric e^4/[(4\pi\electric{\varepsilon_0})^2\mechanical{\hbar}^2]\approx\quantity{27.211}{eV}$. |
| $\energy{\mathrm{Ry}}$ | $\unit{eV}$ | Rydberg energy | $\energy{\mathrm{Ha}}/2\approx\quantity{13.606}{eV}$. |
| $\magnetic{\mu_B}$ | $\unit{J\,T^{-1}}$ | Bohr magneton | $\electric e\mechanical{\hbar}/(2\mechanical{m_e})\approx\quantity{9.274\times10^{-24}}{J\,T^{-1}}$. |
| $\magnetic{\mu_N}$ | $\unit{J\,T^{-1}}$ | Nuclear magneton | $\electric e\mechanical{\hbar}/(2\mechanical{m_p})\approx\quantity{5.051\times10^{-27}}{J\,T^{-1}}$. |
| $\mechanical{\lambda_C}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Electron Compton wavelength | $\mechanical h/(\mechanical{m_e}\mechanical c)\approx\quantity{2.426\times10^{-12}}{m}$. |
| $R_\infty$ | $\unit{m^{-1}}$ | Rydberg constant | $\mechanical{m_e}\electric e^4/(8\electric{\varepsilon_0}^2\mechanical h^3\mechanical c)\approx\quantity{1.097\times10^7}{m^{-1}}$. |
