---
title: "Characteristic Orders of Magnitude"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Use these length, energy, frequency, and time scales to estimate the size of atomic and nuclear effects.

| Scale | Order of magnitude or representative value |
| --- | --- |
| Atomic radius | $\sim\mechanical{a_0}\sim\quantity{10^{-10}}{m}$ |
| Nuclear radius | $\sim\quantity{10^{-15}}{m}$ |
| Atomic binding energy | $\sim\quantity{1}{eV}$ |
| Nuclear binding energy | $\sim\quantity{1}{MeV}$ |
| Electron rest energy $\mechanical{m_e}\mechanical c^2$ | $\quantity{0.511}{MeV}$ |
| Proton rest energy $\mechanical{m_p}\mechanical c^2$ | $\quantity{938}{MeV}$ |
| Typical atomic frequency | $\sim\quantity{10^{15}}{Hz}$ (ultraviolet) |
| Electric dipole (E1) decay lifetime | $\sim\quantity{1}{ns}$ |
| Thermal energy at room temperature | $\sim\quantity{1/40}{eV}$ |
