---
title: "Spherical Tensor Operators"
---

> [!TLDR]
> A spherical tensor of rank $k$ is a family of $2k+1$ operator components. Their commutators with angular momentum follow the same algebra as a spin-$k$ multiplet.

For components $\hat T_q^k$ with $q=-k,-k+1,\ldots,k$, the defining commutators are

$$
\begin{aligned}
[\mechanical{\hat J}_z,\hat T_q^k]&=\mechanical{\hbar}q\hat T_q^k,\\
[\mechanical{\hat J}_\pm,\hat T_q^k]&=\mechanical{\hbar}\sqrt{k(k+1)-q(q\pm1)}\,\hat T_{q\pm1}^k.
\end{aligned}
\tag{11.11}
$$

These relations reproduce the action of $\mechanical{\hat J}_z$ and $\mechanical{\hat J}_\pm$ on $\lvert k,q\rangle$. The operator components therefore transform under rotations as the components of this multiplet.

> [!definition] Definition 11.1. Spherical tensor
> The family $\{\hat T_q^k\}_{q=-k,\ldots,k}$ is a spherical tensor of rank $k$ if it satisfies Eq. (11.11).

The identity $\hat I$ is a rank-zero tensor, or scalar. The position operator $\hat{\mathbf r}$ gives a rank-one tensor with components

$$
\hat r_{+1}^{\,1}=-\frac{\hat x+i\hat y}{\sqrt2},\qquad
\hat r_0^{\,1}=\hat z,\qquad
\hat r_{-1}^{\,1}=\frac{\hat x-i\hat y}{\sqrt2}.
$$

The spherical harmonics $Y_{kq}(\theta,\phi)$ also form such a family when used as multiplication operators.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\hat T_q^k$ | Depends on operator | Spherical tensor component | The operator component with rank $k$ and projection label $q$. |
| $k,q$ | $1$ | Rank and component index | The tensor representation label and the projection within it. |
| $\mechanical{\hat J}_z,\mechanical{\hat J}_\pm$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Angular momentum generators | The axial and ladder generators that act by commutation. |
| $\hat r_q^{\,1}$ | $\unit m$ | Spherical position component | A rank-one combination of Cartesian position operators. |
| $Y_{kq}$ | $1$ | Spherical harmonic | The angular function used as a multiplication operator. |
| $\mechanical{\hbar}$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Reduced Planck constant | The scale in the commutator relations. |
