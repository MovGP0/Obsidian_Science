---
title: "Uncoupled and Coupled Bases"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The uncoupled basis gives the individual axial projections. The coupled basis gives the magnitude and axial projection of the total angular momentum.

The joint Hilbert space is the tensor product of the subsystem spaces. At fixed $j_1,j_2$, its dimension is

$$
\dim\mathcal H_{j_1j_2}=(2j_1+1)(2j_2+1).
\tag{11.4}
$$

The **uncoupled basis** consists of simultaneous eigenstates of $\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf J}}_1^{\,2},\mechanical{\hat J}_{1z},\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf J}}_2^{\,2},\mechanical{\hat J}_{2z}$.

$$
\lvert j_1m_1;j_2m_2\rangle=\lvert j_1m_1\rangle\otimes\lvert j_2m_2\rangle,\qquad
m_i=-j_i,-j_i+1,\ldots,j_i.
$$

The **coupled basis** consists of simultaneous eigenstates of $\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf J}}_1^{\,2},\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf J}}_2^{\,2},\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf J}}^{\,2},\mechanical{\hat J}_z$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf J}}^{\,2}\lvert j_1j_2;JM\rangle
&=\mechanical{\hbar}^{\,2}J(J+1)\lvert j_1j_2;JM\rangle,\\
\mechanical{\hat J}_z\lvert j_1j_2;JM\rangle
&=\mechanical{\hbar}M\lvert j_1j_2;JM\rangle.
\end{aligned}
$$

Both bases are complete and orthonormal in the same space of dimension $(2j_1+1)(2j_2+1)$. The change of basis is unitary.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf J}}_1^{\,2},\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf J}}_2^{\,2},\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf J}}^{\,2}$ | $\unit{J^2\,s^2}$ | Squared angular momenta | The squared magnitudes of the individual and total operators. |
| $\mechanical{\hat J}_{1z},\mechanical{\hat J}_{2z},\mechanical{\hat J}_z$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Axial projections | The individual or total axial components specified by the basis. |
| $j_1,j_2,m_1,m_2,J,M$ | $1$ | Angular momentum labels | The individual and total magnitudes and projection indices. |
| $\mechanical{\hbar}$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Reduced Planck constant | The scale for the projection eigenvalues. |
