title: "Addition of Angular Momentum Exercises"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Apply the angular momentum algebra, Clebsch–Gordan coefficients, and tensor selection rules.

1. Verify Eq. (11.3) directly from Eqs. (11.1) and (11.2).  
   **Result.** The total $\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf J}}$ satisfies the angular momentum algebra.
2. Decompose the tensor product of two spin-$1/2$ representations into $J$ multiplets and give their dimensions.  
   **Result.** $\tfrac12\otimes\tfrac12=0\oplus1$ with dimensions $1+3=4$.
3. Decompose $\tfrac12\otimes1$ and $1\otimes1$ into $J$ multiplets.  
   **Result.** $\tfrac12\otimes1=\tfrac12\oplus\tfrac32$ with dimensions $2+4=6$. Also $1\otimes1=0\oplus1\oplus2$ with dimensions $1+3+5=9$.
4. Calculate $\langle\tfrac12,\tfrac12;\tfrac12,-\tfrac12\mid1,0\rangle$ and $\langle\tfrac12,\tfrac12;\tfrac12,-\tfrac12\mid0,0\rangle$.  
   **Result.** Both coefficients are $1/\sqrt2$.
5. Show that $\sum_{J=\lvert j_1-j_2\rvert}^{j_1+j_2}(2J+1)=(2j_1+1)(2j_2+1)$.  
   **Result.** Use induction or evaluate the arithmetic sum.
6. Give the spherical tensor ranks of $\hat I$, $\mechanical{\hat L}_z$, and $\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf L}}\cdot\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf S}}$.  
   **Result.** $\hat I$ and $\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf L}}\cdot\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf S}}$ are rank-zero scalars under total rotations. $\mechanical{\hat L}_z$ is the $q=0$ component of a rank-one tensor.
7. Use Wigner–Eckart to express $\langle jm'\lvert\mechanical{\hat J}_+\rvert jm\rangle$ through a Clebsch–Gordan coefficient and $\langle j\lVert\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf J}}\rVert j\rangle$.  
   **Result.** $\langle jm'\lvert\mechanical{\hat J}_+\rvert jm\rangle=\mechanical{\hbar}\sqrt{j(j+1)-m(m+1)}\,\delta_{m',m+1}$. The reduced element is $\langle j\lVert\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf J}}\rVert j\rangle=\mechanical{\hbar}\sqrt{j(j+1)(2j+1)}$.
8. Calculate the eigenvalue of $\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf L}}\cdot\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf S}}$ for $j=\ell\pm1/2$ in terms of $\ell$ and $\mechanical{\hbar}$.  
   **Result.** $\mechanical{\hbar}^{\,2}\ell/2$ for $j=\ell+1/2$ and $-\mechanical{\hbar}^{\,2}(\ell+1)/2$ for $j=\ell-1/2$.
9. Show that for two spin-$1/2$ particles, $\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf S}}_1\cdot\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf S}}_2$ gives $\mechanical{\hbar}^{\,2}/4$ on the triplet and $-3\mechanical{\hbar}^{\,2}/4$ on the singlet.  
   **Hint.** Use $\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf S}}_1\cdot\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf S}}_2=(\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf S}}^{\,2}-\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf S}}_1^{\,2}-\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf S}}_2^{\,2})/2$.
10. Derive the Racah recurrence for the coefficients by applying $\mechanical{\hat J}_\pm=\mechanical{\hat J}_{1\pm}+\mechanical{\hat J}_{2\pm}$ to Eq. (11.6).  
    **Result.**
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    &\sqrt{J(J+1)-M(M\pm1)}
    \,\langle j_1m_1;j_2m_2\mid J,M\pm1\rangle\\
    &\quad=\sqrt{j_1(j_1+1)-m_1(m_1\mp1)}
    \,\langle j_1,m_1\mp1;j_2m_2\mid JM\rangle\\
    &\qquad+\sqrt{j_2(j_2+1)-m_2(m_2\mp1)}
    \,\langle j_1m_1;j_2,m_2\mp1\mid JM\rangle.
    \end{aligned}
    $$
11. Express $\sum_{m,q}\lvert\langle j'm'\lvert\hat T_q^k\rvert jm\rangle\rvert^2$ through $\langle j'\lVert\hat T^k\rVert j\rangle$.  
    **Result.** The sum is $\lvert\langle j'\lVert\hat T^k\rVert j\rangle\rvert^2/(2j'+1)$, independent of $m'$, by orthogonality of the Clebsch–Gordan coefficients.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf J}},\mechanical{\hat J}_\pm,\mechanical{\hat L}_z$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Angular momentum operators | The total vector, ladder components, and orbital axial component. |
| $\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf L}}\cdot\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf S}},\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf S}}_1\cdot\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf S}}_2$ | $\unit{J^2\,s^2}$ | Angular momentum dot products | Rotation scalars diagonal in the applicable coupled basis. |
| $j_1,j_2,j,J,m_1,m_2,m,M,\ell$ | $1$ | Angular momentum quantum numbers | The individual and coupled representation and projection indices. |
| $\hat T_q^k$ | Depends on operator | Spherical tensor component | The component whose matrix elements are summed. |
| $\langle j'\lVert\hat T^k\rVert j\rangle$ | Same as $\hat T_q^k$ | Reduced matrix element | The factor independent of magnetic quantum numbers. |
| $k,q$ | $1$ | Tensor rank and projection index | The tensor representation label and its component label. |
| $\mechanical{\hbar}$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Reduced Planck constant | The angular momentum scale. |
