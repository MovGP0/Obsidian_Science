---
title: "Angular Momentum and Spin"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Use angular momentum commutators, eigenvalues, and ladder operators to work with angular momentum states and spin rotations.

| Result | Formula |
| --- | --- |
| Angular momentum algebra | $[\mechanical{J_i},\mechanical{J_j}]=i\mechanical{\hbar}\varepsilon_{ijk}\mechanical{J_k}$, $[\mechanical{J^2},\mechanical{J_i}]=0$ |
| Eigenvalues | $\mechanical{J^2}\lvert j,m\rangle=\mechanical{\hbar}^2j(j+1)\lvert j,m\rangle$, $\mechanical{J_z}\lvert j,m\rangle=m\mechanical{\hbar}\lvert j,m\rangle$ |
| Ladder action | $\mechanical{J_\pm}\lvert j,m\rangle=\mechanical{\hbar}\sqrt{j(j+1)-m(m\pm1)}\lvert j,m\pm1\rangle$ |
| Pauli algebra | $[\sigma_i,\sigma_j]=2i\varepsilon_{ijk}\sigma_k$ |
| Spin rotation | $R_{\hat{\mathbf n}}(\alpha)=\cos(\alpha/2)I-i\sin(\alpha/2)\boldsymbol{\sigma}\cdot\hat{\mathbf n}$ |
| Larmor precession | $\omega_L=\gamma\magnetic B$ (signed frequency) |

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{J_i}$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Angular momentum | Component along axis $i$. |
| $\mechanical{\hbar}$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Reduced Planck constant | Quantum of action. |
| $\alpha$ | $\unit{rad}$ | Rotation angle | Angle about the unit axis $\hat{\mathbf n}$. |
| $\magnetic B$ | $\unit{T}$ | Magnetic flux density | Field that causes precession. |
| $\gamma$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}\,T^{-1}}$ | Gyromagnetic ratio | Signed coefficient that relates field to precession frequency. |
| $\omega_L$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Larmor frequency | Signed angular frequency of precession. |
