---
title: "Useful Identities and Integrals"
---

> [!TLDR]
> These integrals and distributional identities apply to wavefunction normalization, Fourier transforms, and angular expansions.

| Result | Identity |
| --- | --- |
| Gaussian integral | $\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}e^{-ax^2+bx}\,dx=\sqrt{\pi/a}\,e^{b^2/(4a)}$, $\operatorname{Re}a>0$ |
| Exponential moment | $\int_0^\infty x^ne^{-ax}\,dx=n!/a^{n+1}$ |
| Fourier delta identity | $\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}e^{ikx}\,dx=2\pi\delta(k)$ |
| Three-dimensional delta | $\delta^3(\mathbf r)=\delta(x)\delta(y)\delta(z)$ |
| Laplacian of the inverse radius | $\nabla^2(1/r)=-4\pi\delta^3(\mathbf r)$ |
| Legendre orthogonality | $\int_0^\pi P_\ell(\cos\theta)P_{\ell'}(\cos\theta)\sin\theta\,d\theta=2\delta_{\ell\ell'}/(2\ell+1)$ |
| Spherical harmonic orthogonality | $\int Y_{\ell m}^*Y_{\ell'm'}\,d\Omega=\delta_{\ell\ell'}\delta_{mm'}$ |
