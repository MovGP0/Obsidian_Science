---
title: "Perturbation Theory and Scattering"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Use perturbation theory for energy shifts and transitions. Use scattering amplitudes and phase shifts to calculate cross sections.

| Result | Formula |
| --- | --- |
| First-order energy shift | $\energy E_n^{(1)}=\langle n\lvert\energy{\hat V}\rvert n\rangle$ |
| Second-order energy shift | $\energy E_n^{(2)}=\sum_{m\ne n}\frac{\lvert\energy V_{mn}\rvert^2}{\energy E_n-\energy E_m}$ |
| First-order state correction | $\lvert n^{(1)}\rangle=\sum_{m\ne n}\frac{\energy V_{mn}}{\energy E_n-\energy E_m}\lvert m\rangle$ |
| Fermi's golden rule | $\Gamma_{i\to f}=(2\pi/\mechanical{\hbar})\lvert\energy V_{fi}\rvert^2\rho(\energy E_f)$ |
| Rabi oscillations on resonance | $P(\mechanical t)=\sin^2(\Omega_R\mechanical t/2)$ |
| Differential cross section | $d\mechanical\sigma/d\Omega=\lvert\mechanical f(\theta,\phi)\rvert^2$ |
| First Born amplitude | $\mechanical f^B(\theta)=-(\mechanical m/2\pi\mechanical{\hbar}^2)\widetilde V(\mathbf q)$ |
| Total cross section from partial waves | $\mechanical\sigma_{\mathrm{tot}}=(4\pi/k^2)\sum_\ell(2\ell+1)\sin^2\delta_\ell$ |
| Optical theorem | $\mechanical\sigma_{\mathrm{tot}}=(4\pi/k)\operatorname{Im}\mechanical f(0)$ |

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\energy E_n$ | $\unit{J}$ | Energy | Unperturbed energy of state $n$. |
| $\energy V_{mn}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Perturbation matrix element | Coupling between states $n$ and $m$. |
| $\Gamma_{i\to f}$ | $\unit{s^{-1}}$ | Transition rate | Rate from the initial state to final states. |
| $\rho(\energy E_f)$ | $\unit{J^{-1}}$ | Density of states | Number of final states per unit energy. |
| $\Omega_R$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Rabi angular frequency | Rate of resonant population oscillation. |
| $\mechanical f$ | $\unit{m}$ | Scattering amplitude | Amplitude in the outgoing spherical wave. |
| $\mechanical\sigma$ | $\unit{m^2}$ | Cross section | Effective scattering area. |
| $k$ | $\unit{m^{-1}}$ | Wavenumber | Magnitude of the incident wave vector. |
| $\delta_\ell$ | $\unit{rad}$ | Phase shift | Scattering phase of partial wave $\ell$. |
