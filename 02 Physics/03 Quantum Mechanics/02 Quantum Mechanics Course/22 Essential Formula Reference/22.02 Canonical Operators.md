---
title: "Canonical Operators"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The position and momentum representations exchange multiplication and differentiation. The Hamiltonian adds kinetic and potential energy.

| Operator or relation | Formula |
| --- | --- |
| Canonical commutator | $[\hat x_i,\mechanical{\hat p}_j]=i\mechanical{\hbar}\delta_{ij}\hat I$ |
| Momentum in position representation | $\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf p}}=-i\mechanical{\hbar}\nabla$ |
| Position in momentum representation | $\hat{\mathbf x}=i\mechanical{\hbar}\nabla_{\mathbf p}$ |
| Free Hamiltonian | $\energy{\hat H}_{\mathrm{free}}=\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf p}}^2/(2\mechanical m)$ |
| Hamiltonian with a potential | $\energy{\hat H}=\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf p}}^2/(2\mechanical m)+\energy V(\hat{\mathbf x})$ |

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\hat x_i,\mechanical{\hat p}_j$ | $\unit m,\unit{kg\,m\,s^{-1}}$ | Position and momentum | Canonically conjugate Cartesian components. |
| $\mechanical m$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | Sets the kinetic coefficient. |
| $\energy{\hat H},\energy V$ | $\unit J$ | Hamiltonian and potential energy | The total energy operator and its position-dependent term. |
