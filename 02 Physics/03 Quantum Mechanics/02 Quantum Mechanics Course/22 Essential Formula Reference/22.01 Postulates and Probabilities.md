> [!TLDR]
> These formulas give the normalization, measurement, and evolution rules for a pure state.

| Rule | Formula |
| --- | --- |
| State | $\lvert\signal\psi\rangle\in\mathcal H$, $\langle\signal\psi\mid\signal\psi\rangle=1$ |
| Observable | $\hat A=\hat A^\dagger$ |
| Born probability for a nondegenerate outcome | $P(a_n\mid\signal\psi)=\lvert\langle a_n\mid\signal\psi\rangle\rvert^2$ |
| Expectation | $\langle\hat A\rangle=\langle\signal\psi\rvert\hat A\lvert\signal\psi\rangle$ |
| Standard deviation | $\Delta A=\sqrt{\langle\hat A^2\rangle-\langle\hat A\rangle^2}$ |
| State after outcome $a$ with nonzero probability | $\lvert\signal\psi\rangle\mapsto\hat P_a\lvert\signal\psi\rangle/\lVert\hat P_a\lvert\signal\psi\rangle\rVert$ |
| Evolution | $i\mechanical{\hbar}\partial_t\lvert\signal\psi\rangle=\energy{\hat H}\lvert\signal\psi\rangle$ |

See [[02.00 Postulates of Quantum Mechanics]] for the derivations and examples.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\hat A,\Delta A$ | Depends on observable | Observable and standard deviation | The self-adjoint operator and the spread of its outcomes. |
| $P(a_n\mid\signal\psi)$ | $1$ | Probability | The Born probability of outcome $a_n$. |
| $\energy{\hat H},\mechanical{\hbar},t$ | $\unit{J},\unit{J\,s},\unit s$ | Hamiltonian, reduced Planck constant, and time | The quantities in the evolution equation. |
