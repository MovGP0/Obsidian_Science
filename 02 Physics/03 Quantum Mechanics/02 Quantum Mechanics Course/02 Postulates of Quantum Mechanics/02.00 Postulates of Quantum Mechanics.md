> [!TLDR]
> The postulates specify how to represent a quantum system and predict the outcomes of measurements and time evolution.

The experiments in Chapter 1 show why quantum phenomena require a new theory. The **postulates** summarize quantum mechanics as a compact set of assumptions from which its predictions follow.

This chapter emphasizes the physics. It explains what each symbol represents, which assumptions apply, and what follows from them. It assumes familiarity with the mathematical background, including Hilbert spaces, self-adjoint operators, and the spectral theorem.

## Chapter summary

- A normalized vector $\lvert\psi\rangle$ in $\mathcal H$ represents the state up to global phase.
- Observables are self-adjoint operators. Measurement outcomes belong to their real spectra.
- The Born rule gives $P(a_n)=\lvert\langle a_n\mid\psi\rangle\rvert^2$ for a nondegenerate result. The expectation value is $\langle\hat A\rangle=\langle\psi\mid\hat A\mid\psi\rangle$, with standard deviation $\Delta A=\sqrt{\langle\hat A^2\rangle-\langle\hat A\rangle^2}$.
- An ideal projective measurement with result $a$ leaves $\hat P_a\lvert\psi\rangle/\lVert\hat P_a\lvert\psi\rangle\rVert$.
- Time evolution obeys $i\hbar\partial_t\lvert\psi\rangle=\energy{\hat H}\lvert\psi\rangle$. It is unitary, linear, and reversible.
- An eigenstate of a time-independent Hamiltonian evolves by the global phase $e^{-i\energy{E}t/\hbar}$ and is stationary.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $P$ | $1$ | Outcome probability | The probability of the indicated outcome. |
| $a_n,a,\langle\hat A\rangle,\Delta A$ | — | Measurement values and statistics | The outcomes, mean, and standard deviation have the unit of the observable. |
| $\energy{\hat H},\energy{E}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Hamiltonian and energy | The time-evolution generator and its eigenvalue. |
| $\hbar$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Reduced Planck constant | The action scale in time evolution. |
| $t$ | $\unit{s}$ | Time | The independent variable for the evolution. |
