> [!TLDR]
> The hydrogen atom separates into center-of-mass motion and a Coulomb problem. The bound energies depend only on the principal quantum number in the nonrelativistic model.

Hydrogen is a solvable quantum two-body system. Its spectrum agrees closely with observations once relativistic and radiative corrections are included. The hydrogen solution also provides a starting point for methods used on more complex atoms.

The calculation combines separation of variables for a central potential, angular momentum algebra, and a one-dimensional radial equation with a centrifugal term. The nonrelativistic result gives the Bohr energies and an $n^2$ degeneracy in the spatial states. This is larger than the degeneracy required by ordinary rotational symmetry.

## Chapter summary

- Separate the center of mass of the electron–proton system. The relative coordinate has reduced mass $\mechanical{\mu}$ and a Coulomb potential with a centrifugal term for $\ell>0$.
- The spectrum is $\energy E_n=-\energy{E_{\mathrm{Ry}}}/n^2$, with $\energy{E_{\mathrm{Ry}}}=\mechanical m_e\electric e^{\,4}/[2(4\pi\electric{\varepsilon}_0)^2\mechanical{\hbar}^{\,2}]\simeq13.6\,\unit{eV}$ in the infinite-proton-mass approximation.
- The quantum numbers satisfy $n=1,2,\ldots$, $\ell=0,\ldots,n-1$, and $m=-\ell,\ldots,\ell$.
- Each $n$ has $n^2$ spatial states, or $2n^2$ with electron spin before spin-dependent corrections.
- The eigenfunctions are $\signal{\psi}_{n\ell m}=R_{n\ell}Y_{\ell m}$, with associated Laguerre polynomials in $R_{n\ell}$.
- The ground state has $\signal{\psi}_{100}\propto e^{-r/a_0}$, $\langle r\rangle=3a_0/2$, and $\langle\energy V\rangle=2\energy E_1$.
- Electric dipole transitions satisfy $\Delta\ell=\pm1$ and $\Delta m=0,\pm1$.
- Corrections include fine structure at order $\alpha^2\energy{E_{\mathrm{Ry}}}$, the Zeeman effect, and hyperfine structure.

| $\mechanical{\mu},\mechanical m_e$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Reduced and electron masses | The relative-motion mass and its leading approximation. |
| $\energy E_n,\energy{E_{\mathrm{Ry}}},\energy V$ | $\unit{J}$ | Energies | The level energy, Rydberg scale, and Coulomb potential. |
| $\signal{\psi}_{n\ell m},R_{n\ell}$ | $\unit{m^{-3/2}}$ | Wave functions | The position amplitude and its radial factor. |
| $Y_{\ell m},n,\ell,m$ | $1$ | Spherical harmonic and quantum numbers | The angular factor and its orbital labels, with the principal label. |

| $\alpha$ | $1$ | Fine-structure constant | The dimensionless coupling that sets the correction scale. |
| $\electric e,\electric{\varepsilon}_0$ | $\unit C,\unit{F\,m^{-1}}$ | Charge and permittivity | The constants in the Coulomb potential. |
| $\mechanical{\hbar}$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Reduced Planck constant | The action scale of the spectrum. |
