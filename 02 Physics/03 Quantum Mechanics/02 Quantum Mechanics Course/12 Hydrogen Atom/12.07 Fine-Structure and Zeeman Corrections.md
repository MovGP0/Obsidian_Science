---
title: "Fine-Structure and Zeeman Corrections"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Fine structure corrects the nonrelativistic Coulomb spectrum. Magnetic fields produce additional Zeeman shifts.

The result $\energy E_n\propto-1/n^2$ is the leading nonrelativistic spectrum.

## Fine structure

The fine-structure corrections are of order $\alpha^4\mechanical m_e\signal c^2$, or $\alpha^2\energy{E_{\mathrm{Ry}}}$.

- Relativistic kinetic correction $-\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf p}}^{\,4}/(8\mechanical m_e^{\,3}\signal c^2)$.
- Spin–orbit coupling proportional to $\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf L}}\cdot\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf S}}$.
- Darwin term proportional to $\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf r)$.

The sum is

$$
\Delta\energy E_{\mathrm{fs}}
=-\energy{E_{\mathrm{Ry}}}\frac{\alpha^2}{n^3}
\left(\frac1{j+1/2}-\frac{3}{4n}\right).
$$

Here $j=\ell\pm1/2$ when both values are allowed. The shift depends on $n,j$. For $n=2$, the states are $2S_{1/2}$, $2P_{1/2}$, and $2P_{3/2}$. The characteristic scale before the $n,j$ factors is $\alpha^2\energy{E_{\mathrm{Ry}}}$, of order $10^{-3}\,\unit{eV}$.

> [!note] Orbital quantum number
> The source states that $\ell$ ceases to be a good quantum number. The three central fine-structure terms shown here commute with $\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf L}}^{\,2}$, so $\ell$ still labels the states. The energy shift is independent of $\ell$ at fixed $n,j$, so the $2S_{1/2}$ and $2P_{1/2}$ levels remain degenerate at this order.

## Zeeman effect

An external magnetic field adds $\energy{\hat H}_Z=-\magnetic{\hat{\boldsymbol{\mu}}}\cdot\magnetic{\mathbf B}$. Within a fixed-$j$ multiplet, project the magnetic moment onto the total angular momentum to get $\magnetic{\hat{\boldsymbol{\mu}}}=-g_J\magnetic{\mu}_B\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf J}}/\mechanical{\hbar}$. Here $g_J$ is the **Landé factor** and $\magnetic{\mu}_B=\electric e\mechanical{\hbar}/(2\mechanical m_e)$ is the **Bohr magneton**. Thus

$$
\energy{\hat H}_Z=\frac{g_J\magnetic{\mu}_B}{\mechanical{\hbar}}\,
\mechanical{\hat{\mathbf J}}\cdot\magnetic{\mathbf B}.
$$

For a field weak compared with the fine-structure splitting, $j,m_j$ label the states in the projected description. For $\magnetic{\mathbf B}=\magnetic B\hat{\mathbf z}$, each level shifts by $\Delta\energy E=g_Jm_j\magnetic{\mu}_B\magnetic B$, lifting the degeneracy in $m_j$.

## Stark effect

An external electric field breaks additional symmetries and changes the energy levels. See [[16.00 Perturbation Theory]].

## Hyperfine structure

Coupling of electron spin and proton spin gives hyperfine splitting. In atomic hydrogen it produces the $21\,\unit{cm}$ line at about $1420\,\unit{MHz}$, which is important in radio astronomy.
