> [!TLDR]
> Hydrogen emits photons at energy differences between levels. Electric dipole selection rules determine which transitions can produce lines.

An emission transition from $\lvert n\ell m\rangle$ to $\lvert n'\ell'm'\rangle$ with $\energy E_n>\energy E_{n'}$ gives a photon of energy $\mechanical{\hbar}\signal{\omega}=\energy E_n-\energy E_{n'}$. The electric dipole matrix element is

$$
\electric{\mathbf d}_{fi}=-\electric e\,\langle n'\ell'm'\lvert\hat{\mathbf r}\rvert n\ell m\rangle.
$$

The [[11.09 Electric Dipole Selection Rules|electric dipole selection rules]] give $\Delta\ell=\pm1$, $\Delta m=0,\pm1$, and $\Delta s=0$ in this spin-independent approximation. Parity and the angular momentum carried by the field constrain $\Delta\ell$.

## Principal series

- **Lyman series.** Transitions to $n'=1$ are ultraviolet. The first line has $\signal{\lambda}\simeq121.6\,\unit{nm}$.
- **Balmer series.** Transitions to $n'=2$ include visible lines. The first line is $\mathrm H_\alpha$ at about $656\,\unit{nm}$.
- **Paschen series.** Transitions to $n'=3$ are infrared.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\energy E_n,\energy E_{n'}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Energy levels | The initial and final bound-state energies. |
| $\signal{\omega},\signal{\lambda}$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}},\unit m$ | Photon angular frequency and wavelength | The spectral quantities determined by the energy gap. |
| $\electric{\mathbf d}_{fi}$ | $\unit{C\,m}$ | Dipole matrix element | The electric dipole amplitude between the final and initial states. |
| $\electric e,\hat{\mathbf r}$ | $\unit C,\unit m$ | Elementary charge and position | The constants and operator in the electron dipole. |
| $n,\ell,m,s$ | $1$ | Quantum numbers | The principal, orbital, magnetic, and spin labels. |
| $\mechanical{\hbar}$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Reduced Planck constant | The factor that converts the photon angular frequency into energy. |
