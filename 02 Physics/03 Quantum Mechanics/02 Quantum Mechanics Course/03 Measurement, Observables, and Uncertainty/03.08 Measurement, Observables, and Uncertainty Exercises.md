---
title: "Measurement, Observables, and Uncertainty Exercises"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Practice commutators, uncertainty bounds, and the statistics of successive measurements.

1. Calculate $[\mechanical{\hat x}^{\,2},\mechanical{\hat p}]$ and $[\mechanical{\hat x},\mechanical{\hat p}^{\,2}]$.
   **Result.** $2i\hbar\mechanical{\hat x}$ and $2i\hbar\mechanical{\hat p}$.
2. Show that $[\mechanical{\hat x},f(\mechanical{\hat p})]=i\hbar f'(\mechanical{\hat p})$ for an analytic function $f$ on a suitable domain.
3. Verify explicitly that $\signal{\psi}(\mechanical{x})=(2\pi\sigma^2)^{-1/4}e^{-\mechanical{x}^2/(4\sigma^2)}$ gives $\Delta\mechanical{x}\,\Delta\mechanical{p}=\hbar/2$.
4. Show that an eigenstate of $\hat A$ has $\Delta A=0$. Use the Robertson inequality to show that if $\langle\psi\mid[\hat A,\hat B]\mid\psi\rangle\ne0$, then $\lvert\psi\rangle$ cannot be an eigenstate of $\hat A$.
5. For $\lvert\psi\rangle=\cos(\theta/2)\lvert+\rangle+e^{i\phi}\sin(\theta/2)\lvert-\rangle$ in the $z$ basis, calculate $\Delta\mechanical{S}_z$.
   **Result.** $\Delta\mechanical{S}_z=(\hbar/2)\lvert\sin\theta\rvert$.
6. An excited atomic state has lifetime $\tau=\quantity{10}{ns}$. Find the energy line width $\energy{\Gamma}=\hbar/\tau$ in electronvolts and the corresponding wavelength width near $\signal{\lambda}_0=\quantity{500}{nm}$.
   **Result.** $\energy{\Gamma}\simeq\quantity{6.6\times10^{-8}}{eV}$ and $\Delta\signal{\lambda}\simeq\signal{\lambda}_0^2\energy{\Gamma}/(hc)\simeq\quantity{1.3\times10^{-14}}{m}$.
7. For a Gaussian state with $\Delta\mechanical{x}=\sigma$, find the probability of a position more than $3\sigma$ from the mean. Compare with the classical distribution.
   **Result.** Approximately $2.7\times10^{-3}$.
8. Set $\hat A=\sigma_x$ and $\hat B=\sigma_z$. Show that $[\hat A,\hat B]=-2i\sigma_y$. Which pure states attain equality in the Robertson inequality?
   **Result.** $\langle\sigma_x\rangle\langle\sigma_z\rangle=0$. The Bloch vector lies in the $xy$ or $yz$ plane. For the eigenstates of $\sigma_y$, both sides equal one.
9. Find $\Delta\mechanical{x}$ at time $t$ for a free Gaussian packet with $\Delta\mechanical{x}(0)=\sigma$ and $\Delta\mechanical{p}(0)=\hbar/(2\sigma)$. See [[04.04 Wave Packets]].
   **Result.** $\Delta\mechanical{x}(t)=\sigma\sqrt{1+[\hbar t/(2\mechanical{m}\sigma^2)]^2}$. The packet spreads.
10. **Schrödinger inequality.** Use Cauchy–Schwarz and retain the real part to prove

    $$
    (\Delta A)^2(\Delta B)^2
    \ge\tfrac14\lvert\langle[\hat A,\hat B]\rangle\rvert^2+
    \tfrac14\lvert\langle\{\hat A-\langle\hat A\rangle,\hat B-\langle\hat B\rangle\}\rangle\rvert^2.
    $$

    State when the second term contributes.
11. **Semiclassical limit.** Show that the harmonic oscillator eigenstate $\lvert n\rangle$ gives $\Delta\mechanical{x}\,\Delta\mechanical{p}=(n+\tfrac12)\hbar\gg\hbar/2$ as $n\to\infty$. Discuss the relation to the classical limit.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{\hat x},\mechanical{x},\Delta\mechanical{x},\sigma$ | $\unit{m}$ | Position and width | The position operator, coordinate, and Gaussian standard deviation. |
| $\mechanical{\hat p},\Delta\mechanical{p}$ | $\unit{kg\,m\,s^{-1}}$ | Momentum and width | The momentum operator and momentum standard deviation. |
| $\signal{\psi}(\mechanical{x})$ | $\unit{m^{-1/2}}$ | Position wave function | The normalized one-dimensional Gaussian amplitude. |
| $h,\hbar$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Planck constants | The Planck constant and its reduced form. |
| $\Delta A,\Delta B$ | — | Standard deviations | The spreads of outcomes for the respective observables. |
| $\Delta\mechanical{S}_z$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Spin uncertainty | The standard deviation of the $z$ projection. |
| $\theta,\phi$ | $\unit{rad}$ | Spin preparation angles | The mixing angle and relative phase. |
| $\tau,t$ | $\unit{s}$ | Lifetime and elapsed time | The excited-state lifetime and the duration of free evolution. |
| $\energy{\Gamma}$ | $\unit{eV}$ | Energy line width | The characteristic width due to the finite lifetime. |
| $\signal{\lambda}_0,\Delta\signal{\lambda}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Wavelength and line width | The reference wavelength and the corresponding width. |
| $c$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Speed of light | The speed of light in vacuum. |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | The free particle mass. |
