title: "Time Reversal"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Time reversal is antiunitary. It reverses momenta and angular momenta. If it commutes with the Hamiltonian and squares to $-\hat I$, energy levels have Kramers partners.

Time reversal $\hat\Theta$ acts on these quantities as follows.

$$
t\mapsto-t,\quad
\mathbf r\mapsto\mathbf r,\quad
\mechanical{\mathbf p}\mapsto-\mechanical{\mathbf p},\quad
\mechanical{\mathbf L}\mapsto-\mechanical{\mathbf L},\quad
\mechanical{\mathbf S}\mapsto-\mechanical{\mathbf S}.
$$

The operator $\hat\Theta$ is antiunitary. In particular, it conjugates complex coefficients. For a spinless particle, it acts on the position wave function by $\hat\Theta\signal{\psi}(\mathbf r)=\signal{\psi}^*(\mathbf r)$. When $[\hat\Theta,\energy{\hat H}]=0$ for a time-independent Hamiltonian, $\signal{\psi}^*(\mathbf r,-t)$ is also a solution of the Schrödinger equation.

For spin $1/2$ in the usual $z$ basis, $\hat\Theta=-i\sigma_y\hat K$, where $\hat K$ is complex conjugation. For half-integer spin, $\hat\Theta^2=-\hat I$.

## Kramers theorem

For a time-reversal invariant system with an odd number of spin-$1/2$ fermions, every energy level is at least twofold degenerate. When $\hat\Theta^2=-\hat I$, the states $\lvert\signal{\psi}\rangle$ and $\hat\Theta\lvert\signal{\psi}\rangle$ are linearly independent.

An external magnetic field or magnetic order can break time-reversal symmetry. The symmetry then no longer protects the doublet, and an applied field can split the partners. Time-reversal breaking also plays a role in phenomena such as the Hall effect.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $t,\mathbf r$ | $\unit{s},\unit{m}$ | Time and position | Time changes sign and position is invariant under time reversal. |
| $\mechanical{\mathbf p}$ | $\unit{kg\,m\,s^{-1}}$ | Momentum | Changes sign under time reversal. |
| $\mechanical{\mathbf L},\mechanical{\mathbf S}$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Orbital and spin angular momentum | Both angular momenta change sign under time reversal. |
| $\magnetic{\mathbf B}$ | $\unit{T}$ | Magnetic field | A fixed external field can lift Kramers degeneracy. |
