---
title: "Symmetries as Unitary Operators"
---

> [!TLDR]
> A unitary symmetry leaves the Hamiltonian unchanged. A time-independent generator of a continuous symmetry is a conserved observable.

> [!definition] Definition 17.1 · Unitary symmetry
> A unitary symmetry of a quantum system is a unitary operator $\hat U$ on $\mathcal H$ that commutes with its Hamiltonian.
>
> $$
> \hat U^\dagger\energy{\hat H}\hat U=\energy{\hat H}
> \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
> [\hat U,\energy{\hat H}]=0.
> $$

A unitary $\hat U$ preserves transition probabilities, since $\lvert\langle\signal{\phi}\mid\signal{\psi}\rangle\rvert^2=\lvert\langle\hat U\signal{\phi}\mid\hat U\signal{\psi}\rangle\rvert^2$. Wigner's theorem also permits antiunitary symmetries, such as [[17.05 Time Reversal|time reversal]]. If $\hat U$ commutes with the Hamiltonian, it maps energy eigenstates to eigenstates at the same energy. This leads to degeneracy when the symmetry connects linearly independent states.

## Continuous symmetries

A continuous one-parameter unitary group has $\hat U(0)=\hat I$ and

$$
\hat U(\lambda)=e^{-i\hat G\lambda/\mechanical{\hbar}},\qquad
\hat G=i\mechanical{\hbar}\left.\frac{\mathrm d\hat U}{\mathrm d\lambda}\right\rvert_{\lambda=0}.
$$

The infinitesimal generator $\hat G$ is self-adjoint.

> [!theorem] Theorem 17.1 · Quantum Noether theorem
> The condition $[\hat U(\lambda),\energy{\hat H}]=0$ for all $\lambda$ is equivalent to $[\hat G,\energy{\hat H}]=0$.

Differentiate the commutation relation at $\lambda=0$ to get the condition on $\hat G$. Conversely, if $\hat G$ commutes with $\energy{\hat H}$, its exponential commutes as well. A time-independent $\hat G$ is therefore a constant of motion.

## Discrete symmetries

For a finite-order unitary symmetry, $\hat U^n=\hat I$ for some positive integer $n$. Parity is an example with $n=2$. The eigenvalues of $\hat U$ are $n$th roots of unity, and energy eigenspaces can be organized by their symmetry properties.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\energy{\hat H}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Hamiltonian | The energy operator that the symmetry preserves. |
| $\hat U$ | $1$ | Symmetry operator | The unitary that transforms the state. |
| $\hat G,\lambda$ | Depends on symmetry | Generator and group parameter | Their product has units of action. |
