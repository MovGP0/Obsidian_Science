---
title: "Brillouin Zones"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The first Brillouin zone contains one representative of each Bloch wave number modulo $2\pi/a$. Extended, reduced, and repeated zone schemes display the same band information.

The equivalence $k\sim k+2\pi/a$ gives three ways to display the bands.

- The **extended-zone scheme** places successive bands in successive Brillouin zones along the full $k$ axis.
- The **reduced-zone scheme** plots every band $\energy E_n(k)$ over $[-\pi/a,\pi/a)$. Several dispersion curves then appear above the same interval.
- The **repeated-zone scheme** copies the reduced-zone display periodically along the full $k$ axis.

> [!definition] Definition 19.1 · First Brillouin zone
> For a one-dimensional lattice with period $a$, the first Brillouin zone is $[-\pi/a,\pi/a)$. It contains exactly one representative of each equivalence class modulo $2\pi/a$.
