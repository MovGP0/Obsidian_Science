---
title: "Kronig–Penney Model"
---

> [!TLDR]
> A periodic array of repulsive delta barriers has allowed bands where the transfer relation admits a real Bloch wave number.

The Kronig–Penney model in its delta-barrier form uses

$$
\energy V(x)=\alpha\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}\delta(x-na),\qquad \alpha>0.
$$

The particle is free in each interval $(na,(n+1)a)$. For energy $\energy E>0$, the wave function there is $\signal\psi(x)=A e^{iqx}+B e^{-iqx}$, where $q=\sqrt{2\mechanical m\energy E}/\mechanical{\hbar}$.

> [!note] Wave function between barriers
> The source denotes the free solution by $u(x)$ in this step. The plane waves $e^{\pm iqx}$ describe $\signal\psi(x)$. Its periodic factor is $\signal u(x)=e^{-ikx}\signal\psi(x)$.

At a barrier, $\signal\psi$ is continuous and its derivative jumps by $(2\mechanical m\alpha/\mechanical{\hbar}^2)\signal\psi(na)$. Combining these matching conditions with $\signal\psi(x+a)=e^{ika}\signal\psi(x)$ gives

$$
\cos(ka)=\cos(qa)+\frac{\mechanical m\alpha}{\mechanical{\hbar}^2 q}\sin(qa).
\tag{19.5}
$$

Let $f(q)$ be the right-hand side. A real $k$ exists exactly where $\lvert f(q)\rvert\le1$. Those intervals are allowed bands. Intervals where $\lvert f(q)\rvert>1$ are gaps. The term proportional to $1/q$ changes the oscillations substantially at small $q$. At large $q$, $f(q)$ approaches $\cos(qa)$. The first allowed band is the narrowest in $q$ for the parameters shown, and the forbidden intervals become narrower along the $q$ axis at high $q$.

> [!note] Limits of barrier strength
> As $\alpha\to0$, the gaps close and the dispersion becomes $\energy E=\mechanical{\hbar}^2q^2/(2\mechanical m)$ with $k=\pm q$ modulo $2\pi/a$. As $\alpha\to\infty$, each cell becomes an isolated well. The bands shrink to discrete well energies, as in the limit of vanishing coupling between cells.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\alpha$ | $\unit{J\,m}$ | Delta-barrier strength | The coefficient of each Dirac delta. |
| $a$ | $\unit{m}$ | Lattice period | The spacing between adjacent barriers. |
| $k,q$ | $\unit{rad\,m^{-1}}$ | Bloch and free-region wave numbers | The translation label and the wave number between barriers. |
| $\energy E$ | $\unit{J}$ | Energy | The quantity that determines $q$. |
| $f(q)$ | $1$ | Transfer relation | Its magnitude determines whether the energy is allowed. |
