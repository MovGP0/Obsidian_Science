---
title: "Bloch Theorem"
---

> [!TLDR]
> A Bloch eigenfunction is a plane wave multiplied by a lattice-periodic function. Each Bloch wave number has a discrete sequence of band energies.

> [!theorem] Theorem 19.1 · Bloch theorem
> For a Hamiltonian with potential of period $a$, choose the generalized energy eigenfunctions in the form
>
> $$
> \signal{\psi}_{nk}(x)=e^{ikx}\signal{u}_{nk}(x),
> \tag{19.3}
> $$
>
> where $\signal{u}_{nk}(x+a)=\signal{u}_{nk}(x)$ and $k\in[-\pi/a,\pi/a)$. The band index $n=0,1,2,\ldots$ enumerates the energies $\energy{E}_n(k)$ at each fixed $k$.

**Proof.** By Eq. (19.2) in [[19.01 Discrete Translation Symmetry]], the states can be chosen to diagonalize both $\hat T_a$ and $\energy{\hat H}$. The equation $\hat T_a\signal{\psi}=e^{-ika}\signal{\psi}$ gives $\signal{\psi}(x+a)=e^{ika}\signal{\psi}(x)$. Define $\signal u(x)=e^{-ikx}\signal{\psi}(x)$. Then

$$
\signal u(x+a)
=e^{-ik(x+a)}\signal{\psi}(x+a)
=e^{-ikx}\signal{\psi}(x)
=\signal u(x).
$$

At fixed $k$, the Hamiltonian is equivalent to a self-adjoint operator acting on the periodic factor on a cell of length $a$, with periodic boundary conditions. Its spectrum is discrete and countable, with $\energy{E}_0(k)\le\energy{E}_1(k)\le\energy{E}_2(k)\le\cdots$.

The translation property takes the form

$$
\signal{\psi}_{nk}(x+a)=e^{ika}\signal{\psi}_{nk}(x).
$$

The factor $e^{ikx}$ sets the phase advance per cell. The function $\signal u_{nk}$ modulates it with the lattice period. The quasimomentum $\mechanical{\hbar}k$ need not equal the mechanical momentum. It is the crystal counterpart of momentum. At fixed $n$, $\energy E_n(k)$ gives the dispersion of that band.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $a$ | $\unit{m}$ | Lattice period | The spatial period of the modulation. |
| $k$ | $\unit{rad\,m^{-1}}$ | Bloch wave number | Sets the phase advance $ka$ per cell. |
| $\mechanical{\hbar}k$ | $\unit{kg\,m\,s^{-1}}$ | Quasimomentum | Momentum label associated with discrete translations. |
| $\energy E_n(k)$ | $\unit{J}$ | Band energy | The eigenvalue at band index $n$ and wave number $k$. |
