> [!TLDR]
> These six worked exercises cover energy statistics, motion in a box, spin evolution, interference, and a complete harmonic-oscillator analysis.

Chapter 15: [[00 Expectation Value and Uncertainty|Expectation Value, Variance, and Quantum Uncertainty]]. Each linked note contains the complete question and its worked solution.

Use Exercises 1–3 to separate energy certainty, zero mean momentum, and nonzero position or momentum spread. Before each calculation, identify the basis that makes the relevant observable simple. A stationary energy state need not have definite position or momentum.

In Exercises 4 and 5, predict which quantities stay constant before expanding the time-dependent state. Check phase and rotation signs at the initial time. Exercise 6 then combines number-state selection rules, the uncertainty bound, mean motion, and normalization. Compare its Gaussian ground state with the non-Gaussian box states to explain why only the former reaches the minimum product in these examples.
