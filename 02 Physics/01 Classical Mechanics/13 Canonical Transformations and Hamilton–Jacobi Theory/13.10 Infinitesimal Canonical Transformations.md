---
title: "Infinitesimal Canonical Transformations"
---

> [!TLDR]
> An infinitesimal canonical transformation acts on any phase-space function through its Poisson bracket with a generator.

A canonical transformation is **infinitesimal** if $Q_k=q_k+\varepsilon\xi_k(q,p)$ and $P_k=p_k+\varepsilon\eta_k(q,p)$, with small $\varepsilon$. The condition for canonicity to first order requires a local function $G(q,p)$, the **infinitesimal generator**, with

$$
\xi_k=\frac{\partial G}{\partial p_k},\qquad
\eta_k=-\frac{\partial G}{\partial q_k}.
$$

Equivalently, $\delta f=\varepsilon\{f,G\}$ for every $f(q,p)$.

## Examples

- **Translation along $x$.** With $G=\mechanical{p}_x$, one has $\delta\mechanical{x}=\varepsilon$, $\delta\mechanical{y}=\delta\mechanical{z}=0$, and $\delta\mechanical{\vec p}=0$.
- **Rotation about $z$.** With $G=\mechanical{L}_z$, one has $\delta\mechanical{x}=-\varepsilon\mechanical{y}$ and $\delta\mechanical{y}=\varepsilon\mechanical{x}$.
- **Time evolution.** With $G=\energy{H}$, one has $\delta f=\varepsilon\{f,\energy{H}\}=\varepsilon\,df/dt$ for $f(q,p)$ without explicit time dependence. The Hamiltonian generates the time evolution.

**Relation to Noether's theorem.** For a time-independent generator, a symmetry of the Hamiltonian satisfies $\{G,\energy{H}\}=0$. Thus $G$ is a first integral. This is the Hamiltonian form of Noether's theorem in this case.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $q_k,p_k,Q_k,P_k$ | — | Canonical variables | The original and transformed coordinates and momenta. |
| $G,\varepsilon,\xi_k,\eta_k$ | — | Transformation quantities | The generator, small parameter, and coefficients of the changes. |
| $\mechanical{x},\mechanical{y},\mechanical{z}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Cartesian coordinates | The coordinates transformed by a translation or rotation. |
| $\mechanical{p}_x,\mechanical{\vec p}$ | $\unit{kg\,m\,s^{-1}}$ | Linear momentum | The translation generator and the momentum vector. |
| $\mechanical{L}_z$ | $\unit{kg\,m^2\,s^{-1}}$ | Axial angular momentum | The generator of rotations about the $z$ axis. |
| $\energy{H}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Hamiltonian | The generator of time evolution. |
| $t$ | $\unit{s}$ | Time | The independent variable for the motion. |
