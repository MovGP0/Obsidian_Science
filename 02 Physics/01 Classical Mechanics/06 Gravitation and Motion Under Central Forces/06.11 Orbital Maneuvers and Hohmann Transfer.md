---
title: "Orbital Maneuvers and Hohmann Transfer"
---

> [!TLDR]
> A Hohmann transfer uses an elliptical orbit and two velocity changes to move between coplanar circular orbits.

An orbital maneuver uses a thruster to change a spacecraft's velocity by $\Delta\mechanical{v}$. In the impulsive approximation, the velocity changes at a fixed position. This puts the spacecraft on a different Keplerian orbit about the central body.

**Hohmann transfer.** For a two-impulse transfer between coplanar circular orbits of radii $\mechanical{r}_1$ and $\mechanical{r}_2>\mechanical{r}_1$, use an ellipse with periapsis $\mechanical{r}_1$ and apoapsis $\mechanical{r}_2$. For orbits about the Sun, these points are the perihelion and aphelion.

The first velocity change, at periapsis, is

$$
\Delta\mechanical{v}_1=\sqrt{\frac{\mechanical{G}\mechanical{M}}{\mechanical{r}_1}}
\left(\sqrt{\frac{2\mechanical{r}_2}{\mechanical{r}_1+\mechanical{r}_2}}-1\right).
$$

The second velocity change, at apoapsis, is

$$
\Delta\mechanical{v}_2=\sqrt{\frac{\mechanical{G}\mechanical{M}}{\mechanical{r}_2}}
\left(1-\sqrt{\frac{2\mechanical{r}_1}{\mechanical{r}_1+\mechanical{r}_2}}\right).
$$

The total is $\Delta\mechanical{v}_{\mathrm{tot}}=\Delta\mechanical{v}_1+\Delta\mechanical{v}_2$. The transfer takes half the period of the ellipse.

$$
t_H=\pi\sqrt{\frac{(\mechanical{r}_1+\mechanical{r}_2)^3}{8\mechanical{G}\mechanical{M}}}.
$$

### Example — Earth-to-Mars Transfer

Use circular solar orbits of radii $\mechanical{r}_1=\quantity{1}{AU}$ and $\mechanical{r}_2=\quantity{1.524}{AU}$. The transfer orbit has a period of approximately $(1.262)^{3/2}$ years, or 1.418 years. The transfer takes about 0.71 years, or 8.5 months. Launch windows repeat about every 26 months, the synodic period of Earth and Mars.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\Delta\mechanical{v},\Delta\mechanical{v}_1,\Delta\mechanical{v}_2,\Delta\mechanical{v}_{\mathrm{tot}}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Velocity change | The speed changes from individual burns or their total. |
| $\mechanical{r}_1,\mechanical{r}_2$ | $\unit{m}$ | Orbit radius | The radii of the initial and final circular orbits. |
| $\mechanical{G}$ | $\unit{m^3\,kg^{-1}\,s^{-2}}$ | Gravitational constant | The constant in Newton's law of gravitation. |
| $\mechanical{M}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Central mass | The mass of the central body. |
| $t_H$ | $\unit{s}$ | Transfer time | Half the period of the elliptical transfer orbit. |
| — | $\unit{s}$ | Synodic period | The time between repeated relative alignments of Earth and Mars. |
