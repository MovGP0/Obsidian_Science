title: "Work and Energy"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Reference formulas for work, kinetic and potential energy, and power.

- **Work and kinetic energy.** $\energy{W}=\int\mechanical{\vec F}\cdot d\mechanical{\vec r}$ and $\energy{K}=\tfrac12\mechanical{m}\mechanical{v}^2$.
- **Work–energy theorem.** Net work satisfies $\energy{W}=\Delta\energy{K}$.
- **Conservative force.** $\mechanical{\vec F}=-\nabla\energy{U}$ and $\energy{E}=\energy{K}+\energy{U}$.
- **Instantaneous power.** $\energy{P}=\mechanical{\vec F}\cdot\mechanical{\vec v}$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\energy{W},\energy{K},\energy{U},\energy{E}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Work and energies | Work, kinetic energy, potential energy, and total mechanical energy. |
| $\mechanical{\vec F}$ | $\unit{N}$ | Force | The applied or net force. |
| $\mechanical{\vec r}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Position | The vector that traces the path. |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | The moving particle's mass. |
| $\mechanical{v},\mechanical{\vec v}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Speed and velocity | The magnitude and vector of velocity. |
| $\energy{P}$ | $\unit{W}$ | Power | The instantaneous rate of work. |
