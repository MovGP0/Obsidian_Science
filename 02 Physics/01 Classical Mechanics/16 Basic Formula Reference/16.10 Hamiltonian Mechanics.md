> [!TLDR]
> Reference equations for Hamiltonian motion, Poisson brackets, and Liouville's theorem.

- **Hamiltonian.** $\energy{H}=\sum_k p_k\dot q_k-\energy{L}$.
- **Hamilton's equations.** $\dot q_k=\partial\energy{H}/\partial p_k$ and $\dot p_k=-\partial\energy{H}/\partial q_k$.
- **Poisson bracket.** $\{f,g\}=\sum_k[(\partial f/\partial q_k)(\partial g/\partial p_k)-(\partial f/\partial p_k)(\partial g/\partial q_k)]$, with $\{q_k,p_l\}=\delta_{kl}$.
- **Time evolution.** $df/dt=\{f,\energy{H}\}+\partial f/\partial t$.
- **Liouville's theorem.** Hamiltonian flow preserves phase-space volume.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\energy{H},\energy{L}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Hamiltonian and Lagrangian | The functions related by the Legendre transformation. |
| $q_k,p_k,\dot q_k,\dot p_k$ | — | Canonical variables and rates | The coordinates, momenta, and their time derivatives. |
| $t$ | $\unit{s}$ | Time | The independent variable for the motion. |
