title: "Newtonian Dynamics"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Reference formulas for force, momentum, friction, and motion in a rotating frame.

- **Newton's second law.** $\mechanical{\vec F}=\mechanical{m}\mechanical{\vec a}=d\mechanical{\vec p}/dt$, with $\mechanical{\vec p}=\mechanical{m}\mechanical{\vec v}$ for a particle of constant mass.
- **Action and reaction.** $\mechanical{\vec F}_{B\to A}=-\mechanical{\vec F}_{A\to B}$.
- **Static friction.** $\lvert\mechanical{\vec f}_s\rvert\le\mechanical{\mu}_s\mechanical{N}$.
- **Kinetic friction.** $\lvert\mechanical{\vec f}_d\rvert=\mechanical{\mu}_d\mechanical{N}$.
- **Rotating frame with constant angular velocity and common inertial origin.** $\mechanical{m}\mechanical{\vec a}'=\mechanical{\vec F}-2\mechanical{m}\mechanical{\vec\omega}\times\mechanical{\vec v}'-\mechanical{m}\mechanical{\vec\omega}\times(\mechanical{\vec\omega}\times\mechanical{\vec r}')$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{\vec F},\mechanical{\vec f}_s,\mechanical{\vec f}_d,\mechanical{N}$ | $\unit{N}$ | Forces | The net applied force, static and kinetic friction, and normal force magnitude. |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | The constant mass of the particle. |
| $\mechanical{\vec p}$ | $\unit{kg\,m\,s^{-1}}$ | Momentum | The product of mass and velocity. |
| $\mechanical{\vec a},\mechanical{\vec a}'$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-2}}$ | Accelerations | The inertial and rotating-frame accelerations. |
| $\mechanical{\vec v},\mechanical{\vec v}'$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Velocities | The inertial and rotating-frame velocities. |
| $\mechanical{\vec r}'$ | $\unit{m}$ | Position | The rotating-frame position vector. |
| $\mechanical{\vec\omega}$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Angular velocity | The constant rotation rate of the frame. |
| $\mechanical{\mu}_s,\mechanical{\mu}_d$ | $1$ | Friction coefficients | The static and kinetic friction coefficients. |
