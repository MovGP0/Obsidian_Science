> [!TLDR]
> Reference formulas for the center of mass, momentum, collisions, and rockets.

- **Center of mass.** $\mechanical{\vec R}=(1/\mechanical{M})\sum_i\mechanical{m}_i\mechanical{\vec r}_i$.
- **Total momentum and external force.** $\mechanical{\vec P}=\mechanical{M}\mechanical{\vec V}=\sum_i\mechanical{m}_i\mechanical{\vec v}_i$, $\mechanical{M}\mechanical{\ddot{\vec R}}=\mechanical{\vec F}_{\mathrm{ext}}$.
- **One-dimensional elastic collision.** $\mechanical{v}'_1=[(\mechanical{m}_1-\mechanical{m}_2)\mechanical{v}_1+2\mechanical{m}_2\mechanical{v}_2]/(\mechanical{m}_1+\mechanical{m}_2)$.
- **Perfectly inelastic collision.** $\mechanical{v}'=(\mechanical{m}_1\mechanical{v}_1+\mechanical{m}_2\mechanical{v}_2)/(\mechanical{m}_1+\mechanical{m}_2)$.
- **Reduced mass.** $\mechanical{\mu}=\mechanical{m}_1\mechanical{m}_2/(\mechanical{m}_1+\mechanical{m}_2)$.
- **Rocket equation.** $\Delta\mechanical{v}=\mechanical{u}\ln(\mechanical{m}_0/\mechanical{m}_f)$ for constant exhaust speed without external forces.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{M},\mechanical{m}_i,\mechanical{\mu},\mechanical{m}_0,\mechanical{m}_f$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Masses | The total, component, reduced, and initial and final rocket masses. |
| $\mechanical{\vec R},\mechanical{\vec r}_i$ | $\unit{m}$ | Position | The center-of-mass and individual position vectors. |
| $\mechanical{\vec P}$ | $\unit{kg\,m\,s^{-1}}$ | Total momentum | The sum of the momenta of the particles. |
| $\mechanical{\vec V},\mechanical{\vec v}_i,\mechanical{v}_i,\mechanical{v}'_1,\mechanical{v}',\mechanical{u},\Delta\mechanical{v}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Velocities and speed change | The center-of-mass, collision, and rocket velocities. |
| $\mechanical{\vec F}_{\mathrm{ext}}$ | $\unit{N}$ | Net external force | The force that accelerates the center of mass. |
