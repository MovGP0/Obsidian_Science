> [!TLDR]
> Reference equations for the Lagrangian, the action, and Noether conservation laws.

- **Lagrangian.** $\energy{L}(q,\dot q,t)=\energy{T}-\energy{U}$.
- **Conjugate momentum.** $p_k=\partial\energy{L}/\partial\dot q_k$.
- **Euler–Lagrange equation.** $d(\partial\energy{L}/\partial\dot q_k)/dt=\partial\energy{L}/\partial q_k$.
- **Cyclic coordinate.** If $\partial\energy{L}/\partial q_k=0$, then $p_k$ is constant.
- **Hamilton's principle.** $\energy{S}=\int\energy{L}\,dt$ with $\delta\energy{S}=0$ for fixed endpoints.
- **Noether's theorem.** A continuous variational symmetry gives a conserved quantity.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\energy{L},\energy{T},\energy{U}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Lagrangian and energies | The Lagrangian, kinetic energy, and potential energy. |
| $\energy{S}$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Action | The time integral of the Lagrangian. |
| $q_k,\dot q_k,p_k$ | — | Generalized variables | The coordinates, velocities, and conjugate momenta. |
| $t$ | $\unit{s}$ | Time | The independent variable for the motion. |
