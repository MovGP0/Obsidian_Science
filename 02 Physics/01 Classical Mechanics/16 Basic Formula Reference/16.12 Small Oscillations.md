title: "Small Oscillations"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Reference equations for linearized mechanics, normal modes, and oscillator chains.

- **Quadratic Lagrangian.** $\energy{L}=\tfrac12\dot{\vec\eta}^{\,T}M\dot{\vec\eta}-\tfrac12\vec\eta^{\,T}V\vec\eta$.
- **Mode equation.** $V\vec u_\alpha=\signal{\omega}_\alpha^2M\vec u_\alpha$.
- **General oscillation.** $\vec\eta(t)=\sum_\alpha\vec u_\alpha[A_\alpha\cos(\signal{\omega}_\alpha t)+B_\alpha\sin(\signal{\omega}_\alpha t)]$ for positive frequencies.
- **Periodic linear chain.** $\signal{\omega}(\signal{q})=2\sqrt{\mechanical{k}/\mechanical{m}}\lvert\sin(\signal{q}\mechanical{a}/2)\rvert$, with $\signal{q}=2\pi j/(N\mechanical{a})$.
- **Long-wavelength sound speed.** $\mechanical{c}_s=\mechanical{a}\sqrt{\mechanical{k}/\mechanical{m}}$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\energy{L}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Quadratic Lagrangian | The linearized kinetic term minus the potential term. |
| $\vec\eta,\vec u_\alpha,A_\alpha,B_\alpha$ | — | Modal quantities | The deviation, eigenvectors, and modal amplitudes with units set by normalization. |
| $M,V$ | — | Mass and stiffness matrices | The quadratic kinetic and potential coefficients. |
| $\signal{\omega},\signal{\omega}_\alpha$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Angular frequencies | The chain frequency and the normal-mode frequencies. |
| $\signal{q}$ | $\unit{m^{-1}}$ | Wavenumber | The spatial phase rate of a lattice wave. |
| $\mechanical{a}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Lattice spacing | The distance between neighboring sites. |
| $\mechanical{k}$ | $\unit{N\,m^{-1}}$ | Spring stiffness | The stiffness between neighboring masses. |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | The mass at each site. |
| $\mechanical{c}_s$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Sound speed | The long-wavelength propagation speed. |
| $t$ | $\unit{s}$ | Time | The independent variable for the motion. |
