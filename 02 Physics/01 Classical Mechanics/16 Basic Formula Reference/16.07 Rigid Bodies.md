> [!TLDR]
> Reference formulas for rigid-body velocity, moment of inertia, torque, and precession.

- **Velocity field.** $\mechanical{\vec v}_P=\mechanical{\vec v}_O+\mechanical{\vec\omega}\times(\mechanical{\vec r}_P-\mechanical{\vec r}_O)$.
- **Inertia and rotational energy.** $\mechanical{I}=\int\mechanical{\rho}\mechanical{d}_\perp^2\,d\mechanical{V}$, $\energy{K}_{\mathrm{rot}}=\tfrac12\mechanical{I}\mechanical{\omega}^2$ for rotation about a fixed axis.
- **Parallel-axis theorem.** $\mechanical{I}=\mechanical{I}_G+\mechanical{M}\mechanical{d}^2$.
- **Fixed-axis torque.** $\mechanical{\tau}=\mechanical{I}\ddot\theta$.
- **Standard moments of inertia.** $\mechanical{I}_{\mathrm{disk}}=\mechanical{M}\mechanical{R}^2/2$ about the disk axis, $\mechanical{I}_{\mathrm{sphere}}=(2/5)\mechanical{M}\mechanical{R}^2$ about a diameter, and $\mechanical{I}_{\mathrm{rod}}=\mechanical{M}\mechanical{L}^2/12$ about a transverse center axis.
- **Slow precession of a fast top.** $\mechanical{\Omega}_{\mathrm{prec}}\simeq\mechanical{M}\mechanical{g}\mechanical{d}/(\mechanical{I}_3\mechanical{\omega}_s)$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{\vec v}_P,\mechanical{\vec v}_O$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Velocities | The velocities of the two body points. |
| $\mechanical{\vec r}_P,\mechanical{\vec r}_O,\mechanical{d},\mechanical{d}_\perp,\mechanical{R},\mechanical{L}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Positions and lengths | Body-point positions, offsets, radius, and rod length. |
| $\mechanical{\vec\omega},\mechanical{\omega},\mechanical{\omega}_s,\mechanical{\Omega}_{\mathrm{prec}}$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Angular velocities | The rotation, spin, and precession rates. |
| $\mechanical{I},\mechanical{I}_G,\mechanical{I}_3$ | $\unit{kg\,m^2}$ | Moments of inertia | The axis, center-of-mass, and spin-axis moments. |
| $\mechanical{\rho}$ | $\unit{kg\,m^{-3}}$ | Mass density | The density in the inertia integral. |
| $\mechanical{V}$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Volume | The volume integrated over the body. |
| $\energy{K}_{\mathrm{rot}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Rotational kinetic energy | The energy of fixed-axis rotation. |
| $\mechanical{M}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | The total body mass. |
| $\mechanical{\tau}$ | $\unit{N\,m}$ | Torque | The component about the fixed axis. |
| $\theta$ | $\unit{rad}$ | Rotation angle | The angular displacement about the fixed axis. |
| $\mechanical{g}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-2}}$ | Gravitational acceleration | The local field magnitude. |
