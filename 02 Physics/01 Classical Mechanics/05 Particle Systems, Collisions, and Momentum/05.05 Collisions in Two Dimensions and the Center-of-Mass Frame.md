---
title: "Collisions in Two Dimensions and the Center-of-Mass Frame"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Two particles have equal and opposite momenta in the center-of-mass frame. An elastic collision preserves their relative speed.

For a two-dimensional collision, introduce the **relative velocity** $\mechanical{\vec v}_{\mathrm{rel}}=\mechanical{\vec v}_1-\mechanical{\vec v}_2$. In the center-of-mass frame, the momenta are $\pm\mechanical{\mu}\mechanical{\vec v}_{\mathrm{rel}}$, where

$$
\mechanical{\mu}=\frac{\mechanical{m}_1\mechanical{m}_2}{\mechanical{m}_1+\mechanical{m}_2}
$$

is the **reduced mass**.

An elastic collision conserves the magnitude of the relative velocity. The *scattering angle* $\mechanical{\theta}^{*}$ specifies its change of direction.

**Galilean transformation.** Add the center-of-mass velocity $\mechanical{\vec V}$ to find the laboratory velocities.

$$
\mechanical{\vec v}_i^{\mathrm{lab}}=\mechanical{\vec V}+\mechanical{\vec v}_i^{\mathrm{CM}}.
$$

For a stationary target, $\mechanical{\vec v}_2=\vec 0$, so $\mechanical{\vec V}=[\mechanical{m}_1/(\mechanical{m}_1+\mechanical{m}_2)]\mechanical{\vec v}_1$.

### Example — Elastic Scattering of Equal Masses

If $\mechanical{m}_1=\mechanical{m}_2$ and the target is initially at rest, the two final laboratory velocities are perpendicular whenever both are nonzero. This follows from momentum and kinetic energy conservation, which give $\mechanical{\vec v}'_1\cdot\mechanical{\vec v}'_2=0$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{m}_1,\mechanical{m}_2$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | The masses of the two particles. |
| $\mechanical{\mu}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Reduced mass | The effective mass of the relative motion. |
| $\mechanical{\vec v}_1,\mechanical{\vec v}_2,\mechanical{\vec v}'_1,\mechanical{\vec v}'_2,\mechanical{\vec v}_i^{\mathrm{lab}},\mechanical{\vec v}_i^{\mathrm{CM}}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Particle velocity | The initial or final velocity of a particle in the stated frame. |
| $\mechanical{\vec v}_{\mathrm{rel}}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Relative velocity | The velocity of the first particle relative to the second. |
| $\mechanical{\vec V}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Center-of-mass velocity | The velocity of the center of mass in the laboratory frame. |
| $\pm\mechanical{\mu}\mechanical{\vec v}_{\mathrm{rel}}$ | $\unit{kg\,m\,s^{-1}}$ | Center-of-mass momenta | The equal and opposite momenta of the two particles in their center-of-mass frame. |
| $\mechanical{\theta}^{*}$ | $\unit{rad}$ | Scattering angle | The change in direction of the relative velocity. |
