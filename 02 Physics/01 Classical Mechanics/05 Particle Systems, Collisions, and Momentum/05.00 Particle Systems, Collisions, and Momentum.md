title: "Particle Systems, Collisions, and Momentum"

> [!TLDR]
> The center of mass, total momentum, and total angular momentum describe the collective motion of a particle system.

Earlier chapters examined the motion of a single point particle. In a system of several particles, new conserved quantities and laws describe the collective behavior. The center of mass, total momentum, and total angular momentum describe the system as a whole. This chapter applies them to collisions and introduces systems with variable mass.

## Chapter Summary

- The [[05.02 Center of Mass of a System|center of mass]] $\mechanical{\vec R}=(1/\mechanical{M})\sum_i\mechanical{m}_i\mechanical{\vec r}_i$ moves like a particle of mass $\mechanical{M}$ under net external force $\mechanical{\vec F}_{\mathrm{ext}}$.
- The [[05.03 Total Momentum|total momentum]] $\mechanical{\vec P}=\mechanical{M}\mechanical{\vec V}$ is conserved when the net external force is zero.
- In an [[05.04 Elastic and Inelastic Collisions in One Dimension|elastic one-dimensional collision]], the final velocities follow from conservation of momentum and kinetic energy. The limits include velocity exchange, rebound, and acceleration of a light target.
- In a perfectly inelastic collision the bodies join with $\mechanical{v}'=(\mechanical{m}_1\mechanical{v}_1+\mechanical{m}_2\mechanical{v}_2)/(\mechanical{m}_1+\mechanical{m}_2)$. The kinetic energy loss is $\tfrac12\mechanical{\mu}(\mechanical{v}_1-\mechanical{v}_2)^2$.
- The [[05.05 Collisions in Two Dimensions and the Center-of-Mass Frame|reduced mass]] $\mechanical{\mu}=\mechanical{m}_1\mechanical{m}_2/(\mechanical{m}_1+\mechanical{m}_2)$ describes the relative motion.
- The [[05.07 Angular Momentum|angular momentum]] of a particle is $\mechanical{\vec L}=\mechanical{\vec r}\times\mechanical{\vec p}$. It is conserved about the center of a central force, and the total is conserved for an isolated system.
- The [[05.08 Variable-Mass Systems and the Rocket Equation|rocket equation]] gives $\Delta\mechanical{v}=\mechanical{u}\ln(\mechanical{m}_0/\mechanical{m}_f)$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{\vec R},\mechanical{\vec r},\mechanical{\vec r}_i$ | $\unit{m}$ | Position | The center-of-mass or particle position. |
| $\mechanical{M},\mechanical{m}_i,\mechanical{m}_1,\mechanical{m}_2$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | The total mass and the masses of individual particles. |
| $\mechanical{\vec F}_{\mathrm{ext}}$ | $\unit{N}$ | Net external force | The force from outside the system. |
| $\mechanical{\vec P},\mechanical{\vec p}$ | $\unit{kg\,m\,s^{-1}}$ | Linear momentum | The momentum of the system or a particle. |
| $\mechanical{\vec V}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Center-of-mass velocity | The rate of change of the center-of-mass position. |
| $\mechanical{v}_1,\mechanical{v}_2,\mechanical{v}'$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Collision velocity | The initial velocities and the common final velocity for a perfectly inelastic collision. |
| $\mechanical{\mu}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Reduced mass | The effective mass of the relative motion. |
| $\mechanical{\vec L}$ | $\unit{kg\,m^2\,s^{-1}}$ | Angular momentum | The moment of linear momentum about the reference point. |
| $\Delta\mechanical{v},\mechanical{u}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Rocket speed gain and exhaust speed | The ideal speed increase and the relative exhaust speed. |
| $\mechanical{m}_0,\mechanical{m}_f$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Rocket mass | The initial and final masses of the rocket. |
