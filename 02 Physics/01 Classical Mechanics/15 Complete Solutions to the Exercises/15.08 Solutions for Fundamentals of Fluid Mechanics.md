> [!TLDR]
> Worked solutions apply hydrostatics, continuity, Bernoulli's equation, and viscous flow.

See [[08.14 Fluid Mechanics Exercises]] for the exercise list.

## Exercise 1. Pressure below the sea surface

*Use depth $\mechanical{h}=\quantity{100}{m}$ and seawater density $\mechanical{\rho}=\quantity{1025}{kg\,m^{-3}}$.*

The gauge pressure is $\mechanical{p}=\mechanical{\rho}\mechanical{g}\mechanical{h}\simeq\quantity{1.005\times10^6}{Pa}$, about $9.92$ atmospheres.

## Exercise 2. Buoyancy on aluminum

*An aluminum body of density $\mechanical{\rho}_{\mathrm{Al}}=\quantity{2700}{kg\,m^{-3}}$ and volume $\mechanical{V}=\quantity{10^{-3}}{m^3}$ is submerged in water.*

The buoyant force is $\mechanical{F}_A=\quantity{9.81}{N}$. The weight is $\mechanical{W}=\quantity{26.49}{N}$. The apparent weight is $\mechanical{W}-\mechanical{F}_A=\quantity{16.68}{N}$.

## Exercise 3. Venturi tube

*The areas are $\mechanical{A}_1=\quantity{4}{cm^2}$ and $\mechanical{A}_2=\quantity{1}{cm^2}$. The upstream speed is $\mechanical{v}_1=\quantity{0.5}{m\,s^{-1}}$.*

Continuity gives $\mechanical{v}_2=\mechanical{v}_1\mechanical{A}_1/\mechanical{A}_2=\quantity{2}{m\,s^{-1}}$. For water at equal elevation, Bernoulli's equation gives $\Delta\mechanical{p}=\tfrac12\mechanical{\rho}(\mechanical{v}_2^2-\mechanical{v}_1^2)=\quantity{1875}{Pa}$.

## Exercise 4. Torricelli's law

*The opening is $\mechanical{h}=\quantity{0.5}{m}$ below the free surface.*

The exit speed is $\mechanical{v}=\sqrt{2\mechanical{g}\mechanical{h}}=\sqrt{9.81}\simeq\quantity{3.13}{m\,s^{-1}}$.

## Exercise 5. Floating wood

*Wood of density $\mechanical{\rho}_l=\quantity{700}{kg\,m^{-3}}$ floats in water.*

The submerged fraction is $700/1000=70\%$. Thus $30\%$ of the volume lies above the water.

## Exercise 6. Isothermal atmosphere

*At $\thermal{T}=\quantity{273}{K}$, find the scale height and the pressure ratio at an altitude of $\quantity{5}{km}$.*

For molecular mass $\mechanical{m}$, the scale height is $\mechanical{H}=k_B\thermal{T}/(\mechanical{m}\mechanical{g})\simeq\quantity{7990}{m}$. Thus $\mechanical{p}(\quantity{5}{km})/\mechanical{p}_0=e^{-5/8}\simeq0.535$.

## Exercise 7. Poiseuille flow

*Use tube radius $\mechanical{R}=\quantity{5}{mm}$, length $\mechanical{L}=\quantity{1}{m}$, pressure drop $\Delta\mechanical{p}=\quantity{100}{Pa}$, and viscosity $\mechanical{\eta}=\quantity{10^{-3}}{Pa\,s}$.*

The volumetric flow is $\mechanical{Q}=\pi\mechanical{R}^4\Delta\mechanical{p}/(8\mechanical{\eta}\mechanical{L})\simeq\quantity{2.45\times10^{-5}}{m^3\,s^{-1}}$.

## Exercise 8. Bernoulli's equation from Newton's law

*Derive Bernoulli's equation along a streamline.*

For a fluid element of length $ds$ and cross section $dA$, the force along the streamline is $-(d\mechanical{p}/ds)dA\,ds-\mechanical{\rho}\mechanical{g}(d\mechanical{z}/ds)dA\,ds$. By Newton's law this equals $\mechanical{\rho}\,dA\,ds\,\mechanical{a}$. In steady flow, $\mechanical{a}=\tfrac12d(\mechanical{v}^2)/ds$. Divide by the volume and integrate along the streamline to get Bernoulli's equation.

## Exercise 9. Bernoulli's equation for irrotational flow

*Show that Bernoulli's relation applies across streamlines when $\nabla\times\mechanical{\vec v}=0$.*

For ideal incompressible irrotational flow, write $\mechanical{\vec v}=\nabla\Phi$ where a velocity potential exists. Euler's equation gives

$$
\nabla\left(\frac{\partial\Phi}{\partial t}+\frac{\mechanical{v}^2}{2}+\frac{\mechanical{p}}{\mechanical{\rho}}+\mechanical{g}\mechanical{z}\right)=0.
$$

The quantity in parentheses is spatially constant in the connected fluid. In steady flow the Bernoulli constant is therefore common to different streamlines.

## Exercise 10. Sound speed in helium

*Use heat capacity ratio $\gamma=5/3$, temperature $\thermal{T}=\quantity{300}{K}$, and atomic mass $\mechanical{m}=\quantity{6.64\times10^{-27}}{kg}$.*

The speed is $\mechanical{c}_s=\sqrt{\gamma k_B\thermal{T}/\mechanical{m}}\simeq\quantity{1019}{m\,s^{-1}}$.

## Exercise 11. Reynolds number for a car

*Calculate the Reynolds number using air density $1.2$, speed $30$, length $4$, and dynamic viscosity $1.8\times10^{-5}$ in SI units.*

The Reynolds number is $\mathrm{Re}=\mechanical{\rho}\mechanical{v}\mechanical{L}/\mechanical{\eta}=1.2\cdot30\cdot4/(1.8\times10^{-5})=8\times10^6$. This is a high-Reynolds-number flow, for which turbulence is expected.

## Exercise 12. Tsunami

*Estimate the wave speed in water of depth $\mechanical{h}=\quantity{4}{km}$.*

The shallow-water speed is $\mechanical{v}=\sqrt{\mechanical{g}\mechanical{h}}=\sqrt{9.81\cdot4000}\simeq\quantity{198}{m\,s^{-1}}\simeq\quantity{713}{km\,h^{-1}}$.

## Exercise 13. Draining cylindrical tank

*Find the emptying time.*

Apply Torricelli's law and continuity to a tank of cross section $\mechanical{A}$, opening area $\mechanical{A}_f$, and initial water height $\mechanical{H}$. Integrating $\mechanical{A}\,d\mechanical{h}/dt=-\mechanical{A}_f\sqrt{2\mechanical{g}\mechanical{h}}$ gives $t=(\mechanical{A}/\mechanical{A}_f)\sqrt{2\mechanical{H}/\mechanical{g}}$.
