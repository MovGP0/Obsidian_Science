---
title: "Lagrangian for Systems of Multiple Particles"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Pair potentials that depend only on separation give translation and rotation symmetry. For two bodies, the Lagrangian separates into center-of-mass and relative motion.

For $N$ particles interacting through pair potentials $\energy{U}_{ij}(\lVert\mechanical{\vec r}_i-\mechanical{\vec r}_j\rVert)$,

$$
\energy{L}=\sum_i\tfrac12\mechanical{m}_i\lVert\mechanical{\vec v}_i\rVert^2
-\sum_{i<j}\energy{U}_{ij}(\lVert\mechanical{\vec r}_i-\mechanical{\vec r}_j\rVert).
$$

Invariance under translation, rotation, and time translation gives conservation of $\mechanical{\vec P}$, $\mechanical{\vec L}$, and $\energy{E}$ through [[11.07 Noether's Theorem]].

**Center-of-mass and relative coordinates.** For two particles, introduce $\mechanical{\vec R}=(\mechanical{m}_1\mechanical{\vec r}_1+\mechanical{m}_2\mechanical{\vec r}_2)/\mechanical{M}$ and $\mechanical{\vec r}=\mechanical{\vec r}_1-\mechanical{\vec r}_2$. Then

$$
\energy{L}=\tfrac12\mechanical{M}\lVert\mechanical{\dot{\vec R}}\rVert^2
+\tfrac12\mechanical{\mu}\lVert\mechanical{\dot{\vec r}}\rVert^2-\energy{U}(\lVert\mechanical{\vec r}\rVert),
$$

with $\mechanical{M}=\mechanical{m}_1+\mechanical{m}_2$ and $\mechanical{\mu}=\mechanical{m}_1\mechanical{m}_2/\mechanical{M}$. The center of mass moves at constant velocity. The relative motion is that of one particle of mass $\mechanical{\mu}$ in a central potential.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\energy{L},\energy{E}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Lagrangian and energy | The dynamical function and the conserved total energy. |
| $\energy{U}_{ij},\energy{U}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Pair potential energy | The interaction energy of a particle pair. |
| $\mechanical{m}_i,\mechanical{m}_1,\mechanical{m}_2,\mechanical{M},\mechanical{\mu}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | The individual, total, and reduced masses. |
| $\mechanical{\vec r}_i,\mechanical{\vec r},\mechanical{\vec R}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Position | The particle, relative, or center-of-mass position. |
| $\mechanical{\vec v}_i,\mechanical{\dot{\vec r}},\mechanical{\dot{\vec R}}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Velocity | The particle, relative, or center-of-mass velocity. |
| $\mechanical{\vec P}$ | $\unit{kg\,m\,s^{-1}}$ | Total momentum | The sum of individual linear momenta. |
| $\mechanical{\vec L}$ | $\unit{kg\,m^2\,s^{-1}}$ | Total angular momentum | The sum of individual angular momenta. |
