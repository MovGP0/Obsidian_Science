---
title: "Lagrangian of a Rigid Body"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Use center-of-mass coordinates and orientation angles to write the Lagrangian of a rigid body.

A rigid body in gravity has six generalized coordinates. Three specify the center of mass, and three specify orientation. The usual orientation coordinates are Euler angles $(\mechanical{\phi},\mechanical{\theta},\mechanical{\psi})$.

**Kinetic energy.** König's decomposition gives $\energy{T}=\tfrac12\mechanical{M}\mechanical{V}^2+\energy{T}_{\mathrm{rot}}$, with $\energy{T}_{\mathrm{rot}}=\tfrac12\sum_i\mechanical{I}_i\mechanical{\omega}_i^2$ in principal axes.

**Angular velocity in Euler angles.** Use $\mechanical{\phi}$ for rotation about the laboratory $\hat z$, $\mechanical{\theta}$ for nutation about the new $\hat x$, and $\mechanical{\psi}$ for spin about the body-fixed $\hat z$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mechanical{\omega}_1&=\mechanical{\dot\phi}\sin\mechanical{\theta}\sin\mechanical{\psi}+
\mechanical{\dot\theta}\cos\mechanical{\psi},\\
\mechanical{\omega}_2&=\mechanical{\dot\phi}\sin\mechanical{\theta}\cos\mechanical{\psi}-
\mechanical{\dot\theta}\sin\mechanical{\psi},\\
\mechanical{\omega}_3&=\mechanical{\dot\phi}\cos\mechanical{\theta}+\mechanical{\dot\psi}.
\end{aligned}
$$

**Symmetric top.** With $\mechanical{I}_1=\mechanical{I}_2$,

$$
\energy{T}_{\mathrm{rot}}=\tfrac12\mechanical{I}_1
(\mechanical{\dot\phi}^{\,2}\sin^2\mechanical{\theta}+\mechanical{\dot\theta}^{\,2})
+\tfrac12\mechanical{I}_3(\mechanical{\dot\psi}+\mechanical{\dot\phi}\cos\mechanical{\theta})^2.
$$

For the top about a fixed point in vertical gravity, the rotational inertia is taken about that point. With center of mass a distance $\mechanical{d}$ along the symmetry axis, $\energy{L}=\energy{T}_{\mathrm{rot}}-\mechanical{M}\mechanical{g}\mechanical{d}\cos\mechanical{\theta}$. Angles $\mechanical{\phi}$ and $\mechanical{\psi}$ are cyclic. Their conserved conjugate momenta $\mechanical{p}_\phi,\mechanical{p}_\psi$ reduce the problem to an equation in $\mechanical{\theta}$ alone.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{\phi},\mechanical{\theta},\mechanical{\psi}$ | $\unit{rad}$ | Euler angles | The orientation coordinates. |
| $\mechanical{\dot\phi},\mechanical{\dot\theta},\mechanical{\dot\psi},\mechanical{\omega}_i$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Angular rates | Euler angle rates and angular velocity components. |
| $\mechanical{M}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | The total mass of the rigid body. |
| $\mechanical{V}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Center-of-mass speed | The magnitude of the center-of-mass velocity. |
| $\mechanical{I}_i$ | $\unit{kg\,m^2}$ | Principal moments | The moments about the principal axes. |
| $\energy{T},\energy{T}_{\mathrm{rot}},\energy{L}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Energies and Lagrangian | The total kinetic energy, rotational kinetic energy, and Lagrangian. |
| $\mechanical{g}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-2}}$ | Acceleration due to gravity | The magnitude of downward gravitational acceleration. |
| $\mechanical{d}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Pivot distance | The distance from the fixed point to the center of mass. |
| $\mechanical{p}_\phi,\mechanical{p}_\psi$ | $\unit{kg\,m^2\,s^{-1}}$ | Conjugate momenta | The conserved momenta associated with the two cyclic angles. |
