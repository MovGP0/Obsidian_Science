---
title: "Angular Momentum of a Rigid Body"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The angular momentum of a rigid body is the sum of its angular momentum about the center of mass and the contribution from center-of-mass motion.

For a rigid body in general motion, the total angular momentum about $O$ is

$$
\mechanical{\vec L}_O=\mechanical{\vec L}_G+\mechanical{M}\mechanical{\vec R}\times\mechanical{\vec V}_G.
$$

Here $\mechanical{\vec L}_G$ is the angular momentum about the center of mass, $\mechanical{\vec R}$ is the position of the center of mass relative to $O$, and $\mechanical{\vec V}_G$ is its velocity. This decomposition is analogous to the decomposition of kinetic energy.

**Angular momentum equation.** About a fixed point in an inertial frame, or about the center of mass even when it moves,

$$
\frac{d\mechanical{\vec L}}{dt}=\mechanical{\vec\tau}_{\mathrm{ext}}.
$$

The three components supplement Newton's second law $\mechanical{M}\mechanical{\dot{\vec V}}_G=\mechanical{\vec F}_{\mathrm{ext}}$, which also has three components. Together there are six equations for the six degrees of freedom: three translational and three rotational.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{\vec L},\mechanical{\vec L}_O,\mechanical{\vec L}_G$ | $\unit{kg\,m^2\,s^{-1}}$ | Angular momentum | The angular momentum about the reference point or the center of mass. |
| $\mechanical{M}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Total mass | The mass of the rigid body. |
| $\mechanical{\vec R}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Center-of-mass position | The displacement from $O$ to the center of mass. |
| $\mechanical{\vec V}_G$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Center-of-mass velocity | The velocity of the center of mass in the inertial frame. |
| $\mechanical{\dot{\vec V}}_G$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-2}}$ | Center-of-mass acceleration | The rate of change of center-of-mass velocity. |
| $\mechanical{\vec\tau}_{\mathrm{ext}}$ | $\unit{N\,m}$ | Net external torque | The sum of the external torques about the stated point. |
| $\mechanical{\vec F}_{\mathrm{ext}}$ | $\unit{N}$ | Net external force | The sum of the forces from outside the rigid body. |
| $t$ | $\unit{s}$ | Time | The independent variable for the motion. |
