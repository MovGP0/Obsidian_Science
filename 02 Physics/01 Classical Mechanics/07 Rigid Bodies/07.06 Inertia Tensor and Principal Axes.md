---
title: "Inertia Tensor and Principal Axes"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The inertia tensor maps angular velocity to angular momentum. It is diagonal in a basis of principal axes.

For a rigid body in three dimensions, the total angular momentum about the center of mass is

$$
\mechanical{\vec L}=\int\mechanical{\rho}\mechanical{\vec r}\times(\mechanical{\vec\omega}\times\mechanical{\vec r})\,dV
=\mechanical{\mathbf I}\mechanical{\vec\omega}.
$$

The **inertia tensor** $\mechanical{\mathbf I}$ is a symmetric $3\times3$ matrix with entries

$$
\mechanical{I}_{ij}=\int\mechanical{\rho}(\mechanical{r}^2\delta_{ij}-\mechanical{r}_i\mechanical{r}_j)\,dV.
$$

**Principal axes.** Since $\mechanical{\mathbf I}$ is real and symmetric, it is diagonalizable. There is an orthonormal basis $\{\hat e_1,\hat e_2,\hat e_3\}$ such that $\mechanical{\mathbf I}=\operatorname{diag}(\mechanical{I}_1,\mechanical{I}_2,\mechanical{I}_3)$. The diagonal entries are the **principal moments of inertia**. Symmetry can determine principal axes. The symmetry axis of a cylinder is a principal axis. For a homogeneous sphere, any orthonormal basis is a principal-axis basis.

**Kinetic energy in principal axes.**

$$
\energy{K}_{\mathrm{rot}}=\tfrac12\sum_i\mechanical{I}_i\mechanical{\omega}_i^2.
$$

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{\vec L}$ | $\unit{kg\,m^2\,s^{-1}}$ | Angular momentum | The total angular momentum about the center of mass. |
| $\mechanical{\vec\omega}$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Angular velocity | The rate and axis of the rigid-body rotation. |
| $\mechanical{\rho}$ | $\unit{kg\,m^{-3}}$ | Mass density | The mass per unit volume. |
| $\mechanical{\vec r},\mechanical{r},\mechanical{r}_i$ | $\unit{m}$ | Position | The position relative to the center of mass, its magnitude, and its components. |
| $\mechanical{\mathbf I},\mechanical{I}_{ij}$ | $\unit{kg\,m^2}$ | Inertia tensor | The linear map from angular velocity to angular momentum and its components. |
| $\mechanical{I}_1,\mechanical{I}_2,\mechanical{I}_3$ | $\unit{kg\,m^2}$ | Principal moments of inertia | The eigenvalues of the inertia tensor. |
| $\mechanical{\omega}_i$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Angular velocity component | The component along principal axis $i$. |
| $\energy{K}_{\mathrm{rot}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Rotational kinetic energy | The kinetic energy associated with rotation. |
