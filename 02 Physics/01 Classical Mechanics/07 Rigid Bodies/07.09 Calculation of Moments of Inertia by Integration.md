---
title: "Calculation of Moments of Inertia by Integration"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Integrate the squared distance from the rotation axis over the mass distribution to find its moment of inertia.

## Uniform Solid Cone

A cone of mass $\mechanical{M}$, base radius $\mechanical{R}$, and height $\mechanical{H}$ has its symmetry axis along $\hat z$. Its density is $\mechanical{\rho}=\mechanical{M}/(\pi\mechanical{R}^2\mechanical{H}/3)$. The cross-section at height $\mechanical{z}$ has radius $\mechanical{r}(\mechanical{z})=\mechanical{R}(1-\mechanical{z}/\mechanical{H})$. Treat each cross-section as a thin disk.

$$
\mechanical{I}_z
=\int_0^H\tfrac12(\mechanical{\rho}\pi\mechanical{r}^2(\mechanical{z})\,dz)\mechanical{r}^2(\mechanical{z})
=\tfrac12\mechanical{\rho}\pi\int_0^H\mechanical{r}^4(\mechanical{z})\,dz
=\tfrac3{10}\mechanical{M}\mechanical{R}^2.
$$

## Solid Torus

For a torus, let $\mechanical{R}$ be the distance from the symmetry axis to the center of the circular cross-section, and let $\mechanical{r}$ be the cross-section radius. The moment about the symmetry axis is $\mechanical{I}_z=\mechanical{M}(\mechanical{R}^2+\tfrac34\mechanical{r}^2)$. As $\mechanical{r}\to0$, this approaches the thin ring result $\mechanical{M}\mechanical{R}^2$.

## Disk with a Central Hole

A disk of outer radius $\mechanical{R}$ has a central circular hole of radius $\mechanical{r}$. Its moment about the perpendicular axis through the center is $\mechanical{I}=\tfrac12\mechanical{M}(\mechanical{R}^2+\mechanical{r}^2)$, where $\mechanical{M}$ is the mass remaining after the hole is removed. Obtain this result by direct integration or subtract the inertia of the removed disk from that of the full disk.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{M}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | The total mass of the solid. |
| $\mechanical{R}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Main radius | The cone's base radius or the major radius of the torus. |
| $\mechanical{H},\mechanical{z}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Height | The total cone height or the height of a cross-section above its base. |
| $\mechanical{r}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Cross-section radius | The cone slice radius or the minor radius of the torus. |
| $\mechanical{\rho}$ | $\unit{kg\,m^{-3}}$ | Mass density | The uniform mass per unit volume. |
| $\mechanical{I}_z,\mechanical{I}$ | $\unit{kg\,m^2}$ | Moment of inertia | The moment about the symmetry axis. |
