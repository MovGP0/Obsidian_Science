---
title: "Euler's Equations"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Euler's equations describe rigid-body rotation in a frame fixed to the principal axes.

In a body-fixed frame aligned with the principal axes, the rotational equations of motion are

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mechanical{I}_1\mechanical{\dot\omega}_1-(\mechanical{I}_2-\mechanical{I}_3)\mechanical{\omega}_2\mechanical{\omega}_3
&=\mechanical{\tau}_1,\\
\mechanical{I}_2\mechanical{\dot\omega}_2-(\mechanical{I}_3-\mechanical{I}_1)\mechanical{\omega}_3\mechanical{\omega}_1
&=\mechanical{\tau}_2,\\
\mechanical{I}_3\mechanical{\dot\omega}_3-(\mechanical{I}_1-\mechanical{I}_2)\mechanical{\omega}_1\mechanical{\omega}_2
&=\mechanical{\tau}_3.
\end{aligned}
$$

**Torque-free case.** If $\mechanical{\vec\tau}=\vec 0$, the equations describe free rotation. For three distinct principal moments, the body-frame components of $\mechanical{\vec\omega}$ generally change with time. This is *free precession* or *Euler precession*. Rotation about the intermediate principal axis is unstable under small disturbances.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{I}_1,\mechanical{I}_2,\mechanical{I}_3$ | $\unit{kg\,m^2}$ | Principal moments of inertia | The moments about the three principal axes. |
| $\mechanical{\vec\omega},\mechanical{\omega}_1,\mechanical{\omega}_2,\mechanical{\omega}_3$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Angular velocity | The rotation vector and its components in the body-fixed frame. |
| $\mechanical{\dot\omega}_1,\mechanical{\dot\omega}_2,\mechanical{\dot\omega}_3$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-2}}$ | Angular velocity derivatives | The time derivatives of the body-frame components. |
| $\mechanical{\vec\tau},\mechanical{\tau}_1,\mechanical{\tau}_2,\mechanical{\tau}_3$ | $\unit{N\,m}$ | Torque | The net external torque and its body-frame components. |
