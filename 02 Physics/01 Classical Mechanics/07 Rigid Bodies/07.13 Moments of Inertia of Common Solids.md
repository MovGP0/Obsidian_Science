> [!TLDR]
> The moment of inertia depends on both the shape of the body and the chosen axis.

**Table 7.1** Moments of inertia of common solids.

| Body | Axis | $\mechanical{I}$ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Thin rod, length $\mechanical{L}$ | Through center, perpendicular to rod | $\mechanical{M}\mechanical{L}^2/12$ |
| Thin rod, length $\mechanical{L}$ | Through one end, perpendicular to rod | $\mechanical{M}\mechanical{L}^2/3$ |
| Thin ring, radius $\mechanical{R}$ | Through center, perpendicular to plane | $\mechanical{M}\mechanical{R}^2$ |
| Thin ring, radius $\mechanical{R}$ | Along a diameter | $\mechanical{M}\mechanical{R}^2/2$ |
| Uniform disk, radius $\mechanical{R}$ | Through center, perpendicular to plane | $\mechanical{M}\mechanical{R}^2/2$ |
| Uniform disk, radius $\mechanical{R}$ | Along a diameter | $\mechanical{M}\mechanical{R}^2/4$ |
| Solid cylinder, radius $\mechanical{R}$ | Symmetry axis | $\mechanical{M}\mechanical{R}^2/2$ |
| Solid sphere, radius $\mechanical{R}$ | Along a diameter | $2\mechanical{M}\mechanical{R}^2/5$ |
| Thin spherical shell, radius $\mechanical{R}$ | Along a diameter | $2\mechanical{M}\mechanical{R}^2/3$ |
| Rectangular block, $\mechanical{a}\times\mechanical{b}\times\mechanical{c}$ | Through center, parallel to side $\mechanical{c}$ | $\mechanical{M}(\mechanical{a}^2+\mechanical{b}^2)/12$ |

### Example — Minimum Period of a Compound Pendulum

A uniform rod of length $\mechanical{L}$ is suspended at distance $\mechanical{d}$ from its center of mass. The moment of inertia and the small-amplitude period are

$$
\mechanical{I}=\frac{\mechanical{M}\mechanical{L}^2}{12}+\mechanical{M}\mechanical{d}^2,
\qquad
\signal{T}=2\pi\sqrt{\frac{\mechanical{L}^2/12+\mechanical{d}^2}{\mechanical{g}\mechanical{d}}}.
$$

Minimizing over $\mechanical{d}$ gives $\mechanical{d}^*=\mechanical{L}/\sqrt{12}\approx0.289\mechanical{L}$. The minimum period is $\signal{T}_{\min}=2\pi\sqrt{2\mechanical{L}/(\mechanical{g}\sqrt{12})}$. Near this pivot position, small changes in the pivot distance have little effect on the period. The source relates this to precision pendulum clocks.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{I}$ | $\unit{kg\,m^2}$ | Moment of inertia | The rotational inertia about the specified axis. |
| $\mechanical{M}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | The mass of the rigid body. |
| $\mechanical{L},\mechanical{R},\mechanical{a},\mechanical{b},\mechanical{c}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Body dimensions | The rod length, radius, or side lengths of the block. |
| $\mechanical{d},\mechanical{d}^*$ | $\unit{m}$ | Pivot distance | The distance from the center of mass to the pivot and its optimal value. |
| $\signal{T},\signal{T}_{\min}$ | $\unit{s}$ | Period | The small-amplitude period and its minimum over pivot positions. |
| $\mechanical{g}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-2}}$ | Acceleration due to gravity | The magnitude of the downward acceleration in free fall. |
