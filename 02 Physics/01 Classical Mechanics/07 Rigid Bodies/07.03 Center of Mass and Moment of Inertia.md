---
title: "Center of Mass and Moment of Inertia"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The center of mass gives the mass-weighted location of a rigid body. The moment of inertia measures how its mass is distributed about an axis.

**Center of mass.** For a continuous rigid body,

$$
\mechanical{\vec R}=\frac{1}{\mechanical{M}}\int\mechanical{\rho}(\mechanical{\vec r})\mechanical{\vec r}\,dV.
$$

> [!definition] Definition 7.1 — Moment of Inertia
> The **moment of inertia** of a rigid body about an axis is
>
> $$
> \mechanical{I}=\int\mechanical{\rho}(\mechanical{\vec r})\mechanical{d}_{\perp}^{\,2}(\mechanical{\vec r})\,dV,
> $$
>
> where $\mechanical{d}_{\perp}$ is the perpendicular distance from the point to the axis.

Moment of inertia plays the role in rotation that mass plays in translation. It measures resistance to angular acceleration.

**Examples.**

- Thin rod, transverse axis through its center: $\mechanical{I}=\mechanical{M}\mechanical{L}^2/12$.
- Thin rod, transverse axis through one end: $\mechanical{I}=\mechanical{M}\mechanical{L}^2/3$.
- Disk, perpendicular axis through the center: $\mechanical{I}=\mechanical{M}\mechanical{R}^2/2$.
- Solid sphere, axis through the center: $\mechanical{I}=(2/5)\mechanical{M}\mechanical{R}^2$.
- Thin spherical shell, axis through the center: $\mechanical{I}=(2/3)\mechanical{M}\mechanical{R}^2$.
- Solid cylinder, symmetry axis: the same expression as for a disk.

### Example — Moment of Inertia of a Disk

For a uniform disk of radius $\mechanical{R}$ and mass $\mechanical{M}$, the surface mass density is $\mechanical{\sigma}=\mechanical{M}/(\pi\mechanical{R}^2)$. Integrate in polar coordinates.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mechanical{I}
&=\int_0^R\int_0^{2\pi}\mechanical{\sigma}\mechanical{r}^2\mechanical{r}\,dr\,d\phi
=2\pi\mechanical{\sigma}\int_0^R\mechanical{r}^3\,dr\\
&=2\pi\mechanical{\sigma}\frac{\mechanical{R}^4}{4}
=\frac{\mechanical{M}\mechanical{R}^2}{2}.
\end{aligned}
$$

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{\vec R}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Center-of-mass position | The mass-weighted mean position of the rigid body. |
| $\mechanical{M}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Total mass | The mass of the entire rigid body. |
| $\mechanical{\rho}$ | $\unit{kg\,m^{-3}}$ | Mass density | The mass per unit volume at each point. |
| $\mechanical{\vec r}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Position vector | The position of the volume element. |
| $dV$ | $\unit{m^3}$ | Volume element | The infinitesimal volume used in the integral. |
| $\mechanical{I}$ | $\unit{kg\,m^2}$ | Moment of inertia | The second mass moment about the axis. |
| $\mechanical{d}_{\perp}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Axis distance | The perpendicular distance from a mass element to the axis. |
| $\mechanical{L}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Rod length | The distance between the ends of the rod. |
| $\mechanical{R},\mechanical{r}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Radius | The body radius or the polar distance in the disk integral. |
| $\mechanical{\sigma}$ | $\unit{kg\,m^{-2}}$ | Surface mass density | The mass per unit area of the uniform disk. |
| $\phi$ | $\unit{rad}$ | Polar angle | The angular integration coordinate in the disk. |
