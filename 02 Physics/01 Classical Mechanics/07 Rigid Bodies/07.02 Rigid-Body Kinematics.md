---
title: "Rigid-Body Kinematics"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The velocity of any point of a rigid body follows from the velocity of a reference point and the body's angular velocity.

For a point $P$ on a rigid body,

$$
\mechanical{\vec v}_P=\mechanical{\vec v}_O+\mechanical{\vec\omega}\times
(\mechanical{\vec r}_P-\mechanical{\vec r}_O).
$$

Here $O$ is any point on the body and $\mechanical{\vec v}_O$ is its velocity. The angular velocity $\mechanical{\vec\omega}$ is the same for every choice of $O$.

**State of motion.** The pair $(\mechanical{\vec v}_O,\mechanical{\vec\omega})$ determines the instantaneous motion.

- **Pure translation.** $\mechanical{\vec\omega}=\vec 0$, so all points have the same velocity.
- **Pure rotation.** The points on the axis are at rest. Relative to a point on that axis, $\mechanical{\vec v}=\mechanical{\vec\omega}\times\mechanical{\vec r}$.
- **General motion.** The instantaneous motion combines translation and rotation in a screw motion.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{\vec v}_P,\mechanical{\vec v}_O,\mechanical{\vec v}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Point velocity | The velocity of a point fixed to the rigid body. |
| $\mechanical{\vec r}_P,\mechanical{\vec r}_O,\mechanical{\vec r}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Position vector | The position of a point in the chosen reference frame or relative to the rotation axis. |
| $\mechanical{\vec\omega}$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Angular velocity | The rate and axis of rotation of the body. |
