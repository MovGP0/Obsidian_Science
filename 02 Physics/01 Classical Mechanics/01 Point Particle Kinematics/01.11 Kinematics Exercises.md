title: "Kinematics Exercises"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Use the kinematic equations to calculate motion with constant acceleration, projectile motion, and circular motion.

## Exercise 1

**Basic.** A car starts from rest with constant acceleration $\mechanical{a}=\quantity{2.5}{m\,s^{-2}}$. How long does it take to reach $\mechanical{v}=\quantity{30}{m\,s^{-1}}$? How far does it travel?

**Result.** $t=\quantity{12}{s}$ and $\mechanical{x}=\quantity{180}{m}$.

## Exercise 2

**Basic.** A projectile launches from ground level at speed $\mechanical{v}_0=\quantity{20}{m\,s^{-1}}$ and angle $\mechanical{\alpha}=30\unit{{}^\circ}$. Calculate its range and maximum height.

**Result.** $\mechanical{x}_g\approx\quantity{35.3}{m}$ and $\mechanical{y}_h\approx\quantity{5.10}{m}$.

## Exercise 3

**Basic.** A wheel of radius $\mechanical{R}=\quantity{0.30}{m}$ rotates at $\quantity{300}{rpm}$. Calculate the angular speed, tangential speed, and centripetal acceleration at the rim.

**Result.** $\mechanical{\omega}=\quantity{31.4}{rad\,s^{-1}}$, $\mechanical{v}=\quantity{9.42}{m\,s^{-1}}$, and $\mechanical{a}_c=\quantity{296}{m\,s^{-2}}$.

## Exercise 4

**Basic.** A river flows at $\mechanical{V}=\quantity{1.5}{m\,s^{-1}}$. A boat with speed $\mechanical{v}'=\quantity{2.0}{m\,s^{-1}}$ relative to the water points perpendicular to the flow. Calculate its velocity relative to the bank and the angle from the intended direction.

**Result.** $\mechanical{v}\approx\quantity{2.5}{m\,s^{-1}}$ and $\mechanical{\theta}=\arctan(1.5/2.0)\approx36.9\unit{{}^\circ}$.

## Exercise 5

**Intermediate.** A projectile launches vertically upward at $\mechanical{v}_0=\quantity{25}{m\,s^{-1}}$. How long does it take to return to the launch point?

**Result.** $t=2\mechanical{v}_0/\mechanical{g}\approx\quantity{5.10}{s}$.

## Exercise 6

**Intermediate.** A ball falls from rest from height $\mechanical{h}$. What is its impact speed? What fraction of $\mechanical{h}$ does it fall in the last second if $\mechanical{h}=\quantity{80}{m}$?

**Result.** $\mechanical{v}=\sqrt{2\mechanical{g}\mechanical{h}}\approx\quantity{39.6}{m\,s^{-1}}$. The fraction is approximately $0.43$.

## Exercise 7

**Intermediate.** For motion with constant acceleration, show that the average velocity over $[0,T]$ is $(\mechanical{v}_0+\mechanical{v}(T))/2$.

## Exercise 8

**Intermediate.** A satellite in a circular orbit around Earth at height $\mechanical{h}=\quantity{400}{km}$ has tangential speed $\mechanical{v}\approx\quantity{7.7}{km\,s^{-1}}$. Calculate the orbital period.

**Result.** $\signal{T}\approx\quantity{92.2}{min}$.

## Exercise 9

**Challenge.** A projectile launches from height $\mechanical{h}_0$ at speed $\mechanical{v}_0$ and angle $\mechanical{\alpha}$. Show that the horizontal range is

$$
\mechanical{x}_g
=\frac{\mechanical{v}_0\cos\mechanical{\alpha}}{\mechanical{g}}
\left(\mechanical{v}_0\sin\mechanical{\alpha}+
\sqrt{\mechanical{v}_0^2\sin^2\mechanical{\alpha}+2\mechanical{g}\mechanical{h}_0}\right).
$$

## Exercise 10

**Challenge.** A particle moves in a circle at constant speed $\mechanical{v}$. Verify that in polar coordinates $(\mechanical{r},\mechanical{\theta})$, $\mechanical{\dot r}=0$ and $\mechanical{\dot\theta}=\mechanical{v}/\mechanical{R}$ for increasing $\mechanical{\theta}$. Show that $\mechanical{\vec a}=-\mechanical{\dot\theta}^{\,2}\mechanical{R}\hat e_r$.

## Exercise 11

**Intermediate.** Show that the acceleration for general motion along a curve is $\mechanical{\vec a}=\mechanical{a}_\tau\hat\tau+(\mechanical{v}^2/\mechanical{R})\hat n$. Identify $\mechanical{R}$ as the radius of the osculating circle.

## Exercise 12

**Intermediate.** A particle follows $\mechanical{r}(t)=\mechanical{R}\cos(\mechanical{\omega}t)$ and $\mechanical{\theta}(t)=\mechanical{\omega}t$ in polar coordinates. Find the trajectory in the $\mechanical{x}\mechanical{y}$ plane and describe the motion.

**Result.** $\mechanical{x}=\mechanical{R}\cos^2(\mechanical{\omega}t)$ and $\mechanical{y}=\mechanical{R}\cos(\mechanical{\omega}t)\sin(\mechanical{\omega}t)$. The trajectory is a circle of radius $\mechanical{R}/2$ centered at $(\mechanical{R}/2,0)$.

> [!note] Source notation
> The source's parameterization uses a signed radial coordinate. For the convention $\mechanical{r}\ge 0$, replace a negative radius by its magnitude and add $\pi$ to the polar angle.

## Exercise 13

**Basic.** A car brakes with $\mechanical{a}_\tau=\quantity{-3}{m\,s^{-2}}$ from an initial speed of $\mechanical{v}_0=\quantity{20}{m\,s^{-1}}$. Find the braking distance and braking time.

**Result.** $\mechanical{d}=\mechanical{v}_0^2/(2\lvert\mechanical{a}_\tau\rvert)=\quantity{66.7}{m}$ and $t=\mechanical{v}_0/\lvert\mechanical{a}_\tau\rvert=\quantity{6.67}{s}$.

## Exercise 14

**Intermediate.** Two trains travel in opposite directions on parallel tracks at $\mechanical{v}_1=\quantity{25}{m\,s^{-1}}$ and $\mechanical{v}_2=\quantity{30}{m\,s^{-1}}$. How long does a passenger on the first train take to see the full length of the second train pass if the second train is $\mechanical{L}=\quantity{200}{m}$ long?

**Result.** $t=\mechanical{L}/(\mechanical{v}_1+\mechanical{v}_2)\approx\quantity{3.64}{s}$.

## Exercise 15

**Challenge.** A particle moves along the logarithmic spiral $\mechanical{r}(\mechanical{\theta})=\mechanical{r}_0e^{a\mechanical{\theta}}$ with constant $\mechanical{\dot\theta}$. Show that the angle between the velocity vector and the radius vector is constant. The source gives $\arctan(1/a)$ for this angle, which corresponds to the outward branch with $a>0$ and $\mechanical{\dot\theta}>0$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $t$ | $\unit{s}$ | Elapsed time | The time from rest to the target speed. |
| $\mechanical{a}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-2}}$ | Acceleration | The constant acceleration of the car. |
| $\mechanical{v}, \mechanical{v}_0$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Speed or velocity | The speed or signed one-dimensional velocity, including its initial value. |
| $\mechanical{x}, \mechanical{y}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Cartesian position | The car's distance from its starting point or the coordinates of the particle in the plane. |
| $\mechanical{\alpha}$ | $\unit{rad}$ | Launch angle | The angle of launch above the horizontal. |
| $\mechanical{x}_g, \mechanical{y}_h$ | $\unit{m}$ | Projectile range and height | The horizontal landing distance and maximum vertical position. |
| $\mechanical{R}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Radius or radial scale | The radius of a circle or the scale in the polar trajectory. |
| $\mechanical{\omega}$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Angular speed | The rotation rate of the wheel. |
| $\mechanical{a}_c$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-2}}$ | Centripetal acceleration magnitude | The magnitude of the inward acceleration at the wheel rim. |
| $\mechanical{V}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | River speed | The speed of the water relative to the bank. |
| $\mechanical{v}'$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Boat speed | The speed of the boat relative to the water. |
| $\mechanical{\theta}$ | $\unit{rad}$ | Drift angle | The angle between the boat's velocity relative to the bank and its intended direction. |
| $\mechanical{\theta}, \mechanical{\dot\theta}$ | $\unit{rad}, \unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Polar angle and angular velocity | The angle from the reference direction and its rate of change. |
| $\mechanical{g}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-2}}$ | Acceleration due to gravity | The magnitude of the downward acceleration in free fall. |
| $\mechanical{h}, \mechanical{h}_0$ | $\unit{m}$ | Height | The initial fall or launch height, or the altitude of the satellite. |
| $T$ | $\unit{s}$ | Interval duration | The duration of the interval over which velocity is averaged. |
| $\signal{T}$ | $\unit{s}$ | Orbital period | The time required for one complete orbit. |
| — | 1 | Fraction of fall distance | The portion of the initial height covered in the last second. |
| $\mechanical{r}, \mechanical{r}_0$ | $\unit{m}$ | Radial coordinate | The radius in the polar trajectory and its value at zero angle in the spiral. |
| $\mechanical{\dot r}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Radial velocity | The rate of change of the radial coordinate. |
| $\mechanical{\vec a}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-2}}$ | Acceleration vector | The rate of change of the particle's velocity vector. |
| $\mechanical{a}_\tau$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-2}}$ | Tangential acceleration | The rate of change of speed along the path. |
| $\mechanical{d}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Braking distance | The distance the car travels before stopping. |
| $\mechanical{v}_1, \mechanical{v}_2$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Train speeds | The speeds of the two trains relative to the tracks. |
| $\mechanical{L}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Train length | The length of the second train. |
| $a$ | $\unit{rad^{-1}}$ | Spiral parameter | The rate of change of the logarithm of radius with polar angle. |
