---
title: "Coupled Oscillators with Multiple Degrees of Freedom"
---

> [!TLDR]
> A chain of coupled masses has collective normal modes. As the number of masses increases, the model describes wave propagation and dispersion.

Three or more coupled oscillators exhibit energy transfer between modes, wave dispersion, and collective behavior.

**Chain of three oscillators.** Three bodies of equal mass $\mechanical{m}$ are arranged in a line. Four springs of stiffness $\mechanical{k}$ connect the masses to each other and to the fixed ends. The displacement coordinates are $\mechanical{\eta}_1,\mechanical{\eta}_2,\mechanical{\eta}_3$. The mass and stiffness matrices are

$$
\mechanical{M}=\mechanical{m}\mathbb{I},
\qquad
\mechanical{V}=\mechanical{k}
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & -1 & 0\\
-1 & 2 & -1\\
0 & -1 & 2
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

The stiffness eigenvalues are $\mechanical{V}_j=2\mechanical{k}[1-\cos(j\pi/4)]$ for $j=1,2,3$. The mode angular frequencies are $\signal{\omega}_j=\sqrt{(2\mechanical{k}/\mechanical{m})[1-\cos(j\pi/4)]}$. The corresponding mode shapes are proportional to $\sin(jn\pi/4)$ for $n=1,2,3$.

**Continuum limit.** As the number of masses $N$ tends to infinity in the continuum limit, the chain becomes a continuous string. The discrete eigenvalue problem becomes a differential-operator problem.

$$
\frac{\partial^2\mechanical{\eta}}{\partial t^2}
=\signal{c}^2\frac{\partial^2\mechanical{\eta}}{\partial\mechanical{x}^2},
\qquad
\signal{c}=\mechanical{a}\sqrt{\frac{\mechanical{k}}{\mechanical{m}}},
$$

where $\mechanical{a}$ is the lattice spacing and $\signal{c}$ is the wave speed. A string of length $\mechanical{L}$ with fixed ends has normal modes proportional to $\sin(n\pi\mechanical{x}/\mechanical{L})\cos(\signal{\omega}_nt+\signal{\phi}_n)$, where $\signal{\omega}_n=n\pi\signal{c}/\mechanical{L}$. This harmonic spectrum is the basis for the theory of vibrating strings and stringed instruments.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{m},\mechanical{M}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass and mass matrix | The common mass and its matrix representation. |
| $\mechanical{k},\mechanical{V}$ | $\unit{N\,m^{-1}}$ | Spring constant and stiffness matrix | The spring stiffness and the matrix of the coupled system. |
| $\mechanical{\eta}_1,\mechanical{\eta}_2,\mechanical{\eta}_3$ | $\unit{m}$ | Displacement | The position of each mass relative to equilibrium. |
| $\mechanical{V}_j$ | $\unit{N\,m^{-1}}$ | Stiffness eigenvalue | The stiffness associated with mode $j$. |
| $\signal{\omega}_j,\signal{\omega}_n$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Mode angular frequency | The angular frequency of a chain or string mode. |
| $\mechanical{\eta}$ | $\unit{m}$ | String displacement | The displacement of the string at a given position and time. |
| $\mechanical{x}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Position | The coordinate along the string. |
| $t$ | $\unit{s}$ | Time | The independent variable for the motion. |
| $\mechanical{a}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Lattice spacing | The distance between adjacent masses. |
| $\signal{c}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Wave speed | The propagation speed along the string. |
| $\mechanical{L}$ | $\unit{m}$ | String length | The distance between the fixed ends. |
| $\signal{\phi}_n$ | $\unit{rad}$ | Initial phase | The phase of string mode $n$ at zero time. |
