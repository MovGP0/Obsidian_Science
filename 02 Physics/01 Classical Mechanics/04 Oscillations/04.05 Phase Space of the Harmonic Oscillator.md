---
title: "Phase Space of the Harmonic Oscillator"
---

> [!TLDR]
> A harmonic oscillator follows an ellipse of constant energy in phase space. In action–angle coordinates, only its angle changes with time.

The phase space $(\mechanical{x},\mechanical{p})$ gives a geometric description of the motion.

**Trajectory equation.** From $\energy{E}=\mechanical{p}^2/(2\mechanical{m})+\tfrac12\mechanical{k}\mechanical{x}^2$,

$$
\frac{\mechanical{x}^2}{2\energy{E}/\mechanical{k}}
+\frac{\mechanical{p}^2}{2\mechanical{m}\energy{E}}=1.
$$

This is an ellipse centered at the origin with semiaxes $\sqrt{2\energy{E}/\mechanical{k}}$ and $\sqrt{2\mechanical{m}\energy{E}}$. Its area is $\pi\sqrt{2\energy{E}/\mechanical{k}}\sqrt{2\mechanical{m}\energy{E}}=2\pi\energy{E}\sqrt{\mechanical{m}/\mechanical{k}}=2\pi\energy{E}/\signal{\omega}$.

**Direction of motion.** Hamilton's equations $\mechanical{\dot x}=\mechanical{p}/\mechanical{m}$ and $\mechanical{\dot p}=-\mechanical{k}\mechanical{x}$ give clockwise motion in the $(\mechanical{x},\mechanical{p})$ plane for $\energy{E}>0$. The oscillation phase advances uniformly at angular frequency $\signal{\omega}$. In coordinates scaled by the semiaxes, the ellipse becomes a circle.

**Liouville conservation.** The area of the ellipse stays constant under Hamiltonian flow, in agreement with [[12.07 Liouville's Theorem]].

**Action–angle variables.** The action is $J=\oint\mechanical{p}\,dx/(2\pi)=\energy{E}/\signal{\omega}$. Its conjugate coordinate is the angle $\signal{\theta}=\signal{\omega}t+\signal{\theta}_0$. In these variables, $\energy{H}=\signal{\omega}J$, so $\dot J=0$ and $\dot{\signal{\theta}}=\signal{\omega}$.

**Meaning.** The oscillator is exactly integrable. At fixed action $J$, the phase runs around a circle $S^1$, a one-dimensional torus. The same description extends to integrable systems with more degrees of freedom. See [[13.13 Action–Angle Variables for Systems with Multiple Degrees of Freedom]].

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{x},\mechanical{\dot x}$ | $\unit{m},\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Position and velocity | The displacement from equilibrium and its rate of change. |
| $\mechanical{p}$ | $\unit{kg\,m\,s^{-1}}$ | Momentum | The canonical momentum of the oscillator. |
| $\mechanical{\dot p}$ | $\unit{N}$ | Momentum derivative | The rate of change of momentum. |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | The mass of the oscillator. |
| $\mechanical{k}$ | $\unit{N\,m^{-1}}$ | Spring constant | The stiffness of the spring. |
| $\energy{E},\energy{H}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Mechanical energy and Hamiltonian | The constant energy and its Hamiltonian representation. |
| $\signal{\omega}$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Angular frequency | The rate of advance of the oscillator phase. |
| $J$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Action variable | The area enclosed by the phase-space trajectory divided by $2\pi$. |
| $\signal{\theta},\signal{\theta}_0$ | $\unit{rad}$ | Phase angle | The canonical angle and its initial value. |
| $t$ | $\unit{s}$ | Time | The independent variable for the oscillation. |
