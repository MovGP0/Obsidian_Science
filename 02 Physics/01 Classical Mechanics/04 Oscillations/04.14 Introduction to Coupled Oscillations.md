---
title: "Introduction to Coupled Oscillations"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Two oscillators connected by a spring have a symmetric mode and an antisymmetric mode. Exciting one mass can transfer energy to the other.

Two oscillators coupled by a spring show *normal modes* and *energy transfer*.

**Symmetric system.** Let two bodies of equal mass $\mechanical{m}$ have displacements $\mechanical{x}_1$ and $\mechanical{x}_2$ from equilibrium. A spring of constant $\mechanical{k}$ connects each mass to a wall. A spring of constant $\mechanical{k}'$ connects the masses. Their equations are

$$
\mechanical{m}\mechanical{\ddot x}_1=-\mechanical{k}\mechanical{x}_1+\mechanical{k}'(\mechanical{x}_2-\mechanical{x}_1),
\qquad
\mechanical{m}\mechanical{\ddot x}_2=-\mechanical{k}\mechanical{x}_2-\mechanical{k}'(\mechanical{x}_2-\mechanical{x}_1).
$$

**Normal modes.**

- $\mechanical{x}_1+\mechanical{x}_2$ oscillates at $\signal{\omega}_+=\sqrt{\mechanical{k}/\mechanical{m}}$. In this symmetric mode, the middle spring does not deform.
- $\mechanical{x}_1-\mechanical{x}_2$ oscillates at $\signal{\omega}_-=\sqrt{(\mechanical{k}+2\mechanical{k}')/\mechanical{m}}$. This is the antisymmetric mode.

See [[14.00 Small Oscillations and Normal Modes|Chapter 14]] for the general method.

**Beats between modes.** When only one mass is initially excited, the superposition of modes transfers energy between the masses. The signed displacement envelopes vary at $(\signal{\omega}_--\signal{\omega}_+)/2$. The energy exchange follows the squared envelopes.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | The common mass of each oscillator. |
| $\mechanical{x}_1,\mechanical{x}_2$ | $\unit{m}$ | Displacement | The displacement of each mass from equilibrium. |
| $\mechanical{\ddot x}_1,\mechanical{\ddot x}_2$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-2}}$ | Acceleration | The second time derivative of each displacement. |
| $\mechanical{k},\mechanical{k}'$ | $\unit{N\,m^{-1}}$ | Spring constant | The stiffness of a wall spring or the coupling spring. |
| $\signal{\omega}_+,\signal{\omega}_-$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Normal-mode angular frequency | The angular frequency of the symmetric or antisymmetric mode. |
