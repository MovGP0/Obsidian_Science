---
title: "Superposition of Oscillations"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Oscillations at the same frequency add to another sinusoid. Nearby frequencies produce beats, and perpendicular oscillations can trace Lissajous figures.

For $\mechanical{x}_1=\mechanical{A}_1\cos(\signal{\omega}t)$ and $\mechanical{x}_2=\mechanical{A}_2\cos(\signal{\omega}t+\signal{\varphi})$, the sum is another harmonic oscillation.

$$
\mechanical{x}_1+\mechanical{x}_2=\mechanical{A}\cos(\signal{\omega}t+\signal{\psi}),
$$

where

$$
\mechanical{A}=\sqrt{\mechanical{A}_1^2+\mechanical{A}_2^2+2\mechanical{A}_1\mechanical{A}_2\cos\signal{\varphi}},
\qquad
\tan\signal{\psi}=\frac{\mechanical{A}_2\sin\signal{\varphi}}
{\mechanical{A}_1+\mechanical{A}_2\cos\signal{\varphi}}.
$$

**Beats.** For two nearby angular frequencies $\signal{\omega}_1$ and $\signal{\omega}_2$ and equal amplitudes,

$$
\mechanical{A}\cos(\signal{\omega}_1t)+\mechanical{A}\cos(\signal{\omega}_2t)
=2\mechanical{A}\cos\!\left(\frac{\signal{\omega}_1-\signal{\omega}_2}{2}t\right)
\cos\!\left(\frac{\signal{\omega}_1+\signal{\omega}_2}{2}t\right).
$$

The rapid oscillation at angular frequency $(\signal{\omega}_1+\signal{\omega}_2)/2$ has a slow signed envelope at $\lvert\signal{\omega}_1-\signal{\omega}_2\rvert/2$. The peaks of the envelope magnitude repeat at twice the envelope's angular frequency. This modulation is called **beating**.

**Perpendicular oscillations and Lissajous figures.** The oscillations $\mechanical{x}=\mechanical{A}\cos(\signal{\omega}_1t)$ and $\mechanical{y}=\mechanical{B}\cos(\signal{\omega}_2t+\signal{\varphi})$ trace a Lissajous figure. The path closes when $\signal{\omega}_1/\signal{\omega}_2$ is rational.

> [!note] Analytical Mechanics
> The harmonic oscillator can be integrated explicitly in the Newtonian, Lagrangian, Hamiltonian, and action–angle formulations. The chapters on Lagrangian and Hamiltonian mechanics use it as a reference example.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{x}_1,\mechanical{x}_2,\mechanical{x},\mechanical{y}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Displacement | The individual or perpendicular displacement components. |
| $\mechanical{A},\mechanical{A}_1,\mechanical{A}_2,\mechanical{B}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Amplitude | The peak magnitude of the specified oscillation. |
| $\signal{\omega},\signal{\omega}_1,\signal{\omega}_2$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Angular frequency | The rate of advance of the phase of the specified oscillation. |
| $\signal{\varphi},\signal{\psi}$ | $\unit{rad}$ | Phase angle | The phase of the second oscillation relative to the first and the resulting phase of their sum. |
| $t$ | $\unit{s}$ | Time | The independent variable for the oscillations. |
