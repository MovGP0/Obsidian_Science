title: "Systems with Infinitely Many Degrees of Freedom"
---

> [!TLDR]
> For strings, membranes, fluids, and fields, differential operators take the place of finite matrices in the normal-mode problem.

Extending the normal-mode method to continuous systems replaces matrices with differential operators. A string of length $\mechanical{L}$ under tension $\mechanical{T}$ and with linear mass density $\mechanical{\lambda}$ satisfies

$$
\mechanical{\lambda}\frac{\partial^2\eta}{\partial t^2}=\mechanical{T}\frac{\partial^2\eta}{\partial\mechanical{x}^2}.
$$

**String fixed at both ends.** Separation of variables gives sinusoidal modes. The wave speed is $\mechanical{c}=\sqrt{\mechanical{T}/\mechanical{\lambda}}$. See [[14.13 Acoustic Spectrum of a Vibrating String]].

**Rectangular membrane.** For a rectangle with fixed edges and side lengths $\mechanical{L}_x,\mechanical{L}_y$, a mode with amplitude $\eta_0$ is

$$
\eta_{nm}(\mechanical{x},\mechanical{y},t)=
\eta_0\sin\left(\frac{n\pi\mechanical{x}}{\mechanical{L}_x}\right)
\sin\left(\frac{m\pi\mechanical{y}}{\mechanical{L}_y}\right)
\cos(\signal{\omega}_{nm}t),
$$

with

$$
\signal{\omega}_{nm}=\mechanical{c}\pi\sqrt{\frac{n^2}{\mechanical{L}_x^2}+\frac{m^2}{\mechanical{L}_y^2}}.
$$

The membrane frequencies are generally not integer multiples of the fundamental.

**Electromagnetic waves.** The vacuum Maxwell equations in unbounded space give a continuous spectrum. A wave vector $\signal{\vec k}$ and a polarization specify a normal mode. Quantization of these harmonic modes is a basis of quantum electrodynamics.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{L},\mechanical{L}_x,\mechanical{L}_y$ | $\unit{m}$ | Lengths | The string length and membrane side lengths. |
| $\mechanical{T}$ | $\unit{N}$ | Tension | The tension in the string. |
| $\mechanical{\lambda}$ | $\unit{kg\,m^{-1}}$ | Linear mass density | The mass per unit length of the string. |
| $\mechanical{x},\mechanical{y},\eta,\eta_{nm},\eta_0$ | $\unit{m}$ | Position and displacement | The spatial coordinates, displacements, and modal amplitude. |
| $\mechanical{c}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Wave speed | The propagation speed of the string or membrane wave. |
| $\signal{\omega}_{nm}$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Angular frequency | The frequency of a membrane mode. |
| $\signal{\vec k}$ | $\unit{m^{-1}}$ | Wave vector | The spatial wave vector of an electromagnetic mode. |
| $t$ | $\unit{s}$ | Time | The independent variable for the wave. |
