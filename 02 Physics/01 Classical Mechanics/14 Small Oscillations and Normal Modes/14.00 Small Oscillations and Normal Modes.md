title: "Small Oscillations and Normal Modes"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Near stable equilibrium, the linearized equations reduce to an eigenvalue problem. The motion is a superposition of normal modes.

Consider small oscillations of a system with $n$ degrees of freedom near stable equilibrium. In the Lagrangian formulation, linearizing about equilibrium reduces the equations of motion to an eigenvalue problem. The solution is a superposition of **normal modes**, each with its own natural frequency. The chapter also introduces nonlinear systems and chaos.

## Chapter summary

- Near equilibrium the quadratic Lagrangian is $\energy{L}=\tfrac12\dot{\vec\eta}^{\,T}M\dot{\vec\eta}-\tfrac12\vec\eta^{\,T}V\vec\eta$.
- The generalized eigenvalue problem is $V\vec u_\alpha=\signal{\omega}_\alpha^2M\vec u_\alpha$.
- At a nondegenerate stable equilibrium, the $n$ frequencies are real and positive, and the eigenvectors can be chosen $M$-orthogonal.
- A normal-mode contribution has the form $\vec\eta_\alpha=\vec u_\alpha A_\alpha\cos(\signal{\omega}_\alpha t+\signal{\varphi}_\alpha)$.
- The normal coordinates $Q_\alpha$ are independent harmonic oscillators.
- In a linear chain, $\signal{\omega}(\signal{q})=2\sqrt{\mechanical{k}/\mechanical{m}}\,\lvert\sin(\signal{q}\mechanical{a}/2)\rvert$.
- Nonlinear topics include the amplitude-dependent period of the pendulum, the KAM theorem, Lyapunov exponents, and the logistic map.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\energy{L}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Lagrangian | The quadratic Lagrangian for small oscillations. |
| $\vec\eta,\vec\eta_\alpha,\vec u_\alpha$ | — | Deviations and mode vector | The full displacement, modal contribution, and normalized pattern. |
| $M,V$ | — | Mass and stiffness matrices | The quadratic kinetic and potential coefficients. |
| $A_\alpha,Q_\alpha$ | $\unit{J^{1/2}\,s}$ | Normal amplitude and coordinate | The amplitude and time-dependent coordinate of a mass-normalized mode. |
| $\signal{\omega}_\alpha,\signal{\omega}$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Angular frequencies | The mode frequency and lattice wave frequency. |
| $\signal{\varphi}_\alpha$ | $\unit{rad}$ | Phase | The constant phase of mode $\alpha$. |
| $\signal{q}$ | $\unit{m^{-1}}$ | Wavenumber | The spatial phase rate of a lattice wave. |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | The mass at each site of the chain. |
| $\mechanical{k}$ | $\unit{N\,m^{-1}}$ | Spring stiffness | The stiffness between neighboring sites. |
| $\mechanical{a}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Lattice spacing | The distance between sites. |
| $t$ | $\unit{s}$ | Time | The independent variable for the motion. |
