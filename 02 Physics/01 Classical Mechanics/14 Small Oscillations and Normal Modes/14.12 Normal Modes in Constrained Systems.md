---
title: "Normal Modes in Constrained Systems"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Three masses with free ends have a zero-frequency translation mode and two internal oscillation modes.

**Example. Three equal masses on a linear chain.** Two springs of stiffness $\mechanical{k}$ connect the adjacent masses $\mechanical{m}$. The ends are free. Let $\mechanical{x}_1,\mechanical{x}_2,\mechanical{x}_3$ denote displacements. The Lagrangian and matrices are

$$
\energy{L}=\tfrac12\mechanical{m}(\mechanical{\dot x}_1^2+\mechanical{\dot x}_2^2+\mechanical{\dot x}_3^2)
-\tfrac12\mechanical{k}\bigl[(\mechanical{x}_1-\mechanical{x}_2)^2+(\mechanical{x}_2-\mechanical{x}_3)^2\bigr],
$$

$$
M=\mechanical{m}I,\qquad
V=\mechanical{k}\begin{pmatrix}1&-1&0\\-1&2&-1\\0&-1&1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

The eigenvalues of $V$ are $0,\mechanical{k},3\mechanical{k}$. The angular frequencies and mass-normalized eigenvectors are

- $\signal{\omega}_0=0$ and $\vec u_0=(1,1,1)^T/\sqrt{3\mechanical{m}}$. The three masses translate together, with no spring deformation.
- $\signal{\omega}_1=\sqrt{\mechanical{k}/\mechanical{m}}$ and $\vec u_1=(1,0,-1)^T/\sqrt{2\mechanical{m}}$. The center mass stays at rest, and the outer masses move in opposite phase.
- $\signal{\omega}_2=\sqrt{3\mechanical{k}/\mechanical{m}}$ and $\vec u_2=(1,-2,1)^T/\sqrt{6\mechanical{m}}$. The center mass moves in opposite phase to the outer masses.

> [!note] Source wording
> The source gives zero energy for the translation mode. The mode has zero restoring potential energy. Uniform translation with nonzero velocity carries kinetic energy.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{m},M$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass and mass matrix | The mass of each body and the matrix of kinetic coefficients. |
| $\mechanical{k},V$ | $\unit{N\,m^{-1}}$ | Spring stiffness and stiffness matrix | The stiffness of each spring and the potential Hessian. |
| $\mechanical{x}_i$ | $\unit{m}$ | Displacement | The displacement of mass $i$. |
| $\mechanical{\dot x}_i$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Velocity | The velocity of mass $i$. |
| $\energy{L}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Lagrangian | The kinetic energy minus the spring energy. |
| $\signal{\omega}_0,\signal{\omega}_1,\signal{\omega}_2$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Angular frequencies | The translation frequency and the two internal frequencies. |
| $\vec u_0,\vec u_1,\vec u_2$ | $\unit{kg^{-1/2}}$ | Mode vectors | The three mass-normalized displacement patterns. |
