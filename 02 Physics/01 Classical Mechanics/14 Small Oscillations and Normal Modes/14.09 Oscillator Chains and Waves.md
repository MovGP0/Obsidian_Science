---
title: "Oscillator Chains and Waves"
---

> [!TLDR]
> A periodic chain has collective wave modes set by the mass and the nearest-neighbor springs.

Consider a linear chain of $N$ equal masses $\mechanical{m}$. Springs of stiffness $\mechanical{k}$ connect nearest neighbors, with periodic boundary conditions. The equations of motion are

$$
\mechanical{m}\ddot\eta_n=\mechanical{k}(\eta_{n+1}-2\eta_n+\eta_{n-1}).
$$

**Normal modes.** Use $\eta_n(t)\propto e^{i(\signal{q}n\mechanical{a}-\signal{\omega}t)}$ with wavenumber $\signal{q}$ and lattice spacing $\mechanical{a}$. Substitution gives

$$
-\mechanical{m}\signal{\omega}^2=\mechanical{k}(e^{i\signal{q}\mechanical{a}}-2+e^{-i\signal{q}\mechanical{a}})=-2\mechanical{k}\bigl(1-\cos(\signal{q}\mechanical{a})\bigr).
$$

The dispersion relation is

$$
\signal{\omega}(\signal{q})=2\sqrt{\frac{\mechanical{k}}{\mechanical{m}}}\left\lvert\sin\left(\frac{\signal{q}\mechanical{a}}2\right)\right\rvert.
$$

- **Long wavelengths.** As $\signal{q}\to0$, $\signal{\omega}\simeq\lvert\signal{q}\rvert\mechanical{a}\sqrt{\mechanical{k}/\mechanical{m}}$. The sound speed is $\mechanical{c}_s=\mechanical{a}\sqrt{\mechanical{k}/\mechanical{m}}$.
- **Edge of the first Brillouin zone.** As $\signal{q}\to\pi/\mechanical{a}$, the frequency approaches its maximum $2\sqrt{\mechanical{k}/\mechanical{m}}$.

**Continuum limit.** Let $N\to\infty$ and $\mechanical{a}\to0$ with $\mechanical{a}^2\mechanical{k}/\mechanical{m}=\mechanical{c}^2$ constant. The chain becomes continuous, and the equation of motion becomes the wave equation.

$$
\frac{\partial^2\eta}{\partial t^2}=\mechanical{c}^2\frac{\partial^2\eta}{\partial\mechanical{x}^2}.
$$

This is a starting point for the classical field theory of waves.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | The mass at each lattice site. |
| $\mechanical{k}$ | $\unit{N\,m^{-1}}$ | Spring stiffness | The stiffness of each nearest-neighbor coupling. |
| $\eta_n,\eta$ | $\unit{m}$ | Displacements | The discrete and continuous displacements. |
| $\mechanical{a},\mechanical{x}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Lattice spacing and position | The separation of sites and the continuous position. |
| $\signal{q}$ | $\unit{m^{-1}}$ | Wavenumber | The spatial phase rate of the wave. |
| $\signal{\omega}$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Angular frequency | The temporal phase rate of the wave. |
| $\mechanical{c}_s,\mechanical{c}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Wave speeds | The long-wavelength sound speed and the continuum wave speed. |
| $t$ | $\unit{s}$ | Time | The independent variable for the motion. |
