---
title: "Comparison of the Formulations of Mechanics"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Newton's, Lagrange's, and Hamilton's formulations use different variables, equations, and methods for constraints and symmetries.

| Aspect | Newton | Lagrange | Hamilton |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Variables | $\mechanical{\vec r}_i$ | $q_k$ | $q_k,p_k$ |
| Equations | $\mechanical{m}\mechanical{\ddot{\vec r}}=\mechanical{\vec F}$ | $d(\partial\energy{L}/\partial\dot q_k)/dt=\partial\energy{L}/\partial q_k$ | $\dot q_k=\partial\energy{H}/\partial p_k$, $\dot p_k=-\partial\energy{H}/\partial q_k$ |
| Number and order | $3N$ second-order equations | $n$ second-order equations | $2n$ first-order equations |
| Constraints | Explicit | Implicit in reduced coordinates | Implicit in reduced coordinates |
| Constraint forces | Calculated directly | Eliminated for ideal constraints in reduced coordinates | Eliminated in reduced coordinates |
| Coordinate changes | Can require substantial work | Straightforward in generalized coordinates | Broad choice of canonical coordinates |
| Symmetries | Identified case by case | Cyclic coordinates and variational symmetries | Symplectic groups and canonical generators |
| Quantization | No direct route in this comparison | Uses the action $\energy{S}$ | Uses Poisson brackets $\{\cdot,\cdot\}$ |

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{\vec r}_i$ | $\unit{m}$ | Position | The position of particle $i$. |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | The particle mass in Newton's equation. |
| $\mechanical{\vec F}$ | $\unit{N}$ | Force | The net force in Newton's equation. |
| $q_k,p_k$ | — | Generalized variables | The coordinates and conjugate momenta. |
| $\energy{L},\energy{H}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Lagrangian and Hamiltonian | The two dynamical functions. |
| $\energy{S}$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Action | The time integral of the Lagrangian. |
| $t$ | $\unit{s}$ | Time | The independent variable for the motion. |
