## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| — | $\unit{J}$ | Vehicle kinetic energy | About $\quantity{5\times10^5}{J}$ at $\quantity{30}{m\,s^{-1}}$. |
| — | $\unit{J}$ | Earth–Sun binding energy | About $\quantity{2.6\times10^{33}}{J}$. |
| — | $\unit{J}$ | Earth's rotational energy | About $\quantity{2.1\times10^{29}}{J}$, using $\mechanical{I}\simeq0.331\mechanical{M}_T\mechanical{R}_T^2$. |
| — | $\unit{J}$ | Internal energy of one mole of ideal monatomic gas | About $\quantity{3700}{J}$ at $\quantity{300}{K}$. |
| — | $\unit{min}$ | ISS orbital period | About $\quantity{92}{min}$. |
| — | $\unit{km\,h^{-1}}$ | Typical GPS satellite speed | About $\quantity{14000}{km\,h^{-1}}$. |
| — | $\unit{Hz}$ | Quartz watch oscillator frequency | $\quantity{32768}{Hz}$. |
| — | $\unit{d}$ | Chandler period | About $433$ days. |
| $\mechanical{I}$ | $\unit{kg\,m^2}$ | Moment of inertia | The Earth's axial moment of inertia used in the rotation estimate. |
| $\mechanical{M}_T$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Earth mass | The mass used in the moment-of-inertia estimate. |
| $\mechanical{R}_T$ | $\unit{m}$ | Earth radius | The radius used in the moment-of-inertia estimate. |
