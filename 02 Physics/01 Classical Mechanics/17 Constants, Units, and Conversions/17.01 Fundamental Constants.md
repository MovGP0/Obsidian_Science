> [!TLDR]
> Rounded SI values for constants used in mechanics and related topics.

The table includes a representative value of gravitational acceleration near Earth's surface.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $c$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Speed of light in vacuum | $\quantity{2.998\times10^8}{m\,s^{-1}}$. |
| $\mechanical{G}$ | $\unit{m^3\,kg^{-1}\,s^{-2}}$ | Gravitational constant | $\quantity{6.674\times10^{-11}}{m^3\,kg^{-1}\,s^{-2}}$. |
| $\mechanical{g}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-2}}$ | Gravitational acceleration | $\quantity{9.81}{m\,s^{-2}}$ near Earth's surface. |
| $h$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Planck constant | $\quantity{6.626\times10^{-34}}{J\,s}$. |
| $\hbar$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Reduced Planck constant | $h/(2\pi)\simeq\quantity{1.055\times10^{-34}}{J\,s}$. |
| $\thermal{k}_B$ | $\unit{J\,K^{-1}}$ | Boltzmann constant | $\quantity{1.381\times10^{-23}}{J\,K^{-1}}$. |
| $\electric{e}$ | $\unit{C}$ | Elementary charge | $\quantity{1.602\times10^{-19}}{C}$. |
| $\mechanical{m}_e$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Electron mass | $\quantity{9.109\times10^{-31}}{kg}$. |
| $\mechanical{m}_p$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Proton mass | $\quantity{1.673\times10^{-27}}{kg}$. |
| $N_A$ | $\unit{mol^{-1}}$ | Avogadro constant | $\quantity{6.022\times10^{23}}{mol^{-1}}$. |
| $\electric{\varepsilon}_0$ | $\unit{F\,m^{-1}}$ | Vacuum permittivity | $\quantity{8.854\times10^{-12}}{F\,m^{-1}}$. |
