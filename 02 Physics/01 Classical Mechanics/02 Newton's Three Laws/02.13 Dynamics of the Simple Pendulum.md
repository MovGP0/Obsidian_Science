---
title: "Dynamics of the Simple Pendulum"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The tangential component of weight gives the equation of motion of a simple pendulum.

The simple pendulum is an application of Newton's second law. A body of mass $\mechanical{m}$ hangs from a cord of length $\mechanical{\ell}$ in a uniform gravitational field. The coordinate $\mechanical{\theta}$ is the angle from the downward vertical.

**Forces.** The tension $\mechanical{\vec T}$ acts along the cord toward the suspension point. The weight $\mechanical{m}\mechanical{\vec g}$ acts vertically downward.

**Equation of motion.** The tangential component of Newton's second law gives

$$
\mechanical{m}\mechanical{\ell}\mechanical{\ddot\theta}
=-\mechanical{m}\mechanical{g}\sin\mechanical{\theta}.
$$

Tension acts radially, so it contributes no tangential component.

**Radial component.** The inward radial equation is

$$
\mechanical{T}-\mechanical{m}\mechanical{g}\cos\mechanical{\theta}
=\mechanical{m}\mechanical{\ell}\mechanical{\dot\theta}^{\,2}.
$$

It gives $\mechanical{T}=\mechanical{m}(\mechanical{\ell}\mechanical{\dot\theta}^{\,2}+\mechanical{g}\cos\mechanical{\theta})$.

**Small oscillations.** For small $\mechanical{\theta}$, $\sin\mechanical{\theta}\approx\mechanical{\theta}$. The equation becomes $\mechanical{\ddot\theta}+(\mechanical{g}/\mechanical{\ell})\mechanical{\theta}=0$, the harmonic oscillator equation with angular frequency $\signal{\omega}=\sqrt{\mechanical{g}/\mechanical{\ell}}$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | The mass of the pendulum bob. |
| $\mechanical{\ell}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Cord length | The distance from the suspension point to the bob. |
| $\mechanical{\theta}$ | $\unit{rad}$ | Pendulum angle | The angular displacement from the downward vertical. |
| $\mechanical{\ddot\theta}$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-2}}$ | Angular acceleration | The second time derivative of the pendulum angle. |
| $\mechanical{\vec T}, \mechanical{T}$ | $\unit{N}$ | Tension | The force of the cord on the bob and its magnitude. |
| $\mechanical{\vec g}, \mechanical{g}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-2}}$ | Gravitational acceleration | The local downward acceleration vector and its magnitude. |
| $\mechanical{\dot\theta}$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Angular velocity | The rate of change of the pendulum angle. |
| $\signal{\omega}$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Angular frequency | The angular frequency of small oscillations. |
