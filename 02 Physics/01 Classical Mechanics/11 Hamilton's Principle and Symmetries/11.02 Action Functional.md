---
title: "Action Functional"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The action assigns a number to a path by integrating the Lagrangian between two fixed times.

> [!definition] Definition 11.1 — Action
> For a system with Lagrangian $\energy{L}(q,\dot q,t)$, the action of path $q(t)$ from $t_1$ to $t_2$ is
>
> $$
> S[q]=\int_{t_1}^{t_2}\energy{L}(q(t),\dot q(t),t)\,dt.
> $$

The action is a functional. It assigns a number to each path with endpoints $q(t_1)=q_1$ and $q(t_2)=q_2$. Its unit is energy times time, $\unit{J\,s}$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $S$ | $\unit{J\,s}$ | Action | The time integral of the Lagrangian evaluated on the path. |
| $\energy{L}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Lagrangian | The function integrated along the path. |
| $q,\dot q,q_1,q_2$ | — | Coordinates and velocities | The path, its time derivative, and its endpoints. |
| $t,t_1,t_2$ | $\unit{s}$ | Time | The independent variable and its endpoint values. |
