---
title: "Energy Function and Jacobi Function"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The Jacobi function is the velocity-space form of the Hamiltonian when the Legendre transformation is invertible.

For $\energy{L}(q,\dot q,t)$, define the **Jacobi function**

$$
\energy{h}(q,\dot q,t)=\sum_k\dot q_k\frac{\partial\energy{L}}{\partial\dot q_k}-\energy{L}.
$$

It uses the same variables as the Lagrangian. The Hamiltonian $\energy{H}(q,p,t)$ uses the phase-space variables $(q,p)$. Expressing $\dot q$ in terms of $p$ gives $\energy{h}(q,\dot q(q,p,t),t)=\energy{H}(q,p,t)$.

**Conservation.** If $\partial\energy{L}/\partial t=0$, then $d\energy{h}/dt=0$ along physical paths.

**Mechanical energy.** For $\energy{L}=\energy{T}-\energy{U}$ with $\energy{T}$ homogeneous quadratic in $\dot q$ and $\energy{U}$ independent of velocity, $\energy{h}=\energy{T}+\energy{U}=\energy{E}$.

For rheonomic constraints, the kinetic energy can also contain terms that are linear in the generalized velocities or independent of them. In general, $\energy{h}\ne\energy{E}$.

For example, a bead slides on a rail in the $xy$ plane. The rail rotates at a constant angular velocity $\mechanical{\omega}$, and gravity is absent. With radial coordinate $\mechanical{r}$, the kinetic energy is

$$
\energy{T}=\tfrac12\mechanical{m}(\mechanical{\dot r}^{\,2}+\mechanical{r}^2\mechanical{\omega}^2).
$$

The constraint is rheonomic. The Jacobi function is

$$
\energy{h}=\tfrac12\mechanical{m}(\mechanical{\dot r}^{\,2}-\mechanical{r}^2\mechanical{\omega}^2)\ne\energy{T}.
$$

It is constant because $\partial\energy{L}/\partial t=0$, while the kinetic energy increases as the bead moves outward.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\energy{L},\energy{h},\energy{H}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Dynamical functions | The Lagrangian, Jacobi function, and Hamiltonian. |
| $\energy{T},\energy{U},\energy{E}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Mechanical energy and terms | The kinetic, potential, and total mechanical energies. |
| $q,\dot q,\dot q_k,p$ | — | Phase variables | The generalized coordinates, velocities, and conjugate momenta. |
| $t$ | $\unit{s}$ | Time | The independent variable for the motion. |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | The mass of the bead. |
| $\mechanical{r}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Radial coordinate | The distance from the rotation axis. |
| $\mechanical{\dot r}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Radial velocity | The rate of change of radial position. |
| $\mechanical{\omega}$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Angular velocity | The constant rotation rate of the rail. |
