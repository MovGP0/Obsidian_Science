---
title: "Energy, Momentum, and Angular Momentum as Conserved Charges"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Spatial translations, rotations, and time translations give momentum, angular momentum, and energy conservation.

**Momentum from translation.** Under $\mechanical{\vec r}_i\to\mechanical{\vec r}_i+\varepsilon\hat n$, the generator is $\hat n$ for every particle. For exact invariance, the boundary term is zero. The Noether charge is

$$
Q=\sum_i\frac{\partial\energy{L}}{\partial\mechanical{\dot{\vec r}}_i}\cdot\hat n
=\mechanical{\vec P}\cdot\hat n.
$$

The momentum component along $\hat n$ is conserved.

**Angular momentum from rotation.** For $\mechanical{\vec r}_i\to\mechanical{\vec r}_i+\varepsilon\hat n\times\mechanical{\vec r}_i$, the generator is $\hat n\times\mechanical{\vec r}_i$. The charge is

$$
Q=\sum_i\frac{\partial\energy{L}}{\partial\mechanical{\dot{\vec r}}_i}
\cdot(\hat n\times\mechanical{\vec r}_i)=\hat n\cdot\mechanical{\vec L}.
$$

The angular momentum component about the axis is conserved.

**Energy from time translation.** Translating $t\to t+\varepsilon$ changes the integration limits. The conserved quantity for time translation is the **energy function**.

$$
\energy{E}=\sum_k\dot q_k\frac{\partial\energy{L}}{\partial\dot q_k}-\energy{L}.
$$

With time-independent coordinates and constraints and $\energy{L}=\energy{T}-\energy{U}$, where $\energy{T}$ is homogeneous quadratic in velocity and $\energy{U}$ is velocity independent, $\energy{E}=\energy{T}+\energy{U}$.

| Symmetry | Conserved quantity |
| --- | --- |
| Spatial translation | Total momentum $\mechanical{\vec P}$ |
| Rotation | Total angular momentum $\mechanical{\vec L}$ |
| Time translation | Energy function $\energy{E}$ |

### Example — Particle in a Uniform Gravitational Field

For $\energy{L}=\tfrac12\mechanical{m}\lVert\mechanical{\dot{\vec r}}\rVert^2-\mechanical{m}\mechanical{g}\mechanical{z}$, horizontal translations leave the Lagrangian invariant, so $\mechanical{p}_x,\mechanical{p}_y$ are constant. A vertical translation changes the potential term, and $\mechanical{p}_z$ is not conserved. Rotations about the vertical axis conserve $\mechanical{L}_z$. The Lagrangian has no explicit time dependence, so $\energy{E}$ is conserved.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{\vec r}_i$ | $\unit{m}$ | Position | The position of particle $i$. |
| $\mechanical{\dot{\vec r}}_i$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Particle velocity | The time derivative of position. |
| $\energy{L}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Lagrangian | The function invariant under the stated symmetry. |
| $\mechanical{\vec P}$ | $\unit{kg\,m\,s^{-1}}$ | Total momentum | The sum of canonical momenta of the particles. |
| $\varepsilon$ | — | Transformation parameter | The displacement, rotation angle, or time shift for the symmetry. |
| $Q$ | — | Noether charge | The conserved quantity associated with the stated symmetry. |
| $\mechanical{\vec L},\mechanical{L}_z$ | $\unit{kg\,m^2\,s^{-1}}$ | Angular momentum | The total angular momentum and its vertical component. |
| $\energy{E},\energy{T},\energy{U}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Energy | The conserved energy function and the kinetic and potential terms. |
| $q_k,\dot q_k$ | — | Generalized coordinate and velocity | The configuration parameter and its time derivative. |
| $t$ | $\unit{s}$ | Time | The variable shifted in a time translation. |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | The mass of the particle. |
| $\mechanical{g}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-2}}$ | Acceleration due to gravity | The magnitude of the downward acceleration in free fall. |
| $\mechanical{z}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Height | The vertical position in the uniform field. |
| $\mechanical{p}_x,\mechanical{p}_y,\mechanical{p}_z$ | $\unit{kg\,m\,s^{-1}}$ | Momentum components | The horizontal and vertical components in Cartesian coordinates. |
