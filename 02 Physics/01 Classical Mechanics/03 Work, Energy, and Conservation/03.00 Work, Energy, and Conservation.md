title: "Work, Energy, and Conservation"

> [!TLDR]
> Work relates force to energy. In many problems, energy conservation gives information about motion directly.

Newton's laws determine motion through a second-order differential equation. In many problems, conserved quantities such as energy give enough information to describe the motion without integrating that equation. Work and energy have a mathematical structure that also underlies the Lagrangian formulation, in which energy plays a central role.

## Chapter Summary

- [[03.02 Work Done by a Force|Work]] is the scalar $\energy{W}=\int\mechanical{\vec F}\cdot d\mechanical{\vec r}$, measured in joules.
- [[03.03 Kinetic Energy and the Work–Energy Theorem|Kinetic energy]] is $\energy{K}=\tfrac12\mechanical{m}\mechanical{v}^2$. The work–energy theorem gives $\energy{W}=\Delta\energy{K}$ for the net force.
- [[03.04 Conservative Forces and Potential Energy|Conservative forces]] have a potential $\energy{U}$ with $\mechanical{\vec F}=-\nabla\energy{U}$ and satisfy $\nabla\times\mechanical{\vec F}=\vec 0$ where differentiable.
- [[03.05 Conservation of Mechanical Energy|Mechanical energy]] $\energy{E}=\energy{K}+\energy{U}$ is conserved in the absence of nonconservative forces.
- [[03.06 Power|Power]] is $\energy{P}=\mechanical{\vec F}\cdot\mechanical{\vec v}$.
- [[03.08 Energy Diagrams and Qualitative Analysis of Motion|Energy diagrams]] identify accessible regions, turning points, and stable and unstable equilibria.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\energy{W}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Work | The integral of force along the path. |
| $\mechanical{\vec F}$ | $\unit{N}$ | Force | The force acting on the particle. |
| $\mechanical{\vec r}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Position vector | The position of the particle on its path. |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | The mass of the particle. |
| $\mechanical{v},\mechanical{\vec v}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Speed and velocity | The magnitude and vector rate of change of position. |
| $\energy{K},\Delta\energy{K}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Kinetic energy | The energy of motion and its change. |
| $\energy{U}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Potential energy | The energy associated with position in a conservative field. |
| $\energy{E}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Mechanical energy | The sum of kinetic and potential energies. |
| $\energy{P}$ | $\unit{W}$ | Power | The rate of doing work. |
