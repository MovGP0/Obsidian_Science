---
title: "Work and Energy Exercises"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Use work and energy balances to solve problems involving forces, motion, and dissipation.

## Exercise 1

**Basic.** Calculate the work of the force $\mechanical{F}(\mechanical{x})=3\mechanical{x}^2-2\mechanical{x}$, given in newtons for $\mechanical{x}$ in metres, from $\mechanical{x}_A=0$ to $\mechanical{x}_B=\quantity{4}{m}$.

**Result.** $\energy{W}=(64-16)\unit{J}=\quantity{48}{J}$.

## Exercise 2

**Basic.** A mass $\mechanical{m}=\quantity{2}{kg}$ falls from height $\mechanical{h}=\quantity{5}{m}$. Use energy to find the impact speed.

**Result.** $\mechanical{v}=\sqrt{2\mechanical{g}\mechanical{h}}\approx\quantity{9.90}{m\,s^{-1}}$.

## Exercise 3

**Basic.** A ball starts from rest at the top of a smooth incline of height $\mechanical{h}=\quantity{2}{m}$. Find the speed at the bottom.

**Result.** $\mechanical{v}\approx\quantity{6.26}{m\,s^{-1}}$.

## Exercise 4

**Basic.** In suitable units, an object has potential $\energy{U}(\mechanical{x})=\mechanical{x}^4-4\mechanical{x}^2$. Find and classify the equilibria.

**Result.** $\mechanical{x}=0$ is an unstable local maximum. $\mechanical{x}=\pm\sqrt 2$ are stable minima.

## Exercise 5

**Intermediate.** A block of mass $\mechanical{m}=\quantity{1}{kg}$ slides down an incline of height $\mechanical{h}=\quantity{3}{m}$, angle $\mechanical{\alpha}=30\unit{{}^\circ}$, and coefficient of kinetic friction $\mechanical{\mu}_d=0.1$. Find its speed at the bottom.

**Result.** $\mechanical{v}=\sqrt{2\mechanical{g}\mechanical{h}(1-\mechanical{\mu}_d\cot\mechanical{\alpha})}\approx\quantity{6.98}{m\,s^{-1}}$.

## Exercise 6

**Intermediate.** A car of mass $\mechanical{m}=\quantity{1200}{kg}$ brakes from $\mechanical{v}=\quantity{30}{m\,s^{-1}}$ to rest over $\quantity{50}{m}$. Calculate the average braking force and the dissipated energy.

**Result.** The signed force component along the initial direction of motion is $\mechanical{F}=\quantity{-10800}{N}$. The energy dissipated is $\Delta\energy{E}=\quantity{5.4\times10^5}{J}$.

## Exercise 7

**Intermediate.** In the source's scaled coordinates, verify that $\mechanical{\vec F}=(\mechanical{y},\mechanical{x},0)$ is conservative. Find its potential.

**Result.** $\nabla\times\mechanical{\vec F}=\vec 0$ and $\energy{U}(\mechanical{x},\mechanical{y})=-\mechanical{x}\mechanical{y}$.

## Exercise 8

**Intermediate.** Calculate the average power of a motor that lifts a mass $\mechanical{m}=\quantity{100}{kg}$ through height $\mechanical{h}=\quantity{20}{m}$ in $\Delta t=\quantity{10}{s}$.

**Result.** $\energy{P}=\mechanical{m}\mechanical{g}\mechanical{h}/\Delta t\approx\quantity{1962}{W}\approx\quantity{2.6}{hp}$.

## Exercise 9

**Challenge.** For the Morse potential $\energy{U}(\mechanical{x})=\energy{U}_0(1-e^{-\mechanical{x}/\mechanical{a}})^2$, find the equilibrium position, small-oscillation angular frequency, and dissociation energy.

**Result.** $\mechanical{x}_0=0$, $\signal{\omega}=\sqrt{2\energy{U}_0/(\mechanical{m}\mechanical{a}^2)}$, and $\energy{E}_{\mathrm{diss}}=\energy{U}_0$.

## Exercise 10

**Challenge.** A particle moves in $\energy{U}(\mechanical{x})=-A/\mechanical{x}+B/\mechanical{x}^2$ with $A,B>0$. Find the equilibrium points and describe the motion qualitatively for negative energies.

## Exercise 11

**Intermediate.** A ball of mass $\mechanical{m}=\quantity{0.2}{kg}$ compresses a horizontal spring by $\Delta\mechanical{x}=\quantity{0.05}{m}$. The spring constant is $\mechanical{k}=\quantity{800}{N\,m^{-1}}$. On release the ball separates from the spring and moves across a horizontal surface with $\mechanical{\mu}_d=0.3$. Find the distance it travels.

**Result.** The spring stores $\energy{E}_{\mathrm{spring}}=\tfrac12\mechanical{k}(\Delta\mechanical{x})^2=\quantity{1}{J}$. The magnitude of the work of friction is $\energy{W}_{\mathrm{fr}}=\mechanical{\mu}_d\mechanical{m}\mechanical{g}\mechanical{d}$. Thus $\mechanical{d}\approx\quantity{1.70}{m}$.

## Exercise 12

**Intermediate.** In the source's scaled coordinates, determine whether $\mechanical{\vec F}=(\mechanical{x}\mechanical{y},\mechanical{x}^2/2,0)$ is conservative. If so, find the potential.

**Result.** $\nabla\times\mechanical{\vec F}=(0,0,\mechanical{x}-\mechanical{x})=\vec 0$. The potential is $\energy{U}(\mechanical{x},\mechanical{y})=-\mechanical{x}^2\mechanical{y}/2$.

## Exercise 13

**Intermediate.** For $\energy{U}(\mechanical{r})=-\alpha/\mechanical{r}+\beta/\mechanical{r}^2$ with $\alpha,\beta>0$ and angular momentum $\mechanical{L}$, find the equilibrium radius of a circular orbit and the frequency of small radial oscillations.

**Result.** With $\beta'=\beta+\mechanical{L}^2/(2\mechanical{m})$, the radius is $\mechanical{r}_0=2\beta'/\alpha$ and $\signal{\omega}^2=\energy{U}_{\mathrm{eff}}''(\mechanical{r}_0)/\mechanical{m}$.

## Exercise 14

**Basic.** A ball of mass $\mechanical{m}=\quantity{0.5}{kg}$ slides on a smooth rod of length $\mechanical{L}=\quantity{2}{m}$ inclined at $30\unit{{}^\circ}$. It starts from rest. Find its speed at the lower end.

**Result.** The vertical drop is $\mechanical{h}=\mechanical{L}\sin(30\unit{{}^\circ})=\quantity{1}{m}$. Thus $\mechanical{v}=\sqrt{2\mechanical{g}\mechanical{h}}\approx\quantity{4.43}{m\,s^{-1}}$.

## Exercise 15

**Challenge.** For a satellite in an elliptical orbit, show that $\langle\energy{K}\rangle=-\energy{E}$ and $\langle\energy{U}\rangle=2\energy{E}$, where the averages are over one orbit. Use the virial theorem for $\energy{U}=-\alpha/\mechanical{r}$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{F},\mechanical{\vec F}$ | $\unit{N}$ | Force | The signed force along the path or the force vector. |
| $\mechanical{x},\mechanical{y},\mechanical{x}_A,\mechanical{x}_B,\mechanical{x}_0$ | $\unit{m}$ | Position | The coordinates and their values at the endpoints or equilibrium. |
| $\energy{W}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Work | The integral of the force over displacement. |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | The mass of the body in each exercise. |
| $\mechanical{h}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Height | The vertical distance through which the body moves. |
| $\mechanical{v}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Speed | The speed of the moving body at the stated point. |
| $\mechanical{g}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-2}}$ | Acceleration due to gravity | The magnitude of the downward acceleration in free fall. |
| $\energy{U},\energy{U}_0$ | $\unit{J}$ | Potential energy and scale | The potential energy and its limiting value in the Morse model. |
| $\mechanical{\alpha}$ | $\unit{rad}$ | Inclination | The angle of the plane above the horizontal. |
| $\mechanical{\mu}_d$ | 1 | Coefficient of kinetic friction | The ratio of the kinetic friction magnitude to the normal force. |
| — | $\unit{m}$ | Braking distance | The distance the car travels before stopping. |
| $\Delta\energy{E}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Energy dissipated | The positive magnitude of the kinetic energy lost during braking. |
| $\Delta t$ | $\unit{s}$ | Duration | The time taken to lift the mass. |
| $\energy{P}$ | $\unit{W}$ | Average power | The rate of work averaged over the lifting interval. |
| $\mechanical{a}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Morse length scale | The length that controls the range of the Morse potential. |
| $\signal{\omega}$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Angular frequency | The angular frequency of small oscillations about equilibrium. |
| $\energy{E}_{\mathrm{diss}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Dissociation energy | The energy needed to reach infinite separation from the minimum. |
| $A,\alpha$ | $\unit{J\,m}$ | Attractive potential coefficient | The coefficient of the inverse-distance term. |
| $B,\beta,\beta'$ | $\unit{J\,m^2}$ | Inverse-square potential coefficient | The coefficient of the inverse-square term before or after including the angular momentum contribution. |
| $\Delta\mechanical{x}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Spring compression | The amount by which the ball compresses the spring. |
| $\mechanical{k}$ | $\unit{N\,m^{-1}}$ | Spring constant | The stiffness of the spring. |
| $\energy{E}_{\mathrm{spring}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Spring energy | The energy stored in the compressed spring. |
| $\energy{W}_{\mathrm{fr}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Friction loss | The positive magnitude of the work of friction. |
| $\mechanical{d}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Sliding distance | The distance traveled against friction. |
| $\mechanical{r},\mechanical{r}_0$ | $\unit{m}$ | Radial distance | The distance from the force center and its value at equilibrium. |
| $\mechanical{L}$ | $\unit{kg\,m^2\,s^{-1}}$ | Angular momentum | The conserved angular momentum in the central potential. |
| $\mechanical{L}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Rod length | The length of the smooth inclined rod. |
| $\energy{U}_{\mathrm{eff}}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Effective potential energy | The central potential energy plus the centrifugal barrier. |
| $\langle\energy{K}\rangle,\langle\energy{U}\rangle$ | $\unit{J}$ | Average energies | The time averages of the kinetic and potential energies over an orbit. |
| $\energy{E}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Orbital energy | The constant total mechanical energy of the satellite. |
