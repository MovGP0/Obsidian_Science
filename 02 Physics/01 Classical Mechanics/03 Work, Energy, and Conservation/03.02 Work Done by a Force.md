---
title: "Work Done by a Force"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Work is the line integral of force along the path of a body.

> [!definition] Definition 3.1 — Work Done by a Force
> A force $\mechanical{\vec F}$ acts on a point that moves along path $\gamma$ from $A$ to $B$. The **work** is
>
> $$
> \energy{W}_{A\to B}=\int_\gamma\mechanical{\vec F}\cdot d\mechanical{\vec r}
> =\int_A^B\mechanical{\vec F}\cdot d\mechanical{\vec r}.
> $$

Work is a scalar with unit $\unit{J}=\unit{N\,m}$. The force does positive work when it has a component in the direction of motion. It does negative work when that component opposes the motion.

**Constant force.** For constant $\mechanical{\vec F}$, $\energy{W}=\mechanical{\vec F}\cdot\mechanical{\vec d}$, where $\mechanical{\vec d}=\mechanical{\vec r}_B-\mechanical{\vec r}_A$ is the displacement. The work is independent of the path.

**Straight path.** For a path along the $\mechanical{x}$ axis with force component $\mechanical{F}_x(\mechanical{x})$, $\energy{W}=\int_{x_A}^{x_B}\mechanical{F}_x(\mechanical{x})\,dx$.

### Example — Work Done by Weight

Consider a displacement from $\mechanical{\vec r}_A$ to $\mechanical{\vec r}_B$ in a uniform gravitational field. The weight is $\mechanical{\vec F}=-\mechanical{m}\mechanical{g}\hat{\jmath}$, so

$$
\energy{W}=-\mechanical{m}\mechanical{g}(\mechanical{y}_B-\mechanical{y}_A)
=\mechanical{m}\mechanical{g}(\mechanical{y}_A-\mechanical{y}_B).
$$

The work depends only on the difference in height between the endpoints.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{\vec F}, \mechanical{F}_x$ | $\unit{N}$ | Force | The applied force vector or its component along the path. |
| $\energy{W}, \energy{W}_{A\to B}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Work | The energy transferred by the force along the path. |
| $\mechanical{\vec r}, \mechanical{\vec r}_A, \mechanical{\vec r}_B$ | $\unit{m}$ | Position vector | The position along the path or at either endpoint. |
| $\mechanical{\vec d}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Displacement | The final position vector minus the initial position vector. |
| $\mechanical{x}, x_A, x_B$ | $\unit{m}$ | Position coordinate | The position along a straight path and its endpoints. |
| $\mechanical{y}_A,\mechanical{y}_B$ | $\unit{m}$ | Height | The vertical positions of the endpoints. |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | The mass of the body on which gravity acts. |
| $\mechanical{g}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-2}}$ | Acceleration due to gravity | The magnitude of the downward acceleration in free fall. |
