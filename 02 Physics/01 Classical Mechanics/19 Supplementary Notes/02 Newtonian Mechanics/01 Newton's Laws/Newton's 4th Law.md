aliases:
  - Newton's Law of Universal Gravitation
---
Every point mass attracts every single other point mass by a force pointing along the line intersecting both points.

The force is directly proportional to the product of the two masses and inversely proportional to the square of the distance between the point masses.

$$F = \frac{G m_1 m_2}{r^2}$$

Where
  - $F$ is the gravitational force between the two masses.
  - $G$ is the gravitational constant ($G = 6.674 \times 10^{-11} \, \text{Nm}^2/\text{kg}^2$).
  - $m_1$ and $m_2$ are the two masses.
  - $r$ is the distance between the centers of the two masses.
