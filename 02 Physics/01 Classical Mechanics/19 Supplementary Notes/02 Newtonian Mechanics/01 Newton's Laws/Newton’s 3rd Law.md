> [!note] Description
> To every action, there is an equal and opposite reaction.

> [!note] Alternate Description
> Whenever a particle A exerts a force on another particle B, B simultaneously exerts a force on A with the same magnitude in the opposite direction.

The third law implies that internal forces cancel in pairs, so the total momentum of an isolated system is conserved (see [[Conservation of Linear Momentum]]).
