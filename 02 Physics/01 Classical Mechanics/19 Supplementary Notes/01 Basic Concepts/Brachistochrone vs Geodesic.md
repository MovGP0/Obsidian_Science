A **geodesic** is a curve on a curved surface with stationary length. Locally, it is the shortest path between two nearby points. A long geodesic is not always the shortest path, for example a great-circle arc longer than half a circle.

A **brachistochrone** is a curve between two points that minimizes the time it takes for a particle to slide without friction under the influence of gravity.
