| Relation / Name                    | Formula                                                                                 | Quantities Linked                             | Domain / Regime               | Notes                                                                |
| ---------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Mass–energy (rest energy)          | $E = mc^{2}$                                                                            | $E$, mass $m$, speed of light $c$             | Relativity                    | Energy equivalent of rest mass.                                      |
| Energy–momentum invariant          | $E^{2} = (pc)^{2} + (mc^{2})^{2}$                                                       | $E$, momentum $p$, $m$, $c$                   | Relativity                    | Massless case: $E = pc$.                                             |
| 4-momentum component               | $P^{\mu} = \left(\tfrac{E}{c}, \mathbf{p}\right)$                                       | $E$, 3-momentum $\mathbf{p}$                  | Relativity                    | Invariant $P^{\mu}P_{\mu} = m^{2}c^{2}$.                             |
| Stress–energy density              | $E = \displaystyle\int T^{00}\,d^{3}x$                                                  | $E$, stress–energy tensor $T^{\mu\nu}$        | Field theory (inertial frame) | Total (frame-dependent) energy from field densities.                 |
| Hamiltonian from Lagrangian        | $E \equiv H = \sum_i \dot q_i\,\frac{\partial L}{\partial \dot q_i} - L$                | $E$, generalized coords $q_i$, Lagrangian $L$ | Analytical mechanics          | Noether charge for time translations (when $L$ has no explicit $t$). |
| Hamilton–Jacobi (action–energy)    | $E = -\,\frac{\partial S}{\partial t}$                                                  | $E$, action $S$                               | Analytical mechanics          | Links energy to time derivative of action.                           |
| Planck–Einstein (energy–frequency) | $E = h\nu = \hbar\omega$                                                                | $E$, frequency $\nu$, angular freq. $\omega$  | Quantum, waves                | Fundamental quantum relation.                                        |
| de Broglie via wavelength          | $E = \sqrt{(hc/\lambda)^2 + (mc^{2})^{2}}$                                              | $E$, wavelength $\lambda$, $m$, $c$, $h$      | Relativistic quantum          | Photons: $E = hc/\lambda$.                                           |
| Massless wavevector form           | $E = \hbar c\,k$                                                                        | $E$, wavevector magnitude $k = 2\pi/\lambda$  | Waves, massless quanta        | Equivalent to $E = h\nu$.                                            |
| Time-evolution generator           | $i\hbar\,\partial_t \Psi = \hat H \Psi$                                                 | $E$ (via $\hat H$), state $\Psi$              | Quantum mechanics             | Energy is the generator of time translations.                        |
| Energy expectation                 | $E = \langle \hat H \rangle$                                                            | $E$, Hamiltonian operator $\hat H$            | Quantum mechanics             | Mean energy in a given state.                                        |
| Energy–time uncertainty            | $\Delta E\,\Delta t \gtrsim \hbar/2$                                                    | $\Delta E$, $\Delta t$, $\hbar$               | Quantum (operational)         | $t$ is not an operator; bound is context-dependent.                  |
| First law (thermo)                 | $dU = \delta Q - \delta W$                                                              | Internal energy $U$, heat $Q$, work $W$       | Thermodynamics                | Often $dU = T\,dS - p\,dV + \mu\,dN + \dots$.                        |
| Partition function link            | $U = -\partial_{\beta}\ln Z,\ \beta = 1/(k_B T)$                                        | $U$, $Z$, temperature $T$, $k_B$              | Stat. mechanics               | Canonical ensemble relation.                                         |
| Equipartition (classical)          | $\langle E\rangle = \tfrac{1}{2}k_B T$ per quadratic d.o.f.                             | $E$, $T$, $k_B$                               | High-T classical limit        | Breaks down at low $T$ / quantized spectra.                          |
| Blackbody energy density           | $u = aT^{4},\ a=\tfrac{\pi^{2}k_B^{4}}{15\hbar^{3}c^{3}}$                               | Energy density $u$, $T$, constants            | Radiation thermodynamics      | Stefan–Boltzmann law for energy density.                             |
| Landauer bound (info–energy)       | $E_{\min} = k_B T \ln 2$                                                                | $E$, $T$, $k_B$                               | Thermodynamics of information | Minimal heat dissipation per bit erased.                             |
| Electric potential energy          | $U = qV$                                                                                | $U$, charge $q$, potential $V$                | Electromagnetism              | Differential form $dU = q\,dV$.                                      |
| EM field energy                    | $U = \displaystyle\int \frac{\varepsilon_0 E^{2} + B^{2}/\mu_0}{2}\,d^{3}x$             | $U$, fields $\mathbf{E},\mathbf{B}$           | Electromagnetism              | Field energy density integrated over space.                          |
| Dipole energies                    | $U = -\,\mathbf{p}\!\cdot\!\mathbf{E},\quad U = -\,\boldsymbol{\mu}\!\cdot\!\mathbf{B}$ | $U$, dipoles, fields                          | Electromagnetism              | Electric dipole $\mathbf{p}$, magnetic moment $\boldsymbol{\mu}$.    |
| Work–energy theorem                | $\Delta E = W = \displaystyle\int \mathbf{F}\!\cdot\!d\mathbf{x}$                       | $E$, force $\mathbf{F}$, displacement         | Mechanics                     | Relates net work to change in mechanical energy.                     |
| Power definition                   | $P = dE/dt$                                                                             | Power $P$, energy $E$, time $t$               | General                       | Instantaneous rate of energy transfer.                               |
| Newtonian gravitational potential  | $U = -\dfrac{G m_1 m_2}{r}$                                                             | $U$, masses $m_1,m_2$, distance $r$           | Newtonian limit               | Useful low-velocity approximation.                                   |
| Dimensional analysis               | $[E] = M\,L^{2}\,T^{-2}$                                                                | Energy dimensions                             | General                       | Base SI units relation for energy.                                   |
