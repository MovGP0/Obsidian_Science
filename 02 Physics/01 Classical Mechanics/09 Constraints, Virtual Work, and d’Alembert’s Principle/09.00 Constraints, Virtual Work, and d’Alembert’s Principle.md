> [!TLDR]
> Analytical mechanics uses coordinates adapted to the geometry and formulates equations that account for ideal constraints.

The earlier chapters used vector mechanics. They wrote Newton's equations in Cartesian components and added equations that constrained the coordinates. This approach becomes difficult when a system has many constraints or uses non-Cartesian coordinates. Analytical mechanics introduces *generalized coordinates* adapted to the geometry. Its equations of motion can account for ideal constraints directly.

## Chapter Summary

- [[09.02 Holonomic and Nonholonomic Constraints|Holonomic constraints]] take the form $f(\mechanical{\vec r},t)=0$. Nonintegrable velocity constraints are nonholonomic.
- For $N$ particles and $K$ independent holonomic constraints, there are $n=3N-K$ [[09.03 Generalized Coordinates and Degrees of Freedom|degrees of freedom]], described by coordinates $q_k$.
- A [[09.04 Virtual Displacements|virtual displacement]] $\delta\mechanical{\vec r}$ is compatible with the constraints at fixed time.
- The [[09.05 Principle of Virtual Work|principle of virtual work]] gives $\sum_i\mechanical{\vec F}_i\cdot\delta\mechanical{\vec r}_i=0$ in equilibrium. Ideal reactions do zero virtual work.
- [[09.06 d’Alembert’s Equation in Symbolic Form|d’Alembert’s equation]] gives $\sum_i(\mechanical{\vec F}_i-\mechanical{m}_i\mechanical{\vec a}_i)\cdot\delta\mechanical{\vec r}_i=0$.
- The [[09.07 Generalized Forces|generalized forces]] are $Q_k=\sum_i\mechanical{\vec F}_i\cdot\partial\mechanical{\vec r}_i/\partial q_k$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{\vec r},\mechanical{\vec r}_i$ | $\unit{m}$ | Position | The configuration or particle position. |
| $t$ | $\unit{s}$ | Time | The independent variable in the constraint. |
| $q_k$ | — | Generalized coordinate | An independent configuration parameter. |
| $\delta\mechanical{\vec r},\delta\mechanical{\vec r}_i$ | $\unit{m}$ | Virtual displacement | A displacement compatible with the constraints at fixed time. |
| $\mechanical{\vec F}_i$ | $\unit{N}$ | Active force | The applied force on particle $i$. |
| $\mechanical{m}_i$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | The mass of particle $i$. |
| $\mechanical{\vec a}_i$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-2}}$ | Acceleration | The actual acceleration of particle $i$. |
| $Q_k$ | — | Generalized force | The coefficient of the coordinate variation in the virtual work. |
