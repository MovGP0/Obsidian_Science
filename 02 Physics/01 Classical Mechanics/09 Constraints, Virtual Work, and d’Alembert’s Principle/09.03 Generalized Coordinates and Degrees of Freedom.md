---
title: "Generalized Coordinates and Degrees of Freedom"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Independent holonomic constraints reduce the number of coordinates needed to specify a configuration.

> [!definition] Definition 9.2 — Degrees of Freedom and Generalized Coordinates
> For $N$ points in three dimensions subject to $K$ independent holonomic constraints, the number of degrees of freedom is $n=3N-K$. The **generalized coordinates** $q_1,\ldots,q_n$ are independent parameters such that
>
> $$
> \mechanical{\vec r}_i=\mechanical{\vec r}_i(q_1,\ldots,q_n,t)
> $$
>
> for each point.

**Examples.**

- **Planar simple pendulum.** $N=1$, $K=2$ for the plane and fixed cord length, and $n=1$. Use $q=\mechanical{\theta}$.
- **Planar double pendulum.** $N=2$, $K=4$ for the two planar restrictions and two fixed lengths, and $n=2$. Use $q_1=\mechanical{\theta}_1$ and $q_2=\mechanical{\theta}_2$.
- **Particle on a surface.** With $\mechanical{z}=f(\mechanical{x},\mechanical{y})$, $N=1$, $K=1$, and $n=2$. Use $q_1=\mechanical{x}$ and $q_2=\mechanical{y}$.
- **Free rigid body.** $n=6$: three translations of the center of mass and three rotations.

**Choice of coordinates.** Generalized coordinates are not unique. Locally, the pendulum can use $\mechanical{\theta}$ or $\mechanical{x}=\mechanical{\ell}\sin\mechanical{\theta}$ where that coordinate determines the configuration. Choose coordinates that reflect the symmetry of the problem.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{\vec r}_i$ | $\unit{m}$ | Position vector | The Cartesian position of particle $i$. |
| $q_1,\ldots,q_n$ | — | Generalized coordinates | The independent parameters that specify a configuration. |
| $t$ | $\unit{s}$ | Time | The explicit time argument for a moving constraint. |
| $\mechanical{\theta},\mechanical{\theta}_1,\mechanical{\theta}_2$ | $\unit{rad}$ | Pendulum angles | The angles used as generalized coordinates. |
| $\mechanical{x},\mechanical{y},\mechanical{z}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Cartesian coordinates | The coordinates of a particle on the surface. |
| $\mechanical{\ell}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Pendulum length | The distance from the pivot to the bob. |
