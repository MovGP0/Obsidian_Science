> [!TLDR]
> Choose coordinates that satisfy the constraints and use symmetries to reduce the equations of motion.

## Spherical Pendulum

A mass $\mechanical{m}$ hangs from a cord of length $\mechanical{\ell}$ and can move on a sphere. The constraint $\lVert\mechanical{\vec r}\rVert=\mechanical{\ell}$ leaves two coordinates $(\mechanical{\theta},\mechanical{\phi})$, with $\mechanical{\theta}$ measured from the downward vertical. The squared speed is $\mechanical{v}^2=\mechanical{\ell}^2(\mechanical{\dot\theta}^{\,2}+\sin^2\mechanical{\theta}\,\mechanical{\dot\phi}^{\,2})$. The Lagrangian is

$$
\energy{L}=\tfrac12\mechanical{m}\mechanical{\ell}^2
(\mechanical{\dot\theta}^{\,2}+\sin^2\mechanical{\theta}\,\mechanical{\dot\phi}^{\,2})
+\mechanical{m}\mechanical{g}\mechanical{\ell}\cos\mechanical{\theta}.
$$

The angle $\mechanical{\phi}$ is cyclic, so $\mechanical{p}_\phi=\mechanical{m}\mechanical{\ell}^2\sin^2\mechanical{\theta}\,\mechanical{\dot\phi}=\mechanical{L}_z$ is constant. The other equation is

$$
\mechanical{\ddot\theta}=\sin\mechanical{\theta}\cos\mechanical{\theta}\,\mechanical{\dot\phi}^{\,2}
-\frac{\mechanical{g}}{\mechanical{\ell}}\sin\mechanical{\theta}.
$$

In the conical case with nonzero constant $\mechanical{\theta}$, $\mechanical{\dot\phi}^{\,2}=\mechanical{g}/(\mechanical{\ell}\cos\mechanical{\theta})$.

## Particle on a General Surface

A particle is constrained by $\mechanical{z}-f(\mechanical{x},\mechanical{y})=0$. Use the two independent coordinates $q_1=\mechanical{x}$ and $q_2=\mechanical{y}$. With $f_x$ and $f_y$ the partial derivatives of $f$, $\mechanical{\dot z}=f_x\mechanical{\dot x}+f_y\mechanical{\dot y}$. The kinetic energy is a quadratic form.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\energy{T}&=\tfrac12\mechanical{m}(\mechanical{\dot x}^2+\mechanical{\dot y}^2+\mechanical{\dot z}^2)\\
&=\tfrac12\mechanical{m}\bigl[(1+f_x^2)\mechanical{\dot x}^2+
2f_xf_y\mechanical{\dot x}\mechanical{\dot y}+(1+f_y^2)\mechanical{\dot y}^2\bigr].
\end{aligned}
$$

The potential energy is $\energy{U}=\mechanical{m}\mechanical{g}\mechanical{z}=\mechanical{m}\mechanical{g}f(\mechanical{x},\mechanical{y})$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | The mass of the pendulum bob. |
| $\mechanical{\ell},\mechanical{\vec r}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Length and position | The cord length and the bob's position relative to its pivot. |
| $\mechanical{\theta},\mechanical{\phi}$ | $\unit{rad}$ | Pendulum angles | The polar angle and azimuth. |
| $\mechanical{\dot\theta},\mechanical{\dot\phi}$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Angular velocities | The rates of change of the two angles. |
| $\mechanical{\ddot\theta}$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-2}}$ | Polar angular acceleration | The second time derivative of the polar angle. |
| $\mechanical{v}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Speed | The speed of the pendulum bob. |
| $\energy{L}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Lagrangian | Kinetic energy minus potential energy for the pendulum. |
| $\mechanical{g}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-2}}$ | Acceleration due to gravity | The magnitude of the downward acceleration in free fall. |
| $\mechanical{p}_\phi,\mechanical{L}_z$ | $\unit{kg\,m^2\,s^{-1}}$ | Axial angular momentum | The conserved momentum conjugate to azimuth. |
| $\mechanical{x},\mechanical{y},\mechanical{z},q_1,q_2$ | $\unit{m}$ | Surface coordinates | The Cartesian coordinates and the two independent coordinates on the surface. |
| $\mechanical{\dot x},\mechanical{\dot y},\mechanical{\dot z}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Cartesian velocity | The velocity components of the constrained particle. |
| $\energy{T},\energy{U}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Kinetic and potential energy | The energy of motion and the gravitational energy. |
