---
title: "Systems with Rheonomic Constraints"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Time-dependent constraints can make the Hamiltonian differ from the total mechanical energy.

For a time-dependent constraint $f(\mechanical{\vec r},t)=0$, the particle velocities include a term from explicit time dependence of the position. In the kinetic energy, $\partial\mechanical{\vec r}_i/\partial t$ produces a term $\energy{T}_1$ linear in $\dot q$ and a term $\energy{T}_0$ independent of $\dot q$. The Hamiltonian generally differs from the total mechanical energy.

### Example — Bead on a Rotating Straight Guide

A bead slides without friction along a rigid guide through the origin. The guide rotates in the $xy$ plane at constant angular speed $\mechanical{\omega}$. The imposed angle $\mechanical{\theta}=\mechanical{\omega}t$ is rheonomic. The free coordinate is the distance $\mechanical{r}$ from the origin. With gravity neglected, $\energy{T}=\tfrac12\mechanical{m}(\mechanical{\dot r}^{\,2}+\mechanical{r}^2\mechanical{\omega}^2)$ and $\energy{U}=0$. The Hamiltonian is

$$
\energy{H}=\frac{\mechanical{p}_r^2}{2\mechanical{m}}-\tfrac12\mechanical{m}\mechanical{\omega}^2\mechanical{r}^2
\ne\energy{T}+\energy{U}.
$$

The negative quadratic term is an effective centrifugal potential from the imposed rotation.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{\vec r},\mechanical{\vec r}_i,\mechanical{r}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Position | The Cartesian position or the bead's distance from the origin. |
| $t$ | $\unit{s}$ | Time | The independent variable in the constraint. |
| $\dot q$ | — | Generalized velocity | The time derivative of a generalized coordinate. |
| $\energy{T},\energy{T}_1,\energy{T}_0$ | $\unit{J}$ | Kinetic energy | The total kinetic energy and its terms of degrees one and zero in generalized velocity. |
| $\mechanical{\omega}$ | $\unit{rad\,s^{-1}}$ | Angular speed | The imposed rotation rate of the guide. |
| $\mechanical{\theta}$ | $\unit{rad}$ | Guide angle | The imposed orientation in the plane. |
| $\mechanical{\dot r}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Radial speed | The signed rate of change of the bead's radial position. |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | The mass of the bead. |
| $\energy{U},\energy{H}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Potential energy and Hamiltonian | The physical potential energy and the reduced Hamiltonian. |
| $\mechanical{p}_r$ | $\unit{kg\,m\,s^{-1}}$ | Radial momentum | The momentum conjugate to the radial coordinate. |
