---
title: "Holonomic Constraints in Differential Form"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Differentiate a holonomic constraint at fixed time to obtain a linear condition on virtual displacements.

A holonomic constraint $f(\mechanical{\vec r},t)=0$ requires

$$
\sum_i\frac{\partial f}{\partial\mechanical{\vec r}_i}\cdot\delta\mechanical{\vec r}_i=0.
$$

For ideal reactions, this gives the multiplier representation $\mechanical{\vec\Phi}_i=\lambda\,\partial f/\partial\mechanical{\vec r}_i$ for one independent constraint.

For a nonintegrable constraint $\sum_i\vec a_i\cdot d\mechanical{\vec r}_i+a_t\,dt=0$, admissible virtual displacements satisfy $\sum_i\vec a_i\cdot\delta\mechanical{\vec r}_i=0$. This restricts the variations in d’Alembert’s equation.

> [!note] Analytical Mechanics
> Generalized coordinates are central to analytical mechanics. Select $n$ coordinates adapted to the geometry. They let you formulate the equations of motion in Lagrangian or Hamiltonian form. A good choice can simplify the problem substantially.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{\vec r},\mechanical{\vec r}_i$ | $\unit{m}$ | Position | The system configuration or the position of particle $i$. |
| $\delta\mechanical{\vec r}_i,d\mechanical{\vec r}_i$ | $\unit{m}$ | Displacement | The virtual or actual change in particle position. |
| $t,dt$ | $\unit{s}$ | Time | The time coordinate and its differential. |
| $\mechanical{\vec\Phi}_i$ | $\unit{N}$ | Constraint reaction | The force exerted by the constraint on particle $i$. |
| $\lambda$ | — | Lagrange multiplier | The scalar that converts a constraint gradient into a force. |
