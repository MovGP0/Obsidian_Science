---
title: "Continuity Equation"
---

> [!TLDR]
> Conservation of mass requires the same mass flow rate across every section of a steady streamtube.

For steady flow, mass conservation gives the **continuity equation**. For a streamtube with cross-sectional areas $\mechanical{A}_1$ and $\mechanical{A}_2$ and section speeds $\mechanical{v}_1$ and $\mechanical{v}_2$,

$$
\mechanical{\rho}_1\mechanical{A}_1\mechanical{v}_1
=\mechanical{\rho}_2\mechanical{A}_2\mechanical{v}_2.
$$

For an incompressible liquid of constant density, $\mechanical{A}_1\mechanical{v}_1=\mechanical{A}_2\mechanical{v}_2$. The volume flow rate $\mechanical{Q}=\mechanical{A}\mechanical{v}$ is constant along the tube.

### Example — Pipe Constriction

Water flows through a pipe of area $\mechanical{A}_1=\quantity{4}{cm^2}$ at speed $\mechanical{v}_1=\quantity{1}{m\,s^{-1}}$. In a constriction of area $\mechanical{A}_2=\quantity{1}{cm^2}$, $\mechanical{v}_2=\mechanical{v}_1\mechanical{A}_1/\mechanical{A}_2=\quantity{4}{m\,s^{-1}}$. The speed increases by a factor of four.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{\rho}_1,\mechanical{\rho}_2$ | $\unit{kg\,m^{-3}}$ | Mass density | The densities at the two sections. |
| $\mechanical{A}_1,\mechanical{A}_2$ | $\unit{m^2}$ | Cross-sectional area | The areas of the two streamtube sections. |
| $\mechanical{v}_1,\mechanical{v}_2$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Flow speed | The speeds through the two sections. |
| $\mechanical{A},\mechanical{v}$ | $\unit{m^2},\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Local area and speed | The cross-sectional area and the mean speed at a given section. |
| $\mechanical{Q}$ | $\unit{m^3\,s^{-1}}$ | Volume flow rate | The volume passing through a section per unit time. |
