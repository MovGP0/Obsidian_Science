> [!TLDR]
> Compute inertia tensors, transform electromagnetic fields, and contract the curvature tensor of a sphere.

**6.1** Place eight identical masses at the vertices of a cube. Take one corner as the origin and align the coordinate axes with the edges. Find the inertia tensor.

**6.2** How does the tensor from Problem 6.1 change if you remove one mass?

**6.3** For the masses in Problem 6.2, rotate the coordinate axes by 20° about one of the axes. Find the new coordinates of the masses and use them to compute the inertia tensor.

**6.4** Use a similarity transformation to verify the inertia tensor in Problem 6.3.

**6.5** Derive the vector wave equation by taking the curl of Faraday’s law and substituting the curl of the magnetic field from the Ampère–Maxwell law.

**6.6** An observer measures an electric field of $\quantity{5}{V\,m^{-1}}$ along the $z$-axis and zero magnetic field. What fields does a second observer moving at $c/4$ along the positive $x$-axis measure?

**6.7** An observer measures a magnetic field of $\quantity{1.5}{T}$ along the $z$-axis and zero electric field. What fields does a second observer moving at $c/4$ along the positive $x$-axis measure?

**6.8** Show that $\electric{\vec E}\cdot\magnetic{\vec B}$ is invariant under a Lorentz transformation.

**6.9** In the two-dimensional Euclidean plane, the polar line element is $ds^2=d\mechanical{r}^2+\mechanical{r}^2d\theta^2$. Show that the Riemann tensor vanishes.

**6.10** Find the Ricci tensor and scalar of the two-dimensional sphere in [[6.3 The Riemann curvature tensor|Section 6.3]].

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | Mass at each vertex. |
| $\mechanical{\mathbf I}$ | $\unit{kg\,m^2}$ | Inertia tensor | Maps angular velocity to angular momentum. |
| $\theta$ | $\unit{rad}$ | Rotation angle | Angle through which the axes rotate. |
| $\electric{\vec E}$ | $\unit{V\,m^{-1}}$ | Electric field | Field measured in an inertial frame. |
| $\magnetic{\vec B}$ | $\unit{T}$ | Magnetic flux density | Field measured in an inertial frame. |
| $c$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Speed of light | Vacuum propagation speed. |
| $R$ | $\unit{m^{-2}}$ | Ricci scalar | Scalar contraction of the curvature tensor. |
| $\mechanical{r}$, $ds$ | $\unit{m}$ | Radial coordinate and line element | Polar radius and infinitesimal distance in the plane. |
