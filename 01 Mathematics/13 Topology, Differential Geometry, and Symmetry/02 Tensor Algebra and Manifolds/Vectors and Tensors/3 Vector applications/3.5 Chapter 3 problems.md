> [!TLDR]
> Apply vector operations to motion, forces, and electric and magnetic fields.

### 3.1 Frictionless ramp with horizontal and vertical axes

Find the acceleration of the box on the frictionless ramp using Cartesian axes with $x$ horizontal to the right and $y$ vertically upward.

### 3.2 Static friction

The maximum static friction is $\mechanical{\mu}_s|\mechanical{\vec F}_n|$. Find the minimum coefficient $\mechanical{\mu}_s$ that keeps a box of mass $\mechanical{m}$ at rest on a ramp with an incline of $20^\circ$.

### 3.3 Pushing a box uphill

A worker pushes a box of mass $\mechanical{m}$ up a $\quantity{2}{m}$ ramp with a force of $\quantity{10}{N}$. The incline is $25^\circ$ and the coefficient of kinetic friction is $0.33$. Find the speed at the top for a push parallel to the ramp and a box that starts from rest and reaches the top.

### 3.4 Centripetal force on a hammer

A hammer thrower swings a $\quantity{7.26}{kg}$ hammer on a $\quantity{1.22}{m}$ cable. Find the centripetal force needed at a speed of $\quantity{22}{m\,s^{-1}}$.

### 3.5 Acceleration through a turn

A Formula 1 car travels on a curve of radius $\quantity{10}{m}$ and slows uniformly from $\quantity{180}{mi\,h^{-1}}$ to $\quantity{120}{mi\,h^{-1}}$ in $\quantity{2}{s}$. Find the magnitude and direction of its acceleration when the speed is $\quantity{150}{mi\,h^{-1}}$.

### 3.6 Electric field and force on an electron

Use the three charges $\electric{q}_1$, $\electric{q}_2$, and $\electric{q}_3$ in [[3.3 The electric field|Fig. 3.18]] to find the electric field at $(\mechanical{x},\mechanical{y})=(0,0)$. Then find the electric force on an electron at the origin.

### 3.7 Charge density from the field

The electric field in spherical coordinates is

$$
\electric{\vec E}
=\left[\frac5{\mechanical r}\hat r
+\frac2{\mechanical r}\sin\theta\cos\phi\,\hat\theta
-\frac1{\mechanical r}\sin\theta\cos\phi\,\hat\phi\right]\unit{N\,C^{-1}},
$$

with $\mechanical r$ evaluated numerically in meters. Find the volume charge density $\electric{\rho}$.

### 3.8 Electric field from potential

The electric potential in cylindrical coordinates is $\electric V(\mechanical r,\phi,\mechanical z)=\mechanical r^2\sin\phi\,\exp(-3/\mechanical z)$, with coordinates evaluated numerically in meters and potential in volts. Find $\electric{\vec E}$.

### 3.9 Charge density from potential

Use Poisson's equation and the potential in Problem 3.8 to find $\electric{\rho}$.

### 3.10 Magnetic force

A particle has charge $\electric q=\quantity{-4}{nC}$ and velocity $\mechanical{\vec v}=(2.5\times10^4\hat i+1.1\times10^4\hat j)\,\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$. Find the magnitude and direction of the magnetic force when $\magnetic{\vec B}=(1.2\times10^{-3}\hat i+5.6\times10^{-3}\hat j-3.2\times10^{-3}\hat k)\,\unit{T}$.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{\mu}_s$ | 1 | Coefficient of static friction | The maximum static friction divided by the normal force magnitude. |
| $\mechanical{\vec F}_n$ | $\unit{N}$ | Normal force | The force perpendicular to the ramp. |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | The mass of the box. |
| $\mechanical{x}$, $\mechanical{y}$, $\mechanical{z}$, $\mechanical{r}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Position coordinates | Cartesian, radial, or axial positions. |
| $\theta$, $\phi$ | $\unit{rad}$ | Angular coordinates | The zenith angle and azimuth in the field expressions. |
| $\electric q$, $\electric{q}_1$, $\electric{q}_2$, $\electric{q}_3$ | $\unit{C}$ | Electric charge | The charges used in the field and force problems. |
| $\electric{\vec E}$ | $\unit{N\,C^{-1}}$ | Electric field | The force per unit positive charge. |
| $\electric{\rho}$ | $\unit{C\,m^{-3}}$ | Volume charge density | The charge per unit volume. |
| $\electric V$ | $\unit{V}$ | Electric potential | The potential in Problem 3.8. |
| $\mechanical{\vec v}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-1}}$ | Velocity | The velocity of the charged particle. |
| $\magnetic{\vec B}$ | $\unit{T}$ | Magnetic flux density | The field in Problem 3.10. |
