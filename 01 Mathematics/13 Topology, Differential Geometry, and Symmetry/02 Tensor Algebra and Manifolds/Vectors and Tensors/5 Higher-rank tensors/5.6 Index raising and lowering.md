---
title: "5.6 Index raising and lowering"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The metric lowers an index, and the inverse metric raises it.

The [[5.5 Metric tensor|metric]] converts between covariant and contravariant components through contraction. To lower an index, contract it with the metric.

$$
A_i=g_{ij}A^j. \tag{5.19}
$$

To raise an index, contract it with the inverse metric.

$$
B^j=g^{ij}B_i. \tag{5.20}
$$

The same operations work on each slot of a higher-rank tensor.

$$
\begin{aligned}
A^j{}_k&=g^{ij}A_{ik},\\
C^i{}_{jk}&=g_{js}C^{is}{}_k,\\
T^{ijk}&=g^{il}T_l{}^{jk}.
\end{aligned} \tag{5.21}
$$
