> [!TLDR]
> Practice metric components, index raising, and covariant differentiation.

### 5.1 Tensor subtraction

Show that the difference of two tensors of the same type is a tensor.

### 5.2 Metric from basis vectors

Find the spherical metric components by taking dot products of the coordinate basis vectors.

### 5.3 Metric from the coordinate transformation

Use the derivatives following [[5.5 Metric tensor|Equation (5.16)]] to derive Equation (5.17).

### 5.4 Spherical differential operators

Use the spherical scale factors to verify the formulas in [[2 Vector operations|Chapter 2]] for the gradient, divergence, curl, and Laplacian.

### 5.5 Cylindrical Christoffel symbols

In cylindrical coordinates $(r,\phi,z)$, show that $\Gamma^2{}_{12}=\Gamma^2{}_{21}=1/r$.

### 5.6 Inverse spherical metric

Find the inverse metric $g^{ij}$ for spherical coordinates.

### 5.7 Raising an index

Use $g^{ij}$ to raise the index of $A_i=(1,r^2\sin\theta,\sin^2\theta)$.

### 5.8 Metric on a sphere

On the surface of a sphere of radius $R$,

$$
\mathrm ds^2=R^2\,\mathrm d\theta^2+R^2\sin^2\theta\,\mathrm d\phi^2.
$$

Find $g_{ij}$ and $g^{ij}$.

### 5.9 Connection on a sphere

Find the Christoffel symbols for the surface in Problem 5.8.

### 5.10 Covariant derivative of the metric

Show that the covariant derivative of the metric is zero.
