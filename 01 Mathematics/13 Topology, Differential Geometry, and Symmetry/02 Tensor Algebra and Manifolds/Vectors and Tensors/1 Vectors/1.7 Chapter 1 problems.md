> [!TLDR]
> Practice vector components, vector addition, coordinate conversions, and basis normalization.

### 1.1 Components and direction

a. A vector $\mechanical{\vec B}$ of magnitude $\quantity{18}{m}$ points along the negative $x$-axis. Find $\mechanical{B}_x$ and $\mechanical{B}_y$.

b. For $\mechanical{C}_x=\quantity{-3}{m\,s^{-1}}$ and $\mechanical{C}_y=\quantity{5}{m\,s^{-1}}$, find the magnitude of $\mechanical{\vec C}$ and the angle it makes with the positive $x$-axis.

### 1.2 Vector addition

The magnitude of $\mechanical{\vec A}$ is $\quantity{11}{m\,s^{-2}}$ and its angle with the positive $x$-axis is $65^\circ$. The components of $\mechanical{\vec B}$ are $\mechanical{B}_x=\quantity{4}{m\,s^{-2}}$ and $\mechanical{B}_y=\quantity{-3}{m\,s^{-2}}$.

a. Find the Cartesian components of $\mechanical{\vec C}=\mechanical{\vec A}+\mechanical{\vec B}$.

b. Find the magnitude and direction of $\mechanical{\vec C}$.

### 1.3 Position and displacement

The positive $y$-axis points north and the positive $x$-axis points east.

a. Travel $\mechanical{r}=\quantity{22}{km}$ from the origin along a line $35^\circ$ south of west. Find your Cartesian position $(\mechanical{x},\mechanical{y})$.

b. Travel $\quantity{6}{mi}$ south from the origin and then $\quantity{2}{mi}$ west. Find the distance and direction of your final position from the origin.

### 1.4 Polar unit vectors

Find the Cartesian components of $\hat r$ and $\hat\theta$ at (a) $\theta=180^\circ$, (b) $\theta=45^\circ$, and (c) $\theta=215^\circ$.

### 1.5 Cylindrical coordinates

a. Find the Cartesian coordinates for $\mechanical{r}=\quantity{2}{m}$, $\phi=35^\circ$, and $\mechanical{z}=\quantity{1}{m}$.

b. Find the cylindrical coordinates for $(\mechanical{x},\mechanical{y},\mechanical{z})=(3,2,4)\,\unit{m}$.

### 1.6 Cylindrical unit vectors

a. Show that $\hat r$ points along the positive $x$-axis when $\phi=0$.

b. Find the direction of $\hat\phi$ when $\phi=90^\circ$.

### 1.7 Spherical coordinates

a. Find the Cartesian coordinates for $\mechanical{r}=\quantity{25}{m}$, $\theta=35^\circ$, and $\phi=110^\circ$.

b. Find the spherical coordinates for $(\mechanical{x},\mechanical{y},\mechanical{z})=(8,10,15)\,\unit{m}$.

### 1.8 Spherical unit vectors

a. Show that $\hat\theta$ points along the negative $z$-axis when $\theta=90^\circ$.

b. Find the directions of $\hat r$ and $\hat\phi$ when $\theta=\phi=90^\circ$.

### 1.9 Magnetic field around a straight wire

A long straight wire carries a steady current $\electric I$. As discussed in [[3.4 The magnetic field]], its field is

$$
\magnetic{\vec B}=\frac{\magnetic{\mu}_0\electric I}{2\pi\mechanical R}\hat\phi,
$$

where $\mechanical R$ is the perpendicular distance from the wire to the observation point and $\magnetic{\mu}_0$ is the vacuum permeability. Find $\magnetic{\vec B}$ in Cartesian coordinates. The azimuthal unit vector $\hat\phi$ is the same in cylindrical and spherical coordinates about the wire axis.

### 1.10 Normalize the basis vectors

Find $\hat e_1$, $\hat e_2$, and $\hat e_3$ for

$$
\vec e_1=5\hat i-3\hat j+2\hat k,\qquad
\vec e_2=\hat j-3\hat k,\qquad
\vec e_3=2\hat i+\hat j-4\hat k.
$$
