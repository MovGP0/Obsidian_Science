> [!TLDR]
> Practice vector products, derivatives, work, and torque.

### 2.1 Scalar product and angle

For $\vec A=3\hat i+2\hat j-\hat k$ and $\vec B=\hat j+4\hat k$, find $\vec A\cdot\vec B$ and the angle between them.

### 2.2 Cross product

For $\vec J=2\hat i-\hat j+5\hat k$ and $\vec K=3\hat i+2\hat j+\hat k$, find $\vec L=\vec J\times\vec K$. Show that $\vec L$ is perpendicular to both input vectors.

### 2.3 Vector product identities

Show that $\vec A\cdot\vec B=A_xB_x+A_yB_y+A_zB_z=|\vec A|\,|\vec B|\cos\theta$ and $|\vec A\times\vec B|=|\vec A|\,|\vec B|\sin\theta$.

### 2.4 Scalar triple product

Use the vectors from Problems 2.1 and 2.2 to find $\vec J\cdot(\vec A\times\vec B)$. Compare it with $(\vec J\times\vec A)\cdot\vec B$.

### 2.5 Triple vector product

Use the vectors from Problems 2.1 and 2.2 to find $\vec J\times(\vec A\times\vec B)$. Compare it with $(\vec J\times\vec A)\times\vec B$ and $\vec B\times(\vec J\times\vec A)$.

### 2.6 Partial derivatives

For $f(x,y)=x^2+3y^2+2xy+3x+5$, find $\partial f/\partial x$ and $\partial f/\partial y$.

### 2.7 Gradient

For $\phi=x^2+y^2$, find $\nabla\phi$ at $(\mechanical{x},\mechanical{y})=(\quantity{3}{cm},\quantity{-2}{cm})$.

### 2.8 Divergence

Find the divergence of $\vec C=5xy\,\hat i-3x\,\hat j+5z^2\hat k$.

### 2.9 Curl

Find the curl of the field in Problem 2.8.

### 2.10 Laplacian

Find the Laplacian of the function in Problem 2.6.

### 2.11 Work done by gravity

The work done by a constant force $\mechanical{\vec F}$ along a displacement $\mathrm d\mechanical{\vec r}$ is the scalar product $\mathrm d\energy{W}=\mechanical{\vec F}\cdot\mathrm d\mechanical{\vec r}$. A car of mass $\quantity{1200}{kg}$ moves $\quantity{50}{m}$ down a hill at $20^\circ$ below the horizontal. Find the work done by gravity, a vertically downward force of magnitude $\mechanical{m}\mechanical{g}$.

### 2.12 Torque on a bolt

Push on a wrench with a force $\mechanical{\vec F}$ of magnitude $\quantity{25}{N}$ at a distance $\quantity{12}{cm}$ from the rotation point. The torque is $\mechanical{\vec\tau}=\mechanical{\vec r}\times\mechanical{\vec F}$, where $\mechanical{\vec r}$ points from the rotation point to the point of application. Find the direction of the force that maximizes the torque and the resulting magnitude.

## Physical quantities

| Symbol | Unit | Quantity | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mechanical{x}$, $\mechanical{y}$ | $\unit{cm}$ | Position coordinates | The coordinates in Problem 2.7. |
| $\energy{W}$ | $\unit{J}$ | Work | The work done by a force. |
| $\mechanical{\vec F}$ | $\unit{N}$ | Force | The force of gravity or the applied force on the wrench. |
| $\mathrm d\mechanical{\vec r}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Displacement element | The change in position in the work expression. |
| $\mechanical{m}$ | $\unit{kg}$ | Mass | The mass of the car. |
| $\mechanical{g}$ | $\unit{m\,s^{-2}}$ | Gravitational acceleration | The magnitude of Earth’s gravitational acceleration. |
| $\mechanical{\vec\tau}$ | $\unit{N\,m}$ | Torque | The moment of the force about the bolt. |
| $\mechanical{\vec r}$ | $\unit{m}$ | Lever-arm vector | The displacement from the rotation point to the point of application. |
