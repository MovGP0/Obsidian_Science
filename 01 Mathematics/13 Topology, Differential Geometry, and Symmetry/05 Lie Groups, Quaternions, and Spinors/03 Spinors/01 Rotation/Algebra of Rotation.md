## Representations of Rotations

There are different methods to represent rotations:

- [[Rotation#Axis-Angle Vector|Axis-Angle Vector]]: parametrizes $SO(3)$
- [[Rotation using Matrices]]: realize $SO(n)$
- [[Rotation using Complex Numbers]]: unit complex numbers realize $U(1)\cong SO(2)$; [[Quaternions]]: unit quaternions realize $SU(2)$, a double cover of $SO(3)$
- [[Spinors]]: transform under $SU(2)=\mathrm{Spin}(3)$, a double cover of $SO(3)$
