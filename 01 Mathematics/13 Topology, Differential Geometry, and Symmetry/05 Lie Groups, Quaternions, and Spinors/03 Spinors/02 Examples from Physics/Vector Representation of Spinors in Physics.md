
The spinor
$$\ket{\phi} = \alpha \ket{\uparrow} + \beta \ket{\downarrow}$$
with $|\alpha|^2 + |\beta|^2 = 1$ can be represented as vector
$$\phi = \begin{bmatrix} \alpha \\ \beta \end{bmatrix}$$

| Variable | Description                                    |
| -------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| $\phi$   | Spinor                                         |
| $\alpha$ | Complex probability amplitude of the Spin-Up state; the probability is $\lvert\alpha\rvert^2$   |
| $\beta$  | Complex probability amplitude of the Spin-Down state; the probability is $\lvert\beta\rvert^2$ |
