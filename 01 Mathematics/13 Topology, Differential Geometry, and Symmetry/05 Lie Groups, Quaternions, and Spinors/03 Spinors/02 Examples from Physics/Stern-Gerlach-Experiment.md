- Spin-Up ($\ket{\uparrow}$) and Spin-Down ($\ket{\downarrow}$) are 
	- 180° apart in **Physical Space** ($\ket{\uparrow}$, $\ket{\downarrow}$)
	- 90° apart in **Abstract State Space** ($\ket{\uparrow}$, $\ket{\downarrow}$, $-\ket{\uparrow}$, $-\ket{\downarrow}$)

**Born Rule**
	The probability to measure the state $\ket{\beta}$ in the state $\ket{\alpha}$ is $P = |\braket{\beta|\alpha}|^2$.
	Thus, if two states $\ket{\alpha}$ and $\ket{\beta}$ are mutually exclusive (perfectly distinguishable), they must be orthogonal $\braket{\alpha|\beta} = 0$

$$\braket{\uparrow|\downarrow} = 0$$
