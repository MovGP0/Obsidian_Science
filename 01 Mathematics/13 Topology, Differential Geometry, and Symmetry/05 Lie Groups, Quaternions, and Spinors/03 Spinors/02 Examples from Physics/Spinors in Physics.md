- Bosons (Forces)
	- Spin1 Particles - Vectors
		- Gluons, Photon, W+ Boson, W- Boson, Z0 Boson 
	- Spin0 Particles - Scalars
		- Higgs boson
- Fermions (Matter)
	- Spin 1/2 - Spinors

## Bosons

- Integer Spin
- The spin quantum number $s$ is an integer. The maximal projection of the intrinsic angular momentum is $s\hbar$, and its magnitude is $\sqrt{s(s+1)}\hbar$.
- Their wavefunctions are not spinors
- Many particles can occupy the same state at the same time
  (See [[Spin-Statistics-Theorem]])

## Fermions

- Half-Integer Spin
- The spin quantum number is $s = \frac{n}{2}$, where $n$ is an odd integer. The maximal projection of the intrinsic angular momentum (spin) is $s\hbar$, and its magnitude is $\sqrt{s(s+1)}\hbar$.
- Their wavefunctions are spinors
- Representation by an bispinor field
- Particles can not occupy the same state at the same time
  (See [[Spin-Statistics-Theorem]])
