---
title: "Category of Categories"
---

> [!TLDR]
> The category $\mathbf{Cat}$ has small categories as objects and functors as morphisms.

The category $\mathbf{Cat}$ has small [[Category|categories]] as objects and [[Functor|functors]] as morphisms. Composition is the composition of functors, and the identity morphisms are the identity functors.

> [!note]
> A category of *all* categories would lead to a size paradox. Thus $\mathbf{Cat}$ contains only small categories, whose objects and morphisms form sets.
