Scalar product
$$ c = a\cdot b $$
$$ c = a_i\,b_i = \sum_i a_i\,b_i $$

Magnitude and angle of vectors (components in an orthonormal basis)
$$ \forall a,b\in\mathbb{R}^n: a\cdot b = |a|\,|b|\,\cos\gamma $$
$$ \cos\gamma = \frac{a\cdot b}{|a|\,|b|}, \quad a,b\neq0 $$
